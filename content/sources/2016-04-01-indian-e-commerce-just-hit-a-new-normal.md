## Core Argument
New e-commerce policy shifts India's internet retail from unregulated free-for-all to balanced marketplace with clear rules.

## Key Points
- Marketplaces must act as neutral platforms; sellers control pricing and discounts, not marketplace algorithms.
- Foreign-funded online players banned from inventory-led models; group companies capped at 25% revenue to prevent monopoly control.
- Offline and online retailers now play by equal rules, eliminating capital-driven pricing distortions that favored e-commerce.

## Open Question
Will stricter regulations slow innovation and foreign investment in Indian e-commerce, or will they create sustainable long-term competition?

## India Angle
India's e-commerce sector transitions from unregulated VC-driven competition to rule-based marketplace, directly affecting how Flipkart, Amazon, Snapdeal operate and how Indian sellers compete with offline retail giants like Reliance and Future Group.
