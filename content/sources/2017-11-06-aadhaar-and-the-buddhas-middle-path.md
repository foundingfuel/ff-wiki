## Core Argument
Complex policy problems require nuanced middle-path thinking, not binary for/against polarisation.

## Key Points
- Technology trade-offs exist between convenience and security; systems evolve but never reach perfection.
- Decision-makers must listen to diverse voices to understand real fears, not dismiss concerns as ideology.
- India should adopt a precautionary middle path on Aadhaar rather than copying permissive or prohibitive global models.

## Open Question
How can policymakers genuinely explore nuanced middle paths when public discourse and media incentivise polarised for/against positions?

## India Angle
Aadhaar exemplifies India's policy dilemma: balancing state capacity-building with individual privacy rights. India must navigate between permissive (US) and prohibitive (EU) approaches, finding indigenous solutions suited to Indian context and capabilities.
