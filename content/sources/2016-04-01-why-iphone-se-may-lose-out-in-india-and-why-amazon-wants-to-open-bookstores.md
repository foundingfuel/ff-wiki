## Core Argument
iPhone SE's success depends on market fit: China wants small+powerful+affordable; India demands cheap above all else.

## Key Points
- Import duties inflate iPhone SE price to Rs 39,000, making existing iPhone 6/6S more attractive to Indian buyers
- Chinese first-time buyers seek compact premium devices; Indian buyers prioritize affordability over form factor
- Local manufacturers (Xiaomi, Micromax) already own sub-Rs 10,000 segment; Apple won't enter price war despite SE launch

## Open Question
Can Apple succeed in India without manufacturing locally when import duties fundamentally break its pricing strategy, or will it be forced to compromise its product-centric philosophy?

## India Angle
iPhone SE will struggle in India due to 47% import duties making it Rs 39,000—undercutting existing iPhone 6 pricing. Indian consumers want cheaper phones, not smaller ones; local manufacturers already dominate sub-Rs 10,000 segment. Apple's refusal to manufacture locally in India limits its competitive advantage unlike in China.
