## Core Argument
High startup valuations signal a structural shift in India's capital markets favoring entrepreneurs over investors.

## Key Points
- Entrepreneurs now negotiate from strength as capital competition increases; investors must justify why founders should accept their money.
- Global and local capital competition is importing venture ecosystem dynamics from the US, reshaping how ideas are financed in India.
- Corporate venturing by tech giants and Indian software firms signals mainstream industries will eventually face similar capital-to-enterprise power shifts.

## Open Question
Will high valuations and startup enthusiasm sustain as a structural feature of India's economy, or does it remain a speculative bubble limited to tech sectors?

## India Angle
India's venture capital ecosystem is maturing rapidly, with global capital competing for Indian startups and local VHNWIs entering the market—shifting power dynamics from investors to entrepreneurs for the first time, replicating the US model domestically.
