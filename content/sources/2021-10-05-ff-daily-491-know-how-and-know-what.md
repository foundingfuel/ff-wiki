## Core Argument
Strategy without execution know-how is meaningless; implementation discipline matters more than brilliant ideas.

## Key Points
- Know-how (execution) is as critical as know-what (strategy) in achieving goals.
- The 4 Disciplines framework—focus, lead measures, scoreboard, accountability—converts strategy into actionable execution.
- Entrenched leaders often understand strategy but lack operational mechanisms to implement disruptive responses.

## Open Question
If leaders understand what disruption requires, why is the execution gap so hard to close—is it organizational inertia, capability gaps, or misaligned incentives?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders often excel at strategy (know-what) but struggle with execution and operational know-how. This disconnect between vision and implementation is a critical barrier to scaling Indian startups and organizational transformations.
