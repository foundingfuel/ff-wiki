## Core Argument
Leadership effectiveness depends on market conditions: peacetime requires protocol and delegation; wartime demands protocol violation and paranoia.

## Key Points
- Peacetime CEOs empower through scalability; wartime CEOs obsess over execution details and existential threats.
- Context determines leadership style—no universal management approach works across all business conditions.
- Incentive misalignment (clicks, traffic targets) can corrupt institutional credibility and editorial standards.

## Open Question
How can a CEO develop both peacetime and wartime capabilities to pivot effectively when conditions shift, without becoming ineffective in either mode?

## India Angle
Indian founders and CEOs transitioning between growth and crisis phases need to recognize contextual leadership shifts; applies to startups scaling rapidly then facing market downturns, and to traditional media outlets facing digital disruption.
