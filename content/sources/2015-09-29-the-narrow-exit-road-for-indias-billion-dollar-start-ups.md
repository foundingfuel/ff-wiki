## Core Argument
Indian unicorns must achieve profitability within three years or face severely constrained exit options.

## Key Points
- Indian e-commerce companies trade at 40-60X earnings multiples, not the 200X of pure-play internet businesses, limiting IPO valuation potential.
- Current GMV-driven spending models cannot sustain valuations; Flipkart needs $400M+ annual earnings to justify a $20B valuation.
- Competitive intensity and cash-burn dynamics create a paradox: scaling erodes unit economics, yet slowing growth reduces enterprise value.

## Open Question
How can Indian unicorns transition from growth-at-all-costs to profitable scaling without losing competitive position to better-funded rivals or triggering a valuation reset?

## India Angle
Indian unicorns face structurally narrower exit paths than Western counterparts due to lower trading multiples, immature public markets, and the challenge of achieving profitability at scale—critical for the $300M+ earnings needed to justify current valuations at IPO.
