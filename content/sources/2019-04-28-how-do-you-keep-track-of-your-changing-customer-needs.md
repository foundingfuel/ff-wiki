## Core Argument
Enterprises survive digitization by centering customer jobs-to-be-done, not chasing technology trends.

## Key Points
- Digitization is a strategic inflection point requiring fundamental business rethinking, not just tech implementation
- Understanding why customers hire your product—their underlying jobs—provides clarity amid technological noise
- Keeping customer needs central helps enterprises distinguish between core survival issues and shiny distractions

## Open Question
How can enterprises systematically discover and track evolving customer jobs-to-be-done without becoming paralyzed by constant change?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises navigating digitization waves must avoid chasing technology trends and instead anchor strategy in understanding evolving customer needs—critical for SMEs and established companies competing globally.
