## Core Argument
Effective data design emerges from business user leadership, low-fidelity prototyping, and multi-metric frameworks grounded in reality.

## Key Points
- Business users must lead process design; embed data analysts within teams to identify pain points collaboratively.
- Prototype in Excel or Google Drive before coding; iterate until 20-30% improvement proven before scaling.
- Build dashboards around three metrics across different buckets to avoid single-metric bias distorting decisions.

## Open Question
How do organisations balance the need for comprehensive data visibility against the risk of decision paralysis when multiple metrics pull in conflicting directions?

## India Angle
Small and medium Indian companies face resource constraints; this pragmatic, iterative approach to data infrastructure is directly applicable to Indian startups and mid-market firms building operational capabilities.
