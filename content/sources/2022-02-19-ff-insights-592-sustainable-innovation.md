## Core Argument
Transformative businesses rarely start disruptive; they evolve through champions navigating uncertainty like leaves in tornadoes.

## Key Points
- Disruptive potential emerges through evolution, not initial design—Google and Walmart were sustaining innovations that became transformative.
- India's poverty economics reveal massive untapped markets; 75% live below Rs 100/day, requiring radically different business models.
- Leader personality cults mislead entrepreneurs; mimicking Jobs' rudeness or Musk's eccentricity without their substance creates bad bosses.

## Open Question
How can entrepreneurs plan for and nurture unpredictable evolution while building viable businesses today, especially in resource-constrained Indian markets?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs must recognize that transformative businesses often start with incremental innovations and evolve unpredictably; the poverty economics discussion highlights market opportunities in underserved segments like rural India and the 'average poor' consumer
