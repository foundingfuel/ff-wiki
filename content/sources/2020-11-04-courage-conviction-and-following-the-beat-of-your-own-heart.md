## Core Argument
Following passion requires courage to abandon security and permission to reimagine your purpose repeatedly.

## Key Points
- Passion is neither hereditary nor easily quantifiable; it emerges through exposure and experimentation over time.
- Role models demonstrate two types: steadfast, all-consuming passion versus malleable, adaptable passion across different domains.
- Fear of feasibility and late starts paralyzes decisions; true commitment dissolves pros-cons analysis entirely.

## Open Question
How do you distinguish between societal permission to explore (privilege) and genuine freedom to pursue unconventional paths without safety nets?
