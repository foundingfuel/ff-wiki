## Core Argument
Remote work demands new meeting disciplines; tools like Calendly, Zoom, Otter.ai restore control and sanity.

## Key Points
- Virtual work eliminates informal spontaneous interactions, requiring intentional meeting scheduling infrastructure.
- Unrestricted calendar access creates meeting overload; boundaries and automation protect leader time and energy.
- Recording and transcription tools shift focus from note-taking to active listening and create searchable knowledge archives.

## Open Question
How do we preserve the spontaneity and serendipity of in-person collaboration while imposing structure needed for remote work efficiency?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and distributed teams adopting remote work need practical tools to manage meeting overload—a challenge amplified by timezone coordination across India's dispersed workforce and hierarchical organizational structures.
