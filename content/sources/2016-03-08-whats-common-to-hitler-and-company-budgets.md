## Core Argument
Obsession with short-term targets destroys strategic capacity; budgeting must serve strategy, not ambition.

## Key Points
- Budgeting should align with strategic intent, not become mechanical quarterly ritual divorced from purpose
- Leaders who reject contrarian advice and overcommit resources to win immediate battles lose the war
- Maintaining strategic reserves—financial, human, organizational—is essential for navigating crises and unexpected shifts

## Open Question
How do leaders distinguish between inspirational 'never retreat' conviction and delusional overcommitment that destroys organizational resilience?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and managers often fall into the same trap of chasing quarterly targets at the cost of strategic reserves and long-term resilience, especially in family-run businesses and startups where founder ambition overrides counsel.
