## Core Argument
China's economic slowdown masks systemic efficiency and long-term thinking India could adopt.

## Key Points
- India's projected faster growth rate is changing Chinese perception of India's economic relevance and openness to engagement.
- China balances short-term commercial interests with long-term preservation, seen in measured archaeological and environmental approaches.
- China's technocratic approach achieves environmental cleanup and anti-corruption results that democratic India struggles to replicate.

## Open Question
Can India replicate China's pragmatic long-term thinking and technocratic execution while maintaining democratic pluralism, or are these fundamentally incompatible?

## India Angle
India's faster growth trajectory is reshaping perceptions of Indian economy globally and presents opportunities for academic/business partnerships, while India lags in environmental cleanup pragmatism and anti-corruption enforcement despite democratic advantages.
