## Core Argument
Covid-19 reversed global decline in home cooking; entrepreneurs must reframe cooking as convenient, not cumbersome.

## Key Points
- Home-cooked food carries emotional, nostalgic, and health value distinct from commercial restaurant food.
- Younger generations perceive 'going out' as integral to eating out; pre-lockdown generations separated these concepts.
- Pandemic forced cooking as necessity then activity; some sustained it, proving cooking accessibility when framed differently.

## Open Question
How can entrepreneurs make home cooking convenient and rewarding for generations wired to see 'going out' as essential to dining, without relying on crisis-driven behavioural shifts?

## India Angle
Home cooking as cultural practice and health imperative in India; tension between modernisation (eating out) and traditional values (ghar ka khana); Indian food entrepreneurs navigating lifestyle shifts and pandemic-induced behavioural changes.
