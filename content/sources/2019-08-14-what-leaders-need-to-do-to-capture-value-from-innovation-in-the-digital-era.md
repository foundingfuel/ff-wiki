## Core Argument
Digital technologies fundamentally alter innovation scope and process; leaders must rethink how organizations pipeline, accelerate, and convert ideas.

## Key Points
- Digital enables product, process, customer experience, and business model innovation simultaneously across all sectors.
- AI acts as both general-purpose technology and invention method, lowering R&D costs and solving new problem classes.
- Traditional innovation challenges (pipeline, velocity, batting average) remain but require different organizational and process approaches in digital era.

## Open Question
How can companies balance the need for continuous digital innovation with building sustainable, profitable business models when even leaders like Uber and Amazon struggle with profitability despite disruption?

## India Angle
Indian companies must rethink innovation management as digital technologies (AI, IoT, automation) reshape products and processes. Examples like Niramai and Ola show how Indian startups and businesses can leverage digital innovation for customization and new business models, but face challenges in sustainable scaling.
