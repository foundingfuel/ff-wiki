## Core Argument
Leaders must develop frameworks to navigate moral ambiguity rather than seeking false certainty in complex decisions.

## Key Points
- Leaders need shields against external polarization to maintain team focus on mission and values.
- Badaracco's five-question framework helps navigate grey-zone decisions with no clear right answers affecting stakeholders.
- Decision-making requires balancing analytical skills, human judgment, and ethical reasoning in business dilemmas.

## Open Question
How can leaders apply rational frameworks to inherently emotional decisions when choices equally hurt different stakeholder groups?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders navigating polarized discourse while managing ethically ambiguous decisions affecting employees and stakeholders—relevant amid India's polarized public discourse and diverse organizational values.
