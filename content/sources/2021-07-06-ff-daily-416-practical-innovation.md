## Core Argument
Practical innovation solving immediate problems matters more than speculative breakthroughs; India must prioritize accordingly.

## Key Points
- We obsess over transformative innovations while ignoring practical solutions already available—boarding planes faster, for instance.
- GDP growth is a flawed metric; it rises equally from harm (accidents, deforestation) and progress, masking inequality.
- Vaccine hesitancy in India stems from fear and distrust, not cost or religion; honest risk comparison builds trust better than dismissal.

## Open Question
How do we incentivize and celebrate practical innovation when human psychology gravitates toward glamorous, disruptive breakthroughs that often serve vanity over utility?

## India Angle
India faces dual challenges: vaccine hesitancy undermining public health progress, and the need to prioritize practical innovations solving immediate problems (healthcare access, sanitation, education) over speculative ventures that consume resources without addressing ground realities.
