## Core Argument
Breakthrough technologies promise immense benefits but require careful governance to mitigate harms and ethical risks.

## Key Points
- Drones moving from military to civilian use (delivery, healthcare) but face geopolitical tensions and regulatory gaps.
- Quantum computing attracting massive VC investment with competing platforms; commercialization race intensifies among tech giants.
- CRISPR gene-editing shows cancer-fighting promise but demands cautious, incremental testing with transparent communication.

## Open Question
How can societies accelerate adoption of transformative technologies while building adequate safeguards against misuse and unintended consequences before mass deployment occurs?

## India Angle
India lacks clear regulatory frameworks for emerging tech like drones and quantum computing; opportunities exist for Indian startups and enterprises to leapfrog, but policy must balance innovation with security and ethical concerns.
