## Core Argument
Economies must be reformed to serve human and ecological well-being, not vice versa.

## Key Points
- Current economic models optimize GDP growth at the expense of holistic human well-being and environmental sustainability.
- India has viable alternatives to Visa/Mastercard (UPI, RuPay, Nexus) but adoption requires shifting entrenched consumer behavior.
- True prosperity requires recoupling economy with society through caring for environment and others, not exploiting both for shareholder wealth.

## Open Question
How can India build indigenous financial infrastructure and shift to well-being-centered economics when the 1-2% with purchasing power resists behavioral change away from established global payment systems?

## India Angle
India must decouple economic growth from human well-being and build indigenous payment systems (RuPay, UPI, Nexus) to reduce dependence on Western financial infrastructure while rethinking the purpose of its economy.
