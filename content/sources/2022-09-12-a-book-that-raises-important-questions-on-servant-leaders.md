## Core Argument
Corporate governance must prioritize societal values and all stakeholders over shareholder returns alone.

## Key Points
- 20th-century governance models prioritizing shareholder value have harmed society and environment.
- Tata group's complexity as conglomerate challenges conventional governance principles and single-shareholder dominance.
- Genuine corporate responsibility requires structural change beyond CSR and philanthropy.

## Open Question
How can a diversified conglomerate be truly values-driven while operating within corporate governance frameworks legally designed to serve shareholder interests above all?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates like Tata must navigate tension between values-driven leadership and shareholder capitalism; critical for how Indian business groups govern themselves amid global expansion and domestic stakeholder expectations.
