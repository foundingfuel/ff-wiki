## Core Argument
A consultant's true success lies in making themselves dispensable by building client capability.

## Key Points
- Effective consultants develop client self-reliance, not ongoing dependency relationships.
- Success means the client no longer needs the consultant's frequent involvement or validation.
- The consultant-client relationship should be contractual and mission-driven, not ego-driven.

## Open Question
How do consultants balance their business model of ongoing engagement with their stated mission of making clients independent?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often struggle with dependency on external consultants; building internal leadership capability is critical for sustainable transformation in Indian companies navigating growth and complexity.
