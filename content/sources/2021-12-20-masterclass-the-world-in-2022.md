## Core Argument
Global economy faces paradox: geopolitical fragmentation collides with climate integration needs.

## Key Points
- US-China-Russia trilateral conflict reshapes security and economic decoupling, affecting supply chains and inflation globally.
- China's demographic shift and consumption transition will restore Western labour bargaining power and raise borrowing costs.
- Climate and cleantech become economic opportunities, not just policy mandates; but geopolitical tensions complicate necessary cooperation.

## Open Question
How can nations cooperate on existential climate threats while locked in escalating geopolitical and technological competition?

## India Angle
India navigates contradictory geopolitical and climate interests: US-aligned on security, China-aligned on climate action. Strategic positioning amid US-China decoupling, supply chain shifts, and labour market dynamics will reshape India's economic trajectory and regional role.
