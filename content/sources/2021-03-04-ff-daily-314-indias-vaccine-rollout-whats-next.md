## Core Argument
India's vaccine rollout falters due to government micro-management and mistrust of private sector capacity.

## Key Points
- Government hospitals lack capacity to proactively reach populations; private sector collaboration essential but hampered by regulatory mistrust.
- Unrealistic Rs 250 price cap (Rs 100 margin) forces loss-leader strategies; inefficient supply chain creates hidden costs and wastage.
- Excessive government control of logistics and vaccination timings creates bottlenecks; need to leverage existing pharma distribution networks.

## Open Question
How can India shift from controlling every aspect of vaccine delivery to enabling private sector participation without sacrificing equity or losing program oversight?

## India Angle
India's vaccine rollout reveals critical gaps in state capacity and government-private sector collaboration; systemic inefficiencies in logistics, pricing controls, and micro-management undermine rollout speed and efficiency compared to developed nations.
