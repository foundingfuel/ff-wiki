## Core Argument
Rashid Khan's transcendent voice resulted from rigorous traditional training that enabled effortless mastery across ragas.

## Key Points
- Khan's deep, sonorous voice was a gateway drug for initiates into Hindustani classical music.
- Guru Nisar Hussain Khan's strict taalim—years of sargams and paltas before ragas—built Khan's legendary stamina.
- Khan balanced classical purity with popular collaborations, proving versatility need not compromise artistic integrity.

## Open Question
How do we preserve rigorous traditional training standards in modern contexts where corporal punishment is unacceptable, yet maintain the depth Khan achieved?
