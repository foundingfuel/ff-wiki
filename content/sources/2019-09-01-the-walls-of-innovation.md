## Core Argument
Innovation becomes real only when organizations make hard trade-offs and reshape culture, not through speeches and projects.

## Key Points
- Organizations confuse innovation rhetoric with action; speeches and training programs fail without sustained systemic effort.
- True commitment emerges through trade-offs: stopping experiments during revenue decline, tolerating failure, and protecting innovation teams.
- Established routines and cultural inertia are the real barriers; sensemaking through historical perspective matters amid disruption noise.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance the short-term pressure of quarterly results with the long-term patience required for genuine innovation to take root?

## India Angle
Indian organizations struggle to embed innovation beyond rhetoric; requires understanding trade-offs, cultural inertia, and ecosystem complexity specific to India's business context.
