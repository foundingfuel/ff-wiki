## Core Argument
Holacracy promises distributed authority but may simply replace vertical bureaucracy with horizontal complexity.

## Key Points
- Holacracy replaces hierarchies with self-organizing circles, enabling entrepreneurial employee behavior and distributed decision-making.
- Critics argue it hides rather than removes hierarchy, creates procedural gatekeeping, and conflicts with human organizational instincts.
- Early adoption shows mixed results: Zappos saw 14% employee exodus; impact depends on company size and culture readiness.

## Open Question
Can a system designed to eliminate hierarchy actually function without recreating it through procedural complexity and informal power dynamics?

## India Angle
Indian companies experimenting with flat structures face similar tensions between Western management ideals and local hierarchical norms; relevant for tech startups and scaling enterprises seeking alternative models.
