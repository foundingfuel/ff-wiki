## Core Argument
Purpose-driven work rooted in devotion creates better outcomes than ambition-driven achievement seeking validation.

## Key Points
- Hanuman's Ramayana succeeded because it was written from devotion to Ram, not desire for fame or recognition
- India's elderly care must transition from family-based to integrated systems like Singapore's to enable aging in place with dignity
- Physical bookstores survive pandemic through community connection and adaptation, not just ecommerce competition

## Open Question
How can India maintain its traditional family-care values while building institutional elderly care systems that serve diverse family circumstances across its many regions?

## India Angle
India's family-based care systems and traditional structures must evolve to address elderly care as urbanization and nuclear families increase; the Hanuman parable reflects Indian philosophical values of duty and purpose over ambition—relevant to how Indian leaders and institutions approach work
