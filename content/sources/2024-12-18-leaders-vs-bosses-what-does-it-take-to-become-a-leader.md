## Core Argument
Leaders earn trust through integrity, humility, and people-first decisions; bosses rely on authority and short-term optimization.

## Key Points
- Leaders prioritize human dignity over profit maximization, even during crises; bosses choose expedient cost-cutting.
- Equanimity and self-mastery—drawn from ancient Indian philosophy—are non-negotiable leadership traits in volatile times.
- Long-term value creation requires value-based decisions that incur short-term costs but build credibility and ecosystem loyalty.

## Open Question
How do leaders maintain authenticity and people-first values while still making tough, economically necessary decisions that harm individuals—and can this tension ever be fully resolved?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders redefining leadership through indigenous philosophy (Gita), addressing regional income disparities, and navigating crises with people-first approaches—a counter to Western management models often adopted uncritically.
