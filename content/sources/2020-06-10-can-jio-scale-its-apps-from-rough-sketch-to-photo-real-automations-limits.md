## Core Argument
Building apps at scale requires marketing acumen and network effects, not just infrastructure engineering.

## Key Points
- Jio's 338M users and 5-hour daily usage provide scale, but don't guarantee app success without design excellence and network effects.
- App scaling is fundamentally a marketing and design problem, distinct from infrastructure engineering—Zoom's dominance over JioConnect proves the point.
- Automation has hard limits; human judgment and adaptability remain irreplaceable in complex, precision-required tasks.

## Open Question
How can large user bases and infrastructure advantages translate to viral consumer apps when marketing, design, and network effects remain unpredictable and science lacks systematic prediction methods?

## India Angle
Jio's platform ambitions and app scaling challenges directly test whether India's largest telecom can leverage 338M users to build consumer apps competitively; also examines how Indian companies can innovate at micro-market level through technology platforms.
