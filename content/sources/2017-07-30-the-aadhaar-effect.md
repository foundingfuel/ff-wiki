## Core Argument
Aadhaar debate requires multidisciplinary lens beyond privacy to understand identity, money, and state evolution.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar's original intent as identity system has expanded into complex socio-political ecosystem with competing narratives.
- Privacy debate obscures deeper questions about money, identity evolution, and state capacity in digital era.
- Understanding Aadhaar requires voices from history, philosophy, politics, entrepreneurship, and AI—not siloed technical analysis.

## Open Question
How do we balance Aadhaar's potential for financial inclusion and state efficiency against legitimate privacy and autonomy concerns without reducing the debate to ideological positions?

## India Angle
Aadhaar is a foundational India-specific project reshaping identity, financial inclusion, and state capacity. Understanding its implications across privacy, governance, and societal impact is critical for Indian policymakers, technologists, and citizens navigating digital transformation at scale.
