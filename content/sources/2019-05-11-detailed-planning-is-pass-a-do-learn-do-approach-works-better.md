## Core Argument
Do-learn-do cycles with feedback beats detailed planning for complex, dynamic execution.

## Key Points
- Nandan Nilekani succeeded at Aadhaar by constantly seeking feedback and adapting, not by detailed upfront planning.
- Agile methodology mirrors this—software development shifted from waterfall to iterative cycles as environments became too dynamic.
- Vision provides direction; flexibility, feedback loops, and willingness to learn enable execution in uncertain conditions.

## Open Question
How do you maintain coherent long-term vision while constantly adapting to feedback without losing strategic direction or becoming reactive?

## India Angle
Drawing from Nandan Nilekani's Aadhaar success, illustrates how Indian leaders can execute massive, complex public-sector projects amid skepticism by embracing adaptive cycles over rigid planning—relevant for large-scale infrastructure and government-led initiatives in India.
