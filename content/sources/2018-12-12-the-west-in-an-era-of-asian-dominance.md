## Core Argument
The West's decline is inevitable; it must adapt strategically rather than resist China's rise.

## Key Points
- China's WTO entry (2001) injected 800M workers into global capitalism, causing creative destruction in developed economies.
- Western elites benefited from globalization while masses suffered wage stagnation, fueling Trump's election as inevitable backlash.
- For 1,800 of past 2,000 years, China and India were world's largest economies; current Western dominance is historical aberration.

## Open Question
How can the West maintain influence and prosperity in an Asian-dominated era if it cannot reverse globalization's structural effects on domestic wages and employment?

## India Angle
India's re-emergence as a major economic power alongside China reshapes global power dynamics; Indian policymakers must navigate strategic positioning in a multipolar world and prepare for shift from Western-led to Asian-led global order.
