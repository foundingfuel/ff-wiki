## Core Argument
Customer-centric culture and direct intuition over proxies drive sustainable innovation and competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- View customers as perpetually dissatisfied; experiment, fail fast, and double down on delight.
- Resist management proxies like surveys; develop intuition through first-hand customer experience.
- Large established companies risk irrelevance without leadership that actively drives cultural change toward innovation.

## Open Question
How can large, complex organizations maintain the intuition-driven, experimental culture Bezos describes while managing the systems and governance required at scale?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and incumbents like Flipkart face existential pressure from global giants like Amazon; Bezos's playbook offers lessons for how Indian leaders can build customer-centric, innovation-driven cultures to compete. India is positioned as a laboratory for contemporary management case studies.
