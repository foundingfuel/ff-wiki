## Core Argument
Post-pandemic work requires leaders to rebuild trust, shift mindsets, and redesign culture around autonomy rather than surveillance.

## Key Points
- Productivity increases with remote work when leaders coach autonomously; surveillance erodes trust and causes burnout.
- Trust in hybrid models requires both swift trust (competence) and emotional trust (empathy, frequent contact, shared values).
- Leaders must become continuous learners, facilitating digital-first connection and equity in hybrid environments with asynchronous workflows.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain culture and control when employees refuse office return, yet physical spaces are redesigned for social distancing that already prevents full co-location?

## India Angle
Indian leaders like ICICI Lombard's Bhargav Dasgupta are navigating hybrid models in inter-generational homes with poor infrastructure; India's rapid digital transformation and hierarchical management cultures must evolve toward coach-based leadership and trust-based models.
