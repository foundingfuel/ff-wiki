## Core Argument
Platform collaboration across fragmented healthcare stakeholders can create public utility solving India's health system fragmentation.

## Key Points
- India's Rs 10 lakh crore healthcare spending is fragmented into microscopic pieces across 8 lakh crore private sector spending.
- Strong public health functions for surveillance, epidemiology, contact tracing absent—even in well-designed systems—exposed by COVID-19.
- Swasth app demonstrates that diverse institutions (hospitals, startups, insurers, experts) can build collaborative electronic backbone to connect fragmented players.

## Open Question
How can a decentralized, open-source platform navigate data privacy, governance, and regulatory oversight without a single coordinating authority?

## India Angle
India's fragmented healthcare system (Rs 8 lakh crore in microscopic private pieces) needs platform-based integration. COVID-19 exposed absence of public health surveillance/epidemiology function. Swasth app models collaborative ecosystem approach to bridge gap between primary care, hospitals, insurers, and pharmacies at scale.
