## Core Argument
Large organisations must institutionalise startup thinking while maintaining execution capability to survive disruption.

## Key Points
- Established firms need dual structures: 'zero-to-one' creation units and 'one-to-hundred' execution units with technology as enabler.
- Diverse, non-traditional talent pools enable organisations to question conventions and make bold bets in disrupted markets.
- Leadership in crisis means giving hope and faith in people, not having all answers; grounded vision requires validating core assumptions.

## Open Question
How can large organisations maintain the startup mentality's speed and risk-taking while preserving the execution discipline and consistency that scale demands?

## India Angle
Indian large enterprises (Future Group, Nestlé India, Star India, Tata, Wipro) are learning to operate with startup mentality while maintaining scale—critical for competing in global markets and retaining talent in India's fast-disrupting economy.
