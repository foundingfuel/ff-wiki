## Core Argument
Pandemic revealed quantum computing's resilience; remote-capable research thrives while traditional labs struggle.

## Key Points
- Quantum computing, AI/ML, HPC uniquely suited to remote work and retain pandemic-era funding despite economic downturn.
- Simulating quantum systems beyond current supercomputer capability; potential applications in drug discovery and molecular modeling.
- Career transition requires theoretical flexibility—author pivoted from experimental to theoretical work when labs closed.

## Open Question
How can India build world-class quantum research talent and infrastructure when funding is uncertain and top researchers often work remotely for foreign labs?

## India Angle
India's quantum computing capabilities lag globally; building research talent pipeline and funding infrastructure critical as US/China advance. Modi's 'quantum jump' rhetoric misses technical reality—India needs sustained funding and talent retention.
