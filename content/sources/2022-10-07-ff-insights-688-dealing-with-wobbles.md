## Core Argument
Successful leaders reframe crises by understanding stakeholders' underlying concerns, not just surface reactions.

## Key Points
- CEOs must adapt quickly when external shocks occur—strategy isn't static, it evolves with stakeholder perspective shifts.
- Indian startups caught in funding winter must survive layoffs while maintaining trust and culture during contraction.
- Inflation disproportionately harms the powerless; fighting it requires painful recession that hits the already-vulnerable hardest.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance rapid adaptation to external crises with maintaining organizational culture and employee trust when forced to make painful decisions like mass layoffs?
