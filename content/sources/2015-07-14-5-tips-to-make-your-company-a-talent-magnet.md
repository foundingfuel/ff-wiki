## Core Argument
Compelling vision, purposeful leadership, and deliberate culture-building are startups' primary weapons for talent attraction.

## Key Points
- Craft visceral vision through future scenarios rather than abstract statements to make impact tangible to candidates.
- Lead recruitment conversations with organizational purpose (why) before product details to inspire deeper resonance.
- Intentionally shape culture early through hiring aligned people; values crystallize from initial team composition.

## Open Question
How can startups with limited resources and uncertain products compete for talent against well-funded unicorns offering security and equity upside, when culture and vision are invisible until lived?

## India Angle
Indian startups face acute talent competition from tech giants and rival startups; these tactics help smaller Indian companies attract quality engineers without massive recruitment budgets, a critical constraint in India's startup ecosystem.
