## Core Argument
Human networks and embedded knowledge outperform digital systems when navigating India's complex, evolving infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Informal brotherhood of truckers, dhaba owners, and road-warriors create distributed real-time navigation system beyond GPS.
- Technology fails at critical moments; human intelligence, local knowledge, and trust-based relationships fill the gap.
- Shift from autonomous adventure to monitored interdependence reflects India's rapid infrastructure evolution and surveillance.

## Open Question
Can India's informal, trust-based road networks sustain as surveillance and digital systems become ubiquitous, or will they be replaced by algorithmic solutions that lack local context?

## India Angle
India's informal networks and human-centered problem-solving compensate for technological gaps; demonstrates how social capital and distributed trust systems work across Indian highways despite infrastructure vulnerabilities.
