## Core Argument
Reliability built through systematic problem diagnosis and adaptive execution wins customer loyalty and competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Root-cause analysis of customer complaints (250→45 items) reveals systemic problems, not random failures.
- Cross-functional accountability with fixed timelines and monthly reviews creates ownership and measurable progress.
- Crisis adaptation (remote call centers, app, YouTube tutorials) deepens customer relationships when service delivery breaks.

## Open Question
How much of Breze's success is replicable in contexts with weaker digital infrastructure, lower customer literacy, or different seasonal/climatic pressures?

## India Angle
Indian manufacturing and service companies often struggle with quality control and customer reliability; Breze's systematic approach to problem-solving and adaptation during crises offers a model for Indian businesses operating in similar sectors (appliances, consumer durables) facing similar infrastructure and service delivery challenges.
