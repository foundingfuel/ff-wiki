## Core Argument
Consolidation success depends on execution and customer habit-building, not just competitive elimination.

## Key Points
- M&A valuations mislead; real value emerges from post-acquisition execution and unit economics sustainability.
- Flipkart risks burning $120-130M if it fails to integrate Jabong while managing three apparel fronts simultaneously.
- Amazon Prime's habit-creation model (convenience, invisibility, ecosystem lock-in) is the real competitive threat, not acquisition moves.

## Open Question
Can Flipkart simultaneously execute the complex Jabong turnaround while defending against Amazon's habit-forming ecosystem strategy, or is consolidation a distraction from the larger battle?

## India Angle
E-commerce consolidation in India requires execution excellence beyond deal-making; Amazon's habit-creation strategies pose bigger threats to Flipkart than M&A moves in the emerging Indian market.
