## Core Argument
Slower, deeper information beats daily news for understanding systemic risks and real change.

## Key Points
- Daily news misses structural problems visible only through infrequent, comprehensive data—2007-08 financial crisis example.
- Indian education system transplanted classroom teaching online without creativity, ignoring 76% without internet access.
- Tejpal verdict highlights gaps in India's legal system for prosecuting sexual assault in workplaces despite 2013 reforms.

## Open Question
How do institutions and citizens prioritize meaningful information over attention-grabbing news when institutional incentives reward the latter?

## India Angle
India's education system exposed by pandemic reveals systemic failures in digital adaptation; only 24% of households have internet access. Also relevant: India's legal framework for workplace sexual assault cases tested through high-profile Tejpal verdict.
