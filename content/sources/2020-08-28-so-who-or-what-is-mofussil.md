## Core Argument
Small-town India's newfound wealth is driving reverse migration of domestic workers, reshaping India's informal labour market.

## Key Points
- Mofussil towns now actively seek household services at premium wages, reversing traditional metro-centric labour migration.
- Better living conditions (private rooms, family accommodation) in small towns attract workers away from cramped metro homes.
- Platform intermediaries like Helper4U address trust and formality gaps in unorganised domestic labour across 130+ towns.

## Open Question
How can platform models for domestic labour simultaneously address worker dignity (privacy, agency) and employer expectations in a sector still defined by informality and social inequality?

## India Angle
Post-COVID reverse migration of domestic labour from metros to small towns reveals wealth concentration in mofussil India and emerging demand for organised labour platforms in tier-2/3 cities—a structural shift in India's informal economy.
