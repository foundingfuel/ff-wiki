## Core Argument
True leadership means acting on the mission even when unasked, while acknowledging vulnerability prevents burnout.

## Key Points
- Inner sense of captaincy: taking responsibility for collective outcomes beyond formal job boundaries.
- Leaders must acknowledge vulnerability and seek help; persistence without self-awareness is self-destructive.
- Organizations should build cultures where every member feels ownership of the voyage, not just their role.

## Open Question
How do you cultivate personal ownership and initiative among team members without creating unsustainable expectations or burnout?

## India Angle
Indian founders and leaders often operate under similar pressure dynamics as their Western counterparts, but with fewer institutional support systems and board oversight; building cultures of shared responsibility and psychological safety is critical for Indian entrepreneurship ecosystem health.
