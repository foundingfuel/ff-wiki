## Core Argument
Tech regulation via bans creates opportunities but masks deeper issues: propaganda, polarization, and surveillance abuse.

## Key Points
- App bans weaponize tech policy but displace rather than solve problems—PUBG ban benefits local startups but didn't address Tencent's actual involvement.
- Removing ad channels (Facebook) doesn't prevent propaganda or polarization; false solutions emerge when regulators misdiagnose root causes.
- Predictive algorithms in law enforcement pose existential risks unmatched by commercial platforms due to state monopoly over violence.

## Open Question
How can democracies harness predictive technology's benefits while preventing its weaponization by state actors who face no market accountability?

## India Angle
India faces dual tech challenges: geopolitical app bans creating market opportunities for local players (PUBG/Fau-G), while also adopting surveillance technologies like predictive policing without adequate safeguards—mirroring global tech governance failures.
