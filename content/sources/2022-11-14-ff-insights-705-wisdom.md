## Core Argument
Wisdom balances self-interest with others' needs and adapts, shapes, or selects environments appropriately.

## Key Points
- Wise people balance their needs, others' needs, and institutional/environmental impact; ignorant people see black-and-white, self-interested choices.
- R K Swamy Hansa succeeded by combining integrity, professional management autonomy, and strategic risk-taking on complex mandates like IRS.
- Timekeeping standardization enabled industrial coordination; similarly, institutional frameworks require shared standards and aligned incentives.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance giving autonomy to professional managers while maintaining supervisory oversight without micromanaging or losing control?

## India Angle
Indian family-led businesses and institutional leaders can learn from R K Swamy Hansa's model of integrity-driven leadership and long-term institution-building over personal aggrandisement, contrasting with more flamboyant Indian business figures.
