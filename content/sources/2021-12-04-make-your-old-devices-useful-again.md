## Core Argument
Old devices deserve second lives through creative repurposing before environmental disposal.

## Key Points
- Legacy phones and tablets can stream audiobooks/music without battery drain on primary devices.
- Outdated tablets become functional digital photo frames or webcams with minimal additional investment.
- Repurposing extends device utility, reduces e-waste, and challenges throwaway consumer tech culture.

## Open Question
How do consumers balance privacy concerns with free/cheap apps that request excessive permissions, when paid alternatives cost significantly more?

## India Angle
India's growing middle class accumulates electronic devices rapidly; repurposing them addresses both affordability constraints and e-waste challenges in a country with limited recycling infrastructure.
