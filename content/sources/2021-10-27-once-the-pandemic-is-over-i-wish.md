## Core Argument
Pandemic's end means different things to different Indians—reflecting pre-existing inequities in mobility, work stability, and opportunity.

## Key Points
- Migrant workers cite systemic barriers: unaffordable trains, online booking gaps, touts blocking rural return after 2+ years away
- Informal sector workers (watchmen, tea vendors, farmers) prioritize lost income recovery and basic stability over aspirational goals
- Youth reveal pandemic's digital divide: privileged students gained creative communities online; others lost schooling, sleep, social connection entirely

## Open Question
Does pandemic recovery require systemic fixes (transport accessibility, formal sector inclusion) or individual entrepreneurial adaptation—and who decides?

## India Angle
Post-pandemic recovery aspirations reveal structural inequalities in India: migrant workers face affordability barriers in transport, informal sector workers struggle with lost income, while privileged youth benefit from remote flexibility. Policy must address ground-level accessibility and informal economy revival.
