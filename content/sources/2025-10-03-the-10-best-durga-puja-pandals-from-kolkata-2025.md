## Core Argument
Durga Puja pandals evolve as artistic and political commentary platforms reflecting India's social consciousness.

## Key Points
- Pandals increasingly address contemporary Indian issues: Bengal famine, Gaza conflict, caste systems, language politics.
- Live performers and 360-degree pratimas represent shift toward immersive, multi-sensory artistic experiences.
- Budget constraints and rainfall led to innovative smaller pandals gaining prominence over big-budget productions.

## Open Question
How should pandals balance artistic integrity with sensitive social themes—when does commentary become exploitative (e.g., using marginalized groups as props)?

## India Angle
Durga Puja as a cultural barometer reflecting contemporary Indian concerns—Bengal famine, language politics, caste, and social issues embedded in festival narratives specific to Kolkata's creative ecosystem.
