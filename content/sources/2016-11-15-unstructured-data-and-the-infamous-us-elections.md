## Core Argument
Unstructured data holds 80% of business value; connecting it requires intentional metadata linking, not waiting for perfect algorithms.

## Key Points
- Structured polling data alone missed crucial context; unstructured information from social media and ground conversations proved invaluable in 2016 US elections.
- 80% of business-relevant information exists in unstructured formats (text, emails, social posts) yet remains untapped due to analysis difficulty.
- Organizations can manually create metadata connectors today across fragmented tools to link unstructured data, rather than waiting for automated solutions.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the pragmatic need to manually extract insights from dispersed unstructured data today against the expectation that AI and machine learning will eventually automate this process?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises and policymakers must recognize unstructured data as a competitive advantage; building organizational capabilities to extract insights from dispersed, unformatted information across tools and teams is critical for India's digital transformation and decision-making quality.
