## Core Argument
MSMEs must adopt climate-friendly technologies via accessible financing, but basic financing access remains the prerequisite.

## Key Points
- MSMEs both cause and suffer from climate change, yet few know climate financing exists or can access it.
- Simplified climate finance instruments like default guarantees could help, but require active pursuit by institutions.
- Risk: aggressive climate financing push may distract from solving foundational financing problems for informal businesses.

## Open Question
Should systems solve basic financing access first, or can climate financing simultaneously address both climate mitigation and financial inclusion for MSMEs?

## India Angle
Indian MSMEs, which form the backbone of the economy, are trapped in informal financing cycles and lack awareness of climate financing options. This article advocates for climate finance institutions to actively pursue MSMEs through simplified instruments, directly addressing India's dual challenge of MSME formalization and climate action.
