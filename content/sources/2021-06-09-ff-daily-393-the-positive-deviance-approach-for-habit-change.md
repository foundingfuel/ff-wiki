## Core Argument
Positive deviance—building on existing capabilities rather than imposing change—drives sustainable behaviour transformation.

## Key Points
- Listen to frontline workers; they already possess solutions the system hasn't surfaced or valued.
- Shift happens through peer influence and norm-change, not expert directives or charts.
- Hospital infections dropped when staff felt heard and empowered to innovate for themselves.

## Open Question
How do you scale positive deviance beyond small group discussions without losing the participatory essence that makes it work?

## India Angle
Indian hospitals and healthcare systems face similar infection control challenges; positive deviance could shift how government and private healthcare institutions drive behaviour change without top-down mandates—relevant for India's healthcare reforms and worker engagement in public systems.
