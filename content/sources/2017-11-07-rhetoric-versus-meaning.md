## Core Argument
Polarized rhetoric on Aadhaar obscures moderate public sentiment exploring the technology cautiously.

## Key Points
- Social media amplifies extreme voices while silent majority evaluates Aadhaar pragmatically, like any major decision.
- Insults and polarization on digital platforms don't represent actual public opinion; most people listen to both sides.
- Media narratives miss the nuanced, exploratory stance of ordinary citizens evaluating transformative policy.

## Open Question
How can institutions and media better amplify the moderate, thoughtful voices of citizens who are actually engaging seriously with complex policy questions rather than the loudest ideologues?

## India Angle
Aadhaar debate in India is polarized online but moderate in reality; Indian citizens approach transformative policy pragmatically rather than ideologically, yet media narratives miss this ground truth.
