## Core Argument
Focus innovation on jobs customers need done, not correlations; pair tech with democratic deliberation.

## Key Points
- Correlation-driven innovation misleads; understand complete customer context—who, what job, circumstances.
- Technology outpacing democracy creates peril; deliberative processes must evolve alongside innovation.
- Leadership, culture, organisational design matter as much as technology; advantages are temporary.

## Open Question
How can India scale rapid technological infrastructure (Aadhaar) while simultaneously building the democratic deliberation capacity to govern its societal impacts equitably?

## India Angle
Directly addresses India's Aadhaar and digital infrastructure debate, arguing that technology innovation (like digital ID systems) must be paired with democratic process innovation to ensure citizen-state dynamics are redesigned thoughtfully, not just efficiently.
