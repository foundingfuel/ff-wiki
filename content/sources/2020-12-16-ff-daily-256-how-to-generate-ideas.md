## Core Argument
Continuous learning builds neural connections that generate creativity, ideas, and resilience across personal and organisational domains.

## Key Points
- Learning new skills creates neural connections that enhance cognitive flexibility and recovery capacity from setbacks.
- Ideas emerge from deliberate intellectual engagement, not passivity—reading, cross-disciplinary work, and rehearsal stimulate creative thinking.
- Vulnerability and shame about not knowing are obstacles to growth; reframing them as learning opportunities is essential.

## Open Question
If learning and growth are essential to sanity and resilience, why do institutional and cultural barriers to continuous learning persist, especially in hierarchical organisational structures?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organisations must prioritise continuous learning and cross-disciplinary thinking to build institutional capability—evident in how RN transformed Indian aeronautics and computing through persistent intellectual curiosity and learning habits.
