## Core Argument
Ancient Harappan civilizations offer Indians tangible, physical connection to overlooked indigenous heritage.

## Key Points
- Harappan sites in India (Gujarat, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP) reveal sophisticated city planning, water management, and urban design.
- Interfaith harmony in Ahmedabad (Muslim caretaker maintaining Hindu shrine for generations) mirrors ancient civilizational pluralism.
- Visiting Lothal and Dholavira transforms abstract textbook history into lived, embodied understanding of India's architectural and engineering legacy.

## Open Question
How do we bridge the gap between scholarly debate over Aryan migration and deciphering Harappan script, without letting political or nationalist narratives oversimplify an ancient, complex reality?

## India Angle
Reclaiming India's Harappan heritage sites within Indian territory (Lothal, Dholavira, Rakhigarhi) rather than only viewing them through the lens of Pakistani Indus Valley sites; reconnecting contemporary Indians to pre-colonial civilizational identity and continuity.
