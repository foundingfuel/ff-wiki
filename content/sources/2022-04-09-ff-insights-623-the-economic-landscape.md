## Core Argument
System complexity defeats policy intent; simplification and inequality are India's dual economic challenges.

## Key Points
- GST's initial failure stemmed from bureaucratic complexity; Nilekani's simplification principle proved transformative
- Workplace flexibility debates reveal generational divide on remote work—young tech talent rejecting leader mandates
- Carbon emissions concentrate among wealthy globally; inequality now exceeds country-level disparities

## Open Question
How can India achieve GST's efficiency gains while addressing that economic growth increasingly concentrates emissions among its wealthiest citizens?
