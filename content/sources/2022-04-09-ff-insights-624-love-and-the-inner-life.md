## Core Argument
Inner life and meaning-making are survival mechanisms against modern emptiness and digital distraction.

## Key Points
- Love transcends physical presence through spiritual connection and inner contemplation, offering refuge from desolation.
- Social media's dopamine-reward loop rewires children's brains to crave rapid stimulation over sustained attention.
- Future technologies surprise us because we underestimate how humans actually behave and repurpose tools unexpectedly.

## Open Question
How do we cultivate deep inner life and sustained attention in an age designed to fragment both?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and leaders must balance high performance culture with inner life and meaning-making; particularly urgent as digital addiction spreads among Indian youth and workplace stress intensifies.
