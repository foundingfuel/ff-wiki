## Core Argument
Innovation requires questioning received wisdom and taking untrodden paths, not following conventional success formulas.

## Key Points
- Disruptive innovation demands 'Why not?' mindset; past success guarantees nothing in changing contexts.
- Question answers, not just answer questions—interrogate assumptions and established wisdom continuously.
- Learn from unconventional role models: ordinary entrepreneurs and street-level innovators reveal true patterns of change.

## Open Question
If taking the 'road less travelled' is essential for innovation, how do we systematically encourage such non-conformity within organizations built on standardization and process?

## India Angle
Indian innovators and entrepreneurs can draw inspiration from homegrown role models like Sarabhai, Kurien, and Kalam who questioned the status quo; also highlights Indian-specific innovation examples from traffic vendors to women entrepreneurs.
