## Core Argument
Apply disciplined mental models and circles of competence to navigate complexity; principles vs power defines global order.

## Key Points
- Munger's investing wisdom: stay within circles of competence, bet only with odds advantage, judge businesses by sustainable competitive advantage.
- Charlie's three-basket approach filters ruthlessly—yes, no, too-tough-to-understand—avoiding speculative placer mining for overlooked nuggets.
- India faces binary choice: principles-governed international system requiring unified opposition to invasions, or flexible power-politics approach to preserve strategic options.

## Open Question
How can India maintain economic development path and strategic flexibility while also upholding principles-based international order that requires unified moral stance against invasions?

## India Angle
India must decide whether to operate within principles-based international order or power politics; Russia-Ukraine war creates dilemma for Indian neutrality, economic development priorities, and strategic options amid China threat.
