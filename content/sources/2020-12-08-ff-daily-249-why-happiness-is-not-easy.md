## Core Argument
Happiness requires direct experience control, not external maximization; pandemic surfaces workplace efficiency and equity tensions.

## Key Points
- Csikszentmihalyi argues happiness eludes because universe isn't designed for human comfort; chronic dissatisfaction is inherent
- Private vaccine markets in India risk inequity by allowing wealthy to bypass public queue, creating two-tier access
- AI tools like Clockwise and Headroom optimize meeting time but raise questions about surveillance and attention measurement

## Open Question
How do we balance individual wellbeing optimization (AI-driven meeting management) with collective equity (vaccine access) when systems reward those who can afford or engineer advantage?
