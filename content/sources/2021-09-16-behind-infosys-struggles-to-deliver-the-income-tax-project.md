## Core Argument
Indian IT firms win prestigious government projects but face structural margin pressure and reputational risk.

## Key Points
- Government projects offer large contract values but razor-thin margins due to IT companies' dollar-revenue-based cost structures.
- Information vacuum between government silence and corporate opacity prevents learning from failures and accountability.
- Complex large-scale projects demand expertise transfer and capability building that don't easily scale across organizations.

## Open Question
How can Indian IT services companies simultaneously need large government projects for topline growth, maintain profitability, build organizational capability, and protect brand reputation when structural incentives push toward failure?

## India Angle
Indian IT services companies struggle with government projects despite their strategic importance for domestic diversification; reveals structural misalignment between IT sector's export-focused model and India's government procurement realities.
