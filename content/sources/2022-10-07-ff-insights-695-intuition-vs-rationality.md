## Core Argument
Effective leadership requires balancing intuition with rationality, humble learning over control, and systems thinking over linear design.

## Key Points
- Trial and error with humility beats rigid adherence to pre-set plans in complex systems.
- Admitting vulnerability and incomplete mental models enables real learning from mistakes.
- Systems thinkers flow within systems; design thinkers impose solutions from outside them.

## Open Question
How do policymakers and organizations institutionalize the willingness to admit mistakes and change course when career incentives and accountability structures punish visible failure?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers and institutional leaders often impose rigid, scientific-industrial frameworks on India's vast informal economy without understanding its inherent systems logic. Embracing intuitive systems thinking could unlock innovation in informal sectors while respecting their organic complexity rather than forcing formalization.
