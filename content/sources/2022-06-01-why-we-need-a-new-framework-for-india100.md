## Core Argument
India needs a new S-curve: shift from growth size to shape, embed entrepreneurial values, strengthen rule of law and individual freedoms.

## Key Points
- Young India demands financial independence, female empowerment, and active citizenship—not just employment or passive progress.
- Growth without inclusion failed; next 25 years require good jobs at scale, high literacy, and protection for 10-15M annual workers.
- Modern prosperity requires values shift: individualism, risk acceptance, enlightenment, rule of law—currently weak in Indian mindset and institutions.

## Open Question
How can India simultaneously pursue rapid economic growth and structural equality when limited state capacity means the government must choose between redistributive intervention and enabling markets?

## India Angle
India must fundamentally reshape its economy's structure and social values by 2047, moving from growth-focused to inclusive development while strengthening state capacity, rule of law, and individual freedoms—critical for managing 10M+ annual population additions and bridging widening inequality.
