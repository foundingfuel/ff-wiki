## Core Argument
Remote work success requires local autonomy and differentiated employer brands, not centralized control.

## Key Points
- Local leaders need decision-making authority to attract and retain top talent in emerging markets.
- Clear employer value proposition differentiates firms from competitors—L'Oréal and Unilever examples show localized talent strategies work.
- Remote models require cultural alignment across teams; organizational change, not just technical systems, enables talent attraction.

## Open Question
How do companies balance global brand consistency with localized talent propositions and decision-making autonomy without fragmenting organizational coherence?

## India Angle
Indian companies competing for global talent must develop differentiated employer propositions; Unilever's success in India through leadership programs serves as a local benchmark. Remote work amplifies talent competition across geographies.
