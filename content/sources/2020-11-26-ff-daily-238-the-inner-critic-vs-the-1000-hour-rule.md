## Core Argument
Masterpiece emerges when inner critic is silenced, yet only after deliberate practice accumulates foundation.

## Key Points
- The inner critic prevents creative flow; removing distractions unlocks best work.
- Success may require both deliberate practice and moments of unselfconscious execution.
- Psychological permission to act without judgment paradoxically enables excellence.

## Open Question
Does mastery require relentless criticism for improvement, or transcendence of criticism for optimal performance?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and entrepreneurs often struggle with perfectionism and harsh self-criticism—cultural factors amplified by competitive education systems. Understanding when to silence the inner critic versus leveraging deliberate practice is crucial for building high-performing teams and scaling personal mastery.
