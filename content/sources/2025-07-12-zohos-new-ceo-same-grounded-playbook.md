## Core Argument
Zoho's grounded, learning-by-doing culture positions it to navigate AI disruption better than hype-driven competitors.

## Key Points
- Zoho's onboarding philosophy: learn by doing, not training manuals; forces ownership and product mastery through immersion.
- Crisis-driven wisdom: surviving dot-com crash and telecom collapse shaped decentralised, long-term thinking culture still relevant today.
- AI as existential challenge: SaaS industry must reinvent as agentic AI threatens traditional cloud business model; Zoho's 10-year AI investment and engineering depth are advantages.

## Open Question
Can Zoho's grounded, methodical engineering culture move fast enough to compete in an AI-driven world where speed and disruption favour first-movers?

## India Angle
Indian SaaS companies like Zoho demonstrate how bootstrapped, long-term thinking and engineering-first culture can compete globally against VC-funded rivals; relevant for Indian tech entrepreneurs navigating AI disruption and building sustainable businesses.
