## Core Argument
Geopolitical fragmentation will create loose, layered alliances rather than hard trade blocs over five years.

## Key Points
- US-China hegemonic rivalry is entrenched; full decoupling unlikely but supply chains will reconfigure gradually.
- Middle East and Ukraine conflicts pose severe trade/economic risks; tensions will persist without near-term resolution.
- Indo-Pacific flashpoints (Taiwan, South China Sea) threaten critical global supply chains; multipolarity requires complex alliance management.

## Open Question
How can nations maintain economic interdependence with rivals while building resilience against geopolitical shocks?

## India Angle
India's atmanirbharta push and BrahMos missile sale to Philippines position it within emerging multi-layered alliances; India must navigate US-China rivalry while maintaining supply chain resilience and strategic autonomy.
