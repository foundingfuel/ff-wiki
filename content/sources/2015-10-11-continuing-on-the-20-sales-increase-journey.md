## Core Argument
Storytelling frameworks enable average salespeople to match top performers, unlocking 20% sales growth.

## Key Points
- Top 20% salespeople consistently outsell averages by 2x; all are natural storytellers
- Four story patterns—connection, success, clarity, influence—address each sales phase systematically
- Stories override objections better than facts; they replace mental narratives, not fight them

## Open Question
If storytelling is innate and universal, why do only 20% of salespeople excel at it, and can it truly be taught at scale to the next 30%?

## India Angle
Indian B2B sales teams, particularly in solution sales and IT services, can adopt storytelling frameworks to close the performance gap between top and average salespeople—critical for scaling mid-market companies.
