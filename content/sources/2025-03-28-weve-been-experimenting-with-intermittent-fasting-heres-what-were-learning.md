## Core Argument
Real change begins with discomfort, not resolution; requires honest self-assessment and expert guidance.

## Key Points
- Pre-diabetes diagnosis forced confrontation with denial about fitness and eating habits; fear catalyzed behavioral change.
- Nutritionist provided evidence-based guidance on calorie quality over quantity; gradual dietary shifts proved more sustainable than dramatic overhauls.
- Intermittent fasting emerged organically from discipline in meal timing; metabolic switching and autophagy provided physiological explanation for observed health improvements.

## Open Question
If intermittent fasting works, how do we distinguish genuine physiological benefit from placebo effect and lifestyle discipline that would work with any structured eating pattern?
