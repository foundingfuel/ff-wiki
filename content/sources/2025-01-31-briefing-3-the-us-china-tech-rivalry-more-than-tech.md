## Core Argument
US-China tech competition extends beyond innovation to geopolitics, reshaping global supply chains and regulatory frameworks.

## Key Points
- DeepSeek's efficiency challenges Western AI dominance and questions American innovation superiority narrative
- Semiconductors, green energy, and AI regulations are flashpoints where US export controls drive Chinese self-sufficiency
- Divergent regulatory models (US voluntary, EU risk-based, China state-sovereign) fragment global governance landscape

## Open Question
Does restricting China's tech access actually slow it down, or does it accelerate Chinese innovation and create alternative ecosystems that threaten Western dominance differently?

## India Angle
India must navigate US-China tech rivalry while building domestic semiconductor, AI, and EV capabilities. The CHIPS Act model and supply chain diversification present opportunities for Indian manufacturing and innovation, but regulatory fragmentation risks isolating India if it doesn't align strategically.
