## Core Argument
Align sales incentives to lifetime customer relationships, not just transaction volume.

## Key Points
- Sales teams measured only on volume perpetuate outdated, transactional business models.
- Customer lifetime value requires incentive structures that reward relationship-building and service.
- Changing how salespeople are rewarded drives organizational culture and customer strategy alignment.

## Open Question
How do you measure and incentivize relationship-building in the short term when revenue targets demand immediate results?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs often rely on transactional sales metrics; shifting to relationship-based incentives requires cultural change in how sales teams are managed and rewarded—critical for building sustainable businesses in competitive Indian markets.
