## Core Argument
AI-assisted coding introduces speed, authority, and opacity risks that outpace organisational governance capabilities.

## Key Points
- 63% of organisations already deploying AI code assistants; adoption accelerating faster than safety mechanisms.
- AI tools evolving from passive helpers into active agents with infrastructure modification authority and broad permissions.
- Machine-velocity code propagates failures as quickly as fixes, creating cascading 'blast radius' outages at scale.

## Open Question
How can organisations maintain human accountability and meaningful oversight when AI systems operate at machine speed and hold operational authority, yet remain opaque in their decision-making logic?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises rapidly adopting AI coding assistants without equivalent governance frameworks; a critical risk as India's IT services sector and growing tech companies scale AI-assisted development at pace.
