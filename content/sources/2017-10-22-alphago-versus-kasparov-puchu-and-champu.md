## Core Argument
AI's emergence challenges fundamental human roles—knowledge transfer, emotional bonding, parenting—requiring reimagining what humans uniquely offer.

## Key Points
- AlphaGo's tabula rasa learning disproves singularity pessimism but raises questions about human redundancy and purpose.
- AI intermediaries like Siri are subtly displacing parents as primary knowledge sources and emotional anchors for children.
- Kasparov's work suggests humans must redefine value beyond tasks algorithms can perform—wisdom, emotional connection, moral judgment.

## Open Question
If AI excels at information retrieval and problem-solving, what irreplaceable human function remains—and can emotional bonds survive when children outsource curiosity to machines?

## India Angle
Indian parents and educators face the challenge of preparing children for a future where AI intermediates knowledge transfer and parent-child relationships; questions about cultural transmission and mentorship in AI-mediated environments are particularly acute in India's tradition-bound families.
