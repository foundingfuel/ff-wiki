## Core Argument
US strategic chaos and fiscal constraints force allies to assume defense burdens while reshaping global economic norms.

## Key Points
- US tariff unpredictability is deliberate strategy; disruptive but not economy-breaking due to low trade dependence.
- Defense shift: fewer US troops abroad, more weapons sales to allies—allies must spend 3.5% GDP on defense by 2035.
- America's $38T debt creates secular pressure on defense; allies cannot rely on US security commitments.

## Open Question
Can democratic institutions constrain presidential power when Congress has delegated its authority to the executive, especially in international affairs?

## India Angle
India must navigate unpredictable US tariff policy, manage shifting defense alliances, and prepare for accelerated arms competition in Asia as US withdraws troop commitments while pushing allies toward self-reliance.
