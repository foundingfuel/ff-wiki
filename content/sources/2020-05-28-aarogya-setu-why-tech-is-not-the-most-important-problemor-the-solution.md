## Core Argument
Contact tracing app effectiveness depends on institutional capacity and trust, not technology alone.

## Key Points
- Tech solutions alone cannot solve pandemic response without institutional infrastructure and citizen trust.
- Aarogya Setu debates masked the real issue: whether India's systems could execute contact tracing effectively.
- Success hinges on factors outside the app—testing capacity, isolation facilities, compliance, and coordination.

## Open Question
How can governments balance legitimate public health needs with surveillance safeguards when institutional accountability mechanisms are weak?

## India Angle
India's Aarogya Setu app became a flashpoint for debates on surveillance vs. public health in a developing economy context, revealing gaps between tech solutions and ground-level execution capacity.
