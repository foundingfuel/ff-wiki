## Core Argument
Media bias operates through subtle language choices that reflect audience politics, not just editorial intent.

## Key Points
- Newspapers use distinct word choices reflecting local political preferences, shaping reader perception of identical events.
- Managers must actively listen and adapt communication protocols to support neurodiverse employees in remote settings.
- Understanding disease progression stages requires different treatment approaches, challenging simplistic cure-focused thinking.

## Open Question
If media outlets simply give readers what they want through language choices, can journalism serve a truth-seeking function or is it inevitably captured by audience preferences?

## India Angle
Indian media organizations face similar bias challenges; understanding language patterns in coverage of sensitive topics (caste, religion, politics) could help Indian journalists recognize their own editorial slants and serve diverse reader bases more equitably.
