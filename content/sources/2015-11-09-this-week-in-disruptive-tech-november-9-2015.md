## Core Argument
Technology amplifies human capability; success requires human needs at core, not just technical innovation.

## Key Points
- AI, 3D printing, and sharing platforms are extensions of computing as 'bicycles of the mind'—tools to amplify human capability.
- Scaling without addressing externalities creates liability: regulatory, safety, and labor classification challenges emerge across sectors.
- Sharing economy distributes existing resources efficiently but doesn't create new value—questioning true economic impact of platforms.

## Open Question
If sharing economy only redistributes resources without creating new value, how can platforms justify venture funding and claims of economic transformation?

## India Angle
India has emerging opportunities in AI, 3D printing, and sharing economy platforms, but needs regulatory frameworks similar to global debates around Uber. Indian startups copying Western models risk reputational damage locally.
