## Core Argument
Resilience emerges from emotional equanimity, separating facts from fear, and deep listening across differences.

## Key Points
- Crisis leaders must reframe immediate priorities and reset objectives, not pursue long-term goals during turbulence.
- Separate first arrow (unavoidable pain) from second arrow (mind's interpretation); suffering is optional and manageable.
- Deep listening reveals others' mental models and exposes our own biases, enabling collaborative problem-solving in uncertainty.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance decisive action during crisis with the reflective, empathetic practices (deep listening, presence) that take time—when speed is often critical?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders navigating repeated crises (pandemic waves, economic shocks) need frameworks for emotional regulation and collective resilience; applies across family businesses, startups, and large organisations facing uncertainty
