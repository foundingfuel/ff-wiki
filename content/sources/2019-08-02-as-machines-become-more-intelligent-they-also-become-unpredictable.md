## Core Argument
Intelligent algorithms are inherently unpredictable; we must choose between explainability and real-world resilience.

## Key Points
- Deep learning systems create patterns humans can't reverse-engineer or explain, even to designers.
- The predictability-resilience paradox: explicit rules ensure transparency but limit problem-solving; messy data creates resilience but opacity.
- As algorithms make life-altering decisions (hiring, credit, healthcare), unexplainability becomes ethically and practically untenable.

## Open Question
How can organizations deploy increasingly intelligent but fundamentally opaque algorithms in high-stakes domains without violating principles of human accountability and transparency?

## India Angle
As Indian enterprises adopt AI for critical decisions—credit scoring, hiring, healthcare—the predictability-resilience paradox becomes urgent. India's regulatory frameworks and organizational cultures must grapple with opaque algorithms making life-altering decisions for citizens.
