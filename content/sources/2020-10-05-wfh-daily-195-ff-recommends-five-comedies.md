## Core Argument
Lockdown forced comedians to innovate content formats, creating engaging alternatives to traditional stand-up.

## Key Points
- Sorabh Pant created YouTube show with diverse guests during lockdown, expanding comedy beyond traditional venues.
- Punit Pania's solo show explores corporate life and career identity through comedic lens.
- Schitt's Creek offers escapism during difficult times, showcasing how storytelling provides psychological relief.

## Open Question
How do comedians sustain income and audience engagement when traditional live performance venues are unavailable, and will digital formats permanently reshape the comedy industry?
