## Core Argument
TikTok users craft identity through simple narratives reflecting India's paradox: modern aesthetics, traditional values.

## Key Points
- Social skills now practiced vicariously through 15-second videos rather than face-to-face interactions.
- Modernity expressed through clothes and consumption; deep value systems remain anchored to family and tradition.
- Complex realities reduced to simple binaries; nuance rejected as pedantic by impatient generation.

## Open Question
Can a generation socialized through atomic, screen-based interactions develop genuine social competence, or will parasocial bonds replace real relationships?

## India Angle
TikTok adoption in India reveals how Gen Z and millennials navigate modernity while preserving traditional values; shows tension between superficial westernization and deep cultural conservatism unique to Indian society.
