## Core Argument
Multidisciplinary thinking prevents blind spots; understanding comes from large, relevant sample sizes across domains.

## Key Points
- Specialization creates blind spots; 'the frog in the well knows nothing of the ocean.'
- Three buckets of knowledge: 13.7B years (physics/geology), 3.5B years (biology), 20K years (human history).
- Real education is lifetime awareness, not knowledge accumulation; freedom requires attention and discipline.

## Open Question
How do professionals balancing deep specialization for career advancement also maintain the breadth needed for multidisciplinary thinking?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders often operate in siloed expertise (engineering, finance, management)—multidisciplinary thinking could unlock innovation in complex problems like education reform, scaling startups, and navigating India's dynamic regulatory landscape.
