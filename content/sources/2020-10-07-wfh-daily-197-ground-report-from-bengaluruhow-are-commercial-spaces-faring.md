## Core Argument
Bengaluru's commercial districts face a silent crisis: massive vacancy and closures invisible to policymakers and investors.

## Key Points
- Koramangala, Bengaluru's startup epicenter, shows 132+ vacant establishments with many unlisted; restaurants district mostly shuttered.
- Absence of 'TO-LET' boards masks true economic damage; actual vacancy rates across India likely far underestimated.
- Commercial space crisis represents lost livelihoods, jobs, and ecosystem disruption beyond visible statistics.

## Open Question
How do policymakers and investors make informed decisions about economic recovery when true vacancy data remains hidden and unmeasured?
