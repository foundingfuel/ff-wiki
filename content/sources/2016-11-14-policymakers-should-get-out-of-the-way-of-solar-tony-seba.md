## Core Argument
Business model innovation, not government subsidy, drives solar disruption; policymakers should enable competition, not direct investment.

## Key Points
- Zero-money-down PPA model democratized solar by converting $20-30k upfront cost to affordable $80-100/month payments.
- Silicon Valley's experimentation culture enabled discovery of viable solar business models when government investment alone failed.
- Global competition (China's market entry) drove cost collapse more than policy; government should step back from picking winners.

## Open Question
If business model innovation matters more than government investment or technology advancement, why do developing nations lack the entrepreneurial infrastructure to discover similar models for their energy challenges?

## India Angle
India can accelerate solar adoption by removing regulatory barriers and enabling business model innovation (like PPAs) rather than direct government subsidies. The solar cost revolution driven by global competition offers India a rare window to leapfrog centralized grid dependence.
