## Core Argument
Molnupiravir's limited efficacy demands cautious, targeted deployment in India, not mass marketing-driven adoption.

## Key Points
- Drug shows limited efficacy in unvaccinated individuals; effectiveness in vaccinated people unknown.
- Indiscriminate use risks antiviral resistance, particularly concerning given aggressive Indian pharma marketing practices.
- Should be restricted to early symptomatic use in high-risk, unvaccinated patients within five days of symptom onset.

## Open Question
How can India prevent pharmaceutical companies from circumventing clinical guidelines through aggressive marketing while still enabling timely access to potentially life-saving treatments?

## India Angle
India's emergency authorization and pharmaceutical rollout of Molnupiravir requires careful regulation to prevent indiscriminate use and antibiotic resistance—a systemic challenge in Indian healthcare where aggressive marketing often overrides clinical guidelines.
