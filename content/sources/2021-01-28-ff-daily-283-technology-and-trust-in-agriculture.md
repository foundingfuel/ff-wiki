## Core Argument
Technology can bridge trust gaps in Indian agriculture by enabling instant, objective quality verification at point of transaction.

## Key Points
- AgNext uses computer vision and spectral analysis to verify farm produce quality in 30 seconds, solving information asymmetry in agricultural trade.
- Founder Bhamra combined IIT/IIM education with ground research via 'Bharat darshan' before building a capital-efficient solution backed by ICAR.
- Trust deficit in Indian agriculture stems from lack of objective standards and rapid testing—a systemic market failure, not just operational problem.

## Open Question
Technology can verify quality objectively, but can it address the deeper institutional and relational trust deficit between farmers, traders, and government that persists beyond transaction verification?

## India Angle
AgNext addresses a critical Indian agriculture pain point—trust deficit in farm produce trading—through technology, enabling smallholder farmers to compete in formal value chains and reduce exploitation at transaction points.
