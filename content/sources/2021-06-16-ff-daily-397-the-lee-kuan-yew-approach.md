## Core Argument
Pragmatism over ideology: test solutions empirically, extract principles from success, never accept dead ends.

## Key Points
- Lee Kuan Yew's approach: start with the problem, not theory; choose solutions with highest success probability.
- Classical liberalism as framework: equal opportunity, efficiency, compassion for those unable to succeed.
- Extract principles from working solutions rather than applying predetermined theories to problems.

## Open Question
Can a pragmatic, ends-justify-means approach be sustained without ideological moorings, or does it eventually require philosophical grounding to prevent authoritarianism?

## India Angle
India's governance and institutional capacity could benefit from Lee Kuan Yew's pragmatic, results-focused approach over ideology-driven policymaking. His emphasis on efficiency, meritocracy, and solving problems empirically offers a model for Indian leaders navigating complex development challenges.
