## Core Argument
Technology must be demoted from saviour to tool; human insight and humanities remain irreplaceable.

## Key Points
- Overestimating technology's transformative power blinds us to incremental, context-dependent change.
- Journalism example shows information access—not technological innovation—solves real crises.
- Humanities-grounded thinking reveals enduring human needs technology cannot replace or resolve.

## Open Question
If we demote technology, how do we harness its genuine capabilities without reverting to inefficiency or ignoring legitimate innovation in sectors like healthcare and governance?

## India Angle
India's tech-first development narrative risks over-reliance on technological solutions for complex social problems—from governance to education to healthcare—without addressing underlying human, cultural, and systemic dimensions that require humanistic thinking.
