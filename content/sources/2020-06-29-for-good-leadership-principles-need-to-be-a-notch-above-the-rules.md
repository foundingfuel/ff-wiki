## Core Argument
Principles must transcend rules; true principles hurt when upheld, defining principled leadership.

## Key Points
- Rules are explicit regulations; principles are fundamental truths requiring conscience and courage to uphold.
- Technology disrupts rules across sports, employment, and business; principles guide adaptation beyond compliance.
- Employees are volunteers choosing to invest time; treating them as such is the new employment principle.

## Open Question
If principles must hurt to be authentic, how do organizations scale principled behavior across large, diverse teams without sacrificing coherence or creating ethical inconsistency?

## India Angle
Indian leaders often operate in contexts where rules are ambiguous or context-dependent; this framework helps navigate between blind rule-following and principled decision-making, crucial for building trust in Indian organizations where personal integrity heavily influences reputation.
