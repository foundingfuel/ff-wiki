## Core Argument
Elites prefer funding solutions to poverty over systemic reforms that require personal sacrifice.

## Key Points
- Philanthropy masks complicity: rich donate to problems they helped create while avoiding accountability.
- Language matters: discussing inequality versus poverty frames responsibility differently and threatens elite interests.
- Taboo enforcement: wealthy circles discourage questioning how money is made, only celebrating how it's given.

## Open Question
Can inequality be solved through voluntary elite participation without structural power redistribution, or does real change require confronting those who benefit from inequality?

## India Angle
India's growing wealth inequality and the role of philanthropists, business leaders, and policymakers in either perpetuating or addressing systemic inequality through structural reform versus charity-based approaches.
