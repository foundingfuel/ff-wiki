## Core Argument
Easing business regulations is essential to Make in India's success, particularly for SME growth.

## Key Points
- Small business owners must personally navigate government approvals, diverting attention from core operations and growth.
- Regulatory burden falls heaviest on MSMEs who lack dedicated compliance teams that large corporations employ.
- Systematic reform of India's business regulatory environment is prerequisite for manufacturing sector acceleration.

## Open Question
How can governments balance legitimate safety, environmental, and labour protections with reducing bureaucratic friction without creating loopholes or compromising accountability?

## India Angle
India's regulatory burden disproportionately stifles small manufacturers, undermining Make in India ambitions. SME owners lose productive hours to bureaucratic compliance rather than business growth, perpetuating India's poor ease-of-doing-business ranking (142nd in 2014).
