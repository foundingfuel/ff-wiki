## Core Argument
Minority groups succeed when authentic; allies must actively reject microaggressions to build inclusive workplaces.

## Key Points
- Being open and vulnerable about identity builds trust, engagement, and authentic professional relationships.
- Allies must actively challenge microaggressions rather than treat minorities differently or ignore bias.
- LGBTQ+ progress shows narrative change works; similar strategies can accelerate inclusion for all minorities at work.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance encouraging vulnerability and authenticity without creating unsafe spaces or enabling oversharing that could be weaponized against minorities?

## India Angle
India's recent repeal of Section 377 marks a turning point; Indian organizations now face the challenge of building truly inclusive workplaces where LGBTQ+ and minority employees can be authentic. The article directly references India's colonial legacy and the need to learn from global progress on gay rights to accelerate inclusion in Indian corporate culture.
