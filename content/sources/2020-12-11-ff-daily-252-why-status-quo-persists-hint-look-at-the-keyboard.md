## Core Argument
Status quo persists due to switching costs and misaligned incentives, not because it's optimal.

## Key Points
- QWERTY keyboard persists despite better alternatives because of network effects and switching costs.
- Vested interests resist change when personal compensation depends on maintaining current systems.
- Questioning inherited practices requires deliberate effort; most organizations default to 'we've always done it this way.'
- Knowledge management tools enable pattern recognition across disparate ideas, unlocking innovation.

## Open Question
How do leaders create sufficient psychological safety and incentive alignment to question inherited practices without destabilizing organizational continuity?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often inherit colonial-era or inherited systems without questioning; breaking status quo thinking is critical for Indian companies to compete globally and innovate at scale.
