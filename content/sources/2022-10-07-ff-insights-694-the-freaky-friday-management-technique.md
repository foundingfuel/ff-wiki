## Core Argument
Temporary role-swapping between conflicting leaders builds empathy and uncovers systemic issues that hierarchy and dialogue alone cannot resolve.

## Key Points
- Role reversal forces leaders to experience operational constraints and pressures of other teams firsthand.
- Understanding structural problems precedes behaviour change; empathy without systems insight remains incomplete.
- Cross-functional conflict often masks process failures, not personal incompetence—switching roles exposes root causes.

## Open Question
How sustainable is behavioural change from temporary role-swapping when leaders return to their original positions and power structures remain unchanged?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and managers navigating complex organisational conflicts and cultural diversity can adopt empathy-driven techniques like role-swapping to build cross-functional understanding, crucial in large enterprises and startups with siloed teams.
