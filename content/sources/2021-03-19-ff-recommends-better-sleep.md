## Core Argument
Better sleep requires systemic lifestyle redesign, not willpower; it's foundational to cognitive and physical performance.

## Key Points
- Sleep quality clears brain toxins, consolidates learning, and prevents weight gain—ignoring it has compounding costs.
- Quantity myth debunked: quality sleep matters more than fixed eight-hour targets; personalised rhythm is key.
- Meditation, physical routine, and meal timing work synergistically; isolated fixes don't address root causes of insomnia.

## Open Question
If sleep is this critical to performance and learning, why does Indian work culture continue to valorise sleep deprivation as a sign of commitment?

## India Angle
Indian work culture glorifies overwork and hustle; this challenges the 'Macho Man' mentality prevalent in Indian newsrooms and startups where sleep deprivation is worn as a badge of honour.
