## Core Argument
Market leaders now disrupt through bundling, not innovation—Slack and Zoom face imitation, not disruption.

## Key Points
- Microsoft bundled Teams with Office 365 to grow from zero to 115M users while Slack stalled at 12M
- Disruptive innovation theory fails when incumbents copy designs and leverage distribution; definitions blur
- Conflicts arise when commerce and academia merge—distinct moral values create 'monstrous hybrids' without knowledgeable flexibility

## Open Question
If bundling and imitation are now how market leaders compete, does Clayton Christensen's disruptive innovation theory still hold, or must it be reframed for platform-dominated markets?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies like Reliance Jio are adopting Microsoft's bundling strategy against established players like Zoom, mirroring global patterns of disruptive imitation. India ranks 20th in global AI index, raising questions about innovation versus imitation as a competitive strategy.
