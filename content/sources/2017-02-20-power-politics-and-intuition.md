## Core Argument
India's institutions face deep structural challenges: criminal politics, personality-centric leadership, and digital divides require systemic reform.

## Key Points
- Criminal candidates thrive because voters accept symbiotic relationships with criminals modelled on film archetypes.
- Infosys clash reflects tension between founder-led cultures and global corporate governance requirements.
- India's consumers have mobile phones but lack basic services; internet access doesn't translate to banking adoption in rural areas.

## Open Question
Why do Indian voters rationally support criminals if they understand corruption harms them, and can this be solved through transparency alone?
