## Core Argument
AI winners redesign business systems and value creation; optimizers of old models become obsolete.

## Key Points
- AI reshapes entire value chains—not just tasks. Winners rethink strategy, capabilities, and competition fundamentally.
- Data readiness from a decade ago now separates leaders from laggards. New entrants must build from basics.
- Incumbent assets are liabilities if tied to legacy models; repurposing them requires systemic redesign, not incremental change.

## Open Question
How can established players break free from legacy habits and assets without destroying the core business that funds transformation?

## India Angle
Indian incumbents in retail, fintech, agriculture, and SaaS must urgently audit data capabilities and rethink business models—not just adopt AI tools. Startups like BharatPe, Infra.Market, and agritech firms risk being disrupted if they optimize old models rather than redesign value creation.
