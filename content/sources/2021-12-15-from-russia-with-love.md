## Core Argument
Russia punches above its weight in geopolitics by leveraging intrigue and positioning, not economic strength.

## Key Points
- Putin inserts himself as arbitrator in unrelated global disputes (OPEC, Turkey, India, Germany, Taliban), securing long-term influence.
- Russia wields disproportionate geopolitical power despite marginal economy, underfunded military, and GDP smaller than Guangdong.
- U.S. hegemony is ending; Russia exploits chaos by securing influence at minimal cost while U.S. and China spend billions.

## Open Question
How can a declining state with weak fundamentals sustain outsized geopolitical influence when underlying economic and military capacity continues to erode?

## India Angle
India is explicitly mentioned as complaining to Putin about Chinese border incursions, illustrating how India navigates great power competition and must engage strategically with Russia despite economic weakness—relevant for Indian foreign policy and regional security strategy.
