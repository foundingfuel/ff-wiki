## Core Argument
Haier reinvents itself as a platform of micro-enterprises to avoid disruption and accelerate innovation.

## Key Points
- Dismantled hierarchy and middle management to create flat marketplace of ideas and autonomous micro-entrepreneurs
- Flattened organizational boundaries to speed up communication between customers, employees and external partners
- Shifted compensation from fixed salary to value created, making employees responsible for their own P&L

## Open Question
Can radical decentralization and employee autonomy actually scale without fragmentation, or does Haier risk losing cohesion and brand identity as independent micro-enterprises proliferate?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates and traditional manufacturers (appliances, textiles, FMCG) face similar commoditization pressures; Haier's micro-entrepreneurship model offers a blueprint for how large Indian incumbents can foster innovation and stay relevant against digital-native disruption.
