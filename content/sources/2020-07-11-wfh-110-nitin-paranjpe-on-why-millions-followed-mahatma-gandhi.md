## Core Argument
Authentic conviction and unwavering belief inspire followership; leaders must lead themselves first.

## Key Points
- Great leaders don't attract followers through strategy alone; they inspire through authentic conviction and point of view.
- Willingness to act alone demonstrates commitment that paradoxically attracts others; authenticity precedes followership.
- Indian leadership lesson: Gandhi's example shows conviction matters more than initial support or guaranteed success.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance authentic conviction with the need to build consensus and bring stakeholders along in complex modern organizations?
