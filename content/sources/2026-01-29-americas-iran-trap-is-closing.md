## Core Argument
US-Iran nuclear brinkmanship risks regional war with catastrophic consequences for global energy and economies.

## Key Points
- Trump administration weighs preventive strikes on Iran's nuclear facilities versus internal destabilization campaigns.
- Gulf monarchies refuse US use of territory for strikes, fearing Iranian retaliation more than US displeasure.
- Strait of Hormuz disruption would spike oil prices, insurance costs, and trigger energy-driven inflation globally.

## Open Question
How can the US pursue regime pressure without triggering regional war when regional allies refuse support and Iran retains significant military capacity?

## India Angle
India is heavily dependent on Middle Eastern oil and gas imports; any regional conflict risks energy security, inflation, and economic stability. India must also navigate diplomatic relationships with US, Iran, Saudi Arabia, and UAE simultaneously.
