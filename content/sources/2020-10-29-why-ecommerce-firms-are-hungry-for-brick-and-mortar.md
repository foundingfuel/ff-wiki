## Core Argument
Ecommerce firms acquire traditional retail when regulation erodes their pricing edge and experiments take too long.

## Key Points
- Government action and trader pressure eliminated ecommerce's pricing advantage as a growth lever in India.
- Acquisitions like Flipkart-ABFRL offer faster growth than organic product innovation and data analytics experiments.
- Rating systems amplify human biases, creating liability even when intended to establish trust.

## Open Question
How do platforms maintain fairness in ratings-based systems when discrimination is inevitable, and any corrective measure risks being called discriminatory itself?

## India Angle
Indian ecommerce giants like Flipkart are acquiring brick-and-mortar assets due to regulatory constraints from trader pressure and loss of pricing advantage—a uniquely Indian dynamic where government action and organized retail lobbying reshape competition.
