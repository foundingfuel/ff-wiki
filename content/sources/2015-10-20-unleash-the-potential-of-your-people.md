## Core Argument
Organizations unlock competitive advantage by enabling internal talent growth through aggressive delegation and embracing crisis as learning.

## Key Points
- Reckless delegation to juniors reveals hidden capacity; inexperience isn't a barrier when people are relentless learners.
- Sharing business challenges openly drives voluntary upskilling; people self-organize to solve problems without external pressure.
- Internal talent mobility reduces hiring burden while improving retention and productivity—especially critical in India's talent-scarce environment.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically identify and nurture relentless learners at scale without creating a two-tier workforce of high-potential and stagnant employees?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises facing talent scarcity can unlock internal potential through delegation and learning culture rather than external hiring—critical for scaling businesses in competitive markets.
