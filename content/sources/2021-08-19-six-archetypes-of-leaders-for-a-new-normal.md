## Core Argument
Resilient leaders for the new normal embody diverse archetypes—jungle gyms, creative coders—who blend cross-sector learning with adaptive thinking.

## Key Points
- Jungle gym leaders thrive by cross-pollinating learnings across sectors, building resilience and spotting unexpected opportunities.
- Creative coders who combine technical acumen with user-centric design solve business problems and drive innovation.
- Meena Ganesh's pivot from NIIT/Microsoft to healthcare demonstrates how diverse experiences create competitive advantage in scaling ventures.

## Open Question
Does the article's emphasis on diverse, unconventional career paths apply equally to leaders in traditional hierarchies, or is it primarily valuable for entrepreneurs and digital natives?

## India Angle
Indian leaders like Meena Ganesh and Neha Singh exemplify new archetypes needed for India's digital transformation and scaling challenges; relevant for building resilient Indian organizations navigating post-pandemic economy.
