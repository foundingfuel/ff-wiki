## Core Argument
Technology spreads both truth and lies fast; automation displaces jobs but may create new ones; personalised education offers promise but requires iteration.

## Key Points
- Social platforms amplify lies faster than fact-checkers can debunk them, threatening democracy via polarised minorities.
- Automation like Pepper replaces boring work; ATMs expanded banking jobs, but exponential tech growth may outpace human value creation.
- Personalised EdTech (AltSchool) shows promise but demands iterative refinement and significant capital investment.

## Open Question
Can technology solve the problems it created—detecting misinformation, moderating abuse—without compromising freedom of speech, and will this happen before exponential automation outpaces new job creation?

## India Angle
India's rapid digital adoption creates parallel challenges: misinformation spreads fast on WhatsApp and social media; automation threatens jobs in BPOs and retail; EdTech solutions must work within India's diverse infrastructure constraints.
