## Core Argument
Organizations fail by optimizing parts instead of studying systems; checklists and failure analysis prevent costly mistakes.

## Key Points
- Healthcare, teaching, law, finance ignore routine failures instead of systematically studying patterns and devising solutions.
- Work-from-anywhere enables global talent access and diversity but requires leadership alignment—Meta's dispersed executives amid crisis raises questions.
- India's booster strategy ignores mRNA/protein-mix science; Covovax available but unapproved, showing failure to systematize evidence-based decisions.

## Open Question
Can distributed leadership teams function effectively during organizational crises, or does proximity remain essential for real-time strategic alignment?

## India Angle
Indian companies adopting WFA must balance global talent access with building cohesive cultures; government health policy decisions (booster strategy) show risk of not studying routine failures systematically
