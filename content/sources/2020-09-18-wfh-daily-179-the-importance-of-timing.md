## Core Argument
Timing of decisions—from earnings calls to daily work schedules—explains 20% of performance variance yet remains neglected.

## Key Points
- Time-of-day affects workplace performance by 20%; afternoon earnings calls show more negativity regardless of fundamentals.
- Rs 39,402 crore EPF withdrawals (212% jump vs 2019) reveal crisis: 21M salaried jobs lost by August 2020.
- Three states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) account for half of EPF withdrawals due to lockdown impact on industrial sectors.

## Open Question
If timing explains 20% of performance variance, why do organizations still neglect 'when' decisions compared to obsessing over 'what,' 'how,' and 'who'?

## India Angle
Indian workforce facing unprecedented job losses and EPF withdrawals; timing of earnings calls affects stock prices; formal sector salaried jobs in India dropped 24% (86M to 65M) by August 2020, concentrated in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu.
