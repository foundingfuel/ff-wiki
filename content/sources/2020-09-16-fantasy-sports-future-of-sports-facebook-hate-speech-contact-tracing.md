## Core Argument
Technology amplifies existing human forces—both good and destructive—requiring systemic redesign, not just engineering fixes.

## Key Points
- Dream Sports' $2.5B valuation reflects fantasy sports surge (2M to 100M users), but faces uneven state-level regulation and job creation scrutiny.
- Facebook's Law of Amplification: platforms connect both knowledge-seekers and hate groups equally, creating vicious cycles especially in fragile democracies.
- Contact tracing success requires cross-border cooperation and systemic redesign, not just tech development—a lesson for India's governance infrastructure.

## Open Question
Can Facebook (or any platform) be fixed through internal engineering and content moderation, or does its business model fundamentally require structural redesign that no company will voluntarily undertake?

## India Angle
India's fantasy sports boom (100M users, $2.5B unicorn) faces regulatory fragmentation across states; Facebook's amplification of hate speech poses acute risks in polarized Indian context; contact tracing efforts reveal governance capability gaps India must address.
