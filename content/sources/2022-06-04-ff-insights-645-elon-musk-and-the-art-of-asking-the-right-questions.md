## Core Argument
Framing the right question matters more than finding answers; this shapes which problems entrepreneurs choose to solve.

## Key Points
- Musk prioritizes physics-based first-principles thinking over business convention to gain edge.
- He asks 'What problems will most influence the future?' to identify three pivotal domains.
- Question quality determines answer accessibility; reframing unlocks solutions invisible to others.

## Open Question
If asking the right question is harder than answering it, how do entrepreneurs develop the metacognitive skill to know which questions matter most before solving them?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders can adopt Musk's first-principles thinking and question-framing approach to identify and solve India-specific problems in energy, technology, and infrastructure without being constrained by conventional wisdom.
