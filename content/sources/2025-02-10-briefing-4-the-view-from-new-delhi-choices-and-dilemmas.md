## Core Argument
India must strategically navigate competing pressures from the US, China, and Europe while managing limited leverage in each relationship.

## Key Points
- Trump administration prioritizes business interests over friendships; India must appear generous while giving little away.
- China's approach to India fluctuates with US-China relations; India must reduce economic dependency on Chinese imports.
- European regulatory frameworks (carbon tax, data protection) create friction with India's business-friendly policies despite historical socialist influence.

## Open Question
How can India maintain strategic autonomy and domestic political legitimacy while making concessions to powerful external actors who each demand different terms?

## India Angle
India faces unprecedented geopolitical pressure navigating relationships with the US (under Trump), China (border tensions, economic dependency), and Europe (regulatory conflicts), while balancing domestic political demands with international strategic interests.
