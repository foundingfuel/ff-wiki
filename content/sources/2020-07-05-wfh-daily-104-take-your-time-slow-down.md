## Core Argument
Depth and deliberation beat speed; emotional awareness builds resilience in volatile times.

## Key Points
- FOMO-driven consumption (2.5X podcasts, endless webinars) creates illusion of progress while draining cognitive capacity.
- Emotional intelligence begins with self-awareness—naming feelings specifically triggers better management and resilience.
- Leaders build trust by admitting assumptions are wrong, protecting messengers, and learning visibly from failures.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the need to move fast, inspire confidence, and admit uncertainty simultaneously without eroding team momentum?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers, particularly in tech and startups, are especially vulnerable to FOMO-driven overwork culture; emotional intelligence and deliberate slowing down are counterintuitive but necessary for sustainable high performance in India's competitive startup ecosystem.
