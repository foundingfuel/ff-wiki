## Core Argument
Crises force rapid behavioral change; India must institutionalize learnings in health, climate, supply chains.

## Key Points
- Four unprecedented experiments emerging: remote work, online learning, reduced emissions, heightened hygiene awareness.
- India can replicate Singapore/China pandemic management: testing, isolation, resource mobilization, citizen compliance.
- Crisis-driven behavioral shifts must be sustained post-pandemic to address climate change and public health systematically.

## Open Question
How can temporary crisis-driven behavioral changes (remote work, reduced travel, hygiene focus) be institutionalized into permanent systems without requiring ongoing crisis as motivator?

## India Angle
India must strengthen public health systems and supply chain resilience post-COVID; opportunity to leapfrog in online learning (Byju's, Toppr models) and hygiene awareness, critical for developing economy challenges.
