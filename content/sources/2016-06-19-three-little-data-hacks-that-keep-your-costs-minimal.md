## Core Argument
Resource-constrained teams can derive valuable business insights through hybrid manual-automated data approaches without Big Data infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Manual data extraction combined with Excel macros can yield rich insights for niche problems when automated solutions aren't cost-effective.
- Prototype data-gathering strategies first to test hypotheses before investing in comprehensive data infrastructure or collection.
- Prioritize easiest-access data sources (APIs, social platforms) over complex algorithmic solutions to solve immediate business questions.

## Open Question
How do teams scale these scrappy data hacks without losing the contextual understanding and agility that comes from manual processes?

## India Angle
Indian startups and bootstrapped businesses can extract competitive data insights without large capital investment by combining manual effort with basic tools—critical for India's resource-constrained entrepreneurship ecosystem.
