## Core Argument
Attrition requires understanding root causes and systemic factors, not blame games or predictive fantasies.

## Key Points
- Leaders often blame HR or use attrition to mask performance gaps instead of examining their own role in retention
- Macro pull factors (economic growth, wage competition, new entrants) interact with company-specific push factors to drive attrition
- Building talent through culture and nurturing is harder but more sustainable than competing on wage premiums

## Open Question
How can organisations accept attrition as inevitable given macro-environmental forces while still taking accountability for retention within their control?
