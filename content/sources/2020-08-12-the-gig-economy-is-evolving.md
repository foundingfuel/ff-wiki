## Core Argument
Gig economy evolution requires systemic solutions beyond binary employee-contractor divide through market feedback and innovation.

## Key Points
- California's AB5 law exposed tension between platform economics and worker protections; companies resist employee classification.
- Khosrowshahi proposes 'third way'—benefit funds for gig workers—acknowledging system responds to pressure for change.
- Emerging 'passion economy' platforms enable individual differentiation, entrepreneurship; complements but doesn't solve core gig economy issues.

## Open Question
Can benefit funds and platform innovation adequately address worker security without fundamentally restructuring the gig economy business model, or is employment classification inevitable?

## India Angle
India's gig economy (ride-hailing, delivery, freelance platforms) faces similar employment classification pressures; government must design regulatory frameworks balancing worker protections with platform scalability and affordability for consumers.
