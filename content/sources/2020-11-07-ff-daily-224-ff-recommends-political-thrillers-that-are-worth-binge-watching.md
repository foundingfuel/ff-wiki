## Core Argument
Political thrillers offer edge-of-seat drama exploring power, media spin, and moral ambiguity across democracies.

## Key Points
- Indian films (New Delhi Times, Shanghai, Raajneeti) examine corruption and political intrigue rooted in Hindi heartland realities.
- International classics (Borgen, Frost/Nixon) depict leadership vulnerabilities and media's role in shaping political narratives.
- Genre blends personal stakes with systemic power plays, making politics viscerally human rather than abstract.

## Open Question
Do political thrillers educate viewers about real governance failures, or do they cynically reinforce distrust in all institutions?

## India Angle
Indian political thrillers like New Delhi Times, Shanghai, and Raajneeti reflect how India's film industry explores corruption, media manipulation, and power struggles—themes deeply resonant with Indian audiences navigating complex political realities.
