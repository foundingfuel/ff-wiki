## Core Argument
Industrial Revolution fundamentally reshaped society; India must navigate sustainability transitions without repeating historical damage.

## Key Points
- 1769 marks Industrial Revolution's pivot—reshaping economics, morality, gender roles, and governance globally.
- India faces concurrent crises: 16-month education loss and accelerating climate emergency requiring systemic recovery.
- Sustainable innovation requires 'built-in' redesign, not retrofitting—P&G's approach suggests rethinking production from inception.

## Open Question
How can India industrialize rapidly to lift people out of poverty while simultaneously adopting sustainability practices that developed nations ignored during their own Industrial Revolution?

## India Angle
India is explicitly named as a nation undergoing industrialization post-Industrial Revolution. The education crisis and sustainability challenges disproportionately affect India's development trajectory and ability to leapfrog into sustainable industrial practices rather than repeating Western mistakes.
