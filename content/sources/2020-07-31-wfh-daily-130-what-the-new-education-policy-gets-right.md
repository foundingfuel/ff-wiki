## Core Argument
NEP's flexibility and multidisciplinarity free Indian students from administrative constraints to pursue talent-aligned learning.

## Key Points
- Credit banking and inter-university transfers allow students to pause, re-enter, and choose institutions based on fit.
- Abolishing stream-based silos enables subject choice freedom rather than administrator-prescribed academic paths.
- Board exam flexibility and multiple attempts reduce high-stakes gatekeeping in Indian secondary and tertiary education.

## Open Question
The NEP is a visionary guideline lacking concrete implementation plans—how will schools and universities operationalize flexibility without infrastructure, teacher training, and systemic incentive restructuring?

## India Angle
India's New Education Policy addresses systemic constraints in Indian higher education—rigid streams, inflexible timelines, credit transferability—that have locked students into predetermined paths. For Indian educators and policymakers, this signals a shift toward student-centric flexibility, though implementation capacity remains unclear.
