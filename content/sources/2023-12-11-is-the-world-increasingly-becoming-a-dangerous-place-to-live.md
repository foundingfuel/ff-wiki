## Core Argument
Humanity faces converging existential threats from AI-nuclear nexus, climate change, and pandemic risk.

## Key Points
- AI-enabled nuclear weapons and cyber manipulation of early warning systems create accidental war risk.
- Climate change causes cascading crises: floods, droughts, biodiversity loss, and societal destabilization.
- US-China military cooperation agreement on AI offers fragile hope but lacks transparency on implementation.

## Open Question
Can bilateral US-China cooperation on AI governance prevent accidental nuclear war when global non-proliferation mechanisms are collapsing?

## India Angle
India faces overlapping existential risks from nuclear proliferation in South Asia, climate-induced migration and resource scarcity, and AI-weaponization by major powers—requiring strategic autonomy and defensive preparedness alongside climate adaptation.
