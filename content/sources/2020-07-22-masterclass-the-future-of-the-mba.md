## Core Argument
MBA education must evolve from knowing to doing-being; technology enables just-in-time, lifelong, diverse learning.

## Key Points
- MBA curricula overemphasize 'knowing' while neglecting 'doing' and 'being'—practical skills, ethics, and leadership capacity.
- Technology (5G, AR) enables on-demand, lifelong learning, breaking rigid program structures and democratizing access beyond geography.
- Diversity and generalism matter more than traditional credentials; reimagined selection should prepare for tomorrow, not yesterday.

## Open Question
If MBA shifts to lifelong, modular, diverse learning pathways, how do institutions maintain rigor, credential value, and coherent identity while serving 16-year-olds and 60-year-olds simultaneously?

## India Angle
Indian MBA institutions must reimagine purpose beyond wealth maximization, embrace lifelong learning through tech (5G/AR), and democratize access beyond traditional elite pools—critical as India scales middle-class leadership needs.
