## Core Argument
Pandemic forces professionals to rebuild productivity rituals; success requires intentional structure, not just remote flexibility.

## Key Points
- WFH eliminated old productivity frameworks; workers must create new rituals aligned to personal circadian rhythms.
- Structure matters more without office environment; morning/evening assemblies, time-blocking, and asynchronous protocols prevent chaos.
- Sustainable productivity requires discipline, mindfulness, and selective abandonment of habits; not optimization alone.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain cohesion and spontaneous collaboration in hybrid/remote models where async work is necessary but team bonding suffers?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers navigating WFH transition post-pandemic; relevant for Indian companies designing hybrid work models and employee productivity systems in absence of traditional office structures.
