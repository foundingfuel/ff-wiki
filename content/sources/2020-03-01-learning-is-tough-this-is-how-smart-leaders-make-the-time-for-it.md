## Core Argument
Smart leaders embed continuous learning into daily work flow, curate knowledge wisely, and learn from peer communities.

## Key Points
- Learning embedded in everyday work context is more sustainable than formal training programs.
- Leaders must curate from abundance of knowledge rather than struggle with scarcity.
- Smart peers in learning communities challenge perspectives more effectively than solo learning.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance urgent operational demands with deep learning time when both compete for attention in activity-intense environments?

## India Angle
Indian leaders face acute pressure to stay relevant amid rapid tech disruption and competitive intensity; making deliberate learning time is critical for Indian organizations to avoid complacency and sustain competitive advantage.
