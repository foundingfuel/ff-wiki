## Core Argument
Buildings reflect organizational predictions; post-pandemic offices need flexible strategy, not rigid plans.

## Key Points
- Institutional buildings resist change; commercial spaces adapt constantly—offices must shift toward adaptive design philosophy.
- Brand's framework: all buildings are predictions that prove wrong; strategy trumps planning in uncertain conditions.
- Post-pandemic work is hybrid and fragmented; leaders must design offices with manoeuvring room for unpredictable scenarios.

## Open Question
How can legacy institutional office buildings be retrofitted for adaptive use when they're structurally designed to resist organizational change?

## India Angle
Indian companies are pushing employees back to offices designed for pre-pandemic work models; leaders must redesign physical and operational strategies to accommodate hybrid preferences and modern work expectations rather than enforcing old plans.
