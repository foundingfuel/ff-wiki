## Core Argument
India faces dual crises: unregulated data mining and technological joblessness without privacy or labor policy frameworks.

## Key Points
- Citizens unknowingly surrender personal data to private entities via routine transactions with no regulatory oversight.
- Data has become the most valuable resource; companies mining it to create intimate behavioral profiles for competitive advantage.
- Large Indian employers planning 90% workforce reduction through automation, threatening middle-class stability without transition support.

## Open Question
How can India harness data-driven innovation and automation benefits while protecting citizen privacy and preventing mass economic disruption to its middle class?
