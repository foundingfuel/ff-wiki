## Core Argument
Aadhaar's potential was lost not to bad technology, but to poor governance, ego-driven battles, and courts deciding tech policy.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar needed iterative refinement via an oversight body, not destroyed by courts mediating technical complexity.
- Supreme Court lacked technical expertise to adjudicate foundational identity infrastructure; judges froze innovation in time.
- No regulatory framework exists for data storage, misuse penalties, or stakeholder consultation—the real governance failure.

## Open Question
How can India enable transformative infrastructure when courts address tech policy, bureaucracy lacks consultative mechanisms, and both proponents and opponents reject iterative improvement?

## India Angle
Aadhaar's failed potential to leapfrog India's governance infrastructure—a system that could address inequality and inefficiency but was crippled by adversarial implementation, lack of regulatory oversight, and judicial intervention on technical matters outside courts' domain.
