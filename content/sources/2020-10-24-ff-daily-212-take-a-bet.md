## Core Argument
Align values with strengths; bet on predictions to expose blind spots and calibrate confidence.

## Key Points
- Values and strengths must coexist; misalignment causes frustration and poor performance regardless of ability.
- Think probabilistically about future uncertainties rather than seeking false certainty about outcomes.
- Use 'would you bet on this?' to discipline forecasts and uncover what others know that you don't.

## Open Question
How do you know if a conflict between values and strengths reflects genuine misalignment or merely short-term discomfort in pursuing difficult but meaningful work?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organizations often struggle with aligning personal values to career success; the asafoetida example shows how India's economic self-reliance depends on questioning inherited assumptions about what we can or cannot produce domestically.
