## Core Argument
AI reshuffles knowledge economy power dynamics by coordinating workflows, not just automating tasks.

## Key Points
- AI acts as coordinator restructuring workflows and organizations, shifting power between workers, firms, and tool providers
- Four key tensions emerge: workers vs tools, providers vs firms, consolidators vs disrupted industries, individuals vs incumbents
- Historical parallels like shipping containers show AI as infrastructure that redefines value chains and economic structures

## Open Question
How do incumbent knowledge workers and firms adapt when AI becomes the primary coordinator of value creation, and who captures the extracted value?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge economy workers and tech firms face restructuring as AI becomes a coordination layer; talent arbitrage advantages erode while new skill demands emerge. Policy must address labor displacement in IT services and BPO sectors while fostering AI-native business models.
