## Core Argument
Abstracted digital systems weaken first-principles thinking and causal reasoning, eroding confidence in understanding how the world works.

## Key Points
- Modern products designed for use, not understanding, creating asymmetry between builders and users.
- Neuroplasticity shows hands-on engagement strengthens causal reasoning, spatial intuition, and problem decomposition skills.
- Risk is not lost capability but lost confidence—the belief that one can take things apart and understand them independently.

## Open Question
How do we preserve spaces for friction and making in a world that optimizes for frictionless, efficient use of tools whose inner logic remains hidden?

## India Angle
For India, where engineering and hands-on problem-solving have been core to education and workforce development, this shift risks eroding the tinkering culture that built India's IT and manufacturing sectors. It also raises questions about how India's education system balances AI adoption with maintaining deep technical literacy.
