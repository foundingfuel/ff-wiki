## Core Argument
Incentive-driven growth without cultural foundation creates unintended harms; ambition without ethics invites future judgment.

## Key Points
- Metrics-focused scaling (IT services, platforms) prioritizes growth over culture, masking systemic issues.
- He Jiankui's gene-editing case shows how today's villain may become tomorrow's pioneer if long-term benefits emerge.
- Tech dominance through vertical integration repeatedly fails; companies overestimate ability to control entire value chains.

## Open Question
How do we judge ambition—by today's moral and safety standards or by potential 100-year outcomes that justify current risks and rule-breaking?

## India Angle
Indian IT services and tech platforms (Oyo, cab-hailing apps) exemplify how growth-at-all-costs metrics erode culture and ethical practices; relevant to how Indian tech companies scale globally.
