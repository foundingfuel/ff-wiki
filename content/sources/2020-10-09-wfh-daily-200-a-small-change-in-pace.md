## Core Argument
Publishing frequency and audience engagement evolve as WFH becomes permanent rather than temporary.

## Key Points
- WFH Daily pivots from daily to Monday-Saturday cadence after 200 editions, signaling WFH normalcy shift.
- Generational divide emerges: older generation values stadium experience; younger prefers TV/digital sports consumption.
- Digital presence becoming prerequisite for discovery, employment, and serendipitous opportunities in post-pandemic world.

## Open Question
If WFH is now permanent and the newsletter's scope has expanded beyond just work-from-home topics, does the name change adequately reflect the broader lifestyle transformation, or does it dilute the original mission clarity?

## India Angle
Indian media and newsletter landscape adapting to pandemic-driven WFH culture; generational shifts in how Indians consume sports and digital engagement reflect broader transformation in work and lifestyle habits.
