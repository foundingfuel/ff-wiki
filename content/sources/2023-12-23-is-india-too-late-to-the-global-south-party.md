## Core Argument
India has a geopolitical opportunity to lead the Global South, but lacks the funding and track record to guarantee success.

## Key Points
- Developing nations resent Western abandonment during Covid/Ukraine, and distrust China's debt-trap model and declining investment.
- India is testing a leadership role by offering practical solutions: green finance, biofuels, digital infrastructure—areas where it has domestic advantages.
- India faces a structural constraint: it cannot fund its Global South ambitions without Western and Japanese partnerships, risking its independence narrative.

## Open Question
Can India genuinely lead the Global South while depending on US and Japanese funding and partnership, or does this undermine its claimed independence from major powers?

## India Angle
India is positioned as a potential leader for the Global South, offering an alternative to Western and Chinese influence. This represents a strategic opportunity for India to expand geopolitical influence and shape development narratives, but requires demonstrating tangible solutions beyond rhetoric.
