## Core Argument
Europe's new Iron Curtain signals sustained geopolitical realignment requiring India to recalibrate foreign policy and strategic positioning.

## Key Points
- NATO is dramatically escalating military presence in Eastern Europe, signaling long-term confrontation with Russia.
- Western and non-Western readings of Putin and the conflict differ fundamentally, creating alignment fractures.
- A 1,000km eastward shift of Cold War boundaries is hardening into a new geopolitical fault line.

## Open Question
How can non-aligned nations like India maintain strategic autonomy and economic relationships while managing pressure to choose sides in a hardening bipolar world?

## India Angle
India must navigate a hardening geopolitical divide between the West and Russia-aligned powers, affecting its strategic autonomy, defense partnerships, and economic ties with both blocs.
