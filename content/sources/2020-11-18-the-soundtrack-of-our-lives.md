## Core Argument
Music creates empathy, builds community resilience, and catalyzes personal and social transformation across generations.

## Key Points
- Music transmits values and empathy across generations, from mother's womb to childhood rituals shaping worldview.
- Music serves as bridge from personal passion to community action—fundraising, environmental care, safety initiatives in Bandra.
- Isolation paradoxically reconnects us to music's transformative power, grounding meaning in shared soundtracks during disconnection.

## Open Question
How do we sustain music's community-building power in an era of algorithmic, personalized listening that atomizes rather than unifies shared soundtracks?

## India Angle
Indian urban life—Bandra community initiatives, Celebrate Bandra, Kripa Foundation—shows how music and cultural expression can drive local action and social cohesion, particularly relevant to how Indians build meaning and community in metropolises.
