## Core Argument
TikTok reveals intimate consumer creativity; marketers ignoring it risk missing India's identity transformation.

## Key Points
- TikTok's 15-second format enables ordinary Indians to become creators, building intimate followings without institutional gatekeepers.
- Traditional FMCG, broadcast, and finance brands remain disconnected from 200M+ core customers actively engaging on the platform daily.
- Platform reveals changing Indian identity—blend of modernity and tradition—requiring deeper consumer behaviour understanding beyond stereotypes.

## Open Question
If TikTok represents a genuine shift in how Indians consume and express identity, why do established brands treat it as a fad rather than a strategic signal about their customer base's evolving values?

## India Angle
Indian brands and marketers are ignoring TikTok despite 200M+ Indian users, particularly among 'Buntys and Bablis'—Tier 2/3 consumers reshaping content and identity. This represents a fundamental shift in how Indian consumers create, consume and express themselves, requiring marketers to reimagine engagement strategies beyond traditional broadcast models.
