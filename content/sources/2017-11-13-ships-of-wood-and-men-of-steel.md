## Core Argument
Great leadership demands steel-like resilience and unwavering conviction through extreme adversity and uncertainty.

## Key Points
- Shackleton's survival story teaches entrepreneurs why conviction and character matter more than conditions.
- Mentorship and trusted relationships are essential anchors when organizations lose institutional support.
- Founders must internalize that no external protection exists; only internal fortitude and team trust sustain ventures.

## Open Question
How do you cultivate the 'steel' in men (and women) when modern organizational structures emphasize psychological safety and comfort over adversity testing?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders navigating uncertain environments need resilience models; cultural context of trust-based ecosystems and mentorship networks (like Ramkumar's influence) is distinctly relevant to how Indian organizations build leadership.
