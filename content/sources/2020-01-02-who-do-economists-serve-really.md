## Core Argument
Economists must listen to real people's problems, not impose ideological theories divorced from social reality.

## Key Points
- Mainstream economics treats people as data points, ignoring human dignity and complex socio-economic transitions.
- Free trade theory ignores 'stickiness'—people cannot easily relocate when jobs disappear; transition costs are borne by communities.
- Well-being metrics must replace GDP; policymakers should measure what citizens actually value, not economic abstractions.

## Open Question
If economics is 'too important to be left to economists,' who defines the right questions and who decides which citizen voices matter most in policymaking?

## India Angle
India's development depends on economists and policymakers understanding ground realities—Banerjee and Duflo's work in Indian and African communities shows how mainstream economics fails the poorest; India needs policy grounded in citizen aspirations, not just GDP metrics.
