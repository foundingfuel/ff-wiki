## Core Argument
Language localization unlocks India's $30B digital opportunity by serving non-English majority markets ignored by mainstream internet companies.

## Key Points
- Only 4% of Indians speak English fluently; less than 0.01% of internet content is in Indian languages, creating massive gap.
- Internet companies understand English-speaking 'India' but not vernacular-using 'Bharat,' underestimating user acquisition and product-market fit challenges.
- New Indian language internet users are simultaneously adopting smartphones and internet for first time, requiring different engagement and patience than legacy PC-to-mobile users.

## Open Question
How do capital-efficient, venture-backed companies balance the need to bootstrap and prove unit economics before scaling, when market opportunity requires patient, long-term user behavior change investments?

## India Angle
India's 96% non-English speaking population represents a $30B digital opportunity; requires fundamentally different business models and user acquisition strategies than English-focused startups to achieve real digital inclusion.
