## Core Argument
Words and stories shape beliefs and outcomes; institutional credibility determines whether narratives influence behavior.

## Key Points
- Power of narrative: clever use of language and existing beliefs can resolve conflicts without force or direct confrontation.
- Trust in institutions matters: the merchant's success depended on the yaksha's authority being accepted by bandits.
- Self-examination critical: Scientific American's audit of its own biased history is necessary for institutions to shape just futures.

## Open Question
How do societies distinguish between narratives that help people escape danger and narratives that mask bias or manipulation?

## India Angle
Ancient Indian narratives demonstrate how trust, credibility and institutional authority shape economic behavior—relevant for modern India's regulatory frameworks, institutional reputation, and how policy communication shapes compliance and market behavior.
