## Core Argument
Effective communication relies on ruthless editing and framing ideas as curiosity-driven solutions, not moral imperatives.

## Key Points
- Cut content aggressively—remove 50% twice to find the essential message beneath.
- Frame issues as ideas inviting curiosity rather than problems demanding moral judgment to avoid audience fatigue.
- Authentic vulnerability beneath professional facades unlocks genuine human connection in discourse.

## Open Question
How do communicators balance the need for simplification and curiosity-driven messaging with the moral urgency of issues like discrimination or health crises that demand emotional response?
