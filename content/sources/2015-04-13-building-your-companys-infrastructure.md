## Core Argument
Build four-layer infrastructure—physical, digital, intellectual, emotional—before using it, not after.

## Key Points
- Physical infrastructure creates immediate presence but is easily outdone; intellectual infrastructure builds lasting differentiation.
- Digital infrastructure alone is purposeless railroad without content; emotional infrastructure created by inspired people drives memorability.
- All infrastructure requires continuous maintenance and evolution; entrepreneurs must budget perpetually, not build-once-and-forget.

## Open Question
If differentiation is short-lived and infrastructure requires constant reinvestment, how do resource-constrained entrepreneurs decide which layers to prioritize first without overextending?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs often bootstrap with limited resources; this framework helps them prioritize infrastructure investments strategically rather than pursuing visible status symbols, enabling sustainable scaling.
