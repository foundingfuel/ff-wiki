## Core Argument
Online-first entry via established platforms minimizes risk and capex for global retailers in high-growth markets.

## Key Points
- Costco avoided costly brick-and-mortar failures (Home Depot, Best Buy) by launching on Tmall first.
- Platform partnerships provide instant customer access and reduce commercial real estate and staffing costs.
- Rapid sales ($3.5M in 24 hours on Singles Day) prove market demand exists without physical presence.

## Open Question
Does online-first strategy permanently disadvantage brands needing physical 'brand experience,' or is this a temporary launch phase before inevitable offline expansion?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce is following China's trajectory; Indian retailers and global brands entering India should consider platform-first strategies on Flipkart/Amazon to minimize capex while testing market fit, similar to Costco's Tmall approach.
