## Core Argument
Beauty and indigenous wisdom offer practical frameworks for reimagining work, cities, and sustainability in India.

## Key Points
- Helper4U formalizes India's unorganized labour by building trust and enabling wage negotiation across 130+ towns.
- Indigenous low-tech solutions outperform sensor-driven smart cities for water management, flood resilience, and sustainability.
- Beauty as intentional practice connects personal wellbeing to systemic change in how we design systems and work.

## Open Question
How do we scale low-tech indigenous solutions in an era of digital transformation without romanticizing tradition or sacrificing efficiency gains?

## India Angle
India's unorganized labour market and indigenous knowledge systems offer untapped models for sustainable urban development and worker dignity—Helper4U demonstrates labour formalization at scale; indigenous tech solutions are particularly relevant for India's urban challenges and water-scarce regions.
