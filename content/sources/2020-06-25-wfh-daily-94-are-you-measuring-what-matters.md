## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs using scientific method outperform those relying on intuition; measurement must prioritize impact over convenience.

## Key Points
- Startups applying scientific method achieved higher customer acquisition, revenue, and willingness to pivot or exit.
- WFH productivity gains may mask burnout costs; measuring what matters requires caring about long-term wellness, not just output.
- Digital disruption in education mirrors entertainment: technology enables mission fulfillment, not replacement of core value.

## Open Question
How can organizations reconcile increased productivity metrics with unreported burnout and unsustainable work intensity in remote settings?
