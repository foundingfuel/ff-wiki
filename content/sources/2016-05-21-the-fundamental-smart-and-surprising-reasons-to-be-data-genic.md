## Core Argument
Data-driven insights from modest data practices enable customer-centricity, new revenue opportunities, and cross-business innovation.

## Key Points
- Customer behavior data reveals misalignments between company assumptions and actual user preferences
- Incremental data analysis can uncover entirely new business opportunities without sophisticated infrastructure
- Data-led insights are easily transferable across business units, creating scalable competitive advantages

## Open Question
If data is so accessible and valuable, why do most companies continue investing in expensive, sophisticated data infrastructure rather than acting on simpler insights they already have?

## India Angle
Indian digital businesses and content platforms can adopt 'Little Data' approaches to optimize operations without expensive infrastructure, drive customer-centricity, and discover new revenue streams—particularly relevant for bootstrapped startups and SMEs
