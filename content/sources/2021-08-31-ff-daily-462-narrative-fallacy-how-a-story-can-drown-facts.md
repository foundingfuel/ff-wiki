## Core Argument
Narrative fallacy—where compelling stories drown facts—undermines evidence-based policymaking and institutional trust.

## Key Points
- Stories are cognitively stickier than data; demagogues exploit this by injecting false certainty into uncertainty
- Feynman's mindset—admitting ignorance with humility—opens minds to nuance and critical evaluation of claims
- India's monetisation pipeline risks court reversal if narrative of 'value unlock' masks poor governance, as telecom/coal auctions showed

## Open Question
How can leaders admit uncertainty and humility while still projecting the confidence necessary to drive major policy reforms or organisational change?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers and leaders must guard against narrative fallacy when designing major reforms like the NMP; courts and institutions need critical thinking capacity to resist politically compelling but factually unsound stories that undermine policy implementation
