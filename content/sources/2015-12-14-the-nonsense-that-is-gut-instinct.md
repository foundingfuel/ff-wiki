## Core Argument
Gut instinct alone is unreliable; it must be grounded in evidence and systematic thinking.

## Key Points
- Gut instinct is experience-based but can contradict itself, be biased by recent events, and is easily manipulated.
- Structured processes and tools generate exponentially more ideas than intuition alone (300+ vs 35-40 solutions).
- Expert instinct matters only when backed by evidence and rigorous rational analysis first.

## Open Question
If instinct is merely encoded experience, how do we distinguish between reliable expert intuition and manipulated, biased instinct in high-stakes decisions?

## India Angle
Relevant for Indian entrepreneurs and leaders like Harsh Mariwala who must balance gut instinct with data-driven decision-making in building innovative organisations; design thinking adoption in Indian corporates needs this scrutiny.
