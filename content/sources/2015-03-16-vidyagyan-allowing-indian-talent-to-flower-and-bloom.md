## Core Argument
Residential schools for underprivileged talent can unlock India's human potential and break cycles of deprivation.

## Key Points
- VidyaGyan provides world-class education free to talented students from families earning under Rs 2 lakh annually.
- Community disconnect risk emerges as students gain English proficiency and exposure, requiring intentional bridging mechanisms.
- Market-driven test coaching threatens merit-based selection, creating unequal playing field despite intentions.

## Open Question
How can VidyaGyan retain students' ties to their communities and ensure they contribute locally once exposed to global opportunities, without limiting their ambitions?

## India Angle
Addresses India's critical problem of wasted human potential among poor but talented youth; demonstrates philanthropic model solving access gaps that government institutions alone cannot fill in tier-2 Indian towns.
