## Core Argument
Governments can innovate like private sector by borrowing tools, attracting lateral talent, and solving problems before technology.

## Key Points
- Large organizations in public and private sectors face identical constraints and opportunities regardless of sector
- Innovation requires problem-first thinking; technology is a tool, not an end goal
- Government needs hybrid leaders with private-sector experience who can regulate without sacrificing citizen welfare

## Open Question
How can governments attract top private-sector talent and adopt entrepreneurial methods without compromising accountability, transparency, and public interest over profit?

## India Angle
India's government capacity and public service delivery depend on attracting entrepreneurial mindsets and private-sector tools into bureaucracy. Aadhaar, lateral hiring, and frugal innovation are Indian-specific examples of how this can work.
