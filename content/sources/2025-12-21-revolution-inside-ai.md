## Core Argument
AI's true revolution is moving from language models to world models and safety-first governance.

## Key Points
- Leading AI researchers (LeCun, Hinton, Bengio) are abandoning LLM-centric approaches for world models that capture causality and physical understanding.
- World models enable reliable AI in safety-critical domains like robotics, climate modeling, and scientific discovery through simulation rather than language prediction.
- China and leading labs are shifting toward domain-specific, purpose-built AI systems over generic LLMs, with safety governance becoming central to research agendas.

## Open Question
If world models and causal reasoning are superior to LLMs, why did LLMs dominate commercial and public discourse for so long, and what barriers prevent this scientific consensus from reshaping industry direction faster?

## India Angle
India must develop indigenous AI research capabilities beyond LLM replication. The global shift toward world models and safety-first approaches offers India an opportunity to leapfrog into domain-specific AI (science, climate, healthcare) rather than chasing LLM scale—requiring policy frameworks for specialized AI development and geopolitical autonomy in AI research.
