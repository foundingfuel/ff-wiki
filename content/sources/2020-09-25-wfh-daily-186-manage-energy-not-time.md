## Core Argument
Manage employee energy through rituals, not time optimization, to prevent pandemic-era burnout.

## Key Points
- WFH workers spend 3+ hours longer working but feel more fatigued despite saved commute time.
- Energy renewal across body, emotions, mind, spirit requires intentional rituals made automatic.
- Organizations must invest in people's wellbeing, not extract more output from finite time.

## Open Question
How can organizations enforce energy management rituals without creating another layer of surveillance or performative wellness theater in remote work settings?

## India Angle
Indian organizations transitioning to WFH must shift from time-based productivity metrics to energy management systems; critical for retaining talent in high-intensity work culture where burnout is endemic.
