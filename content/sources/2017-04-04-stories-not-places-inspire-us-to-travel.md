## Core Argument
Stories, not visuals, drive travel decisions and create memorable experiences that people actually value.

## Key Points
- Taj Mahal attracts 68 lakh visitors yearly vs Bibi Ka Maqbara's 6.6 lakh due to narrative, not architecture.
- Shared stories create neural synchronization between teller and listener, enabling vicarious experience.
- Airbnb's 'Magical Trips' succeeded by selling curated experiences and narratives over destination features.

## Open Question
If memories are what we ultimately keep from experiences, should tourism industry focus on creating memorable moments or selling the stories that encode those moments?

## India Angle
Indian tourism boards rely on visual advertising while missing the narrative power that drives travel decisions. Taj Mahal vs Bibi Ka Maqbara exemplifies how India's rich heritage remains underutilized without compelling stories attached.
