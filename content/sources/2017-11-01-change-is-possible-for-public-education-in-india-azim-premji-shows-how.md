## Core Argument
Systemic education change requires shifting from projects to institution-building and partnerships with government.

## Key Points
- Good education develops multiple dimensions: cognitive, physical, social, emotional, ethical—not just grades.
- Dearth of well-trained education professionals identified as major bottleneck; Azim Premji University created to address.
- Foundation work evolved from executing projects (10 years) to building institutions and partnerships for scale.

## Open Question
How can philanthropic initiatives scale systemic education improvement when government ownership and political will remain variable across states?

## India Angle
Public education in India serves 200M+ children across 1.5M government schools; addressing systemic improvement in disadvantaged districts requires institutional partnerships and trained education professionals—a uniquely Indian scale and equity challenge.
