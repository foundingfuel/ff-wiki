## Core Argument
Transformation begins when leaders listen to frontline workers and cascade vision through entire organization.

## Key Points
- Best Buy's turnaround started with CEO spending first days in stores listening to employees, not executives.
- Banking sector must shift from incremental change to transformative vision cascaded from boardroom to middle management.
- India's rural healthcare collapse stems from 1990s privatization policies—shows cost of ignoring systemic leadership and implementation.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the need for rapid transformation with ensuring it becomes systemic across all levels, especially in large hierarchical organizations like banks?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and bankers must adopt Joly's ground-level listening approach and transformative vision to navigate digital disruption; India's healthcare crisis shows costs of inadequate leadership and policy transformation at scale.
