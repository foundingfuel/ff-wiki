## Core Argument
Epic's fight against Apple reflects deeper tension between platform control and ecosystem value in digital markets.

## Key Points
- Epic strategically challenged Apple's 30% commission and app distribution monopoly across multiple jurisdictions simultaneously.
- Apple's walled garden promises user protection but extracts value through gatekeeping; debate is whether extraction exceeds value delivered.
- Open protocols (Beckn model) present alternative to platform intermediaries, enabling direct market participant interaction without gatekeepers.

## Open Question
Can Apple's user protection benefits justify its 30% commission and exclusive control, or does the digital distribution cost warrant a lower fee and open ecosystem?

## India Angle
Beckn protocol offers India an alternative to Apple's walled-garden model; implications for Indian startups and digital platforms like Swiggy, Uber competing against gatekeepers; relevant to India's digital infrastructure approach (Aadhaar, UPI model).
