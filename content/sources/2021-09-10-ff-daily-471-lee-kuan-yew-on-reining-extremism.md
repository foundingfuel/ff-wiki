## Core Argument
Countering extremism requires multilateral coalitions involving moderate Muslim governments and discrediting violent ideology.

## Key Points
- Lee Kuan Yew warned of Pakistan-terror nexus as persistent threat requiring long-term strategic management.
- Effective counter-terrorism demands moderate Muslim scholars delegitimize extremist ideology from within Islam.
- Global coalition-building with China, Russia, India essential; isolated US action insufficient against jihadism.

## Open Question
How can India maintain strategic autonomy while part of a Western-led multilateral coalition against extremism, especially with China's competing interests in the region?

## India Angle
India faces direct security threats from Pakistan-backed terror networks and must navigate geopolitical realignment post-Afghanistan; also grapples with education disruption and tech regulation similar to China's tensions with Big Tech.
