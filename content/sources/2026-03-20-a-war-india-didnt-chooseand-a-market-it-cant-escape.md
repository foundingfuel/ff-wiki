## Core Argument
Interconnected global systems transmit distant geopolitical shocks into Indian portfolios through predictable, avoidable economic channels.

## Key Points
- Hormuz closure chokes 20% of global oil flow; each $10 crude rise shaves ~0.5% GDP growth for import-dependent India.
- Rupee depreciation silently erodes purchasing power for Indian households 90% invested in INR assets—fees, healthcare, travel become costlier.
- Multiple uncorrelated risks (AI, private credit, geopolitics) share same financial circuitry, amplifying systemic fragility beyond headline indices.

## Open Question
How can India protect itself from geopolitical shocks it cannot influence and didn't consent to participate in?

## India Angle
India's heavy crude oil import dependence (80%+) and rupee vulnerability expose it to geopolitical shocks it has no control over. A Gulf conflict triggers cascading effects: oil inflation, rupee depreciation, FII outflows, and portfolio losses for Indian households with 90%+ wealth in INR-denominated assets.
