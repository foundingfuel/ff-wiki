## Core Argument
Pressure is universal, non-linear, and mysterious—some collapse while others thrive, reshaping their life trajectory.

## Key Points
- Pressure affects everyone identically at surface level, but mental responses are highly variable and poorly understood.
- The risk-reward seesaw makes pressure simultaneously perilous and captivating; unpredictability at high stakes draws people in.
- A minority actively seek pressure moments while most avoid them; outcomes determine whether pressure crushes or elevates.

## Open Question
If pressure's mental effects are highly individual and cryptic, how can organizations systematically help people perform better under it rather than collapse?

## India Angle
Indian organizations and leaders navigating high-pressure environments—from startups to large enterprises—need frameworks to understand pressure's mental mechanics, not just external performance metrics. The cinema digitization story shows how Indian industries undergo technological transitions under pressure.
