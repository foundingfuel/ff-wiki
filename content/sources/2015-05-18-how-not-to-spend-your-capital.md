## Core Argument
Capital scarcity forces harder problem-solving; how you spend capital matters more than how much you raise.

## Key Points
- Growth without external funding is possible through internal cash accruals and disciplined cost management.
- Scarce capital drives innovative problem-solving; abundance can lead to inefficient spending decisions.
- Systematic questioning of every expense—'what if we had no money?'—reveals hidden efficiencies and non-essential costs.

## Open Question
Does scarcity-driven efficiency create sustainable competitive advantage, or does it limit speed-to-market when competitors with abundant capital move faster?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and scaling businesses face acute capital constraints; this argues for disciplined capital deployment and operational efficiency as competitive advantages in a resource-scarce environment.
