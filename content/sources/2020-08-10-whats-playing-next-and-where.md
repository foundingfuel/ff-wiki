## Core Argument
Entertainment habits reset permanently; content must compete harder as switching costs vanish in digital abundance.

## Key Points
- Streaming removes switching costs—viewers abandon films guilt-free, forcing content to work harder for attention
- Gaming democratized by smartphones dominates entertainment revenue; majority gamers span ages and genders, not just youth
- Social and multiplayer elements persist even in personal-device consumption; theatres survive by becoming curated social spaces

## Open Question
If entertainment is increasingly personal-device-based with low switching costs, can theatres as public spaces genuinely resurge, or do they become niche experiences for the affluent?

## India Angle
India's entertainment consumption is rapidly shifting post-Jio; affordable smartphones and cheap data are democratizing gaming and streaming, forcing theatres and content creators to compete in a new attention economy while still serving theatres as valuable public spaces.
