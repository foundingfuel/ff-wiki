## Core Argument
Layoffs signal ecosystem maturity through corrective feedback, not bubble burst, if startups learn to execute sustainably.

## Key Points
- Inflated valuations anchor unrealistic expectations across the entire startup ecosystem, creating unsustainable benchmarks.
- Young founders lack execution experience; VC-fueled discounts mask whether growth reflects real value or artificial demand.
- Layoffs may indicate healthy self-correction and feedback loops rather than systemic collapse, if companies learn.

## Open Question
Can a young, VC-funded ecosystem genuinely self-correct through painful feedback loops, or will systemic pressures eventually force a reckoning that destroys value indiscriminately?

## India Angle
Indian startups face a unique ecosystem challenge where inflated valuations (Flipkart, Snapdeal) create unrealistic benchmarks, inexperienced founders struggle with scaling, and VC-driven unit economics mask true business viability—questions about whether the Indian startup bubble will burst or self-correct.
