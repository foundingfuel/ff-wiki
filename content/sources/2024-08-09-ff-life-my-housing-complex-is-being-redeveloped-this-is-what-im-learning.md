## Core Argument
Housing redevelopment requires due diligence, transparency frameworks, and understanding developer incentives—not just optimism.

## Key Points
- Examining developer credentials and track records prevents costly delays and financial loss for residents.
- Corruption in permissions adds Rs 200-500/sqft costs; transparency mechanisms and regulatory pressure are changing developer behaviour.
- Residents must balance financial incentives, lifestyle upgrades, and long-term sinking fund arrangements carefully.

## Open Question
How can residents meaningfully evaluate developer credibility when information asymmetry is high and corruption remains embedded in the permission system?

## India Angle
Housing redevelopment in Indian cities like Mumbai involves navigating corruption, regulatory complexity, and coordination challenges—a deeply local problem affecting millions of urban residents seeking better living standards.
