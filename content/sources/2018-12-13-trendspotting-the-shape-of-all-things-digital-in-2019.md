## Core Argument
India's digital infrastructure shift enables rapid business iteration across untapped consumer segments at unprecedented scale.

## Key Points
- Telecom consolidation: Jio dominates, combining telecom, content, and commerce into integrated ecosystem threatening Amazon/Flipkart.
- TV viewership eroding as smartphones become primary entertainment; younger audiences shift to streaming over traditional broadcasts.
- 500M+ internet users and low-cost data create fertile ground for startup experimentation in categories previously unimaginable.

## Open Question
India's digital growth trajectory mirrors neither US nor China timelines, yet expectations remain anchored to historical precedent—how do policymakers and businesses build strategy for an economically unprecedented situation?

## India Angle
India's unprecedented convergence of cheap data, affordable smartphones, and 500M+ internet users creates a unique third-wave digital economy unlike anywhere globally—with implications for startups, telecom consolidation, media disruption, and retail competition.
