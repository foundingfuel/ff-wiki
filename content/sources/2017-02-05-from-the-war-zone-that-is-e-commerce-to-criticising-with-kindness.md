## Core Argument
Indian e-commerce faces defining moment as capital-driven scaling gives way to strategic repositioning and Chinese competition.

## Key Points
- Echo chambers and bias blind sides entrepreneurs; critical listening and discernment separate signal from noise.
- Indian e-commerce unicorns caught between scaling pressures and funding scarcity; 2017 marks transition from founder to investor-driven models.
- Chinese internet giants positioned to capitalize on cooled Indian market frenzy; Silicon Valley-China competition will reshape Indian internet landscape.

## Open Question
How can Indian entrepreneurs maintain learning agility and pattern recognition when operating in information-saturated, bias-prone environments dominated by competing narratives?
