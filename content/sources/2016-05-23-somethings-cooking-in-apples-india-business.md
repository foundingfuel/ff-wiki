## Core Argument
Apple must shift from hardware manufacturing to services strategy to navigate India's constraints and market saturation.

## Key Points
- India smartphone market dominated by price-sensitive consumers; only 3.4% buy premium devices, limiting Apple's addressable market.
- Indian government demands local manufacturing and bans refurbished phone sales, conflicting with Apple's global strategy and partner capabilities.
- Cook transitioning Apple from Jobs' deliberate hardware-focused strategy to emergent services-software model in uncharted territory.

## Open Question
How can Apple maintain premium pricing and experience-centric model while conforming to India's manufacturing mandates and competing against cheap local alternatives?

## India Angle
Apple faces unique India challenges: government pressure for local manufacturing, refurbished phone sales restrictions, and need to compete in price-sensitive market while maintaining premium positioning. India represents critical growth opportunity but requires business model adaptation.
