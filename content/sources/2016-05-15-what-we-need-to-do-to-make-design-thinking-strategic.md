## Core Argument
Design thinking becomes tactical without strategic vision; speed alone doesn't guarantee competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Design thinking's iterative loop mirrors OODA—fast execution wins tactics, not long-term strategy.
- Observation-driven problem definition is powerful but insufficient without strategic context and intent.
- Speed to market can mask short-sighted responses; true innovation requires game-changing vision beyond iteration.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain both the grounded, iterative insight of design thinking and the long-term strategic vision needed to avoid tactical dead-ends?

## India Angle
Indian companies rushing to adopt design thinking risk tactical fixes over strategic transformation; critical for scaling innovators and large organizations navigating rapid market changes.
