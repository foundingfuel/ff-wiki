## Core Argument
Brands must cede emotional ownership to customers who become prosumers, creating self-regulating, buzz-driven competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Smartphone connectivity enables customers to amplify opinions globally, forcing brands to embrace consumer co-creation.
- Prosumers—customers who produce and consume content—create higher-credibility reviews that self-regulate business quality.
- Effort justification (behavioural science) means customers value initiatives they invest energy in, even if imperfect output.

## Open Question
If 90% of customers remain passive (1:9:90 rule), can true emotional brand ownership be achieved, or does the strategy rely on a vocal minority whose views may not represent majority sentiment?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises, especially traditional FMCG brands like Pepsi, must recognize that Indian consumers—empowered by smartphones and social media—now expect emotional ownership of brands. This requires rethinking how companies engage with customers through platforms like IPL, not just broadcast marketing.
