## Core Argument
Life's work completion depends on aligning vocation with inner purpose, not external milestones or competitive validation.

## Key Points
- Arbitrary external markers obscure deeper questions about authentic purpose and what truly constitutes completion.
- Integration of vocation and avocation—doing work that feeds both body and soul—signals genuine achievement.
- Competitive achievement cycles never end; satisfaction emerges from intrinsic alignment, not world recognition.

## Open Question
How do we balance society's need for externally measurable contribution with an individual's need for internal spiritual and personal fulfillment?
