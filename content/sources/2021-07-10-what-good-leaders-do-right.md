## Core Argument
Good leaders combine self-confidence with humility, build diverse teams, and shape inclusive cultures intentionally.

## Key Points
- Self-confidence tempered with humility and openness to iteration drives better outcomes and impact.
- Surrounding yourself with differently-thinking people sharpens decision-making and challenges mental models.
- Leaders set culture from the top through conscious effort—open-door policies, transparency, and psychological safety.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance leveraging their impostor syndrome for better individual performance while ensuring their teams actually believe in them and feel confident working under their leadership?
