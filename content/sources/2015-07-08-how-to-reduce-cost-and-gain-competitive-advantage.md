## Core Argument
Outsource non-core costs aggressively; protect core competencies ruthlessly to sustain competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Reckless outsourcing of core activities erodes competitive advantage—Dell lost control of production to Asus.
- Separate 'core' costs (competitive advantage drivers) from 'non-core' costs; only outsource the latter.
- For core costs, reduce non-customer-facing expenses via offshoring, automation, alliances—never compromise perceived quality.

## Open Question
How do companies know which capabilities will remain strategically valuable 10+ years hence, given rapid technological disruption?

## India Angle
Indian manufacturers and IT services firms often face pressure to accept outsourcing work without building core capabilities—this framework helps them distinguish between sustaining competitiveness and pure cost arbitrage, critical as they compete globally.
