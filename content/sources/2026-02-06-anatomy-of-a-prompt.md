## Core Argument
Prompt quality determines AI output quality; vague intent yields statistically average, defensible mediocrity, not insight.

## Key Points
- Prompts compress human intent into machine signals; unclear intent causes models to default to statistically safe, bland middle ground.
- Good prompts require discipline across four structural layers: role, task, audience, output—each shapes thinking itself.
- Principles (rigor, scepticism, originality) must be explicit; without them, models optimize for acceptability over accuracy or depth.

## Open Question
If AI systems are fundamentally predictive engines optimizing for statistical acceptability, can prompt engineering ever escape the median—or does disciplined prompting merely make mediocrity more efficient?

## India Angle
Indian organizations adopting AI must recognize that prompt design is a critical capability—poorly designed prompts lead to mediocre outputs that sound confident but lack insight, a particular risk for Indian enterprises scaling AI without investing in prompt discipline and intent-clarity frameworks.
