## Core Argument
Economics' reductionist methods fail climate and social challenges; cooperation and deliberation must replace competition-based frameworks.

## Key Points
- Arrow's Impossibility Theorem proves voting alone cannot convert diverse preferences into unified policy without prior deliberation.
- Over-simplifying human identities and values into game-theory players strips away history, context, and what makes solutions work in reality.
- Money's precision hides unmeasurable values like justice and happiness that citizens actually demand from policymakers.

## Open Question
If deliberation is essential before voting on complex issues, how do democracies actually decide on urgent crises like climate change without becoming paralyzed?

## India Angle
India's policymakers must move beyond GDP-obsessed growth models toward inclusive, cooperative frameworks that account for diverse citizen values and nature-based limits—critical for climate action and social cohesion in a multi-lingual, multi-value society.
