## Core Argument
Annual reviews are being replaced by real-time tracking or social rating systems, each with distinct risks.

## Key Points
- Companies replacing annual reviews with minute-by-minute tracking via wearables and Big Data analytics
- Social evaluation systems mirror e-commerce ratings but risk groupthink, bias, and stifling diversity
- Measurement-heavy systems create perverse incentives (quota culture) rather than genuine performance improvement

## Open Question
How can organizations measure employee performance objectively without either surveillance dystopia or social conformity pressure overriding actual merit?
