## Core Argument
Nations prosper through relentless innovation ecosystems, not military conquest or cheap labor alone.

## Key Points
- Taiwan's ITRI enabled Giant to dominate bicycles via carbon-fiber innovation, not cost competition.
- US strategic blunders in Afghanistan reveal limits of technocratic power; empires fail when they ignore local context.
- e-RUPI digital vouchers can transform Indian social policy if backed by institutional coordination like Taiwan's model.

## Open Question
How can India build institutional capacity like Taiwan's ITRI when governance fragmentation and short-term political cycles undermine sustained R&D investment?

## India Angle
India faces a critical choice: emulate Taiwan's public-private R&D model to move beyond low-cost manufacturing, or risk becoming a permanent 'graveyard' of unrealized potential. e-RUPI's potential shows India can innovate in fintech and social policy, but only with institutional backbone like ITRI.
