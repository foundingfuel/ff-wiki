## Core Argument
Abstract corporate values fail unless translated into lived stories and concrete behaviors across organizations.

## Key Points
- Generic values like 'integrity' and 'customer-centricity' are table stakes, not differentiators—move them to codes of conduct.
- Employees assign conflicting meanings to abstract values; shared understanding requires story listening and narrative inquiry.
- Stories of values being lived in real moments create conviction and embedding far better than workshops or plaques.

## Open Question
How do you scale story-based values embedding across geographically dispersed, hierarchically diverse organizations without it becoming performative?

## India Angle
Indian companies often adopt Western corporate values uncritically; this article addresses how to make values culturally resonant and actionable across hierarchies—critical for large Indian conglomerates managing diverse workforces.
