## Core Argument
Excellence requires relentless learning, integrity under pressure, and refusing to compromise values for opportunity.

## Key Points
- Ray's obsessive learning—watching 100+ films, pre-writing screenplays before viewing—was foundational to mastery
- He declined Cannes jury invite rather than accept second-class treatment, prioritizing dignity over prestige
- Talent and hard work insufficient; navigating systemic prejudice while maintaining principles defined his legacy

## Open Question
How do emerging talent and institutions balance global ambition with refusal to accept diminishing treatment, especially when rejecting opportunities carries real costs?

## India Angle
Ray's centenary celebrates India's most globally celebrated filmmaker who navigated international prejudice while maintaining integrity; relevant to how Indian creators/leaders should approach global recognition without compromising principles
