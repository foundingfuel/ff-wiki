## Core Argument
Political rhetoric can captivate audiences despite lacking substantive logic, masking empty argumentation with performance.

## Key Points
- Kanhaiya Kumar's carefully staged persona—outsider, humble dress, broken English plea—manipulated audience sympathy to exceed speaking time.
- His 12-minute monologue deployed rhetoric over logic, making emotional appeals without substantive argument on nationalism.
- Intelligent political actors succeed through persuasion mastery, not intellectual depth, reshaping how audiences perceive ideas.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between genuine conviction and calculated political performance when both employ similar emotional and cultural signaling tactics?

## India Angle
Examines how Aadhaar—a flagship Indian identity system—is debated among policymakers and intellectuals; also observes political rhetoric and persuasion tactics within Indian public discourse and their implications for governance.
