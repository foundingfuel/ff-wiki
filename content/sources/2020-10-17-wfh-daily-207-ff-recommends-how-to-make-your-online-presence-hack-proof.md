## Core Argument
Personal cybersecurity requires disciplined protocols; most people neglect basic defenses despite constant breach risks.

## Key Points
- Data breaches are routine; compromised credentials enable identity theft, financial fraud, and account takeover globally.
- Two-factor authentication, regular password changes, and screen locks are foundational but widely ignored defenses.
- Public Wi-Fi, unsecured devices, and cloud backups pose ongoing risks requiring conscious behavioral change.

## Open Question
How do individuals balance necessary digital convenience (cloud backups, public networks, assistants) with the friction required for genuine security?

## India Angle
Indian remote workers and WFH professionals face heightened cybersecurity risks with limited digital literacy; data breaches affecting Indian users are common, making practical security protocols essential for protecting financial and personal information.
