## Core Argument
Acknowledging fear and uncertainty, rather than ignoring them, is the first step to building resilience.

## Key Points
- Fear is an emotion we're not trained to acknowledge, leading us to ignore or deny it during crises.
- Humans are evolutionarily primed to adapt when challenges are identified; we navigate uncertainty daily without realizing it.
- Embracing uncertainty without demanding guarantees—accepting 'there's no ground'—requires equanimity but enables resilience.

## Open Question
How can individuals practically move from intellectually understanding uncertainty to emotionally accepting it without external validation or control?
