## Core Argument
Slow, meditative reading of timeless wisdom passages counters modern speed culture and builds concentration, peace, and purpose.

## Key Points
- Speed culture dominates modern life: 10-minute groceries, 2X podcasts, bullet-point news consumption.
- Passage Meditation—slow, word-by-word reading of inspirational texts—systematically builds concentration and inner peace.
- Regular meditative practice prioritized daily generates resources to handle personal anxieties and improve judgment.

## Open Question
How can individuals sustain a daily 30-minute meditation practice in a system designed to maximize speed and efficiency, and what structural changes are needed to make slowness culturally rewarded rather than penalized?

## India Angle
Indian spiritual traditions (Rumi, Lao Tzu, Saint Francis) offer counterbalance to modern India's adoption of speed culture; relevant for knowledge workers and leaders seeking sustainable personal practices amid rapid business cycles.
