## Core Argument
Without mediator leverage and mutual interests, Tata-Mistry conflict will persist like Ambani feud before matriarch intervention.

## Key Points
- Mediation requires mutuality of interests, ecosystem pressure, and a credible mediator with sufficient stature and leverage.
- Institutional investors and regulators lack real threat perception; public campaigns alone cannot sustain pressure indefinitely.
- Family patriarchs hardening stances, loyalists executing orders—mirrors Ambani conflict but lacks a higher authority to broker peace.

## Open Question
If mediation becomes viable, can a patriarch who seeks complete exit of the other party ever genuinely apologize and create the mutual ground necessary for settlement?

## India Angle
India's largest conglomerates lack established conflict resolution mechanisms; institutional investors and regulators remain poorly positioned to influence outcomes in family-controlled groups, exposing governance gaps unique to Indian corporate structure.
