## Core Argument
Quality conversations convert experiences into learning; they are the root of personal and organizational change.

## Key Points
- Reflection transforms random happenings into meaningful experiences; conversations enable this synthesis.
- Chasing proxies (titles, status, wealth) masks the real work of continuous learning and unlearning.
- Diverse perspectives in conversation pop filter bubbles and unlock growth unavailable in isolation.

## Open Question
How do we build cultures of reflective conversation in fast-paced, results-driven Indian organizations obsessed with visible markers of success?

## India Angle
For Indian leaders navigating rapid organizational change and scaling, conversation as a core capability is critical—yet often neglected in hierarchical business cultures where reflection and peer learning are undervalued.
