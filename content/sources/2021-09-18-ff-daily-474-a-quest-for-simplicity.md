## Core Argument
Simplicity and intrinsic values, not job type or money, drive sustainable work satisfaction and wellbeing.

## Key Points
- Technology promised simplification but increased complexity, loneliness, and cognitive overload in pre-pandemic world.
- Job satisfaction stems from finding accomplishment and intrinsic goals within any role, not job title or salary.
- GDPR-style regulations strengthen Big Tech dominance by creating compliance barriers that hurt smaller, local competitors.

## Open Question
How can individuals genuinely simplify lives when digital complexity is now structurally necessary for work, learning, and human connection post-pandemic?

## India Angle
Indian professionals increasingly navigate complex tech-driven workplaces while seeking work-life balance; GDPR-style regulations could reshape how Indian tech companies compete globally; cultural values around simplicity and intrinsic meaning in work resonate deeply in Indian context.
