## Core Argument
Indian millennials have unique potential but face structural constraints from family expectations and educational systems.

## Key Points
- Indian millennials are constrained by parental ROI expectations and lack of learning agility compared to Western peers.
- Smart companies align organizational culture, processes, and digital platforms with millennial values for engagement.
- Education system failures prevent Indian millennials from applying learning and leveraging innate digital capabilities.

## Open Question
Can Indian millennials' digital potential and entrepreneurial spirit overcome entrenched family expectations and educational system rigidity that constrains their learning agility and application of knowledge?

## India Angle
Indian millennials differ significantly from Western counterparts due to parental expectations, limited internet penetration beyond urban middle class, and an education system not yet aligned with their digital strengths. Understanding this nuance is critical for India to leverage its demographic dividend.
