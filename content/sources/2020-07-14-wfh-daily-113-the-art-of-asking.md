## Core Argument
Strategic asking and counterintuitive people investments build sustainable competitive advantage during crises.

## Key Points
- Direct asking often succeeds—Dyson's willingness to share personal letters when simply asked.
- Salary increases during downturns retain talent and build loyalty when competitors cut costs.
- Non-linear disruption: digital ad value collapsed 100x, signaling need for fundamental business model rethinking, not scaling assumptions.

## Open Question
Does paying premium salaries during economic contraction protect talent long-term, or merely defer harder restructuring decisions that weaker competitors will face?

## India Angle
Indian companies like Wipro, ICICI Bank, and Cap Gemini India are navigating post-pandemic talent retention through salary increases—a strategic move relevant to India's competitive talent market and growing worker expectations.
