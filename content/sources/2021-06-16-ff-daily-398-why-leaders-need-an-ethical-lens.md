## Core Argument
Leaders must adopt ethical lens beyond legal compliance; societal values evolve faster than laws.

## Key Points
- Laws lag behind society's evolving ethical expectations; legal compliance is necessary but insufficient.
- Ethical breaches damage reputation overnight, as Nikhil Kamath's cheating incident showed in startup ecosystem.
- Leaders must proactively sense emerging societal trends before they become media crises forcing defensive responses.

## Open Question
How can leaders identify and respond to emerging ethical norms before they crystallize into public outrage, when these norms are often invisible at the corporate level until too late?

## India Angle
Indian startup leaders like Nikhil Kamath face heightened ethical scrutiny from society and media; ethical lapses damage brand reputation and founder credibility in ways legal compliance alone cannot protect. India's evolving societal values now demand leaders operate beyond legal boundaries.
