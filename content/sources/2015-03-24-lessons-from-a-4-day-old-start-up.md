## Core Argument
Success breeds arrogance; failure teaches humility necessary to build something meaningful from scratch.

## Key Points
- Embrace setbacks as redirection—being fired led to Founding Fuel's creation rather than resentment.
- Ship imperfect MVPs early; iterate based on real user feedback rather than pursuing perfection in isolation.
- Sacrifice comfort and unlearn corporate habits; lean operations demand willingness to give up perks and status.

## Open Question
How can founders maintain hunger and stay foolish when previous success has conditioned them to expect validation and comfort?

Indian media entrepreneurship: founding a business publication in India's competitive media landscape; navigating from corporate comfort to bootstrap startup lean operations.
