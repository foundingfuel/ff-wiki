## Core Argument
Indian ecommerce needs a Nirma moment: affordable mass-market solutions beyond smartphones and clothes.

## Key Points
- Internet content consumption ≠ internet retail consumption; mobile replaced TV but hasn't displaced offline for home goods.
- Festival sales driven by cheap smartphones, discounted electronics, and excess inventory, not genuine demand expansion.
- Investors idle capital seeking ventures beyond saturated urban ecommerce narratives; untapped opportunity in mass-market household affordability.

## Open Question
How can entrepreneurs create mass-market affordable solutions for household goods when ecommerce currently succeeds only in high-margin, impulse categories like smartphones and fashion?

## India Angle
Indian ecommerce has matured into a smartphones-and-apparel play but lacks the 'Nirma moment'—affordable, mass-market solutions for everyday household needs that offline retail still dominates. Investors see saturation in urban-centric models and a gap between internet consumption and retail conversion.
