## Core Argument
Genius is learnable through curiosity and observation, not innate; apply this to WFH productivity challenges.

## Key Points
- Leonardo's genius came from cultivated skills like curiosity and observation, not supernatural talent—applicable to modern work culture
- Companies that maintain or increase marketing spend during downturns recover strongest, requiring strategic reallocation not blanket cuts
- WFH enables easier relationship-building with clients outside normal work discussions, creating new service opportunities

## Open Question
How do organizations nurture deep colleague relationships and mentorship in WFH settings when physical proximity traditionally enabled this?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and companies adopting WFH face similar challenges around relationship-building, infrastructure strain, and maintaining competitive positioning during economic downturns—particularly relevant as Indian firms navigate post-pandemic work models.
