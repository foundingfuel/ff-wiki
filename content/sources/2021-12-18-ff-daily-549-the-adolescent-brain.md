## Core Argument
Adolescent brain pruning is evolutionary optimization; understanding this rewires how adults should parent and lead young people.

## Key Points
- Adolescent brains undergo neural pruning—shedding inefficient neurons—not sign of immaturity but optimization.
- Frontal cortex maturation extends into mid-twenties; patience during this window correlates with higher adult IQ.
- Neural Darwinism principle: programmed overproduction followed by competitive pruning creates optimized circuits.

## Open Question
If pruning is necessary for optimization, how much environmental pressure or stress during adolescence helps versus harms the selection process?

## India Angle
Indian parents and educators managing adolescent behavior during extended lockdowns need neuroscience-based empathy; applies to Indian schools reopening and parent-child dynamics in Indian households.
