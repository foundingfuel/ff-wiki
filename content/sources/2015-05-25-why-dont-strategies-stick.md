## Core Argument
Strategy fails to stick because of information asymmetry; narratives make abstract strategy concrete and retellable.

## Key Points
- Only 5% of workforce understands company strategy despite engaging presentations; curse of knowledge creates disconnect.
- Clarity stories use consistent narrative structure (purpose, action, turning point, future vision) to make strategy relatable.
- Stories enable visualization, relatability, and retelling—critical for scaling strategy comprehension across hierarchies.

## Open Question
How do organisations ensure story consistency and authentic retelling at scale without over-scripting and losing the emotional resonance that makes stories work?

## India Angle
Indian organisations often rely on hierarchical top-down strategy communication; the curse of knowledge is particularly acute in cross-functional teams across geographies. Narrative-driven strategies can help bridge understanding gaps in diverse, multi-level organisations.
