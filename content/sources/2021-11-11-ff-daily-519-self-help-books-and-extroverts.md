## Core Argument
Extrovert ideal dominates modern work culture, masking introverts' genuine strengths and reshaping personality over character.

## Key Points
- Dale Carnegie's rise reflected cultural shift from character-based to personality-driven hiring and advancement criteria.
- Self-help tradition normalized the belief that salesmanship and social fluency are universal professional requirements.
- Open vs. closed systems thinking reveals life success requires adaptive, collaborative skills beyond competitive game-winning.

## Open Question
If introverts possess genuine strengths and strategic value, why does organizational culture continue to systemically reward extroversion despite evidence of its limitations?

## India Angle
India's corporate culture increasingly mirrors Western extrovert ideals through multinational influence, creating barriers for introverted talent and leadership. Self-help and personality-based assessment culture is rapidly growing in Indian business education and HR practices.
