## Core Argument
Technology reflects engineers' values; leaders must embed empathy and ethics into culture and systems.

## Key Points
- Tech is not neutral—it embeds the values and biases of those who build it.
- Leaders define themselves by how they respond to mistakes, not the mistakes themselves.
- Building inclusive AI requires both 'fuzzies' (humanities) and 'techies' collaborating on design.

## Open Question
How can organizations embed empathy and ethics into tech design when scale and speed incentivize shortcuts, and how do you prevent conversational AI from inheriting societal biases despite good intentions?
