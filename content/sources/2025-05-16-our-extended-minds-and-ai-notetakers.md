## Core Argument
AI notetakers extend human cognition, enabling better focus, inclusivity, and institutional memory in modern work.

## Key Points
- Extended mind theory: tools like AI recorders become integral to cognitive processes, not external aids.
- Recording reduces cognitive load during meetings, freeing mental space for genuine engagement and quality contributions.
- Transcripts democratize information within teams by making decisions and details independently verifiable, reducing bias and dependency on individual memory.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the accountability benefits of recording conversations with the need for psychological safety and creative risk-taking in brainstorming sessions?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and journalists adopting AI notetakers to enhance productivity and inclusivity in distributed teams; relevant for India's growing remote work culture and need to democratize information access across organizations.
