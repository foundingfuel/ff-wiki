## Core Argument
Organisational purpose—not hierarchy or incentives—is the essential glue binding high-performing teams and societies.

## Key Points
- Purpose transcends command-and-control: peer-driven models like DaanUtsav mobilise free agents through shared belief, not salary.
- Corporate leaders conflate career progression with life meaning; true purpose energises across ranks and sectors.
- Great organisations reinforce purpose repeatedly—Medtronic invites patients to annual meetings, keeping mission alive.

## Open Question
How do you sustain passionate volunteer engagement and purpose-driven culture as movements scale, without formalising into bureaucratic structures that destroy the very thing that made them powerful?

## India Angle
Indian organisations—both corporate and non-profit—struggle to articulate and embed higher purpose. DaanUtsav demonstrates how peer-driven, volunteer-led models can scale without traditional hierarchies, offering lessons for Indian businesses seeking meaning beyond profit.
