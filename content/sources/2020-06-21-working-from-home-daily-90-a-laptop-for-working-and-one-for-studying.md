## Core Argument
Self-awareness and neighbourhood responsibility are essential for thriving during pandemic-driven work transformations.

## Key Points
- Emotional intelligence—awareness of one's feelings—distinguishes being swept by emotions from mastering them.
- Practical WFH setup requires right tools: Dell Vostro (₹50-60k) for professionals, Asus Vivobook (₹30k) for students.
- Pandemic isolation has fractured community bonds; Gandhi's Swadharma asks us to reclaim neighbourhood responsibility.

## Open Question
How do we balance necessary physical isolation for pandemic safety with Gandhi's call for active neighbourhood engagement and community responsibility?
