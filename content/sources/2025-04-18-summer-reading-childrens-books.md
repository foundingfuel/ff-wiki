## Core Argument
Children's books create transformative spaces where adults rediscover themselves through their children's reading journeys.

## Key Points
- Children's literature is complex art form engaging adult writer, child reader, and adult re-reader simultaneously
- Indian publishers (Tara, Tulika) and creators (Ravishankar, Eriyat) producing world-class children's books with distinct cultural narratives
- Summer reading offers both escape and mirror—children find characters that reflect their struggles and aspirations

## Open Question
How do children today find resonance with classic characters when overwhelmed by infinite digital options, and does algorithmic curation replicate the serendipitous discovery of lending libraries?

## India Angle
Highlights Indian children's publishing renaissance (Tara Books, Tulika Books, Studio Eeksaurus) and how contemporary Indian authors are creating globally competitive literature; contrasts '90s Indian reading landscape with today's digital abundance for Indian children.
