## Core Argument
Global trade is rewiring around geopolitical lines; tariffs mask deeper structural realignment toward Asia and strategic autonomy.

## Key Points
- Tariff uncertainty is driving contingency planning, not retreat; major powers hedge through investment and regional agreements.
- Trade centre of gravity shifting from transatlantic axis to Eurasia; Asia-Europe trade now dominates global commerce.
- Economic-security concerns reshaping supply chains via 'China+1', friend-shoring strategies; strategic autonomy increasingly trumps market access.

## Open Question
How can countries pursue genuine strategic autonomy while remaining tightly integrated in global supply chains where moving away from China is structurally difficult?

## India Angle
India is quietly deferring trade negotiations with the US while strengthening Indo-Pacific partnerships and maintaining Russia ties—balancing market access against geopolitical risk in a realigned global order where strategic autonomy increasingly shapes commercial decisions.
