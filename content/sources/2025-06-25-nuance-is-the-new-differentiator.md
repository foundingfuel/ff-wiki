## Core Argument
Data alone misses behaviour rooted in cultural context; AI amplifies this gap unless paired with human intuition.

## Key Points
- COD preference reflects offline shopping logic (inspect, verify, then pay) rather than distrust of digital systems.
- AI can surface linguistic and cultural patterns at scale but risks treating summaries as complete understanding.
- Meaningful insights live in silence, hesitation, and unspoken context—cues that don't survive data compression.

## Open Question
How do we leverage AI's capacity to process scale and speed without losing the tacit, non-verbal dimensions of human behaviour that matter most in building for India?

## India Angle
Indian consumer behaviour is shaped by offline transaction patterns (store shopping, bus ticket buying) that transfer to digital contexts; AI-driven insights risk missing these cultural subtleties unless paired with human intuition and contextual understanding.
