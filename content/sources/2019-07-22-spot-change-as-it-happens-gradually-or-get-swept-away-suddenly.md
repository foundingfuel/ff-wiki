## Core Argument
Leaders who spot gradual change through ground immersion, rigorous reflection, and external perspectives avoid sudden disruption.

## Key Points
- Prescient leaders train their eye through ground-level immersion, observation, and curiosity rather than office-bound decision-making.
- Rigorous reflection with mentors and thought leaders weaves small shifts into grand insights; this must be embedded, not optional.
- Pursuits outside work refresh perspective, enabling leaders to see patterns more clearly when they return to their core work.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the need for constant ground-level observation and rigorous reflection with the operational demands of running organizations?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations must develop the capability to spot gradual shifts (like TikTok's rise, automation's impact on women) before they become disruptive crises, essential given India's rapidly evolving business landscape and regulatory environment.
