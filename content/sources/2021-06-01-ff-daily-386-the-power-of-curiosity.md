## Core Argument
Relentless questioning and follow-up are foundational to avoiding organizational and strategic disasters.

## Key Points
- Children ask fearlessly; adults suppress questions from fear of appearing foolish, with potentially catastrophic outcomes
- Knowing what you don't know (unknown unknowns) requires deliberate intellectual humility and persistent curiosity
- Questions must be articulated AND followed up on; mere awareness of gaps without action wastes intelligence

## Open Question
How do you institutionalize relentless questioning in hierarchical cultures where speaking up still carries social risk?

## India Angle
Indian professionals often default to hierarchical deference over questioning; building a culture of fearless inquiry is critical for innovation and decision-making in Indian enterprises and institutions.
