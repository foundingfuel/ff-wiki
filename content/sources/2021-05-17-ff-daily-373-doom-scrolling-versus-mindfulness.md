## Core Argument
Mindfulness and institutional purpose are antidotes to digital noise and societal drift in India.

## Key Points
- Doom scrolling traps us in past regrets or future anxiety; mindfulness frees us to be present.
- Indian media industry must rethink business models as digital transformation accelerates consumer behaviour shifts.
- Institutions like TIFR succeed through visionary leadership grounded in clarity of purpose, not just adaptation.

## Open Question
How can institutions and individuals maintain purposeful focus and reflection in an accelerating digital ecosystem that incentivizes distraction and fragmentation?

## India Angle
Indian media and institutions face particular challenges in maintaining quality discourse and purpose amid digital disruption; need for mindful leadership in building institutions like TIFR that endure.
