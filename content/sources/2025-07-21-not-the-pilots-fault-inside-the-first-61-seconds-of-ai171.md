## Core Argument
AI171 crash likely caused by undetected avionics failure, not pilot error, weeks after FAA warned of fuel system risks.

## Key Points
- Ram Air Turbine deployed seconds after takeoff suggests systems failure before aircraft left ground.
- No alerting system warned pilots of dual engine failure, indicating possible electrical or avionics malfunction.
- Boeing 787 fuel shutoff valves have known failure modes; Air India skipped recommended 2018 FAA locking mechanism inspection.

## Open Question
If software can autonomously shut off fuel without pilot intervention, how can airlines and regulators ensure such failures are detectable and preventable before catastrophic consequences?

## India Angle
Air India crash investigation reveals potential systemic failures in Boeing 787 maintenance and oversight in India; raises questions about Indian aviation regulator compliance with FAA directives and aircraft certification standards.
