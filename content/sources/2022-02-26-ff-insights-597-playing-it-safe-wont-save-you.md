## Core Argument
Great leaders make continuous small bets regardless of conditions; waiting for perfect timing guarantees failure.

## Key Points
- Resilient companies embrace small failures as signals they're taking sufficient risks, not avoiding them.
- Starting innovation early is non-negotiable; money cannot compress what time and iteration have already built.
- Prudence without progress kills companies; leaders must balance frugality in bets with boldness in scaling winners.

## Open Question
How do leaders know when a bet is 'small enough' to mitigate risk while remaining meaningful enough to yield learning?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders often default to risk-averse behavior during economic uncertainty; building a culture that tolerates small bets—even in downturns—is critical for long-term competitiveness in a fast-changing market.
