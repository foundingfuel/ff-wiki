## Core Argument
Bihar elections reveal how governance quality and crisis response matter less than personality cults and caste equations in Indian politics.

## Key Points
- Bihar's electoral outcomes directly shape national politics through 40 Lok Sabha seats, making state governance a national concern.
- Voters prioritize caste equations and leader personalities over governance quality or crisis response like pandemics and labour migration.
- Nitish Kumar's political survival depends on alliance-building rather than independent strength, limiting his transformative capacity.

## Open Question
If governance quality doesn't influence voters and caste equations dominate electoral outcomes, how can state capacity meaningfully improve in Bihar or similar contexts?

## India Angle
Bihar's 40 parliamentary seats make state elections pivotal to national politics; reflects broader patterns of caste politics, personality-driven governance, and institutional capacity challenges endemic to Indian electoral systems.
