## Core Argument
Platform networks redistribute power from intermediaries to informed edge nodes, reshaping industries and discourse.

## Key Points
- Airbnb's distributed network of local hosts mimics edge computing—processing intelligence at source rather than centre.
- Social media amplifies fringe voices into mainstream debates, influencing individual choices beyond rational evaluation.
- Tech giants concentrate power despite democratization rhetoric, requiring regulatory intervention.

## Open Question
If platforms democratize by empowering edge nodes, why do they simultaneously concentrate power in the hands of platform owners?

## India Angle
Indian travel and hospitality sectors face platform disruption (Airbnb, OTAs) similar to global markets; social media's influence on Indian public discourse and decision-making mirrors global trends but with unique cultural/political dynamics.
