## Core Argument
Pandemic-driven work changes reshape Indian automotive ownership decisions and maintenance priorities.

## Key Points
- Stationary cars require weekly maintenance routines: disengage hand brakes, idle for 8-10 minutes, drive 1-2 km to preserve battery and oil.
- Prioritize interior cleanliness and hygiene over exterior; service centres neglect thorough internal cleaning.
- Buy new cars early to avoid wait times; consider ride-sharing alternatives if conflict exists about ownership necessity.

## Open Question
Should Indian consumers reconsider car ownership as essential when ride-sharing, motorcycles, and WFH blur traditional transportation needs—or is vehicle ownership culturally necessary despite practical arguments against it?
