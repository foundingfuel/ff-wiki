## Core Argument
Rashid Khan's genius lay in mastering classical form while selectively innovating within tradition.

## Key Points
- Child prodigy trained in Rampur-Sahaswan tradition, later synthesized Kirana style influences
- Maintained classicist purity despite lucrative film offers, choosing concert circuit over cinema
- Institutional support via ITC Sangeet Research Academy enabled access to masters and diverse gurus

## Open Question
How can classical Indian music maintain artistic purity while ensuring economic sustainability and audience growth in contemporary India?

## India Angle
Rashid Khan represents the decline of classical Hindustani music patronage in India; his life reflects challenges facing Indian classical artists in balancing tradition with commercial pressures and institutional support.
