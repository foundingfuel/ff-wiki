## Core Argument
Leaders must learn compartmentalisation and focus to manage multiple crises while adapting to distributed work models.

## Key Points
- Compartmentalisation—managing multiple crises simultaneously without losing focus—is learnable through deliberate training like West Point methods.
- Indian managers resisting WFH need new skillsets to measure outcomes rather than presence; HR must support this transition.
- Treating people with dignity during crises requires proportional enforcement and transparency, not humiliation or authoritarian approaches.

## Open Question
How can leaders simultaneously build the psychological resilience to compartmentalise crises while creating supportive, dignity-preserving workplace cultures that don't default to old command-and-control methods?

## India Angle
Indian managers need new skillsets to assess performance outside office settings; Indian leadership must balance pandemic enforcement with human dignity and proportionality
