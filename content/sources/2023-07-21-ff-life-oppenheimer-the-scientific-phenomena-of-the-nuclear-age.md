## Core Argument
Oppenheimer's story reveals how scientific breakthroughs force reckoning with unintended moral consequences.

## Key Points
- Oppenheimer and Einstein shared conviction that nuclear weapons required urgent ethical consideration despite personal disagreements.
- Fermi estimation teaches problem-solving through breaking complexity into manageable pieces and accepting productive failure.
- Art (film, literature) illuminates the human cost of technological power in ways biography and physics alone cannot.

## Open Question
How do we honor scientific genius while reckoning with its destructive applications—and who bears moral responsibility for consequences scientists cannot control?

## India Angle
India's own nuclear weapons program and deterrence doctrine make understanding Oppenheimer's legacy and the moral complexities of nuclear science directly relevant to Indian policymakers, scientists, and citizens navigating nuclear responsibility.
