## Core Argument
Technology addiction hijacks dopamine reward systems identically to drug addiction, demanding deliberate behavioral change.

## Key Points
- Internet engagement triggers dopamine loops identical to cocaine/heroin, creating compulsion without satiety.
- Backlit screens suppress melatonin by 22%, fragmenting sleep and compounding addiction vulnerability.
- Disconnecting reveals compensatory behaviors—anxiety, junk food, smoking—when dopamine sources are denied.

## Open Question
If technology addiction is neurologically equivalent to drug addiction, are individual willpower experiments (like digital sabbaths) sufficient, or do we need systemic/policy interventions around device design and workplace norms?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and adolescents face rising technology addiction with limited clinical awareness; IPH's work in Mumbai highlights a gap in workplace wellness and mental health frameworks for Indian professionals.
