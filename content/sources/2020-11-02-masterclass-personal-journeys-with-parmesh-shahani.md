## Core Argument
Refusing to conform and investing in self-knowledge enables leaders to bridge silos and drive systemic change.

## Key Points
- Self-acceptance and refusal to hide differences paradoxically becomes a source of influence and credibility.
- Mentorship validating your unique perspective matters more than conforming to institutional norms.
- Converting frustration about privilege gaps into actionable connection and platform-building drives real impact.

## Open Question
How do you scale impact on marginalised communities (trans people on streets) while operating within privileged institutional spaces without becoming complicit in the very system that marginalises them?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations must grapple with inclusion, particularly LGBTQ+ rights at work, and create spaces where diverse voices thrive—critical for India's emergence as a progressive economy.
