## Core Argument
Tech MNCs innovating for India's 6 billion-person market create products that succeed globally.

## Key Points
- GE's MAC 400 ECG solved India's affordability and infrastructure constraints, then sold worldwide.
- India R&D centres now design for domestic market needs first, generating competitive advantages elsewhere.
- Reverse innovation model: emerging-market solutions (cash payments, offline video, low bandwidth) become globally relevant.

## Open Question
How can Indian startups compete with global MNCs innovating for India's market if multinationals have deeper resources and global distribution channels?

## India Angle
India as both a talent hub and a 6-billion-person market opportunity; MNCs must innovate for Indian constraints (power, cost, connectivity) which creates reverse innovation potential for global markets.
