## Core Argument
Leading Gen Y requires redefining success metrics beyond traditional hierarchies and combating information overload through purposeful engagement.

## Key Points
- Gen Y employees demand meaning and purpose, not just compensation or titles
- Information overload paralyzes decision-making; leaders must filter and contextualize data
- Engagement requires dialogue and empowerment, not top-down directives

## Open Question
How do leaders maintain organizational coherence and accountability when empowering Gen Y workers who demand autonomy and purpose alongside traditional corporate structures?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs managing Gen Y workforces in rapidly growing sectors must balance traditional command structures with purpose-driven engagement—a distinctly Indian organizational challenge as youth demographics shift workplace expectations.
