## Core Argument
Honest disclosure of mental health conditions enables empathy, transforms workplace relationships, and unlocks professional potential.

## Key Points
- Author's bipolar diagnosis explained performance lapses to boss Seema, shifting frustration into understanding and support.
- Transparency about mental illness allowed manager to adapt expectations and behavior, strengthening professional collaboration.
- Open conversation became foundation for decade-long creative partnership that produced published book and established new editorial niche.

## Open Question
How do high-performance work cultures balance productivity expectations with accommodation for mental health conditions without creating stigma or lowered standards?
