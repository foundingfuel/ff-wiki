## Core Argument
Large corporations can unlock value in fragmented industries by applying cross-sector expertise and platform models.

## Key Points
- Haier used manufacturing and Internet technologies to cut egg farm setup time from 3 years to 270 days
- Platform approach connects small producers with modern tech, equipment providers, and financing—solving fragmentation
- Cross-industry knowledge transfer (manufacturing TQM to agriculture) drives efficiency gains benefiting all stakeholders

## Open Question
Can large conglomerates genuinely empower small producers as equal partners, or does platform control ultimately concentrate power and margins in their hands?

## India Angle
Indian agriculture and food production are dominated by small, unorganized players like China's egg industry. Haier's platform model—applying manufacturing best practices to fragmented sectors—offers a blueprint for Indian conglomerates to create value in agritech, food processing, and rural supply chains while solving efficiency and quality problems.
