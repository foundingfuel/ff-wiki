## Core Argument
Self-directed learning through books and peer dialogue rivals formal education; transparency in AI protects high-stakes decision-making.

## Key Points
- Self-education via structured reading, critical analysis, and peer discussion can replace expensive formal credentials for non-academic paths
- AI transparency prevents misuse in high-stakes situations and expresses respect for human decision-making authority
- Urban Indian teenage girls face rising mental health crises driven by social media comparison, while parents remain unaware of scope and solutions

## Open Question
If self-directed learning is equally effective, why do people still pursue expensive formal degrees—is it credentialing, structure, or community that truly matters?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating mid-career breaks and continuous learning need alternative models beyond formal education; urgent for urban Indian parents to understand teenage mental health crises exacerbated by social media comparison culture.
