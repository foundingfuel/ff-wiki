## Core Argument
Organizations must encourage experimentation and mistake-making to unlock creativity and navigate post-pandemic uncertainty.

## Key Points
- Kids naturally take chances; adults are educated out of creativity by systems that stigmatize mistakes.
- Post-COVID recovery is uneven and unpredictable; companies must focus capital on long-term opportunities beyond current noise.
- Inclusive workplace policies (like LGBTQ support) signal progressiveness and attract talent across India.

## Open Question
How can companies create cultures that tolerate productive failure while maintaining accountability and operational standards?

## India Angle
Indian companies must unlearn the fear of failure to compete globally; data shows formal sector recovery depends on understanding uncertainty; LGBTQ inclusion at Tata Steel signals how progressive Indian companies attract talent.
