## Core Argument
Public sector banking transformation requires culture change through governance, leadership, and talent reform, not just technology.

## Key Points
- Intangible factors (governance, leadership, culture) drive 50-75% of transformation success; often overlooked for operational fixes.
- External governance constraints from 50-year-old regulations and bureaucratic hierarchies prevent strategic agility in public sector banks.
- Proactive digital leadership with customer-employee focus possible despite public sector constraints, but requires systemic policy change.

## Open Question
If transformation is demonstrably possible (as Bank of Baroda proved pre-2018), why does government ownership and control systematically erode those gains post-merger, and can this paradox be resolved?

## India Angle
Indian public sector banks face structural constraints (governance, HR rigidity, government control) that limit transformation compared to private peers. Success requires fundamental policy reforms and cultural shift in government-owned institutions to compete in digital banking era.
