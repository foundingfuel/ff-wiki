## Core Argument
Effective writing requires disciplined reading, clear mission statements, and deliberate word choice over volume.

## Key Points
- Read analytically, not passively: engage with ideas, question authors, highlight meaningful passages.
- Write with a mission statement: define audience, purpose, relevance, and desired influence before drafting.
- Eliminate redundancy and vary sentence rhythm: brevity, simplicity, and structural clarity create impact.

## Open Question
How do writers balance staying true to a mission statement while allowing serendipitous discoveries and new information to reshape their argument?

## India Angle
Indian writers and professionals aspiring to improve written communication—critical for startups, media, academia, and public discourse—can apply these foundational principles regardless of context.
