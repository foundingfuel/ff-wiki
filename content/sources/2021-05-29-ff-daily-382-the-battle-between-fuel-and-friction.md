## Core Argument
Innovation fails not from weak ideas, but from underestimating psychological friction opposing change.

## Key Points
- Fuel (appeal) alone insufficient; must address friction (psychological forces opposing change).
- Adding more features/benefits can intensify friction rather than overcome it.
- Understanding resistance forces is essential before designing change strategy.

## Open Question
If adding fuel intensifies friction, how do innovators calibrate the balance between making ideas attractive and removing obstacles without feedback from actual resistance?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and change-makers must understand friction forces when introducing new ideas in contexts with deep-rooted skepticism and diverse stakeholder interests—critical for product launches, policy advocacy, and organizational transformation.
