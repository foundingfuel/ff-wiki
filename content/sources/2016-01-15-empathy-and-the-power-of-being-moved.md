## Core Argument
Empathy, not wealth, drives generosity; entrepreneurs who cultivate empathy move others toward shared goals.

## Key Points
- Being moved to action stems from empathy, not from having more resources; poor give proportionally more than rich.
- Emotion is the connective tissue linking motivation, persuasion, leadership, and entrepreneurial success across all stakeholder relationships.
- Entrepreneurs must shift from 'How do I sell?' to 'How do I understand what moves people to give?' through empathy.

## Open Question
If empathy and connectedness are universal human drives, why do systems and incentives (like wealth accumulation) systematically erode generosity rather than amplify it?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and business leaders can leverage empathy—rooted in shared struggle and cultural values—to build authentic customer relationships and organizational cultures that move people, especially in a market where lower-income consumers demonstrate higher generosity despite having less.
