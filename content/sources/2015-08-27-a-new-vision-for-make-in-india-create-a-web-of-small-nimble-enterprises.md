## Core Argument
Automation fears are overblown; new decentralized enterprise models powered by technology can create jobs and distribute opportunity.

## Key Points
- Technology changes production systems but human forces reshape institutions to protect jobs and livelihoods before elimination occurs.
- Gandhi's Manchester mills insight: factory design persisted despite technology change; mindsets lag technology capability for distributed coordination.
- India must design new enterprise archetypes—small, nimble, dispersed—using modern communication tech instead of replicating centralized factory models.

## Open Question
How can India create the institutional and cultural conditions for decentralized, collaborative enterprise models when hierarchical control remains the default management mindset?

## India Angle
India's Make in India policy risks job losses from automation; policymakers must reimagine enterprise models dispersed across regions to absorb youth employment while remaining globally competitive.
