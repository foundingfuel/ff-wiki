## Core Argument
Authentic personal impact comes from self-discovery and living values, not strategic branding tactics.

## Key Points
- Start with 'why' and self-discovery through coaching, not LinkedIn optimization tactics.
- Avoid modeling famous personalities; retain your unique signature especially during crises.
- Authenticity and consistency matter more than curated online personas or constructed purpose.

## Open Question
If building a personal brand requires constant effort, how do you distinguish between healthy self-awareness and performative inauthenticity?

## India Angle
Indian leaders increasingly chase personal branding as status symbol; the article urges focusing on authentic self-discovery and living values rather than curated social media personas—critical for India's hierarchical organizational culture.
