## Core Argument
Money choices reflect generational values shaped by economic contexts; financial resilience requires multiple capital forms.

## Key Points
- Two generations value money differently: socialist-era Indians prioritize functionality and longevity; Western-raised millennials prioritize affordability and experiences.
- Financial resilience depends on three cushions: savings, goodwill networks, and knowing your 'enough point'—not income alone.
- Younger generation prioritizes values alignment and present wellbeing over long-term career building; structural changes force frequent job-switching for salary growth.

## Open Question
How can younger generations build long-term security and reserve capital when economic instability and structural costs force short-term decision-making and debt accumulation?
