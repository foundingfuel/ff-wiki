## Core Argument
Leadership and strategy require understanding human behavior, simple rules, and political economy beyond textbook solutions.

## Key Points
- Simple rules of thumb often outperform complex models in decision-making and strategy.
- Large infrastructure projects like Aadhaar need proper implementation ecosystems, not just the core innovation.
- Politicians must craft narratives beyond corruption-fighting to drive change and voter support in democracies.

## Open Question
How can leaders balance the appeal of simple, intuitive decision-making rules with the complexity of real-world implementation challenges and stakeholder coordination?

## India Angle
Indian businesses navigating global partnerships (Tata Steel-Thyssenkrupp), the role of political narratives in India's largest democracy, and India's growing importance to global tech firms like Amazon.
