## Core Argument
True mastery requires knowing when to violate foundational principles, not just apply them.

## Key Points
- Machines evolving from executing programmed rules to discovering novel methods—a fundamental shift in capability.
- Greatness demands rule-breaking: excellence requires principle application; mastery requires principle violation.
- Self-programming systems (neural nets, genetic algorithms) create possibility for humans to learn from machines.

## Open Question
If machines increasingly surprise us with their methods, not just results, how do humans maintain meaningful control over AI systems and ensure human values guide their autonomous decisions?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and leaders must understand when to break rules and apply principles flexibly—crucial for competing in AI-driven markets and navigating generational shifts in entrepreneurship and finance.
