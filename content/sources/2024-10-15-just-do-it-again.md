## Core Argument
Over-correction toward digital-first strategy destroyed Nike's omnichannel strength and customer connection.

## Key Points
- Donahoe's D2C pivot eliminated wholesale partnerships that accounted for 50%+ of sales and deep customer reach.
- Assumption that Gen Z only buys online ignored their equal demand for premium physical experiences and newness.
- Eliminating product categories by sport severed brand equity built over decades; reorganizing by gender commoditized premium positioning.

## Open Question
How do legacy brands balance genuine digital transformation with preservation of category expertise and wholesale networks that built their moat?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates and legacy brands (Reliance, ITC, Tata) face similar pressures to digitize; Donahoe's misstep warns against over-indexing on D2C without maintaining omnichannel strength, critical for India's diverse retail landscape.
