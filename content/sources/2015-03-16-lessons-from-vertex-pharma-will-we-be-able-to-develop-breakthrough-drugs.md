## Core Argument
Breakthrough drug development requires distinctive philosophy, elite talent, integrated leadership, and ecosystem support India currently lacks.

## Key Points
- Founder vision shapes culture: Boger rejected screening dogma, betting on rational molecule design despite institutional inertia.
- CEO integrates across disciplines: Boger unified chemists, biologists, crystallographers with unwavering confidence through creative abrasion.
- Ecosystem gaps are structural: India lacks Harvard-equivalent research institutions and academia-industry networks that generate competitive stimulus.

## Open Question
How can India develop breakthrough drug companies without world-class research institutions and frontier scientists—or must it first build the ecosystem before entrepreneurship becomes possible?

## India Angle
India lacks the ecosystem ingredients Vertex leveraged: world-class research institutions providing competitive stimuli, deep scientific talent with breakthrough ambitions, and the venture capital infrastructure to fund high-risk drug development. The academia-industry gap is structural.
