## Core Argument
Virtual-real convergence demands experimentation, but unintended consequences reveal risks of moving fast without understanding system complexities.

## Key Points
- Virtual and real worlds are converging rapidly; pandemic accelerated adoption of simulations, chatbots, and digital interaction.
- Gene editing experiments show how powerful tech can backfire when we don't fully understand downstream effects of interventions.
- Innovation requires bold experimentation, but systems thinking and long-term consequence mapping must accompany technological leaps.

## Open Question
How do we encourage the experimentation necessary for breakthrough innovation while protecting against irreversible harms in domains like gene editing or AI?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies and researchers must grapple with virtualisation's implications for work culture, skill development, and ethical boundaries in emerging tech like gene editing—especially given India's biotech ambitions and large remote workforce.
