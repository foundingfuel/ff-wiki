## Core Argument
Effective leadership in crisis requires listening to human input before deciding, not just analyzing data.

## Key Points
- Flight director Kranz avoided 'satisficing' by insisting engineers know, not guess—rigorous listening over assumptions
- Top managers work through personal dialogue with subordinates; technical data supplements human intelligence
- Listening feeds decision-making: acquire information, test against experience, synthesize total picture

## Open Question
How do leaders at scale maintain the listening capability Kranz demonstrated with 20 simultaneous inputs when managing hundreds or thousands of people?

## India Angle
Indian leaders in complex crisis situations (startups scaling rapidly, large organizations navigating disruption) need listening capability as core competency; also relevant to women's advancement in Indian workplaces where implicit bias requires leaders who listen to lived experiences
