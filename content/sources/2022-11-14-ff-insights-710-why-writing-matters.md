## Core Argument
Clear thinking precedes clear writing; India's translation revolution democratises knowledge access across linguistic communities.

## Key Points
- Good writing reveals good thinking; rare thinkers communicate with precision and understanding of context.
- Bhashini ecosystem demonstrates collaborative AI leveraging policy, tech, academia, and citizens to translate Indian knowledge at scale.
- Online boundary-blurring requires nuanced professional judgment; gender dynamics complicate boss-employee connection norms differently.

## Open Question
If translation technology can assist but not replace translators, how do we build sustainable livelihoods and incentives for human translators in an AI-augmented ecosystem?

## India Angle
Translation infrastructure (Bhashini) is critical for India's linguistic diversity to unlock knowledge access, economic participation, and soft power across 1,200 languages; workplace dynamics around boss-employee boundaries reflect evolving professional norms in India's digital-first workforce.
