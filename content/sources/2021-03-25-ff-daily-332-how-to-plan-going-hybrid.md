## Core Argument
Hybrid work success requires balancing individual flexibility with team cohesion through equal access and minimum coordination standards.

## Key Points
- Processes aren't bureaucratic overhead—they enable scaling; startups should implement them from day one, not later.
- Hybrid models risk creating information asymmetry where office-present employees advance faster; level the playing field with universal remote meeting protocols.
- Senior leadership visibility in offices while mid-level workers go hybrid signals that office presence correlates with advancement, undermining remote productivity.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain spontaneous serendipitous connections and mentorship that happen in offices while ensuring remote workers aren't disadvantaged in career progression?

## India Angle
Indian startups and scale-ups need to adopt process discipline early—Wipro and GE's Six Sigma adoption shows this is proven in Indian context. Hybrid work planning is critical as Indian companies build remote-capable talent ecosystems and compete globally for talent.
