## Core Argument
Toxic workplace culture damages employee mental health; leaders must prioritize psychological safety as business imperative.

## Key Points
- 80% of Indian white-collar workers report adverse mental health symptoms; workplace stress is primary culprit.
- Toxic behaviour—unfair treatment, abusive management, cutthroat competition—drives burnout, anxiety, depression across Indian firms.
- Leaders who support mental health recovery (like Anant's approach) enable resilience; toxic environments force talent exodus.

## Open Question
How can organizations shift from valuing toxic-but-results-driven leaders to psychologically safe cultures when hierarchical norms reward fear-based management in India?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces show 40% of workforce experiencing poor mental health due to toxic behaviours endemic to hierarchical, patriarchal systems where toxic management is culturally tolerated as results-driven; costs employers ₹110,000 crore annually.
