## Core Argument
Organizations must cultivate leaders who embrace challenge, admit error, and nurture dissenting voices to survive.

## Key Points
- Bill Gates's ability to spend 5 hours rethinking to find truth over ego models intellectual humility leaders lack.
- Gig mindsetters—emerging employees challenging tradition—remain unseen; leaders must actively scan for and enable them.
- Older generations underestimate climate change scale; young Indians face existential emigration anxieties leaders don't grasp.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically identify and empower dissenting voices without destabilizing core operations or rewarding contrarianism for its own sake?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations must recognize 'gig mindsetters'—employees challenging status quo—to build resilience. Climate anxiety among young Indians (Global South perspective) reveals generational disconnect on existential risks that older leadership underestimates.
