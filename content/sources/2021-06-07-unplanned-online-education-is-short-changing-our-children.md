## Core Argument
Online education merely transplanted flawed offline pedagogy without redesigning for digital, failing millions of Indian students.

## Key Points
- Only 24% of Indian households have internet access; digital divide deepens educational inequality.
- Schools mechanically copied classroom structure to Zoom without reimagining teaching methodology or assessment.
- Students exploit outdated exam systems (Discord cheating) because passive online learning lacks engagement and relevance.

## Open Question
If schools cannot adapt their teaching philosophy to digital platforms, how do we ensure equitable learning outcomes for students across vastly different access and engagement levels?

## India Angle
India's rapid shift to online education during COVID exposed the digital divide affecting 76% of households without internet access, while schools failed to reimagine pedagogy—a systemic failure unique to India's unequal educational infrastructure.
