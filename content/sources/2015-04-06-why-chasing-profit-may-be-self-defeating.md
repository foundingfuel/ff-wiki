## Core Argument
Profitable growth emerges from serving customers first; profit becomes a byproduct, not the primary goal.

## Key Points
- Inside-Outside thinking prioritizes customer enrichment over revenue targets and ROI maximization.
- Outside-Inside approach (competitors' model) starts with company capabilities and fails to generate sustained demand.
- Customer loyalty and market success follow when companies consistently protect customer interests above profit motives.

## Open Question
How do purpose-driven companies justify to shareholders and boards that deprioritizing ROI actually generates superior long-term returns, especially in markets with quarterly earnings pressures?

## India Angle
Indian companies often pursue aggressive growth and profit-maximization strategies; this suggests they should instead align with customer needs and purpose-driven missions to build sustainable competitive advantages and loyal customer bases in India's expanding markets.
