## Core Argument
Humans are complex; mastering curiosity over comparison unlocks potential and builds resilience.

## Key Points
- Complexity of human behavior exceeds scientific predictability due to internal structure and interdependencies.
- Privacy education must become societal DNA; DPA should lead public awareness like RBI does for banking.
- Transform envy into curiosity about others' journeys to spark career innovation and personal growth.

## Open Question
How can India educate citizens about privacy as fundamental right when immediate survival needs (food, water, shelter) take precedence in the public consciousness?

## India Angle
Justice Srikrishna's call for public education on privacy rights reflects India's challenge of building data literacy while addressing basic needs; Indians must understand privacy as fundamental right even as government prioritizes security and development.
