## Core Argument
Indian politicians prioritise election victory above all, often contradicting personal beliefs to play the political game successfully.

## Key Points
- Politicians' primary motivation is winning elections, not personal enrichment or ideology.
- Personal beliefs often contradict public positions on religion, caste, and morality during campaigns.
- Political success requires understanding unwritten rules and playing the game as expected, not as one believes.

## Open Question
If politicians don't genuinely believe the positions they advocate publicly, how can citizens distinguish authentic policy commitments from electoral theatre?

## India Angle
Indian politicians operate within a unique electoral system where winning elections drives all behaviour, sometimes divorced from personal beliefs. Understanding this gap between private conviction and public action is critical for Indian citizens, policymakers, and those seeking to engage with the political system.
