## Core Argument
China's credit-driven growth model is unsustainable; global supply chains and economies face existential risk from impending Chinese financial crisis.

## Key Points
- China's extraordinary growth was artificially powered by excess credit and risky investments, not organic development.
- Global supply chains have become dangerously dependent on China, creating systemic vulnerability.
- The structural transformation underway in China will reshape geopolitics, trade rules, and the next financial crisis's origin point.

## Open Question
If China's transformation is inevitable and systemic, what timeline do economies have to build resilient alternatives before the credit model collapses?

## India Angle
India faces direct exposure through supply chain disruptions (iPhone 13s shortage mentioned), manufacturing competition, and potential economic contagion from China's credit crisis. Rising geopolitical tensions also reshape India's trade and strategic positioning.
