## Core Argument
Sustainable business success requires moral compass, infinite game thinking, and empathetic leadership over win-at-all-costs mentality.

## Key Points
- Winning mindset must shift from finite (beating competitors) to infinite game (adapting, collaborating, long-term).
- Leadership requires empathy, vulnerability, optimism and fairness—exemplified by coaches who build trust and strong teams.
- Business must balance delivering today while developing future products; structure and culture both matter in organizations.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain short-term performance and profitability while investing in long-term innovation and social responsibility without compromising either?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and entrepreneurs need these mental models as India scales globally—balancing ambition with ethics (Uber lesson), understanding infinite game mindset for sustainable growth, and recognizing the importance of inclusive leadership and social responsibility.
