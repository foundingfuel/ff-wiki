## Core Argument
Economic systems must shift from centralized, machine-like models to decentralized, locally-empowered complex systems thinking.

## Key Points
- Capitalism and democracy principles conflict; IP monopolies on vaccines expose this tension in practice.
- Centralized top-down solutions fail complex problems; Mumbai and Vietnam show decentralized local solutions work better.
- Old economics treats world as machine needing right levers; humans are complex, adaptive systems requiring new frameworks.

## Open Question
How can governments enable local empowerment and decentralized solutions while maintaining equity, preventing fragmentation, and ensuring vulnerable populations aren't left behind?

## India Angle
India's lockdown exposed the fragility of centralized top-down governance; vaccine inequality and migrant crises disproportionately impact Indian citizens. India needs decentralized, locally-empowered systems to build resilience and address development goals.
