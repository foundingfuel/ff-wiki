## Core Argument
Technology enables connection, but human empathy and love remain irreplaceable in crisis and work.

## Key Points
- Zoom's success stems from ease of access and solving real user pain points, not just technical sophistication.
- Serendipity and persistence matter: Anurag Kashyap's banned film found audience through piracy and chance intervention.
- Post-pandemic workplace requires balancing digital tools with human connection and emotional intelligence.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale technology-enabled remote work while preserving the human connection and emotional bonds that make work meaningful?

## India Angle
India's rapid remote work adoption during COVID-19 mirrors global shifts; Indian entrepreneurs like those using Zoom must balance access with security; local media/creative industries (Anurag Kashyap example) navigate censorship and distribution disruption.
