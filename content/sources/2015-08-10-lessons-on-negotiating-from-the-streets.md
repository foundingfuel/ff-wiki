## Core Argument
Negotiation is conversational art; street market practices mirror and teach corporate negotiation techniques effectively.

## Key Points
- Relationships form the foundation of any negotiation; warmth and pleasantries precede transactions.
- Clarity on desired outcomes and strategic sacrificial elements prevent getting lost in complex negotiations.
- Patience, positivity, and competitive intelligence on pricing create leverage and favorable closures.

## Open Question
How do these relationship-centric negotiation principles scale in impersonal, high-stakes corporate or cross-border deals where personal warmth cannot substitute for hard terms?

## India Angle
Uses Indian street market and family shopping culture as framework for understanding corporate negotiation—directly applicable to Indian business contexts where relationship-building remains central to deal-making.
