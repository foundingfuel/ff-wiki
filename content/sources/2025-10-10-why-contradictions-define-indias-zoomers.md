## Core Argument
India's Zoomers are defined by paradoxes: abundant talent but fragmented collaboration, global vision but local constraints.

## Key Points
- India celebrates individual ambition but undervalues long-term collaboration; star culture thrives while teamwork lags.
- The hustle required to enter corporations is suppressed once inside by rigid hierarchy and formality, even in startups.
- Zoomers possess global strategic thinking but are boxed into local ecosystem opportunities—engineering talent wasted on consumer apps instead of capital-intensive, sunrise sectors.

## Open Question
How can India harness its youngest workforce's global vision and entrepreneurial drive when structural constraints—weak safety nets, hierarchical cultures, family pressure, and limited capital-intensive opportunities—force them into stability-seeking, risk-averse careers?

## India Angle
India's Gen Z navigates unique contradictions—global ambition constrained by local hierarchies, individual hustle suppressed by rigid systems, and risk-taking hampered by lack of safety nets. These tensions are distinctly Indian, rooted in family structures, caste/class dynamics, and institutional inertia absent in Western contexts.
