## Core Argument
Effective giving requires direct engagement with local needs and systemic thinking about interconnected causes.

## Key Points
- Pandemic has decimated livelihoods and created food insecurity; damage to child nutrition is permanent and urgent.
- Traditional donor-NGO model faces fatigue and credibility gaps; direct engagement with local workers offers alternative giving pathways.
- Causes are interconnected—livelihoods, food security, health—requiring holistic rather than siloed philanthropic strategies.

## Open Question
How can individual donors effectively scale impact through direct micro-interventions while maintaining accountability and avoiding patronizing approaches to vulnerable populations?

## India Angle
India faces acute pandemic-driven crises in livelihoods and food security; philanthropic response must align with grassroots needs and local contexts rather than top-down solutions. Premji's mother's work reflects India's post-independence nation-building ethos now needed again.
