## Core Argument
Effective learning requires both focused and diffuse thinking; note-taking apps enable knowledge capture and organization.

## Key Points
- Handwritten digital notes improve retention better than typing; Penultimate bridges analog and digital note-taking
- Organize notes to see connections and align new learning with existing knowledge; Workflowy enables powerful yet simple organization
- Personal knowledge management systems built through systematic tagging create valuable long-term repositories of insights and ideas

## Open Question
How do workers balance the cognitive benefits of handwritten notes with the searchability and organizational efficiency of typed digital systems?

## India Angle
Indian remote workers and knowledge professionals need efficient note-taking systems to manage learning and productivity; local subscription pricing (Rs 850/year for Evernote) makes digital tools accessible for Indian professionals transitioning to WFH.
