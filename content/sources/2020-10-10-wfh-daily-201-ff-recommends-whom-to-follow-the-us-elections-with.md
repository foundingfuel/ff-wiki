## Core Argument
Follow data-driven analysts and specialist journalists to understand US elections beyond partisan noise.

## Key Points
- Nate Cohn and Nate Silver provide refined polling models with landscape explanations beyond raw numbers.
- Specialist journalists covering White House, media dynamics, race, and pandemic offer essential context for interpreting election significance.
- Science and policy experts like Ashish Jha and Ed Yong connect pandemic response to electoral outcomes and national mood.

## Open Question
How can non-US audiences discern reliable international election coverage when media itself is a contested battleground in the campaign?

## India Angle
Indian audiences seeking to understand US elections and their global implications need trusted analytical voices; India's own media literacy and ability to parse international events affects domestic policy discourse and business decisions.
