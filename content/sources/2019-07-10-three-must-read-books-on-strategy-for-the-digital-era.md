## Core Argument
Traditional strategy frameworks remain valid but insufficient; digital era demands new tools to address disruption by established digital giants.

## Key Points
- Digital giants (Amazon, Google, Facebook) pose greater threat to incumbents than startups due to larger, stickier networks and data advantages.
- Classical frameworks (Porter's 5 Forces, generic strategies) explain but don't adequately guide strategy formulation in digitally-transformed industries.
- India experienced delayed digital disruption; true strategic inflection arrived post-2010 with smartphones and 4G, compressing adaptation timelines.

## Open Question
Can traditional strategy frameworks be adapted for digital disruption, or do fundamentally new conceptual tools need to replace them entirely?

## India Angle
India's late digital adoption (post-2010 with 3G/4G) means Indian incumbents face compressed timelines to compete with global digital giants; banks and traditional businesses must rapidly rethink strategy frameworks designed for pre-digital eras.
