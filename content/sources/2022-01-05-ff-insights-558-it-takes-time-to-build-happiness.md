## Core Argument
Happiness and meaningful change require patience and time; destruction is easy, building is hard.

## Key Points
- All positive life changes—relationships, learning, behaviour shifts—take extended time; bad changes happen instantly.
- Strong habits resist change despite awareness; new knowledge translates slowly into behaviour without sustained effort.
- Crisis reveals opportunities for fundamental business pivots, but scaling requires conviction and systems thinking, not panic.
- Building sustainable impact (like rural women's agribusiness) is slower than destruction but defines us as leaders.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the urgency demanded by crisis with the patience required for genuine organisational and cultural transformation?

## India Angle
For Indian leaders and organisations navigating rapid change, the article underscores that sustainable transformation—whether personal habit change or business pivots—requires patience and systems thinking, not quick fixes. Illustrated through rural women entrepreneurs adapting during COVID-19, showing how Indian contexts demand long-term capability building alongside crisis opportunism.
