## Core Argument
Digital focus requires intentional friction—separating creation, communication, and curation into distinct environments.

## Key Points
- Internal vs external triggers shape whether actions align with goals or become distractions.
- Minimalist tools and deliberate constraints (offline writing, blocked websites) enhance sustained attention.
- Physical or virtual environment separation signals context to the brain and others around you.

## Open Question
How can knowledge workers maintain focus when their jobs structurally demand constant connectivity and responsiveness?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers face intense connectivity pressures and open-office distractions; tools and frameworks for focused work directly address productivity challenges in India's growing remote/hybrid work landscape.
