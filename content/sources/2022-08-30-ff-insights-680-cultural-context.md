## Core Argument
Cultural context creates different webs of meaning; ignoring it derails global business and organizational strategy.

## Key Points
- Companies like Coca-Cola and Merrill Lynch failed internationally by exporting home-market assumptions without understanding local cultural interpretations.
- Decision-making requires alignment with personal True North to avoid accumulating small directional errors into major strategic failures.
- Electoral and organizational systems can exhaust participants when poorly designed; voter fatigue in France mirrors potential Lok Sabha election dynamics.

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically embed cultural understanding into decision-making when speed and global scale often incentivize standardization over localization?

## India Angle
Indian businesses expanding globally must understand that local cultural contexts shape consumer meaning-making differently; also relevant to India's coalition politics and electoral fatigue mirroring France's systemic challenges.
