## Core Argument
2024 marks a geopolitical inflection—nationalist democracies, US-China uncertainty, and wars without winners reshape global order.

## Key Points
- US election outcome carries extreme geopolitical risk; Republican win could destabilize Sino-US relations and Taiwan.
- Global democracies electing nationalist leaders mirrors 1920s trajectory—threatens multilateralism and minority protections.
- China's growth slowdown to 4-5% is manageable; doomsayers have repeatedly miscalled China's economic resilience for 30 years.

## Open Question
How can democracies simultaneously strengthen institutions against election denial while empowering nationalist movements that undermine pluralism?

## India Angle
India faces a complex 2024: US election uncertainty affects Indo-US ties; nationalist trends globally mirror India's own political trajectory; China's slowdown (4-5% growth) impacts India's trade and geopolitical positioning in Asia; South Asian elections shape regional stability.
