## Core Argument
VC bias against women entrepreneurs stems from systemic questioning patterns, not merit disparities in pitches.

## Key Points
- Only 3% of US VC funding reaches female-founded companies despite growing numbers of female entrepreneurs
- Studies show identical pitches scored lower when presented by women, regardless of judge gender
- Women asked risk-focused questions; men asked opportunity-focused questions by same VCs

## Open Question
If bias is unconscious and affects both male and female judges equally, how do individual entrepreneurs overcome systemic patterns without systemic intervention?

## India Angle
Indian venture ecosystem mirrors global gender bias patterns; women founders face similar systemic barriers in pitches and funding access, compounded by cultural factors around entrepreneurship visibility
