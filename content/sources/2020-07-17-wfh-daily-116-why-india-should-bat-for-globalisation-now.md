## Core Argument
India should leverage globalisation to avoid middle-income trap and unlock exponential growth.

## Key Points
- Startups fear global expansion due to cost, knowledge gaps, and unfamiliar territories—but these are opportunities.
- India's cost advantage enables unmatched competitive positioning in global markets.
- Economic isolation at this geopolitical turning point is strategically risky for India's development trajectory.

## Open Question
How can India balance protectionist sentiment and inward focus with the urgent need for global integration to avoid stagnation?

## India Angle
Indian startups must overcome hesitation about global expansion; India risks middle-income trap without embracing international trade, investment, and innovation partnerships despite current protectionist impulses.
