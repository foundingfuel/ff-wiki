## Core Argument
Digital infrastructure and thoughtful community understanding reshape India's competitive advantage and social impact globally.

## Key Points
- Nilekani's journey from Infosys to Aadhaar shows how entrepreneurs can shift from business to systemic social change.
- India Stack's 2 billion December 2020 UPI transactions demonstrate scaled digital infrastructure enabling inclusive economic participation.
- Understanding power dynamics and community voice—not assumptions—is essential for social enterprises to achieve lasting impact.

## Open Question
How can India's digital infrastructure success and policy influence scale without replicating the same power imbalances and community voice issues that Novogratz highlights in development work?

## India Angle
India's emergence as a global knowledge economy player through figures like Nilekani; digital infrastructure (Aadhaar, UPI, India Stack) reshaping India's economic and social capacity; policy influence extending India's tech-as-social-good model globally.
