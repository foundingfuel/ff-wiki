## Core Argument
Organisations fostering decline prioritise transactions over conversations, culture fit over diversity of thought.

## Key Points
- Culture fit hiring becomes groupthink at scale; hire disagreeable givers and original thinkers to drive evolution.
- Absence of genuine conversation and connection drives disengagement no technology or engagement programs can redeem.
- Challenge and growth must be continuous; mundane work without dialogue signals organisational decline.

## Open Question
How can organisations balance cultural cohesion during growth stages with the need for diversity of thought to prevent stagnation and decline?

## India Angle
Indian organisations transitioning from startup mode to scale face acute risk of calcifying culture; need deliberate practices to prevent groupthink and maintain human-centred workplaces as they grow.
