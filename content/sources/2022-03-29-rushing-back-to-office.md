## Core Argument
CEOs rushing back to office without reimagining work cling to legacy power structures rather than adapt to new realities.

## Key Points
- Legacy thinking and loss of status symbols drive mandates, not productivity evidence
- Generational divide: young workers reject fixed office models; leaders resist egalitarianism of remote work
- Trust-building virtually requires new leadership skills; snooping erodes psychological safety instead

## Open Question
If productivity during pandemic was proven and flexibility improves hiring/retention, why do powerful leaders insist on full office presence—is it about work or about reclaiming lost status and control?

## India Angle
Indian tech and conglomerate leaders (Infosys, etc.) are pushing return-to-office mandates without reimagining work, risking talent exodus to competitors offering flexibility; generational divide between senior leaders and young tech workers is acute in India's knowledge economy.
