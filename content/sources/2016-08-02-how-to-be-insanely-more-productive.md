## Core Argument
True productivity means staying relevant through continuous learning, not working longer hours.

## Key Points
- Productivity without purpose fails—clarify intent before optimizing time management systems.
- Staying relevant demands unlearning and evolution, not just repeating familiar tasks.
- Compress learning via premium content, summaries, and access to world-class minds across disciplines.

## Open Question
How do you ruthlessly prioritize what to learn when the world is multidisciplinary and accelerating, and you cannot master all domains?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders face rapid market change and competitive pressure; relevance requires continuous learning and ruthless prioritization—critical for India's talent-intensive economy where skills decay faster than elsewhere.
