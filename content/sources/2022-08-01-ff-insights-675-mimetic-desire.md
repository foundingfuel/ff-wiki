## Core Argument
Mimetic desire operates in two cycles: negative (rivalry, scarcity) and positive (shared purpose, abundance).

## Key Points
- Humans imitate desires rather than forming intrinsic wants—a flywheel that accelerates non-linearly in both directions
- Positive mimetic cycles unite people around common goods and transform organisations from within
- IPL streaming shift reveals loss of communal experience as individual devices replace shared viewing spaces

## Open Question
How can organisations intentionally trigger the critical inflection point where mimetic desire shifts from destructive rivalry to generative abundance without manipulation?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations can leverage mimetic desire cycles to build culture—positive cycles unite teams around shared vision; negative cycles drive rivalry. Understanding this is critical as India scales from family-run to professional organisations.
