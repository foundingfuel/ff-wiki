## Core Argument
Policymakers avoid hard questions by creating polarizing narratives instead of engaging uncomfortable trade-offs.

## Key Points
- Privacy versus lifesaving intervention: technology enables both breaching rights and saving lives simultaneously.
- Immigrant narratives counter macro-economic assumptions; they sustain economies rather than damage local livelihoods.
- Polarization prevents dialogue; tribal opinion silos replace deep listening across difference.

## Open Question
How do democracies make ethical policy when the right choice involves violating fundamental rights or accepting uncomfortable truths that threaten political narratives?

## India Angle
India faces acute policy dilemmas—algorithmic privacy breaches in healthcare, immigrant labor narratives, informal sector realities (93% of workforce)—that require listening to diverse voices rather than polarizing politics that deflect from urgent issues.
