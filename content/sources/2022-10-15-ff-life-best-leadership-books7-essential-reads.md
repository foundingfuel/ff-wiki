## Core Argument
Leadership emerges contextually through individuals; institutions outlive leaders only when many people contribute.

## Key Points
- Leaders are shaped by context, not inherently gifted; vulnerability and failure are integral to leadership.
- Leadership and management are distinct; management executes present, leadership prepares future—few excel at both.
- Measure leaders by intent and moment of rise, not mythical characteristics or outcomes alone.

## Open Question
If leadership is contextual and situational, how do we systematically develop it in people before the crisis moment arrives?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and institutions (ISRO, family structures, post-independence context via Nehru-Bose) must move beyond mythologizing individual leaders to building institutional capability that transcends personalities.
