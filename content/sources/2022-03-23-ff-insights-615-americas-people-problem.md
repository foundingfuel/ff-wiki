## Core Argument
Economic vitality requires scale; underpopulation creates mediocrity across services, products, and opportunities.

## Key Points
- America needs more people to sustain superpower status and avoid small-town economic degradation
- Market discipline, not geopolitics, drives supply chain decisions; companies optimize for profit, not resilience
- Tech industry struggles with ethical accountability; worksheets alone won't shift business model incentives

## Open Question
If supply chain companies are profiting from shortages despite crises, what forces them to build redundancy—profit motive or regulation?

## India Angle
India faces inverse demographic challenge—abundance of young people requiring jobs and opportunity. US population strategy offers cautionary lesson: immigration as economic lever. India must focus on retaining talent, creating quality jobs in smaller towns to prevent depopulation like rural India.
