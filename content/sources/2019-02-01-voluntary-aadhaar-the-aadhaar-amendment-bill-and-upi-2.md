## Core Argument
Aadhaar amendment signals political consensus on digital identity as India's infrastructure despite unresolved privacy tensions.

## Key Points
- Supreme Court judgment left doors open for voluntary private-sector Aadhaar use; amendment bill codifies voluntary adoption and offline verification.
- Cross-party consensus on Aadhaar utility despite public posturing—Congress-ruled states mandating Aadhaar for subsidies even as party opposes nationally.
- UPI crossed Rs 1 trillion monthly value, signaling India Stack's transformative potential; but complex algorithms embed human biases into systems at scale.

## Open Question
How can India harness Aadhaar's proven efficiency for financial inclusion while protecting citizens from surveillance and algorithmic bias embedded in systems designed with insufficient transparency?
