## Core Argument
Top talent quits when dismissed; leaders ignoring this risk losing people to competitors.

## Key Points
- Talented people invest deeply in skills and demand agency over how they're applied.
- Eric Yuan left Cisco/Webex after his smartphone-friendly proposal was dismissed, creating a disruptive competitor.
- Gig workers in Japan find flexibility valuable compared to rigid employment, but this may not last as market pressures increase.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance the need for hierarchical decision-making with the reality that top talent has options and will leave if unheard?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies and startups must recognize that top talent—especially engineers and leaders—will leave if their ideas are dismissed. India's talent retention crisis in tech requires listening to employee input, not just extracting value. Also relevant: gig economy regulation and worker satisfaction patterns for Indian platforms like Ola, Swiggy.
