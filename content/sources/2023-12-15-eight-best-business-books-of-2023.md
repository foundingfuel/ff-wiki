## Core Argument
Effective leadership requires humility, innovation through recombination, strong character, and boards guided by moral compass not maps.

## Key Points
- India must invest in education and health, embrace non-hierarchical culture, and pivot toward manufacturing services and tech-led industries.
- Innovation combines existing solutions applied to new problems; success depends more on learning speed and character than innate talent.
- Board effectiveness hinges on behavioural judgment, courage to dissent, and navigating weak signals with moral compass rather than predetermined maps.

## Open Question
How can India simultaneously build world-class governance and innovation ecosystems while creating millions of jobs in a rapidly skill-disrupted economy where 40% of current skills may become obsolete within 24 months?

## India Angle
India's economic growth depends on investing in people, shifting from low-cost assembly to tech-led industries, improving corporate governance, and building essential future-ready skills—core themes across multiple books reviewed.
