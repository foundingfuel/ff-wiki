## Core Argument
Curiosity and bottom-up leadership without formal authority drive cognitive growth and organizational transformation.

## Key Points
- Children ask 40,000 questions by age five; questioning is the primary engine for cognitive development and knowledge acquisition.
- Murali led without title by building confidence in juniors, breaking hierarchical cultures, and creating psychological safety through informal influence.
- Curious organizations that encourage questioning and flatten hierarchies develop adaptive, innovative teams better equipped for change.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale curiosity-driven, non-hierarchical cultures without losing direction or accountability when formal authority is deliberately diminished?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces often perpetuate hierarchy and discourage questioning; adopting curiosity-driven cultures and inclusive leadership models like Murali's could transform how Indian teams innovate and develop talent.
