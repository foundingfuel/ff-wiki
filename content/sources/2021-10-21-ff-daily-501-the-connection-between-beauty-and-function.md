## Core Argument
Reconnecting beauty with function requires recovering latent human skill and building sustainable, ecologically coherent systems.

## Key Points
- High MLA turnover in UP weakens legislative checks and concentrates power among dynasts; institutional knowledge erodes with 92.6% first-time elected members.
- Masterpieces like Tofuku-ji temple exemplify skill transcending current assumptions—embedding aesthetic and functional excellence creates spaces that feel inevitably right.
- Eisenstein's vision rejects alienation between survival and artistry; making ordinary objects with care suggests economy rooted in wholeness rather than endless growth.

## Open Question
If transcendent skill to embed 'wholeness' in all things is latent within us, why do our political systems and economic structures systematically erode the conditions for its expression?

## India Angle
India's legislative institutions, particularly state assemblies like UP's, face similar challenges of weak continuity and institutional learning; the tension between beauty/function mirrors India's development paradox—pursuing growth while losing craftsmanship and systemic coherence.
