## Core Argument
Iconic institutions require reformers who innovate within constraints and challenge counterproductive policies systematically.

## Key Points
- Dr Shetty redesigned hospital construction to cut costs 80%, proving creative constraint-solving beats conventional approaches.
- Reformers face institutional resistance—Semmelweis ridiculed for hand-washing, hospital rankings burned—requiring long-term conviction.
- Local institutions investing in communities create social value beyond price, contrasting with extractive global platforms.

## Open Question
How do reformers sustain momentum against institutional resistance when the payoff requires decades, especially in resource-constrained India?

## India Angle
India's institutional capacity depends on reformers willing to challenge flawed policies (like Dr Shetty's tax fight) and build scalable models for public good. Local businesses vs global platforms raises questions about India's development model and taxation fairness.
