## Core Argument
India must pragmatically engage Taliban to protect interests despite ideological reservations and delayed response.

## Key Points
- Taliban's tone shifted from peace overtures to aggression as battlefield victories increased, narrowing India's diplomatic window.
- India declined engagement with Taliban on principle while Russia, China, Iran built relationships, now facing strategic disadvantage.
- Shamelessness in politics rewards audacious actors who ignore norms; India's cautious approach may lose influence in anarchic geopolitics.

## Open Question
Can India establish credible diplomatic relations with Taliban after years of principled rejection, or has the moment for influence already passed?

## India Angle
India faces a critical geopolitical challenge as Taliban gains power in Afghanistan—risk of terror strikes, need to balance principles with strategic interests, and urgency to establish diplomatic channels before losing leverage.
