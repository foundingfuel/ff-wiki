## Core Argument
Internal consultants gain organisational credibility but risk skill stagnation without external exposure and diverse experiences.

## Key Points
- Internal consultants leverage existing system knowledge and relationships but lack external perspective that external consultants provide.
- Career advancement as internal consultant depends on organisation's maturity in valuing non-operational roles and providing diverse project opportunities.
- External consulting exposes professionals to cross-industry best practices and networks, building specialised expertise internal roles cannot replicate.

## Open Question
How can internal consultants maintain competitiveness and skill development without the natural exposure external consulting provides, while organisations struggle to recognise the strategic value of non-operational roles?

## India Angle
Indian organisations increasingly adopting internal consulting models during digital transformation; relevant for leaders navigating career transitions in growing Indian enterprises and global firms with India operations
