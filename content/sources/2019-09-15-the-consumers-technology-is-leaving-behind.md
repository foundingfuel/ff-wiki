## Core Argument
Tech innovation systematically excludes elderly consumers due to poor design, not user incompetence.

## Key Points
- India's elderly population will double to 16% by 2041; 340 million above 60 by 2050.
- Poor design (volume, timeouts, interface changes) locks elderly out of ATMs, apps, financial services.
- Solution: designers must empathize with elderly users; hire older workers; treat seniors as core market.

## Open Question
If markets solve problems with sufficient demand, why has elderly user exclusion persisted despite growing demographic pressure in India?

## India Angle
India's ageing population (340 million by 2050) faces severe digital exclusion as tech products ignore elderly consumers. Critical for India's inclusive growth and financial inclusion goals, especially given ATM/banking reliance.
