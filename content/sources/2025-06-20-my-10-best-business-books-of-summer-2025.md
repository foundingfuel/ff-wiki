## Core Argument
Summer 2025 books reveal that effective leadership requires managing attention, character, and others' autonomy—not control.

## Key Points
- CEO lifecycle challenges conventional wisdom on selection and grooming processes
- Attention-seeking society undermines focus and reflection; letting others act freely builds happiness
- Indian retail, finance policy, and institutional leadership demonstrate transformation through consumer-centricity and stakeholder management

## Open Question
How do leaders build trust and meaning at work while simultaneously accepting they cannot control others' actions or perceptions?

## India Angle
Indian context emerges in retail transformation (Shoppers Stop), economic policy history, and IIM leadership—showing how Indian institutions and businesses navigate change and develop leaders
