## Core Argument
Family businesses work best when generational differences are acknowledged, not erased.

## Key Points
- Rhea adapted to mother's leadership style by bringing inquiry-based approach to flat organisation.
- Indian joint families persist due to economic necessity, cultural tradition, and lack of social insurance.
- Intergenerational co-residence creates both friction and learning in family enterprises.

## Open Question
How can young professionals in family businesses innovate and evolve organisational culture while respecting deep kinship obligations and economic interdependencies that bind Indian families together?

## India Angle
Indian men's multi-generational living arrangements reflect systemic factors unique to India—weak social safety nets, expensive housing, tax incentives for family employment, and deep cultural kinship norms—shaping how family businesses operate and work-life integration differs fundamentally from Western models.
