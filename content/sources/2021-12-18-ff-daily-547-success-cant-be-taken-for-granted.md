## Core Argument
Success is fragile; sustained competitive advantage requires constant evolution, not resting on past achievements.

## Key Points
- No company is immune to decline—even industry leaders face vulnerability without continuous adaptation
- Product obsession and customer insight beat marketing spend; Sugar Cosmetics scaled through owned and earned media
- Communication strategy matters in crises; framing (percentages vs raw numbers, genre shifts) influences behavior change

## Open Question
If all companies eventually decline, what separates those that manage graceful transitions from those that collapse catastrophically—is it luck, culture, or systematic foresight?

## India Angle
Indian companies like Sugar Cosmetics demonstrate how to build sustainable competitive advantage through product obsession and earned media rather than expensive paid advertising—a model particularly relevant for capital-constrained Indian startups competing against legacy brands.
