## Core Argument
Master email through systematic triage, keyboard optimization, and ruthless compartmentalization to reclaim time.

## Key Points
- Categorize emails into three actions: mark done, remind later, or schedule thoughtful response.
- Keyboard shortcuts eliminate mouse friction; saves accumulated hours monthly through micro-efficiencies.
- Custom inbox splits based on priority (birthdays, family, news, learning) prevent missed signals.

## Open Question
How do these individual productivity hacks scale across teams when email remains the primary communication channel rather than being replaced by better systems?

## India Angle
Relevant for Indian knowledge workers and managers juggling multiple email accounts and high-volume communication; applicable to distributed teams across Indian organizations managing Masterclass/training operations.
