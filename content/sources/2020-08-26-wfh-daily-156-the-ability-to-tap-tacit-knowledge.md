## Core Argument
Organizations miss creative solutions by ignoring tacit knowledge embedded in frontline workers and experienced practitioners.

## Key Points
- Tacit knowledge from experienced workers often outperforms formal instrumentation and educated guesses.
- Generational gaps in perspectives on money, careers, and emerging technologies require intentional dialogue and mutual learning.
- Frontier fields like quantum computing demand long-term commitment while younger generations navigate financial pressures forcing job mobility.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically extract and value tacit knowledge from frontline workers when hierarchies and formal credentials typically discount such wisdom?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often underutilize tacit knowledge held by frontline workers and experienced practitioners; bridging generational perspectives on money, career transitions, and emerging fields like quantum computing reflects India's evolving workforce dynamics.
