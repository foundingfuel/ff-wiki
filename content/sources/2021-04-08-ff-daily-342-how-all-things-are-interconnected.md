## Core Argument
Breakthroughs emerge from unexpected connections; siloed thinking limits innovation and persuasion.

## Key Points
- Watson's ornithology-to-genetics pivot shows how cross-disciplinary exposure sparks breakthroughs
- Chinese state media's 'constructive journalism' alienates foreign audiences by avoiding complexity and negative stories
- Institutions measuring output (quantity) over impact (audience understanding) miss feedback loops essential for effectiveness

## Open Question
How do institutions balance specialization's efficiency with interconnectedness's innovation potential without becoming unfocused?

## India Angle
India's media and communication strategies must recognize interconnectedness of science, culture, and narrative—unlike China's compartmentalized approach. Indian institutions should avoid siloed specialization that misses systemic connections.
