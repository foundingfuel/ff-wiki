## Core Argument
AI adoption must be proactive, pragmatic, and focused on solving India's development challenges rather than resisting job displacement.

## Key Points
- AI is advancing in narrow domains (vision, speech) but far from general intelligence or human language understanding.
- Automation already displacing workers globally; India must reframe AI debate beyond job loss to development opportunity.
- India can use AI to deliver personalized health, education, finance at scale—the only viable path to inclusive growth.

## Open Question
How can India pursue AI-driven productivity gains while protecting workers displaced by automation, especially in a labor-surplus economy still building basic services?

## India Angle
India can leverage AI strategically to scale healthcare, education, and financial services to 1B+ people, bypassing traditional infrastructure bottlenecks that would take decades. Critical for moving from $2K to $20K per capita income.
