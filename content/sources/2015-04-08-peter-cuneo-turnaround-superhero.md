## Core Argument
Successful turnarounds require simultaneous financial discipline and cultural transformation backed by visible leadership action.

## Key Points
- Poor leadership unwillingness to make hard decisions causes most company failures, not industry fundamentals.
- Culture change must happen immediately alongside financial fixes; talk without action destroys credibility.
- Public recognition of success and failure normalizes risk-taking; employees believe new values only through observed behavior.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the urgency of rapid transformation with the reality that deep cultural change takes years to demonstrate credibility?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates and family-run businesses often resist hard decisions and cultural change; Cuneo's framework of simultaneous financial and cultural overhaul is directly applicable to turnarounds in Indian manufacturing, media, and consumer goods sectors.
