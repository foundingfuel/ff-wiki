## Core Argument
Snow leopard expeditions reveal mountains as places of spiritual encounter, ecological fragility, and human limitation.

## Key Points
- Snow leopards are elusive high-altitude predators that descend only during winter months, making sightings rare and weather-dependent.
- Spiti Valley's extreme terrain—deep crevices hidden by snow, mercurial weather—demands careful planning and gradual acclimatization for visitors.
- Himalayan ecosystems support interdependent fauna from blue sheep to bearded vultures, forming delicate food chains vulnerable to disruption.

## Open Question
How can adventure tourism to fragile high-altitude regions generate economic opportunity for local communities without degrading the very ecosystems and wildlife that attract visitors?

## India Angle
Highlights Himachal Pradesh's Spiti Valley as critical snow leopard habitat and adventure tourism destination, showcasing India's unique high-altitude ecosystems and conservation challenges.
