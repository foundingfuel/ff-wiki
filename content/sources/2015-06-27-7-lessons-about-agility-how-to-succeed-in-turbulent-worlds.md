## Core Argument
Agility requires integrating strategy, innovation, and distributed leadership culture, not just process flexibility.

## Key Points
- Agility means rapid response to change; it differs fundamentally from slow Darwinian adaptation.
- Truly agile companies distribute agile leadership across all levels, from CEO to frontline staff.
- Different contexts demand different types of agility—supply chain, strategic, operational—not one-size-fits-all approaches.

## Open Question
How do large, hierarchical organizations maintain distributed agile leadership without losing strategic coherence and decision-making speed across silos?

## India Angle
Indian startups and growing companies often struggle with balancing rapid scaling while maintaining distributed leadership and agile culture; this framework is critical as Indian firms navigate global competition and market turbulence.
