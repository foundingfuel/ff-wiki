## Core Argument
Buying social proof is easy, cheap, and undetectable—undermining authentic influence measurement in India.

## Key Points
- Fake follower services operate openly in India at scale, offering manually-created profiles for Rs400-3000.
- Twitter's algorithms cannot reliably distinguish organic from inorganic growth when profiles are manually crafted.
- No bulletproof method exists to verify genuine influence; credibility markers are easily gamed and untrustworthy.

## Open Question
How can digital platforms maintain authentic influence measurement when verification is technically impossible and economic incentives to cheat are ubiquitous?

## India Angle
Indian social media influencer economy is built on readily available fake follower services; exposes systemic vulnerability in how Indian entrepreneurs, politicians, and media figures build credibility online.
