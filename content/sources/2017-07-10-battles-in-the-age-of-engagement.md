## Core Argument
Tech giants winning by engagement and integration, not scale; traditional moats of scale and scope are obsolete.

## Key Points
- Facebook/Google earn ~$1.60 per user monthly despite billions of users; model increasingly threatened by ad blockers
- Amazon and Apple integrate into consumer lives transactionally; they make themselves 'irreplaceable' rather than attention-grabbing
- Traditional economies of scale and scope no longer protect firms; venture capital and network effects dislocate market structures instantly

## Open Question
If engagement-based models like Amazon and Apple are truly superior, why do Facebook and Google remain more valuable by market cap, and will this gap persist or collapse?

## India Angle
Indian startups and traditional businesses must recognize that tech-driven disruption is rewriting competitive moats. Indian retailers, fintech players, and legacy firms face existential threats from global tech giants entering their markets; they must pivot from scale/scope advantages to engagement-based models.
