## Core Argument
Mistakes and accidents drive innovation, but growth pressure corrupts intent in edtech and beyond.

## Key Points
- Oxford vaccine's superior 0.5+1 dosing regimen discovered by trial administration mistake; efficacy needs validation
- WhiteHat Jr transformation from yogi narrative to scandal reveals VC-funded edtech prioritizing growth over values and child safety
- Windows 1's 35-year existence shows some technologies persist while others transform radically; change is non-linear

## Open Question
How can India's edtech sector scale to address genuine learning gaps when VC incentives systematically pressure founders to compromise on ethics, data safety, and the cultural understanding that education is a sacred service?
