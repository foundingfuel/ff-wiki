## Core Argument
Technology's purpose transcends business—it must be guided by empathy and human impact.

## Key Points
- Empathy shapes strategic decisions: Nadella's personal tragedy reframed Microsoft's work as life-enabling, not just commercially viable.
- Narrative power drives change: Nations and organizations are built on stories people tell, not just resources or military might.
- Workplace autonomy matters more than location: Hybrid fatigue stems from control, not from splitting time between home and office.

## Open Question
How can organizations implement genuine employee autonomy in hybrid work without losing coordination and culture-building that in-person presence enables?

## India Angle
Indian tech leaders and enterprises must balance rapid digital transformation with human-centered values; hybrid work adoption in India's IT sector requires rethinking control-based management culture inherited from traditional hierarchies.
