## Core Argument
Institutional change requires outsider leadership willing to challenge comfortable falsehoods despite systemic pressure.

## Key Points
- New leaders should target high-probability wins with aligned teams rather than frontal system-wide attacks.
- Institutional incest—protecting insiders over truth—erodes public trust in media, business, and governance.
- Moral courage to expose uncomfortable truths, even at financial cost, ultimately rebuilds institutional credibility.

## Open Question
How can leaders balance short-term organizational survival (protecting revenue from Catholic advertisers) against long-term institutional integrity and public trust?

## India Angle
Indian media and businesses are widely perceived as compromised; Spotlight demonstrates how institutional courage and moral clarity—absent in Indian systems—can rebuild trust and credibility.
