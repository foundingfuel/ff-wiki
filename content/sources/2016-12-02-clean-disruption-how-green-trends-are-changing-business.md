## Core Argument
Converging EV, battery, solar and autonomous vehicle technologies will disrupt energy and transportation fundamentally within 14 years.

## Key Points
- EVs are 10x cheaper to operate, virtually maintenance-free, more powerful than ICE cars; battery cost decline drives adoption.
- Self-driving + car-sharing cuts per-km cost 10x; parking and car ownership become obsolete; transforms asset utilization from 4% to 60-70%.
- Solar costs dropped 250x since 1970; by 2020, solar+storage undercuts transmission; decentralized generation replaces central power model.

## Open Question
How will India's fragmented regulatory framework, grid infrastructure, and vehicle financing systems adapt fast enough to capture leapfrog opportunity before incumbent industries entrench?

## India Angle
India can leapfrog to EV and solar infrastructure like it did with mobile phones, bypassing legacy systems. EVs could store 37% of daily electricity demand; solar cost parity enables decentralized energy models suited to India's distributed population.
