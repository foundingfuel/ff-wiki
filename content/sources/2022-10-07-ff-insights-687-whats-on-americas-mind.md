## Core Argument
America's core anxiety: whether China's faster growth inevitably means economic dominance despite per-capita poverty gaps.

## Key Points
- Chinese official statistics may overstate growth by 1.7% annually; real economy could be 16% smaller than reported
- China needn't match US per-capita wealth to surpass it in aggregate economic power due to 4x population difference
- Past predictions of US economic displacement (Japan) underestimated wealth-per-person gaps; China's trajectory differs fundamentally

## Open Question
If China's growth continues slowing while population remains 3x larger, at what point does aggregate GDP dominance become inevitable regardless of statistical accuracy or per-capita comparisons?

## India Angle
India's own growth trajectory and economic ambitions exist in shadow of China-US competition; understanding US anxieties about Chinese rise informs India's strategic positioning in a multipolar world
