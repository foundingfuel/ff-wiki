## Core Argument
Execution fails when leaders lack facilitation, culture misaligns with strategy, and stakeholders operate in isolated silos.

## Key Points
- Listening and cross-stakeholder collaboration remain rare in Indian organisations bound by silos and functional identities.
- Culture must be customer-driven and lived by leaders, not a list of clichés displayed on walls.
- Transformation happens post-workshop through sustained behaviour change, focus-setting, and distinguishing urgent from important work.

## Open Question
How do you shift entrenched organisational behaviours and break low-trust environments when the real work happens after workshops, not during them?

## India Angle
Indian organisations struggle with execution due to siloed thinking, weak listening culture, and inability to prioritise—critical blockers as India Inc scales growth ambitions post-Modi era
