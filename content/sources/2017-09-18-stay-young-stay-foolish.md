## Core Argument
Continuous learning through open, curious communities—not fear of sounding foolish—is essential to navigate accelerating change.

## Key Points
- Open, diverse communities outperform siloed expert teams by bringing multidisciplinary perspectives to complex problems.
- Psychological safety to ask 'foolish questions' unlocks deeper learning and knowledge-sharing across domains.
- India's rapid growth requires adaptive learning cultures; staying young means remaining unafraid to unlearn and question assumptions.

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically create cultures where people feel safe asking 'foolish' questions without reverting to hierarchical expertise-gatekeeping?

## India Angle
India's rapid GDP growth (15-year doubling) demands continuous learning from leaders and policymakers to navigate unprecedented change; participatory community learning models like Aadhaar debates are critical for India-specific policy challenges
