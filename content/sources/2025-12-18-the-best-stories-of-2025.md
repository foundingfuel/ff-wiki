## Core Argument
Structural shifts—digital crises, AI hype, labour precarity, systemic fragility—define 2025's deeper business currents.

## Key Points
- Trust now erodes in real-time through digital amplification and social media; frontline listening beats fast response
- AI and data abundance create false confidence; nuance, context, and what's unsaid matter more than metrics
- Manufacturing efficiency and operational excellence become liabilities when organisations can't adapt to regulatory or contextual shifts

## Open Question
How can high-performance systems designed for stability remain resilient when external contexts shift faster than internal cultures can adapt?
