## Core Argument
Test cricket's decline reflects positioning failure, not product failure; scarcity and premium positioning unlock value.

## Key Points
- Test cricket's natural audience is affluent, patient, older—like Wimbledon or Masters; misreading this causes positioning failures.
- Scarcity, ritual, and five-day attention span are strengths in noise-saturated economy, not weaknesses to fix.
- BCCI's leadership can reshape ecosystem through calendar, stadiums, place-based rituals, and overseas travel incentives.

## Open Question
How can Test cricket remain a premium product while boards depend on short-term T20 franchise revenue—and who enforces this discipline if not the BCCI unilaterally?

## India Angle
India's BCCI has unique power to reshape global Test cricket through strategic positioning, calendar management, and stadium development—but requires moving from rule-taker to steward. This shapes India's soft power and economic opportunity.
