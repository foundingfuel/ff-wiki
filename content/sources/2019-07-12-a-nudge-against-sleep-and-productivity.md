## Core Argument
Tech companies deliberately engineer addiction by competing against sleep to maximize engagement and spending.

## Key Points
- Tech platforms use behavioral nudges and free trials to create habit-forming subscriptions that exploit self-improvement psychology.
- Netflix, Apple, Amazon deploy billions to capture attention by directly competing against sleep and family time.
- Indians face unique vulnerability to these tactics without policy frameworks or public debate on Big Tech regulation.

## Open Question
If self-improvement products cause cancellation guilt that prevents rational decision-making, how can consumers make truly autonomous choices about their subscriptions?

## India Angle
India is the largest market for Big Tech outside the US, with Indians among the most vulnerable to manipulative design patterns. Indian policymakers have not yet engaged with breakup debates happening in the US, leaving Indian consumers unprotected.
