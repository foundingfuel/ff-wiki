## Core Argument
Collaborative economy unlocks underutilized human and physical assets through networks, bypassing traditional institutions.

## Key Points
- On-demand delivery is efficiency, not sharing; true sharing economy requires asset/behavior exchange between peers.
- Professional services marketplaces disrupt traditional institutions by directly connecting skilled individuals with clients.
- Collaborative principles extend beyond consumer goods to finance, logistics, energy, and distributed manufacturing.

## Open Question
How do platforms balance genuine peer-sharing with profit-maximizing commercial entities that extract value without meaningful community participation or asset ownership by providers?

## India Angle
India's gig economy and platform businesses (Ola, Swiggy, OLX) need clearer conceptual frameworks to navigate asset utilization, worker classification, and sustainable value creation beyond pure on-demand delivery models.
