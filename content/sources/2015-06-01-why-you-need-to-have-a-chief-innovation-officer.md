## Core Argument
A chief innovation officer institutionalizes innovation systems when charismatic leaders unavailable to evangelize change.

## Key Points
- Innovation must shift from few individuals to org-wide capability through systems, processes, and culture.
- CIO's core role: creating sustainable organizational systems, not replacing CEO as sole innovator.
- Effective CIOs need enthusiasm, credibility, and influence to sustain innovation through inevitable slow cycles.

## Open Question
How can a dedicated CIO role truly democratize innovation without becoming a bottleneck, especially if the role itself concentrates innovation accountability?

## India Angle
Indian companies struggle with top-down decision-making and lack innovation DNA; a CIO role can help democratize innovation across Indian enterprises while maintaining alignment with market needs and building sustainable capability.
