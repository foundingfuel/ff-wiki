## Core Argument
Banking leaders must prioritize culture over compliance to govern effectively in digital disruption era.

## Key Points
- Culture-first governance creates sustainable risk management better than rules-based legacy approaches in banking.
- Boards must navigate 14 cultural trade-offs consciously—openness vs control, innovation vs conformity—with clear understanding of costs.
- Leadership sets cultural tone; transformation requires holistic, dynamic thinking beyond linear cause-effect relationships.

## Open Question
How can banks balance necessary regulatory compliance with the cultural flexibility needed for digital innovation without creating governance gaps or risk exposure?

## India Angle
Indian banking sector faces radical transformation over next decade; leaders must shift from legacy compliance-driven models to culture-first approaches to build resilient, inclusive institutions suited to digital age and multigenerational workforces.
