## Core Argument
Organizations need separate structures for innovation (loonshots) and operations (franchises) to succeed.

## Key Points
- Loonshots thrive in small, high-stakes teams with intrinsic motivation; franchises need predictability and rank incentives.
- Startups like Spotify and Gaana disrupted industries because they weren't constrained by incumbent franchise structures.
- India produces few globally impactful technologies due to government policies and institutional design discouraging risky innovation.

## Open Question
How can large organizations maintain the structural separation between loonshot and franchise teams while fostering productive idea flow between them?

## India Angle
India lacks globally impactful technology innovations despite talent; government policies and organizational structures inhibit loonshot thinking needed for breakthrough inventions.
