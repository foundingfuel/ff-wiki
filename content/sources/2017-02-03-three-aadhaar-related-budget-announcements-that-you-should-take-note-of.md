## Core Argument
Small policy steps using Aadhaar can unlock digital payments and credit access for India's financially excluded masses.

## Key Points
- BHIM app, Aadhaar Pay, and merchant credit schemes target three layers of digital exclusion in India's economy.
- Aadhaar Pay enables biometric transactions for 350M without smartphones, but faces merchant adoption and authentication failure hurdles.
- Transaction data can replace collateral, helping micro-entrepreneurs access formal credit—if infrastructure and interoperability challenges are solved.

## Open Question
Can government-led digital payment infrastructure overcome operational weaknesses (50% authentication failures, slow merchant POS adoption) and privacy concerns fast enough to genuinely include the excluded?

## India Angle
Budget announcements show India's strategy to use Aadhaar and digital payments to financially include 350M+ people without smartphones or formal banking access—a uniquely Indian challenge given the digital divide and merchant POS adoption gaps.
