## Core Argument
AI excels at incremental improvement but threatens transformational breakthroughs by pulling decisions toward statistical averages.

## Key Points
- AI optimizes around historical data and statistical means, treating deviation as noise rather than potential breakthrough.
- Transformational innovations—printing press, AWS, water sanitation—emerged from edge insights that data couldn't initially justify.
- Organizations must explicitly separate incremental and transformational innovation, reserving capital and governance for non-consensus bets.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically protect and fund deviations when most deviations fail, and AI-driven efficiency creates pressure to eliminate 'inefficient' experiments?

## India Angle
Indian organizations scaling rapidly with AI adoption risk converging toward median performance, losing the deviation-driven breakthroughs needed for competitive advantage in global markets. Leaders must deliberately protect non-consensus bets.
