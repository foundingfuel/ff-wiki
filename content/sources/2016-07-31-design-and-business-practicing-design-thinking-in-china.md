## Core Argument
Design thinking unlocks value chain evolution from OEM supplier to branded innovator by understanding ecosystems, not just products.

## Key Points
- IDEO China evolved from supporting global product prototyping to helping domestic companies build brands and move up value chain.
- Design thinking requires ethnographic research, observation, and empathy—asking 'why' repeatedly to uncover true customer motivations beyond stated preferences.
- Hunter Gatherer case shows designing entire ecosystem—farms, supply chain, retail, business model, staff training—creates sustainable competitive advantage.

## Open Question
How do companies balance the time and cost investment required for deep ethnographic design research against pressure for rapid market entry and ROI, especially in price-sensitive emerging markets?

## India Angle
Indian companies transitioning from low-cost manufacturing to branded, value-added businesses can adopt IDEO's design thinking framework—moving beyond product design to ecosystem redesign. Relevant for Indian startups and legacy manufacturers seeking to build consumer brands and navigate changing customer preferences around quality, safety, and sustainability.
