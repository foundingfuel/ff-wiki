## Core Argument
VC industry deserves nuance; startups and media companies must navigate growth, culture, and business model transitions simultaneously.

## Key Points
- VC backing doesn't program irresponsibility; founders and their choices shape culture at scale
- Indian publishers face existential crisis: newsprint inflation forces urgent digital subscriber model adoption
- Audience retention requires managing pricing, segmentation, and cross-category engagement—not just acquisition

## Open Question
How can Indian media companies and startups build sustainable business models and cultures when facing simultaneous supply shocks, market corrections, and the need to scale?

## India Angle
Indian startups (Paytm, Zomato, Freshworks) facing IPO setbacks and scrutiny on VC governance; Indian newspapers struggling with newsprint costs and digital transition, unlike established global models like Financial Times.
