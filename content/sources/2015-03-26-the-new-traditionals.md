## Core Argument
Family businesses win by mastering core competencies, controlling costs, and partnering strategically—not by copying large competitors.

## Key Points
- Concentrate on core competencies and niches rather than competing directly with larger, better-funded corporate brands.
- Use specialist partnerships and modular ecosystems to scale cheaply rather than building everything in-house.
- Apply ROI discipline to technology adoption; innovation driven by financial constraints often outperforms big-budget corporate experiments.

## Open Question
How can family businesses professionalise and scale without eroding the loyalty, networks, and cultural DNA built over generations that are sources of their competitive advantage?

## India Angle
Indian family businesses must balance professional growth with preserving core competencies and heritage; cost discipline and focus on niches offer competitive advantages against larger corporate players in India's fragmented markets.
