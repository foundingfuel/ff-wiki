## Core Argument
Judicious M&A strategy requires disciplined questioning, strategic clarity, and willingness to divest when value creation ends.

## Key Points
- M&A must serve clear growth ambitions, not be an end in itself; guard against media-driven acquisition frenzy
- Strategic acquisitions demand board-backed conviction to bid without reserve; Nihar deal required full commitment despite competitive risk
- Successful integration requires post-acquisition capability—overhaul operations, extract synergies, and know when to exit for value realization

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the conviction needed to win strategic acquisitions (Nihar at full value) with the discipline to avoid overpaying in competitive bidding situations?
