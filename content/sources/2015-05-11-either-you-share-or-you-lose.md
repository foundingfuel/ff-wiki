## Core Argument
Sharing economy enables Indian entrepreneurs to access resources at fractional cost, disrupting legacy businesses and consolidating underutilized infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Sharing leverages unutilized assets and infrastructure, reducing entry barriers for startups and micro-entrepreneurs in India.
- Generational mindset shift away from ownership stigma expands addressable markets for brands through fractional-cost access.
- Requires unpacking existing business models to identify shareable resources—transforming cost structures and competitive positioning.

## Open Question
Does sharing economy truly democratize entrepreneurship, or does it primarily benefit those with existing assets to share and technology access?

## India Angle
Sharing economy addresses India's high setup costs, fragmented infrastructure, and regulatory burden—enabling micro-entrepreneurs and asset consolidation in a cost-constrained market where shared access beats ownership.
