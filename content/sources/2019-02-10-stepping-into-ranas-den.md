## Core Argument
New CEO must dismantle founder's centralized control to rebuild trust and institutional stability amid regulatory scrutiny.

## Key Points
- Ravneet Singh Gill inherits severely compromised bank with halved stock price and disputed NPA assessments.
- Rana Kapoor's hyper-centralized, WhatsApp-driven leadership created organizational paralysis and accountability gaps.
- Founder retains largest shareholding and potential board seat despite being forced to resign as CEO—structural ambiguity threatens turnaround.

## Open Question
How can a new professional CEO credibly lead institutional transformation when the deposed founder-promoter retains majority shareholding and potential board representation?

## India Angle
YES Bank's institutional crisis reflects deeper governance challenges in Indian banking—centralized leadership, RBI regulatory tensions, and founder control dynamics that require transformation for sustainable private sector banking.
