## Core Argument
Systematize newsletter consumption through separate infrastructure, curated tools, and deliberate habits to reclaim focus.

## Key Points
- Unbundling newsletters into separate email IDs and clients reduces cognitive load and prevents inbox competition.
- Tools like Stoop and Unibox sort newsletters by sender/category, enabling better discovery and catch-up on missed editions.
- Intentional habits—inbox splitting, time-blocking, selective subscriptions—are essential since no system can consume all newsletters.

## Open Question
How do professionals balance staying informed across multiple quality sources without newsletters becoming a distraction from their primary work?
