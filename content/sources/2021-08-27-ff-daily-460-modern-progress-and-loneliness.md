## Core Argument
Modern progress paradoxically intensifies human loneliness and disconnection despite technological advances enabling connection.

## Key Points
- Technology enables convenience but erodes face-to-face interaction and community bonds essential for human wellbeing.
- India's healthcare underinvestment (under 1% GDP) forces families into poverty; individual district-level solutions prove systemic gaps exist.
- EarthEnable's flaxseed resin innovation shows entrepreneurial design can solve extreme poverty sustainably, mimicking ground-level problem-solving needed in India.

## Open Question
How do societies harness technology's connective potential while restoring the communal structures and physical spaces that humans fundamentally need for wellbeing?

## India Angle
India's healthcare crisis (less than 1% GDP spending vs China's 3%) and dirt floor poverty (75% in Rwanda parallels Indian rural conditions) reveal systemic failures where modern progress paradoxically worsens human connection and wellbeing; individual initiatives like Dr Bharud's oxygen units expose gaps in state capacity.
