## Core Argument
Indian institutions must prioritize generational renewal over incumbent comfort to unlock untapped talent and competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Rohit Sharma's willingness to step aside from T-20 contrasts with India's typical pattern of leaders prolonging tenures
- High bench strength (Jaiswal, Rinko Singh, Ravindran) ready for opportunity, but blocked by extended careers of aging players
- Format-specific teams and workload management require systemic restructuring, not just individual decisions

## Open Question
How do organizations balance respecting institutional knowledge and stability from senior leaders with creating genuine opportunities for younger talent to develop at the highest level?

## India Angle
Indian leadership across cricket, politics, and business struggles with generational transitions; incumbents rarely cede power voluntarily. Cricket offers a case study for how to manage succession planning and develop next-gen talent at scale.
