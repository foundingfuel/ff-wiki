## Core Argument
Effective coaches enable self-discovery through listening and questioning, not expertise or directives.

## Key Points
- Coaches create awareness by helping people examine fossilized beliefs and expand choice of actions.
- Domain expertise and star status don't guarantee coaching effectiveness; different skillset required.
- Deep listening, empathic questioning, and holding space without interference drive coachee ownership.

## Open Question
How do coaches maintain credibility and contextual understanding while deliberately staying outside domain expertise and avoiding directive advice?

## India Angle
Indian cricket team's new head coach appointment (Anil Kumble) highlights how coaching principles apply to high-pressure Indian institutions; reveals gaps between star talent and effective leadership in Indian contexts.
