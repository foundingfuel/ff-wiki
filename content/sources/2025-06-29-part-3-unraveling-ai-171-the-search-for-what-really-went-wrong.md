## Core Argument
Understanding aircraft failures requires connecting cascading cause-and-effect chains, not isolated clues, then testing through simulation.

## Key Points
- Multiple evidence-based hypotheses explain how technical, mechanical, and human factors interact in catastrophic failures.
- High-fidelity simulators test failure chains against real data to determine which sequence matches actual aircraft behavior.
- No single clue solves the puzzle; truth emerges from proving how one failure triggered the next in sequence.

## Open Question
How can investigators distinguish between correlation and causation when multiple system failures occur simultaneously and interact in complex ways?

## India Angle
India's aviation safety infrastructure and crash investigation capabilities (AAIB) are tested by complex modern aircraft failures; relevant for building indigenous technical expertise and regulatory credibility.
