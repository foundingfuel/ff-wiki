## Core Argument
Data quality determines policy outcomes; garbage methodology produces precise but wrong answers regardless of scale.

## Key Points
- Miscalibrated instruments and flawed measurement methods produce confidently wrong results for over a century.
- Indian election forecasting reveals significant voter shifts (gender, regional) only visible through rigorous survey data.
- High-stakes data like unemployment lack real-time accuracy checks; election forecasts uniquely self-correct on result day.

## Open Question
If surveys capture human dishonesty and measurement errors persist invisibly across domains, how do we validate data-driven policy decisions beyond electoral forecasting?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers, election forecasters, and development institutions must grapple with data quality and measurement integrity—critical for electoral predictions, health policy, and governance decisions affecting 1.4 billion people.
