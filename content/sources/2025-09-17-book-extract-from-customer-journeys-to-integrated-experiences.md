## Core Argument
Customer experience integration shifts power from brands to retailers who own decision-making moments.

## Key Points
- Real customers don't follow linear journeys; they respond to friction points and impulses unpredictably.
- Sephora's Color IQ solves high-friction decisions early, building trust and data to orchestrate entire experience ecosystem.
- Power has shifted from brand loyalty to owning customer choice; retailers controlling decision moments become kingmakers, not just distributors.

## Open Question
How can independent beauty brands maintain equity and customer loyalty when retailers controlling the customer interface also become kingmakers deciding which brands survive?

## India Angle
Indian beauty retailers and brands must recognize the shift from product-centric to experience-centric models; companies like Nykaa are already moving toward this, but most traditional beauty brands remain vulnerable to retailer control if they don't build direct customer relationships and owned ecosystems.
