## Core Argument
Cholas offer cultural and intellectual riches beyond cinema—accessible through curated learning pathways for armchair, amateur, and scholarly exploration.

## Key Points
- Cholas ruled for over a thousand years, spanning South India and Indian Ocean trade routes with unmatched naval prowess.
- Their temples, bronzes, administrative systems like Kudavolai, and artistic achievements remain functional and studied by modern scholars.
- Multiple engagement levels—reading, watching, museum visits, temple tourism—allow Indians to deepen understanding of this heritage.

## Open Question
How can India balance popularizing historical heritage through mass entertainment (Mani Ratnam films) with rigorous scholarship, ensuring depth isn't lost in accessibility?
