## Core Argument
History shows transformative technologies need regulatory clarity and commercial legalization to unlock economic potential.

## Key Points
- Legalizing commercial internet use in 1993 catalyzed the economic boom; similar regulatory clarity needed for Aadhaar
- Facial recognition's imperfections and privacy risks require public experience-building before deployment, not after
- Intuition and rationality must coexist; data abundance alone doesn't replicate human judgment capability

## Open Question
How can India enable digital innovation (Aadhaar, digital commerce) while genuinely protecting privacy when regulatory frameworks remain ambiguous and technology risks are incompletely understood by the public?

## India Angle
Aadhaar's regulatory journey mirrors internet's early commercial struggles; India must balance innovation enablement with privacy safeguards as digital infrastructure scales
