## Core Argument
Crisis leadership requires picking what matters most, then building systems to respond decisively.

## Key Points
- Selective focus: choose what's essential, discard distractions—applies to crisis decisions and daily work.
- Accountability over self-interest: leaders must sacrifice personal gain to maintain shareholder and stakeholder trust.
- Scenario planning with trigger points: prepare multiple futures and predetermined responses, not reactive crisis management.

## Open Question
How do leaders maintain focus on fundamentals when crisis demands constant pivoting and scenario recalculation?

## India Angle
Indian leaders navigating crisis (like ICICI Bank's 2008 experience) must balance stakeholder trust with long-term organizational resilience—critical in a market where reputation erosion directly impacts talent and capital access.
