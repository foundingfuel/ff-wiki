## Core Argument
Embracing discomfort through exposure to diverse perspectives drives personal and intellectual growth.

## Key Points
- True learning requires stepping outside comfort zones and listening to unfamiliar viewpoints.
- Narratives and stories have moral responsibility; they should illuminate contemporary truths, not claim absolute answers.
- Diversity of thought—across disciplines and ideologies—creates richer understanding than singular frameworks.

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically create space for discomfort and diverse perspectives without becoming paralyzed by relativism or loss of conviction?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders increasingly face disruption and uncertainty; cultivating comfort with discomfort—and intellectual humility—is essential for navigating rapid change and building inclusive organizations
