## Core Argument
Business model innovation, not strategy alone, determines future success in disrupted markets.

## Key Points
- All businesses must create, deliver, and capture value; most focus only on capturing value.
- Successful innovations combine business model shifts with multiple innovation types, creating new competitive categories.
- Business Model Canvas and Lean Canvas are practical tools to design, test, and iterate business models.

## Open Question
If business models are critical to success, why do most organizations still prioritize strategy over systematic business model innovation and redesign?

## India Angle
Indian businesses like Aravind Eye Hospitals, InterviewStreet, Zipdial, and Freecharge demonstrate that innovative business models—not just strategy—drive competitive advantage. Indian entrepreneurs must prioritize business model innovation to compete globally and disrupt their markets.
