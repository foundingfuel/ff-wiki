## Core Argument
Distilling pathogens to essential triggers mirrors biological simplicity; DNA vaccines could democratize vaccine access globally.

## Key Points
- Immune training works by triggering response to minimal pathogen features, not full pathogens.
- DNA vaccines represent ultimate simplification: inject genetic code, let body produce antibodies.
- Vaccine affordability and speed matter morally when healthcare systems face exponential demand.

## Open Question
How do we balance vaccine development speed with safety testing and equitable global distribution when the disease spreads exponentially?

## India Angle
India's vaccine manufacturing capacity and ability to scale production cheaply is critical for global immunisation; DNA vaccine approach could make vaccines affordable for India's large population if development accelerates.
