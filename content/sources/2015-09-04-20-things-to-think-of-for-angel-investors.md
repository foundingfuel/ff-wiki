## Core Argument
Angel investing succeeds through founder obsession, patient capital, and mentorship—not financial returns or portfolio spray-and-pray strategies.

## Key Points
- Angel returns rarely beat Indian small-caps; invest only money you can afford to lose completely.
- Commit time alongside capital; involvement transforms learning for both investor and founder through the journey.
- Back founders unflinchingly through misalignment, failure, and personal crises—your role is ecosystem support, not exit optimization.

## Open Question
If angel investors should prioritize founder support over financial returns, and most won't beat market benchmarks, what sustains angel investing as a viable activity beyond ego and hobby?

## India Angle
Angel investing in India faces unique challenges: high infrastructure costs, dollar-denominated salaries against rupee-based customer payments, and a nascent ecosystem requiring patient capital and founder mentorship beyond Western models.
