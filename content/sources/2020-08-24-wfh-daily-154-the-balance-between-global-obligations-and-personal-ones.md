## Core Argument
Balance global obligations and personal duties through rooted cosmopolitanism—serving humanity while honoring close relationships.

## Key Points
- Rooted cosmopolitanism acknowledges duties to strangers and special obligations to those close to us simultaneously.
- Forgiveness and reconciliation are distinct processes; understanding this difference enables healing even without relationship restoration.
- Crisis moments force prioritization choices between professional duty and personal protection—both valid.

## Open Question
How do individuals ethically resolve conflicting obligations when crisis forces an either-or choice between duty to strangers and protection of loved ones?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating dual obligations—career ambitions and family/community responsibilities—amid rapid urbanization and global work opportunities. Relevant to understanding how Indian talent balances cosmopolitan aspirations with rooted values.
