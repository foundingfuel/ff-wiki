## Core Argument
Government power lies in rule-setting, not budget size; effective state intervention requires capability, not ideology.

## Key Points
- East Asian 'miracle' economies used small budgets but massive regulatory power to drive growth.
- Data gaps obscure reality: Africa's true pandemic toll emerged only through unconventional measurement methods.
- Writing disciplines thinking; untested ideas lack precision and completeness regardless of confidence.

## Open Question
How can governments exercise powerful rule-setting influence responsibly when many governments lack the capability or integrity to deploy such power effectively?

## India Angle
India's policymakers must recognize that government influence extends beyond budget size—through regulation and planning—as demonstrated by East Asian success. India's own premature pandemic declarations reflect the risk of misreading data; stronger institutional capacity for data collection and interpretation is critical for effective state intervention.
