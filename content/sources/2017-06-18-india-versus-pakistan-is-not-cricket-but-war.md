## Core Argument
India-Pakistan cricket is weaponized as proxy war, revealing society's inability to embrace nuance and moral ambiguity.

## Key Points
- Cricket match framed as war, players as gladiators, losing teams face real violence and social ostracism
- Colonial history of British divide-and-rule continues shaping brown nations' enmity while colonizers watch amused
- Modern discourse demands absolute patriotic stance; questioning national binary labeled anti-national, suppressing dissent

## Open Question
How can individuals in polarized societies maintain moral integrity when institutions and social pressure demand binary patriotic declarations?
