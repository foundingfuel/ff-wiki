## Core Argument
Leaders need unwavering faith paired with brutal facts; systems thinking prevents narrow solutions from damaging interconnected ecosystems.

## Key Points
- Churchill created separate Statistical Office to receive unfiltered brutal facts, enabling sound decisions despite charisma that might distort information flow.
- Systems thinking reveals how specialised solutions—fertilisers, digital tech, agricultural reforms—create unintended consequences in other parts of interconnected systems.
- Indian farm reforms risk harming small farmers without cooperative structures; Bihar's APMC removal shows open markets don't guarantee fair prices without systemic safeguards.

## Open Question
How can India's agricultural reforms empower small farmers while preventing corporate capture, when cooperative structures remain nascent and Bihar's cautionary example shows open markets alone don't ensure fair prices?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and policymakers must balance unwavering faith with brutal fact-confrontation, especially during crises like COVID-19. Agricultural reforms expose systemic tensions: market distortions, MSP dependencies, and small farmer vulnerability require systems thinking to avoid unintended consequences across interconnected value chains.
