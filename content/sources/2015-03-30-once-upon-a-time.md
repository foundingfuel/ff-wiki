## Core Argument
Business storytelling creates neural coupling that makes audiences anticipate messages, making it essential for leaders driving change.

## Key Points
- Stories trigger speaker-listener neural synchronization, enabling audiences to anticipate and remember messages better than data-driven approaches.
- Connection, clarity, influence and success stories serve different leadership purposes—from building rapport to overcoming resistance to change.
- Storytelling is a learnable skill and competitive advantage for leaders in sales, product, strategy communication and organisational transformation.

## Open Question
If storytelling is so powerful for connecting and influencing, how do leaders distinguish between authentic narrative and manipulative persuasion, especially when driving unpopular organisational changes?

## India Angle
Indian leaders across startups, corporates and government often struggle with articulating vision and driving change; storytelling offers a practical, culturally-resonant tool that bridges hierarchical communication gaps common in Indian organisations.
