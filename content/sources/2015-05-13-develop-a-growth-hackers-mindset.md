## Core Argument
Growth hacking is a mindset prioritizing scalable user acquisition over traditional marketing spend.

## Key Points
- Create awesome products delivering memorable experiences; users become organic advocates with 70-92% believability.
- Leverage network effects, scarcity, ecosystem lock-in, and behavioral incentives to drive growth at minimal cost.
- Growth hacking is industry-agnostic and mindset-based, applicable to tech, telecom, retail, and hardware companies alike.

## Open Question
If growth hacking relies on word-of-mouth and organic adoption, how do early-stage companies with unknown products bootstrap the initial user base to reach critical mass?

## India Angle
Indian startups and established companies like Reliance Communications can adopt growth hacking to compete globally without heavy marketing budgets—critical for resource-constrained Indian market.
