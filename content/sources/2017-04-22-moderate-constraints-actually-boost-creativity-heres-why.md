## Core Argument
Constraints paradoxically unlock creativity by forcing focus, discipline, and resourcefulness.

## Key Points
- Hemingway's six-word story demonstrates how tight constraints produce powerful creative output.
- Jugaad (Indian frugal engineering) embodies constraint-driven innovation: doing more for less with passion.
- Artificial self-imposed constraints—sleep time, work hours, material minimalism—sharpen focus and unlock deeper thinking.

## Open Question
Can artificially imposed constraints on children build character and resourcefulness, or do they breed resentment and damage relationships—as Munger suggests?

## India Angle
Jugaad—doing more with less—is embedded in Indian psyche and business practice. The article reframes this as a universal principle of constrained creativity, validating India's informal systems and frugal engineering as legitimate innovation frameworks, not just survival tactics.
