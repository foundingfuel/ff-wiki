## Core Argument
Democratic systems fail when electoral cycles and media attention spans prevent long-term deliberation on existential challenges.

## Key Points
- Elected governments prioritize next election over long-term policy solutions like climate change.
- Media fragmentation—from TRP-driven TV to social media—has collapsed public discourse into shallow cycles.
- Non-partisan citizen deliberation forums (like Think Long Committee) can bypass electoral gridlock and design systemic solutions.

## Open Question
How can democracies maintain both representative legitimacy and the institutional capacity for long-term thinking when electoral cycles and media economics push toward short-termism?

## India Angle
India's elected politicians and media are trapped in short-term cycles, failing to deliberate on long-term challenges like climate change. India needs non-partisan citizen forums like California's Think Long Committee to drive systemic thinking beyond electoral cycles.
