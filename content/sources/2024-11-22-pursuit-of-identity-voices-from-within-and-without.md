## Core Argument
Cinema reveals humanity's universal quest to reconcile imposed identity labels with authentic self-expression across contexts.

## Key Points
- MAMI Festival democratized access by moving from scattered venues to compact, community-focused locations with budget constraints.
- Curators strategically programmed films exploring gender fluidity, belonging versus fitting in, and identity formation across cultures.
- Digital spaces enable alternative identities and belonging that may be unavailable in physical reality, particularly for marginalized individuals.

## Open Question
How do we honor transformative online identities and communities while addressing parental concerns about internet dependency and authentic human connection?

## India Angle
Indian audiences have limited access to global cinema; MAMI provides critical window to understand emerging identity narratives shaping world culture and artistic expression.
