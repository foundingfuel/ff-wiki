## Core Argument
Mathematical models and Big Data risk obscuring human reality; wisdom traditions offer grounding absent in purely quantitative analysis.

## Key Points
- Mathematics cannot explain everything; overconfidence in quantifiable metrics blinds policymakers to unmeasurable forces like social trust.
- Economists model complex systems like physicists but ignore underlying assumptions and real-world context in pursuit of mathematical elegance.
- Indian job data contradicts economic models; anecdotal youth unemployment evidence reveals gap between measured and lived economic reality.

## Open Question
If mathematical rigor obscures truth, how do we build reliable policy frameworks without abandoning quantitative tools entirely?
