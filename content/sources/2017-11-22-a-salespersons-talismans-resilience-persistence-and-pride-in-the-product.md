## Core Argument
Salespeople must genuinely believe in and take pride in their product; without it, they cannot authentically serve customers.

## Key Points
- A barman's fierce pride in hotel values and product quality transforms a customer interaction into a lesson on integrity
- True product belief comes from understanding organizational purpose and living its values consistently, regardless of external circumstances
- Personal resilience and persistence through adversity reveal character and commitment to serving others with dignity

## Open Question
How do you cultivate genuine pride in a product across an organization when many employees are transactional—working for survival rather than mission?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and salespeople can learn from global service excellence standards; Bagchi's reflection on pride in product applies to building competitive Indian businesses that compete on quality and values, not just cost.
