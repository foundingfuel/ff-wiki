## Core Argument
Distinguish competency from integrity issues; self-penalize deliberately unethical decisions to preserve long-term reputation.

## Key Points
- Competency issues (honest mistakes) warrant disclosure and apology; integrity issues demand self-penalty.
- Public disclosure, unconditional apology, and self-imposed penalties prevent worse legal consequences.
- Airbnb's proactive accountability despite short-term costs led to unicorn status and stakeholder trust.

## Open Question
How does a company credibly self-penalize while maintaining operational viability and shareholder trust, especially in India's litigious environment?

## India Angle
Indian companies like Cadbury India, PepsiCo, Coca-Cola, and Nestle India have faced major reputation crises; guidance on managing integrity vs. competency issues is directly applicable to Indian corporate governance and consumer trust.
