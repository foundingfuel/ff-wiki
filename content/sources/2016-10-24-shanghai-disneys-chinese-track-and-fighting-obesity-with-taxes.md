## Core Argument
MNCs succeed in emerging markets by embedding local culture into global strategy, not transplanting Western formulas unchanged.

## Key Points
- Disney's 'glocal' strategy—authentically Disney yet distinctly Chinese—learned from failures in Paris and Hong Kong.
- Local competitor Wanda leverages cultural nationalism and aggressive pricing to challenge Disney's $5.5B investment.
- Customer experience and service quality may differentiate Disney, but cultural resonance remains the core competitive battleground.

## Open Question
Can superior customer experience and hygiene standards overcome a local competitor's cultural legitimacy advantage and price leadership in an emerging market?

## India Angle
Indian companies expanding globally must balance global brand standards with deep local cultural understanding; local competitors often win by emphasizing cultural pride and price—a pattern relevant to Indian startups competing against MNCs in their home market.
