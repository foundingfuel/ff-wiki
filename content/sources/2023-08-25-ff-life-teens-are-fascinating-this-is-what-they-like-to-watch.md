## Core Argument
Gen Z's streaming choices reveal their fascination with dystopian narratives, power dynamics, and technology's dark potential.

## Key Points
- Gen Z gravitates toward shows exploring systemic power imbalances, morality, and social structures through nuanced storytelling.
- Preference for sci-fi and dystopian genres suggests teen awareness of technology's capacity for harm, not just progress.
- Shows with flawed characters, cliffhangers, and ambiguity resonate more than traditional narratives with clear resolutions.

## Open Question
Do teens watch these shows to critically interrogate systems, or do they serve as escapist catharsis that actually depoliticizes dissent?

## India Angle
Reveals how Indian Gen Z (consuming content via JioCinema, Netflix, Crunchyroll) engage with global narratives; signals shift in Indian youth media consumption patterns and what entertainment choices signal about their worldview and aspirations.
