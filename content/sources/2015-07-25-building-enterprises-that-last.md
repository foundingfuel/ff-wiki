## Core Argument
Building lasting enterprises requires founders to balance scaling ambition with product-led differentiation and culture, not just capital.

## Key Points
- Professional CEOs help when founders have real competency gaps; blanket replacement isn't a universal playbook.
- Founders must remain paranoid about disruption and avoid confusing fundraising success with business success.
- Efficient capital use and sustainable customer franchises matter more than burning money chasing blind scale.

## Open Question
How can Indian entrepreneurs balance the pressure to raise capital and scale aggressively when competitors are doing so, without sacrificing sustainable product differentiation and unit economics?

## India Angle
India's entrepreneurial ecosystem is maturing but lacks the talent pool and institutional playbooks of the US. Founders must navigate scaling without simply copying Western models of professional CEO replacement; India needs home-grown solutions for building durable enterprises.
