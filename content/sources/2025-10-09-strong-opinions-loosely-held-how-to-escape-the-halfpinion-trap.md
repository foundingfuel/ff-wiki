## Core Argument
Leaders trapped in halfpinions lose credibility; diverse perspectives and willingness to change minds are essential.

## Key Points
- Halfpinions—one-sided opinions ignoring contrary facts—flourish in algorithm-driven, polarized information ecosystems.
- Echo chambers and confirmation bias create illusion of certainty while distorting reality, impairing strategic decisions.
- Four habits—diverse sources, relationships, courage to change minds, and intellectual humility—counter halfpinions at scale.

## Open Question
How can leaders cultivate genuine intellectual humility and perspective-taking when organizational culture, algorithms, and tribal identity all reward certainty and quick judgment?

## India Angle
Indian leaders navigating WhatsApp-driven information ecosystems and polarized organizational cultures must actively counter halfpinions to maintain credibility and execution in fractured stakeholder environments.
