## Core Argument
Innovation requires both technical expertise and humanistic empathy; storytelling merged with data drives organizational transformation.

## Key Points
- Liberal arts 'fuzzies' provide empathy and human insight that techies lack; both needed for innovation
- Internal politics and human nature, not technology, block digital transformation in organizations
- Narratives connected to numbers create compelling business valuations; stories without data are empty

## Open Question
If empathy and liberal arts are critical to innovation, why do tech-driven organizations continue privileging technical expertise over humanistic skills when hiring and promoting leaders?

## India Angle
Indian politicians' decision-making logic and corruption narratives directly shape policy environment for businesses; understanding political mind essential for navigating reforms and digital initiatives in India's context.
