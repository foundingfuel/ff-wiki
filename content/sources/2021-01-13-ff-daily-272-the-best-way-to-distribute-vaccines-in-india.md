## Core Argument
India should prioritize unvaccinated populations by screening for prior infection to stretch scarce vaccine doses.

## Key Points
- 50-70% of Indians may have natural immunity, reducing vaccination need significantly
- Antibody testing before vaccination can spare doses for truly vulnerable uninfected populations
- Evidence-based allocation solves demand-supply gap during vaccine shortage period

## Open Question
How reliable are self-reported infection history and commercial serological tests at scale in India's diverse, informal healthcare landscape, and what are equity implications of excluding previously infected populations?

## India Angle
India faces acute vaccine scarcity; leveraging natural immunity data from serological testing could optimize limited doses for truly vulnerable populations, addressing equity and resource constraints specific to India's scale and diversity.
