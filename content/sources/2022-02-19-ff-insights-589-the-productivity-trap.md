## Core Argument
Productivity obsession creates anxiety spirals; serialising work and tolerating postponement reduces both.

## Key Points
- Productivity optimization paradoxically accelerates rushing; focus on one major project at a time instead.
- Accept anxiety about postponed tasks rather than starting everything simultaneously to ease discomfort.
- India's para sports ecosystem lacks stipends and policy recognition despite athlete achievement, limiting career transitions.

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers in high-pressure environments (especially India's startup culture) practically implement single-focus serialisation when organisational expectations demand constant context-switching?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organisations often face acute pressure to do more with less; Burkeman's serialisation principle challenges the hustle culture prevalent in Indian startups and corporate environments, while the disability inclusion story highlights India's policy gaps in para sports and workplace accessibility.
