## Core Argument
Living wills clarify autonomy and protect families from defaulting to technology-driven suffering without meaning.

## Key Points
- ICU creates emotional vortex; families decide without medical literacy or bandwidth while under financial and psychological stress.
- Doctors can predict last months but rarely prepare patients; patients seek orientation and closure, not false reassurance.
- Advance care planning is only exit from being trapped between biology and medical technology escalation.

## Open Question
How do you balance patient autonomy with relational decision-making rooted in family consent, especially in India's collectivist cultures where individual choices are rarely truly independent?

## India Angle
India's 2023 Supreme Court guidelines on living wills simplify end-of-life decision-making, but implementation requires cultural shift in how families, doctors communicate about death—rare in Indian medical and family contexts.
