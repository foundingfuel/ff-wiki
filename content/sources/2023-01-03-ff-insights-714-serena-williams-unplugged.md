## Core Argument
Systems need diversity and variability to evolve; forcing singular choices, especially for women, stifles resilience.

## Key Points
- Diversity—biological, cultural, social—is essential fuel for long-term system evolution and resilience
- Women athletes shouldn't have to choose between excellence and family; this forced binary reflects systemic unfairness
- Suppressing experimentation and variability makes systems brittle; only adaptive, diverse systems survive volatility

## Open Question
How do we preserve system resilience through diversity without losing the accountability and direction that some forms of control provide?

## India Angle
India's emerging female workforce and women entrepreneurs face similar systemic pressures around balancing ambition with family expectations; Williams' critique of unfair choices for women applies to Indian women in leadership and competitive fields
