## Core Argument
Techies and institutions must turn analytical lens inward to recognize amoral mental frames and social externalities.

## Key Points
- Tech companies study customers but rarely examine their own tribal patterns and mental frames that enable harm.
- Mere sector growth doesn't translate to worker benefits; labour share fell despite GVA growth in India.
- Disinformation-for-hire is a boom industry with India prominently featured as both buyer and victim of campaigns.

## Open Question
If design thinking and anthropology are mainstream business tools, why do tech companies and economists still operate in tunnel vision about their own systemic impacts?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies and government institutions need to adopt anthropological thinking to address systemic blindness about social impacts, worker welfare, and disinformation—critical for building sustainable institutions and inclusive growth.
