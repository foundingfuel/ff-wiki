## Core Argument
Reliance's centralized, results-obsessed culture is undermining its 4G project execution despite project management expertise.

## Key Points
- Top-down decision-making and lack of local empowerment slowed fiber rollout and tower placement across India
- Managing 100+ vendors independently without systems integrator proved harder than managing Bechtel partnership on refineries
- Culture change attempts (Google-inspired walls, employee initiatives) remain superficial; fundamental command-control model unchanged

## Open Question
How can an organization maintain the discipline and accountability that drove past project successes while adopting the empowerment and trust needed for complex, distributed execution in modern telecom?

## India Angle
Reliance's struggle with 4G launch reveals how India's top conglomerates must balance command-and-control cultures with modern organizational needs; also exposes infrastructure challenges (right-of-way, municipal barriers) specific to India's telecom rollout
