## Core Argument
Post-pandemic fashion is moving from athleisure excess to sustainable, functional classics with authentic craftsmanship.

## Key Points
- Societal shifts in economy, weather and neighbourhood drive longer-lasting trends over fleeting Instagram fads.
- Sustainable materials and transparency of manufacture are now non-negotiable; greenwashing by fast fashion brands must be avoided.
- Capsule wardrobes and versatile layering reduce consumption while enabling personal style expression through smart mixing.

## Open Question
How do consumers distinguish genuine sustainability from marketing-driven greenwashing when most fast fashion brands now claim eco-credentials?

## India Angle
Indian fashion consumers are shifting toward sustainable, locally-made pieces (Hidesign, Monks of Method) and island-lifestyle aesthetics tied to Indian geography (French Pondicherry, Indiranagar), reflecting broader consumer maturity post-pandemic and growing awareness of greenwashing in fast fashion.
