## Core Argument
Privacy requires constant vigilance and small trade-offs; most convenience-for-privacy exchanges are unnecessary.

## Key Points
- Personal data is publicly exposed via unsecured databases in Indian organizations; search for your name to discover what's leaked.
- App permissions are routinely misused for targeted advertising; decline blanket access and grant only context-specific permissions.
- Privacy protection doesn't demand sacrificing convenience; use tools like ad-blockers and manual data entry to retain both.

## Open Question
How do users balance the network effects of popular apps (WhatsApp) against privacy risks when switching to private alternatives isolates them from contacts?

## India Angle
Indian organizations and government departments lag in data security practices, making citizens vulnerable to identity theft and misuse; practical privacy guidance for Indian users of WhatsApp, Cred, and other apps widely used locally.
