## Core Argument
Crisis response requires immediate compassion first; systemic reform must recoup economy with humanity and technology with ethics.

## Key Points
- Immediate humanitarian response must precede political or ideological debate during crises.
- India's post-independence choice of centralized, top-down development rejected decentralized worker-ownership models.
- Pandemic revealed importance of local engagement, transparent communication, and long-term investments in people and institutions.

## Open Question
How can India transition from entrenched centralized economic structures to decentralized, bottom-up models without destabilizing existing institutions and power dynamics?

## India Angle
India must choose between top-down Nehruvian economic models and bottom-up, decentralized approaches rooted in Gandhian and Kumurappa's vision—critical for inclusive wealth creation and worker agency in post-pandemic recovery.
