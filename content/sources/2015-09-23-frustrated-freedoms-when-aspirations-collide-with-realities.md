## Core Argument
Rising aspirations colliding with unfair opportunity structures create social instability that undermines economic growth sustainability.

## Key Points
- Frustrated freedom—when agency exceeds opportunity—fuels anger at systemic unfairness and violence against established order.
- India's 'Bollyworld' scenario shows glamour and growth coexisting with rising violence, creating unstable tension that risks declining to lower growth rates.
- Inequality gaps widen as capital-rich seize opportunities while less-resourced struggle; perception of unfairness erodes institutional trust and citizen confidence.

## Open Question
How can policymakers address fairness perceptions and opportunity distribution without sacrificing growth, when redistribution alone provides minimal relief and inequality metrics remain contested?

## India Angle
India's rapid economic growth masks widening inequality and concentrated opportunity, creating frustrated aspirations particularly among rural youth, Patels in Gujarat, and urban migrants—threatening institutional stability and political cohesion essential for sustained development.
