## Core Argument
Sustained innovation requires executive sponsorship, storytelling, execution focus, and strategic alignment over ideation alone.

## Key Points
- Tata Innovista engages 30,000 people across 65 companies in decade-long innovation momentum through structured programs.
- Stories overcome fear of failure; focus shifts from individual innovators to collaborative execution and delivery struggles.
- Strategic alignment ('compass over map') ensures innovations drive business impact, not just incremental change.

## Open Question
How do you center stories around individuals while emphasizing innovations are collective efforts, and can group-wide platforms truly harness diversity of competencies without diluting focus?

## India Angle
The Tata Group's innovation infrastructure provides a model for how large Indian conglomerates can sustain entrepreneurial culture across diverse companies and geographies, critical for competing globally while maintaining strategic coherence.
