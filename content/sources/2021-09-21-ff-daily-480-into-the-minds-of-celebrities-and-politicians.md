## Core Argument
Building high-performing teams requires psychological safety, candid disagreement, and reframing conflict as essential to innovation.

## Key Points
- Resilience and bouncing back: Amitabh Bachchan's comeback narrative challenges premature dismissals and demonstrates value of persistence.
- Psychological safety enables diverse teams: Explicit acknowledgment of potential offense combined with non-judgmental framing creates space for authentic voices.
- STEM inspiration through storytelling: Early exposure via role models and hands-on learning builds critical thinking rather than just technical skills.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need for psychological safety with accountability when disagreements or offenses occur, especially in high-stakes defense and policy environments?
