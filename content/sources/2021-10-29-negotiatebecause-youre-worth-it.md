## Core Argument
Women's negotiation skills and confidence directly determine lifetime earnings; preparation and likability are equally critical.

## Key Points
- Women forgo $650K-$1M over 45 years by not negotiating starting salary; preparation with clear justification is foundational.
- Negotiation framed as 'chore' rather than 'game' reduces likelihood women negotiate; mindset shift needed.
- Balance self-advocacy with likability by understanding counterparty's interests and proposing holistic deal, not serial demands.

## Open Question
How can women assertively negotiate without triggering social backlash ('bossy,' 'aggressive') that men rarely face?

## India Angle
Indian women face similar wage negotiation gaps and cultural stigma around self-advocacy; addressing these patterns could significantly narrow India's gender pay gap and improve women's lifetime earnings
