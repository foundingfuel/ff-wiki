## Core Argument
Building trusted edtech requires user-centric design, outcome focus, and cultivating growth mindsets in entrepreneurs and learners.

## Key Points
- Work backwards from employer needs and labour data to build credible education programmes with proven employment outcomes.
- One-on-one mentoring and real-world capstone projects create portfolio evidence employers value over credentials alone.
- Growth mindset—viewing challenges as learning opportunities—is essential for Indian entrepreneurs to unlearn fixed mindset conditioning.

## Open Question
How can edtech platforms scale personalised mentorship and internship-quality experiences to millions of learners without losing the one-on-one trust-building that drives employment outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and professionals must unlearn fixed mindsets ingrained by education systems to compete globally; edtech is critical infrastructure for India's workforce reskilling as job tenures shorten and skill obsolescence accelerates.
