## Core Argument
Craftsmanship in knowledge work generates meaning equal to physical trades; success redefined beyond material accumulation.

## Key Points
- Code, writing, consulting—all knowledge work can embody craftsmanship if approached with care and skill development.
- Imposing uniformity (one language, one metric of success) destroys cultural diversity and local meaning—evidenced by partition.
- True success is autonomy, relationships, rest, and contribution—not wealth or status signaling.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale while preserving individual craftsmanship and meaning-making in knowledge work without creating hierarchies or burnout?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and organizations must recognize craftsmanship in digital/information work; language policy debates show risks of imposing homogeneity on diverse contexts—applicable to organizational culture and national policy.
