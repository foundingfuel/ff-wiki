## Core Argument
Faith requires surrendering preconceptions to truth; belief clings to predetermined outcomes for security.

## Key Points
- Belief insists truth match preconceived wishes; faith opens mind unreservedly to unknown.
- Attempting to grasp life creates disappointment; understanding requires letting go and observing flow.
- Idols—including cherished beliefs—block access to reality; legitimate use is expressing truth, not possessing it.

## Open Question
How does one distinguish genuine openness to truth from rationalized confirmation bias masquerading as faith?
