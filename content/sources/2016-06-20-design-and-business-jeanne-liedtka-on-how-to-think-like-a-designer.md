## Core Argument
Design thinking democratises innovation by making problem-solving a teachable skill for all employees, not just creative geniuses.

## Key Points
- Design thinking is a process-driven approach distinct from individual creative genius—replicable across organisations.
- Innovation must scale beyond disruptive breakthroughs to include incremental improvements accessible to every employee.
- Organisations need tools, processes, and psychological safety to enable non-experts to participate in innovation conversations.

## Open Question
How do organisations balance belief in universal innovation potential with the reality that some people resist stepping into uncertainty, and how do tools alone overcome cultural barriers to participation?

## India Angle
Indian organisations, often hierarchical and command-driven, can democratise innovation by shifting from 'great leader' mythology to design thinking as a scalable, teachable process—critical for competing globally while leveraging broad talent pools.
