## Core Argument
Parents must weigh genuine but small Covid risks against substantial developmental and mental health costs of continued online schooling.

## Key Points
- 55-60% of Indian children already infected; mild outcomes; hospitalization and poor outcomes extremely rare
- Keeping children home risks obesity, screen time addiction, stunted social skills, and potential mental health epidemic
- Risk management requires weighing Covid transmission against documented losses from isolation, not choosing safety in isolation

## Open Question
How do parents make rational risk decisions when psychological anxiety about Covid persists despite epidemiological evidence suggesting children face minimal severe illness risk?

## India Angle
Indian parents facing school reopening decisions post-Covid lockdowns; policy varies by state (Mumbai, Delhi, Karnataka); epidemiological data specific to Indian child infection rates (55-60% antibody prevalence); addresses 350 million children under 15 in India
