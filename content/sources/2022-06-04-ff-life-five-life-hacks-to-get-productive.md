## Core Argument
Small behavioural adjustments—time awareness, speaking skills, sensory triggers—unlock productivity gains.

## Key Points
- Time blindness (aphantasia) requires active management through tools and self-awareness testing
- Presentation literacy is now a critical skill amplified by internet reach and global audience access
- Sensory anchors like scent create neural pathways that enhance memory recall under pressure

## Open Question
The article celebrates life hacks as quick wins but doesn't address whether these tactical fixes address root causes of poor time management, communication anxiety, or whether they merely mask systemic productivity issues.
