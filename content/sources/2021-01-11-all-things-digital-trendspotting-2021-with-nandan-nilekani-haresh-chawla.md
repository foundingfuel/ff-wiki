## Core Argument
Digital is no longer optional; it provides strategic optionality to navigate pandemic-driven transformation.

## Key Points
- Companies need digital optionality—multiple channels to reach customers—not just digital presence.
- India Stack enables transaction-led growth through embedded lending, credit access via data aggregation.
- Government builds digital infrastructure backbone; companies hook into APIs to create services.

## Open Question
How will India balance transaction-led digital growth and financial inclusion without compromising privacy, data security, and equitable access across income levels?

## India Angle
India's digital stack (Aadhaar, UPI, India Stack) enables a transaction-led, inclusive internet model distinct from Western/Chinese approaches. India can transition from prepaid to postpaid economy, drive embedded lending, and become a SaaS powerhouse while using digital infrastructure for vaccine rollout and economic recovery.
