## Core Argument
India faces a Model T moment with Aadhaar; policy must evolve now, not after deployment.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar and India Stack platforms enable unprecedented reach but require urgent privacy and data protection laws.
- Multiple uses of identity infrastructure create both opportunity and risk—policy must guide which sectors use it.
- India's unequal development means exclusion stems not from Aadhaar itself but how it is used and governed.

## Open Question
How can India balance the urgent scaling of transformative digital infrastructure with the time needed for proper regulatory safeguards and democratic debate?

## India Angle
India must urgently establish privacy, data protection, and regulatory frameworks for Aadhaar and India Stack before they scale—unlike Ford's Model T, which evolved infrastructure gradually. This is critical for bridging India's multiple economies and preventing exclusion through misuse of identity infrastructure.
