## Core Argument
Cross-generational collaboration accelerates personal reinvention and exposes emerging talent to wider audiences.

## Key Points
- Younger musicians learn ragas and Western music simultaneously, creating deeper integrated knowledge than sequential learning.
- Working with young talent forces established artists to reinvent themselves and stay relevant to contemporary contexts.
- Mentorship creates mutual value: exposure for juniors, continuous learning and evolution for seniors.

## Open Question
How do masters balance preserving classical tradition with enabling younger musicians to take it 'vertically and horizontally forward' in fundamentally new directions?

## India Angle
Indian classical music ecosystem: how maestros like Zakir Hussain mentor younger generation to preserve and innovate within tradition while embracing global influences—relevant model for knowledge transfer across Indian institutions and organizations.
