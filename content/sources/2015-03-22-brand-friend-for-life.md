## Core Argument
Brand advocates through superior user experience outperform paid advertising and generate zero-cost, high-credibility growth.

## Key Points
- 92% of consumers trust earned media (friend/family recommendations) over traditional advertising across all channels.
- Google rejected expensive marketing plans, betting instead on product excellence to create natural brand advocacy.
- Word-of-mouth buzz is inversely proportional to advertising spend—visibility rises as costs fall, defying traditional marketing logic.

## Open Question
If organic advocacy is superior and zero-cost, why do established companies continue massive advertising budgets—is it risk aversion, legacy thinking, or does advertising serve purposes beyond direct customer conversion?

## India Angle
Indian companies often rely heavily on traditional advertising spend; this advocates a shift toward product excellence and organic advocacy—particularly relevant for Indian startups with limited marketing budgets competing against established players.
