## Core Argument
Learning from nature's efficiency principles transforms design, infrastructure, and organizational systems.

## Key Points
- Da Vinci's reverence for nature as superior design mentor echoes modern ecological design practices.
- Viewing cities as organisms with metabolic flows applies systems thinking to urban planning.
- Japan's shift from reactive disaster response to preventive systems shows institutional transformation potential.

## Open Question
How can India build institutional capacity and ecosystems for sustained excellence (sports, disaster management, sustainable design) when results take years but public and political attention is short-term?

## India Angle
India's sports infrastructure and disaster preparedness systems lag behind global standards; ecodesign principles could inform sustainable urban development as Indian cities expand rapidly.
