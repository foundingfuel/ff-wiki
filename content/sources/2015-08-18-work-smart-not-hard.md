## Core Argument
Expertise requires deliberate practice with feedback, not automated hard work or unconscious competence.

## Key Points
- Four-stage competence model: unconscious incompetence to unconscious competence; automized skills plateau expertise.
- Deliberate practice demands focused effort, ego-shedding, active feedback-seeking, and weekly iteration on deficits.
- Hard work alone doesn't build expertise; pausing to reflect and adapt approach separates excellence from mere effort.

## Open Question
How do high-performing individuals balance the discipline of deliberate practice with the pressures of earning a living and running day-to-day operations?

## India Angle
Indian professionals often equate hard work with success but lack structured deliberate practice frameworks; this approach is particularly relevant for Indian entrepreneurs scaling beyond technical expertise to business acumen.
