## Core Argument
AI and autonomous vehicles are reshaping talent markets; online education democratises skills needed for emerging industries.

## Key Points
- Tech giants forming AI governance partnerships to ensure beneficial, safe applications across sectors.
- Autonomous vehicle competition creates $10M-valued talent; drives demand for specialized online education.
- Online education (Georgia Tech $7K masters, Udacity nanodegrees) enables mid-career skill upgrading without traditional barriers.

## Open Question
Will online microcredentials genuinely create new talent pools or simply credential-wrap skills workers would acquire anyway through self-study and experience?

## India Angle
India's talent pool in AI and autonomous vehicles is being sourced globally; online education platforms like Udacity offer Indian engineers affordable skill upgrades without geographic barriers, but India needs policy frameworks for autonomous vehicles and AI governance.
