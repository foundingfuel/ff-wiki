## Core Argument
Intense work enables happiness only when purpose, emotional stability, fitness, and life-stage align perfectly.

## Key Points
- Flow requires deep meaning and absorption, not just long hours—most people mistake quantity for quality.
- Personal circumstances (family stability, health, fitness) are preconditions; without them, overwork becomes slavery.
- Sustainable intensity demands 'landings'—life phases can't sustain 250+ hours indefinitely; timing and stage matter critically.

## Open Question
If intense work is only fulfilling for those with ideal preconditions (meaning, stability, fitness, life-stage), how do we build systems that don't celebrate a model only accessible to the privileged few?

## India Angle
Indian startup founders and high-performers often glorify 250+ hour months; this challenges that narrative by examining when intense work enables 'flow' vs. burnout, relevant for Indian entrepreneurship culture.
