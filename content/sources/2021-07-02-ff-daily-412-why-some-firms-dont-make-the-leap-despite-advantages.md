## Core Argument
Organizations fail to capitalize on strategic advantages when internal commitments and incentives remain misaligned with stated change.

## Key Points
- Kodak invented digital camera technology but failed to pivot because organizational structures prioritized film business over transformation.
- Strategy statements and high-level commitment don't work without addressing competing internal commitments across the entire organization.
- Technology and incentive redesign (like Argentina's Plan Nacer) can gradually shift organizational behavior toward new priorities without wholesale restructuring.

## Open Question
How can organizations distinguish between genuine strategic advantages worth pursuing and false starts that justify not disrupting core business models?

## India Angle
Indian companies and policymakers must understand why strategic advantage alone doesn't guarantee success—relevant for legacy firms modernizing and government agencies implementing reforms like healthcare IT systems
