## Core Argument
WPP kills historic brand names to signal disruption, masking investor pressure and organizational identity confusion.

## Key Points
- WPP killed J Walter Thompson's 127-year legacy by merging Wunderman Thompson with VMLY&R into VML.
- CEO Mark Read prioritizes stock market value over institutional heritage; company market cap halved since 2018.
- Leadership vacuum: no creatives from Thompson or Y&R at decision table; tech-focused executives dominate strategy.

## Open Question
How do agency holding companies reconcile the need for rapid cost-cutting and market repositioning with the client relationships and talent loyalty built over decades—or is that heritage now a liability?

## India Angle
Indian advertising industry faces similar pressures from tech platforms and consulting firms; local agencies may face consolidation and identity loss as global holding companies chase investor valuations over legacy relationships.
