## Core Argument
Hard empathy—imaginatively understanding radically different lived experiences—is essential for navigating complex social and governance challenges.

## Key Points
- Hard empathy requires effortful imagination into experiences we haven't lived, not just relating to familiar emotions.
- Systems like marriage, water crises, and censorship reveal how individual choices are shaped by structural constraints and historical injustice.
- Cynicism about others' motives masks our own failure to extend genuine understanding across difference and privilege.

## Open Question
How do we cultivate hard empathy at scale when cognitive and emotional biases make us default to cynicism about others' motivations?

## India Angle
India's governance and civil society must develop hard empathy across deep divides—urban-rural, rich-poor, majority-minority—to design inclusive policy and navigate social tensions. Also reflects Indian attitudes toward relationships, wealth, and systemic misogyny.
