## Core Argument
Informal, playful rituals dissolve hierarchies and humanize leaders, enabling psychological safety and inclusion.

## Key Points
- Sunday club format creates safe space for senior and junior members to interact as equals, not titles.
- Silly rituals and humor deliberately strip away formality, melting interpersonal ice between individuals.
- Junior members observing leaders' vulnerability leads to seeing them as human, not distant heroes.

## Open Question
How do informal, unstructured cultural practices scale across large distributed teams without losing their organic, spontaneous essence?

## India Angle
Indian team leadership under Gavaskar demonstrates how informal culture-building practices can dissolve hierarchies in competitive, performance-driven environments—relevant for Indian organizations navigating remote work and cross-generational teams.
