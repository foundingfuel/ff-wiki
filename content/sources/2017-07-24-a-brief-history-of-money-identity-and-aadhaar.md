## Core Argument
Data is infinite oil; India must protect it or citizens will be plundered like Middle East squandered finite resources.

## Key Points
- Data differs fundamentally from oil: infinite, more valuable for building economies, but requires governance frameworks.
- Citizens unconsciously surrender biometric data without understanding its worth, repeating historical resource-exploitation cycles.
- Privacy cannot be absolute but must have caveats, similar to how fundamental rights to earn money are limited by consensus laws.

## Open Question
How can India monetize data wealth through Aadhaar while protecting citizens from exploitation, when citizens don't yet understand data's value and governance mechanisms remain undefined?

## India Angle
Aadhaar represents India's opportunity to leverage data as a strategic asset, but also exposes the risk of exploiting citizens' biometric data like Middle Eastern nations squandered oil—India must establish governance frameworks that treat data as infinite currency requiring protection, not extraction.
