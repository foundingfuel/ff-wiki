## Core Argument
Post-conflict peace doesn't guarantee prosperity; institutional design and fiscal discipline matter more than external factors.

## Key Points
- Sri Lanka's crisis stemmed from post-war power structures, debt accumulation, and revenue-draining tax cuts—not the civil war itself.
- Police reform RCT in Maharashtra shows institutional design (help desks, female officers) increases case registration without awareness campaigns.
- Mastery experiences during stress recovery require dedicated resources; passive rest alone may be insufficient for resilience.

## Open Question
How can nations sustain institutional reforms and fiscal discipline after conflict when political incentives push toward short-term populism and patronage?

## India Angle
Sri Lanka's crisis offers India critical lessons on managing post-conflict economies, debt sustainability, and regional geopolitics—especially given India's own development trajectory and role as a regional power balancing China's influence.
