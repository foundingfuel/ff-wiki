## Core Argument
Founder CEOs must separate roles and cede control when company needs differ from personal capabilities.

## Key Points
- Deshpande asked 'is this my company or our company?' in 1997, triggering eventual CEO transition 16 years later.
- Founder must build meaningful external pursuits to avoid micromanaging successor and maintain board-level value.
- Managing relationships with loyal long-tenured employees becomes fraught when new CEO restructures but founder retains ownership.

## Open Question
How can a founder-chairman maintain influence and protect legacy relationships without undermining the new CEO's authority and decision-making autonomy?

## India Angle
Indian founders scaling from startup to listed company face unique pressures around succession and control; Deshpande's deliberate transition offers a blueprint for Indian IT services leaders navigating generational shifts.
