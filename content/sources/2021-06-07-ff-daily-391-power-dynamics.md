## Core Argument
Power dynamics shape institutional responses; India failed to learn from pandemic history, risking future crises.

## Key Points
- Love of power is insatiable and grows with experience, making power-holders increasingly tyrannical over time.
- India's pandemic response lacked medical consensus, policy lagged science, and labor migration patterns shifted permanently.
- Lab-leak theory dismissed as conspiracy despite credible researchers investigating; institutional resistance to alternative explanations.

## Open Question
How can institutions built on power hierarchies ever genuinely learn from crises when those hierarchies actively resist scrutiny and alternative explanations?

## India Angle
India's pandemic response exposed institutional weaknesses and consensus-building failures; highlights how policy lags behind science due to supply-demand mismatches in decision-making, with grassroots political realignment implications.
