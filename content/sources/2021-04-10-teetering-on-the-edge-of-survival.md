## Core Argument
Pandemic exposed systemic failures protecting women workers; resilience alone cannot replace institutional safety nets.

## Key Points
- Women face 23.5% lower re-employment post-pandemic; sector-specific gender discrimination blocks alternative work.
- Informal workers like taxi drivers lost 50%+ income; social insolvency masked as individual hardship.
- Women's labour market participation remains precarious despite education; structural barriers prevent advancement.

## Open Question
Should policy focus on enabling women's resilience and entrepreneurship, or fundamentally restructure institutional systems that exclude them from stable employment?

## India Angle
India's pandemic impact on women's informal workforce; systemic gender discrimination in male-dominated sectors; inadequate social safety nets for vulnerable workers; gendered labour market exclusion (ambulance drivers, taxi services).
