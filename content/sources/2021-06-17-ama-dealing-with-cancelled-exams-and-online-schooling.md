## Core Argument
Schools must shift from syllabus-finishing to learning curation or risk irrelevance in digital age.

## Key Points
- Teachers should curate online information like museum curators, not push rote syllabi completion.
- New Indian higher-ed institutions developing critical thinking skills competitive with Western universities.
- Schools responsible for supporting bottom 50% of students, not just showcasing top performers.

## Open Question
How do structured systems foster creativity and critical thinking without abandoning accountability and standardization that allows student mobility across institutions?

## India Angle
Indian schools face systemic pressure to adopt rote learning and marks-based selection; new education policy allows flexibility but institutions haven't evolved pedagogy. Online transition exposed gaps in digital readiness and teacher training. Indian higher ed now competitive with Western institutions, reducing brain drain pressure.
