## Core Argument
Humans systematically overestimate emotional impact of events because we adapt faster than we predict.

## Key Points
- Affective forecasting fails: lottery winners and paraplegics return to baseline happiness within a year.
- RBI's single-mandate inflation targeting is too simplistic for India's complex pandemic recovery needs.
- Modern networks (data, capital, goods flows) exceed human comprehension at scale, creating unpredictable system behaviour.

## Open Question
If humans inevitably adapt to circumstances and complex systems are incomprehensible, how should policymakers design interventions that actually create lasting change?

## India Angle
India's RBI must move beyond simplistic inflation targeting to manage pandemic recovery, fiscal deficits exceeding 14% GDP, and foreign capital flows—requiring integrated policy addressing growth, stability, and infrastructure simultaneously.
