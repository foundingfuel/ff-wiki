## Core Argument
Sustainable entrepreneurial success requires intrinsic motivation, intellectual humility, and generosity over extrinsic rewards.

## Key Points
- Allow yourself to say 'I don't know' and listen rather than perform certainty in a distraction-filled world.
- Innovators succeed through associating ideas, questioning assumptions, observing carefully, networking, and experimenting relentlessly.
- Deep expertise and few big bets beat resume-padding across many industries; intrinsic rewards matter more than prestige.

## Open Question
How do ambitious early-career professionals balance the need to build expertise in depth against organisational pressure to demonstrate breadth across industries?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs often chase status through prestigious roles and rapid job-hopping; this challenges that mindset by emphasizing deep expertise, intrinsic motivation, and sustainable success over resume-building.
