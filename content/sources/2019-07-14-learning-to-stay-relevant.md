## Core Argument
Staying relevant requires learning through doing, not passive knowledge accumulation in a digitally transformed world.

## Key Points
- Effective learning demands real-world application and continuous practice with immediate feedback loops, not theoretical study.
- Incumbent companies face existential threats from established digital giants, requiring leaders to re-learn strategy fundamentals.
- Younger, non-linear career expectations force organizations to rethink retention, development, and what expertise means.

## Open Question
How can organizations create learning systems that bridge knowledge gaps fast enough when the rate of change itself keeps accelerating?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and professionals must rapidly upskill in digital strategy and new frameworks to stay relevant as incumbents face disruption from digital giants; generational shifts in workforce expectations demand new organizational approaches.
