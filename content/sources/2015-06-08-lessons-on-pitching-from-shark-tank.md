## Core Argument
Effective pitching requires personalization, founder credibility, financial rigor, and concrete demonstration over passion alone.

## Key Points
- Customize presentations for specific investors by researching their interests and past investments.
- Investors back founders first, ideas second—personal credibility and connection stories matter most.
- Know every number supporting your valuation; vague answers or guesses destroy credibility instantly.

## Open Question
How do founders balance authentic storytelling with financial rigor when investors want both passion and spreadsheets, but these can sometimes be at odds?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs pitching to domestic and international investors need these credibility-building skills; applicable to startup ecosystems in India where founder credibility often determines funding success.
