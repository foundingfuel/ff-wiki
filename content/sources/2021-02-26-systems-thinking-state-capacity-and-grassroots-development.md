## Core Argument
State capacity emerges from systems interplay; grassroots changemakers, not institutions alone, drive real development.

## Key Points
- State failure isn't isolated but result of political-economic-social system interactions creating vicious cycles
- Electoral democracy over-relied upon; other institutions need strengthening to prevent elite capture and ensure equity
- Focusing on structures and processes rather than events reveals root causes of crises like deindustrialization

## Open Question
How do you strengthen state capacity when the political system benefits from the very dysfunctions (predatory extraction, cross-subsidization) that perpetuate underdevelopment?

## India Angle
India's state capacity crisis stems from systemic interplay between political, economic, and social systems; elite capture of institutions prevents grassroots solutions; understanding structures over events critical for policy reform.
