## Core Argument
India can invert data ownership from corporations to individuals through law and technology, creating competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Indians will generate unprecedented data before becoming economically rich due to digitization and infrastructure like Aadhaar and UPI
- Current tech giants (Google, Facebook, Amazon) dominate because they control user data; individuals lack agency to monetize their own information
- Data empowerment architecture allows users to consent to data-sharing between entities for personal benefit, requiring major policy, technology and business model shifts

## Open Question
How can India overcome resistance from businesses profiting from data monopolies and the technical/user comprehension complexity that doomed early UPI adoption?

## India Angle
India can pioneer a data empowerment model where individuals own and control their personal data through consent frameworks and technology, differentiating itself from the US/China model where corporations extract value from user data.
