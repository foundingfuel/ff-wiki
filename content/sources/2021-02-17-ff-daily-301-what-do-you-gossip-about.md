## Core Argument
What you gossip about reveals true interests; solving problems upstream requires institutional coordination and moral accountability.

## Key Points
- Crick discovered his calling by noticing what he naturally gossiped about, not formal qualifications.
- Delhi school reforms fail upstream because MCD schools (Classes 1-5) lack quality; later interventions cannot bridge the gap.
- McKinsey prioritized profit over ethics in OxyContin case, revealing consulting's moral hazard when detached from community impact.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance profit-driven incentives with moral responsibility when expert advice shapes life-and-death outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian organizations and leaders often follow Western consulting models uncritically; Delhi school reforms show how upstream problem-solving must account for India's institutional fragmentation (MCD vs. state systems). Moral questions about consulting's role in society are particularly urgent for India's development agenda.
