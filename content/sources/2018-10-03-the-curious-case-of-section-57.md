## Core Argument
Supreme Court's ambiguous Section 57 ruling leaves Indian businesses uncertain whether Aadhaar-based authentication remains viable.

## Key Points
- Court struck down private sector Aadhaar use citing privacy risks from contractual arrangements without statutory safeguards.
- Ambiguous judgment creates two competing interpretations: permanent ban vs. conditional future availability for private players.
- Major businesses (Reliance, Airtel, Paytm, Kotak) face operational uncertainty while awaiting clarity on eKYC viability.

## Open Question
How can India balance Aadhaar's efficiency benefits for private sector authentication with genuine individual privacy protections without explicit statutory frameworks?

## India Angle
Aadhaar's dual purpose—government subsidy delivery vs. private sector authentication—creates regulatory ambiguity that directly impacts Indian fintech, telecom, and banking businesses relying on rapid customer onboarding.
