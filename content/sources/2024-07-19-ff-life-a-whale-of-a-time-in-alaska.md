## Core Argument
Alaska cruise offers curated experiences that eliminate decision-making, appealing to travelers seeking structured leisure.

## Key Points
- Celebrity Edge cruise integrates onboard entertainment, dining, and shore excursions into a seamless 7-day package
- Ketchikan's tourism economy benefits from 9,000+ daily cruise visitors, supporting local crafts like totem pole carving
- Indigenous Alaskan culture—totem poles, salmon heritage, bear and whale ecosystems—forms the experiential backbone of cruise stops

## Open Question
How sustainable is Ketchikan's dependency on 9,000 daily cruise tourists for economic viability, and what cultural impact does mass tourism have on indigenous heritage preservation?
