## Core Argument
Understanding Xi's China requires examining its state capacity, political economy, and cultural context to anticipate global implications.

## Key Points
- Xi Jinping wields unprecedented regime and state capacity compared to other authoritarian leaders, enabling aggressive global ambitions.
- Corruption fuelled China's 1990s-2000s boom but created systemic risks; recent crackdowns on tech/finance may dampen growth.
- China's negotiation strategy with India reveals asymmetric power dynamics and fundamentally different worldviews between the nations.

## Open Question
How can India strategically engage with a more powerful, assertive China while China's own internal governance contradictions (growth-limiting crackdowns) remain unresolved?

## India Angle
India must understand China's political consolidation, strategic thinking, and negotiation tactics to navigate bilateral relations and the US-China tech competition's spillover effects on Indian businesses and security.
