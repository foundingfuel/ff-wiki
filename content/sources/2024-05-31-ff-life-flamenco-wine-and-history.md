## Core Argument
Andalusia's layered history—shaped by successive civilisations—reveals how identity, culture and politics interweave across time.

## Key Points
- Andalusia's 781-year Muslim rule (711-1492) created unique fusion of Christian, Islamic, and African cultures.
- Historical conquest patterns show identity struggles: violent subjugation alternating with cultural assimilation and melding.
- Past is embedded in present: architecture, Flamenco, cuisine, festivals all reflect Andalusia's composite civilisational heritage.

## Open Question
How can regions celebrate layered histories shaped by conquest and violence without romanticising past subjugation or erasing competing narratives?
