## Core Argument
Technology regulation requires simultaneous legal frameworks and technological guardrails, not one without the other.

## Key Points
- India's Account Aggregator enables data pooling for financial assessments but lacks foundational data protection law.
- Tim Cook transformed Apple through operations excellence, custom tech, and services expansion over 10+ years as CEO.
- Oliver Sacks found meaning in physical constants during mortality—a reflection on how we anchor ourselves amid uncertainty.

## Open Question
How can India implement revolutionary data-sharing systems without a comprehensive data protection law in place, and what are the unintended consequences?

## India Angle
India's Account Aggregator system and data protection framework highlight the need for balancing technological innovation with legal safeguards—a critical challenge for Indian policymakers building digital finance infrastructure.
