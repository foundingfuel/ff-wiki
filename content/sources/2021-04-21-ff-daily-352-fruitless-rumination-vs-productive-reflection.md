## Core Argument
Productive reflection requires solving problems and releasing thoughts; sustained focus requires emotional regulation.

## Key Points
- Rumination loops drain focus; productive reflection ends when a tentative solution emerges.
- Selective attention and emotional inhibition are neural skills that separate high performers from anxious ones.
- Ability to disengage from distressing thoughts and redirect attention protects mental health and performance.

## Open Question
How do individuals systematically develop emotional immunity and selective attention in crisis-prone environments without suppressing legitimate concerns?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers struggle with information overload and anxiety during crises (pandemic, economic uncertainty); better focus and reflection practices are critical for sustained performance in high-stress environments like India's competitive job market.
