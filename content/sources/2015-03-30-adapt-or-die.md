## Core Argument
Organizations fail not from bad tactics but from outdated management DNA preventing innovation and strategic renewal.

## Key Points
- Traditional management structures inhibit game-changing innovation and lock organizations into obsolete strategies too long.
- Organizations need foundational DNA redesign, not just tactical fixes, to become instinctively innovative and adaptable.
- Innovation at multiple levels—from operations to business models—is the only sustainable defense against irrelevance.

## Open Question
If innovation truly requires DNA-level organizational restructuring, how can incumbent organizations realistically achieve this while maintaining operational continuity and stakeholder confidence?

## India Angle
Indian organizations—many still operating on 20th-century management models—need fundamental DNA restructuring to compete globally. Family-run businesses and large conglomerates particularly struggle with strategic renewal, making Hamel's 'Management 2.0' framework directly applicable to India's transformation challenges.
