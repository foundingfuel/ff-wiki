## Core Argument
Selfless commitment to present moment outperforms ego-driven striving toward distant goals.

## Key Points
- Ego-climbers stay misaligned with reality, always chasing external outcomes while missing immediate value
- Preparation and planning matter, but must be paired with present-moment focus and acceptance of conditions
- True performance emerges from devotion to the process itself, not attachment to personal achievement

## Open Question
How do you maintain rigorous goal-orientation and ambition while simultaneously practicing selfless detachment from outcomes?

## India Angle
Pirsig's time in India and Mount Kailash pilgrimage provides a non-Western philosophical lens on achievement and purpose relevant to Indian leaders navigating ego-driven vs. purpose-driven organizational cultures.
