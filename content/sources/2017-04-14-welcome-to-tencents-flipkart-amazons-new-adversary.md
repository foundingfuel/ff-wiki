## Core Argument
Flipkart's $1.4B raise from Tencent, Microsoft, eBay reframes Indian e-commerce as Amazon vs. everyone else.

## Key Points
- Tiger Global orchestrated a complex multi-party deal where each investor gains strategic footholds in India's e-commerce.
- Tencent's entry signals plans to build a WeChat-like super-app leveraging India Stack, payments, and Aadhaar.
- True valuation likely 1.6-2X GMV, lower than headline $11.6B, due to complex swaps, ratchets, and anti-dilution clauses.

## Open Question
If Flipkart acquires Snapdeal primarily to add SoftBank as a backer, does financial engineering distract from operational excellence needed to compete with Amazon's operational efficiency?

## India Angle
Flipkart's fundraising and strategic partnerships reshape India's e-commerce battlefield, positioning it against Amazon with Chinese capital and tech giant backing—a shift that could determine which player dominates India's digital commerce and fintech future.
