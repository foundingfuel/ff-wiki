## Core Argument
Elite performance requires deliberate worst-case preparation, incremental effort accumulation, and fearless boundary-pushing paired with deep personal anchors.

## Key Points
- Prepare systematically for worst-case scenarios rather than just optimizing for ideal outcomes
- Incremental effort compounds over time; small daily sacrifices create compounding advantage
- Innovation requires pushing psychological boundaries daily; the mind must be convinced before the body performs

## Open Question
How do organizations scale Phelps' extreme 24/7 commitment model without burning out talent, especially in India's emerging high-performance sectors?

## India Angle
Indian high-performance teams and entrepreneurs can learn Phelps' mental models—systematic preparation for worst-case scenarios, incremental excellence, and fearless innovation—applicable to competitive business environments and organizational culture-building.
