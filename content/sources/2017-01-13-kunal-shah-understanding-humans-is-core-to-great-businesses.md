## Core Argument
Understanding human psychology and behaviour—not just business fundamentals—drives entrepreneurial success.

## Key Points
- Studying philosophy and understanding human nature more valuable than MBA for building great businesses.
- Shame-based childhood training creates lifelong fear of failure; early failures are essential learning journeys.
- Trust, empowerment and behavioural signals (MacBook offer) attract ethical employees who take ownership.

## Open Question
How do founders cultivate trust-based cultures that require vulnerability when traditional Indian parenting and education systems prioritize shame-based discipline?

## India Angle
Indian founders must overcome shame-based cultural conditioning around failure to build risk-taking startups; human-centric product design critical for digital payments adoption in India's transition to online transactions.
