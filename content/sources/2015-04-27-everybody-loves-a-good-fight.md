## Core Argument
Freshdesk's India-built SaaS model threatens Western incumbents by delivering comparable quality at lower cost.

## Key Points
- Freshdesk operates from India with 30-40% cost advantage while maintaining San Francisco presence and global ambitions.
- CEO Mathrubootham rejects Silicon Valley orthodoxy (MVP, Lean Startup) for B2B, building complete products upfront based on deep domain knowledge.
- Competitor attacks on Freshdesk's nationality suggest existential threat; $94M funding signals investor confidence in India-born tech talent.

## Open Question
If SaaS requires massive scale to achieve profitability, can Freshdesk sustain its India cost advantage when forced to match larger competitors' global infrastructure and R&D spending?

## India Angle
Indian SaaS company leveraging build-in-India cost advantage (30-40%) to compete globally; challenges Western incumbents; demonstrates India's potential in B2B software despite nationalist attacks from competitors.
