## Core Argument
Large corporations need entrepreneurial culture, talent models, and asset separation to build viable digital businesses.

## Key Points
- New digital businesses require breaking free from corporate bureaucracy and building higher-velocity cultures with different hiring practices.
- Six post-Covid archetypes emerging: remote services, collaboration platforms, dynamic talent deployment, resilient supply chains, data utilization, and digital retail.
- Critical tension: borrowing assets from parent organization without slowing new venture with legacy constraints and processes.

## Open Question
How can large corporations extract needed assets and talent from existing operations without importing the bureaucratic slowness that makes new digital ventures uncompetitive?

## India Angle
Indian large corporations and conglomerates must rethink how to build agile digital businesses post-Covid while managing legacy bureaucracies. The six archetypes (remote services, collaboration, gig economy, supply chain resilience, data utilization, digital retail) are particularly relevant for India's services sector, e-commerce expansion, and manufacturing modernization.
