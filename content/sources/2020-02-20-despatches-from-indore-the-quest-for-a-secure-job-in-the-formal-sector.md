## Core Argument
Formal employment remains scarce aspiration for India's working poor amid economic slowdown and informal sector dominance.

## Key Points
- 93% of Indian workforce in informal sector; formal jobs increasingly rare despite aspirations and education efforts.
- Household survival strategies: Rs 20,000 salary barely covers family of six; meticulous budgeting and small savings essential.
- Permanent formal employment provides security (provident fund, insurance, paid leave) that drives pursuit despite financial constraints.

## Open Question
If formal sector jobs remain scarce despite educational hubs and smart city infrastructure in Indore, what structural reforms can actually shift workers from informal to formal employment at scale?

## India Angle
Explores the structural challenge of India's informal economy (93% of workforce) and the aspirational pursuit of formal sector jobs among working-class Indians, revealing ground-level impact of economic slowdown on livelihoods and household finances.
