## Core Argument
Effective digital asset organization requires balance between accessibility and structure; tools exist to solve this systematically.

## Key Points
- Cluttered desktops and overly rigid folder systems both fail; optimal organization balances quick access with findability.
- Specialized tools (Eagle for images, Calibre for documents, Obsidian for notes) solve distinct digital management problems efficiently.
- As visual content consumption grows, image organization and searchability become critical to personal and professional productivity.

## Open Question
How do individuals choose between competing tools and systems when no single platform optimally solves all digital organization needs?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and entrepreneurs increasingly manage digital-first workflows; practical systems for organizing digital assets directly impact productivity and decision-making in India's growing remote/hybrid work culture.
