## Core Argument
Unchecked ambition, institutional connivance, and weak accountability media enable financial fraud cycles.

## Key Points
- Ambitious leaders seeking legitimacy can spiral into reckless expansion when validation fails
- Business-politics nexus and institutional corruption create conditions for fraud to flourish undetected
- Media captured by power players loses watchdog function, enabling crises to metastasize

## Open Question
If history shows clear patterns of fraud enabled by ambition, politics, and weak media, why do societies keep repeating these cycles despite documented warnings?
