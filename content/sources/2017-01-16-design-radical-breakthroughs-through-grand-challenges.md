## Core Argument
Grand challenges with clear goals and prizes unlock radical innovation by attracting global investment and cross-disciplinary talent.

## Key Points
- Prize-based competitions attract 10-100x more investment than the prize amount itself, creating positive ROI for innovation.
- Audacious but achievable goals motivate teams across nations and disciplines to solve humanity's most difficult problems.
- Milestone prizes fund intermediate breakthroughs, reducing risk and maintaining momentum toward grand objectives.

## Open Question
How do we ensure grand challenges produce sustainable, adoptable innovations rather than one-off achievements that lack commercial viability or real-world implementation?

## India Angle
Team Indus demonstrates how Indian startups can compete globally on moonshot challenges; shows how grand challenges can attract young talent and mentorship in India's emerging space ecosystem.
