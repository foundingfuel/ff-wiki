## Core Argument
India's 2024 elections marked a democratic reset where voters rejected authoritarianism and mainstream media lost monopoly over political narrative.

## Key Points
- Electorate defeated ruling party despite its command of money, media, and establishment—signaling limits to institutional capture.
- Independent journalists and social media emerged as alternative voices to mainstream TV's single narrative on elections.
- Citizens demanded institutional integrity, constitutional primacy, and accountability—core anxieties about India's democratic direction.

## Open Question
If hope for institutional reform is genuine, what systemic changes will prevent future governments from similarly concentrating power—or will electoral cycles alone prove insufficient?

## India Angle
India's 2024 Lok Sabha elections reveal citizens' expectations for institutional integrity, constitutional values, and democratic accountability—testing whether democracy can constrain majoritarian power and protect minority rights.
