## Core Argument
Asking the right questions reveals what people actually need, not what they say they want.

## Key Points
- Stated preferences often mask true underlying motivations and constraints.
- Remote work requires intentional documentation systems to replicate tacit knowledge transfer.
- Organizational transparency and codified knowledge are prerequisites for geographic flexibility.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the cultural cost of extreme documentation transparency with the operational necessity for remote work effectiveness?

## India Angle
Indian remote-work organizations face acute knowledge-loss challenges due to informal mentorship cultures; GitLab's documentation model offers scalable solution for distributed Indian tech teams navigating work-from-anywhere transitions.
