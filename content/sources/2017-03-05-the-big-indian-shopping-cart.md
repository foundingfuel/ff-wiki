## Core Argument
Indian e-commerce must innovate business models and leverage technology to find sustainable profitability amid intense global competition.

## Key Points
- Domestic e-commerce players undergoing massive restructuring as global competition intensifies
- Smartphone ownership growth and declining data costs create massive market opportunity for India
- No company has yet found sustainable business model; profitability remains unsolved challenge

## Open Question
How can Indian e-commerce companies achieve profitability and scale when proven business models remain elusive despite technological capabilities and market tailwinds?

## India Angle
India's e-commerce sector at inflection point: domestic players (Flipkart, Snapdeal) facing restructuring while global competitors (Amazon, potential Chinese entrants) vie for market share in a massive opportunity driven by smartphone penetration and data cost decline.
