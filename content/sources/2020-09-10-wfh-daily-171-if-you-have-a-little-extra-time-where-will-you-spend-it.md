## Core Argument
How you allocate extra time reveals priorities; WFH forces recalibration of work-life integration patterns.

## Key Points
- Ponzen's five-hour allocation exercise exposes true priorities—work, family, community, or self-care
- Netflix CEO Hastings believes in-person collaboration essential; managers struggle with remote oversight and trust
- Research shows managers can be trained to lead effectively remotely despite initial discomfort

## Open Question
If extra time allocation reveals true values, why do organizational cultures still reward office presence over actual productivity outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and managers increasingly navigating WFH policies; managers struggling with remote oversight mirrors challenges in Indian corporate culture that historically emphasized office presence and visible authority
