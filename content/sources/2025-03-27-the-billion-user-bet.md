## Core Argument
India needs systemic unlocks in technology, capital, entrepreneurship, and formalization to harness one billion users for growth.

## Key Points
- UPI's success validated Nilekani's 2015 WhatsApp Moment prediction, proving network effects and DPI can transform sectors.
- India's 13 high-GDP districts, low productivity ($7/hr vs $82 in US), and informal economy (85% unformalized) present structural challenges.
- Four unlocks—AI for billion Indians, tokenization for capital efficiency, one million startups, and formalization—are proposed remedies by 2035.

## Open Question
Can technology and policy alone overcome India's deep structural inequalities, or do cultural, behavioral, and institutional shifts require equally bold intervention?

## India Angle
India's economic growth has plateaued; Nilekani proposes four 'unlocks'—technology, capital, entrepreneurship, formalization—to address spatial inequality, low productivity, and informal economy by 2035, positioning DPI as India's competitive advantage.
