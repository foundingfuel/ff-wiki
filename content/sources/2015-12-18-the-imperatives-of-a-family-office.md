## Core Argument
Family offices enable entrepreneurs to manage wealth strategically, not reactively, aligning assets with long-term objectives and family governance.

## Key Points
- Family offices shift wealth management from reactive opportunity-chasing to strategic planning aligned with personal objectives.
- Choice between single-family, multi-family, or hybrid models depends on asset size, available talent, and willingness to pay for professional advisors.
- Transparent fee-only advisor models outperform commission-based advice; alignment of incentives is critical to wealth preservation.

## Open Question
How can smaller-asset families or early-stage entrepreneurs justify the cost and complexity of formal family office structures before wealth reaches critical scale?

## India Angle
Family offices are nascent in India but increasingly necessary as entrepreneurial wealth accumulates, stock options proliferate, and business succession becomes critical—a tool adapted to Indian family structures and tax/legal environments.
