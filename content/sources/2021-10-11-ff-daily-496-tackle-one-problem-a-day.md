## Core Argument
Breaking overwhelming change into one daily problem builds momentum and confidence to navigate disruption.

## Key Points
- Lucy overcame decision paralysis by committing to solve one unfamiliar challenge per day, building confidence through small wins.
- Algorithmic hiring systems eliminate qualified candidates by over-specifying job requirements and misinterpreting skill descriptions across domains.
- Multidisciplinary problem-solving requires mutual respect and deep domain fluency, not surface-level understanding across fields.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the speed and scale advantages of automation with the human judgment needed to avoid rejecting capable candidates during talent-scarce periods?

## India Angle
Indian SMEs and large companies facing talent scarcity and organizational change need both adaptive problem-solving mindsets (like Lucy's) and careful vetting of algorithmic hiring to avoid losing qualified candidates in a tight labor market.
