## Core Argument
Indian corporations mouth inclusion but protect powerful offenders, rendering workplace harassment laws toothless.

## Key Points
- 40% of surveyed companies haven't set up mandatory internal complaints committees; most give staff 30 minutes instead of required 1.5-4 hours training.
- Senior executives remain untouchable; organizations prioritize 'indispensable' offenders over victim safety, forcing women to exit or suffer silently.
- Leadership conflates sexual harassment with physical aggression only; lack of genuine cultural change despite compliance frameworks.

## Open Question
How can regulation force genuine cultural change when power hierarchies and informal networks systematically protect high-ranking offenders regardless of formal POSH infrastructure?

## India Angle
Indian corporations nominally comply with POSH Act but fail in implementation—weak internal committees, insufficient training, and executive protection networks undermine legal intent. Cultural transformation in India Inc lags behind diversity rhetoric.
