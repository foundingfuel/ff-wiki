## Core Argument
HR must apply design thinking to enable disruptive innovation, not just manage processes.

## Key Points
- Disruptive businesses blur lines between employees and customers; HR must multiply ideas, not just manage operations.
- Small behavior changes rooted in understanding underlying motivation drive outsized impact on culture and outcomes.
- Design thinking for HR means observing root causes, redesigning interfaces, and aligning incentives to shift employee behavior.

## Open Question
How can HR balance standardization and control with the behavioral flexibility required for disruptive innovation?

## India Angle
Indian companies scaling up need HR functions that match agile business strategies; examples from Indian retail startups and Coca-Cola India show how design thinking in HR drives culture change and employee engagement.
