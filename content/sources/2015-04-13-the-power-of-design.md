## Core Argument
Design thinking—combining empathy, analytical rigour, and iteration—solves complex problems by starting with customer needs.

## Key Points
- Design thinking is systematic problem-solving starting with customer empathy, not aesthetics or technology alone.
- Process has three core stages: empathy/framing, ideation beyond obvious, prototyping and iterating with end-users.
- Applies to 'wicked problems' like healthcare access and poverty; increasingly adopted by tech giants and venture capital.

## Open Question
How can design thinking scale beyond innovation labs in tech-savvy companies to reach the grassroots problems it claims to solve, especially in resource-constrained Indian contexts?

## India Angle
Design thinking offers Indian businesses and social enterprises a framework to address massive challenges like healthcare access, poverty, and iodine deficiency. The Jeevan Bindi example shows how design-led solutions can serve India's underserved populations affordably and effectively.
