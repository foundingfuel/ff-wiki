## Core Argument
Brands must treat social media as permanent theatre where audience holds power and context collapses instantly.

## Key Points
- Social media eliminates separation between front-stage and backstage; gaffes broadcast instantly with no forgetting mechanism.
- Brand communicators must stress-test ads for worst-case scenarios across social, moral, and political dimensions before launch.
- Indian context demands deeper cultural sensitivity to religion, caste, and community sensitivities than global brand guidelines anticipate.

## Open Question
How can brands authentically engage contested cultural territories (faith, feminism, identity) without inviting polarized backlash in a hypervigilant social media ecosystem?

## India Angle
Indian brands operating in India's deeply stratified society face unique risks when ads touch religion, caste, gender—requiring cultural intelligence beyond Western advertising playbooks.
