## Core Argument
Curiosity evolves from seeking novelty into directed understanding; focus means mastering capabilities, not industry boundaries.

## Key Points
- Diversive curiosity (novelty-seeking) must mature into epistemic curiosity (deep understanding) for meaningful expertise.
- Amazon's dominance stems from three core capabilities—customer insight, logistics, technology—not product or competitor focus.
- Post-pandemic work will permanently shift; flexibility and software innovation will reshape how organizations think about physical presence.

## Open Question
How do individuals and organizations balance the constant stimulation of diversive curiosity in digital environments with the discipline required for deep epistemic learning?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and large company leaders often struggle with focus; Amazon's capability-based strategy offers a model for Indian businesses to redefine focus beyond traditional industry silos and compete globally.
