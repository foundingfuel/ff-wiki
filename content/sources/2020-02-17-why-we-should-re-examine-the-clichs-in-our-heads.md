## Core Argument
Unexamined assumptions limit our thinking; questioning clichés reveals better solutions.

## Key Points
- Counter-intuitive principles (less is more, slow, antifragile) outperform conventional wisdom in real-world applications.
- Information overload and structure paradoxically harm learning; depth and reflection matter more than breadth.
- Building resilience requires embracing volatility; suppressing uncertainty makes systems fragile.

## Open Question
How do we systematically identify which clichés to question when most exist for practical reasons, without falling into contrarianism for its own sake?
