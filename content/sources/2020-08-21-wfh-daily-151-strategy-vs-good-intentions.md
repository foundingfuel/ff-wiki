## Core Argument
Strategy is meaningless without resource allocation that aligns with stated values and priorities.

## Key Points
- Resource allocation reveals true priorities—where money, time, energy flow shows actual strategy, not stated intentions.
- Indian retailers negotiating survival need banks to extend loan moratoriums; rent negotiations with malls remain unresolved.
- Game theory shows individual actions (mask-wearing, business decisions) create signals affecting collective outcomes and economy.

## Open Question
How do individuals and organizations bridge the gap between aspirational values and resource constraints during crises when hard choices are unavoidable?
