## Core Argument
US-China relationship oscillates between confrontation and cooperation, reshaping global geopolitics, markets, and technology leadership.

## Key Points
- Trump signals pragmatism on China despite campaign rhetoric, but administration remains internally divided on Taiwan deterrence strategy.
- China demonstrates technological parity (AI, nuclear fusion, 6th-gen fighters) while offering lower-cost alternatives to US finance and security.
- US-China rivalry will determine international markets, technology futures, and stability—with spillovers for allied nations and multilateral order.

## Open Question
How can Trump administration simultaneously pursue tariff restraint, rescue TikTok, and prepare military deterrence against China without triggering the economic conflict it appears to avoid?

## India Angle
India sits between US-China competition (QUAD, I2U2 vs BRICS), faces supply chain risks from tariffs/decoupling, and must navigate tech sovereignty amid AI/semiconductor race while managing its own strategic autonomy.
