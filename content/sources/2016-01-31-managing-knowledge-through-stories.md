## Core Argument
Stories convert tacit knowledge into explicit knowledge, enabling organizations to retain critical expertise despite employee turnover.

## Key Points
- Tacit knowledge (know-how rooted in experience) is invisible in handover notes but essential for operations.
- Storytelling transforms individual experience into shareable organizational knowledge unconsciously already practiced.
- NASA and Delta Airlines learned costly lessons: lost expertise causes operational failures, delays, and safety risks.

## Open Question
If storytelling is how people naturally make sense of experience, why don't organizations capture and systematize these stories until facing crisis or loss?

## India Angle
Indian companies with high employee turnover and decentralized operations lose critical tacit knowledge; storytelling as knowledge management is particularly valuable in organizations where formal documentation is weak and institutional memory is fragile.
