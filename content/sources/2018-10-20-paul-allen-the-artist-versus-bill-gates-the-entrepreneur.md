## Core Argument
Entrepreneurial success requires both visionary strategy and technical execution; personality clashes often obscure complementary strengths.

## Key Points
- Allen pioneered the licensing model that made Microsoft dominant; Gates executed and controlled ruthlessly.
- Allen prioritized identity beyond business; Gates fused his identity entirely with Microsoft's success.
- Gates publicly praised Allen only after death, suggesting competition masked their interdependence during life.

## Open Question
If both founders were essential to Microsoft's dominance, why did Gates see Allen as an impediment rather than indispensable partner while he was alive?
