## Core Argument
Salaried employment provides crucial execution skills, industry knowledge, and networks that significantly increase entrepreneurial success rates.

## Key Points
- Most successful entrepreneurs start in salaried roles; experience in target industry increases venture survival odds.
- Corporate jobs teach execution discipline and systems that startups need, though large-company structures can hinder necessary flexibility.
- Employment enables savings for bootstrap funding, life partnership stability, and team-building with former colleagues—key success factors.

## Open Question
How can employees gain entrepreneurial mindset and take initiative within structured organizations without becoming too constrained by systems to adapt to startup chaos?

## India Angle
Indian educated youth are increasingly rejecting salaried employment as 'uncool' due to startup culture messaging; the article argues Indian entrepreneurs need corporate experience first to succeed, citing examples like NDDB (Kurien) and HDFC (Parekh).
