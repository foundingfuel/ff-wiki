## Core Argument
Facebook's harms stem from business model requiring user addiction, not just toxic culture or leader intent.

## Key Points
- Business model determines company decisions: selling user data to advertisers creates structural incentive misalignment.
- Addiction mechanics, data exploitation, and platform weaponisation are three distinct but interconnected problems.
- India among countries where Facebook/WhatsApp fuelled violence, polarisation through fake news and hate speech.

## Open Question
If the business model is fundamentally flawed, can culture change or ethical leaders actually fix Facebook, or does structural reform (non-profit conversion, regulation) become necessary?

## India Angle
India explicitly mentioned as case study of Facebook/WhatsApp misuse spreading fake news and fanning hatred that polarised society; regulatory challenge for Indian policymakers on platform accountability
