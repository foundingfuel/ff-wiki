## Core Argument
Mastery requires curated learning: integrate financial literacy, leadership discipline, and strategic subtraction—not addition.

## Key Points
- Personal finance systems fail ordinary citizens; financial literacy is foundational for wealth-building and entrepreneurial success.
- Leadership thrives on noticing people, managing contradictions, and staying disciplined—learned across cricket, business, and psychology.
- Success is defined by what you eliminate—habits, commitments, comparisons—not what you accumulate or change externally.

## Open Question
How do individuals build genuine confidence and agency in systems (financial, organizational) designed to disadvantage them, when books offer personal solutions but structural problems persist?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and entrepreneurs need frameworks for personal finance literacy (especially given market gaps for ordinary citizens), leadership capability in family/startup contexts, and navigating workplace dynamics in hierarchical settings. Books on financial products and entrepreneurial fundamentals directly address gaps in Indian market accessibility.
