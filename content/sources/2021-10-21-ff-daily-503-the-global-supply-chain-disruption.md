## Core Argument
Global supply chains optimized for cost-efficiency, not resilience, are breaking under pandemic-driven demand shifts.

## Key Points
- Pandemic shifted consumption from services to goods; individual buying and stockpiling spiked globally.
- Ocean freight costs surged 15-fold; nine carriers control 90% capacity—near-monopoly pricing power.
- 24/7 port operations fail without aligned trucking, rail, and labor—relay ecosystem requires synchronized actors.

## Open Question
How can policymakers enforce 24/7 operations across fragmented supply chains when incentives misalign—ports run 24/7 but truckers won't pick up at 3 am?

## India Angle
Indian manufacturers and exporters depend heavily on global supply chains; disruptions increase input costs, delay deliveries, and reduce competitiveness. Indian ports and logistics need urgent modernization to handle 24/7 operations; regulatory barriers prevent round-the-clock trucking and warehousing.
