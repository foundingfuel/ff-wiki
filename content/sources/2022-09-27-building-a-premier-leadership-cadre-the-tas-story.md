## Core Argument
Systematic leadership development rooted in stakeholder values and nation-building purpose creates intrapreneurs who transform organizations.

## Key Points
- TAS officers developed as intrapreneurs thinking like owners; placed in challenging problems without coddling to drive transformation.
- Gandhian principle of Antyodaya and stakeholder capitalism—community thriving is prerequisite for business success—embedded in decision-making.
- Cross-industry, cross-functional exposure across independent group companies fostered leadership mindset that transcended individual business boundaries.

## Open Question
How can centralized leadership development programmes maintain rigor and cross-company mobility in federated group structures where autonomous companies resist talent mandates and control career paths?

## India Angle
TAS exemplifies how Indian conglomerates can develop leaders with nation-building mindset; model relevant for building organizational capability amid India's complex regulatory and infrastructure challenges post-1991 reforms.
