## Core Argument
Platform strategy now defines competitive survival; understanding their unintended consequences is urgent.

## Key Points
- Platforms invert traditional pipeline economics; firms not adopting platform rules risk obsolescence.
- Platform successes (Zomato Gold) often contain structural conflicts between aggregators and participants that explode.
- Regulation lag: Big Tech dominance persists because incumbents lack tools and mindset to compete; self-regulation fails.

## Open Question
How can incumbents or governments effectively regulate platforms without stifling innovation, given that platforms' scale and network effects make traditional competition frameworks obsolete?

## India Angle
India's platform economy (Zomato, Aadhaar, gig work via Fiverr) is rapidly reshaping business competition and governance. Indian leaders and policymakers need frameworks to understand platform dynamics, regulatory tradeoffs, and societal consequences specific to India's scale and heterogeneity.
