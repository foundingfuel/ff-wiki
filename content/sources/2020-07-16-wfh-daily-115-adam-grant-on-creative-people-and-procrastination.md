## Core Argument
Procrastination delays task completion but enables creative breakthroughs through expanded thinking time.

## Key Points
- Adam Grant shows creative people aren't first movers; they procrastinate strategically to refine ideas
- Deep listening requires moving beyond debate to understand others' worldviews and challenge one's own assumptions
- Finding 'why' requires asking uncomfortable questions until surface-level answers collapse into deeper purpose

## Open Question
How do organizations distinguish between productive procrastination that generates originality and unproductive procrastination that kills deadlines?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and creative professionals often face pressure for immediate productivity; understanding procrastination as a creative tool could reshape how Indian organizations evaluate performance and manage innovation teams.
