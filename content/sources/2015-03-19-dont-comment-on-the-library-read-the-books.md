## Core Argument
Evaluate companies on internal metrics and unit economics, not market sentiment or revenue alone.

## Key Points
- Value (internal metrics, unit economics) matters more than price; understand cost-per-user growth sustainability.
- Network effects, not revenue alone, signal real moat; time spent is proxy for future monetization.
- India's regulatory burden and immature VC ecosystem push founders toward Silicon Valley; structural reform needed.

## Open Question
Can India build global internet product companies with structural disadvantages (regulations, VC maturity), or is Silicon Valley relocation inevitable for founders seeking scale?

## India Angle
India lacks entrepreneur-friendly culture, regulatory clarity, and venture capital maturity compared to Silicon Valley. While companies like Flipkart and Druva exist, building global internet product companies from India faces structural headwinds that force founders to relocate or seek overseas capital.
