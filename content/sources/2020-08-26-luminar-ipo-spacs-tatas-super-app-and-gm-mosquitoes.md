## Core Argument
Emerging markets race for super app dominance while breakthrough tech faces implementation and unintended consequence risks.

## Key Points
- Tata Digital's super app must balance three factors: core use case frequency, payment systems with loyalty, and adjacent service ecosystem.
- SPACs democratizing deep-tech funding (lidar sensors, autonomous vehicles) at scale, reshaping how frontier companies go public.
- GM mosquito releases show transformative biotech solutions carry ecological risks requiring rigorous local testing before scaled deployment.

## Open Question
How do breakthrough technologies balance speed-to-scale with precautionary testing when local ecosystems and public trust are at stake?

## India Angle
Tata's super app launch directly challenges Amazon and Jio Platform dominance in India's emerging digital ecosystem; GM mosquitoes example reflects India's vector-borne disease burden (dengue, chikungunya); Luminar/SPACs show global deep-tech funding trends India must navigate for autonomous vehicles sector.
