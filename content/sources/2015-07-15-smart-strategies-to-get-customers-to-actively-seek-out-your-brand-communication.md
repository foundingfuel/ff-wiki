## Core Argument
Customers avoid ads; make communication customer-centric, contextual, and two-way to get them seeking your brand.

## Key Points
- Intrusive, one-way mass advertising triggers customer avoidance; context and location matter.
- Content marketing, rewards, and geo-fencing put customer interest first, inverting the chase dynamic.
- Personalized, location-specific, two-way communication generates higher engagement than generic mass campaigns.

## Open Question
How do brands balance personalization at scale with data privacy concerns and customer trust in collecting location/behavioral data?

## India Angle
Indian brands and SMEs often rely on traditional mass advertising; this framework helps them transition to customer-centric, context-aware communication strategies that work within India's diverse, mobile-first market.
