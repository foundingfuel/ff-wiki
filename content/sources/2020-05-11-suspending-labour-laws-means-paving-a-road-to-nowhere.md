## Core Argument
Wholesale labour law suspension is authoritarian overreach masquerading as reform, rolling back century of worker protections.

## Key Points
- Measured reform is possible without annulling laws; industry has already adapted informally over two decades.
- Suspending protections for vulnerable workers violates constitutional democracy principles and invites social conflict.
- Laws must evolve to balance competing interests; destruction of entire legal frameworks creates anarchy, not competitiveness.

## Open Question
How can India attract global investment and improve competitiveness while maintaining constitutional protections for workers, when leaders see these as mutually exclusive?

## India Angle
UP and MP's suspension of 35+ labour laws in pursuit of competitiveness represents a fundamental misunderstanding of how Indian constitutional democracies should operate, with consequences for worker protections and social stability unique to India's informal economy.
