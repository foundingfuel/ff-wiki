## Core Argument
MBA value proposition shifts in uncertainty; reassess costs, timing, and peer-learning viability before committing.

## Key Points
- Rs 1.5 crore US MBA cost vs opportunity cost of two years away from work growth.
- Peer networking—core MBA value—compromised by online-first delivery and social distancing.
- One-year India/Singapore programs offer faster, cheaper alternative with comparable network access.

## Open Question
How do you evaluate whether peer-bonding and on-campus experience are essential MBA value, or if remote learning plus work experience provides equivalent ROI in a post-COVID world?

## India Angle
Indian professionals increasingly face MBA trade-offs between expensive foreign degrees and domestic options; post-COVID, India-based programs emerge as viable alternatives, shifting traditional CXO pipeline assumptions for Indian firms.
