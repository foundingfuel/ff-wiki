## Core Argument
AI-generated content threatens traditional credential shortcuts, exposing deeper crisis in education quality and employment.

## Key Points
- Kerala chayakadas run a thriving dissertation mill business for unemployed literates and degree-seekers seeking shortcuts.
- AI tools like Spinbot and SCIgen now commoditize content creation, undercutting the informal economy of credential fraud.
- Fundamental mismatch between education output and job market demand creates conditions for both fraud and AI disruption.

## Open Question
If AI makes dissertation mills obsolete, does this solve the underlying education-employment gap or simply expose it more harshly?

## India Angle
Kerala's literate unemployment crisis has created a shadow economy of dissertation mills and academic fraud. AI commoditization now threatens this informal ecosystem, exposing systemic failures in India's education system and skill-job mismatch.
