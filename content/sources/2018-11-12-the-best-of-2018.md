## Core Argument
True entrepreneurship requires purpose-driven leadership, first-principles thinking, and long-term vision over reactive decision-making.

## Key Points
- Engagement over eyeballs: technology firms must measure impact by user permeation, not scale alone.
- B-schools have devolved into placement factories, dehumanizing talent development and weakening India's management capability.
- Purpose-driven enterprises achieve greatness; leaders must resist noise and maintain composure during setbacks.

## Open Question
If Indian B-schools are primarily placement agencies lacking rigorous research culture, where will future leaders with first-principles thinking and systemic understanding emerge?

## India Angle
India's entrepreneurial ecosystem—from startup-corporation partnerships to B-school education crisis to Aadhaar's systemic implications—reflects deeper governance and talent development challenges specific to India's institutions and markets.
