## Core Argument
Post-pandemic work transformation requires leaders to build trust, nurture gig-minded employees, and rethink execution models for complex environments.

## Key Points
- Hybrid work success depends on emotional trust, not just competence—leaders must invest in frequent contact and empathy.
- Organizations need employees with gig mindset who challenge assumptions, take initiative, and operate without waiting for approval.
- Government IT projects fail due to thin margins, unique complexity per project, and inadequate documentation transfer between initiatives.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically identify and nurture employees with challenging, non-conformist traits when traditional performance evaluations don't capture initiative and resilience?

## India Angle
Indian IT companies and leaders face distinct challenges in hybrid work transition, government project execution, and building entrepreneurial cultures amid India's unique political-business context.
