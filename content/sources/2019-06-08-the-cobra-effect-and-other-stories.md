## Core Argument
Well-intentioned tech interventions often produce opposite outcomes due to misaligned incentives and unforeseen human behavior.

## Key Points
- Cobra bounty example shows how financial incentives can create perverse outcomes when system design ignores behavioral responses.
- Concentration of power (Zuckerberg's dual-class shares) enables autonomy but reduces accountability—a structural governance tension.
- Gene editing in-situ approach mirrors moving from mechanical to systemic thinking in complex, living systems.

## Open Question
How do we design technology policies that align incentives with desired outcomes when human behavior is unpredictable and systems are complex?

## India Angle
India-specific example (cobra bounty in Delhi) illustrates unintended consequences of tech policies; relevant for Indian policymakers designing AI regulation and tech incentives.
