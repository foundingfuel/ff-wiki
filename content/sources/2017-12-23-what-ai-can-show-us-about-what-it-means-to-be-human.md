## Core Argument
AI reveals what makes us human by showing us what machines cannot replicate about human intelligence and communication.

## Key Points
- Communication bandwidth paradox: tools become more powerful while our interactions reduce to single bits of information.
- Algorithms are universal processes applicable to everyday life, revealing hidden complexity in seemingly simple human tasks.
- Understanding machine limitations teaches us about distinctly human qualities like idiosyncrasy, context-awareness, and nuanced expression.

## Open Question
How do we harness digital efficiency without homogenizing human expression and losing the idiosyncratic qualities that define authentic human connection?

## India Angle
As India rapidly digitizes and adopts AI/automation, understanding the human cost of efficiency-obsessed systems becomes critical for Indian leaders building tech-enabled organizations and policy frameworks that don't sacrifice human connection and nuance.
