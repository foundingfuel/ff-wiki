## Core Argument
Consumer behaviour post-Covid favours comfort and familiarity over novelty; brands must actively engage to retain share.

## Key Points
- Millennials gravitate toward comfort brands offering predictable experiences during uncertainty
- Nostalgia becomes a purchasing driver when confidence in future is low
- Passive brand desirability insufficient; active market presence required to win share

## Open Question
Which changed shopping habits are temporary crisis responses versus permanent shifts in consumer values and brand loyalty?

## India Angle
Indian retailers and FMCG brands must understand shifting consumer preferences post-Covid; generational differences in shopping behaviour will determine market share winners in India's recovery
