## Core Argument
AI will displace banking jobs; India must learn from its 1950s-80s IT adoption to navigate this transition wisely.

## Key Points
- AI and RPA in Indian banking will cause significant job losses, similar to past IT disruptions.
- Historical parallels from 1950s-80s IT adoption show technology always sparks replacement debates with no easy answers.
- Governments must address reskilling, alternative employment, security, and whether automation serves majority interest.

## Open Question
How can India balance the efficiency gains and financial inclusion benefits of AI-driven digital banking with the need to protect millions of displaced workers and ensure equitable distribution of technology's benefits?

## India Angle
India's rapid digital banking transformation through UPI, demonetization, and AI adoption will displace millions of workers; policymakers must learn from India's 1950s-80s IT banking transition to manage reskilling, regulation, and social impact specific to India's employment context.
