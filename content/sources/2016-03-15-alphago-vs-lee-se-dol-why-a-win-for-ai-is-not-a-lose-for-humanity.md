## Core Argument
AI milestones reveal human psychology about technology more than machine capability itself.

## Key Points
- AlphaGo's victory came 10-15 years earlier than predicted, showing AI acceleration speed.
- Humans struggle to trust machines as black boxes; we seek transparency and intentionality.
- Go's complexity (250 moves vs chess's 35) required AI to learn intuition, not brute force.

## Open Question
If AI becomes indistinguishable from human intelligence, should we demand explainability—and if yes, does that expose the limits of our own understanding of strategy?

## India Angle
India needs to build AI literacy among leaders and public to avoid Kasparov-like resistance; critical for adopting AI in governance, business, and research without suspicion or rejection.
