## Core Argument
Strategic agility requires balancing short-term responsiveness with long-term impact through people, principles, processes, and technology.

## Key Points
- Bear and Hikers Paradigm prioritizes expedient action without long-term thinking; Broken Cup Paradigm creates learning moments from failures.
- Strategic agility depends on four enablers: processes and technologies drive short-term performance; people and principles drive long-term excellence.
- Successful companies like Cisco and P&G align processes and leader behaviors with core values to execute strategy consistently.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain the discipline of long-term principles and people-focused culture while moving fast enough to respond to immediate market threats?

## India Angle
Indian companies scaling rapidly must balance speed with sustainable practices; critical for firms navigating volatility in competitive markets while building lasting organizational culture and trust.
