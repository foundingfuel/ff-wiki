## Core Argument
Minimize costly commission errors over omission errors by understanding decision trade-offs.

## Key Points
- Type 1 (commission) and Type 2 (omission) errors cannot both be minimized simultaneously in decisions.
- Individual investors should accept omission errors to protect capital; fund managers must avoid commission errors to retain fees.
- Average investors benefit from low-cost ETFs over active management unless managers demonstrably outperform consistently.

## Open Question
How should fund managers balance fiduciary duty to generate superior returns against structural incentives that push them toward market-tracking strategies?
