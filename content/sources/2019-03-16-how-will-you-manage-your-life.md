## Core Argument
Living deliberately means prioritizing relationships over career metrics; most people drift into unintended lives by default.

## Key Points
- Success metrics based on others' perceptions drive self-destructive behavior and prevent authentic living.
- Immediate gratification from career pushes people to neglect family, causing regret and alienation later.
- Meaningful relationships deliver exponentially higher happiness returns than professional achievement, but take 20+ years to materialize.

## Open Question
If returns on relationships take 20 years to manifest, how do high-achievers rewire their brains to prioritize delayed gratification in a world that rewards immediate results?

## India Angle
Indian high-achievers, especially from premier institutions, often chase external validation and career success at the cost of personal relationships and mental health—a cultural pattern that needs reexamination
