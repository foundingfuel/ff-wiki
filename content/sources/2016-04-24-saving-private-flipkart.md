## Core Argument
Flipkart's GMV obsession and operational myopia cost it market leadership to Amazon in India's e-commerce race.

## Key Points
- Over-reliance on smartphone sales (50% GMV) made Flipkart vulnerable as Amazon diversified category offerings.
- Product experience gaps—poor search, weak personalization, limited selection—alienated users despite market leadership.
- Strategic distractions (app-only pivot, payment infrastructure experiments) diverted focus from core execution problems.

## Open Question
How can rapidly scaling startups balance growth metrics that attract investors with the product excellence and diversification needed for sustainable market dominance?

## India Angle
Flipkart's strategic missteps reveal critical lessons for Indian startups scaling in competitive markets—overreliance on one metric (GMV) and failure to diversify weakened India's e-commerce champion against global competitor Amazon.
