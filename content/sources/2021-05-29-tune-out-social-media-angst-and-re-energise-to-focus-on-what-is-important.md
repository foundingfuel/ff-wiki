## Core Argument
Reclaim focus from social media distractions by deliberately managing attention and organizing work into purposeful weekly chunks.

## Key Points
- Social media and messaging apps trap intelligent people through FOMO, eroding time for quality work and relationships
- Voluntary attention requires three skills: disengaging from distractions, resisting distraction, and maintaining long-term focus
- Breaking work into weekly chunks with checklists reveals true time availability and clarifies priorities between routine and transformative work

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers balance legitimate civic responsibility to stay informed during crises like pandemics with the psychological need to protect focus and mental health?

## India Angle
Relevant for Indian knowledge workers caught between civic engagement demands (pandemic, political discourse) and deep work requirements; applies to how Indian professionals navigate information overload and social pressure on messaging platforms like WhatsApp
