## Core Argument
Humour and transparency about failure reframe it as learning, enabling leaders and institutions to collaborate effectively.

## Key Points
- Bessemer Venture Partners publicly lists failures via 'Anti-portfolio' to normalise and learn from missed opportunities.
- India's crisis response reveals no single company is large enough; cross-sector collaboration becomes pragmatic necessity.
- Humour dispels blame and allows teams to process failure constructively without defensive behaviour.

## Open Question
How do Indian organisations balance the cultural expectation of infallibility with the leadership need to publicly acknowledge and learn from failure?

## India Angle
Indian startups and institutions must embrace collaborative failure-sharing like Bessemer's model; India's fragmented healthcare sector exemplifies why no single player can solve systemic crises alone, requiring unprecedented cross-sector partnerships.
