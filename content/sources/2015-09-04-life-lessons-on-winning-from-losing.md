## Core Argument
High performers redefine failure as learning; the journey matters more than destination or societal markers.

## Key Points
- Losing and setbacks teach resilience; Mark Wooley returned three times despite missing cutoffs by minutes.
- Background irrelevant to potential; Parvez Malik went from rag-picker to ultra-marathoner in six months.
- Champions adapt in real-time; Sean Maley succeeded by treating 333km as series of micro-challenges, not one plan.

## Open Question
How do you keep high performers grounded after success without dampening their confidence and momentum for future challenges?

## India Angle
Indian context of breaking status quo — from rag-picker Parvez Malik to challenging societal definitions of 'loser'; relevant to how India views merit, background, and resilience in achievement.
