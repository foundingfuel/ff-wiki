## Core Argument
Great Indian leaders embody courage, thoughtfulness, and kindness grounded in continuous professional knowledge.

## Key Points
- Professional competence requires constant study and cannot be assumed upon reaching power—essential for Indian leaders.
- Systems thinking in healthcare—Dr Swaminathan's pandemic preparedness highlights India's vulnerability without primary health infrastructure.
- Moral courage to stand ground against pressure, as Manekshaw did with Indira Gandhi, defines authentic leadership.

## Open Question
How can Indian institutions systematically develop professional knowledge and moral courage in leaders when power often breeds complacency and deference to hierarchy?

## India Angle
Indian leaders across defence, healthcare, and public service must cultivate professional knowledge and systems thinking to navigate complex challenges; addresses India's leadership gaps in critical sectors.
