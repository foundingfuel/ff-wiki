## Core Argument
Facebook's crisis stems from both flawed business model and toxic leadership culture prioritizing speed over responsibility.

## Key Points
- Business model (data collection + ad sales) incentivizes decisions that harm users; culture ('move fast, break things') amplifies this.
- Three categories of harm: addictive design, susceptibility to misuse (Cambridge Analytica, Myanmar, India), and democratic/social destabilization.
- Leadership accountability matters: Zuckerberg and Sandberg's choices and lack of will to change enabled systemic problems.

## Open Question
If the business model is fundamentally flawed, can cultural change alone fix Facebook, or must the model itself be transformed?

## India Angle
India-specific: Facebook/WhatsApp used to spread fake news, fan hatred, polarize society; platform susceptibility to misuse affecting Indian democracy and communal harmony directly mentioned.
