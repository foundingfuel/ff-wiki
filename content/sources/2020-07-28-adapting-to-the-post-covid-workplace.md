## Core Argument
WFH success requires both generational humility and technology adoption; leadership transcends digital literacy.

## Key Points
- Gen Z's always-online comfort is advantage, but not substitute for core leadership instincts and emotional intelligence.
- Unstructured interaction and nonverbal cues critical for organizational culture; remote work removes these informal learning spaces.
- Older leaders must overcome technology friction with curiosity; younger cohorts must recognize peer competition neutralizes digital advantage.

## Open Question
If remote work removes informal mentoring and nonverbal communication spaces where younger employees learn tacit leadership skills, how will future leaders develop the social and emotional capabilities that digital natives currently lack?

## India Angle
Indian businesses navigating hybrid work must balance generational digital divides while leveraging tech-native talent; leadership capability remains non-negotiable across age groups in competitive Indian markets.
