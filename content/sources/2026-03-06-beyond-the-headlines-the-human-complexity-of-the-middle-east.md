## Core Argument
Understanding the Middle East requires moving beyond headlines to lived experiences, history, and cultural expression.

## Key Points
- Monolithic narratives erase individual agency; Iranians and Israelis are complex people, not regime representatives.
- Cultural works—films, books, music—reveal emotional and psychological truths that political analysis alone cannot capture.
- Art and storytelling become acts of resistance, transforming trauma into meaning and freedom.

## Open Question
If art and personal narratives are essential to understanding geopolitical reality, how do we scale that empathetic understanding into actual policy decisions made in crisis moments?

## India Angle
Indian media consumers and policymakers often rely on Western narratives about the Middle East; understanding regional complexity through art and literature builds critical thinking essential for India's own geopolitical positioning and cross-cultural dialogue.
