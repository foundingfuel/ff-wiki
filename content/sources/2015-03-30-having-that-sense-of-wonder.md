## Core Argument
Founders need wonder, not reductive thinking, to create; different competencies suit different organizational phases.

## Key Points
- Brahma (creator), Vishnu (manager), Shiva (destroyer) represent distinct competencies needed across organizational lifecycle.
- Founders excel at creation through wonder but struggle with creative destruction and strategic disengagement.
- Successful enterprises require mutual respect, complementary skills, and clear boundaries between roles—not superhuman versatility.

## Open Question
How can founders maintain their sense of wonder while simultaneously focusing narrowly on one or two product lines to differentiate and succeed?
