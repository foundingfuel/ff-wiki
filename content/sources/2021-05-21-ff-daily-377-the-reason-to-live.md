## Core Argument
Finding meaning in mortality and limiting beliefs liberates us to live fully and imaginatively.

## Key Points
- Beauty and meaning exist in small moments; death's inevitability can inspire rather than paralyze living.
- Play and adaptation are human responses to life's necessities; we can creatively respond to mortality too.
- Wealth concentration in India shows inherited privilege dominance; systemic rules matter less than principles guiding leaders.

## Open Question
How do we balance systemic rules and internal democracy in organizations when concentrating power becomes tempting, especially when leaders face insecurity or resource constraints?

## India Angle
India has the third-largest billionaire population with highest proportion of inherited wealth, raising questions about crony capitalism and wealth concentration; also reflects on how Indian leaders navigate internal party democracy versus autocratic control.
