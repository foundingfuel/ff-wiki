## Core Argument
Aadhaar combines idealistic openness with pragmatic implementation, requiring judiciary intervention to protect privacy while enabling digital transformation.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar shifted from voluntary to mandatory, violating original spirit and consent framework despite eKYC safeguards.
- Project combines Stallmanian idealism (freedom, openness) with Torvaldian pragmatism (Linux-like collaboration).
- Judiciary intervention essential to establish privacy protection precedent India can offer the world.

## Open Question
How can Aadhaar remain true to its voluntary, consent-based origins while governments make it de facto mandatory for citizen access to services?

## India Angle
Aadhaar represents India's ambitious attempt to provide digital identity to 1.3+ billion citizens, raising critical questions about privacy rights, state capacity, and whether India can set global precedent for balancing digital infrastructure with individual freedoms.
