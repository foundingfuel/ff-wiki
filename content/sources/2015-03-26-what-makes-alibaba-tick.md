## Core Argument
Alibaba succeeded by solving local problems with ecosystem-thinking, not by copying Western models.

## Key Points
- Identified gap: Chinese small businesses couldn't connect globally or domestically; Alibaba built the bridge.
- Rejected Western playbook: free listings, TV ads, customer-friendly design tailored to Chinese behavior patterns.
- Built integrated ecosystem: payments, logistics, lending, search—owned what didn't exist rather than waiting.

## Open Question
Does Alibaba's success depend on China's unique conditions (fragmentation, consumer behavior, regulatory environment), or can this playbook be replicated in other emerging markets like India?

## India Angle
India's e-commerce landscape mirrors China's fragmentation: small manufacturers lack buyer connectivity, language/payment barriers exist, and local platforms must outmaneuver global giants by understanding regional psyche and building integrated ecosystems rather than importing Western playbooks.
