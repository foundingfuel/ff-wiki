## Core Argument
IIT education creates overconfident technologists unprepared for ambiguous, real-world entrepreneurial challenges.

## Key Points
- IIT entrance exam measures test-solving speed, not real-world problem-solving or entrepreneurial capability.
- STEM education optimises for well-defined problems; startups demand navigating ambiguity and asking right questions.
- Liberal arts education teaches systems thinking, empathy, and social awareness absent from pure engineering training.

## Open Question
If IIT degrees signal merit and unlock funding for founders, why should talented engineers reject the institution despite its limitations?

## India Angle
IIT degrees dominate India's merit narrative and access to capital; challenging this signals need for broader skill development in Indian entrepreneurship ecosystem
