## Core Argument
Belonging and digital communication clarity are antidotes to burnout and disengagement in hybrid work.

## Key Points
- 62% of employees overwhelmed; belonging through recognition, one-on-ones, and group activities reduces stress.
- 70% of team communication now virtual; digital body language (punctuation, response times, calls) signals trust.
- Leaders must read carefully, write clearly, and use calls to bridge non-verbal cue gaps and prevent disengagement.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale belonging initiatives without creating performative team activities that deepen cynicism among already burnt-out employees?

## India Angle
Indian organizations scaling rapidly face burnout and disengagement crises; hybrid work norms demand new communication protocols that Indian leaders must master to retain talent amid the Great Resignation.
