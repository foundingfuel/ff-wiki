## Core Argument
Global order faces existential fragmentation as major powers abandon international agreements and pursue transactional nationalism.

## Key Points
- US-China tensions may stabilize under Trump, but US defense spending ($1T+) cannot achieve geopolitical objectives across Europe, Middle East, Indo-Pacific.
- Western alliance credibility erodes as US threatens territorial renegotiations; Europe must balance rule-based order with security dependence on America.
- International law collapses when powerful nations ignore signed agreements (Russia-Budapest, Israel-Oslo, Trump-Panama); undermines predictability in global relations.

## Open Question
How can democracies and authoritarian regimes maintain working relationships when the foundation of predictable international law is collapsing?

## India Angle
India navigates US-China tensions, NATO realignment, and trade disruption; must balance great power relationships while protecting its own strategic autonomy and economic interests amid unpredictable Western alliance shifts.
