## Core Argument
Solo wilderness immersion forces confrontation with self-identity and reveals deeper truths about meaning beyond professional achievement.

## Key Points
- WFH paradoxically blurred boundaries, created anxiety, and eroded sense of self despite perceived harmony.
- Turning 50 catalyzed deliberate choice to prioritize self-love over career, triggering transformational journey.
- Wilderness exposure—mountains, temples, local communities—reconnected author to authentic identity and sense of aliveness.

## Open Question
How do professionals sustain this inner transformation upon return to structured urban life and work systems that may not support the values rediscovered on the journey?
