## Core Argument
Companies reduce costs and boost satisfaction by converting customers into unpaid partial or full-time employees.

## Key Points
- Airlines, banks, and couriers outsource tasks to customers via digital platforms without compensation.
- Customers internalize service failures as their own responsibility, reducing complaint likelihood.
- Digital infrastructure and customer time-poverty enable willing participation in this model.

## Open Question
How sustainable is customer satisfaction when the satisfaction stems from customers blaming themselves rather than the brand, and what happens when this model reaches digital adoption saturation?

## India Angle
Indian companies across airlines, banking, and retail can adopt this model to reduce operational costs while maintaining service quality—critical given India's price-sensitive markets and labor cost pressures. However, this requires addressing digital literacy gaps and ensuring equitable burden-shifting.
