## Core Argument
Cultivating questioning skills—not answers—drives innovation and is critical for founders and leaders.

## Key Points
- Educational systems teach answers not questions, suppressing natural curiosity and innovation capacity.
- Structured techniques like Question Formulation Technique and 5 Whys enable systematic questioning at any skill level.
- Strategic questions (why, what-if, how) unlock clarity, ownership, and breakthrough thinking in organisations.

## Open Question
If questioning is vital to innovation, why do schools and organisations continue to reward answer-giving over question-asking, and how do we shift this deeply entrenched incentive structure?

## India Angle
Indian education system and organisations heavily emphasise rote answers over inquiry; founders and leaders need deliberate frameworks to build questioning culture for innovation competitiveness.
