## Core Argument
Single stories limit problem-solving; systems thinking and contextual awareness reveal hidden possibilities.

## Key Points
- Context shapes interpretation: same stimulus read differently based on surrounding information and timing.
- Systems change requires multiple lenses: broad, hidden dynamics, adaptive approaches, and local knowledge integration.
- Emotional connection through care builds loyalty better than authority; meaningful gestures breed commitment.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance vision-oriented change with truly adaptive, locally-determined outcomes without losing strategic coherence?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations need to move beyond single narratives about employees, markets, and solutions—particularly relevant given India's cultural diversity and complexity. The grandmother's approach to worker loyalty through care reflects Indian organizational wisdom that modern leaders can leverage.
