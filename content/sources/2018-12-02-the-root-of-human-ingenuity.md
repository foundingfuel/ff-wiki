## Core Argument
Curiosity is the foundation of innovation; modern corporate culture systematically suppresses it in favor of measurable outcomes.

## Key Points
- Curiosity dismissed as waste of time in corporate culture obsessed with tangible metrics and quarterly results.
- Curious people drive big changes by staying open to learning, experimenting, and building alternative realities.
- Using 'How might we...' questions unlocks collective possibility and constructive debate in teams and organizations.

## Open Question
How can organizations maintain operational efficiency and quarterly accountability while creating space for the unstructured exploration that genuine innovation requires?

## India Angle
Indian corporations often prioritize short-term metrics and quarterly results over curiosity-driven exploration; fostering a culture of questioning and experimentation is critical for Indian startups and enterprises to compete in rapidly transforming global markets.
