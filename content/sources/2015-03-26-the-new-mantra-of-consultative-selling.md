## Core Argument
Transform salespeople into consultants prioritizing customer objectives over immediate sales for lifetime value.

## Key Points
- Consultative selling builds long-term customer relationships by prioritizing customer needs over immediate transactions.
- Recommending competitors when appropriate builds trust and triggers reciprocal goodwill that drives future business.
- Apple Retail demonstrates success through Genius Bars and training studios, monetizing relationships over customer lifetime.

## Open Question
How can companies ensure salespeople genuinely prioritize customer interests over personal/company targets when compensation systems still reward short-term sales?

## India Angle
Indian companies competing globally and domestically need to shift from transactional sales to relationship-based consulting models; this is particularly relevant for Indian retail, tech, and service sectors building brand loyalty in price-sensitive markets.
