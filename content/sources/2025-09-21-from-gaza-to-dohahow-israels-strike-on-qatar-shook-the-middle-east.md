## Core Argument
Israel's Doha strike signals regional realignment where military escalation, economic interests, and great power competition override traditional alliances.

## Key Points
- Israel's strike on Qatar exposed US-Israel contradictions and Arab divisions driven by economic pragmatism over solidarity.
- Netanyahu's coalition instability and personal political survival may be driving military strategy more than national interest.
- Gaza redevelopment plans suggest war's endgame is territorial control and asset capture, not security resolution.

## Open Question
How can Washington maintain its role as regional security guarantor while tacitly endorsing Israel's unilateral escalation and profiteering schemes that antagonize traditional Arab allies?

## India Angle
India must navigate Middle East realignment as traditional US-led order fractures; China's growing influence in Gulf affects India's energy security and regional partnerships; implications for India's balancing act between US and China.
