## Core Argument
Organizational integrity is a reputational investment with ROI; choose values or convenience.

## Key Points
- Integrity enforcement inconsistently applied based on employee hierarchy signals organizational culture more than stated policies.
- High-performer dishonesty is a false economy; reputation attracts better clients, talent, and market valuation long-term.
- Leaders must resolve personal conflict between right and convenient; this choice cascades through organization via social memory.

## Open Question
Can an organization maintain both integrity and competitiveness in a 'scam-ridden' ecosystem, or is ethical choice necessarily a longer, riskier road to success?

## India Angle
In Indian business context where governance lapses are endemic, leaders must choose between short-term competitive advantage (exploiting 'Indian reality') and long-term reputational capital—especially relevant for global aspirations and attracting quality talent and clients.
