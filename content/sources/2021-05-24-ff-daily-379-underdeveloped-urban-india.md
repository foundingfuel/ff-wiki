## Core Argument
Policy obsession with megacities has left smaller Indian cities with severe infrastructure deficits and unmet basic service needs.

## Key Points
- 70% of Indian urban dwellers live in cities under 1M population, yet policy investment concentrates on megacities
- PPP models for water and electricity failed due to illegal connections, poor cost recovery, and private sector financial constraints
- Smaller cities and census towns remain chronically underdeveloped despite being urbanization hotspots

## Open Question
If smaller cities drive bulk of India's urbanization, why do policymakers persist in treating them as secondary despite decades of evidence that mega-city focus fails most urban Indians?

## India Angle
Urban India's infrastructure crisis disproportionately affects 70% of city dwellers in towns under 1M population, revealing policy bias toward megacities and failed PPP models—a systemic governance and planning failure unique to India's urbanization pattern.
