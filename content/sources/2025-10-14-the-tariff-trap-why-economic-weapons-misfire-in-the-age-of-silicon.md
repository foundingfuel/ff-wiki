## Core Argument
Tariffs weaponize outdated trade logic against semiconductors' deeply interdependent global ecosystem, causing strategic self-harm.

## Key Points
- TSMC's monopoly on 90% of advanced chips creates structural vulnerability no tariff can solve.
- Semiconductor manufacturing is a globally orchestrated ecosystem spanning design, equipment, materials, and testing across continents.
- Tariffs increase costs for domestic players like Intel without creating alternative suppliers, representing economic backfire.

## Open Question
How can nations achieve semiconductor self-sufficiency without tariff walls, when building equivalent ecosystems takes decades and hundreds of billions of dollars?

## India Angle
India depends on global chip supply chains for tech startups, electronics manufacturing, and defense capabilities. Tariff wars and supply chain fragmentation directly threaten India's semiconductor ambitions and ATMANIRBHAR goals; India lacks TSMC-equivalent fabs and must navigate geopolitical tensions while building domestic capacity.
