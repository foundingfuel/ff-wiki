## Core Argument
Fundamental leadership practices endure; style adapts but principles remain constant across eras.

## Key Points
- Six timeless practices unite vision, strategy, talent, results, innovation, and self-leadership.
- Post-2020 startups prioritize essentials, viability, and inclusion over growth-at-all-costs.
- Investing in people (gig workers, solopreneurs) creates loyalty and word-of-mouth; right action matters beyond ROI.

## Open Question
If principles are timeless, how do leaders know which practices to emphasize when context shifts radically—as in 2020's remote work transition—without simply adapting style while ignoring strategic restructuring?

## India Angle
Indian startups and leaders navigating post-COVID transformation must balance fundamental leadership principles with new realities—investing in gig worker welfare (The Urban Company model) shows how classic practices like talent development apply to India's informal economy.
