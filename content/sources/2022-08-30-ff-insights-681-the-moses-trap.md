## Core Argument
Visionary leaders who become too attached to their own ideas risk institutional decline and loss of purpose.

## Key Points
- Edwin Land's Polaroid invented breakthrough technology but declined because leadership fell in love with its own ideas.
- Uddhav Thackeray's remaking of Shiv Sena alienated old guard who thrived on street politics and ground-level shakha work.
- Organisational transformation without acknowledging core identity and constituent needs creates internal rebellion and vulnerability to rivals.

## Open Question
How can transformational leaders evolve organisations and ideas without severing connection to the base that built institutional power in the first place?

## India Angle
Applies to Indian organisations and political institutions—how founders/leaders who fall in love with their ideas (like Uddhav Thackeray's transformation of Shiv Sena) risk losing ground to competitors and internal rebellion.
