## Core Argument
Creativity requires deliberate time for ideas to mature; productivity paradoxically comes from strategic stillness.

## Key Points
- Leonardo da Vinci would stare at paintings for hours, making single strokes—proving genius requires incubation time.
- Thoughtless action (tree planting without local knowledge) causes harm; deep engagement beats performative activism.
- Technology's ultimate purpose may be eliminating mortality scarcity, not just reducing material scarcity.

## Open Question
How do we reconcile the modern demand for rapid execution and visible progress with the reality that breakthrough thinking requires extended contemplation and 'marination'?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and entrepreneurs need to embrace 'productive procrastination' — deep thinking time — rather than defaulting to constant busyness; this challenges India's productivity-obsessed work culture.
