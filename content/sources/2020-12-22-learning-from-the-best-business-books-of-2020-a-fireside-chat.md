## Core Argument
Organizations must become agile, adaptive learners capable of confronting reality while preparing for unpredictable crises.

## Key Points
- Pandemic revealed importance of functional government, expert reliance, and transparent communication over ideology.
- Female leaders excelled through empathy, science-based decisions, and stakeholder buy-in—not gender but leadership approach.
- GE's decline shows risks of cultural denial, personal aggrandizement, stock-focused strategy, and inability to adapt.

## Open Question
How can organizations prepare for unpredictable black swan events when no predictive model extends beyond 400 days and certainty is impossible?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders need to adopt crisis leadership practices from global examples; Indian book publishing should prioritize research-backed, balanced narratives over sycophancy or negativity to influence leadership thinking.
