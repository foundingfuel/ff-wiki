## Core Argument
Breaking into India's auto market requires a decade of learning, local design studios, and frugal engineering mindset.

## Key Points
- Indian markets are fundamentally different from other emerging markets; global designs cannot be copied without local adaptation.
- Success requires 98% localization, investing in Indian suppliers, and design studios staffed by Indian engineers who understand local aspirations.
- Leadership must align global talent to market realities; sophisticated design sensibilities must be tempered by India's unique affordability-aspiration paradox.

## Open Question
How can global companies balance the need for sophisticated product design with the risk that their expertise becomes disconnected from emerging market affordability demands?

## India Angle
India's unique market complexity demands tailored strategies, not global copy-paste approaches. Success requires understanding demanding first-time buyers, building local supplier ecosystems, and designing products that balance aspirations with affordability—lessons critical for both global and Indian automakers.
