## Core Argument
Crisis reveals organisations have untapped innovation capacity—focus, resourcefulness, and learning culture enable rapid problem-solving.

## Key Points
- Companies can innovate beyond narrow strategic alignment by leveraging core competencies in unexpected ways.
- Organisational focus and concentrated resources drive speed to market; crisis removes ambiguity about priorities.
- Failed projects yield learning; AI and data analytics amplify capacity to extract and scale insights.

## Open Question
If crisis-driven focus and resource concentration unlock innovation capability, why do normal market pressures not generate equivalent urgency and why is maintaining this mindset in non-emergency conditions so difficult?

## India Angle
Indian companies (Mahindra, Maruti, Mylab Discovery Solutions, Eyestem) demonstrated rapid innovation capability during Covid-19, revealing untapped potential for adjacency-based diversification and agile execution that Indian firms can leverage in normal times for competitive advantage.
