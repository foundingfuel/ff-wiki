## Core Argument
Sustainable fitness requires disciplined basics repeated daily, not magical shortcuts or complicated trendy exercises.

## Key Points
- Consistency and discipline with simple exercises outperform complex routines without commitment across geographies.
- Structured, progressively challenging basic movements address root causes like poor posture from prolonged sitting.
- Mindfulness through breathing and body awareness transforms fitness from external prescription to self-directed habit.

## Open Question
How do you sustain behavioral change when initial motivation fades, and who ensures accountability without external coaching in resource-constrained settings?

## India Angle
Indian professionals, especially those in sedentary tech and corporate roles, face lifestyle-induced pain and deconditioning; this systemic approach offers an accessible, discipline-driven alternative to expensive treatments or trending fitness fads common in India's wellness industry.
