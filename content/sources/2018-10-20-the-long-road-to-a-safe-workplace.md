## Core Argument
Indian companies must shift from compliance checkboxes to genuine culture change to enable harassment reporting.

## Key Points
- 78% of sexual harassment cases go unreported; zero complaints often signal lack of trust, not absence of issues.
- Only 13 of Nifty50 companies report double-digit complaints; most lag because global operations forced early system adoption.
- Real change requires facilitated dialogue on consent and behaviour, not training programmes; culture takes years to embed.

## Open Question
How can organizations move from performative #metoo responses to genuine culture change when deep cultural beliefs about gender and consent remain unexamined?

## India Angle
India Inc lacks systemic safety infrastructure for workplace harassment; most companies show zero complaints despite underreporting being widespread, revealing cultural resistance and weak enforcement compared to global peers.
