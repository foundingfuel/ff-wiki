## Core Argument
Societies must choose between aggressive exploitation and cooperative restraint; institutions determine which strategy prevails.

## Key Points
- Game theory shows pure hawk or dove strategies fail; stable equilibrium requires mixed strategies within populations.
- Meta's metaverse success depends on whether regulatory frameworks can prevent repeating Facebook's data-exploitation model.
- Vaccine rollout decisions require balancing infection risk against long-term safety data—delaying creates its own risks.

## Open Question
How do societies build preventive institutions for emerging technologies without stifling innovation, when the costs of both failure and overregulation are unclear?

## India Angle
India faces hawk-dove dynamics in regulation (tech oversight, vaccine rollout), requiring institutional design that balances innovation with societal protection while building public trust in new technologies and health interventions.
