## Core Argument
Financial services must align with customers' actual life goals and systems, not assume universal adoption of formal solutions.

## Key Points
- Understanding context matters more than promoting technology; the unused LPG cylinder illustrates how rational people reject solutions misaligned with their lives.
- Underserved populations have functional informal financial and practical systems; formal finance must integrate with these, not replace them.
- COVID-19 exposed flawed policy design: debt-reduction criteria for relief excluded those who were financially prudent before the crisis.

## Open Question
How can formal financial systems scale to serve underserved populations without imposing Western-derived assumptions about what people need, when ground realities show people have already built functioning (if informal) solutions?

## India Angle
India's MSME ecosystem and financial inclusion reveal a fundamental mismatch between formal financial products and ground-level realities of underserved populations. Solutions must account for how poor households actually organize their lives and finances within existing social systems, not impose external logic.
