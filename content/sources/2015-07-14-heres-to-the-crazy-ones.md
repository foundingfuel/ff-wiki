## Core Argument
Entrepreneurship attracts and creates psychologically unconventional individuals; this is neither entirely pathological nor celebrated adequately.

## Key Points
- Hypomania and traits like ADHD/Asperger's may enable entrepreneurial success by providing obsessive focus, rapid decision-making, and belief in grand missions.
- Entrepreneurial life creates bipolar-like cycles of extreme highs and lows; a $200 crore company can collapse in years despite scale.
- Indian culture's dismissal of psychological counselling and business media's survivorship bias prevent honest conversation about entrepreneurship's mental toll.

## Open Question
If unconventional psychology is necessary for entrepreneurial ambition, how do we support mental health without diminishing the risk-taking that drives innovation?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs face intense pressure in a winner-takes-all digital economy with limited psychological support infrastructure; cultural stigma around mental health counselling exacerbates entrepreneurial stress.
