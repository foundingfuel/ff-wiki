## Core Argument
2021's key books reveal systemic shifts: companies must go net-positive, meritocracy faces backlash, remote work reshapes organizations.

## Key Points
- Companies must balance profit with social responsibility—serving poor profitably while addressing climate and inequality.
- Meritocracy faces ideological backlash; talent selection based on background increasingly challenges growth narratives.
- Remote work is permanent; organizations must rethink manager roles, employee connection, and organizational culture.

## Open Question
If merit-based advancement breeds resentment rather than acceptance, how do organizations maintain talent pipelines and legitimacy while pursuing innovation-driven growth?

## India Angle
Indian companies and policymakers must grapple with merit-based systems amid inequality, sustainability expectations from global markets, and remote work transitions affecting talent retention and organizational culture.
