## Core Argument
Scale demands IP protection; Alibaba shifts from 'Wild West' to credibility-driven enforcement strategy.

## Key Points
- Counterfeit goods undermine trust at scale—63% of brand goods on Taobao allegedly inauthentic, threatening global expansion.
- Regulatory pressure and brand lawsuits forced systemic change; hiring Apple's anti-counterfeit architect signals strategic commitment.
- Hybrid approach: tech tools (TaoProtect, Blue Stars), partnerships (Microsoft, Irdeto), and liability frameworks turn marketplace weakness into competitive advantage.

## Open Question
How can e-commerce platforms balance open-market access for sellers with guaranteeing authenticity—without becoming gatekeepers that stifle legitimate small merchants?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce platforms face identical IP/counterfeit challenges as Alibaba; Indian sellers on global marketplaces need robust IP frameworks; policy lesson for India's growing digital commerce sector on liability and enforcement mechanisms.
