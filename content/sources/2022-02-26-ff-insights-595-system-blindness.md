## Core Argument
Leaders fail to solve recurring problems because they ignore system dynamics and feedback loops underlying them.

## Key Points
- Building wider roads increases traffic long-term by enabling sprawl and incentivizing more car trips, not solving congestion.
- NSE governance failure reflects institutional blindness to how informal advice networks distort decision-making and accountability.
- Micromobility and digital systems reshape user behaviour unpredictably; designing for intended outcomes alone ignores emergent dynamics.

## Open Question
How can leaders design interventions when systems produce counter-intuitive outcomes that emerge only after implementation, making prevention impossible?

## India Angle
India's urban infrastructure challenges—traffic congestion in metros, regulatory blindness at institutions like NSE, and policy implementation gaps—stem from systemic thinking failures among leaders and policymakers who apply short-term fixes without understanding feedback loops.
