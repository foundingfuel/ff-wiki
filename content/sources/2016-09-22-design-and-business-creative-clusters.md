## Core Argument
Creative clusters succeed organically; government planning and real estate thinking routinely fail.

## Key Points
- Successful clusters (Silicon Valley, Milan) emerged organically, not by policy design or command.
- China's cluster strategy largely failed due to top-down governance, real estate focus, and competitive hoarding.
- Affordability, online connectivity, and genuine entrepreneurial community are more effective than physical spaces.

## Open Question
If clusters can't be planned, what role can government legitimately play—and does heavy investment in incubators like Dream Town repeat past mistakes or represent genuine learning?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers pursuing similar cluster strategies (design hubs, entertainment zones) should note: organic ecosystems outperform planned ones. India risks replicating China's top-down approach; affordability and genuine community matter more than infrastructure.
