## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs survive by leveraging allies' voices to amplify their contributions, not by shrinking.

## Key Points
- Women face penalization for self-advocacy while men are rewarded for same assertiveness behavior.
- Strategic ally-building with influential champions can amplify underheard voices in meetings and decisions.
- Workplace success requires rethinking communication strategy beyond 'soft' disagreement and pleasant challenge tactics.

## Open Question
How do women build genuine influence and credibility when success depends on male champions amplifying their work rather than their own direct advocacy?

## India Angle
Women entrepreneurs and professionals in India face specific workplace penalties for self-advocacy similar to Western contexts; Indian companies need to build ally networks and champion systems to amplify women's contributions in male-dominated sectors.
