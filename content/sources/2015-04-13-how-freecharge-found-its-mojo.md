## Core Argument
Founders who reframe problems, stay grounded, and attract investor trust can build platforms from low-margin categories.

## Key Points
- FreeCharge solved no marketing-cost customer acquisition by bundling coupons with mobile recharge transactions.
- Founder maturity—stepping aside, sharing equity, transparent partnerships—enabled scaling through leadership transitions.
- Technology infrastructure crises and cash scarcity are inevitable; persistence and willingness to relocate operations unlock growth.

## Open Question
How do founders balance rapid growth ambitions with technology infrastructure constraints, and when should they prioritize stability over scale?

## India Angle
FreeCharge's journey exemplifies how Indian founders can build large platforms from unglamorous categories (mobile recharge) by reframing the business model and scaling technology infrastructure—relevant to how Indian startups navigate capital constraints and talent scarcity in tier-2 cities.
