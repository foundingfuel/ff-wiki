## Core Argument
Indian internet unicorns face existential choice between monopoly dominance or failure; current consolidation signals systemic stress.

## Key Points
- Unicorns burning $100M-$3B via discounts/cashbacks without sustainable unit economics or clear moats.
- Strategic consolidation (Paytm acquiring Freecharge, Oyo acquiring Zo) suggests funding pressure and winner-takes-all dynamics.
- Investors focused only on growth rates and next funding round, ignoring cash burn and financial fundamentals.

## Open Question
If consolidation is driven by desperation rather than strategy, will the merged entities achieve sustainability or simply delay inevitable failure while destroying investor capital?

## India Angle
Indian unicorns are consolidating rapidly amid funding pressure, revealing unsustainable unit economics and questioning whether India's startup ecosystem can sustain companies without proven business models.
