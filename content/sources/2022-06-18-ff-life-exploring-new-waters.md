## Core Argument
Sailing is accessible recreation for all Indians via affordable clubs, training, and boat-leasing across multiple coastal cities.

## Key Points
- No prior fitness, swimming ability, or age restrictions needed; structured coaching available nationwide at sailing clubs.
- Multiple entry points: courses from Rs 15,000/season, boat leasing, or club memberships across Mumbai, Goa, Kerala.
- 50+ yacht clubs across 25 Indian cities provide infrastructure; Western coastline offers ideal wind and wave conditions.

## Open Question
How sustainable are sailing clubs and training infrastructure in India beyond major metros, and what prevents broader adoption despite low barriers to entry?

## India Angle
India's Western and Southern coastlines offer accessible, affordable sailing opportunities through clubs and training centres in Mumbai, Goa, Kerala—democratizing a sport often perceived as exclusive to the wealthy.
