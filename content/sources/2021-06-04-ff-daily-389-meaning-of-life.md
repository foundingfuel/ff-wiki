## Core Argument
True fulfillment requires authentic struggle, not engineered comfort; meaningful life demands real accomplishment over hedonic satisfaction.

## Key Points
- The paradox: eliminating mental suffering might eliminate the creativity and resilience that drives human achievement
- Fulfillment comes from authentic challenges and real accomplishments, not engineered happiness or avoiding discomfort
- Entrepreneurs must identify tectonic shifts and think 20 years ahead, trusting radical ideas while maintaining integrity in relationships

## Open Question
How do we balance the pursuit of meaningful achievement (which requires struggle) against the legitimate desire to reduce human suffering and mental illness?
