## Core Argument
High-wage retail models outperform low-wage competitors through operational excellence and employee investment.

## Key Points
- Low-wage strategy traps companies in vicious cycle: poor training, high turnover, inventory/service failures, lower profits.
- Best-performing retailers use four operational choices: offer less, standardize work, empower people, invest long-term.
- Mercadona, Costco, Trader Joe's, QuikTrip all achieve low prices, high wages, and superior financial metrics simultaneously.

## Open Question
If the good jobs model delivers superior financial returns and customer satisfaction, why do so few companies adopt it beyond a handful of successful outliers?

## India Angle
Indian retailers and quick-commerce firms face pressure to minimize labor costs; this research suggests a competitive moat through higher wages, better training, and operational excellence—directly applicable to India's retail transformation and gig economy challenges.
