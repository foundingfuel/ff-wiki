## Core Argument
Oscar predictions reveal how storytelling choices—adapting drug wars into immigration narratives—reflect contemporary cultural anxieties.

## Key Points
- One Battle After Another dominates predictions by reframing source material to address contemporary political anxieties around identity and immigration.
- Best Picture race shows tension between well-liked consensus pick (Sinners) and politically divisive frontrunner, reflecting broader audience polarization.
- Craft categories demonstrate how technical execution (editing, production design, visual effects) increasingly separates tier-one contenders from others.

## Open Question
Will the Academy reward a politically provocative film (One Battle After Another) or retreat to safer, more universally likeable choices (Sinners) when voting pressures mount?
