## Core Argument
Passion requires early freedom to explore, parental trust, and breathing space—not conformity to preset milestones.

## Key Points
- Parents who blur lines between passion/profession/interest plant seeds of possibility in children early
- Unstructured exploration time and gap years provide psychological safety to discover authentic passion without societal pressure
- Passion becomes sustainable only when it's intrinsically driven, all-consuming, and worthy of lifelong commitment

## Open Question
How do institutions and policy systems that demand early career specialization accommodate the non-linear, exploratory journeys passion actually requires?

## India Angle
Indian cultural pressure around 'log kya kahenge' and predetermined career paths (doctor/engineer) constrains passion discovery; contrasts Kiran's unconventional approach with mainstream Indian parenting norms
