## Core Argument
New Tata chairman must balance insider healing with strategic clarity amid governance uncertainty and succession risks.

## Key Points
- Define corporate centre's parenting style explicitly—from activist engagement to hands-off approach—to clarify decision-making boundaries.
- Grooming TCS successor Gopinathan amid credibility gaps is critical given TCS's portfolio importance to Tata group.
- Strategic blueprint redrafting needed but uncertain whether Mistry's plan survives or consultants lead new direction.

## Open Question
How can a new chairman heal organizational trust while simultaneously making hard strategic decisions that may contradict the 'longer view' Tata board seems to favor?

## India Angle
Leadership transition at India's largest conglomerate; sets precedent for non-Parsi leadership and tests governance models in Indian business institutions during crisis management.
