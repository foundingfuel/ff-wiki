## Core Argument
Societies systematically sacrifice happiness for other goals; pandemic forces rethinking what actually matters.

## Key Points
- Happiness paradox: people pursue 'value for money' over enjoyment, forgetting their core goal.
- Post-pandemic India must choose between growth metrics and genuine well-being for citizens.
- Strategic policy (US-India relations, vaccine access) and healthcare progress directly enable or constrain happiness.

## Open Question
If happiness is the ultimate goal, why do individuals and nations consistently optimize for measurable proxies (money, growth, geopolitical influence) instead? What systemic changes would reverse this?

## India Angle
India faces a post-pandemic reckoning about priorities: whether to pursue GDP growth and 'value for money' or genuine well-being. Strategic relationships (Russia, US) and healthcare policies (vaccine uptake, treatments) directly shape Indian citizens' happiness and national capacity.
