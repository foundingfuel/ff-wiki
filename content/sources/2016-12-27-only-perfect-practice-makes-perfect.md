## Core Argument
Mastery requires deliberate practice with feedback, not workshops or mere repetition of tasks.

## Key Points
- Quality of practice matters as much as quantity; expert performance comes from expert-level practice design.
- Deliberate practice requires motivation, task design matching skill level, immediate feedback, and repetition.
- Knowledge transfer through workshops is necessary but insufficient; real mastery happens through structured on-the-job practice.

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically embed deliberate practice feedback loops when most rely on one-time training events and hope for behavior change?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organizations often rely on workshops and training without sustained deliberate practice; applying this framework could transform skill development in Indian companies, from storytelling to technical competencies, moving beyond the 'training event' culture.
