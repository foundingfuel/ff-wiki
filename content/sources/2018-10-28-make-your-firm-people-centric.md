## Core Argument
Organizations succeed when leaders prioritize people-centric culture over compliance mechanisms alone.

## Key Points
- Safe workplaces require senior leaders initiating open conversations, not just compliance frameworks.
- Cultural transformation demands facilitated dialogue and takes time; cannot be mandated overnight.
- Judgment—weighing ethics and acting with incomplete information—is what distinguishes human leadership from systems.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the urgent need for cultural change with the reality that genuine culture transformation takes years, especially in large legacy organizations?

## India Angle
Indian organizations must move beyond compliance-based approaches to build genuinely safe, inclusive workplaces—critical for IT services, government institutions, and India Inc scaling globally amid #metoo accountability.
