## Core Argument
Lockdown revealed that the frantic pursuit of goals obscures the peace found in purposeless presence.

## Key Points
- A pandemic pause mirrored an earlier mountain retreat where the author discovered enlightenment by abandoning deadline-driven anxiety.
- Material progress and spiritual wealth are separate currencies; the old man possessed wisdom amid poverty.
- The unopened suitcase symbolizes a life on hold forcing reckoning with whether constant travel serves true purpose.

## Open Question
How does one reconcile the peace of stillness with the demands of a functioning life that requires constant motion and engagement?
