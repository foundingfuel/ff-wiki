## Core Argument
Individual ownership of human-caused climate change is prerequisite for systemic solutions and India's development model.

## Key Points
- Climate change is human-caused; individuals, organizations, and governments must accept accountability for emissions.
- India's agricultural sector and water scarcity make climate action urgent; extreme weather threatens economic development.
- Seven solution categories—renewable energy, biodiversity conservation, sustainable transport, agriculture, efficiency, circular economy, adaptation—require coordinated action.

## Open Question
How can India pursue rapid economic development while simultaneously achieving climate action goals, given the historical precedent of developed nations building wealth through fossil fuels?

## India Angle
India as third-largest greenhouse gas emitter faces critical climate action imperative due to agricultural dependence, water scarcity, and vulnerability to extreme weather. Must transition from coal-dependent energy and become a climate leadership model for global development.
