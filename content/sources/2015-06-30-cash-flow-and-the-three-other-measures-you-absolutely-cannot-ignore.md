## Core Argument
Cash flow, customer satisfaction, employee perception, and reputation are non-negotiable metrics startups must measure systematically.

## Key Points
- Cash flow is foundational; all other financial parameters can wait for startups.
- Customer satisfaction and employee perception must be measured externally, regularly, and aligned to organizational objectives.
- Reputation is quantifiable capital—track rankings, awards, industry benchmarks systematically from inception.

## Open Question
How do resource-constrained early-stage startups afford expert-led measurement of customer satisfaction, employee perception, and reputation without sacrificing other survival priorities?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs often conflate valuations with value; framework addresses how traditional business families and startup founders can align on non-negotiable metrics, with Wipro and Ujjivan as locally-rooted examples of reputation-building.
