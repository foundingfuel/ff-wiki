## Core Argument
Leaders must integrate physical fitness with mental acuity; evolutionary biology proves movement is fundamental to human excellence.

## Key Points
- CEOs who excel combine intellectual capacity with physical fitness and competitive athleticism.
- Human brain evolved primarily to control movement and physical grace, not just thinking.
- Biological success comes from unconventional action and competitive drive embedded since conception.

## Open Question
If physical fitness is evolutionarily fundamental to human success, why do successful modern leaders often achieve dominance through sedentary intellectual work alone?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and entrepreneurs often neglect physical fitness despite demanding roles; this challenges the cultural tendency to equate success with desk-bound intellectualism rather than integrated physical-mental performance.
