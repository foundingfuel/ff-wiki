## Core Argument
Eye health depends on lifestyle habits; pandemic-driven screen time and sleep disruption require systemic behavioural change.

## Key Points
- Screen overuse disrupts circadian rhythm via melatonin suppression, causing sleep and eyestrain issues.
- Dry eyes result from reduced blinking during focused screen work; preventable through hydration, breaks, and sleep.
- Age-related presbyopia and other vision problems require annual eye tests post-40; lifestyle alone insufficient for some.

## Open Question
The article prescribes lifestyle fixes as primary solutions but acknowledges that despite perfect habits, underlying health issues may require medical intervention—when is self-care sufficient versus when should medical consultation be immediate?
