## Core Argument
Nations must publicly acknowledge historical atrocities to ensure accountability and prevent future harm.

## Key Points
- Germany transformed sites of Nazi oppression into museums and memorials to confront its past.
- Abstract Holocaust Memorial encourages visitors to derive personal meaning from history.
- India should build COVID-19 memorial to acknowledge public health failures and bring closure to bereaved families.

## Open Question
Can memorials alone change minds of those attracted to authoritarian ideologies, or do they serve primarily to console the grieving and educate the already-convinced?

## India Angle
Author draws parallel between Germany's memorialization of Holocaust and Holocaust denial, suggesting India should build a COVID-19 memorial to acknowledge government failures and public health lessons, similar to how Germany confronts its dark past.
