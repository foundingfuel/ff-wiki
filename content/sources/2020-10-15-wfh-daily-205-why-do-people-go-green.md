## Core Argument
Green consumption is driven by status signaling and conspicuous altruism, not purely environmental concern.

## Key Points
- People buy green products to signal prosocial attitudes and gain social approval, especially in public contexts.
- Toyota's Prius design choices—distinctive hatchback body—deliberately make environmental choices visible and conspicuous.
- Organizational culture shapes behavior: women scientists self-censor when repeatedly dismissed, requiring mentorship and psychological safety to contribute fully.

## Open Question
If green consumption is primarily about status signaling rather than environmental impact, how can we design systems that genuinely incentivize sustainable behavior beyond social performance?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations need to understand hidden motives behind employee and consumer behavior—status-seeking drives decisions in Indian corporate culture; also relevant for Indian brands positioning sustainability messaging
