## Core Argument
Rethinking capitalism, work, and life for purpose, execution, and longevity.

## Key Points
- Purpose-driven companies create shared value where social benefit and shareholder returns align, not conflict.
- Relentless execution and vertical integration provide competitive advantage in fast-moving industries.
- Aging societies require reimagining education as lifelong, work as multi-phase, and leveraging crystallized intelligence of older workers.

## Open Question
How can companies simultaneously pursue ambitious growth, military-style execution (Samsung model) and stakeholder-centric purpose without creating internal contradictions?

## India Angle
India's knowledge economy and corporate evolution can learn from global best practices on purpose-driven capitalism, long-term workforce planning, and organizational resilience—especially as Indian companies scale globally.
