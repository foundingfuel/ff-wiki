## Core Argument
Treating business plans as educated guesses enables improvisation and real-time learning over rigid predetermined paths.

## Key Points
- Plans based on limited pre-execution information create false confidence and blind organizations to emerging opportunities.
- Weekly decision-making and just-in-time planning allow teams to respond to reality rather than past assumptions.
- Detailed long-term plans paradoxically reduce strategic flexibility and often become obsolete documentation.

## Open Question
How do you balance the need for organizational coordination and resource allocation with radical short-term improvisation, especially in large teams or capital-intensive industries?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and corporate leaders often over-plan in hierarchical, risk-averse cultures; embracing adaptive 'guessing' could unlock agility in fast-moving markets like tech, e-commerce, and startups where execution speed matters more than perfect foresight.
