## Core Argument
Transform dry case studies into emotionally resonant success stories to drive memorability and persuasion.

## Key Points
- Case studies fail because they're formulaic and jargon-heavy; stories succeed by centering human emotion and struggle.
- Effective success stories connect listener to protagonist's problem, creating resonance that drives understanding and action.
- Stories are memorable and retellable, increasing likelihood clients will champion your solution in internal decision-making.

## Open Question
How do you authentically surface and ethically leverage a client's emotions without exploiting vulnerability or oversimplifying complex problems?

## India Angle
Indian B2B companies, particularly in services and tech, rely heavily on case studies for client acquisition; storytelling approach can differentiate Indian vendors in competitive global markets.
