## Core Argument
Startups fail from internal conflict, not external competition; clear role definition prevents organizational autoimmune collapse.

## Key Points
- Defining singular, unique responsibilities per employee eliminates competition for roles and reduces internal conflict.
- Job assignments are relationships between people, not just task-matching; internal peace enables external resilience.
- Startups with fluid early-stage roles are especially vulnerable to factional strife masquerading as operational failure.

## Open Question
How do you maintain singular role clarity in genuinely early-stage startups where roles must evolve rapidly to pivot or scale?

## India Angle
Indian startups operate in highly fluid, fast-moving environments where role clarity and internal conflict management are critical to survival—especially given resource constraints and the pressure to scale quickly.
