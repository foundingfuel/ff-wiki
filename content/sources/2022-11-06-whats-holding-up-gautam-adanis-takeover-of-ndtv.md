## Core Argument
Regulatory inconsistency and legal precedent disputes delay Adani's NDTV takeover, exposing India's governance gaps.

## Key Points
- SEBI's petition challenges warrant conversion structure and control acquisition definitions in the 2009 Reliance-Roy loan agreement.
- SAT overturned SEBI's order by citing precedent (Victor Fernandes case), leaving regulator forced to appeal to Supreme Court.
- Outcome will determine open offer price; SEBI victory could mandate much higher valuation than Adani's Rs 294 per share.

## Open Question
How should Indian regulators balance consistent application of legal precedent against evolving interpretations of 'control' in complex financial structures, especially for high-stakes corporate takeovers?

## India Angle
Adani's NDTV takeover reveals tensions between India's regulatory frameworks (SEBI), precedent-based jurisprudence, and large corporate M&A strategy; sets precedent for how control acquisition in Indian media is defined and regulated.
