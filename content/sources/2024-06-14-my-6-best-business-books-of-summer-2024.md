## Core Argument
Character, rituals, adaptation, and communication are essential skills for high-performance leaders and teams.

## Key Points
- Character development is foundational to coaching effectiveness; coaches must move beyond narrow career expertise.
- Rituals create psychological anchors that improve performance; bad leadership persists when followers enable or silence dissent.
- Adaptation without legacy creates organizational survival; communication skills determine ability to navigate difficult conversations.

## Open Question
How do individuals balance personal character development with institutional pressures that reward expedience over ethics?

## India Angle
Indian corporate context features ineffective coaching; book selections address adaptation critical for Indian businesses, and include insights from India's former RBI governor on grassroots economics and governance
