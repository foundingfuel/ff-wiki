## Core Argument
Aadhaar integration into midday meals reveals systemic tensions between efficiency, inclusion, and democratic process in India's welfare state.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar-linking debate exposes conflict between direct benefit transfer efficiency and protecting secondary benefits like school attendance and social cohesion.
- Government bypassed democratic checks (Supreme Court interim order on voluntariness) by rushing implementation through Money Bill procedures.
- Authentication technology's imperfection and exclusion errors risk denying benefits to vulnerable populations despite assurances.

## Open Question
Can technology-driven efficiency in welfare delivery coexist with guaranteeing universal inclusion and preserving non-monetary social benefits of welfare schemes?

## India Angle
Examines how Aadhaar integration into midday meal scheme reflects broader tensions in India's approach to digital identity, welfare delivery, and state capacity—balancing efficiency gains against exclusion risks and democratic process.
