## Core Argument
Organizations must balance short-term agility with long-term stability through embedded agility and simple governing rules.

## Key Points
- Embed agility across all employees and processes, not just strategy; simple rules enable complex adaptive behavior.
- Vision provides direction and stability; flexibility in execution allows dynamic response to market turbulence.
- Aggregation of marginal gains (British Cycling) and continuous innovation (Toyota) demonstrate practical embedding of agility.

## Open Question
How do organizations know when they're optimizing for short-term performance at the expense of long-term value, and what governance structures prevent this drift?

## India Angle
Indian firms scaling rapidly often sacrifice stability for speed; this framework shows how to build embedded agility to compete globally while maintaining organizational coherence and long-term value creation.
