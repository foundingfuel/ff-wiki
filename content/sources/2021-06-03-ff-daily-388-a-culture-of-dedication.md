## Core Argument
Commitment and long-term dedication are radical acts that counter societal fragmentation and performance anxiety.

## Key Points
- Dedication as spiritual practice creates joy and sustains work during crises, not just acute threats.
- Indian informal workers hit hardest by double-whammy of job losses and medical debt, eroding savings and stability.
- Choking under pressure stems from overthinking; journaling worries beforehand reduces cognitive overload and improves performance.

## Open Question
How do workers in precarious informal sectors sustain dedication and commitment when immediate survival needs dominate their attention?

## India Angle
