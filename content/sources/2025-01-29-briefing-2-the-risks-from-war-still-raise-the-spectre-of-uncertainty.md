## Core Argument
War risks remain despite Trump's presidency; fragile truces mask deeper structural instability across three regions.

## Key Points
- Israel-Gaza truce is temporary; phases 2-3 lack written guarantees from key parties, risking collapse.
- Iran remains militarily capable despite sanctions; new Russia partnership adds complexity to US options.
- Russia-Ukraine war continues; Persian Gulf threats to oil/gas could trigger global economic disruption.

## Open Question
How can the US manage simultaneous de-escalation in three war zones while preventing regional powers (Iran, Russia) from exploiting vacuums or expanding influence?

## India Angle
India's energy security depends heavily on Persian Gulf stability and Middle East peace; geopolitical uncertainty directly impacts oil/gas prices, inflation, and forex reserves. India must navigate complex diplomacy with US, Iran, Russia, and Gulf states amid shifting alignments.
