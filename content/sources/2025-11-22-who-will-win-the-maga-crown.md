## Core Argument
MAGA's internal fracture over AI, immigration, and sovereignty foreshadows America's technology and geopolitical trajectory.

## Key Points
- Three competing visions: Accelerationists (Thiel), Sceptics (Bannon), Nationalist-Pragmatists (Vance) on AI and H-1B visas.
- H-1B immigration and AI governance are flashpoint divides reflecting deeper ideological tensions within Trump coalition.
- Outcome shapes global talent flows, AI investment patterns, and regulatory standards with international consequences.

## Open Question
Can MAGA sustain political cohesion while pursuing contradictory tech strategies—aggressive AI leadership vs. sovereignty protection vs. labor protectionism—without fracturing before 2026?

## India Angle
India's tech talent pipeline to the US (H-1B visas) and AI development capacity depend on MAGA faction outcomes. Restrictive immigration policy could redirect Indian engineers to other nations; AI regulatory stance will shape global standards affecting Indian startups and enterprises.
