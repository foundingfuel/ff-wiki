## Core Argument
Indian agencies thrive by retaining control, prioritizing local markets, and building decades-long client relationships over chasing global accounts.

## Key Points
- R K Swamy maintained management control and local vision while international partners pursued global clients and sold out.
- Long-term client relationships spanning decades built on integrity and trust contrast with industry norm of frequent agency switching.
- Strategic decision to spin out BBDO India as separate unit allowed both entities to serve different client bases without conflicting interests.

## Open Question
If local market focus is the winning strategy, why have most other Indian agencies abandoned it for international partnerships—is this survival bias or genuine competitive advantage?
