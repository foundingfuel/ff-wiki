## Core Argument
India's funding ecosystem favors herd-like copycat models over creative, domain-expert-backed innovation with moderate risk-return profiles.

## Key Points
- VC model rewards 1-in-10 mega-winners; most copycat startups will fail despite capital flooding e-commerce and asset-light tech.
- Investors lack entrepreneurial experience and domain expertise, defaulting to risk-averse, 'bean counter' thinking and app-sector groupthink.
- Gap exists for moderate-risk ventures unable to secure traditional VC backing or bank collateral; creative capital needed for true innovation.

## Open Question
How can government and investors build genuine investment confidence in non-copycat, domain-specific innovations when the financial system structurally rewards herd behavior and efficiency logic ignores India's unique needs?

## India Angle
Indian venture capital ecosystem is biased toward copycat models (Uber, Airbnb) over genuine innovation; domain-expert investors and creative capital are scarce; banks risk-averse due to NPAs; government's Yozma-inspired IAF must ensure sector diversity beyond herd-mentality tech apps.
