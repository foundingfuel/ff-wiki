## Core Argument
Internet platforms have concentrated wealth and power despite rhetoric of democratization and empowerment.

## Key Points
- Internet founders monetize users through advertising without direct consumer payment, creating wealth extraction model.
- Tech billionaires preach transparency and empowerment while practicing secrecy and extreme privacy for themselves.
- Shift from 1991 government-controlled internet to commercial venture capital investment fundamentally changed internet's purpose.

## Open Question
How can societies preserve the internet's original democratizing potential while accommodating the commercial incentives and venture capital investment that scaled it globally?

## India Angle
India's digital economy relies heavily on data-driven platforms (Google, Facebook, e-commerce) that operate under similar power concentration models; policymakers must consider whether India's regulatory frameworks adequately protect citizens' data and prevent monopolistic control of digital infrastructure and markets.
