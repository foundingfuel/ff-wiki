## Core Argument
Manufactured polarization distracts from meaningful work; true leaders focus on substance over noise.

## Key Points
- Polarization in Indian discourse—nationalist vs anti-nationalist narratives—is strategically designed to keep opponents distracted
- Powerful ideas (like the Indian identity) are constructed from simple elements but evoke profound emotional loyalty
- Leaders distinguish between two types of idiots: those who opine without competence, and those who add value through action

## Open Question
How can one remain emotionally invested in the idea of India while maintaining clinical detachment from the symbols and narratives that create it?
