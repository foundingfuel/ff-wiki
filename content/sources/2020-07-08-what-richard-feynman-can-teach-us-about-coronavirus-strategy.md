## Core Argument
Technology alone solves nothing; effective solutions require integrated packages of tech, culture, ethics, and systems thinking.

## Key Points
- Cargo cult approach: copying form without substance leads to failure—true in lockdowns, education tech, telemedicine.
- Singapore's virus resurgence shows constant adaptation beats one-time victories; migrant labour dormitories exposed blind spots.
- Food delivery consolidation inevitable because fundamentals (high costs, undifferentiated service) force margin-squeezing, not innovation.

## Open Question
If technology is necessary but insufficient, how do policymakers and entrepreneurs decide which non-tech elements to prioritize when resources are scarce?

## India Angle
India's tech-first approach to problems—from education to healthcare to governance—risks cargo cult solutions; the food delivery consolidation mirrors India's winner-take-all dynamics; facial recognition bias has acute implications for Indian policing and surveillance.
