## Core Argument
Socratic inquiry and specialist positioning offer pathways through global consolidation and institutional rigidity.

## Key Points
- Socrates learned excellence from all trades, showing cross-domain knowledge creates clarity and wisdom.
- Global Rule of Three: specialists remain viable by dominating defined markets rather than competing as generalists.
- India's vaccine pricing requires market discovery, not bureaucratic caps; multiple price points and suppliers needed.

## Open Question
How can developing economies balance public health access (price controls) with market efficiency (price discovery) when neither bureaucrats nor centralized planners know optimal pricing?

## India Angle
India's vaccine pricing debate and market dynamics reflect tension between price controls and market efficiency; local/regional businesses in India must navigate Global Rule of Three consolidation; Socratic questioning relevant to Indian institutional decision-making.
