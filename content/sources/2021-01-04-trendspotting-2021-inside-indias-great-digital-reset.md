## Core Argument
India's shift from push to pull economy unlocks the best entrepreneurial moment in a decade for digital disruption.

## Key Points
- Internet consumers are becoming transactors; 700M users now have spending power and willingness to experiment beyond discounts.
- Digital payments, UPI auto-debits, and embedded lending create frictionless micropayments enabling subscription and pull-based consumption models.
- Timing converges: capital-backed second-time founders, experienced professionals, and returning diaspora entering a fragmented, friction-heavy economy ripe for digital disruption.

## Open Question
If entrepreneurial success now depends on capital and timing alignment, how do bootstrapped founders and those without access to VC networks compete in this 'best moment' narrative?

## India Angle
India's digital economy is crossing a tipping point where consumers are becoming transactors at scale; this creates unprecedented opportunity for Indian entrepreneurs to disrupt a fragmented, friction-heavy economy with digital-first businesses across all sectors.
