## Core Argument
Business leaders must balance shareholder value with human purpose, continuous learning, and AI-human collaboration.

## Key Points
- Shareholder value obsession destroys companies; modern leaders must reinvent themselves psychologically and emotionally.
- Learning as habit and practice is core to career growth and joy; curiosity alone is insufficient without action.
- AI lacks morals and context; HR—not IT—should lead implementation; humans must question data, not blindly accept it.

## Open Question
How can organizations pursue meaningful growth and social impact when global economic systems still reward infinite growth and shareholder extraction?
