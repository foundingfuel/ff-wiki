## Core Argument
Indian small businesses face interconnected challenges: labour shortages, platform coopetition tensions, and untapped export potential through ecommerce.

## Key Points
- Migrant workers resist factory work despite wage incentives; systemic inequality and lack of rural opportunity force return.
- Ecommerce platforms position as collaborators while potentially appropriating MSME technology and competing directly.
- MSMEs accessing global markets via ecommerce platforms; $2B exports since 2015, targeting $10B by 2025.

## Open Question
How can Indian MSMEs trust ecommerce platforms as export partners when platforms demonstrably compete with and extract from their suppliers?

## India Angle
Post-COVID labour market dynamics in Indian MSMEs, particularly migrant worker retention, ecommerce platform dynamics for small businesses, and MSME export opportunities through digital channels.
