## Core Argument
Indian boardrooms underestimate governance risks and fail rigorous risk evaluation in major strategic decisions.

## Key Points
- Tata Steel's Thyssenkrupp merger failed due to weak due diligence and insufficient remedy planning despite early warning signs.
- Board failed to evaluate downside scenarios adequately; merger killed by EU Commission without credible union support strategy.
- Corporate hierarchy and informal power structures (Ratan Tata's influence) override independent board judgment and strategic decision-making.

## Open Question
How can Indian boards balance deference to influential founders/chairs with independent strategic evaluation and accountability for major capital decisions?
