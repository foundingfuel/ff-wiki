## Core Argument
Global order breakdown offers India strategic space if it maintains diplomatic autonomy amid US-China rivalry.

## Key Points
- International order collapsing as major powers abandon institutions they built; reduces collaborative capacity for polycrisis.
- China's rise depends on benign global environment; order breakdown may not advantage China despite US withdrawal.
- China views India through US-China prism, not as peer rival; India's diplomatic choices directly affect Beijing's approach.

## Open Question
If the international order continues fragmenting and the US retreats, does India gain more diplomatic space or lose the stable framework necessary for its own rise?

## India Angle
India faces a strategic opportunity and vulnerability as US-China tensions intensify. India must leverage its position as a counterbalance while expanding diplomatic autonomy. China views India through a geopolitical lens tied to US relations, not as an equal peer. India's development trajectory and positioning with the US directly shape its regional influence and ability to shape global outcomes.
