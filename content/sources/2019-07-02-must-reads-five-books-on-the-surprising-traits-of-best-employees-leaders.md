## Core Argument
Best employees have spikes, not well-roundedness; leaders must match strengths to tasks and coach authentically.

## Key Points
- Impactful employees have angular strengths, not all-rounder profiles; match people to tasks deliberately.
- Incompetent leaders flourish due to confidence masking lack of competence and insecure bosses enabling them.
- Meetings waste 40% of executive time due to poor design; rebels and diverse thinkers drive real engagement.

## Open Question
If organizations need rebels and specialists with spikes, how do they balance organizational cohesion and compliance with celebrating non-conformity?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often over-rely on conformity and all-rounder expectations; recognizing employee spikes, authentic rebels, and coaching competence could unlock engagement in India's hierarchical corporate culture.
