## Core Argument
Leadership books offer limited insights; observing real leaders navigate tough situations teaches more.

## Key Points
- Five insights from a book is exceptional; leadership is best learned through direct observation of people in crisis.
- Purpose-driven corporate narratives are disconnected from reality as company lifespans shrink from 90 years (1935) to 15 years (2022).
- India needs more indigenous authors and voices; global south literary contribution to business thinking remains severely underrepresented.

## Open Question
If leadership is best learned through observation, why do successful leaders like Shivakumar continue reading and curating books as a primary intellectual practice?

## India Angle
India lacks sufficient homegrown leadership and business literature; leadership insights are better developed through observing local leaders navigating context-specific challenges than importing Western frameworks.
