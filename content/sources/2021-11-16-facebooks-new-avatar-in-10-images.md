## Core Argument
Facebook's metaverse pivot masks unresolved contradictions between growth obsession and platform responsibility.

## Key Points
- Facebook's scale-driven business model (data→targeting→ads) enabled dominance but created societal harms and regulatory exposure.
- Technical and human moderation efforts failed because growth incentives overrode safety; memo shows founders knew this tradeoff.
- Zuckerberg's metaverse bet is necessary pivot for founder still leading major tech firm, but doesn't fix underlying cultural contradictions.

## Open Question
Does a strategic pivot to metaverse actually resolve Facebook's systemic governance problems, or merely shift them to a new frontier where the same growth-first culture will likely repeat the same failures?

## India Angle
Facebook's content moderation failures in India (Ankhi Das resignation over hate speech) exemplify how platform governance breaks down at scale in diverse markets; Meta's pivot to metaverse sidesteps but doesn't resolve these India-specific accountability challenges.
