## Core Argument
India's neighbourhood-first policy fails because it prioritizes security interests over sustained political relationship-building across parties.

## Key Points
- India backed Sheikh Hasina unequivocally, ignoring opposition parties, leaving it isolated as Bangladesh demands her extradition.
- All South Asian neighbours face internal conflicts or crises; US-China rivalry complicates India's balancing act further.
- India must rebuild relationships with multiple political actors, not just ruling governments, to ensure long-term stability.

## Open Question
How can India maintain security-centric foreign policy while building the deep, multi-stakeholder political relationships necessary to weather neighbourhood transitions?

## India Angle
India's neighbourhood diplomacy faces a critical test as Bangladesh upheaval exposes India's over-reliance on single political leaders over institutional relationships, threatening regional stability and India's strategic interests in South Asia.
