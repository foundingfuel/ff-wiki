## Core Argument
Systems thinking, not fragmented expertise, is essential to solve complex modern problems.

## Key Points
- Compartmentalized education and functional silos prevent holistic understanding of interconnected systems.
- Pandemic revealed deep interdependencies; solving tough problems requires seeing whole systems, not fragments.
- Real-world decision-making under uncertainty requires pragmatism, not theory; follow data and act appropriately.

## Open Question
How do we build systems-thinking capability in individuals trained in narrow specializations without disrupting the efficiency of functional expertise?

## India Angle
Indian organizations and educational institutions similarly operate in silos—by subject, function, department—limiting systemic problem-solving capability essential for navigating India's complex socio-economic challenges and pandemic recovery.
