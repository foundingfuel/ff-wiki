## Core Argument
Data advantage doesn't guarantee success; human insight and organizational culture matter more in creative industries.

## Key Points
- Netflix's subscriber losses despite data access reveal limits of algorithmic decision-making in content creation.
- Strategic decisions come from employee insights and market realities, not big data—Mighty Little Bheem case proves this.
- Both Netflix and Amazon struggled to turn data into hit rates; creative industries need old-world judgment alongside analytics.

## Open Question
If data doesn't drive success in media, what competitive advantage do streaming platforms actually possess, and how sustainable is it?

## India Angle
Highlights Netflix's data-driven limitations using Indian content (Mighty Little Bheem) as case study; connects to broader Indian context via Agnipath veteran employment crisis and gig economy growth (6.8-7.7M workers), showing how data alone cannot solve complex human/organizational problems.
