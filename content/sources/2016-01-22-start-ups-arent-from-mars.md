## Core Argument
Entrepreneurship transcends company size; breaking down startup-corporate silos benefits India's ecosystem.

## Key Points
- Government should simplify laws but not directly fund startups; lacks expertise to run incubators effectively.
- Startup hype ignores deep-tech (AI, IoT, healthcare) while celebrating consumer startups; creates cultural myopia.
- Large companies embracing startup thinking (design thinking, lean methodology) already blur traditional boundaries globally.

## Open Question
If startups and large corporations learn from each other, who drives the disruption and maintains the creative tension necessary for real innovation?

## India Angle
India's startup ecosystem risks ghettoization and over-hype, while large corporations and government miss opportunities for cross-pollination. Smart incumbents like Infosys must learn from startups; government should enable rather than directly fund.
