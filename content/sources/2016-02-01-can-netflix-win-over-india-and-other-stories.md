## Core Argument
Netflix will eventually win India by leveraging glocal content strategy, superior UX, and shifting consumer values around experience over price.

## Key Points
- Netflix targets India's 400M+ online population, 70% mobile-accessed daily, with English-speaking disposable income cohort as early adopters.
- Local competitors like Eros Now and piracy undercut on price, but Netflix counters with exclusive original content and trial-conversion model proven elsewhere.
- Long-term growth depends on content localization, internet penetration expansion to semi-urban/rural Bharat, and users valuing premium experience over free piracy.

## Open Question
Does Netflix's premium pricing and experience-first model truly align with Indian consumer behavior and payment willingness, or is the Uber comparison misleading given vastly different market conditions?

## India Angle
Netflix's India entry tests whether premium global streaming models can succeed against local competitors and piracy in a price-sensitive, mobile-first market with emerging digital consumption habits.
