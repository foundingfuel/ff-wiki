## Core Argument
Social streaming platforms like Netflix Party reshape how dispersed communities experience entertainment and connection during isolation.

## Key Points
- Netflix Party enables geographically dispersed friends to watch movies synchronously with live chat, replicating collective viewing experience.
- Chaotic, unpredictable content becomes more engaging than polished films during lockdown, offering psychological escape from monotony.
- Real-time communal commentary and shared emotional reactions drive platform adoption more than traditional review metrics.

## Open Question
Does technology genuinely recreate the serendipitous, spontaneous joy of physical gathering, or does it merely simulate connection while deepening digital isolation?

## India Angle
Indian youth adopting synchronous streaming tools to maintain social bonds during lockdown; generational shift in how Indians consume entertainment from collective cinema halls to distributed digital gatherings.
