## Core Argument
We operate successfully despite profound ignorance by stopping asking questions about complexity.

## Key Points
- People overestimate understanding; adults stop probing complexity unlike curious children.
- Indian swab-stick case reveals systemic failures when vulnerable workers fill gaps state leaves.
- Toyota's Just-in-Time model faces reckoning as supply chain assumptions break under real-world shocks.

## Open Question
If ignorance enables us to function, does seeking deeper understanding paralyze decision-making, or is willful blindness to systemic failures morally indefensible?

## India Angle
