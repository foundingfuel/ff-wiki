## Core Argument
Crisis leadership is defined by presence and moral ownership, not operational efficiency or damage control tactics.

## Key Points
- Leaders must show up with empathy and truth during crises, not hide behind protocols or delay responses.
- Social media amplifies grief and demands real-time, honest engagement from institutional leaders.
- Moral accountability and systemic reform—not blame assignment—restore public trust and institutional legitimacy.

## Open Question
How can leaders take moral ownership and demonstrate presence while investigations are ongoing and facts remain incomplete?

## India Angle
Tests whether India's most iconic corporate institution (Tata group) can demonstrate ethical leadership and restore institutional trust—critical for India's broader confidence in systems and institutions.
