## Core Argument
Stress isn't heroic; smarter practice and intentional small wins build resilience.

## Key Points
- Stefan Bucher's question reframes stress as a choice, not a virtue to pursue.
- Ultralearning principles—optimize, practice intentionally, drill weaknesses—apply beyond chess to any skill.
- Pandemic wall broken by small daily accomplishments, not heroic endurance.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between healthy pressure for growth and destructive stress that damages performance—especially when cultural narratives celebrate overwork as heroic?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers face pandemic stress without traditional coping outlets (gyms, socializing); applies to growing cohort of high-performance professionals seeking smarter learning approaches.
