## Core Argument
Corporate India must sequence crisis response: virus control first, then protect citizens and economy, not shareholder interests.

## Key Points
- Lockdown severity determines corporate response scale: April 15 lift = minor impact, beyond June = systemic restructuring needed.
- National supply chains make regional green/red zones impossible; economic distress migrates across geographies regardless of restrictions.
- Post-crisis, employees will value corporate resilience equally with performance; high debt eliminates adaptability during shocks.

## Open Question
How can companies authentically prepare employees for April 15 worst-case scenarios without inducing pre-crisis psychological harm through premature fear-setting?

## India Angle
Indian corporates must recalibrate crisis response sequencing (virus → poor citizens → economy → employers → financial system); national supply chains make regional lockdown strategies ineffective; India's weak city-level governance and need for civil services reform exposed
