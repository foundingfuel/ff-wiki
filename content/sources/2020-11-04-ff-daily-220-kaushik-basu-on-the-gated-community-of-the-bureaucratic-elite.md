## Core Argument
Bureaucratic elites' insulation from ground realities fundamentally distorts policymaking in India.

## Key Points
- Top bureaucrats live in efficiency-bubble disconnected from how policies affect ordinary citizens.
- Perquisites like free fuel for senior officials create blind spots in subsidy and economic policy design.
- Policymakers lack empathy for problems they don't personally experience, leading to flawed solutions.

## Open Question
How can policymakers design fair, inclusive policies when institutional structures systematically insulate them from ground realities and consequences?

## India Angle
India's bureaucratic elite operate in disconnected ivory towers, insulated from ground realities by perquisites and systems that shield them from the consequences of policies they design, distorting policymaking for 1.4 billion citizens.
