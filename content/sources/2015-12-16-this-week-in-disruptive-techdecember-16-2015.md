## Core Argument
Disruptive technologies require ethical guardrails built by benevolent designers, not just profitable ones.

## Key Points
- OpenAI and Foundation of Responsible Robotics provide counter-balance to profit-driven AI development in Silicon Valley.
- Blockchain's power lies in the technology, not its creator—Bitcoin's identity matters less than distributed ledger adoption.
- CRISPR gene editing poses risks comparable to AI; both require thoughtful governance before widespread deployment.

## Open Question
How do we incentivize benevolent design of powerful technologies when market forces reward speed and profit over societal benefit?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies and startups need to consider the ethical implications of AI, blockchain, and gene editing early—not as afterthoughts. India's tech talent pool should engage with responsible innovation frameworks before these technologies mature.
