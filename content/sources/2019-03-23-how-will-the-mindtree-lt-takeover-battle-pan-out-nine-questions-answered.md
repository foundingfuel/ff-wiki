## Core Argument
Mindtree founders face existential threat as L&T's hostile bid challenges India's IT company independence and culture.

## Key Points
- Mindtree's unique culture and independence hang in balance as L&T pursues first hostile takeover in Indian IT history.
- Cafe Coffee Day founder Siddhartha's financial pressures forced sale of 20.3% stake, removing critical buffer for founders.
- Founders' limited options: share buyback unlikely, white knight PE rescue improbable, suggesting acquisition seems inevitable.

## Open Question
Can a founder-built culture survive acquisition, or does capitalism inevitably erode the idealism that created the company?
