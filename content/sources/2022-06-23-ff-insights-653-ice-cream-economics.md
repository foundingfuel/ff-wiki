## Core Argument
Markets self-regulate efficiently; state control through coercion breeds shortages and stifles innovation.

## Key Points
- Ice cream market thrives without regulation; electricity fails despite government oversight, revealing incentive misalignment.
- VC market correction shifts focus from growth-at-all-costs to profitability, forcing startup discipline.
- Beginner's mind and play maintain creative freshness—essential for sustained innovation beyond initial success.

## Open Question
If state control systematically fails (electricity vs. ice cream), why does India persist with it rather than liberalizing—and what political economy prevents such reform?

## India Angle
India's electricity crisis mirrors regulatory dysfunction—unlike ice cream, which thrives in a competitive market. State control and wrong incentives create shortages during peak demand, exposing governance failures unique to India's infrastructure challenges.
