## Core Argument
Personal transformation emerges through sensory reconnection with places and authentic human presence over curated experiences.

## Key Points
- Returning to familiar places reveals impermanence; essence lies in savoring unchanging human connections and flavors
- Authenticity emerges beneath tourist-friendly facades when one moves beyond Instagram-ready surfaces into genuine local experiences
- Solitude and nature catalyze self-discovery; dissolving boundaries between self, community and environment enables rebirth

## Open Question
How does one honor genuine cultural heritage and personal memory when places inevitably transform and become packaged for consumption, yet still find authentic human connection within that tension?

## India Angle
Indian diaspora experience and the tension between maintaining cultural identity (chai, bindis) while navigating global spaces; reflects broader Indian experience of nostalgia and roots in an interconnected world
