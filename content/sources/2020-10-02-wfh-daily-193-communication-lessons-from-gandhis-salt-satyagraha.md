## Core Argument
Gandhi's salt satyagraha reveals five communication tactics: universal appeal, low participation barriers, dramatic narrative structure, climactic moments, and strategic media leverage.

## Key Points
- Find universal issues that transcend social divisions to unite diverse audiences
- Design campaigns where participation is accessible to people at multiple levels of involvement
- Structure communications as multi-act narratives with planned climaxes rather than single announcements

## Open Question
How do modern organizations replicate Gandhi's ability to command media attention and global sympathy without access to his moral authority or the clear villain-hero narrative of colonial rule?

## India Angle
Gandhi's communication strategies offer Indian leaders a blueprint for mass mobilization and stakeholder engagement—particularly relevant for startups, social enterprises, and change agents navigating India's diverse, polarized landscape. The salt satyagraha demonstrates how to build broad coalitions across class and religious lines, a core challenge for Indian organizations today.
