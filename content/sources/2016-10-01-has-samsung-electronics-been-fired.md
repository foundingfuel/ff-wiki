## Core Argument
Strategic crisis management requires long-term brand thinking over short-term financial pain.

## Key Points
- Samsung's $1B recall was costly but strategically sound for cementing consumer trust long-term.
- South Korean government patronage creates different risk dynamics than Indian multinationals face.
- Product recall decisions reveal deeper organizational culture: whether profit or consumer safety drives choices.

## Open Question
If Samsung's recall was indeed hasty and avoidable through better quality control, does the long-term brand benefit justify the initial negligence that caused the crisis?
