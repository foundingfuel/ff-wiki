## Core Argument
Webb telescope's infrared technology will transform healthcare, communications, and consumer diagnostics through trickle-down innovation.

## Key Points
- Webb sees infrared light from billions of years ago, revealing universe's early formation and acceleration via dark energy.
- Infrared detection advances from Webb will improve medical diagnostics, manufacturing efficiency, and consumer electronics like TV remotes.
- Space technology investments create spillover effects into everyday household and capital goods, reducing diagnosis costs and errors.

## Open Question
How will breakthrough space science discoveries translate into concrete, affordable applications for ordinary consumers in developing economies like India?

## India Angle
Indian research institutions like TATA Institute of Fundamental Research contribute to Webb's findings; infrared technology spillovers will impact India's healthcare costs, manufacturing diagnostics, and communications infrastructure development.
