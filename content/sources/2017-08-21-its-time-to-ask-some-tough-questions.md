## Core Argument
Infosys crisis reveals unresolved tension between founder conscience-keeping and institutional governance in Indian companies.

## Key Points
- Murthy's governance concerns vs. board autonomy creates no-win scenario for stakeholders and investors.
- CEO Sikka's resignation exposes fragility when leadership lacks support during institutional conflict.
- Founder-board dynamics question whether Indian institutions can mature beyond their creators' shadow.

## Open Question
Can a founder legitimately hold a board accountable to original governance principles, or does doing so inevitably undermine institutional independence and credibility?

## India Angle
Indian institutional leadership vacuum: when founders and boards clash over governance, India's largest IT services company becomes a cautionary tale about whether conscience-keeping or institutional authority should prevail in founder-led organizations.
