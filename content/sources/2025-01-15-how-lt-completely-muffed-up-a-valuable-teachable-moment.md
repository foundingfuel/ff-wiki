## Core Argument
L&T's reactive crisis response squanders opportunity to transform culture; proactive CEO accountability could have prevented reputational damage.

## Key Points
- L&T lacked unified narrative; reactive execution by HR proxies backfired and damaged credibility further.
- Global playbook exists: Nooyi and Nadella showed sincere, direct CEO apologies resolve crises better than disappearance or deflection.
- Board inaction and AM Naik's silence suggest weak governance; no institutional accountability for chairman's conduct toward employees.

## Open Question
Does staying silent during a viral crisis allow it to fade, or does silence itself become the real reputational disaster—and who decides this in Indian companies without strong independent boards?

## India Angle
L&T is India's largest diversified conglomerate; how Indian corporate leaders handle reputation crises and employee relations reflects broader governance gaps in Indian companies versus global peers.
