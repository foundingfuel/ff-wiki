## Core Argument
Oscar nominees 2024 retreat to nostalgic styles rather than pushing cinema into the 2020s.

## Key Points
- Most 2024 nominees could have been made in 1980s; only Barbie and American Fiction feel contemporary.
- High-brow films return with polarizing force after writers' strike, dividing audiences sharply.
- Technical execution matters: Scorsese's masterful blocking and framing outshines Nolan's IMAX limitations.

## Open Question
If Oscars reward technical mastery and storytelling craft equally, why do character chemistry and genuine emotional depth often lose to visual spectacle and biographical prestige?
