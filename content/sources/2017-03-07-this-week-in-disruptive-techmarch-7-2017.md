## Core Argument
Technology can solve problems, but AI and data systems risk centralised control unless guided by democratic principles.

## Key Points
- Facebook's suicide detection algorithm exemplifies AI's power to modify behaviour, raising questions about private and state surveillance.
- Musk's ambitious timelines reflect optimism that drives innovation but obscure technical realities; his interconnected projects aim to transform industries.
- Blockchain's decentralised nature offers an alternative to centralised control, appealing to businesses and potentially protecting democratic values.

## Open Question
How can societies harness technology's benefits (suicide prevention, cost savings, innovation) without enabling totalitarian surveillance and control?

## India Angle
India's digital revolution (Aadhaar, demonetisation push) raises urgent questions about AI, data usage, and privacy. The article's framework for balancing technological benefits with democratic values is directly applicable to Indian policymaking.
