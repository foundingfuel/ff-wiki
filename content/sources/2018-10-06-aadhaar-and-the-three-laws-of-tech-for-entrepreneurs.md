## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs succeed by navigating technology adoption cycles: opposition is inevitable, critical mass is required, regulation shapes feasibility.

## Key Points
- All new technologies face opposition from incumbent stakeholders invested in existing systems.
- Technologies require critical mass adoption; regulatory ambiguity forces entrepreneurs to calculate risk across multiple outcome scenarios.
- Entrepreneurial skill is managing uncertainty—those who accept ambiguity and work within constraints survive regulatory shocks.

## Open Question
If entrepreneurs can 'work around' regulatory ambiguity through creative interpretation, does this weaken the rule of law or demonstrate adaptive resilience in emerging markets?

## India Angle
Aadhaar and India Stack represent India's unique digital infrastructure opportunity; regulatory ambiguity creates both risk and entrepreneurial navigation challenges specific to Indian founders operating in a government-controlled ecosystem.
