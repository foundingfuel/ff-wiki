## Core Argument
Indian DNA vaccine overcomes delivery challenge with non-invasive method, showing 66% efficacy against Delta.

## Key Points
- DNA vaccines are cheaper to produce and safer than live-virus approaches but face delivery challenges into cell nuclei.
- Indian researchers solved delivery without electroporation, enabling mass vaccination feasibility and Delta variant effectiveness.
- Leadership archetypes—Jungle Gym, Creative Coder, Superconnector—matter for post-pandemic organizational transformation and adaptation.

## Open Question
How will India scale this DNA vaccine innovation into manufacturing and distribution at national scale while maintaining quality and accessibility?
