## Core Argument
Businesses must climb a responsibility ladder from financial returns to political health, keeping lower rungs strong.

## Key Points
- Business responsibility has five rungs: investors, value creators, environment, human impact, and political health of societies.
- Storytelling with personal anecdotes makes abstract frameworks like OKRs relatable and actionable to teams.
- Possessions and status matter less for happiness than meeting basic needs; businesses should examine priorities accordingly.

## Open Question
How can businesses navigate competing stakeholder interests across the responsibility ladder when social polarization makes any stance potentially controversial?

## India Angle
Indian businesses like Tanishq face real consequences when navigating social responsibility amid polarized public discourse; the ladder framework is particularly relevant for Indian conglomerates balancing stakeholder interests across complex, diverse societies.
