## Core Argument
Professional systems and governance unlock growth without political patronage in Indian real estate.

## Key Points
- Patil rejected industry norms: hired young CEO, invested in ERP, hired global auditors despite peer skepticism.
- Refused political patronage model even in Mumbai entry, defying conventional Indian business wisdom.
- Growth doubled in three years through professional processes, cash flow discipline, and organizational capability—not political leverage.

## Open Question
Can integrity-driven governance without political connections scale sustainably across India's fragmented real estate markets, or does growth eventually require political alignment?

## India Angle
Real estate sector transformation through professional governance and eschewing political patronage model—directly challenges how Indian businesses traditionally operate; demonstrates alternative growth path without political dependency.
