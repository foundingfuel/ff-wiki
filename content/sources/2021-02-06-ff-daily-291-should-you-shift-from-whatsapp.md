## Core Argument
Messaging app choice hinges on design features limiting viral spread, not privacy promises alone.

## Key Points
- WhatsApp and Signal limit forwarding to 5 people; Telegram lacks such restrictions, enabling misinformation.
- Bi-modal review distributions make online reviews unreliable; fake reviewers submit 12x more reviews than genuine customers.
- Algorithm-free platforms like Signal reduce misinformation risk compared to broadcast-style networks.

## Open Question
How can decentralized messaging platforms balance user privacy with preventing structural misinformation without becoming algorithmic gatekeepers?

## India Angle
India-specific: WhatsApp misinformation spread has created real social havoc; Indian users are at forefront of platform migration debates due to high vulnerability to viral misinformation through forwarding features.
