## Core Argument
Friendliness and empathy, not deception, predict who gains and retains power in human societies.

## Key Points
- Machiavelli's theory assumes humans are selfish and greedy, but empirical psychology contradicts this
- People who rise to power are typically the friendliest and most empathic, not the most ruthless
- Arrogant, deceptive behaviour gets you excluded from groups; trust-building gets you elevated

## Open Question
If friendliness is the actual path to power, why do Machiavellian tactics remain so widespread in business and politics, and what causes the persistence of this misunderstanding?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders often operate in hierarchical, zero-sum competitive environments; this challenges the assumption that Machiavellian tactics are necessary for success, suggesting alternative power models based on empathy and trust could reshape Indian organisational culture.
