## Core Argument
Global conflicts, climate crisis, and tech competition reshape India's strategic, economic, and entrepreneurial landscape fundamentally.

## Key Points
- US-China tech war (CHIPS Act) creates complex ripples for non-American, non-Chinese firms and Indian tech ecosystem
- Russia-Ukraine conflict and China's neighbourhood strategy reposition India as critical Eurasian power amid rising bipolarity
- Climate change demands energy scale-up while fusion breakthrough and green-tech humanitarian costs reshape strategy

## Open Question
How can India harness energy growth needed for development while managing climate impacts and avoiding entanglement in US-China decoupling?

## India Angle
India faces unique challenges across geopolitics (China in neighbourhood, US-China tech war impacts), energy needs amid climate crisis, and domestic economic transformations (Adani saga, new consumer models); 2024 elections and India@100 narrative signal critical transition period
