## Core Argument
India's impatience—reflected in pointless honking and rushed policy—undermines growth and stability; acceptance of constraints yields better outcomes.

## Key Points
- Labour force exodus of 21 million women signals economic participation crisis threatening India's development trajectory.
- Impatience spiral in behaviour (honking, rushed work) produces counterproductive outcomes; Taoist yielding approach more effective.
- Inflation control requires political maturity to accept RBI rate hikes as stability investment, not anti-growth interference.

## Open Question
How can India achieve high growth velocity while acknowledging that impatience and force-pushing actually slow systemic progress—and will politicians accept this paradox?

## India Angle
India faces a labour participation crisis (40% vs 46% in 2017) and gender employment collapse (9% for women), threatening its demographic dividend. Simultaneously, inflation management and public discourse degradation pose governance challenges requiring systemic thinking beyond immediate political cycles.
