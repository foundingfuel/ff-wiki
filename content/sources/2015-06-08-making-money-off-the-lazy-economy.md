## Core Argument
India's on-demand service economy will reshape commerce, labor markets, and wealth distribution through technology-enabled laziness.

## Key Points
- On-demand@home startups exploit Indian consumer preference for personalized service delivery at scale.
- Light-asset, tech-enabled models bypass high infrastructure costs while creating millions of flexible service jobs.
- Entire cities will become addressable markets for small businesses through last-mile delivery platforms.

## Open Question
How will the wealth transfer from consumers to service workers actually play out when gig economy workers lack job security, benefits, and stable income despite higher individual earnings?

## India Angle
India's unique confluence of smartphone penetration, preference for personalized service, low labor costs, and venture capital creates conditions for the world's largest on-demand services economy—fundamentally reshaping local commerce, labor markets, and wealth distribution.
