## Core Argument
Bitcoin's appeal rests on ideology, not investment merit; resembles tulip mania with systemic risks.

## Key Points
- Bitcoin's price surge driven by believers seeking political statement, not rational profit motives.
- Central banks control money supply to maintain trust and prevent economic collapse via inflation.
- Supreme Court lacks practical capacity to regulate peer-to-peer technology; plea misunderstands P2P fundamentals.
- Money's value derives from sovereign backing and societal consensus, not speculative market momentum.

## Open Question
If bitcoin's ideology attracts believers willing to lose money for political reasons, how can traditional regulatory or economic arguments meaningfully address its growth?

## India Angle
India's Supreme Court intervention in cryptocurrency regulation; parallels to Aadhaar debate; RBI's role in managing currency and trust; implications for India's financial system stability.
