## Core Argument
Unseen risks cause more damage than visible ones; preparedness matters more than event magnitude.

## Key Points
- Risk isn't just probability of bad events; it's about target preparedness for surprises.
- India's vaccine distribution requires 10M+ daily vaccinations—an unprecedented scale challenge needing digital backbone.
- Biosecure bubbles show short-term solutions can become long-term existential threats to individuals and systems.

## Open Question
How do policymakers design systems robust against unknown unknowns when preparedness requires anticipating what cannot be anticipated?

## India Angle
India faces unprecedented logistical and systems challenges in vaccinating 1.3 billion people within two years, requiring digital infrastructure at scale (Aadhaar model) and institutional capacity. Hidden risks—like underpreparedness and unforeseen disruptions—could derail vaccine distribution and economic recovery plans.
