## Core Argument
Transformative technologies advance exponentially, but regulatory, ethical and human perception challenges lag behind innovation.

## Key Points
- Self-driving cars face regulatory hurdles and must learn hundreds of human signals beyond technical capability.
- Cryptocurrencies enable legitimate innovation but create security trade-offs that regulators struggle to balance.
- AI advances rapidly (chess mastery in 72 hours) but fundamental risks require proactive governance frameworks.

## Open Question
How do we enable rapid technological innovation while adequately addressing regulatory, security, and ethical risks that emerge only after technical success?

## India Angle
India must prepare for autonomous vehicle regulation, cryptocurrency oversight, and AI governance frameworks—critical for adopting transformative technologies while managing societal risks like job displacement in transport/driving sectors.
