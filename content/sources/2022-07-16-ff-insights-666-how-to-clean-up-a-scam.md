## Core Argument
Crisis leadership requires separating individual wrongdoing from institutional reputation, then rebuilding trust through coordinated action.

## Key Points
- Satyam's survival depended on reframing narrative from company failure to individual fraud, protecting innocent stakeholders.
- Som Mittal's ground-zero presence and government coordination prevented institutional lockdown, enabling employee/client retention.
- SoftBank's model of capital-as-weapon obscured unsustainable business models, revealing tension between growth and profitability.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance protecting an organization's innocent stakeholders from reputational collapse while ensuring genuine accountability for wrongdoing?

## India Angle
Satyam scam was India's largest corporate fraud; how Indian industry bodies and government managed reputation recovery offers lessons for Indian leaders facing corporate crises. SoftBank's India portfolio (Oyo) also affected Indian entrepreneurship ecosystem.
