## Core Argument
Effective negotiation requires playing to your strengths while selectively adopting other styles, not abandoning authenticity.

## Key Points
- Three negotiation styles exist—Accommodator, Assertive, Analyst—each can be effective when applied contextually.
- Cooperative negotiators outperformed assertive ones in lawyer effectiveness studies, contradicting Hollywood stereotypes.
- Personal negotiating style is flexible; people can throttle nondominant styles without replacing core strengths.

## Open Question
How do individuals know when to shift styles and when to remain authentic, especially under pressure or power imbalances?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and professionals negotiating with global partners, investors, or within hierarchical organizational structures benefit from understanding negotiation styles beyond aggressive assertion—a culturally relevant insight given India's cooperative business traditions.
