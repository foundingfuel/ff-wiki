## Core Argument
India's best leaders chase global careers over building iconic Indian companies, jeopardizing the nation's economic transformation.

## Key Points
- HUL's dominance as India's 'CEO factory' has eroded; global tech and consulting firms now attract top talent.
- Historical HUL/ITC leaders prioritized building for India over global advancement; today's leaders reverse this priority.
- India's economic transformation requires homegrown business leaders, not talent exiting to serve global corporations.

## Open Question
How can India retain world-class talent to build indigenous champions when global corporations offer more prestigious, lucrative career paths?

## India Angle
India's top talent is increasingly drawn to global MNCs (McKinsey, Google, Goldman Sachs) rather than building Indian champions. This risks losing the leadership pipeline that built iconic Indian companies like HUL and ITC, critical as India pursues its transformation story.
