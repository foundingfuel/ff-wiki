## Core Argument
Individual genetic choice without guardrails threatens societal diversity and human resilience through collective action.

## Key Points
- Unregulated genetic selection creates a slippery slope from eliminating disease to designer traits, eroding population diversity.
- Tension between individual reproductive autonomy and society's collective interest in genetic and human diversity remains unresolved.
- Culture prioritizes resume virtues (career success) over eulogy virtues (character, kindness), enabling moral mediocrity in public behavior.

## Open Question
How do we protect societal genetic diversity without violating individual reproductive freedom—or do we accept that individual choice inevitably erodes the commons?

## India Angle
India's rapidly growing biotech and fertility sectors will face similar regulatory dilemmas; cultural values around diversity, caste, and family preferences add complexity to genetic choice frameworks that Western democracies grapple with.
