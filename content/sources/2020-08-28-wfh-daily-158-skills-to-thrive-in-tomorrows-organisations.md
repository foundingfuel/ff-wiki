## Core Argument
Leaders must develop AI/tech literacy and intentionally design culture as organizations transform digitally.

## Key Points
- Tech fluency (statistics, programming, AI) is now baseline literacy for all executives, not just engineers.
- Culture is a shared system; leaders must actively define it or employees will default to lowest-common-denominator behaviours.
- Deliberate culture design—like Asana's inversion of hierarchy—requires sustained effort: coaches, experts, and years of refinement.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance moving fast and innovating while simultaneously investing time in deliberate culture-building, especially in competitive markets where speed often wins?

## India Angle
Indian startups and enterprises scaling rapidly often skip deliberate culture-building; this underscores why founders must invest in defining values early, especially as WFH becomes permanent and requires intentional cohesion.
