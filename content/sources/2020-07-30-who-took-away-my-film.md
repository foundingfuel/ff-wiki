## Core Argument
Streaming threatens theatres not via convenience alone, but by enabling participatory, interactive entertainment that aligns with evolving consumer expectations.

## Key Points
- Consumers sample rather than consume full content when friction is removed and options are unlimited.
- Longer-form media thrives when interactive and social; gaming outpaces film in engagement and revenue.
- Indian theatres' cultural role as accessible public spaces differentiates them, but they must evolve beyond passive viewing.

## Open Question
If interactive participation is what consumers truly crave, can movie theatres—inherently passive experiences—survive by merely becoming 'evolved' versions of themselves, or do they need fundamental reimagining as social-interactive platforms?

## India Angle
Indian movie theatres have unique cultural role as public spaces accessible across social strata; face existential threat from streaming but retain advantage if they evolve beyond functional consumption spaces into immersive, interactive experiences.
