## Core Argument
Jackal (judgmental conflict-framing) versus giraffe (empathetic cooperation) communication shapes worldviews and systemic outcomes.

## Key Points
- Jackal communication classifies people as good/bad, right/wrong, provoking defensiveness; giraffe speaks from heart without judgment.
- Two worldviews compete: one sees constant conflict and competition; the other recognises conflict but emphasises harmony, empathy and cooperation.
- Listening without judgment is essential to avoid polarisation, especially during elections and policy reform debates.

## Open Question
If giraffe communication is morally superior, how do we transition a polarised society entrenched in jackal thinking without appearing to judge jackal speakers?

## India Angle
Directly relevant to India's election season and polarized public discourse; calls for non-judgmental listening in Indian governance and business reform debates, especially as capitalism's purpose is contested in Indian policy discussions.
