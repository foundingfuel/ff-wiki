## Core Argument
India must learn to listen across ideological divides to solve shared problems and build democratic institutions.

## Key Points
- Internet and data technologies are creating filter bubbles, isolating us from diverse perspectives.
- Listening without preconceived notions is foundational to solving complex societal problems.
- Data ownership battles threaten privacy; institutions must balance innovation with citizen protection.

## Open Question
How can societies harness technology's benefits (efficiency, connection) while protecting against its tendency to polarize and concentrate power in data monopolies?

## India Angle
India faces acute tensions between data sovereignty (Aadhaar), free speech (Lankesh's murder), and democratic participation. Building consensus across ideological divides is essential for India's development and institutional trust.
