## Core Argument
Aadhaar's digital stack disrupts government efficiency and enables startup ecosystems by creating open, paperless, cashless infrastructure for a billion Indians.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar reached 1 billion registrations in 5.5 years, faster than Android, requiring extraordinary accuracy and scaling precision.
- India Stack layers (e-KYC, eSign, UPI, consent) create open digital infrastructure that reduces service delivery costs by 99% and enables new markets.
- The system empowers the poor through choice of providers and financial inclusion while improving government efficiency and reducing leakages in Rs 2 trillion quarterly spending.

## Open Question
Can volunteer-driven, credible, free technological solutions genuinely solve fundamental problems like poverty and hunger that decades of development haven't addressed?

## India Angle
Aadhaar represents a uniquely Indian approach to solving scale and inclusion challenges through digital infrastructure, enabling financial inclusion, government efficiency, and service delivery for 1.4 billion people at dramatically lower costs than traditional systems.
