## Core Argument
Organizations must abandon dehumanizing metrics and hierarchies to unlock collaborative innovation and Blue Ocean growth.

## Key Points
- Behavioural economics proves humans are irrational; management systems must account for emotions, beliefs, and real behaviour.
- Hierarchical 'thinkers vs doers' models block innovation; frontline employees closest to customers drive breakthrough discovery.
- Understanding non-customers through authentic human interaction, not mechanistic research, reveals new market spaces and growth vectors.

## Open Question
How can firms operationalize 'humanness' and collaborative innovation at scale without reverting to old efficiency-focused metrics that measure everything but impact?

## India Angle
Indian firms, often built on hierarchical models and efficiency-driven cultures, need to shift toward collaborative innovation and humanized management to compete globally and create new market spaces rather than fighting over shrinking pie.
