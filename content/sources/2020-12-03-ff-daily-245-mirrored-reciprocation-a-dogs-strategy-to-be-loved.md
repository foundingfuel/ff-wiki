## Core Argument
Unconditional attention and respect first creates reciprocal loyalty and gets everything you need from others.

## Key Points
- Pay attention, listen, show respect first—reciprocation mirrors what you give, not what you demand.
- Simple human principle works universally: everyone seeks meaning, fulfillment, and feeling valued by others.
- Twitter's manipulated media labels tackle misinformation; India's financial literacy through voices like Monika Halan addresses consumer vulnerability.

## Open Question
If reciprocation is so simple and universal, why do most people and organisations fail to act on it consistently?
