## Core Argument
Leaders ignore AI existential risks like climate denial; global safety agreements needed now before catastrophe.

## Key Points
- Safety guardrails on advanced AI systems are fragile and repeatedly breached within weeks of launch.
- AI can enable bioweapons creation, autonomous warfare, and loss of human control over critical decisions.
- Policymakers dismissing scientist warnings mirrors historical patterns preceding climate, nuclear, and other civilisational crises.

## Open Question
How do we balance accelerating AI development for competitive and economic benefits against genuine existential risks that may be impossible to reverse once triggered?

## India Angle
India's AI policy must not repeat the pattern of denial seen with climate change and nuclear risks. As India becomes a major AI consumer and developer, its policymakers need proactive governance frameworks rather than reactive crisis management, especially given geopolitical vulnerabilities.
