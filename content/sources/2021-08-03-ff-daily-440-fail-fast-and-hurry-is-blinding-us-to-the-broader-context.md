## Core Argument
Unchecked 'fail fast' mentality damages human relationships and obscures situational context.

## Key Points
- Fail fast works for systems; humans require slower, relationship-rebuilding processes with lasting emotional impact.
- Hurrying blinds organisations to broader context and alternative possibilities, reducing strategic clarity.
- Humble Inquiry—slowing down to ask and reflect—builds trust and situational awareness faster than reactive speed.

## Open Question
How can organisations maintain urgency and iteration speed while protecting relational trust and contextual understanding in human-intensive work?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and leaders—especially in tech and startups—are adopting 'fail fast' culture uncritically; this risks damaging team relationships and institutional trust, critical for navigating India's complex, relationship-dependent business environment.
