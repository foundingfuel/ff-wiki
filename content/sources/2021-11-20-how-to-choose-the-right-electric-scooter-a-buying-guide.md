## Core Argument
Buying an electric scooter requires systematic evaluation of range, charging infrastructure, power, and warranty—not just price.

## Key Points
- Battery range (50-75 km), charging time (3-5 hours), and home charging infrastructure are foundational criteria
- Power-range trade-off modes and warranty (3-5 years) are unique to EVs and critical for reliability
- Apartment regulations, grid access, and local charging networks determine practical feasibility, not just vehicle specs

## Open Question
How do individual consumer decisions scale when critical infrastructure (charging networks, housing society policies) remains fragmented and inconsistent across Indian cities?

## India Angle
India's electric two-wheeler market is nascent with 50+ brands competing fiercely; infrastructure gaps (charging stations, apartment regulations) and affordability challenges are critical decision factors for Indian consumers. Policy frameworks like Karnataka's charger installation rules directly impact adoption.
