## Core Argument
Adani's NDTV acquisition exploits regulatory ambiguities and weakened Roy family bargaining positions through systematic structural maneuvers.

## Key Points
- SEBI approved open offer before warrant conversion completed, setting unprecedented regulatory precedent for Indian capital markets.
- MIB guideline amendments on November 9 neutralized Roy family's last legal protection around 51% Indian shareholder requirement.
- Low tender price (Rs 294 vs Rs 406 market) yet 31% shares tendered suggests coordinated investor participation or information asymmetry.

## Open Question
How should regulators balance enabling legitimate corporate transactions against protecting against strategic exploitation of legal ambiguities and governance loopholes?
