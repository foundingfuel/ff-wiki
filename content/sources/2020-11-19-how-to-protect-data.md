## Core Argument
Data protection requires legal mandates and technical design, not reliance on business leaders' judgment calls.

## Key Points
- BigBasket breach exposed gap: no mandatory disclosure laws force companies to rely on discretionary decisions
- India's pending data protection act should enforce strict breach notification timelines and heavy non-compliance penalties
- Privacy-by-design tech can minimize data collection; instead, companies maximize it for business advantage

## Open Question
Should societies regulate data protection through law enforcement or trust companies to self-regulate through technology and ethics?

## India Angle
India's impending data protection act must mandate disclosure timelines and penalties to move beyond human discretion in breach response; also relevant as Indian journalists consider independent platforms amid mainstream media constraints
