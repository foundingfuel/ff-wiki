## Core Argument
Learning fast requires direct observation and engagement with real operations, not abstract analysis.

## Key Points
- J.R.D. Tata's rebuke taught that executives must know essential operating facts, not just delegate information-gathering.
- Moolgaokar's philosophy prioritized clear focus on material facts over data overload—selective attention as leadership skill.
- Choosing Honda over larger competitors because of its demonstrated learning agility reveals how organizations select for capability to transform.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance deep operational knowledge with the focus required for strategy, given the impossibility of mastering all details personally?

## India Angle
TELCO's strategic choice of Honda as partner during India's economic liberalization in 1984 shaped domestic automobile industry's learning trajectory and competitive capability—foundational to modern Indian auto sector.
