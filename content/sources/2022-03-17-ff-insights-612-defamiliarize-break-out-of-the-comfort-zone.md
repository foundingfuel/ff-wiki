## Core Argument
Defamiliarization—making the unseen visible—drives organizational and societal change by breaking comfort zones.

## Key Points
- Crawford's Amazon Echo chart revealed hidden 'ghost workers' and supply chains, forcing Amazon to improve labour practices.
- Defamiliarization works through art, travel, learning—anything that forces us outside comfort zones to see what we normally ignore.
- Moral principles bend under convenience pressure; Substack's algorithmic discovery contradicts its stated values, showing defamiliarization failure.

## Open Question
How can organizations genuinely commit to defamiliarization when immediate business pressures incentivize returning to familiar, convenient practices?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and policymakers need defamiliarization to see hidden costs of tech adoption—labour practices, supply chains, environmental impact—especially as AI and automation scale in India's economy.
