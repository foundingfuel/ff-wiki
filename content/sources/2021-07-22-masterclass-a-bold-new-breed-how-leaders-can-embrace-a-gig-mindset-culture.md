## Core Argument
Leaders must cultivate gig mindset—distributed initiative-taking across hierarchy—to drive organizational resilience in volatile times.

## Key Points
- Gig mindset is about people at any level taking initiative without formal approval, not just top management.
- Traditional performance systems fail to identify unconventional thinkers; look for demonstrated initiative outside formal work contexts.
- Organizational silos and fixed career labels are breaking down; boundaryless thinking and cross-boundary collaboration are now essential.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically identify and nurture people with gig mindset when traditional performance evaluation systems actively penalize unconventional, challenging behavior?

## India Angle
India's organizational culture remains hierarchical and approval-driven; adopting gig mindset is critical for Indian leaders to unlock distributed leadership, especially given talent challenges and need for rapid adaptation in volatile markets.
