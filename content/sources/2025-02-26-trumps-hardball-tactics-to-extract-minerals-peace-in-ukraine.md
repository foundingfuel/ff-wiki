## Core Argument
Trump's minerals-for-peace deal risks cementing Russian territorial gains and fracturing Western unity on Ukraine.

## Key Points
- Most Ukrainian critical minerals are in Russian-controlled regions, giving Putin economic and negotiation leverage over any deal.
- US withdrawal from peacekeeping and sidelining Europe signals NATO erosion and pushes European nations toward strategic independence.
- Trump-Putin statements contradict on peacekeepers and mineral access, leaving peace frameworks open to Russian reinterpretation.

## Open Question
Can a minerals deal that excludes Russian-controlled territories achieve lasting peace, or does excluding these regions validate Putin's territorial claims and destabilize any agreement?

## India Angle
India faces similar resource dependencies on critical minerals; Trump's transactional approach to geopolitics signals unpredictable US partnerships, affecting India's strategic autonomy and mineral security strategies amid US-China competition.
