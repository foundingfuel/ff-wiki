## Core Argument
Indian journalism's reliance on access and corporate favours has eroded editorial independence and reader trust.

## Key Points
- Access journalism forces reporters to parrot corporate narratives or face information cutoffs and retaliation.
- Junket culture, voucher scams, and transcript manipulation undermine journalistic integrity at structural level.
- Readers rightfully distrust media; editors incentivize compromised reporting and sponsors influence story placement.

## Open Question
If journalists acknowledge the system is broken but continue participating in it, what institutional or individual reforms could restore credibility without losing competitive access?

## India Angle
Indian business journalism is compromised by access-based reporting, junket culture, and PR capture—weakening institutional accountability and investor protection in India's startup and corporate ecosystem.
