## Core Argument
Europe faces hard choices on strategic autonomy, defense spending, and great power alignment as its post-war order weakens.

## Key Points
- Europe militarizing without strategic unity on pace or extent; Ukraine conflict creates lasting security architecture questions.
- US-EU trade deal and China negotiations reveal Europe's weakened negotiating position between competing superpowers.
- Technology lag and fragmented investment undermine EU competitiveness despite climate leadership and Draghi Report's $800B+ call.

## Open Question
How can Europe achieve genuine strategic autonomy while remaining militarily dependent on US equipment and economically intertwined with both US and China?

## India Angle
India is mentioned as a player shifting away from post-war rules-based order alongside Trump, Xi, and Putin. Europe's strategic challenges—balancing great powers, tech competition, trade friction—offer lessons for India's own geopolitical positioning and need to build strategic autonomy without dependence on any single power.
