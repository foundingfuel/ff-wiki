## Core Argument
Humility and self-organized small teams unlock breakthrough productivity and innovation in organizations.

## Key Points
- Leaders must acknowledge what they don't know and loosen control to enable breakthroughs.
- Small, self-managed teams (two-pizza rule) eliminate bureaucracy and boost coordination.
- CSR evolves to Corporate Social Justice: mutual value creation with communities, not charity.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need for governance and accountability with the autonomy required for self-organized, productive teams?

## India Angle
Indian founders and managers can adopt humility-driven leadership and flat team structures (like two-pizza teams) to boost productivity in distributed work environments, while rethinking CSR as Corporate Social Justice—particularly relevant as Indian companies scale globally.
