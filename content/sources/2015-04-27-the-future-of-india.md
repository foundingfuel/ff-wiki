## Core Argument
India's workforce boom arrives as automation and winner-takes-all economics eliminate the jobs it's preparing for.

## Key Points
- Middle-class decline spreading from US to India's MNC hubs; managerial positions shrinking, age discrimination rising.
- Value shifted from hardware/manufacturing to software/services; India's e-commerce valuations exceed industrial giants.
- Automation accelerating globally; routine jobs migrate to lowest total-cost locations, not just lowest wages—India uncompetitive despite potential skill improvements.

## Open Question
If automation makes routine manufacturing jobs obsolete and winner-takes-all economics concentrate wealth, what role can India's massive workforce play, and can any government manage the resulting social welfare burden?

## India Angle
India faces a critical mismatch: it's building manufacturing capacity and a massive workforce just as automation and winner-takes-all economics are eliminating routine jobs. Middle-class decline visible in US is emerging in Indian MNCs. India's cost advantage erodes when total costs (automation, logistics, regulation) are factored in, not just wages.
