## Core Argument
Policymaking requires translating abstract concepts into actionable frameworks that account for real-world complexity and unintended consequences.

## Key Points
- Basu reframes Aadhaar not as identity verification but as a transactional partitioning system—showing conceptual rigor needed for transformative tech.
- EU's tiered AI regulation demonstrates how democracies can govern emerging tech responsibly, unlike China's social control approach.
- Gap between policy intent and stakeholder reality: Japan's Olympics shows leaders can be disconnected from public and institutional sentiment.

## Open Question
How can policymakers ensure transformative technologies like Aadhaar deliver promised benefits while adequately safeguarding against risks they may not fully anticipate?

Explores how policymakers like Kaushik Basu navigate real-world implementation gaps in India; examines Aadhaar as transformative infrastructure and regulatory lessons from EU's AI framework relevant to India's own governance challenges.
