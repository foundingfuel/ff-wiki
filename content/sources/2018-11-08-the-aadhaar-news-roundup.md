## Core Argument
Aadhaar's policy contradictions expose how Indian startups navigate regulatory uncertainty in building on public platforms.

## Key Points
- Supreme Court's Section 57 verdict blocks private sector biometric use, forcing fintech to find alternative KYC solutions.
- Virtual Aadhaar faced nine-year implementation delay due to policy resistance, finally enabled by privacy activism.
- Identity systems like Aadhaar can unlock access to financial services, education, and political participation across developing economies.

## Open Question
How can Aadhaar function as an enabler of financial inclusion and state capacity while simultaneously creating policy and legislative risks that deter private sector innovation?

## India Angle
Aadhaar is India's foundational digital identity system; Supreme Court's Section 57 verdict creates regulatory uncertainty for Indian fintech startups relying on biometric authentication, affecting KYC processes and startup risk landscape.
