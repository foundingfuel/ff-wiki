## Core Argument
India's slowdown is real but residents retain optimism; informal sector workers face income pressure amid demand collapse.

## Key Points
- Vegetable vendors see 50% demand drops as consumers shift to cheaper goods, unable to afford premium items.
- GST implementation blamed for wholesale textile market contraction, reducing downstream demand and worker wages.
- Despite economic gloom, Surat residents maintain belief in personal hard work to overcome structural economic challenges.

## Open Question
How can individual mehnat (hard work) solve structural demand collapse caused by policy and macroeconomic cycles that are beyond personal agency?
