## Core Argument
Organisations fail when meanings attached to data drift; AI accelerates this by stabilising outdated categories.

## Key Points
- Data correctness doesn't guarantee good decisions; the semantic meaning attached to categories is the actual leverage point.
- AI intensifies semantic drift by making classification sharper and more consistent, masking when interpretive frames become obsolete.
- Leaders must audit verbs (actions) attached to nouns (categories) and question when decision rules were last updated against current conditions.

## Open Question
How can organisations systematically detect when the meaning of their core categories has shifted, before symptoms like escalations and workarounds become visible?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises scaling rapidly with AI-driven systems (fintech, healthcare, e-commerce) face acute semantic drift risk—categories like 'creditworthy' or 'quality' imported from legacy data may misfire in India's dynamic, heterogeneous market. Leadership must audit whether decision rules reflect current Indian contexts.
