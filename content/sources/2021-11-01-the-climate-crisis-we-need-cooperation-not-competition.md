## Core Argument
Global crises demand cooperation based on ethics and systems thinking, not competition and narrow self-interest.

## Key Points
- Nations prioritize self-interest over collective survival despite climate change transcending borders
- Adam Smith's invisible hand and competitive advantage theories inadequate for systemic problems requiring ethical frameworks
- AI and technology advance faster than ethical guardrails; human self-awareness and purpose must precede innovation

## Open Question
How can nation-states legitimately prioritize global cooperation and equity when democratically accountable only to their own citizens?

## India Angle
India faces acute climate vulnerability and vaccine inequality; must navigate between development needs and global cooperation. Framework for ethics-first policymaking critical for emerging economies often excluded from Western-designed solutions.
