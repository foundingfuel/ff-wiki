## Core Argument
Cultural festivals must choose between corporate dependency and independence; community funding offers creative freedom but structural fragility.

## Key Points
- Corporate sponsors increasingly avoid festivals due to controversy risk and preference for mass-appeal events like sports.
- Bangalore LitFest shifted from failed corporate sponsorship to 140 individual contributors, capping any single donor at 3% of budget.
- Independent funding model protects editorial freedom but raises scalability questions for festivals beyond tier-1 metros.

## Open Question
How can the individual-donor model sustain cultural festivals across India's smaller cities and towns where wealthy patron networks don't exist, without compromising the independence this model provides?

## India Angle
Indian cultural festivals face acute sustainability challenges as corporate sponsors withdraw due to controversy risk and limited ROI. BLF's community-funded model offers a distinctly Indian solution leveraging individual patronage networks, but raises questions about whether this approach scales beyond metropolitan, educated audiences.
