## Core Argument
Champions build mental strength through competence, honest self-reflection, and calculated risk-taking rather than external validation.

## Key Points
- Self-belief stems from improving at something and making better decisions incrementally over time.
- Mental stamina requires finding fresh purpose—either new goals, learning, or rediscovering intrinsic joy in the work.
- Strategic risk-taking involves doing unusual, dangerous moves others haven't prepared for rather than playing it safe.

## Open Question
How do performers maintain intrinsic motivation and fresh purpose when they've already achieved peak success, and does external support (coaches, psychologists) help or burden that process?

## India Angle
Indian high-performers and leaders can learn from Vishy's framework on mental resilience, self-belief through competence-building, and strategic risk-taking—applicable to competitive Indian business environments and startup cultures.
