## Core Argument
Sustained crises require endurance pacing, not sprint mentality; front-load failures to build resilience.

## Key Points
- Science, sports, and crisis management are endurance games requiring stamina, not just creativity or early peaks
- Front-loading failures during team formation (like Entrepreneur First does) reduces co-founder breakups post-launch significantly
- Starbucks accelerating store format changes shows how crises force long-term strategic shifts faster than planned

## Open Question
If crises are endurance events, how do founders maintain motivation and capital runway without early visible wins that prove viability?

## India Angle
Indian MSMEs and entrepreneurs need endurance-based strategies for long-term crisis management; pacing over sprinting applies to Indian startup culture that often burns out founders quickly
