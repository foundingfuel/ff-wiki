## Core Argument
Bollywood faces existential disruption from streaming platforms and South Indian competition requiring strategic repositioning.

## Key Points
- South Indian films dominate Indian box office; Bollywood now collaborates with South producers as competitive disadvantage
- Pandemic accelerated direct-to-streaming strategy, reshaping viewer expectations and theatrical economics irreversibly
- Spectacle-driven franchises (RRR, KGF) prove theatrical survival strategy; streaming cannibalizes mid-budget releases

## Open Question
Can Bollywood revive theatrical releases through spectacle-driven franchises, or has pandemic-induced streaming adoption fundamentally and irreversibly shifted viewer preferences?

## India Angle
Bollywood's structural crisis reflects deeper shifts in Indian consumer behavior and media consumption, while South Indian cinema's dominance signals regional industry maturity and strategic advantage in theatrical exhibition.
