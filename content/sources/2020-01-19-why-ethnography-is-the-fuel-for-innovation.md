## Core Argument
Deep listening through ethnography is essential fuel for innovation and understanding real customer needs.

## Key Points
- Ethnography requires deliberate face-to-face practice; no workshop substitute exists for ground understanding.
- Economists and business leaders must listen to real people's problems, not treat them as abstract data.
- Indian slowdown creates urgency to deeply understand how average Indians across towns are adapting.

## Open Question
How can busy executives balance the time investment required for genuine ethnographic practice with operational demands, without diluting outcomes through outsourcing?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and executives need ethnographic skills to understand real ground realities across India's diverse towns and economic strata, especially as the economy slows and consumer behaviour shifts.
