## Core Argument
Reframe failure as data collection by treating early work as experiments with uncertain outcomes.

## Key Points
- Perfectionism delays launch; lean methodology advocates early release and iteration over polish.
- Experiments have uncertain outcomes by design, reframing 'failure' as learning opportunities.
- Edison's 999 attempts model shows success requires multiple iterations informed by data, not finality.

## Open Question
How do you balance shipping early with maintaining minimum quality standards that preserve credibility and prevent reputational damage?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and professionals often over-prepare before launching; treating work as experiments aligns with lean startup thinking gaining traction in India's startup ecosystem and can help overcome perfectionism barriers.
