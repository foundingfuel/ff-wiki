## Core Argument
Custom-built bicycles reveal how India's quality craft market struggles against consumer price expectations despite global-standard engineering.

## Key Points
- Premium bicycle manufacturing uses aerospace-grade materials and rivals superbike technology, costing Rs 1-4 lakh, yet Indians resist premium bicycle pricing.
- Personal rediscovery: a thoughtfully engineered custom bike rekindled passion lost to workforce demands and motorcycle obsession.
- India's bicycle culture gap: stunning landscapes exist but underutilization stems from lack of quality options and consumer mindset, not geography.

## Open Question
How can artisanal bicycle makers sustain premium businesses in India when consumers spend lavishly on running shoes but balk at equivalent frame costs?

## India Angle
India lacks a culture of understanding and investing in quality bicycles; custom bike builders like Intent Bikes and Cycle Republik are creating a niche market by educating Indians about craftsmanship and design, challenging assumptions about what deserves premium pricing.
