## Core Argument
Storytelling structures—clarity stories, anti-stories, success stories—overcome the four biggest obstacles to organizational change.

## Key Points
- Less than 5% of employees understand their company strategy; abstract language and cognitive bias prevent message retention.
- Clarity stories harness narrative's three powers: easy to understand, remember, and retell—creating multiplier effect beyond leadership.
- Anti-stories must be acknowledged and addressed through narrative, not data or assertions, to overcome employee skepticism.

## Open Question
How do leaders distinguish between legitimate anti-stories rooted in past failures versus cynicism that needs to be overcome through better storytelling?

## India Angle
Indian companies struggle with strategy cascading and employee alignment; storytelling offers a culturally resonant approach to overcome the 'curse of knowledge' and drive transformation in diverse, multilevel organizations.
