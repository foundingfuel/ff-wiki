## Core Argument
Founders must center pitches on how products solve problems, not market size alone.

## Key Points
- Startup pitches lack 'how it works' clarity despite founders understanding products deeply, prioritizing market analysis over solution articulation
- Operating under constraints forces clarity and drives business model innovation; Thyrocare thrived by building franchisee-compatible SOPs for India's fragmented markets
- Detailed explanation of problem-solving mechanism clarifies founder thinking, plugs logical gaps, and builds stronger investment arguments

## Open Question
How can founders balance the need for compelling market opportunity narratives required by investors with the equally critical need to deeply explain their actual solution mechanism?

## India Angle
Indian founders and startups often mimic Western PE/VC pitch formats instead of deeply articulating how products solve local needs; Thyrocare's example shows how Indian entrepreneurs must build systems and SOPs to operate across India's diverse 25+ distinct markets with different cultures.
