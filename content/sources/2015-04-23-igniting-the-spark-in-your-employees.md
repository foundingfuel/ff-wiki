## Core Argument
Employee engagement requires meeting psychological needs through trust, autonomy, and transparency before expecting self-motivation.

## Key Points
- Basic compensation alone doesn't drive commitment; safety, belonging, and esteem needs must be met first.
- Trust-based culture requires autonomy, fair systems, transparent communication, and promise-keeping from leadership.
- Paradox of 'trust but verify' demands systems to identify and swiftly address trust violations.

## Open Question
How do you build genuine trust while simultaneously implementing verification systems that may signal underlying distrust to employees?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises often operate in hierarchical structures with command-and-control cultures; applying Maslow's framework to move beyond basic compensation to trust-based engagement is a transformation challenge for Indian leaders navigating rapid growth and changing workforce expectations.
