## Core Argument
Leaders must align narrative velocity with system readiness or risk collapse when stories outpace capability.

## Key Points
- Stories move faster than systems; when narratives accelerate beyond operational readiness, internal strain becomes public breakdown.
- AI amplifies narrative speed: false information spreads faster than truth, forcing reactive rather than proactive leadership response.
- UPI succeeded because ambition and capability advanced together; airline delays failed because promise outran infrastructure readiness.

## Open Question
How can leaders move with narrative velocity required in modern markets while building the operational discipline and infrastructure to sustain the promises they make?

## India Angle
Indian leaders face acute narrative-capability gaps as stories spread faster than systems can adapt. UPI vs. airline disruptions exemplify the difference. Scaling ambitions without matching infrastructure is a critical risk across Indian startups, regulators, and legacy institutions navigating rapid change.
