## Core Argument
Transformative leaders succeed by pursuing purpose beyond self; society's focus on measurable ends over meaningful means causes burnout.

## Key Points
- Leaders with a compelling 'why' sustain energy for missions extending beyond their lifetimes.
- Society overemphasizes achievement (wealth, fame, size) while neglecting means and sustainable practices.
- Misaligned incentives burn out people and damage environments; corporate culture must shift from extraction to purpose.

## Open Question
How do organizations measure and reward purpose-driven work when society's metrics remain fixed on profit, growth, and shareholder value?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and entrepreneurs face pressure to chase conventional success metrics (wealth, size, status) while burning out themselves and their organizations; redefining 'why' is critical for sustainable Indian business and talent retention.
