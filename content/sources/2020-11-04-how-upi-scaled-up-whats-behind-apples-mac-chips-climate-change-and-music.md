## Core Argument
Platforms scale faster than products; UPI succeeded by being built as infrastructure, not just a payment app.

## Key Points
- UPI reached 2 billion transactions by building as a platform, not a standalone product, enabling rapid ecosystem growth.
- Early skepticism from VCs and fintech players reversed once government support, merchant adoption, and competition drove network effects.
- UPI's architecture allows it to transmit any stored value—coupons, points, miles—unlocking future growth beyond payments.

## Open Question
If UPI's success depends heavily on government support and regulatory backing, can it replicate this in foreign markets like Myanmar where institutional trust and infrastructure may be weaker?

## India Angle
UPI's success demonstrates India's ability to build world-class digital infrastructure and export it globally; shows how India-first platforms can scale faster than traditional fintech models and serve as a model for other emerging markets.
