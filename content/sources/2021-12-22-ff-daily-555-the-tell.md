## Core Argument
Success requires recognizing hidden patterns in behaviour and embracing productive inconsistency over rigid control.

## Key Points
- Overconsistent behaviour and attempts to conceal tells paradoxically reveal more information than natural variation.
- Geopolitical and climate politics create contradictions requiring nuanced navigation rather than binary choices.
- Modern paradoxes emerge when we optimize for single goals while ignoring systemic interdependencies and trade-offs.

## Open Question
How do leaders and policymakers act decisively amid genuine paradoxes where commitments to one value (geopolitics) contradict another (climate action)?

## India Angle
India faces geopolitical paradoxes (US alignment vs China climate cooperation) and must navigate decarbonisation while managing growth pressures similar to emerging economies—requiring leaders skilled at reading hidden signals and managing contradictions.
