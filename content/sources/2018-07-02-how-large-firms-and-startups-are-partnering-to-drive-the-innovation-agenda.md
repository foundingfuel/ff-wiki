## Core Argument
Large firms and startups must partner strategically, with corporations committing to genuine business model change, not just capital.

## Key Points
- Large corporations lack bandwidth for innovation; must partner with startups to access novel ideas and consumer focus.
- Startups prefer VC funding and market access over corporate capital; corporations fail at providing distribution channels.
- Successful partnerships require ideas at the intersection of corporate importance and non-core operations, with clear buyout or outsourcing models.

## Open Question
How can large corporations genuinely commit to sharing market access and ecosystem knowledge with startups when their incentives are to acquire or control innovation rather than enable independence?

## India Angle
Indian large firms struggle with distribution and ecosystem access for startups; VCs are preferred over corporate capital, requiring corporations to rethink partnership models and market access strategies specific to India's startup landscape.
