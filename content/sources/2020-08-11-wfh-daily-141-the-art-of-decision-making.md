## Core Argument
Effective decision-making requires systematic thinking; great leaders succeed by being authentically themselves, not copying predecessors.

## Key Points
- Darwin used structured pros-cons analysis to make life-defining decisions, prioritizing long-term values over immediate preferences.
- Tim Cook succeeded Jobs by staying true to his operational rigor and interrogative style rather than mimicking Jobs' charisma.
- Content creators and companies must work harder to earn attention as consumer choice expands; franchise predictability signals audience preference shift.

## Open Question
How can leaders authentically be themselves while also adapting to changing organizational contexts and stakeholder expectations?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and professionals navigating remote work and organizational transitions can learn from Cook's detail-oriented, interrogative leadership style and Darwin's systematic decision-making—both applicable to scaling teams and managing change in Indian enterprises.
