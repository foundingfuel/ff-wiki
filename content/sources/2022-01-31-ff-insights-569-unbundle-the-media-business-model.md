## Core Argument
Legacy media must unbundle offerings, specialize ruthlessly, and measure economic viability per category to survive.

## Key Points
- Mass-media economics (charging all advertisers for all readers) no longer apply; digital requires honest audits of each section's value.
- Specialized platforms (ESPN, Yelp, TripAdvisor) do focused content better; newspapers lost opportunity to become platform-builders.
- News organizations must choose: compete in everything poorly or specialize and dominate select categories profitably.

## Open Question
How do you preserve journalism's public-interest value (investigative reporting, local coverage) while making brutal economic trade-offs about what to cut?

## India Angle
Indian media houses face similar pressures to legacy Western outlets—bundled business models, advertising dependency, and competition from specialized platforms. Unbundling strategy is critical for Indian publishers to survive digital disruption and remain relevant.
