## Core Argument
Startups die when they chase vanity metrics instead of proving customers actually repeat, refer, and convert.

## Key Points
- Three truths: repeat rates, repeat frequency, and support systems—everything else is distraction from core insight.
- Product-market fit (PMF) is the only validation that matters; growth without it is 'spraying bullets in dark'.
- Cohort analysis must slice across demographics, devices, purchase history, source—not just aggregate numbers on Excel sheets.

## Open Question
How do you balance rapid scaling demands from VCs with the slow, methodical cohort work needed to prove genuine product-market fit?

## India Angle
Indian startups face unique heterogeneity (language, device types, first-time smartphone users, internet access gaps) that demands granular cohort analysis beyond vanity metrics; PMF requires understanding micro-behaviours across fragmented demographics.
