## Core Argument
Crisis leadership separates wheat from chaff: operational firefighting alone fails; visionary CEOs prepare for post-crisis opportunities.

## Key Points
- BCP manuals don't cover unprecedented simultaneous disruptions; leaders must rely on resilience, empathy, and common sense.
- Dual leadership task: manage evolving operational crises while simultaneously envisioning post-crisis opportunities to pull ahead.
- Communication at scale and frequency is non-negotiable; visible, cascading messaging across distributed teams prevents paralysis.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance relentless present-moment firefighting with the cognitive and emotional capacity to strategize for a future that remains radically uncertain?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs navigating unprecedented lockdown disruptions across logistics, insurance, tech, and financial services—testing whether BCP frameworks and leadership resilience can handle cascading crises unique to India's operational complexity.
