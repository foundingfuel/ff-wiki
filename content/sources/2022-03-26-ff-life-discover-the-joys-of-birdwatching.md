## Core Argument
Birdwatching offers accessible therapy, observation skills, and nature connection for Indians post-pandemic.

## Key Points
- Birdwatching develops pattern recognition, focus, and observation skills applicable beyond hobby.
- Pandemic drove surge in birding interest among all ages as alternative to indoor leisure.
- No expertise or elaborate planning needed—accessible from backyards to weekend trips near cities.

## Open Question
How do we scale accessible birdwatching experiences without creating conservation pressures on fragile birding destinations?

Birdwatching as accessible wellness practice across Indian cities and states; pandemic-driven shift in urban Indian leisure habits; family bonding and nature connection in Indian context.
