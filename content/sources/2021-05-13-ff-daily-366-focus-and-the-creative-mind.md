## Core Argument
Protect creative time first, reactive work second—a mindset shift against burnout and wasted potential.

## Key Points
- Block peak cognitive hours for creative work; switch off email/phone to reclaim agency from others' expectations.
- Small constraints (3×3 Post-it lists, hard work boundaries) paradoxically enable focus and prevent task creep.
- Authoritarian pressure (drug prescriptions, top-down governance) forces compliance without understanding; participation enables sustainable behavior change.

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers protect creative autonomy when organizational culture and client expectations demand constant reactivity and availability?

## India Angle
Indian professionals face acute burnout amid always-on work culture; lack of participatory governance in crisis management (COVID example) mirrors systemic inability to empower individuals and communities to self-regulate.
