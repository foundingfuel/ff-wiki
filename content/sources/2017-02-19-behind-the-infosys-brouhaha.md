## Core Argument
Infosys conflict reveals tension between founder control and professional global governance in maturing Indian corporations.

## Key Points
- Founders conflating multiple governance issues to create appearance of chaos, obscuring core severance pay accountability problem.
- Transition from conservative founder-led model to global corporation requires different spending/hiring patterns that founders resist.
- Boards must balance shareholder activism with preventing short-term destabilization while founders relinquish control leverage.

## Open Question
How can Indian boards maintain legitimacy and accountability when founders weaponize governance concerns to reclaim influence they've contractually surrendered?

## India Angle
India's corporate governance maturity is being tested as founders clash with professional management at global Indian companies; institutional investor activism and regulatory oversight remain underdeveloped compared to the US.
