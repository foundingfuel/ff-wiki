## Core Argument
Stories trigger action and commitment better than data alone; essential for organizational change.

## Key Points
- Yunus's microfinance story generated pledges; data-only approaches fail to mobilize stakeholders.
- Concrete human narratives (Ramesh, Vivek) bypass analytical resistance in decision-making committees.
- Storytelling combines emotional connection with facts, creating urgency that statistics cannot.

## Open Question
If stories are more persuasive than data, how do leaders distinguish between manipulative narratives and authentic ones when driving organizational change?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders must move beyond data-driven presentations to narrative-driven decision-making; critical for driving safety culture change in India's manufacturing sector where accident rates remain high.
