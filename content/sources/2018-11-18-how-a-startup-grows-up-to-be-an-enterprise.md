## Core Argument
Indian startups skip adolescence; rapid growth without cultural foundations creates preventable leadership crises.

## Key Points
- Silicon Valley's 40-year ecosystem created mentors, managers, and culture expertise; India's startup wave lacks this institutional maturity.
- Founders treat scaling ventures like extended college dorms—high ideation but weak governance and durable culture infrastructure.
- Flipkart co-founder resignation signals urgent need to embed talent management and ethical frameworks early in growth.

## Open Question
How can India rapidly build the mentoring and governance infrastructure that took Silicon Valley decades, without slowing down the speed-to-scale that defines its competitive advantage?

## India Angle
Indian startups skip the maturation phase that built Silicon Valley's institutional knowledge; founders lack mentorship in governance and culture-building, creating crises like Flipkart's co-founder exits that expose ecosystem immaturity.
