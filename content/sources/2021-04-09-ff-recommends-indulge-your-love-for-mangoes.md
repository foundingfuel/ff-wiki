## Core Argument
Buying quality mangoes requires knowing variety seasonality, verifying source authenticity, and trusting sensory cues over appearance.

## Key Points
- Aroma and firmness matter more than color; smell near stem for authenticity check.
- Different varieties peak April-August; late May-early June offers best availability across all varieties.
- Trusted sourcing is critical to avoid carbide-ripened fruit; refrigeration extends shelf life significantly.

## Open Question
How can consumers reliably verify organic/chemical-free ripening without laboratory testing when visual and olfactory cues can be deceived?

## India Angle
India's 1,500 mango varieties and seasonal availability present unique sourcing, quality, and safety challenges for Indian consumers; carbide ripening and supply chain trust are India-specific concerns.
