## Core Argument
Open digital platforms enable coordinated innovation across sectors to solve population-level development challenges.

## Key Points
- EkStep builds free digital infrastructure enabling multiple actors to innovate on education at scale
- Societal platforms coordinate samaj, sarkar, bazaar—civil society, state, and market—in co-creation
- Rural entrepreneurship platforms like 1Bridge apply platform principles to transform youth into economic actors

## Open Question
How do open platforms sustain themselves and maintain quality when ceding control to diverse innovators across vastly different contexts?

## India Angle
Indian development challenges (education access, rural transformation) require platform-based approaches that coordinate across civil society, government, and market—a model particularly suited to India's scale and resource constraints.
