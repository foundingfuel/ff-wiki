## Core Argument
Passive consumption and intelligence alone are insufficient; combined with imagination and persistence, they unlock real growth.

## Key Points
- Mass media consumption masks emptiness and wastes psychic energy without providing substantive growth or strength.
- Intelligence (IQ) alone cannot solve problems; it must combine with imagination, perseverance, intuition, experience and context.
- Future advantage belongs to those educated to use leisure wisely and take charge of work and free time deliberately.

## Open Question
If AI commoditizes intelligence, how do individuals and organizations cultivate the imagination, persistence, and wisdom needed to remain relevant when IQ becomes a commodity?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and professionals must shift from passive consumption to active skill-building; as AI commoditizes intelligence, Indian talent advantage depends on combining IQ with imagination, persistence, and wisdom in leisure time usage.
