## Core Argument
Learning organisations develop repeatable formulas that enable scaling and transformation across markets and categories.

## Key Points
- PE firms exemplify learning organisations: they start with theory, iterate through do-learn-do cycles, then replicate successful formulas
- Nike's blueprint—establish leadership, endorsement, new categories, higher margins, global expansion—repeats across sports, enabling lifestyle transformation
- Indian q-commerce platforms are real-time learning organisations optimising operations within tight constraints daily

## Open Question
How do learning organisations balance the discipline of repeatable formulas with the flexibility needed to adapt when market conditions fundamentally change?

## India Angle
Indian companies—from startups to established firms—need to build learning organisations that use repeatable, scalable formulas to expand into adjacencies; q-commerce example shows how Indian logistics startups are rapidly iterating on operational models
