## Core Argument
Media has abandoned its democratic responsibility as conscience-keeper by prioritizing entertainment and commercial viability over public education.

## Key Points
- Mainstream media's inability to understand public sentiment led to loss of credibility and relevance.
- Media's dual identity crisis: business imperative versus democratic duty creates unresolved tension.
- Social media democratized agenda-setting but enabled misinformation spread and mob behavior over reasoned discourse.

## Open Question
How can media balance commercial sustainability with democratic responsibility when audiences demand entertainment over education?

## India Angle
India's media ecosystem faces similar pressures: commercial imperatives vs. democratic responsibility; social media's role in polarizing discourse; need for institutional checks on both mainstream and social media influence.
