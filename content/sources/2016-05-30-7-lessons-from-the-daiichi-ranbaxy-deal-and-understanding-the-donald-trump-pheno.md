## Core Argument
Failed M&A deals teach that due diligence, cultural alignment, and ethical standards matter more than synergy on paper.

## Key Points
- Trust but verify: go beyond financial audits to assess ethical standards and stakeholder perceptions before acquisition
- Emotional attachment to deals clouds judgment through selective hearing, confirmation bias, and illusion of attention
- Partner selection requires cultural and value alignment, not just complementary skills; Starbucks-Tata model shows intentional fit matters

## Open Question
How should Indian companies balance rapid growth ambitions with stringent global compliance standards when the financial incentives often favor cutting corners?

## India Angle
Indian pharma companies entering global markets must balance aggressive growth ambitions with rigorous compliance and ethical standards; cultural misalignment between Indian and global partners remains a persistent integration challenge.
