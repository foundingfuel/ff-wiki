## Core Argument
Great action films succeed through pathos and consequences, not spectacle alone.

## Key Points
- Action spectacle elevated by emotional stakes and human consequences differentiates quality films.
- Filmmaking requires collaborative challenge, not auteur control; directorial ego weakens final product.
- Character development and struggle matter more than technical mastery or established premises.

## Open Question
How do directors balance artistic vision with collaborative feedback without diluting their creative intent?

## India Angle
Indian cinema (RRR) achieving Oscar recognition alongside Hollywood blockbusters; questions about what Indian filmmakers can learn from international success patterns.
