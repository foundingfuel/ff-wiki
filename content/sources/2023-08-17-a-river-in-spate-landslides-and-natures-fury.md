## Core Argument
Climate change and modernization are destabilizing Himalayan communities faster than they can adapt or prepare.

## Key Points
- Extreme weather events (flash floods, unseasonal rains) destroying livelihoods and infrastructure in previously stable regions
- Modernization erodes traditional ecological knowledge and adaptive practices that sustained communities for generations
- Rapid environmental changes (wildlife displacement, forest encroachment, water seepage) signal systemic ecological imbalance

## Open Question
How can communities modernize for better living standards without dismantling the traditional knowledge and practices that made them resilient to environmental stress?

## India Angle
Himachal Pradesh faces unprecedented climate extremes and ecological disruption; rapid modernization without planning creates vulnerability to natural disasters and loss of adaptive traditional systems.
