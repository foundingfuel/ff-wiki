## Core Argument
India must choose between ISP revenue protection and open internet access; current ambiguity mirrors EU's indecision.

## Key Points
- Net neutrality debate pits ISPs losing SMS/voice revenue against OTTs and startups demanding equal access.
- Airtel Zero sparked mass activism (#savetheinternet); demonstrates India's civil society organizing capacity on regulation.
- TRAI's consultation approach mirrors EU's rotating ambiguity—India risks regulatory limbo without clear position.

## Open Question
Can India regulate ISPs fairly without either stifling innovation or allowing monopolistic gatekeeping, given conflicting interests of telcos, tech giants, and startups?

## India Angle
India's TRAI faces a critical regulatory decision on net neutrality amid ISP-OTT conflict; the outcome will shape India's internet economics and startup ecosystem competitiveness globally.
