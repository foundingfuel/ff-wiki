## Core Argument
Organisations must embed learning in daily work rhythms and enable personal responsibility to stay future-ready.

## Key Points
- Learning design must integrate with work itself, not exist as separate training programmes.
- Organisations must curate external knowledge and stay aware of societal changes beyond their boundaries.
- Individual employees must own their learning; organisations should remove friction in access and opportunity.

## Open Question
How do organisations balance the need for structured reskilling (54% require significant upskilling) with the empowerment of individual learning ownership when only 10% report readiness?

## India Angle
Indian organisations face acute skills gaps as job redesign accelerates; few are ready for reskilling demands, making learning design and personal responsibility critical for competitiveness.
