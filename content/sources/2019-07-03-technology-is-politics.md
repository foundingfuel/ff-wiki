## Core Argument
Technology choices are political; societies must understand power dynamics embedded in technological systems.

## Key Points
- 5G geopolitics: US-China competition forces countries to choose sides, risking global economic fragmentation.
- Technology fails when designers ignore real-world diversity; AltSchool's pivot shows disruption without stakeholder input falters.
- Forecasting tech impact requires imagining dramatic improvements, not just extrapolating trends—McKinsey/Gates examples.

## Open Question
How can democracies maintain oversight of powerful tech platforms when companies design 1,600-page masterplans to overwhelm public scrutiny?

## India Angle
India must navigate 5G geopolitics between US-China tensions; tech regulation and democratic oversight becoming critical as platforms shape policy without accountability.
