## Core Argument
India's employment and aging realities require accepting imperfect solutions within systemic constraints.

## Key Points
- Indian families absorb elder care through multi-generational living; Western institutionalization model unsuitable
- 77% of Indian households lack steady income; gig platforms creating jobs at realistic wage rates
- Time millionaires challenge work-obsessed cultures to reconsider productivity and purpose definitions

## Open Question
How do we improve gig worker conditions without losing the employment generation that currently absorbs India's labor surplus?

## India Angle
India's aging population and employment crisis demand culturally rooted solutions. Traditional joint family systems enable elder care differently than Western models; gig economy must absorb 20M annual workers despite wage constraints; time millionaires concept challenges India's work obsession.
