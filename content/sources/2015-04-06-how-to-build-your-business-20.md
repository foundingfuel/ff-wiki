## Core Argument
Indian businesses must embed entrepreneurial culture structurally or be disrupted by startups operating at technology-first, capital-light scale.

## Key Points
- Startups disrupt via three mechanisms: direct displacement using technology, dissection of value chains, and diversion to entirely new solutions.
- Indian firms built for high transaction costs created bloated organizations where technology serves operations, not strategy.
- Older firms' internal task forces fail because teams optimized for shelf-stocking cannot think like digital-first consumers.

## Open Question
How can established Indian firms with legacy DNA and middle managers baptized only in growth markets rapidly rewire themselves for technology-first thinking before startups eat their core business?

## India Angle
Indian firms face accelerated disruption due to compressed market evolution, younger management inexperience, transaction-cost-driven organizational bloat, and consumer loyalty collapse. Western companies had stable markets and experienced managers to adapt; Indian companies lack both.
