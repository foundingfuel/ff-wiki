## Core Argument
When institutions lag in updating guidance, individuals must self-upgrade knowledge and behaviours to match evolving virus risks.

## Key Points
- WHO/CDC silently backtracked on aerosol transmission without announcement, requiring citizens to independently upgrade safety protocols.
- Mask effectiveness depends on proper fit and seal testing, not just quality—individual discipline matters more than product choice.
- India's PPE supply scarcity means personal verification, authentic sourcing, and informed upgrades are citizen-led responsibilities.

## Open Question
If institutions cannot be trusted to communicate risks transparently and timely, how do citizens distinguish reliable guidance from misinformation when upgrading their own practices?

## India Angle
India faces acute supply shortages of vaccines and quality PPE; citizens must adapt individually when institutions fail to communicate risk updates transparently. This reflects broader state capacity gaps in crisis communication and quality enforcement.
