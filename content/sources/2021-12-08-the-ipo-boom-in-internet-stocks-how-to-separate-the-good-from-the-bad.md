## Core Argument
Most internet IPOs underperform; success requires accounting transparency, capital efficiency, and optionality—not just tech novelty.

## Key Points
- Only 1 in 6 internet IPOs globally deliver returns above cost of capital; India's pattern mirrors this harsh reality.
- India's consolidation driven by networking (highways, air, banking), SaaS adoption, and formalization—not technology alone.
- Evaluate tech companies by capital efficiency, accounting clarity, stable leadership, and business optionality—not growth narrative.

## Open Question
If best tech businesses historically need minimal capital and 85% of BSE 500 companies underperform, why does investor enthusiasm for capital-intensive internet IPOs persist?

## India Angle
India's internet IPO boom reflects deeper economic consolidation—networking effects, formalization, and capital availability are reshaping which companies survive. The top 20 companies now capture 95% of profits (vs 30% in 2010), favoring tech-enabled businesses and creating a 'winner takes all' market structure specific to India's current transformation.
