## Core Argument
India's development progress measured in numbers masks continuing poverty visible only through direct human observation.

## Key Points
- Statistical poverty reduction metrics obscure ground realities of families living unsheltered on roadsides.
- Policymakers must practice direct observation and compassion—talking to real people, not trusting charts alone.
- India's constitutional democracy and diversity remain aspirational 75 years after independence despite economic gains.

## Open Question
How can India reconcile its genuine achievements in IT, space, and wealth creation with the persistent visibility of mass poverty that remains unresolved by conventional development metrics?

## India Angle
India's paradox: rapid wealth creation and unicorns coexist with persistent mass poverty; policymakers risk losing sight of ground reality through statistical abstraction rather than ground truth.
