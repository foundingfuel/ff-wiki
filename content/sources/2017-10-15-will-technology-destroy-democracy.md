## Core Argument
Technology is not democracy's destroyer; misaligned policy and human tribalism accelerate existing societal fragmentation.

## Key Points
- Job destruction fears from AI/automation are overstated; World Bank estimates <8% job elimination; India should fix current jobless growth first
- Social media amplifies but doesn't cause dissatisfaction; it hardens tribal walls, preventing dialogue across differences
- All powerful technologies require regulation; governments must understand tech use/misuse rather than pursue vendor-driven solutions

## Open Question
If social media is merely an accelerator of existing human tribalism rather than its cause, can regulation alone prevent democratic erosion, or must we address deeper psychological and cultural drivers of polarization?

## India Angle
India must address structural economic issues (logistics, trade policy) limiting job creation in traditional sectors before adopting Industry 4.0 strategies; social media's tribal fragmentation threatens India's pluralistic democracy and inclusive growth narrative
