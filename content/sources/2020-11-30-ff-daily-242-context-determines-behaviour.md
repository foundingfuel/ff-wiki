## Core Argument
Context and social norms drive behaviour more than individual character; leaders shape outcomes by designing environments.

## Key Points
- Descriptive social norms (what others do) influence behaviour more than injunctive ones (what's expected).
- Tony Hsieh built Zappos by deliberately creating a culture of connection, fun, and purpose over profit.
- Continuous self-critique and willingness to not have all answers enables authentic innovation and growth.

## Open Question
If context determines behaviour, how much individual accountability remains, and what happens when context itself is corrupted?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations must recognize how context shapes employee and customer behaviour; Tony Hsieh's culture-first approach offers lessons for Indian startups and enterprises scaling rapidly.
