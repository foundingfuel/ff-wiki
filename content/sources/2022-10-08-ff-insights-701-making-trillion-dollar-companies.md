## Core Argument
Trillion-dollar companies obsess over user feedback and continuous iteration, not charts or presentations.

## Key Points
- Andreessen built Mosaic by fanatically heeding user feedback from internet newsgroups and releasing persistent updates.
- Focus on running code and customer service, not presentations, separates billion-dollar from trillion-dollar companies.
- Indian edtech replicates exam-coaching model; true education requires wonder and questioning, not regimented success formulas.

## Open Question
Can India's edtech ecosystem shift from training-factory mentality to curiosity-driven learning while maintaining commercial viability?
