## Core Argument
Elite productivity comes from batching focused work, not working more hours; strategic intensity beats relentless availability.

## Key Points
- Adam Grant stacks teaching in fall, research in spring/summer to eliminate context-switching and maximize deep focus.
- High-quality work = time spent × intensity of focus; isolation periods and auto-responders enforce commitment to single tasks.
- Business leaders must balance stakeholder expectations on social issues; silence now carries greater reputational risk than taking stands.

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers enforce strict isolation and unavailability in cultures that equate responsiveness with commitment and reliability?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and academics can adopt Grant's batching method to compete globally; Indian business leaders face similar stakeholder pressures on social issues as Western counterparts but often lack frameworks for navigating them
