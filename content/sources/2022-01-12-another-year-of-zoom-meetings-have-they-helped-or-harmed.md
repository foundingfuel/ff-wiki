## Core Argument
Zoom meetings must shift from bonding/venting to collective problem-solving designed to produce actionable insights.

## Key Points
- Meetings without clear purpose become complaint sessions that bond people but produce no solutions.
- Effective coordination requires structured protocols: time limits, turn-taking, problem-focus over grandstanding.
- Systems thinking demands simultaneous development of three layers: operations, technology, and human capability.

## Open Question
How can remote meetings replicate the informal learning and feedback loops that happen in physical proximity during real work?

## India Angle
Indian organizations must adapt to permanent remote work, learn from Arun Maira's systems thinking approach to coordination, and build cultures that solve problems rather than just commiserate about them.
