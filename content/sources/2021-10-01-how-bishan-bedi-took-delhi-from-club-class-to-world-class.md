## Core Argument
Transformational leadership requires challenging existing systems, demanding excellence, and building culture through personal conviction despite institutional resistance.

## Key Points
- Bedi demanded intensive training, fitness standards, and match-simulation practice—radically different from Delhi's casual pre-existing culture.
- He insisted on autonomy from administrators, explicitly stating he wouldn't comply with directives that conflicted with his vision.
- He exposed underperformers and promoted merit-based selection, creating friction but fundamentally reshaping team culture and ambition.

## Open Question
How do leaders maintain institutional support while directly challenging administrators' control and traditional ways of operating, especially in India's hierarchical organizational contexts?

## India Angle
Demonstrates how a visionary leader transformed Delhi cricket from a mediocre ragtag team to a competitive force by introducing professional standards, challenging established hierarchies, and building organizational culture—a template for institutional transformation in India.
