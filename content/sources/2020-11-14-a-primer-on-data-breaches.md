## Core Argument
Most data breaches stem from human error and behavioral predictability, not sophisticated attacks.

## Key Points
- PII market value drives breach targeting; Indian unicorns with millions of users are high-value targets.
- Common causes: misconfigured cloud infrastructure, developer oversights, credential reuse, and social engineering exploits.
- Individual mitigation requires behavioral change: strong passwords, 2FA, password managers, and phishing awareness.

## Open Question
If most breaches are preventable through better practices and behavior change, why do enterprises and individuals continue making the same mistakes despite knowing the risks?

## India Angle
Indian startups handling millions of user records (BigBasket, Zomato, Paytm) face acute breach risks; weak enforcement of data protection standards and rapid cloud migration without adequate security governance creates systemic vulnerability for Indian digital economy.
