## Core Argument
Global supply chains and remote work are messier than technology promises; strategy requires navigating real-world friction.

## Key Points
- Globalization introduces compound deviations at each step—design, manufacturing, sourcing—creating friction Boeing and others underestimated.
- India joins IPEF to counter Chinese economic dominance while balancing deepening bilateral trade entanglements with China.
- Hybrid leadership development requires sensemaking, experimentation, and self-discovery embedded in daily work, not just formal training programs.

## Open Question
How can India pursue economic integration with geopolitical allies (US via IPEF) while managing deepening trade dependency on China, the very threat IPEF aims to counter?

## India Angle
India's strategic pivot away from China-dependent trade toward IPEF partnerships reflects a geopolitical recalibration; domestically, Indian organizations must adapt to globalization's friction and hybrid work leadership models.
