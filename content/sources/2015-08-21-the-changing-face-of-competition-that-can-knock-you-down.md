## Core Argument
Competition is no longer direct; invisible cross-industry and trend-based threats deliver knockout punches faster.

## Key Points
- Technology-powered cross-industry competitors (Apple, Uber, Amazon) bypass traditional competitive moats unexpectedly.
- Behavioral trends (health, ethics, sustainability) emerge as invisible competitors that established brands ignore at peril.
- Rumours and narratives spread faster than truth, creating new competitive threats companies cannot fight directly.

## Open Question
If competitor-focused strategy is futile against invisible threats, what strategic mindset or framework should leaders adopt instead?

## India Angle
Indian banks face M-Pesa-style disruption via payment banks; traditional retailers must compete with Amazon-like models; Indian telecom and financial services vulnerable to cross-industry tech competitors.
