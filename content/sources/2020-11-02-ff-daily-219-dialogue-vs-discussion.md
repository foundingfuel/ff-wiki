## Core Argument
Dialogue creates shared meaning; discussion wins points. Organizations need the former for innovation.

## Key Points
- Dialogue creates new meaning through genuine exchange; discussion is competitive point-batting.
- Covid-19 forces workplace restructuring—remote work normalizes hiring beyond geography and traditional constraints.
- Women disproportionately lost jobs pre-Covid; work-from-anywhere paradigm creates hiring opportunities based on skills, not location or marriage status.

## Open Question
If dialogue requires genuine openness and non-judgment, how can organizations embedded in competitive hierarchies and metrics-driven cultures actually shift from discussion to dialogue at scale?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces traditionally emphasize hierarchy and consensus; Bohm's dialogue framework offers a way to foster genuine co-creation rather than competitive point-scoring, crucial for navigating post-Covid flexibility. Women's disproportionate job losses and work-from-home opportunities create an urgent need for dialogue-based cultures where remote work is normalized.
