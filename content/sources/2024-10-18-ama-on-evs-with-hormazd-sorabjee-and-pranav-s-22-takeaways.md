## Core Argument
EV viability in India depends on home charging access and hybrid transition; infrastructure quality, not quantity, is the bottleneck.

## Key Points
- Home charging is critical for EV economics; public charging unreliability remains India's biggest adoption barrier
- Battery resale degradation and poor used-car values create customer friction despite superior driving experience
- Hybrids emerge as pragmatic transition solution for urban stop-start driving, especially with market leader Maruti driving adoption

## Open Question
If EVs deliver superior user experience and long-term savings, why do customers still hesitate—is it truly infrastructure, or deeper trust and convenience concerns?

## India Angle
India's EV adoption faces unique infrastructure gaps (unreliable public charging, home charging at scale), battery technology choices (LFP vs NMC), and hybrid alternatives gaining traction. Chinese EV leadership presents competitive pressure on domestic manufacturers.
