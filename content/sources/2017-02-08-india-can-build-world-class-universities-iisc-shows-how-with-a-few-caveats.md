## Core Argument
World-class universities emerge from meritocracy, researcher freedom, and selective institutional discipline, not visionary leadership.

## Key Points
- IISc succeeds through hiring best talent, granting research freedom, and maintaining strict meritocracy—not charismatic leadership.
- Indian institutions decay over time unlike Western peers; IISc defies this through consistent commitment to core principles.
- Scale gap remains: IISc's Rs 258 crore external funding pales against MIT labs generating $10M annually with 25+ spinoff companies.

## Open Question
How can IISc reduce government dependence and commercialize research without compromising the basic science focus that built its reputation?

## India Angle
India can build world-class research institutions by following IISc's model of merit-based hiring, researcher autonomy, and government support, but must balance this with commercialization pressures and scale challenges unique to India's institutional ecosystem.
