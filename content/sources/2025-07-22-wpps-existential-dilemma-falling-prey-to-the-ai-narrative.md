## Core Argument
Hiring a tech CEO to fix a culture-driven business signals market desperation, not genuine strategy.

## Key Points
- WPP's board lacks marketing expertise, making it vulnerable to tech-centric groupthink despite AI being a tool, not strategy.
- Culture and creative excellence drive marketing services; financial engineering and imposed tools historically fail in this sector.
- 75% market cap erosion suggests deeper problems than AI can solve; the appointment may signal the company is 'in play' for acquisition.

## Open Question
Can AI-led transformation work in a business fundamentally built on human creativity, relationships, and cultural autonomy, or does imposing tech solutions from the top merely accelerate fragmentation?

## India Angle
Indian marketing and advertising agencies face similar pressures to adopt AI narratives; WPP's struggles offer a cautionary tale about chasing technology over core competencies, relevant for India's growing ad-tech sector navigating between Silicon Valley hype and local client needs.
