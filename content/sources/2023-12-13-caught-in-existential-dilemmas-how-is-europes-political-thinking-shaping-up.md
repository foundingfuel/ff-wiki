## Core Argument
European populism rises as democratic institutions struggle to deliver hope amid poly-crisis, eroding faith in liberal democracy.

## Key Points
- Right-wing populism gains across Europe despite public preference for social-democratic solutions, driven by fear over hope.
- Multiple cascading crises—financial, pandemic, war, climate backsliding—have shaken confidence in 20th century democratic institutions.
- Aging Western European polity losing economic competitiveness faces risk of widespread despair unless democratic commitment revives.

## Open Question
If Europeans intellectually prefer social-democratic solutions but emotionally vote for right-wing populists, how can political parties bridge the gap between rational preference and emotional choice?

## India Angle
India observes Europe's populist rightward shift as a cautionary tale for managing democratic institutions under economic stress; relevant to India's own political polarization, institutional resilience, and balancing democratic participation with institutional capacity.
