## Core Argument
Outcome-based companies thrive by trusting employees' spare time; time-based billers breed distrust and talent exodus.

## Key Points
- IT services' 'time and materials' billing model creates distrust, leading to restrictive moonlighting policies that suffocate talent.
- Swiggy's outcomes-focused model allows spare-time work; Wipro's time-based model forbids it—fundamentally different business philosophies.
- Unreasonable notice periods and employment restrictions harm free labour markets, preventing startups from hiring and damaging industry mobility.

## Open Question
How can established IT services companies transition from time-billing dependency to outcome-based models without destabilizing their business model and client relationships?

## India Angle
Indian IT services industry's legacy model of time-based billing and restrictive employee contracts vs. emerging startup culture—critical for India's talent competitiveness as startups struggle to hire from established firms.
