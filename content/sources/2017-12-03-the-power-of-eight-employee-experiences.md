## Core Argument
Employee experiences must mirror customer experiences through one unified brand promise, not separate employer branding.

## Key Points
- Eight core employee experiences (purpose, autonomy, resources, capability, transparency, simplicity, straight talk, seamless resolution) drive engagement.
- Digital is a 'how', not a 'what'—design thinking and customer mindset matter more than technology alone.
- One brand promise for customers and employees prevents cognitive dissonance and builds authentic organizational culture.

## Open Question
How do organizations practically translate abstract brand values into consistent tangible experiences across diverse geographies, cultures, and hierarchies without oversimplifying or creating new silos?

## India Angle
Indian companies scaling across sectors (pharma, FMCG, manufacturing, banking) must align internal employee experiences with external brand promises to compete globally and retain talent in tight markets.
