## Core Argument
China's startup ecosystem is accelerating through government backing, returned talent, and efficient adaptation of proven models.

## Key Points
- Returned 'sea turtles' with Silicon Valley experience serve as knowledge bridges and cultural translators for Chinese startups.
- Great Firewall created protectionist advantage allowing rapid business model adaptation without US competition interference.
- Government legitimization of startups removes social stigma, enabling mainstream talent participation beyond early outliers.

## Open Question
Can lean startup methodology overcome structural creativity and political limitations, or does cultural change require more than operational efficiency?

## India Angle
India faces similar challenges to China—talent returning from abroad, government innovation incentives, weak IP enforcement, and need for efficient lean methodologies. Understanding China's leap-frog approach offers lessons for India's startup ecosystem acceleration.
