## Core Argument
Iran weaponizes economic disruption to offset military inferiority, turning global energy markets into a strategic battlefield.

## Key Points
- Iran exploits Strait of Hormuz control to coerce energy markets and impose sustained attrition on adversaries.
- Gulf oil production curtailed by 8+ million barrels daily; strategic reserves offer only temporary relief.
- Energy shocks cascade into inflation, supply-chain disruption, and global financial instability beyond military containment.

## Open Question
Can Western military dominance deter economic disruption strategies that operate outside traditional defense frameworks?

## India Angle
India heavily depends on Gulf oil imports and Strait of Hormuz shipping; energy price shocks directly impact inflation, currency, and fiscal stability. Supply disruptions threaten refineries and petrochemical sectors critical to Indian manufacturing and exports.
