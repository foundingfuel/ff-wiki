## Core Argument
Tech companies command premium valuations by building defensible moats around future profit streams, not current earnings.

## Key Points
- FANG and Chinese tech giants outvalue traditional oil, banking, retail giants despite lower current profits.
- Valuation art lies in predicting future profitability and moat sustainability, not just near-term earnings.
- Incumbents must become software-driven or face extinction; startups must understand new moat-building fundamentals.
- Tesla valued 25x higher than BMW despite 25x lower sales, reflecting investor bet on future dominance.

## Open Question
How do investors distinguish genuine future moats (defensible competitive advantages) from speculative bets on unproven business models, especially when valuations far exceed current financial reality?

## India Angle
Indian incumbents in automotive, retail, banking, and manufacturing face similar disruption risks; Indian startups must understand new valuation logic and moat-building to compete globally; Indian VCs need frameworks to evaluate tech companies beyond traditional metrics.
