## Core Argument
Purpose emerges from accepting life's inherent messiness while acting with full commitment, not results.

## Key Points
- Widespread sadness pandemic exists beyond social media façade despite lack of empirical evidence.
- Wisdom traditions offer reframing mechanisms: viewing time as gift, detaching from outcomes, embracing inconvenience as texture not obstacle.
- Purpose requires solitary courage, whole-hearted action, and acceptance that loose ends never fully tie up.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between accepting life's messiness as philosophical wisdom versus using it to rationalize inaction or avoidance of real systemic problems?
