## Core Argument
Indian startups fight platform monopolies through nationalism and coalitions, but lack the systemic rethinking China demonstrated.

## Key Points
- Google Play Store dominance in India faces challenge from startups, coalitions, and government CDAC initiative, but Google's integration with Android ecosystem makes displacement difficult.
- Tech platforms like Zomato, Uber exploit smaller partners—the real solution requires unbundling through open interoperable protocols, not just alternative platforms.
- Tech-human adaptation happens through constant feedback loops: workers adjust to remote tools, then platforms evolve features (Microsoft Teams virtual commute) to optimize human experience.

## Open Question
If open interoperable protocols are the answer to platform monopolies, why do Indian startups continue building alternative closed platforms rather than pushing for systemic architectural change?

## India Angle
India's app store wars reflect deeper platform power dynamics affecting Indian startups; government intervention through CDAC mirrors global policy tensions; contact tracing adoption delays show India faces similar tech-governance coordination challenges as developed nations.
