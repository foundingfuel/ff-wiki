## Core Argument
Future hegemony emerges through asymmetric cooperation, not absolute dominance—neither US nor China can act unilaterally.

## Key Points
- Dual-hegemon world requires complex interdependence across technology, supply chains, finance and security.
- US power to punish through sanctions weakened; China cannot impose absolute constraints without harming itself.
- Global capital flight and economic nationalism will constrain both hegemons' ability to enforce compliance.

## Open Question
If neither superpower can exercise traditional hegemonic control, what prevents destructive competition and fragmentation of global systems?
