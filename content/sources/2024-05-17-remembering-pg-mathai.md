## Core Argument
P.G. Mathai embodied print journalism's exacting standards through mentorship, perfectionism, and gentle accountability.

## Key Points
- Mathai practiced 'shoe-leather reporting'—demanding journalists meet enough sources before writing.
- He mentored through soft admonishment and masterclasses, never raising his voice despite high editorial standards.
- His generation treated written journalism as the highest craft form, with rewriters wielding immense power behind scenes.

## Open Question
How can print journalism's exacting standards and mentorship culture survive in an era of speed, digital metrics, and byline-driven incentives?

## India Angle
Reflects on Indian journalism's golden era of print excellence and editorial craftsmanship, particularly through the institutional memory of India Today and Business World—publications that shaped India's media landscape.
