## Core Argument
Mindful habit formation and self-compassion overcome fitness barriers during disruption.

## Key Points
- Praise incremental progress rather than penalise missed goals; reduces destructive shame cycles.
- Adapt fitness routines to constraints: golf replaces gym; home workouts complement outdoor movement.
- Mindfulness transforms resistance into curiosity—ask why you avoid exercise, not just enforce compliance.

## Open Question
How do you sustain intrinsic motivation for fitness when external structures (gyms, social accountability) disappear, without reverting to guilt-driven compliance?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating WFH fitness during COVID-19 lockdown; democratising fitness access (golf equipment affordability, public facilities) reflects India-specific resource constraints and innovation.
