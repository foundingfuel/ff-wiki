## Core Argument
Year-end anthology of seven stories examining founder psychology, execution risks, and organizational transformation.

## Key Points
- Regulatory compliance and founder ego clash when ambition outpaces governance understanding (Paytm case)
- Exceptional founders balance rational execution with 'good delusions,' sustained by marathon mindset over decade-long horizons
- Strategic overhauls risk alienating core identity; transformation requires grounding in existing strengths, not wholesale dismantling

## Open Question
How can founders maintain ambitious vision and speed while respecting regulatory guardrails and stakeholder relationships without sacrificing growth?
