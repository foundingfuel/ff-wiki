## Core Argument
Enterprises must balance execution excellence with deep questions about purpose, not treat ethical inquiry as distracting overhead.

## Key Points
- Powerful systems (AI, media, infrastructure) need human guidance on purpose or they optimize for wrong goals.
- Business has treated shareholder value as self-evident purpose, sidelining societal impact and democratic responsibility.
- India's scale of execution (Aadhaar, elections, elections) makes unexamined purpose questions particularly consequential.

## Open Question
How can organizations maintain execution speed and efficiency while genuinely deliberating on purpose without becoming paralyzed by endless ethical debate?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises—from media to infrastructure to tech—face pressure to optimize for immediate returns while neglecting broader societal purpose. This is especially acute in governance (elections, Aadhaar rollout) and media, where 'getting it done' often supersedes questions of who benefits and who is harmed.
