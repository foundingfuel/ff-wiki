## Core Argument
Central bank autonomy protects long-term economic stability against government's short-term electoral pressures.

## Key Points
- RBI governor resisted finance minister pressure to lower rates despite government prioritizing growth over inflation control.
- Democratic governments inherently favor short-term measures; apolitical central banks provide necessary systemic counterbalance for sustainability.
- Unresolved conflict between monetary and fiscal policy priorities reveals structural tension in India's governance architecture.

## Open Question
How can central banks maintain credibility and independence while remaining responsive to legitimate government concerns about growth without becoming captive to electoral cycles?

## India Angle
Central to India's institutional credibility: can RBI maintain independence from electoral pressures while government pursues growth? This tension defined India's 2012-13 monetary policy debates and remains critical for institutional strength.
