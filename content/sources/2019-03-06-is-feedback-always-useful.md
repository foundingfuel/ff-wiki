## Core Argument
Feedback on failure teaches nothing about excellence; studying success requires different patterns than studying dysfunction.

## Key Points
- Excellence and failure often share similar root causes, making reverse-engineering failure a flawed strategy
- Leaders must be 'AI bilingual'—combining technical fluency with cultural and ethical understanding for modern governance
- Systems need both critics and problem-solvers; critics expose flaws while pragmatists build solutions

## Open Question
How do organizations identify which feedback drives excellence versus which merely addresses dysfunction, when the characteristics of excellent and failed leaders often overlap?

## India Angle
Indian organizations and leaders often lack frameworks to distinguish between useful and harmful feedback; this applies to both government (Aadhaar debate) and private sector seeking to improve without studying excellence patterns.
