## Core Argument
Business storytelling delivers quantifiable ROI through increased memorability, engagement, and financial value creation.

## Key Points
- Storytelling creates measurable financial value: €17 per word in one case, 2,806% ROI in another experiment.
- Purpose-driven companies with narrative cultures outperform peers 6-15x in revenue and 12x in share price growth.
- Stories stick in memory far better than bullet points—demonstrated by IBM example where audience remembered stories but forgot facts.

## Open Question
How do you convince data-driven executives to invest in storytelling when the ROI metrics themselves are difficult to isolate and quantify in traditional financial terms?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and organizations often rely on data-driven, bullet-point presentations; this argues for storytelling as a measurable tool for engagement and memorability in Indian corporate settings where traditional hierarchy favors hard metrics.
