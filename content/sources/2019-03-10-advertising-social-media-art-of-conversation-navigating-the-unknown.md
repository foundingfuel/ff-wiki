## Core Argument
Social media outrage is becoming normalized; recalibrating conversation and listening is essential for society and brands.

## Key Points
- Social media replaces calm dialogue with cantankerous debate, especially in India's polarized election climate.
- Consumer brands must learn to navigate social media controversy beyond mass media era playbooks.
- The real problem isn't platforms—it's how we engage; we must recalibrate responses and listen across divides.

## Open Question
How can brands and institutions practice genuine listening and dialogue when algorithmic amplification and political polarization make reasoned conversation structurally difficult?

## India Angle
Indian social media discourse is becoming increasingly polarized ahead of elections, with brands caught in the crossfire; Indian leaders and institutions must recalibrate how they communicate and listen across political divides.
