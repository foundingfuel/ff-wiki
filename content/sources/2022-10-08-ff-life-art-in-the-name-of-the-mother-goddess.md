## Core Argument
Durga Puja is a living, evolving street art festival rivaling global institutions, driven by community creativity and emotional storytelling.

## Key Points
- Durga Puja pandals have evolved from religious structures to sophisticated art installations exploring complex themes and emotions.
- Kolkata's economic decline paradoxically preserved artistic merit as cultural currency, enabling creative experimentation without commercialism.
- Pandals build cumulative emotional narratives year-over-year, creating layered meaning for observant repeat visitors.

## Open Question
How can Durga Puja's artistic excellence be preserved and globally recognized without commodification that destroys its organic, community-rooted nature?

## India Angle
Durga Puja represents a unique Indian cultural phenomenon—a mass participatory art festival that rivals global contemporary art institutions, yet remains underrecognized internationally. Reflects how post-colonial cities like Kolkata sustain artistic excellence despite economic decline, offering a model of cultural resilience.
