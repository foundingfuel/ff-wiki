## Core Argument
Working with emotions means neither suppressing nor exploding them, but transforming afflictive patterns through skillful awareness.

## Key Points
- Emotions shouldn't be buried or explosively released; instead, allowed to naturally dissolve through mindful observation
- Fear and disbelief from others can fuel audacity and creative drive if reframed as motivation rather than obstacle
- Solving real problems requires curiosity from lived experience, willingness to learn continuously, and resilience against criticism

## Open Question
How do you sustain the practice of 'letting emotions dissolve' during prolonged crisis when mental distress accumulates faster than awareness can process it?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating WFH during pandemic must develop emotional resilience and convert fear into creative output—applicable across industries from tech to entertainment, where cultural pressures to succeed are intense
