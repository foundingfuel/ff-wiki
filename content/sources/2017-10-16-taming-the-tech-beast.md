## Core Argument
Technology's societal impact demands regulation via dialogue and reflection, not utopian faith or reactive controls.

## Key Points
- Tech companies possess unprecedented power to nudge behaviour; this requires regulatory oversight like nuclear/chemical industries.
- Shallow knowledge via digital platforms is more dangerous than ignorance because it masquerades as understanding.
- Ancient practices—dialogue, deliberation, deep listening—are essential technologies for navigating modern complexity responsibly.

## Open Question
If even Facebook doesn't fully understand its own systems, how can external regulators govern technology responsibly without stifling innovation?

## India Angle
India's rapid digitization trajectory requires urgent governance frameworks to harness growth while preventing tech-driven harms—job displacement, misinformation, democratic erosion—that advanced economies are only now grappling with.
