## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs thrive by navigating regulatory uncertainty; Aadhaar's ambiguous verdict tests their ability to adapt.

## Key Points
- All technologies face opposition from those invested in incumbent systems; this is predictable, not a sign of failure.
- Critical mass matters more than perfect regulatory clarity; investors bet on outcomes between extremes, not on certainty.
- Entrepreneurs succeed by treating uncertainty as baseline operating condition, not a temporary obstacle to be resolved.

## Open Question
If entrepreneurs can work around regulatory ambiguity through creative interpretation, does this undermine the rule of law or prove entrepreneurial resilience?

## India Angle
Aadhaar and India Stack represent foundational infrastructure for Indian entrepreneurs; regulatory ambiguity directly impacts venture capital investment decisions and startup viability in the Indian fintech/identity ecosystem.
