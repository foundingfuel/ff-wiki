## Core Argument
Digital transformation succeeds when employees discover personal strengths first, then align them with technology.

## Key Points
- Inside-out approach gives employees agency by connecting their unique strengths to digital transformation components.
- Fear of technology-driven job loss mirrors past 'Bangalored' anxieties; reframing tech as capability enhancer helps.
- True transformation requires looking inward (self-knowledge) and outward (market alignment) simultaneously, not just adopting tools.

## Open Question
If digital transformation is fundamentally about people and culture, not technology, why do most organisations still lead with technology implementation rather than employee capability mapping?

## India Angle
Indian organisations undergoing digital transformation must adopt inside-out approaches to retain talent and rebuild confidence post-outsourcing fears; relevant as India shifts from cost arbitrage to capability-driven models.
