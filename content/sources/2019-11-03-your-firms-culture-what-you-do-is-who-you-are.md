## Core Argument
Culture defines what companies are; daily decisions reveal values more than mission statements ever can.

## Key Points
- Culture emerges from everyday decisions—contract negotiation, peer feedback, work hours, product details—not corporate goals.
- Innovation requires cultures that tolerate failure and controversy; culture must precede tech transformation, not follow it.
- Ethical leaders must resist pressures to present truth misleadingly; culture binds strategy, implementation, and ethics together.

## Open Question
How do companies maintain integrity-driven cultures under investor pressure and competitive stress when compromise is technically legal and financially tempting?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises like Infosys and Tata face ethical dilemmas where culture determines whether leaders prioritize short-term metrics over integrity; culture-building is critical as Indian companies scale globally and face governance challenges.
