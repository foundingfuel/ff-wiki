## Core Argument
Trade architecture, not diplomacy alone, now shapes strategic autonomy and India must master geo-economics to navigate competing blocs.

## Key Points
- Global trading order fragmenting into overlapping rule systems by sector and technology, not along single fault lines.
- India's strategic space depends on being a credible, predictable alternative to China as manufacturing and sourcing platform.
- FTAs must be treated as geopolitical instruments creating preferential corridors and attracting supply chains escaping China dependence.

## Open Question
Can India maintain genuine diversification and hedging if major powers increasingly demand geopolitical alignment alongside trade relationships, making true non-alignment impossible?

## India Angle
India must leverage trade architecture and FTAs as strategic tools to maintain autonomy between US, China, and Russia while building competitive manufacturing capacity. Trade agreements are now geopolitical instruments, not just economic ones.
