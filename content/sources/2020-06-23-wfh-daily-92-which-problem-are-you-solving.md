## Core Argument
True leadership judgment emerges when ethical choices are embedded in complex business problems, not separated as moral questions.

## Key Points
- Novartis tested whether MBA candidates asked about patient welfare when solving a financial crisis, masking ethics as business strategy.
- Digital communication amplifies misunderstanding; positive and neutral emotions read as more negative in text-based platforms like Slack.
- Controlling attention requires deliberate friction—disabling notifications and setting boundaries—rather than relying on willpower.

## Open Question
How do organizations teach genuine ethical judgment when formal ethical training often produces performative answers rather than internalized values?

## India Angle
Indian business schools and leaders must teach decision-making in complex, real-world contexts where ethics emerge naturally through business trade-offs, not as separate moral questions—critical as India navigates rapid economic transitions.
