## Core Argument
Strategy, operations, and people management must align to reimagine organizations during disruption.

## Key Points
- Physical constraints removed; universities now design cross-disciplinary, geographically distributed courses impossible before.
- Public sector's deep development agenda outperforms private sector's quick-fire approach; mentor-coach beats hire-fire.
- Disconnecting strategy from operational realities and human capacity dooms execution despite good ideas.

## Open Question
How can high-performance private sector cultures adopt public sector's patient development mindset without sacrificing accountability and speed?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and public sector organizations can adopt Harvard's cross-disciplinary learning model and Arundhati Bhattacharya's people development philosophy—particularly relevant as India's corporate sector scales and must shift from transactional to transformational talent management.
