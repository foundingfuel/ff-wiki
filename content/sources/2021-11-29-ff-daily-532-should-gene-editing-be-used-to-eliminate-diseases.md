## Core Argument
Gene editing for disease elimination raises unresolved tensions between medical progress, human diversity, and societal accommodation.

## Key Points
- Eliminating diseases via gene editing may reduce human diversity and exclude perspectives valuable to society.
- Disabilities like autism present challenges partly from lack of accommodation, not the condition itself.
- Governments must decide if parental choice to use gene editing should be permitted despite potential societal costs.

## Open Question
How do we balance individual parental autonomy to use gene-editing technology against the collective social loss of human neurodiversity and alternative perspectives?

## India Angle
India faces unique bioethics challenges given high disease burden, large vulnerable populations, and emerging biotech capability. Gene editing policy decisions will shape how India balances public health gains against diversity, inclusion, and equitable access to such technologies.
