## Core Argument
Quality journalism requires curated consumption; India needs stronger watchdog institutions and deeper investigative reporting standards.

## Key Points
- Traditional outlets (WSJ, FT, Economist) evolve into digital-first platforms with personalized, premium experiences.
- Indian journalism lags in investigative depth and international editorial rigor; The Caravan shows what's possible.
- Fact-checking entities (AltNews, BoomLive) are critical to combat India's fake news factories but remain underfunded.

## Open Question
How can Indian newsrooms fund investigative journalism and global coverage standards when Indian readers resist premium pricing that international subscribers accept?

## India Angle
India lacks robust fact-checking infrastructure (AltNews, BoomLive) compared to global peers; Indian journalism struggles with research depth (The Caravan exception); mainstream Indian outlets underinvest in digital quality and international coverage standards.
