## Core Argument
Sustainable business success requires product excellence, revenue growth, and organizational change—not cost minimization.

## Key Points
- Superior product or experience beats cheaper alternatives across all sectors and geographies.
- Revenue growth focus supersedes cost reduction as primary business lever.
- Organizational capability to implement change is the third critical rule.

## Open Question
How do resource-constrained startups and emerging markets balance premium positioning with affordability demands and accessibility requirements?

## India Angle
Indian businesses often compete on cost; this challenges the race-to-the-bottom and argues for value creation and differentiation as sustainable strategies for Indian founders and corporate leaders.
