## Core Argument
Mastery requires embracing plateaus as part of progress, not obstacles to escape from.

## Key Points
- Society conditions us to chase outcomes; real mastery lives in the process and long plateaus between breakthroughs.
- Priya Ramani's defamation acquittal shifts India's legal standards on sexual harassment evidence, democratizing justice.
- Identifying non-obvious problems (schlep blindness) reveals startup opportunities most entrepreneurs overlook.

## Open Question
How do we culturally revalue process-oriented patience in a society obsessed with rapid outcomes and achievement metrics?

## India Angle
Indian workplace culture often prioritizes rapid advancement and external markers of success; understanding plateau phases is crucial for sustaining high performance in Indian startups and organizations navigating long-term growth.
