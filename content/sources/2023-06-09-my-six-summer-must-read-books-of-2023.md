## Core Argument
Summer reads explore how businesses, leaders, and nations navigate technology disruption, diversity, and relationship-driven success.

## Key Points
- China's 30-year model based on offshoring and low wages is obsolete; must innovate in AI and fintech to compete globally.
- AI's impact spans medicine to agriculture; understanding industry-specific disruption requires fusing sustainability, geopolitics, and regulation.
- Successful startups obsess over problems not solutions; legacy companies fail due to DNA and culture, not capability.

## Open Question
Technology enables unprecedented collaboration tools, yet human relationships and cross-functional trust remain organisational weak points—suggesting tools alone cannot solve culture and bias issues.

## India Angle
India positioned to leverage AI and tech transitions; startup ecosystem must think hardware superpower ambitions; women leadership insights critical for Indian organisations navigating post-pandemic work models.
