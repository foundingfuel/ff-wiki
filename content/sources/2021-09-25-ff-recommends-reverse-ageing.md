## Core Argument
Personal health and sustainability are interconnected; embrace frugality to reverse aging and reduce carbon footprint.

## Key Points
- Triple bottom line philosophy must extend to personal choices: health, society, environment—not just corporate strategy
- Practical diet and movement habits (50% vegetables, 25% protein, walks before meals) deliver energy and longevity benefits
- Supporting local ecosystems and growing food at home creates zero-carbon footprint while reconnecting with frugality

## Open Question
How do individuals make truly sustainable choices when the math is 'fuzzy'—e.g., AC for two hours vs. two-hour bus ride—if systemic carbon accounting remains unclear?
