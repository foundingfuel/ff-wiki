## Core Argument
Temples bridge physical and spiritual realms through architecture, ritual, and art that encode universal human truths.

## Key Points
- Temple architecture follows Agama Shastra—an elaborate science of harnessing energies between physical and spiritual worlds.
- Ritual symbolism (creation, adornment, dissolution) mirrors profound philosophical truths about impermanence and transformation.
- Ancient Indian art and design principles align with modern neuroscience discoveries about how humans perceive meaning and beauty.

## Open Question
How can temples function simultaneously as sacred spaces for spiritual experience and as vessels of codified knowledge and architectural science—without reducing one to the other?

## India Angle
Indian temples as repositories of civilizational knowledge, architecture, and spiritual wisdom; how India's ancient systems (Agama Shastra, temple design) encode understanding of energy, consciousness, and art that modern neuroscience is rediscovering.
