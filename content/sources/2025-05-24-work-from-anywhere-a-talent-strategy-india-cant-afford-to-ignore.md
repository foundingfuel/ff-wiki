## Core Argument
WFA is a talent strategy that decouples work from geography, reversing brain drain and creating development opportunities in India's smaller cities.

## Key Points
- WFA enables hiring beyond metro centers, allowing employees to live anywhere while maintaining productivity and performance.
- RTO mandates produce 10% attrition and reflect control biases rather than productivity concerns; 25-40% in-person engagement is optimal.
- Digital twins and remote operations technology extend WFA beyond desk jobs to manufacturing, healthcare, and industrial roles.

## Open Question
If WFA solves productivity, collaboration, and cost challenges, why are major corporations still mandating RTO—and what would genuinely shift their incentives?

## India Angle
WFA can reverse India's brain drain by enabling talent retention in Tier-2 towns and smaller cities, unlocking regional development and reducing metro concentration. India needs digital nomad visas and local infrastructure to compete globally for remote talent.
