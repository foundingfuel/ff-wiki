## Core Argument
Next-gen family business leaders must earn legitimacy through distinctive skills, not inheritance alone.

## Key Points
- Self-awareness through tools like Clifton StrengthsFinder enables clearer role identification in family ventures.
- Introducing new competencies (design research) creates value without threatening existing business; bridges innovation and legacy.
- Deliberate team-building with complementary strengths—especially non-family members—strengthens organisational culture over nepotism.

## Open Question
How can family businesses balance meritocratic hiring and assessment with the inherent emotional bonds and expectations that come with family ownership?

## India Angle
Family business succession in India requires deliberate skill-building and merit-based assessment; the article models how second-generation leaders can introduce new capabilities (design) while respecting institutional culture and parental entrepreneurial legacy.
