## Core Argument
Disruptive tech platforms face inevitable government scrutiny and regulation as societies grapple with unintended consequences.

## Key Points
- Social media bans during crises prevent misinformation spread but set dangerous precedents for authoritarian control.
- Plant-based and lab-grown meat represent early-stage food system disruption with decade-long regulatory and market implications.
- Facial recognition technology's risks to privacy and equity outweigh benefits, prompting global restrictions and ethical concerns.

## Open Question
How can governments balance legitimate safety/ethical concerns with preventing creeping authoritarianism and surveillance when deploying tech restrictions or regulation?

## India Angle
India is investing in lab-grown meat through CCMB and NRCM despite strong cultural and religious dimensions around food; also faces unique challenges with social media regulation and facial recognition deployment in a diverse, multi-lingual context with high misinformation risk.
