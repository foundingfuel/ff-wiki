## Core Argument
First-rate intelligence requires holding opposing ideas simultaneously while maintaining operational integrity and accountability.

## Key Points
- Hybrid work clarity—defining whether workforce or work-week is hybrid—directly impacts employee lifestyle choices and retention.
- Remote workers risk exclusion from promotions; leaders must rethink trust-building and culture beyond physical proximity.
- Balancing generosity with accountability (patient vs. stupid capital) is essential for cross-sector collaboration and moral leadership.

## Open Question
How can organizations maintain accountability and fairness in hybrid models while genuinely trusting remote workers for advancement and inclusion?

## India Angle
Indian leaders navigating hybrid work post-pandemic must balance operational clarity with cultural trust-building; the ICICI Lombard masterclass shows Indian enterprises grappling with remote work trust and accountability differently than Western counterparts.
