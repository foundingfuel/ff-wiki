## Core Argument
Organizations must institutionalize continuous learning to balance short-term execution with long-term innovation and relevance.

## Key Points
- Most firms excel at either operations or innovation, rarely both simultaneously over sustained periods.
- Disruptive change signals exist at business fringes long before mainstream adoption; curiosity must be cultivated systematically.
- Cultural change requires disciplined, continuous learning systems led by leaders, not episodic offsites or workshops.

## Open Question
How can leaders create psychological safety for experimentation and failure while maintaining accountability for short-term business results and quarterly performance?

## India Angle
Indian companies, especially traditional ones, struggle to balance operational efficiency with innovation; the article's emphasis on learning systems and cross-functional teams is critical for Indian enterprises to stay relevant amid rapid disruption.
