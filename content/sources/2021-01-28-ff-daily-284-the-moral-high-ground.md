## Core Argument
Occupying moral high ground requires offensive reputation management and deep alignment between stated values and actions.

## Key Points
- Moral conflicts operate like warfare—controlling narrative and exposing opponent hypocrisy are strategic imperatives.
- Corporate reputation depends on genuine values alignment, not surface-level messaging; comorbidities weaken fundamentals.
- Apologies require understanding intent versus harm; acknowledging both matters for genuine trust restoration.

## Open Question
How can organizations aggressively defend their moral position without becoming the hypocrite they're attacking?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders must navigate reputation and moral authority in a polarized information environment; Kumar Mangalam Birla's reflection on corporate citizenship and ethical leadership is directly relevant to how Indian conglomerates position themselves amid social scrutiny.
