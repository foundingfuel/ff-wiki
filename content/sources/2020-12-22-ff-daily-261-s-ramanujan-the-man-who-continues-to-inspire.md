## Core Argument
Ramanujan's achievement transformed how Indians perceived their intellectual capability and possibility.

## Key Points
- Ramanujan inspired subsequent Indian mathematicians by proving excellence possible despite scientific scarcity and hardship.
- His example broke psychological bonds of intellectual confinement for aspiring Indian students in the 1920s.
- Chandrasekhar and peers saw Ramanujan as proof that Indian circumstances need not limit global achievement.

## Open Question
How do we replicate Ramanujan's transformative impact on national ambition when institutional barriers and resource constraints persist in modern India?

## India Angle
Ramanujan's example broke intellectual confinement for Indian students in the 1920s; his legacy continues inspiring Indian mathematicians and proving that excellence emerges from resource-constrained environments—crucial for a nation building scientific ambition.
