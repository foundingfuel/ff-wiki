## Core Argument
Complex policy issues like Aadhaar demand nuanced discourse; short-form platforms prevent the qualifications needed to avoid polarisation.

## Key Points
- Twitter's character limit forces lossy compression, eliminating nuance and driving misunderstanding into rancour and extreme positions.
- Aadhaar's impact depends on five interdependent factors: technology, government capacity, business incentives, big ideas, and people—each morally ambiguous.
- India's courts provide crucial checks on government power, but weak state capacity and democratic accountability gaps remain structural vulnerabilities.

## Open Question
How can India strengthen government capacity to deliver public goods via systems like Aadhaar without enabling the misuse of centralised power that weak institutional safeguards cannot adequately constrain?

## India Angle
Examines how Aadhaar—India's digital identity system—requires nuanced understanding of government capacity, technology risks, and individual rights; reflects India's struggle to balance state capability with constitutional protections and democratic accountability.
