## Core Argument
Technology is neutral; outcomes depend on deliberate usage patterns and individual responsibility, not the tools themselves.

## Key Points
- Technology neutrality thesis: outcomes determined by usage intent and discipline, not tool nature
- Remote work requires individual agency and deliberate tech management since most organizations lack frameworks
- Practical digital minimalism: measure consumption, mute notifications, schedule engagement to reclaim focus and time

## Open Question
If technology is truly neutral and remote work is viable, why do organizations continue reverting to office-centric models despite decade-old evidence of effectiveness?

## India Angle
As Indian workplaces adopt remote and hybrid models post-pandemic, deliberate technology use becomes critical for productivity and well-being; management must shift from control-based oversight to enabling individual responsibility.
