## Core Argument
COVID-19 requires sustained test-match discipline, not T20 sprint mentality; India abandoned precautions prematurely.

## Key Points
- India treated COVID-19 as short-term crisis (T20) rather than sustained challenge, causing laxity in masks, crowds, enforcement.
- Political rallies and religious gatherings continued despite pandemic, signaling mixed messaging from leadership and governance failure.
- Myth of herd immunity spread among political class, industrialists, and parts of public health community, eroding vigilance.

## Open Question
How do governments maintain public discipline and precaution over long crises when initial success creates false confidence and competing political/economic pressures?

## India Angle
India's COVID-19 response demonstrates how policy laxity, premature declarations of success, and weak enforcement at multiple governance levels—individual, institutional, political—can derail public health outcomes. Relevant for understanding state capacity challenges in India's institutions.
