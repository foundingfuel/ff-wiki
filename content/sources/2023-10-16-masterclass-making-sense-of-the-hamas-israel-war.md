## Core Argument
Historical injustices and territorial disputes have created cycles of violence with no clear path to resolution.

## Key Points
- 1948-1967: Israel faced existential threats from Arab states; post-1967 occupation created systemic injustice and Palestinian displacement.
- Gaza blockade turned territory into prison; Hamas's 2006 democratic victory was rejected internationally, radicalizing resistance into organized terrorism.
- Regional actors (Arab states, Israel, Western powers) prioritized strategic interests over Palestinian self-determination, perpetuating conflict cycles.

## Open Question
How can lasting peace be achieved when decades of mutual recognition failures, structural injustices, and strategic interests of external actors have hardened positions on all sides?

## India Angle
India must navigate complex Middle East geopolitics carefully given its strategic interests, large diaspora in Gulf, and need to balance relationships with both Israel and Palestine without being drawn into proxy conflicts.
