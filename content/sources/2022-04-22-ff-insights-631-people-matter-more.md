## Core Argument
Organizations succeed when people feel safe, valued as humans, and aligned with clear purpose beyond profit.

## Key Points
- Pixar's response to Toy Story II crisis prioritized psychological safety over blame, embedding trust into culture
- Canva's $40B success came from clarity on constraints—knowing what NOT to do prevents intimidation and focus
- Digital-native operating models require integrated data architecture, accessible tools, and continuous capability-building across teams

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain accountability without weaponizing it, and who determines when mistakes reflect character flaws versus systemic gaps?

## India Angle
Indian companies scaling rapidly often replicate Silicon Valley's accountability-as-punishment culture; Pixar's model and Canva's clarity on brand positioning offer alternatives for Indian founders building high-trust, high-performance organizations during transformation.
