## Core Argument
Deep listening is a learnable skill requiring presence, breath awareness, and resistance to interruption.

## Key Points
- Listening is not innate; it can be developed through practice and conscious attention to breathing and presence.
- Suppressing urge to interrupt and correct allows others to express complete thoughts and builds psychological safety.
- Self-awareness and emotional regulation while listening prevents personal reactions from derailing genuine understanding.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically teach listening as a cultural norm when default human impulse is to interrupt and defend?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces, where hierarchical communication norms often discourage interruption and questioning, would benefit from reframing listening as a learnable leadership skill to improve team dynamics and retention.
