## Core Argument
India needs innovation metrics capturing affordability, inclusivity, sustainability—not just R&D intensity and patents.

## Key Points
- Bloomberg Index's tech-centric metrics misrepresent Indian innovation; accessibility and affordability are the true drivers.
- Aravind Eye Hospitals, Narayana Health, Jaipur Foot show transformative innovation unmeasured by global indices.
- Commercialisation and adoption—not invention—differentiate real innovation; grassroots inclusivity expands innovator base.

## Open Question
How do we track and incentivise grassroots, inclusive, sustainability-focused innovation when global benchmarks and startup enthusiasm reward tech-centric, scalable solutions?

## India Angle
India's innovation must be measured by accessibility, affordability, inclusivity and sustainability—not high-tech R&D intensity. Success lies in solving core problems in health, education, agriculture through grassroots innovation and commercialisation, not mimicking Western metrics.
