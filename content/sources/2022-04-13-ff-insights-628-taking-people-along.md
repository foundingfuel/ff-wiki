## Core Argument
Leaders must cycle between goal-focus and social-scanning to take people along while achieving results.

## Key Points
- Neural networks for goal-focus and social-scanning inhibit each other; successful leaders switch between modes within seconds.
- Post-pandemic work transitions require empathy, mentoring, and listening—not just directives—to build sustainable organizational performance.
- Companies experimenting with flexibility (four-day weeks, work-from-anywhere, sabbaticals) signal new possibilities that shape talent retention and culture.

## Open Question
How can leaders authentically balance genuine people-centricity with business performance metrics when these require competing neural modes and time investments?

## India Angle
Indian organizations transitioning post-pandemic face a critical choice: command-and-control leaders demanding office returns versus empathetic leaders cycling between goal-focus and people-focus. This reflects deeper cultural shifts in how Indian companies build engagement and retain talent amid global competition for workers.
