## Core Argument
India's sudden Covid-19 decline remains scientifically unexplained, prompting investigation into policy, climate, and demographic factors.

## Key Points
- India's Covid cases plummeted in Sept-Oct 2020 despite no vaccines, defying earlier projections of highest global cases.
- Mask mandates (13,000 fined in Mumbai), early lockdowns, and climate factors are investigated as potential causes.
- Contradictory evidence exists: warm-humid climates may reduce spread, but India's air pollution could exacerbate virus transmission.

## Open Question
What actually caused India's Covid decline—enforcement, climate, demography, or something unmeasured—remains unresolved, limiting ability to replicate success elsewhere.

## India Angle
India's dramatic, unexplained Covid-19 decline in Sept-Oct 2020 presents a puzzle for policymakers and scientists—understanding what worked (masks, lockdowns, climate, demographics) could inform future pandemic response and global health strategy.
