## Core Argument
Leaders need vision, authentic ambition-building, and execution ability; India's policy fails on all three fronts.

## Key Points
- Three leadership traits—articulating vision (Jobs), fostering authentic ambition (Campbell), executing strategic pivots (Grove)—are essential but rare.
- India's 25% youth unemployment stems from policy focus on big business and lack of scientific expertise in long-term problem design.
- Age should not limit entrepreneurial contribution; experienced founders like Ashok Soota prove veteran expertise drives innovation and job creation.

## Open Question
If visionary, authentic, execution-capable leaders are essential but scarce, how can systemic policy design compensate for this leadership scarcity to solve India's unemployment crisis?

## India Angle
India's youth unemployment crisis (25%) demands leaders with vision (Jobs), authentic ambition-building (Campbell), and execution capability (Grove). Yet India lacks both such leaders in scaling ventures and policy frameworks that support small businesses—the real job creators—over big business.
