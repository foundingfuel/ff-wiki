## Core Argument
Design thinking in HR enables organizations to simplify people practices and build agile, high-performing cultures.

## Key Points
- Indian businesses lack coherent people strategies despite economic pressures, unlike well-coordinated high-performing teams.
- Agile HR using design thinking simplifies processes and empowers people by reducing bureaucratic friction.
- Organizational transformation succeeds when rooted in people culture, not just digital technology adoption.

## Open Question
If HR departments must 'get out of the way' for agility, what is their strategic role in transformation?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs struggling with people practices amid economic slowdown must adopt agile HR and design thinking to compete globally, learning from high-performing systems like Indian cricket.
