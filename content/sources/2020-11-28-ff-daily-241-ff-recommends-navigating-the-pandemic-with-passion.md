## Core Argument
Pandemic catalyzed rediscovery of home cooking, digital learning, diverse expertise, and shifting gender roles in Indian society.

## Key Points
- Home cooking surge counters global decline, linking kitchen skills directly to health outcomes and generational continuity.
- Social media and smartphones are learning platforms, not just entertainment—discipline in communication yields real-world advantage.
- Fresh expertise perspectives (non-traditional backgrounds) improve inclusivity and audience engagement across sectors like sports and media.

## Open Question
How can behavioral shifts during crisis (men doing household work, home cooking adoption) become permanent cultural change once pandemic pressures ease?

## India Angle
Indian context across home cooking traditions, social media adoption by younger generations, sports beyond cricket, and gender dynamics in households—all accelerated by pandemic disruptions.
