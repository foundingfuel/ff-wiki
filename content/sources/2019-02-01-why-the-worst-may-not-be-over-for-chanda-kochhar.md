## Core Argument
Board's belated action against Kochhar signals governance progress, but risks remain unresolved across courts and US investors.

## Key Points
- ICICI Bank board terminated CEO and clawed back bonuses from 2009, unprecedented in Indian corporate history.
- Multiple threats loom: Kochhar's court challenge, possible US class action suit, CBI criminal conspiracy charges against board members.
- Board's March 2018 clean chit reversal exposed governance lapses and raised questions about timely shareholder protection.

## Open Question
Can decisive punishment after-the-fact truly restore governance credibility, or does the delayed action and subsequent legal challenges undermine the board's authority and shareholder trust?

## India Angle
Landmark moment for Indian corporate governance: first major clawback of CEO bonuses signals board accountability, but raises questions about whether private sector is held to different standards than public sector banking.
