## Core Argument
Deep listening transforms leadership decisions and builds organizational trust during turbulent transitions.

## Key Points
- Listening without judgment reduces defensiveness and enables collective problem-solving in conflicted teams.
- Leaders must understand complexity beyond technical lens; humanities perspective essential for holistic decisions.
- Listening skill directly impacts decision quality, employee engagement, and organizational resilience during change.

## Open Question
If listening requires stepping back from action and noise, how do leaders balance reflective listening with the pace of decision-making demanded in competitive markets?

## India Angle
Indian organizations transitioning from collaboration-dependent models (like Telco's partnership dissolution) need listening-based leadership to navigate change, build trust across hierarchies, and move beyond command-control structures prevalent in traditional Indian business culture.
