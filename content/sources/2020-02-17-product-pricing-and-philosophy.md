## Core Argument
Digital economy shifts power from consumers to platforms, eroding ownership and autonomy in ways meditation alone cannot solve.

## Key Points
- Food delivery platforms trapped in discount-driven competition, forcing partnerships with restaurants instead of premium pricing strategies.
- Digital products enable companies to remotely control features post-sale, fundamentally changing nature of ownership versus atoms-based goods.
- Meditation and mindfulness proposed as counterweight to tech's threats to mental autonomy, but structural tensions remain unresolved.

## Open Question
If digital platforms can unilaterally revoke features post-purchase, can meditation meaningfully protect consumer autonomy, or do we need regulatory intervention?
