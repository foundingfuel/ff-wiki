## Core Argument
How problems are framed fundamentally shapes decisions; India's WhatsApp crisis exemplifies this principle.

## Key Points
- Framing establishes default status quos that most people follow, creating $200M+ consequences.
- WhatsApp's privacy framing triggered mass user exodus and government intervention in India.
- Reframing problems multiple ways and examining how others frame them reduces decision errors.

## Open Question
If framing is so powerful and invisible, how can individuals reliably protect themselves from its distortions in high-stakes decisions?

## India Angle
Indian decision-makers in government and business must recognize how framing shapes policy outcomes (WhatsApp privacy case); India's delayed data protection legislation makes this urgently relevant for protecting 400M+ users.
