## Core Argument
Startups must hire on track-record and ethical fit, not credentials or compensation matching, amid talent market distortions.

## Key Points
- E-commerce funding surge created salary inflation, luring talent seeking quick compensation spikes over learning.
- Track-record, practical testing, and ethical screening matter more than degrees in predicting actual performance.
- Short-termism from accelerated growth culture affects both companies and candidates, eroding commitment and trust.

## Open Question
How can startups compete for talent when well-funded competitors use compensation as a lever, without compromising on values or sustainability?

## India Angle
Indian startups face acute talent acquisition challenges as e-commerce boom inflates salary expectations, forcing founders to rethink hiring beyond compensation to values and ethical conduct fit.
