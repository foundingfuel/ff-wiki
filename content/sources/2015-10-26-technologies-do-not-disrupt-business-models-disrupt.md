## Core Argument
Business model innovation, not technology alone, drives disruption; most large companies lack capability to execute it sustainably.

## Key Points
- 85% of CEOs identify innovation as top priority, but only 6% believe they have required capabilities to survive disruption.
- Innovation spans products, services, processes, and business models; large companies struggle because innovation is cross-functional and ownership is unclear.
- Successful innovators (Google, Amazon, Apple) use fundamentally different models; eight essentials and 120+ practices differentiate sustained innovators from one-off winners.

## Open Question
If innovation is cross-functional with no clear owner and no playbook exists, how can large organizations systematically build this capability at scale and sustain it over time?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs and large companies face the same capability gap as global peers—80% worry about business model disruption but only 6% feel prepared, making this a critical issue for India's corporate transformation and competitiveness.
