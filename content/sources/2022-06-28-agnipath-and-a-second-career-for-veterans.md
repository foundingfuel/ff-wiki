## Core Argument
Armed forces and India Inc must collaborate to bridge veteran employment gap or face massive waste of human capital.

## Key Points
- 60,000 defence personnel exit annually with barely a fraction finding proper second careers; Agnipath will worsen this crisis
- CCBMDO certification courses help bridge hierarchical to collaborative mindset gap, but lack corporate placement ecosystem
- Corporate world demands cost-consciousness, dissent, and persuasion skills absent from regimented military culture; courses alone insufficient

## Open Question
How can short-term certification courses fundamentally rewire military personnel's decision-making culture when most lack exposure to corporate organisations and companies lack hiring infrastructure for defence officers?

## India Angle
India's defence sector transitions 60,000+ personnel annually with minimal civilian job placement; Agnipath will compound this crisis with 46,000 Agniveers needing employment by 2026, requiring systemic solutions across government, armed forces, and private sector
