## Core Argument
AI breakthroughs require management discipline: alternating between optimization and experimentation modes.

## Key Points
- AlphaFold solved protein folding in days versus months by combining reinforcement learning with novel problem-specific approaches.
- DeepMind's 'strike mode' (optimize existing systems) and 'creative mode' (experiment freely) enable breakthrough innovation.
- Real-world problems differ fundamentally from games; solutions require adaptive strategies, not one-size-fits-all AI approaches.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain sustained innovation when shifting between optimization and exploration modes risks both efficiency and momentum?

## India Angle
India lacks equivalent AI research infrastructure like DeepMind; Ant Group-Paytm tensions highlight India-China tech friction and regulatory risks for Indian startups dependent on Chinese capital.
