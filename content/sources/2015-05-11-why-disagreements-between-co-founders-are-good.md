## Core Argument
Disagreements strengthen co-founder trust when managed professionally through clarity, communication and emotional intelligence.

## Key Points
- Disagreements are healthy; they surface alternate viewpoints and improve outcomes—not signs of broken trust.
- Written role clarity, quarterly results sharing, and 360 feedback reduce friction and build accountability among founders.
- Frequent small conversations between leaders prevent larger crises; personal support during adversity deepens trust bonds.

## Open Question
How do founders maintain 'issue-based' disagreements when emotions run high, especially in culturally-driven hierarchies where confrontation threatens social bonds?

## India Angle
Indian founders, often emotionally bonded before starting businesses, struggle to separate personal relationships from professional disagreement—a cultural tendency toward harmony that undermines organizational health and trust-building.
