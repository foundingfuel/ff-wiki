## Core Argument
South Africa can lead global AI governance through Ubuntu philosophy, moving from rule-taker to rule-shaper.

## Key Points
- Current AI models encode algorithmic bias—ChatGPT reflects racial stereotypes, requiring locally-trained models with diverse linguistic data.
- South Africa's moral credibility (apartheid dismantling, nuclear disarmament) positions it to champion ethical AI governance frameworks globally.
- Ubuntu philosophy—'I am because we are'—offers counterweight to techno-dystopia by anchoring AI ethics in shared human responsibility.

## Open Question
South Africa articulates a moral vision for AI governance, but lacks enforcement mechanisms—how can Ubuntu principles translate into binding international AI standards when powerful nations prioritize autonomy over collective governance?

## India Angle
India is developing local language AI models like South Africa—both nations must shape global AI governance rather than merely adopt Western frameworks. India's G20 presidency (2023) and BRICS membership position it similarly to influence AI ethics standards and prevent algorithmic bias against Global South populations.
