## Core Argument
Listed Indian companies must face investor scrutiny equivalent to Silicon Valley startups for market integrity.

## Key Points
- Uber's IPO exposed how unicorns avoid answering critical unit economics questions investors deserve.
- Tata Steel and ITC's boards concealed material information—merger troubles, succession crises—from public shareholders.
- Indian listed entities have fiduciary obligations to disclose material facts; governance standards must match accountability.

## Open Question
How can Indian regulators enforce disclosure standards without stifling business agility, especially in transition periods like leadership succession?

## India Angle
Indian listed companies like Tata Steel and ITC fail transparency standards that Silicon Valley unicorns face; Indian boards avoid shareholder scrutiny despite fiduciary duties, revealing governance gaps in India Inc.
