## Core Argument
Doubt enables learning and progress; certainty creates stagnation and erodes trust in institutions.

## Key Points
- Feynman argues societies overvalue people with answers; real wisdom admits uncertainty and lack of knowledge.
- Mumbai Police brand success stems from avoiding preachy certainty, instead using creative engagement and flexibility.
- VUCA environments reward clarity of direction paired with flexibility in execution, not rigid hierarchical control.

## Open Question
How do leaders communicate vision and drive decisions while genuinely embracing doubt without appearing indecisive or losing stakeholder confidence?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and policymakers often default to false certainty in governance and business; embracing doubt as Feynman advocates could improve decision-making quality and public trust in institutions like Mumbai Police demonstrates.
