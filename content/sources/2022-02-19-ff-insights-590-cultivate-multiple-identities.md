## Core Argument
Multiple identities insulate against job loss while boosting performance; skills matter but degrees persist despite claims otherwise.

## Key Points
- Identity diversification beyond work creates resilience when career fails or transitions occur unexpectedly.
- Skills-based hiring rhetoric doesn't match practice; Oracle, Intel, HP maintain 90%+ degree requirements despite policy shifts.
- Deliberate action required—identifying as parent/artist meaningless without time investment and behavioral alignment.

## Open Question
If building multiple identities requires time and money investments, how do individuals earning subsistence wages in high-cost-of-living contexts actually create this plurality without sacrificing financial security?

## India Angle
Indian professionals face acute identity-work collision given intense competitive pressure and credential-obsession; multiple identity cultivation could build resilience in volatile job markets and help navigate India's skills gap where degrees remain gatekeepers despite hiring promises.
