## Core Argument
Media's moral failure and algorithmic echo chambers undermine democratic discourse; institutional leadership matters for change.

## Key Points
- Indian media degraded into gladiatorial contests driven by profit motives and algorithms, weakening democratic discourse
- Indian millennials differ fundamentally from Western peers; aspirations beyond urban middle class remain understudied and diverse
- Leaders across media, business, and politics must prioritize societal needs over power and profit to restore institutional trust

## Open Question
How can individual conscience-awakening among institutional leaders translate to systemic media reform when economic incentives and algorithms operate at scale?
