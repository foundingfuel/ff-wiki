## Core Argument
VC-backed tech startups dominate narrative; proven entrepreneurs building profitable businesses struggle for capital.

## Key Points
- Ecosystem selects for credentials and growth-at-all-costs, not business fundamentals or profitability.
- Pre-1990s Indian entrepreneurs like Ambani and Mittal built scale through frugality and hard work, not venture funding.
- Modern VCs lack entrepreneurial experience and apply homogeneous templates, creating a class-based divide in access to capital.

## Open Question
If venture capital validation isn't a true measure of business viability, what structural reforms would redirect capital to proven but unglamorous businesses without abandoning innovation incentives?

## India Angle
Indian venture ecosystem favors elite pedigree (IIT/IIM/Ivy League) over proven business execution; excludes profitable bootstrapped businesses and non-tech sectors that built India's foundational wealth.
