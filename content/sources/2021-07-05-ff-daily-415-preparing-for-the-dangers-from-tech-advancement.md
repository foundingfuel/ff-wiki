## Core Argument
Technology advancement increases systemic vulnerability; diverse teams and transparency are rare but essential safeguards.

## Key Points
- Complex technological systems (nuclear, financial, AI) amplify failure consequences exponentially, not just efficiency gains.
- Social media's algorithmic architecture makes misinformation spread faster than correction, destabilizing trust systems.
- Organizations ignore sceptics, lack diversity, and celebrate intuition despite evidence that these increase catastrophic risk.

## Open Question
We know structured tools, diverse teams, and transparency reduce system failure risk, yet institutions continue homogeneous decision-making—why does institutional behaviour resist evidence?

## India Angle
India faces acute vulnerabilities in complex systems—financial infrastructure, healthcare, food security—while social media amplifies misinformation at scale in a largely rural, literacy-diverse population. Lessons from Amartya Sen's famine research show consequences of system failures without proper information flows.
