## Core Argument
Indian ecommerce and tech sectors face dual challenges: domestic market maturation and geopolitical fragmentation.

## Key Points
- Indian ecommerce platforms experimenting with different festive season strategies; growth from tier-2/3 cities remains uncertain post-pandemic.
- US-China tech war creates strategic implications for India's digital independence and technology alignment choices.
- Successful disruptive tech investment requires diversity, open collaboration, and crowdsourcing rather than insular research.

## Open Question
How can India build indigenous disruptive tech capability and investment expertise while geopolitical pressures force tech ecosystem fragmentation?
