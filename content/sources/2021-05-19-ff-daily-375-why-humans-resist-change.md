## Core Argument
Humans resist change because it requires surrendering identity, making transformation harder than learning new skills.

## Key Points
- Identity loss during change creates unconscious resistance stronger than technical learning challenges.
- NFTs and blockchain could democratize art and creative economy in India if adoption barriers overcome.
- Middle class financial distress hidden by appearances; 20M workers withdrew retirement savings during pandemic.

## Open Question
If technology like NFTs can democratize creative opportunities in India, why does cultural resistance to identity transformation prevent individuals and institutions from adopting such changes at scale?

## India Angle
Indian middle class facing hidden distress post-pandemic; emerging technologies like NFTs could democratize opportunities but resistance to change remains a cultural barrier; policy must address dropout rates and retirement savings depletion among organized sector workers.
