## Core Argument
Tech startups' 'win at all costs' scaling model creates monopolies but masks unsustainable economics and shirks social responsibility.

## Key Points
- Scale-fast approach prioritizes user growth over profitability, creating venture-dependent companies without viable business models.
- 2018 data: 83% of IPO companies made no profits, raising questions about economic merit of this strategy.
- Winners bear disproportionate responsibility due to scale, yet the model incentivizes ruthless, accountable-to-nobody behavior.

## Open Question
How can founders balance the legitimate need for rapid scale and market capture with building sustainable, responsible businesses that don't exploit the system?

## India Angle
Indian startups increasingly adopt Silicon Valley's 'scale-at-all-costs' playbook without questioning whether this model suits India's market realities, regulatory environment, or social responsibility expectations. The tension between growth velocity and sustainable business models is particularly acute for Indian founders navigating capital scarcity and diverse stakeholder concerns.
