## Core Argument
US-Israel-Iran war reshapes Middle East energy architecture, fracturing Gulf security and forcing India to recalibrate energy, trade and geopolitical strategies.

## Key Points
- Hormuz interdiction threatens 20% of global oil and substantial LNG; Qatar's Ras Laffan shutdown alone impacts a fifth of global LNG supply.
- Gulf states shift from managed Iran rivalry to reluctant frontline participants; regional security triangle fractured, eroding their economic credibility.
- India must navigate energy security, diaspora protection, remittance stability and Gulf state relationships amid sustained regional disruption without clear endgame.

## Open Question
How long can Gulf states maintain calibrated deterrence and economic stability if projectiles continue striking infrastructure, or will mounting costs force them toward direct confrontation?

## India Angle
India faces critical energy security risks from Hormuz disruptions, LNG supply shocks affecting inflation and fiscal balance; must navigate diplomatic neutrality while protecting diaspora, remittance flows, and Gulf trade relationships amid US-Iran-Israel escalation.
