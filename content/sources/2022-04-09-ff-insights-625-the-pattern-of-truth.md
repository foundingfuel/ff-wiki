## Core Argument
Building robust truth requires cumulative evidence networks, not single findings—lesson applies to business strategy.

## Key Points
- Scientific validity demands replication, literature review, meta-analysis, and nomological networks of evidence.
- Tata's super app integrates learnings from Big Basket's dark store model across group entities for efficient supply chains.
- Solo capitalists represent emerging funding alternative with wealth parity to traditional VCs and PE firms.

## Open Question
How do conglomerates balance leveraging cumulative learnings across units with maintaining agility and avoiding bureaucratic overhead in fast-moving e-commerce?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates like Tata are experimenting with super-app integration and dark store logistics, while India's startup ecosystem is seeing emergence of solo capitalists as alternative funding models—both represent distinctly Indian approaches to scaling and capital deployment.
