## Core Argument
Personal branding is now essential for career success; it's the intersection of what you say and what others say about you.

## Key Points
- Build personal brand across four levels: education, professional work, industry involvement, and recognition; authenticity and consistency matter more than paid awards.
- Social media democratizes personal branding but requires discipline, clear principles, and avoiding self-promotion, sycophancy, and divisive topics to maintain credibility.
- Visual, regular posts aligned with a coherent narrative build reputation; negative comments are acceptable, but stay bounded from corporate mouthpiece role.

## Open Question
How do professionals maintain authentic personal brands while managing the pressure to perform and please stakeholders across multiple social platforms simultaneously?

## India Angle
Indian professionals traditionally relied on institutional credentials and media relationships for reputation; now must navigate democratic social media where personal brand is co-created with audiences, requiring new skills for India's competitive talent markets.
