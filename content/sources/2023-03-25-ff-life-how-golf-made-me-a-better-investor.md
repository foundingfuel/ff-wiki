## Core Argument
Golf teaches risk management, routine discipline, and adaptive strategy—skills that transfer directly to better investing behavior.

## Key Points
- Master fundamentals early through coaching, not shortcuts, to compound over decades of investing.
- Pre-shot routines calm the mind and prevent emotional decisions during volatile market moments.
- Adapt strategy based on conditions: be defensive most days, aggressive only when opportunity emerges against consensus.

## Open Question
If golf teaches defensive discipline and laying-up, how do investors balance safety with the need to capitalize on rare, time-sensitive opportunities without overthinking?

## India Angle
Indian investors often lack formal financial education and rely on tips; this article advocates for systematic thinking and behavioral discipline—critical for a market where retail participation is growing but financial literacy remains low.
