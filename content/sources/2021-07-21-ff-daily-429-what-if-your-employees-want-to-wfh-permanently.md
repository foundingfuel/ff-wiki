## Core Argument
Employees increasingly demand permanent remote work; leaders must balance flexibility with organizational cohesion.

## Key Points
- Tech giants like Google and Apple face employee backlash over hybrid-only policies despite remote work success.
- Perfectionism undermines performance and mental health; excellence through iteration requires embracing mistakes and learning.
- Post-pandemic workforce expects autonomy over location; one-size-fits-all policies risk talent attrition and reduced wellbeing.

## Open Question
How do leaders maintain organizational culture, collaboration, and innovation when employees reject office presence entirely, yet research suggests some co-location benefits exist?

## India Angle
Indian tech and knowledge companies will face similar WFH demands from employees; requires rethinking office-centric culture and talent retention strategies in competitive global talent markets.
