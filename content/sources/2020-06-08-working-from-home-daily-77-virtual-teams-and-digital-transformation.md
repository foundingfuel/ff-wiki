## Core Argument
Effective crisis response requires strong institutions and state capacity, not just speed; India must build these carefully.

## Key Points
- India's 'jald baazi' culture of quick fixes masks weak state capacity; sustainable solutions need time and institutional strength.
- Ping An Bank shifted from 'managing with experience' to 'managing with data', flattening hierarchy while scaling front-line teams.
- Individual courage and freedom to speak up (Gayla Benefield example) matters as much as institutional systems in driving change.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need for speed in crisis response with the need to build institutional capacity and democratic accountability that takes time to develop?

## India Angle
India's state capacity gaps exposed by COVID-19; need to balance speed (jald baazi culture) with institutional strength; risk of centralizing too much power in government without robust checks
