## Core Argument
Physical adversity and discomfort catalyze personal transformation through persistence and reframing.

## Key Points
- Guide's mentorship—'delete that thought'—models how belief systems shape capability and resilience.
- First-day despair is universal; progress requires ignoring comparisons and maintaining personal pace.
- Sensory presence amid suffering (forest scents, framed moment) reveals meaning beyond comfort-seeking.

## Open Question
Why do we voluntarily inflict hardship on ourselves, and does the retrospective meaning-making justify the suffering in the moment?
