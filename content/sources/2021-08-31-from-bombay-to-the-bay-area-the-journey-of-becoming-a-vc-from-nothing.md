## Core Argument
Network strategically, embrace risk early, and learn constantly to build credibility without elite credentials.

## Key Points
- Keep stakeholders informed so they can help you effectively; venture capital is fundamentally about relationships and being prepared.
- Underrepresented founders face systemic bias; thoughtfulness penalized while overconfidence rewarded in fundraising.
- Take calculated risks early, listen to gut instinct combined with data, diversify learning sources across operators and students.

## Open Question
How can underrepresented founders succeed in a system that rewards overconfidence and penalizes thoughtfulness—without compromising values or authenticity?

## India Angle
Indian founders and entrepreneurs can learn from Shruti's unconventional path—building networks strategically, dealing with bias in fundraising, and accessing global VC ecosystem without traditional pedigree. Relevant for Indian women founders seeking capital and breaking into venture ecosystems.
