## Core Argument
Innovation thrives when entrepreneurs focus relentlessly on solving specific market needs with clarity.

## Key Points
- Digital players unbundling traditional banking by hiding bank identity behind payment interfaces.
- MNCs like GE and Cisco built India R&D centers yielding cost savings and market insights.
- Focused entrepreneurship requires clarity of purpose—evident in Branson's Virgin ventures and Bansal's AppDynamics.

## Open Question
How can traditional institutions defend against digital disruption when unbundling makes their core identity commoditized and invisible to customers?

India's transition from cost-arbitrage back office to innovation hub; digital payment disruption challenging traditional banking; MNCs building R&D centers in India for frugal innovation applicable globally
