## Core Argument
Connected car technology forces automotive industry to shift from selling vehicles to building ongoing customer relationships.

## Key Points
- India has 1.4M annual road accidents; OEMs resist safety investments without regulatory mandate, missing moral and market case.
- Connected vehicles shift from electrical/mechanical to software/sensors, enabling services worth $422B by 2022 (88% of value).
- Incumbent carmakers face existential threat if they don't adopt rapid innovation cycles; tech/mobile firms better positioned than traditional OEMs.

## Open Question
How can Indian carmakers balance immediate profitability pressures with long-term survival needs in a software-services-driven automotive future?

## India Angle
India's automotive industry faces dual pressures: worst-in-world road safety record with regulatory gaps, and disruption from connected car technology. Indian OEMs risk being sidelined if they don't shift from transactional, regulation-driven models to innovation-led, service-based ones.
