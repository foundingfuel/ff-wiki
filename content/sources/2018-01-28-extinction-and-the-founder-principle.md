## Core Argument
Suppressing diverse perspectives in discourse mirrors genetic extinction; India's institutions must embrace dissent to survive and evolve.

## Key Points
- The Founder Effect shows how isolated populations lose genetic diversity, leading to extinction through inbreeding.
- Contemporary discourse exhibits intellectual incest: lack of diversity, no room for challenge or nuance.
- Institutions must prioritize velocity and evolution, not just survival; diversity of thought is non-negotiable for this.

## Open Question
How can institutions operationalize tolerance for dissent and nuance when social and political pressures push toward polarization and conformity?

## India Angle
Indian institutions—business schools, regulatory bodies, public discourse—risk intellectual incest when they suppress dissent and diversity of thought, essential for India's institutional evolution and adaptive capacity.
