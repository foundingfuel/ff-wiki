## Core Argument
India's greatest institutions emerged from humble beginnings by combining resourcefulness, vision, and community integration.

## Key Points
- Connemara Library became foundational to India's library network, pioneering public access and shaping institutional norms after independence.
- Sriharikota transformed from remote island with tribal settlements into space hub through grassroots infrastructure building and problem-solving.
- These places succeeded by embedding themselves in local communities rather than imposing top-down change.

## Open Question
How do we preserve the resourcefulness and embedded-ness of these foundational institutions as they scale, digitize, and modernize for contemporary needs?

## India Angle
These sites exemplify India's post-independence institutional building—from libraries establishing national knowledge infrastructure to space programs emerging from resource constraints. They show how Indian institutions adapted colonial legacies and built capacity despite physical and organizational limitations.
