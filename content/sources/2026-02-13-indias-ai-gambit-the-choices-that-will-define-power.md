## Core Argument
India must define what kind of AI power it wants to become, not just adopt technology others build.

## Key Points
- Strategic autonomy requires balancing openness with control; isolation weakens AI development.
- India risks falling behind in AI-driven scientific discovery; scale and OEM positioning matter more than perfection.
- Sectoral regulators must coordinate through whole-of-government approach; centralized strategy with decentralized execution prevents fragmentation.

## Open Question
How can India achieve strategic autonomy in AI while remaining open to global collaboration without losing control over critical capabilities?

## India Angle
India must choose between US-China AI models while building strategic autonomy; decisions on governance structure, foundational model adoption, and AI-as-OEM positioning will determine India's geopolitical leverage and institutional capacity in the next decade.
