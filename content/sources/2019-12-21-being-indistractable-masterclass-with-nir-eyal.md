## Core Argument
Distraction is internal; mastering triggers and scheduling time for values drives productivity.

## Key Points
- Distraction stems from internal discomfort avoidance, not technology; digital detox doesn't work without addressing root causes.
- Traction (intentional action) opposes distraction; schedule time for values, not just output tasks.
- Workplace distraction roots in cultural dysfunction; transparency through joint scheduling with teams rebuilds priorities.

## Open Question
If willpower is malleable and self-belief shapes behavior, how do individuals overcome deeply ingrained cultural patterns of reactivity in hierarchical organizations?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and managers increasingly struggle with distraction in high-pressure, always-on work cultures; understanding internal triggers and time-blocking as cultural dysfunction fixes is directly applicable to Indian workplace transformation.
