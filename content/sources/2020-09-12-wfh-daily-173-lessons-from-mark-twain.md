## Core Argument
Crisis forces Indian businesses to survive, revive, and reimagine; social innovation solves India's structural problems.

## Key Points
- Post-Covid recovery requires dual focus: operational survival and strategic reimagining of market opportunities.
- Garv Toilets solves India's sanitation crisis by combining smart sensors with soft innovations like modularity and vandalism-resistant materials.
- Scaling social innovation in India demands solving systemic maintenance, funding, and operational challenges beyond technology alone.

## Open Question
How can social enterprises like Garv achieve scale when hardware costs 25% more than traditional solutions and CSR funding is drying up?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises navigating post-Covid recovery across sectors (industrial, service, agriculture); social innovation addressing India's sanitation challenges and public health crises through technology and systemic thinking.
