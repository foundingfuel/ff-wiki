## Core Argument
Solving complex problems requires examining what's missing from data, not just what's visible.

## Key Points
- Wald's insight: bullet holes in returning planes revealed safe zones, not vulnerable ones—invisible failures hold crucial lessons.
- Indian migrant workers face erratic employment post-lockdown; 60% surveyed were jobless, exposing structural labour market fragility.
- Toxic online discourse escalates when emotional state and speed override empathy; deliberate pausing prevents damage.

## Open Question
How do institutions systematically correct for survivorship bias when visible data is by definition incomplete, and what governance mechanisms ensure this happens consistently in India's labour and policy sectors?

## India Angle
India's labour crisis post-pandemic mirrors larger systemic blindness—policymakers see visible unemployment but miss structural vulnerabilities in worker welfare, migrant protections, and supply chain resilience that determine long-term economic stability.
