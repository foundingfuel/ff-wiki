## Core Argument
India's crisis response success hinges on digitizing backend government systems, not just frontends.

## Key Points
- Agra Smart City's integrated command centre became COVID war room by combining private consultants, digital tools, and district administration.
- Most government workers cannot work remotely due to paper-based processes, offline systems, and lack of technical infrastructure.
- Crisis relies on individual excellence of committed officers; institutional capability through digital backend transformation remains missing.
- 50 lakh sanitation, 19 lakh police, 26.7 lakh healthcare workers do frontline work while 31+ lakh government employees remain offline.

## Open Question
How can India scale the Agra Model's success to all districts when systemic digital infrastructure remains absent across most government departments, and individual heroism cannot substitute for institutional capability?

## India Angle
India's public sector digitalization gap exposed during COVID-19; most government workers lack remote access capability. Success in Agra and Andhra Pradesh shows coordinated leadership + digital tools can work, but systemic backend transformation needed across 31+ lakh central government employees to prevent service collapse and economic slowdown.
