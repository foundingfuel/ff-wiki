## Core Argument
Replace entrenched beliefs through powerful stories, not facts; logic cannot override illogical belief formation.

## Key Points
- Belief perseverance causes people to reject contradictory evidence and cling to existing worldviews.
- Confirmation bias makes facts ineffective; listeners filter data to reinforce existing beliefs.
- Influence stories create ownership and bypass resistance by using narrative structure rather than logical argument.

## Open Question
How do we validate whether stories produce lasting behavioural change, or merely temporary agreement without genuine belief transformation?

## India Angle
Indian organisations undergoing transformation often face entrenched resistance rooted in past experiences and cultural narratives. Using influence stories rather than data-driven approaches can help Indian leaders navigate change management across hierarchical structures and diverse belief systems.
