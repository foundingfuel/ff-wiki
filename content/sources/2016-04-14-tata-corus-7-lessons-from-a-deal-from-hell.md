## Core Argument
Prestige-driven acquisitions without strategic consensus, proper due diligence, and integration planning destroy shareholder value.

## Key Points
- Lack of internal strategic consensus led to timing errors; Corus was available 10x cheaper years earlier.
- Hands-off integration approach (termed 'Asian style') masked deep operational and cultural misalignments until losses spiraled.
- Failure to recognize industry power shift toward raw material security exposed Corus's structural vulnerabilities too late.

## Open Question
How do Indian companies balance 'humane, long-term thinking' integration approaches with the harsh operational realities and competitive pressures of declining commodity-dependent assets?

## India Angle
Tata Steel's Corus acquisition reveals critical gaps in Indian companies' global M&A playbooks—timing, due diligence, cultural integration, and understanding shifting power dynamics in commodity industries. A cautionary tale for India's aspiring global acquirers.
