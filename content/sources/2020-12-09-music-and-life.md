## Core Argument
Music transcends entertainment to become a vehicle for meaning, connection, and catalysing personal and social transformation.

## Key Points
- Music with purpose—protest, activism, climate awareness—shapes identity and worldview more than passive consumption.
- Personal connections forged through music (love, empathy, memory) create umbilical cords between people and their values.
- A chance encounter with art (video, song, performance) can redirect entire life trajectories toward meaningful work.

## Open Question
How do we systematically cultivate spaces where music and art can trigger transformative realisation, or does transformation require the randomness of accidental discovery?

## India Angle
Indian artists and creators using music as a tool for social meaning, activism, and personal transformation—from protest traditions to climate awareness—reflects how Indian culture intertwines art with purpose.
