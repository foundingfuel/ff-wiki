## Core Argument
Full engagement paradoxically creates mental space; identity shifts matter more than location in work transitions.

## Key Points
- Paradox: busyness from full engagement reduces time-constraint perception and boosts capability feelings.
- Work-from-home is identity change, not location change; mandatory office return triggers psychological resistance.
- Helping others and 'tangential' activities fuel energy and accomplishment, creating positive feedback loops.

## Open Question
If full engagement creates more mental space, why do mandatory return-to-office policies trigger U-shaped adjustment curves rather than renewed engagement?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers navigating hybrid work transitions and the psychological identity shifts from pandemic-induced remote work—critical for Indian companies managing large-scale return-to-office policies and talent retention.
