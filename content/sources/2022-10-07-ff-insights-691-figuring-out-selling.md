## Core Argument
Sales and distribution are hidden but essential business fundamentals that determine success more than product quality alone.

## Key Points
- Sales ability distinguishes superstars across all fields; it's systematically hidden under different titles.
- Great products don't sell themselves; effective distribution must be designed into the product from inception.
- Indian tech giants pursue superapps to lock users in as third-party tracking becomes regulated globally.
- Path dependence matters less than persuasion and selling strategy in market adoption outcomes.

## Open Question
If sales ability is the true differentiator across all fields, why do organizations continue to structure careers and incentives that hide or undervalue it?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates racing to build superapps (Tata Neu, Jio, Paytm, Flipkart) to capture India's massive internet base; sales and distribution critical for startups and large companies navigating India's competitive digital economy
