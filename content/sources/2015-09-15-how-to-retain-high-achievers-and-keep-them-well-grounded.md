## Core Argument
Retain high-achievers by personalizing engagement, building perspective on organizational interdependencies, and formalizing succession planning.

## Key Points
- Recognize high-achievers as individuals; custom-design leadership engagement around their unique needs and ambitions.
- Use 'retention by rotation' with 18-24 month cycles to combat boredom while building ownership of organizational outcomes.
- Formalize reward, recognition, and succession systems—avoid ad-hoc arrangements that erode trust in ambitious talent.

## Open Question
How do you keep high-achievers invested in long-term organizational health when their natural impatience and capability create constant pressure to accelerate their rise?

## India Angle
Critical for Indian startups scaling past survival stage—balancing Type-A talent ambitions with organizational maturity and succession planning, especially given India's competitive talent war and high attrition in growth companies.
