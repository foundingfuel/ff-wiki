## Core Argument
China's demographic collapse reshapes geopolitical power calculations, potentially favoring US-India alignment.

## Key Points
- China's population will halve relative to India by 2100; India reaches 1.53B vs China's ~750M.
- US population dynamics suggest America could regain economic lead over China despite current trajectory.
- Demographic dividend alone insufficient; per capita income and institutional strength matter more than population size.

## Open Question
If demographic trends are known and predictable, why do policymakers still overestimate China's long-term dominance and underweight India's institutional challenges in converting population advantage to prosperity?

## India Angle
India's demographic dividend narrative may be overstated; while India gains population advantage over China, this alone doesn't guarantee economic dominance without addressing productivity, per capita income, and institutional capacity gaps.
