## Core Argument
Professional video presence depends on intentionality and setup knowledge, not budget; affordable alternatives exist for Indian remote workers.

## Key Points
- DSLR/mirrorless cameras with capture cards create broadcast-quality Zoom setups rivaling expensive professional studios.
- Critical components: proper lighting, room depth for bokeh effect, and compatible software matter more than camera price alone.
- Budget-conscious alternatives exist: Logitech C920S and HP W300 offer solid 1080p performance under ₹15,000 for basic needs.

## Open Question
How much does premium video setup actually correlate with perceived competence and engagement in remote work, versus being a status signal?
