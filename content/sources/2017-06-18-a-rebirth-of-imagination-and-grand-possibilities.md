## Core Argument
Leaders who imagine alternative futures and connect diverse dots drive real change; imagination fades when certainty and busyness replace curiosity.

## Key Points
- Imagination decreases with age as knowledge solidifies worldviews; tech-enabled certainty accelerates this loss.
- Leaders who catalyse change have diverse interests, read widely, listen deeply, and connect disparate ideas.
- Meaningful dialogue requires breaking problems into nuanced pieces; social platforms devolve into conflict because they lack this discipline.

## Open Question
How can organisations and societies systematically cultivate imagination and dialogue when modern life's busyness and algorithmic certainty actively erode these capacities?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations need to reclaim imagination as a competitive advantage amid tech saturation and busyness; this applies to how Indian businesses, civil society, and governance can navigate transformation by connecting diverse dots rather than following algorithmic certainty.
