## Core Argument
Visionary leadership and institutional design can build world-class science in India despite systemic constraints.

## Key Points
- Bhabha leveraged personal networks and philanthropic funding to bypass bureaucratic bottlenecks and create TIFR.
- Creating an aspirational work environment—air-conditioning, art, world-class design—attracted and retained top scientific talent.
- Political patronage (Nehru relationship) was crucial to overcoming institutional resistance and securing critical resources.

## Open Question
Can such institution-building succeed without personal connections to power and wealth, or is Bhabha's model inherently dependent on individual charisma and privilege?

## India Angle
Demonstrates how individual vision and institutional design can transform India's scientific capacity despite bureaucratic constraints, colonial-era infrastructure, and limited research culture—a model for building world-class institutions within Indian constraints.
