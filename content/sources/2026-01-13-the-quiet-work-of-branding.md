## Core Argument
Branding has shifted from visibility to sense-making, helping organisations clarify identity during transitions and rebuild trust.

## Key Points
- Branding is sense-making during institutional transitions, helping leaders answer 'what problem do we solve now?'
- Purpose functions as operating logic and decision-framework, not slogans—shaping behaviour, partnerships and credibility.
- Narrative platforms built through continuous dialogue replace one-off campaigns; reputation emerges relationally over time.

## Open Question
If purpose genuinely shapes behaviour and decisions, how do organisations reconcile conflicting stakeholder interests when purpose-driven choices threaten revenue or operational comfort?

## India Angle
Indian institutions—foundations, legacy businesses, learning platforms—navigating rapid transitions need branding as sense-making, not cosmetics. Purpose as operating logic resonates with India's social enterprises and mission-driven organisations seeking authenticity amid scaling challenges.
