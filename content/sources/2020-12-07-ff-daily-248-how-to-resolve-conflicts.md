## Core Argument
Empathy with adversaries, not sympathy, unlocks conflict resolution and organizational trust.

## Key Points
- Empathy means understanding opposing behavior without approving it; crucial for high-stakes negotiations.
- Trust has three pillars: competence-based, communication-based, and contractual; leaders must address all three.
- Founder mental health and depression are systemic issues requiring normalized therapeutic support in startups.

## Open Question
How can leaders cultivate genuine empathy toward those whose actions seem morally indefensible, especially under crisis or competitive pressure?

## India Angle
Indian founders and leaders face acute mental health and conflict resolution challenges in a high-pressure startup ecosystem; empathy-based negotiation skills critical for Indian business culture transitioning from hierarchical to collaborative models.
