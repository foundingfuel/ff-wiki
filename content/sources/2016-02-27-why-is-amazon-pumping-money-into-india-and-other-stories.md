## Core Argument
Amazon is betting heavily on India because it offers a trillions-dollar market opportunity that China denied it.

## Key Points
- Amazon lost China to Alibaba; India represents a second-chance market with massive growth potential and young, connected population.
- Government initiatives (Make in India, Digital India, GST, financial inclusion) are creating favorable conditions for e-commerce scaling.
- Flipkart and Snapdeal are raising capital, while Alibaba lurks as potential investor; the competitive stakes are rising rapidly.

## Open Question
Will Amazon's massive infrastructure and technology investment create an insurmountable moat before Alibaba-backed local players consolidate, or will India's market dynamics prove too complex for foreign dominance?

## India Angle
India represents Amazon's critical opportunity to avoid repeating its China failure. India's demographics, growth trajectory, and government-backed infrastructure initiatives (Make in India, Digital India, GST) create a unique window for e-commerce dominance before entrenched local players and Alibaba consolidate power.
