## Core Argument
Africa exploits great power competition for economic gain without ideological alignment to any single bloc.

## Key Points
- China invested $300B in Africa since 2006, peaking 2016, then sharply declined due to debt crises and project viability issues.
- EU counters with $250B+ in strategic investments aligned with African Union's 2063 vision, emphasizing renewables and education.
- African nations increasingly pragmatic: accepting capital from all rivals (China, EU, US) based on developmental benefits, not geopolitical ideology.

## Open Question
How can China maintain Global South credibility while enforcing loan repayment against nations facing genuine economic hardship, without triggering broader anti-colonial sentiment?

## India Angle
India faces similar competition for influence in Africa and Global South; understanding China-EU rivalry in Africa offers strategic lessons for India's own positioning as an alternative power broker without colonial baggage.
