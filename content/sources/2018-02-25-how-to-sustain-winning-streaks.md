## Core Argument
Sustained winning requires hunger, distributed leadership, team cohesion, and continuous skill innovation—not individual brilliance alone.

## Key Points
- Aggressive hunger to win, channeled through relentless intensity, creates competitive culture that cascades through organization.
- Distributed leadership allows secure leaders to tap expertise, build ownership, and enable peer learning across teams.
- Continuous skill sharpening and adaptation prevent competitive advantages from becoming predictable—requires ongoing reinvention.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain the relentless intensity required for winning streaks without burning out talent or creating unsustainable pressure cultures?

## India Angle
Indian cricket team's winning streak in South Africa demonstrates leadership and team-building principles applicable to Indian enterprises seeking sustained high performance and cultural transformation.
