## Core Argument
Career success now requires self-directed brand-building and skill diversification rather than waiting for mentorship.

## Key Points
- Corporate mentorship is declining due to larger spans of control and higher job turnover.
- Develop deep expertise in one area, then broaden into multiple specialties strategically.
- Personal brand clarity matters—seek external feedback to understand how others perceive your value.

## Open Question
How do you balance deeply understanding your own value while avoiding overconfidence or excessive self-focus in a competitive, uncertain career environment?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating flatter hierarchies and fewer mentors must proactively build personal brands; relevant for Indian startups and MNCs where mentorship structures are weaker than traditional Western corporations.
