## Core Argument
COVID-19 resets how governments, customers view platforms; builders must choose between orchestrating markets or enabling ecosystems.

## Key Points
- Platforms vs pipes: platforms govern interactions between producers and consumers; control points determine ecosystem power.
- Orchestrators (Amazon) control demand and create inequality; enablers (Shopify) provide infrastructure and distribute value.
- COVID accelerates some trends, slows others; strategic businesses must redesign digital experiences and core assets.

## Open Question
How can platforms harness their growing power post-COVID while addressing inequality, privacy concerns, and government regulation without commoditizing themselves or incumbent businesses?

## India Angle
India's platform economy must balance rapid scaling with infrastructure gaps (identity, reputation systems). India Stack provides foundational infrastructure; platform builders should consider whether to orchestrate or enable to avoid deepening inequality, especially in rural markets.
