## Core Argument
Win business by cutting pitch length, eliminating jargon, focusing on client pain points and actionable ideas.

## Key Points
- Short, sharp pitches (20 min max) with energy and humour outperform lengthy credential presentations in virtual meetings.
- Clients have money for good ideas; focus on actionable solutions and emotional connection, not company history.
- Brevity forces clarity: identify pain points, offer few practical solutions, lead with storytelling over slides.

## Open Question
How do firms balance the internal intensity of pitch preparation with the external simplicity clients now demand, without losing strategic depth?

## India Angle
Indian service firms and consultancies—common in advertising, design, IT—must adapt pitch strategies for remote-first client engagement, moving from lengthy credential-heavy presentations to sharp, outcome-focused demos that resonate in virtual settings.
