## Core Argument
Beauty and meaning emerge from quiet collisions—of currents, people, seasons—not grand gestures.

## Key Points
- Serendipity and wandering reveal more than purposeful searching; the parking spot accident led to profound observation.
- Nature's rhythm—migration, tides, cycles—teaches that movement and return are life's pattern, not failure.
- Quiet moments of awe and connection matter more than crowds; joy announces itself softly.

## Open Question
How do we honor the profound meaning we find in nature's quiet moments within societies obsessed with documentation, notification, and narrative?
