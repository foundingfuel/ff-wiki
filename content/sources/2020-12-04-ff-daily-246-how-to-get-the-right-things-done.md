## Core Argument
Success requires choosing what's essential, not doing more; clarity on contribution beats busyness.

## Key Points
- Essentialism means filtering opportunities to identify vital few, not maximizing activity volume.
- Indian businesses must lead consumers through turbulent times with imagination, not follow their stated needs.
- Self-reliance without global partnerships limits access to smarter technologies and efficient systems.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the essentialist focus on fewer priorities with the need to remain agile and responsive to rapid market changes in volatile environments?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders must adopt essentialist thinking to navigate turbulent markets; leaders like Rama Bijapurkar and Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw exemplify how imagination and strategic focus—not just scale—drive value in India's unique consumer and market context.
