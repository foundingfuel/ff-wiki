## Core Argument
Tech firms bypass traditional media by building direct audience relationships, reshaping media economics and power dynamics globally.

## Key Points
- Tech companies compete with media on audience attention and credibility, especially in niche expertise areas like entrepreneurship and technology.
- Independent journalism faces business model crisis from both ad-driven and subscription polarization, compounded by tech platforms' direct-to-audience strategies.
- Indian media startups disadvantaged by FDI restrictions while facing algorithmic competition; gig workers exploit platform design flaws through creative workarounds.

## Open Question
How can independent journalism survive when tech platforms offer superior reach, subject expertise credibility, and capital, while media startups face regulatory constraints and business model obsolescence?

## India Angle
Indian media startups face FDI restrictions while global tech firms build media platforms freely; gig workers (Uber/Ola drivers) in India navigate platform algorithms and real-world constraints; Clubhouse adoption potential given India's oral tradition and WhatsApp voice culture.
