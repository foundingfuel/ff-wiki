## Core Argument
Chatbots mask the real AI race: scientific models that will reshape—or end—human civilization.

## Key Points
- Narrow AI already delivers measurable benefits in agriculture and healthcare; scientific AI will determine humanity's future.
- Global powers (US, China, Korea) compete intensely on scientific AI; public discourse fixates on ChatGPT instead.
- Unregulated scientific AI poses existential risks: self-replication, biological weapons, breached nuclear systems.

## Open Question
How can democracies regulate scientific AI's existential risks without ceding dominance to authoritarian nations that may pursue it without constraint?

## India Angle
India's agricultural AI applications (FarmBeats in Baramati) represent practical narrow AI benefits, but India risks being a consumer of foreign scientific AI rather than a builder. Policy and infrastructure investment in scientific AI research are critical for India's technological sovereignty and future competitiveness.
