## Core Argument
US-China rivalry is reshaping global trade and technology flows; India must strategically position itself amid shifting geopolitical priorities.

## Key Points
- US now securitises all economic flows through national security lens, blocking dual-use tech from reaching China.
- Both US political parties agree on China restrictions and anti-trade stances; divergence lies in treatment of allies like India.
- India can capitalise on China Plus One strategy but risks trade wars and tech controls under either US administration.

## Open Question
How can India simultaneously attract US investment in strategic sectors while maintaining essential trade relationships and technology access without choosing sides in the US-China rivalry?

## India Angle
India faces opportunity and risk in US-China competition: potential beneficiary of China Plus One strategy, but vulnerable to US trade protectionism regardless of administration; must navigate tech restrictions while attracting semiconductor and defence investments.
