## Core Argument
Nuclear weapons proliferation and AI integration compress decision timelines toward accidental Armageddon.

## Key Points
- 13,000 warheads exist globally; 2,800 US-Russia warheads on 10-minute launch alert with no mutual no-first-use commitment.
- Hypersonic missiles with AI targeting evade traditional defenses, shrinking commander decision time to dangerous levels.
- Low-yield earth-penetrating warheads create 20x damage, normalizing nuclear weapon use and lowering conflict escalation thresholds.

## Open Question
How can nations maintain strategic stability when AI-enabled weapons compress decision timelines below human cognitive capability for verification and restraint?

## India Angle
India holds nuclear weapons with no-first-use doctrine but faces pressure to abandon it; vulnerable to potential conflicts in Taiwan Strait and Korean Peninsula with global nuclear implications; positioned between US-Russia-China strategic tensions.
