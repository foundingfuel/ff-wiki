## Core Argument
Pandemic exposed how sports hierarchy masked class divides; democratised fandom while revealing society's lopsided priorities.

## Key Points
- Stadium vs. TV fandom reveals generational divide in how we consume experiences and community.
- IPL's Rs 4,000 crore budget prioritised over World Cup exposes profit-driven decision-making in Indian cricket.
- Sports suspension forced family reconnection; return of games provided psychological relief amid career uncertainty.

## Open Question
How do we reconcile sport's role as both a genuine connector of human relationships and a mechanism that distracts from deeper societal inequities?
