## Core Argument
During slowdowns, consumer desire drives sales; manufacturers must innovate product appeal over volume-focused practices.

## Key Points
- MG Hector and Kia Seltos succeeded with tech-rich design and pricing when sector faced severe demand slowdown
- Indian auto majors historically dumped excess inventory on dealers, creating illusion of growth and eventual dealer collapse
- Slowdowns expose lazy manufacturing habits; only companies designing genuinely desirable cars will survive demand contraction

## Open Question
If design excellence and tech features take two years to develop, how can manufacturers respond quickly enough to shifting consumer preferences during a slowdown?

## India Angle
Indian auto majors face demand slowdown; need to shift from inventory dumping to designing desirable cars. New entrants (MG, Kia) succeeding by combining tech features with attractive pricing—a model Indian manufacturers must adopt.
