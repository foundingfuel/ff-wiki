## Core Argument
Holacracy replaces management hierarchy with dynamic group processes, enabling autonomy while maintaining accountability.

## Key Points
- Holacracy is a social operating system replacing traditional management hierarchy with embedded group processes.
- Workers gain protected autonomy within defined roles while teams dynamically restructure based on organizational purpose.
- System emerged from entrepreneurial experimentation rather than management theory, prioritizing practice over ideology.

## Open Question
How can organizations genuinely devolve decision-making to frontline workers without creating chaos, and does Holacracy's complexity actually deliver the promised autonomy in practice?

## India Angle
Indian startups and growing companies often struggle with hierarchical structures inherited from traditional business culture; Holacracy offers an alternative model for scaling while maintaining agility, relevant as Indian organizations seek to compete globally without losing innovation velocity.
