## Core Argument
Crises expose hidden interdependence; responsible conduct requires balancing competition with stewardship.

## Key Points
- Aviation's tight coupling means one dominant player's failure creates systemic shock across airports, competitors, and regulators.
- Market logic alone cannot address ethical tensions when scarcity stems from institutional misalignment rather than external shocks.
- Industry maturity requires pre-agreed protocols and stabilisers; stewardship means recognizing when mobility becomes public experience, not just private service.

## Open Question
How can competitive markets maintain both efficiency incentives and collective stability when one actor's failure reveals systemic interdependence that markets alone cannot regulate?

## India Angle
Indian aviation ecosystem's stability depends on how market leaders and competitors navigate crises; reveals need for industry-wide protocols and stewardship norms in India's high-growth sectors where one player's failure cascades systemically.
