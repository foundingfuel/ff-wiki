## Core Argument
International schools offer innovative pedagogy but parent-driven grade obsession undermines their educational philosophy.

## Key Points
- 600+ IGCSE and 210 IB schools exist in India, but remain niche despite rapid growth in Tier II cities.
- International education costs 3-4x national curricula; tuitions add Rs 2-2.5 lakh for senior classes, amplifying parental ROI expectations.
- Grade obsession contradicts international boards' innovation in teaching/assessment; students choose 'scoring' subjects over aptitude.

## Open Question
How can international schools maintain pedagogical innovation and student agency when parental investment expectations drive relentless focus on grades and university admissions metrics?

## India Angle
International schools are rapidly expanding in India across metros and Tier II cities, creating a new education paradigm for aspirational Indian families seeking alternatives to CBSE/ICSE while grappling with affordability, grade obsession, and alignment with global university admissions.
