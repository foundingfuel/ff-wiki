## Core Argument
Media investigates because regulators failed; accountability requires all democratic pillars to function.

## Key Points
- Media's role is investigation, not prosecution; Indian Express investigated Kochhar systematically over months.
- Regulators had whistleblower letters but failed fiduciary duty; media filled institutional vacuum.
- Antiquated laws like Angel Tax punish entrepreneurship while regulators sleep on actual corporate fraud.

## Open Question
If media investigates because regulators fail, what ensures media itself is accountable and unbiased in its investigative power?

## India Angle
Indian regulatory authorities failed in fiduciary duty during Kochhar scandal; media stepped in to investigate. Raises questions about institutional accountability and the Fourth Estate's role in Indian democracy when state agencies abdicate responsibility.
