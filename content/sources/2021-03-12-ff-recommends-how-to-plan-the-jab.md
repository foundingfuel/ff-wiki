## Core Argument
Clear communication and practical guidance reduce vaccine hesitancy and systemic confusion in India's largest health intervention.

## Key Points
- Vaccine hesitancy stems from unclear eligibility rules, inconsistent official messaging, and unresolved questions about safety and efficacy.
- System improvements (reduced wait times, more centres, tech fixes) justify early vaccination despite initial chaos.
- Certification requirements and eligibility confusion create opportunities for gaming; transparency and standardised formats prevent delays.

## Open Question
How can India balance rapid vaccination rollout with eligibility safeguards when doctors, patients, and officials interpret rules differently?

## India Angle
India's 1.3 billion-person vaccination drive faces execution challenges: inconsistent government communication, vaccine hesitancy, system inefficiencies, and the need for clear, accessible guidance to scale rapidly across phases.
