## Core Argument
Strategic board games develop cognitive agility and collaboration better than luck-based alternatives.

## Key Points
- Eurogames prioritize strategy and skill over luck, enabling intellectual growth through play.
- Collaborative games like The Crew and Pandemic teach collective problem-solving across generations.
- Accessible entry points (Mint Works at Rs 1,000, 20 minutes) lower barriers to participation.

## Open Question
How do board games sustained engagement and cognitive benefits compare to digital alternatives in the post-pandemic world, given the internet's dominance?

## India Angle
Board game culture is emerging in India post-pandemic; this positions Eurogames as an accessible way for Indian families and individuals to develop strategic thinking and collaborative problem-solving skills.
