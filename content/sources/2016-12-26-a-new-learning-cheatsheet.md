## Core Argument
Experiential, immersive learning embedded in real work transforms how organizations innovate and develop leaders.

## Key Points
- Deep insights emerge from empathetic conversations and ethnographic research, not surveys and large sample sizes.
- Organizations hoard knowledge in employee heads; Design Thinking taps this through collaborative, visual problem-solving.
- Coaching-embedded, challenge-based learning moves business metrics while developing leadership bench simultaneously.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale intimate, high-touch immersive learning across large, geographically dispersed teams without losing its effectiveness?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises need to shift from consultant-dependent growth models to building internal innovation capacity through immersive, experiential learning—especially relevant for large companies and startups navigating rapid change.
