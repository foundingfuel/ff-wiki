## Core Argument
Symbols embed intangible values; India must clarify its national 'why' as it reopens and recovers.

## Key Points
- Symbols gain power only when tied to clear purpose, cause, or belief—not the material itself.
- India's Covid recovery hinges on policy signals (border access, vaccination) and institutional trust-building through consistent symbols.
- Workplace transformation in 2022 India: salary transparency, hybrid work, quality of life matter more than hustle culture alone.

## Open Question
How can India balance its rising resilience rankings and reopening signals with the unfinished work of second-dose vaccination and variant surveillance without losing public trust?

## India Angle
India's jump to 26th in Covid resilience rankings reflects policy shifts (border reopening, vaccination rollout) and institutional capacity. Symbol-driven leadership matters for Indian policymakers building trust; also relevant to how Indian companies embed values amid workplace transformation trends.
