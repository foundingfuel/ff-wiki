## Core Argument
Evaluating 100+ pandals reveals how artistic merit, sustainability, and cultural preservation intersect in festival curation.

## Key Points
- UNESCO recognition and environmental recommendations are reshaping how Kolkata's 3,000+ pandals approach design and sustainability
- Ranking art requires balancing subjective criteria—beauty, message, environmental impact—with personal bias and contextual limitations
- Durga Puja's scale and accessibility (priority passes, infrastructure) transform it into a consumable experience comparable to global festivals

## Open Question
How do critics balance subjective aesthetic judgment with respect for community effort and cultural significance without dismissing genuine artistic achievement?
