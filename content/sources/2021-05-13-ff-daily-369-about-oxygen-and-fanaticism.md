## Core Argument
Fanaticism drives societies to extremes; reason and enlightenment remain antidotes to institutional rigidity.

## Key Points
- IP waivers alone don't unlock vaccine manufacturing—complex multi-step process requires trade secrets, facilities, trials.
- Priestley and Franklin fled fanaticism in Britain to build reason-based systems in America.
- India must focus on expanding manufacturer capacity and procurement efficiency beyond symbolic IP policy wins.

## Open Question
Can policy-level IP reforms address manufacturing bottlenecks when the real constraints are operational expertise, infrastructure, and supply chains?

## India Angle
India's WTO position on vaccine IP waivers reflects developing world interests; article questions whether policy solutions alone address manufacturing capacity gaps in Indian vaccine production.
