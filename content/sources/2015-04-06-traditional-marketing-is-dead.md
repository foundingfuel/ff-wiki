## Core Argument
Growth hacking through product-driven virality replaces interruption marketing as the dominant user acquisition strategy.

## Key Points
- Traditional marketing wastes attention; consumers see 5,000+ messages daily and ignore intrusive advertising.
- Growth hacking embeds acquisition mechanics into product features, making users organic marketers through incentives.
- Successful examples like Dropbox align core functionality with sharing, creating compounding viral loops at scale.

## Open Question
How do traditional non-tech businesses authentically integrate growth hacking into their business models when their core value doesn't naturally lend itself to virality or sharing?

## India Angle
Indian startups must adopt growth hacking over costly traditional advertising; applies to scaling consumer products and SaaS companies in India's price-sensitive, mobile-first market.
