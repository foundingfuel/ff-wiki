## Core Argument
India needs deep-tech startups solving real problems, not app clones; Team Indus exemplifies this possibility.

## Key Points
- Most Indian startups copy foreign models offering convenience; few tackle original technological challenges or real Indian problems.
- Team Indus's lunar landing mission demonstrates Indian engineering capability in one of humanity's hardest grand challenges.
- Unlike internet startups, deep-tech ventures have zero margin for error; requires systems thinking, not iterative tweaking.

## Open Question
How can India scale deep-tech startups when venture capital and ecosystem incentives favor low-risk, high-speed app-based models with quicker returns?

## India Angle
Team Indus represents India's potential to tackle grand technological challenges beyond typical startup models; showcases how Indian engineering can compete globally in space exploration, shifting narrative from app-based convenience startups to deep-tech innovation.
