## Core Argument
Trump's transactional tariff strategy lacks coherent geopolitical vision, pushing Indo-Pacific allies toward China despite US security importance.

## Key Points
- No Indo-Pacific country has capitulated to US tariff pressure; strategic uncertainty forces allies to hedge bets across multiple powers.
- China accelerates market diversification via Belt and Road, insulating itself from tariffs while US offers no compelling alternative to allies.
- US industrial strategy incoherence—tariffs without subsidies, scrapped EV mandates, skepticism of CHIPS Act—undermines reshoring credibility with partners.

## Open Question
How can the US maintain Indo-Pacific alliance leverage without offering either a credible economic alternative to China or predictable long-term strategy?

## India Angle
India faces pressure to choose between US trade demands and strategic autonomy; Apple's India investments show how tariff uncertainty affects supply chain decisions, forcing India to balance US negotiations while managing China tensions and retaining policy independence.
