## Core Argument
MSME revival requires adaptive spending, differentiated approaches, and reformed lending infrastructure beyond traditional banks.

## Key Points
- Government must spend wisely on high-impact MSMEs that employ, generate profit, and pay taxes.
- Embedded and bank-led lending models bypass distribution, risk, and diversification problems plaguing SME lenders.
- Needs vary dramatically: tiny MSMEs need working capital; indebted ones need renegotiation; all need clear criteria-based support.

## Open Question
How can government provide grants/equity to MSMEs without creating corruption ('friends and family' scandals) while simultaneously addressing the moral hazard of protecting inefficient businesses?

## India Angle
MSMEs are critical to India's economy (30% of GDP, 110M jobs). Revival requires targeted government spending, reformed lending models, and tailored interventions—not one-size-fits-all solutions. Direct transfer infrastructure similar to JAM trinity could bypass traditional banking bottlenecks.
