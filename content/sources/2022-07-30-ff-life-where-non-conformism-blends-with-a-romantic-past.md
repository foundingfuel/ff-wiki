## Core Argument
Cities thrive when romantic pasts coexist with vibrant non-conformism and freedom of expression.

## Key Points
- Prague balances Habsburg architectural heritage with underground movements and pop culture seamlessly
- The Lennon Wall symbolizes how dissent becomes art and protest transforms into lasting cultural monuments
- Post-communist Prague embraces modern pop icons like Warhol and Banksy, showing cultural evolution without losing historical roots

## Open Question
Can cities genuinely preserve their romantic historical identity while embracing irreverent, non-conformist modern movements, or does one inevitably dilute the other?
