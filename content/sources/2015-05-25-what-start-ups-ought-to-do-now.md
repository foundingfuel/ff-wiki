## Core Argument
Startups must embrace design thinking: unlearning assumptions, solving holistically, and prioritizing emotional experience.

## Key Points
- Designers unlearn existing mental models to reimagine problems from first principles, not incremental improvement
- Solving for the whole means prototyping with incomplete information and failing fast, not breaking problems into isolated solvable pieces
- Products must consider emotion and holistic user experience alongside utility, cost and risk

## Open Question
How do founders balance the designer's comfort with incomplete information and rapid iteration against investor demands for clear hypotheses, proof points and execution timelines?

## India Angle
Indian startups must adopt design thinking to compete globally; domestic tech ecosystem lacks design-first culture compared to Silicon Valley, creating opportunity for differentiation
