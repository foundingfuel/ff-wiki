## Core Argument
Indian businesses need urgent business model reset using current climate data, not outdated tools, to survive accelerating extreme weather.

## Key Points
- Extreme weather events increased 5X since 1970; India losing in one year what it lost over a decade previously
- Business continuity plans designed for stable conditions won't work; companies must revalidate models for new climate reality
- Double materiality lens required: assess both impact on climate and climate's impact on operations and supply chains

## Open Question
Large companies struggle to understand climate contours well enough to make investment decisions; what toolkit exists for SMEs—which form India's economic bulk—to assess and respond to these risks?

## India Angle
India faces disproportionate climate risk: 34M of 80M global job losses by 2030 will be Indian; $69B annual climate losses now vs $69B for entire 1998-2017 decade; extreme weather events increased 5X since 1970; Indian businesses lack resilience toolkit and must rebuild models for present reality, not decade-old assumptions.
