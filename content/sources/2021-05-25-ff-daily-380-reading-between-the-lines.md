## Core Argument
Remote work requires cognitive empathy; complex systems need decentralized, local solutions over centralized expertise.

## Key Points
- Online work removes nonverbal cues; cognitive empathy and 'reading between lines' become critical leadership skills.
- Economics treating world as mechanical system fails; must recognize humans as complex, self-adapting networks.
- India's lockdown failures prove centralized solutions backfire; Mumbai and Vietnam show dispersed local responsibility works better.

## Open Question
How do you reconcile capitalism's profit motive with democracy's mandate for citizen well-being when both are foundational to modern governance?

## India Angle
India's pandemic response showed failure of top-down centralization; local empowerment and decentralized solutions—like vaccine mixing strategy—offer better models for development policy and crisis management.
