## Core Argument
Pandemic forced behavioural shifts—remote inclusivity, resilience, remote collaboration—worth retaining beyond crisis.

## Key Points
- Remote meetings can democratise workplace voice; intentional practices like hand-raising prevent senior/extrovert dominance.
- Physical office boundaries helped mental separation of work/life; commute became reading time and movement restored.
- Geographic barriers dissolved for social impact work; Google Meet enabled rural engagement previously impossible despite resources.

## Open Question
If remote work enables better inclusion and geographic reach, why do organisations default to office-centric cultures when restrictions lift rather than intentionally designing hybrid for sustained behavioural change?

## India Angle
Indian organisations transitioning to hybrid work can learn from these practices—inclusive remote meetings, resilience frameworks, and geographic decoupling of engagement are particularly relevant for India's distributed workforce and social enterprise sector.
