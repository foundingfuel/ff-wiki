## Core Argument
Kindness and ethics are undervalued in entrepreneurship education and venture selection despite being foundational to sustainable success.

## Key Points
- Credentials are necessary but insufficient—smart people may lack ethics, empathy, or humility required for real impact.
- Kindness is hard because humans carry built-in delusions of centrality, separation, and permanence that obstruct compassionate action.
- Entrepreneurs need ethical frameworks and stress-tested values to resist negative influence when investors or circumstances pressure compromise.

## Open Question
If kindness and ethics are so critical to meaningful achievement, why do funding systems and success metrics systematically reward hustle and growth over character?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and VCs rely heavily on credentials as proxies for capability, but this misses ethical and cultural dimensions critical for sustainable Indian ventures; need for values-driven entrepreneurship in India's startup ecosystem
