## Core Argument
Relevance requires continuous reinvention; disruption gets headlines while adaptation does unglamorous work.

## Key Points
- Disruption dominates discourse while lessons from the 'disrupted' remain invisible and unexamined.
- Staying relevant demands constant choice-making: spotting opportunities, letting go of past successes.
- Business models, not technologies alone, drive disruption; continuous reinvention is the real challenge.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance the urgent pressure to disrupt with the patient, continuous work of staying relevant without being seen as complacent?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises—from established conglomerates to startups—must balance the pressure to disrupt with the unglamorous work of staying relevant; the 'disrupted' narrative is particularly relevant as Indian companies navigate global competition.
