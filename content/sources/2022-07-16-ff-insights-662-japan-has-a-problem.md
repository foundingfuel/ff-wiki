## Core Argument
Japan's hidden poverty stems from rigid corporate cultures and faulty systemic algorithms, not lack of intelligence.

## Key Points
- Real wages in Japan have drifted downward for decades despite perceived wealth, affecting young workers and women disproportionately.
- Overwork culture masks low white-collar productivity; long hours create feedback loop making workers tired and ineffective.
- Stupidity is applying faulty algorithms persistently; systems and tools can keep smart people trapped in wrong solutions.

## Open Question
If Japan's problem is systemic (broken tools and algorithms), how do entrenched institutions and corporate cultures actually change when those within them are rationally locked into faulty systems?

## India Angle
India faces similar wage stagnation and corporate culture challenges; understanding Japan's systemic failures in productivity and seniority-based wages offers cautionary lessons for Indian companies scaling up and managing talent retention.
