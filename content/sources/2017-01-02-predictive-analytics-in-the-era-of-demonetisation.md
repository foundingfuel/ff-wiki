## Core Argument
Use predictive analytics rooted in historical data to navigate temporary market disruptions without panic-driven reactions.

## Key Points
- Distinguish between structural disruption and temporary flux by comparing current metrics to historical trend-lines.
- Build new forecasts by measuring variance between past baselines and present data to establish calibrated responses.
- Past data becomes most valuable during crises when present data is volatile and unreliable for decision-making.

## Open Question
How do you calibrate predictive models when the underlying drivers of change (demonetisation effects) remain in flux and may invalidate historical patterns?

Demonetisation as a shock to Indian digital businesses; how companies navigating cash crunch disruption can use data to stay grounded rather than panic.
