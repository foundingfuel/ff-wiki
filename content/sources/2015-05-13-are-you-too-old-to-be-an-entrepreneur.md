## Core Argument
Age is not a disqualifier for entrepreneurship; context and industry dynamics matter more than founder age.

## Key Points
- B2B businesses benefit from founder age and domain expertise; B2C/social media contexts favor younger founders.
- Technology trajectory matters: cumulative tech favors experience; disruptive tech favors youth.
- Senior entrepreneurs must adapt to resource scarcity and loss of corporate support structures.

## Open Question
If context determines founder age advantage, how do investors systematize this nuance rather than applying blanket age preferences?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs across ages—from Raj's startup to Aravind Eye Hospital—succeed when leveraging experience; investor bias toward young founders is misplaced given India's B2B context and domain expertise requirements.
