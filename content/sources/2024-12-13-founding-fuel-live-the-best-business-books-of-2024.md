## Core Argument
Modern leadership requires authentic self-knowledge, peer coaching networks, and truth-telling cultures to navigate rapid change.

## Key Points
- CEOs need vulnerability, clear purpose, and peer coaching—not imperial expertise—to lead in complex, fast-changing environments.
- Leaders must stay relevant by continuous learning from diverse sources and building networks of truth-tellers across levels.
- AI adoption shows employees learning on personal data first, creating unprecedented opportunity for self-directed upskilling before workplace application.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance being always-on, vulnerable, and accountable in a hyperconnected world while maintaining the psychological safety needed for authentic peer coaching?

## India Angle
Indian leaders face similar challenges of complexity, remote work transitions, and need for peer networks; Shiv's reading practice and CEO feedback cultures are directly applicable to India's growing leadership ecosystem.
