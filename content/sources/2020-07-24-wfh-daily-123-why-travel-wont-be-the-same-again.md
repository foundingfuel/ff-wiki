## Core Argument
COVID-19 permanently reshapes travel, work location, and content consumption patterns; authenticity wins globally.

## Key Points
- Remote work normalizes distributed living; people discover alternatives to city-centric travel patterns.
- Authentic storytelling rooted in local context outperforms imitation of global templates.
- Crises accelerate imagination of alternative futures, reshaping consumer behavior permanently.

## Open Question
If travel and work are permanently decentralized post-COVID, how do cities and tourism-dependent economies adapt their economic models?

## India Angle
Amazon Prime Video's India strategy shows how Indian creators can succeed globally by telling authentic local stories rather than copying Western formats—critical for India's digital content economy and creator ecosystem.
