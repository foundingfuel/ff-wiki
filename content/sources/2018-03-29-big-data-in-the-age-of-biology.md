## Core Argument
Big Data platforms exploit regulatory blindness to enter healthcare; the Age of Biology will reshape fortunes.

## Key Points
- Tech giants (Apple, Google, Facebook, Amazon) are pivoting to healthcare using Big Data and personalized medicine.
- Regulatory authorities lack technical understanding, enabling platforms to scale despite questionable practices and claims.
- Knowledge doubling creates burden that platforms must help solve, making them essential infrastructure despite risks.

## Open Question
If platforms are essential because knowledge doubles faster than humans can learn, how do we regulate them without stifling innovation or creating unequal access to healthcare breakthroughs?

## India Angle
Indian startups and healthcare sector must navigate similar Big Data and personalized medicine trends; regulatory capacity gap mirrors India's struggle to keep pace with tech-driven business models.
