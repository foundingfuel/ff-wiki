## Core Argument
India's fintech regulation must balance innovation, stability, and inclusion while regulators build sector expertise.

## Key Points
- Fintech founders prioritize speed and innovation; regulators prioritize stability and consumer protection—fundamental misalignment.
- US (principles-based), EU (rules-based), and China (experimental-then-restrictive) take different regulatory approaches with tradeoffs.
- India adds inclusion as core regulatory goal, building regulator expertise via innovation hubs and adaptive, risk-based interventions.

## Open Question
Can India's regulator expertise and adaptive approach prevent the unintended consequences that stifled EU innovation or the heavy-handed crackdowns seen in China?

## India Angle
India uniquely balances three goals—financial stability, consumer protection, and financial inclusion—through adaptive, risk-based regulation and regulator expertise-building, differentiating its fintech approach from US, EU, and China models.
