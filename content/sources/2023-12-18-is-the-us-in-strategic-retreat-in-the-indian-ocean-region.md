## Core Argument
US maintains strategic dominance in Indian Ocean; China's overseas presence vulnerable despite growth.

## Key Points
- China's naval bases across Indian Ocean region are geographically exposed to US-India combined military power.
- PLAN's vast sailing distances and single coastline create strategic disadvantages against distributed US-allied forces.
- Western GDP ($50T) and US military spending (40% global) dwarf China's capability ($19T GDP).

## Open Question
If China's position is so vulnerable, why is it investing heavily in these bases, and what triggers the shift from strategic posturing to actual conflict?

## India Angle
India's naval power and geographic position are central to containing Chinese expansion in the Indian Ocean; US-India strategic alignment critical for regional stability and India's strategic autonomy.
