## Core Argument
Companies must harness digital capital, lower marginal costs, and new monetization models to survive technological disruption.

## Key Points
- Digital capital—data, analytics, algorithms—is the competitive moat of the future, enabling new products and services.
- Digitization drastically reduces distribution costs and geographic barriers, enabling startups and micro-businesses to scale rapidly.
- Traditional business dominance now lasts years, not decades; reinvention is continuous rather than episodic.

## Open Question
The article assumes data access and digital infrastructure are equally available to all players, but doesn't address how emerging markets or resource-constrained organizations can build competitive digital capabilities at scale.

## India Angle
Indian companies across retail, manufacturing, and fintech must rapidly build digital capital capabilities and exploit lower marginal costs to compete globally; startups like Paytm and Flipkart exemplify this transition, but most traditional businesses lag in data utilization and digital infrastructure investment.
