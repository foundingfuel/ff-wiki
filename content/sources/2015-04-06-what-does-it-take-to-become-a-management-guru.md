## Core Argument
Management guru status requires path dependence, individual adaptability, dual academic-practitioner focus, and environmental support.

## Key Points
- Sheth succeeded by timing career with field evolution and demonstrating ability to adapt across emerging domains
- Effective gurus bridge academic rigor with practitioner relevance through journals, books, training, and consulting
- Corporate market demand, university mobility, and competition for talent are essential environmental enablers

## Open Question
Why do replicable individual characteristics (hard work, learning zest, collaboration) and seemingly available environments fail to produce home-grown Indian management gurus despite many emigrating Indians achieving guru status?

## India Angle
Indian management academics succeed abroad but rarely achieve guru status in India; suggests systemic gaps in Indian corporate market demand for scholarship, university mobility, and institutional support for thought leadership
