## Core Argument
Indian cocktail culture can flourish when curated by knowledgeable enthusiasts who educate rather than overwhelm.

## Key Points
- Indians gravitate toward simple spirits; cocktails seen as intimidating or overly sweet, lacking awareness of quality mixology.
- Expert practitioners demonstrate seasonality, intentionality, and storytelling transform cocktails from drinks into meaningful social experiences.
- Accessibility matters: simple recipes (hot toddy, sangria) lower barriers; memorable contexts (sunsets, company) create lasting cultural associations.

## Open Question
How can India develop a sophisticated cocktail culture without replicating Western consumption patterns or losing connection to indigenous drinking traditions?

## India Angle
Indians traditionally avoid cocktails, preferring conservative drinking choices; this article addresses the cultural gap between India's drinking preferences and global cocktail culture, exploring how awareness and experimentation can shift consumption patterns.
