## Core Argument
2018 business books reveal shifts in thinking about culture, inequality, technology, and collective responsibility.

## Key Points
- Culture requires psychological safety, vulnerability, and clarity of purpose—not just metrics and OKRs.
- Data literacy matters more than data availability; citizens systematically misunderstand progress against poverty.
- Technology's relationship to human autonomy remains unresolved—benefits all or increases dependence?

## Open Question
Can technology democratize opportunity and reduce inequality, or does it inevitably concentrate power and dependence among users?

## India Angle
Indian authors gaining prominence in business literature; India-specific case studies (Billionaire Raj, inequality through billionaire lens) relevant to understanding governance and wealth concentration in Indian context.
