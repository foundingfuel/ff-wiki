## Core Argument
India's fragmented AI approach leaves it vulnerable to geopolitical dominance and digital colonization.

## Key Points
- AI is the new battleground for global dominance; India is absent from frontier AI race.
- China built closed sovereign ecosystem; India's data and infrastructure remain exposed to foreign control.
- India's AI policy lacks national security perspective and central coordination under PMO.

## Open Question
India's security establishment may be quietly preparing AI strategy, yet public policy remains application-focused—how do leaders reconcile silent preparation with needed public articulation of strategic intent?

## India Angle
India must urgently shift from application-focused AI policy to strategic sovereignty. Without sovereign compute infrastructure, data protection, and security-led governance, India risks digital colonization and strategic vulnerability in the US-China AI dominance contest.
