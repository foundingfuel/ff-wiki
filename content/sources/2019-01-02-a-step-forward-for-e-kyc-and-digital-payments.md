## Core Argument
Aadhaar e-KYC restrictions slow financial inclusion, but digital payments ecosystem advances through UPI and DigiLocker.

## Key Points
- E-KYC suspension reduced IPPB account openings by 80-85%, stalling financial inclusion momentum across India
- Fintech startups lobbying government for Aadhaar amendments to enable voluntary customer data sharing for faster onboarding
- UPI 2.0 and wallet interoperability guidelines accelerate digital payments adoption independent of e-KYC restrictions

## Open Question
How can India balance legitimate privacy protections for Aadhaar with the urgent need for faster financial inclusion through e-KYC?

## India Angle
E-KYC restrictions on Aadhaar have slowed financial inclusion in India, impacting banks and fintech startups while digital payments through UPI grow. Government amendments aim to balance privacy concerns with digital financial infrastructure needs.
