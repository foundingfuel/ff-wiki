## Core Argument
Organizations disconnect intent from results by overhyping change initiatives and letting process override objectives.

## Key Points
- Inflated promises in change initiatives create credibility gaps when delivery falls short, damaging future change efforts.
- Process standardization can gradually eclipse end objectives, turning focus from 'what we deliver' to 'what we do'.
- Calibrated expectation-setting with transparent dialogue about constraints prevents the emperor's-new-clothes syndrome of pretending everything works.

## Open Question
How do leaders build sufficient momentum for change without creating unrealistic expectations that inevitably disappoint and erode organizational trust?

## India Angle
Indian organizations, especially those in rapid growth phases (IT, manufacturing, financial services), frequently struggle with this disconnect—launching ambitious transformation initiatives without ensuring execution capability or managing expectations realistically, leading to credibility loss and talent attrition.
