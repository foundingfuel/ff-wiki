## Core Argument
Meritocratic culture, cross-functional collaboration, and empowering technical experts drive world-class innovation.

## Key Points
- Isro built earth observation excellence by combining specialized expertise from SAC and ISAC through healthy collaboration across silos.
- Decision-making authority delegated to subject matter experts regardless of hierarchy enabled rapid technical innovation and capability building.
- Indigenous technology choices and cost-effective solutions created six times cheaper earth observation data than global competitors, with cascading benefits for agriculture and governance.

## Open Question
How can large, bureaucratic government organizations maintain the meritocratic culture and technical decision-making autonomy that enabled Isro's success as they scale and face political pressures?

## India Angle
Isro's earth observation capability demonstrates how Indian institutions can achieve world-class technological excellence through meritocratic culture and collaborative structures—a model applicable to other Indian public sector organizations and startups facing similar scaling challenges.
