## Core Argument
CEO success depends on four learnable behaviours (DARE), not innate charisma or predetermined destiny.

## Key Points
- 70% of CEOs didn't set CEO goals early; career trajectory is less predetermined than myths suggest.
- Decision-making speed, not quality, distinguishes successful executives; this is learnable at any career stage.
- Reliability is the rarest and most powerful behaviour—doubling promotion chances and separating performers.

## Open Question
If CEO behaviours are learnable and not personality-dependent, why do most organisations still rely on charisma-based hiring and promotion signals?

## India Angle
Indian executives and aspiring leaders can benefit from evidence-based CEO behaviours rather than mythologised leadership models; applicable to India's growing cohort of professional managers and startup founders seeking to scale.
