## Core Argument
Tata Neu is a coalition loyalty programme, not a true super app, hampered by misaligned brand equity and fragmented customer experiences.

## Key Points
- Tata brands lack associative value cohesion—Taj and Titan operate at premium tiers while Croma and Westside target mass markets, making unified rewards ineffective.
- Individual brand loyalty programmes (Titan, Taj, Vistara) have 20+ years of embedded customer preference; diluting them into a common denominator risks defection.
- Tata's 2012 loyalty card failure reveals structural lesson: without high-frequency touchpoints (now BigBasket/Trent) and premium brand clustering, coalitions collapse.

## Open Question
How can a conglomerate successfully unify vastly different brand tiers and loyalty expectations without cannibalizing individual brand equity and diluting premium positioning?

## India Angle
Tata Group's attempt to build a cross-brand loyalty ecosystem in India faces structural challenges around brand positioning, customer expectations, and organizational silos—a uniquely Indian corporate complexity where heritage brands operate at vastly different premium tiers.
