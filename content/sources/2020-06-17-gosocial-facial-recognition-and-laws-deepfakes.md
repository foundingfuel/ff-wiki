## Core Argument
Decentralization, regulation, and detection technology race to address creator monetization, facial recognition bias, and deepfake authenticity.

## Key Points
- GoSocial demonstrates decentralized model where creators capture value directly from users, bypassing traditional intermediaries.
- Facial recognition tech shows systematic bias against non-white faces; regulation alone cannot fix technology's inherent limitations.
- Deepfake detection currently achieves only 65% accuracy; technological arms race between generation and detection remains unresolved.

## Open Question
Can regulation and institutional checks keep pace with exponential technological capability, or will technology always outrun society's ability to govern it?

## India Angle
Indian creators and content producers can leverage decentralized platforms like GoSocial to monetize directly; facial recognition bias disproportionately affects non-white faces, relevant to India's diverse population; deepfake detection challenges India's ability to combat misinformation at scale.
