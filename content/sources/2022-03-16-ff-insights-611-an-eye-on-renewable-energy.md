## Core Argument
India's renewable energy shift creates opportunities but faces supply chain and overcapacity risks from geopolitical disruptions.

## Key Points
- RIL acquiring lithium battery technology signals conglomerate adaptation to energy transition opportunities
- Russia-Ukraine war drives battery metal prices up, complicating EV affordability and supply chains
- EV two-wheeler capacity projected to exceed demand, risking industry glut by 2026-2030

## Open Question
How can India achieve its 50% renewable energy and job creation targets if supply chain costs make EVs unaffordable and overcapacity emerges before demand justifies it?

## India Angle
India's renewable energy transition creates business opportunities for conglomerates like RIL, but supply chain disruptions from geopolitical crises and overcapacity in EV two-wheelers threaten the 50% renewable energy target by 2030 and projected 3.4 million jobs.
