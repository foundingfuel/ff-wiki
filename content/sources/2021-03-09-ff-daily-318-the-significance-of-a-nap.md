## Core Argument
Simple habits like naps and incremental 'plus one' changes unlock sustained productivity and systemic transformation.

## Key Points
- Strategic 30-minute naps restore cognitive performance and afternoon productivity without requiring major lifestyle overhaul.
- 'Plus one' approach makes systemic change digestible by asking each stakeholder for only one incremental step, compounding into transformation.
- Operational simplicity (routine meals, predictable schedules) frees mental energy for high-impact work and innovation.

## Open Question
How do we scale individual productivity hacks and 'plus one' thinking across large, bureaucratic organizations where norms and rituals resist change?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and founders—operating in high-pressure startup and corporate environments—often neglect rest and recovery; adopting structured nap practices and 'plus one' thinking could improve productivity and system-level innovation in Indian organizations facing resource constraints.
