## Core Argument
Offload memory to systems; use your brain's superior strength—making unexpected connections across ideas.

## Key Points
- Human brains excel at pattern-matching across disparate ideas but fail at retrieval; external note systems compensate.
- Digital tools enable visual knowledge graphs that reveal non-obvious connections humans couldn't spot alone.
- Peak performance requires active reframing of learned concepts in your own context, not passive consumption.

## Open Question
If AI eventually matches human pattern-matching ability, what remains uniquely valuable about human learning and sense-making?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and professionals need better systems to manage learning at scale; relevant for startup founders, corporate leaders, and professionals navigating rapid career transitions in India's competitive economy.
