## Core Argument
Businesses must survive crises through cash conservation and reinvention, then revive by adapting to new normals and securing formalised funding.

## Key Points
- Survive phase requires government support, cash conservation, stakeholder communication; revive through operational adaptation and technology adoption.
- MSMEs face three critical blockers: labour, liquidity, and logistics; formalisation, governance, and transparent business plans unlock banker confidence.
- Rural economy and agriculture thriving; manufacturing struggling; services adapting. Sector-specific strategies needed, not one-size-fits-all recovery approaches.

## Open Question
How can government support unlock liquidity for MSMEs without creating moral hazard in a banking system already burdened with NPAs and regulatory scrutiny?

## India Angle
Indian MSMEs and businesses need government support, banking sector reforms, and operational formalisation to survive Covid-19; rural economy and agriculture present growth opportunities; liquidity and governance challenges are distinctly acute in India's fragmented MSME ecosystem.
