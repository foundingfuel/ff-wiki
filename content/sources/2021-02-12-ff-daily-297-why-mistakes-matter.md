## Core Argument
Mistakes are not failures but essential mechanisms for learning and innovation.

## Key Points
- Understanding historical failures prevents repeating same errors; knowledge of field history is preventive learning.
- High-achieving students often fear mistakes; cultivating error-tolerance unlocks deeper learning and creative breakthroughs.
- Biological evolution proves trial-and-error is non-negotiable for progress; mistakes enable novelty impossible otherwise.

## Open Question
How do we cultivate mistake-embracing culture in high-stakes domains (medicine, aviation, law) where errors have severe consequences?

## India Angle
Indian education systems prioritize error-avoidance and perfection-seeking; reframing mistakes as learning tools could transform student mindset and institutional culture, especially in competitive environments.
