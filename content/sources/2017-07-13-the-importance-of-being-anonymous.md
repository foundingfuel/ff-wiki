## Core Argument
Anonymity protects vulnerable voices but requires guardrails against coordinated disinformation and hidden vested interests.

## Key Points
- Anonymity shields people from physical, financial, and social harm for speaking truth or seeking sensitive information.
- Anonymous accounts can amplify messages beyond reputation biases, but enable coordinated trolling and coordinated disinformation.
- Policy needs to balance protection of dissent against transparency for influential voices shaping public discourse on critical issues.

## Open Question
How can societies protect anonymity for vulnerable whistleblowers and dissidents while ensuring transparency and accountability for organized anonymous campaigns that manipulate public opinion on consequential policies?

## India Angle
Critical for India's evolving privacy law and governance frameworks; directly addresses Aadhaar debate and need for transparency in policy discourse while protecting whistleblowers and vulnerable voices.
