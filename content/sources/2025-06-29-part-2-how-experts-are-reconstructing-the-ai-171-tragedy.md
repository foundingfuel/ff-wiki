## Core Argument
India's AI 171 investigation reveals capacity to lead complex, international aviation safety probes while maintaining blame-free investigative culture.

## Key Points
- AAIB must balance rescue operations with evidence preservation under global ICAO norms and international scrutiny.
- Blame-free investigation approach encourages pilot and engineer reporting, enabling systemic rather than punitive learning.
- International participation (UK, US, Boeing, GE) brings forensic expertise while India retains investigative leadership authority.

## Open Question
How can India maintain a truly blame-free, prevention-focused investigation while managing public demands for accountability and Boeing's reputation management interests?

## India Angle
India's AAIB-led crash investigation tests domestic aviation oversight capacity, international coordination, and whether India can balance thorough investigation with maintaining public trust in Air India and Boeing operations—critical for India's aviation sector growth.
