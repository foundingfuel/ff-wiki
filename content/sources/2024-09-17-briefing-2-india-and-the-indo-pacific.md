## Core Argument
India faces a strategic quandary: integrating into China's supply chains to access global value chains while managing geopolitical rivalry and border tensions.

## Key Points
- Indo-Pacific economies face pressure to choose between US and China amid structural trade dependence on Beijing.
- India's 3% trade share in ASEAN is weak; it must deepen FTA engagement to access emerging supply chain hubs.
- India's trade deficit with China and border tensions create a fundamental tension with supply chain integration imperatives.

## Open Question
How can India integrate into global value chains through closer ties with China-dependent economies without reinforcing its own strategic vulnerability to Beijing?

## India Angle
India must navigate US-China rivalry while addressing its trade deficit with China, low ASEAN trade share (3%), and border security concerns—requiring strategic FTA engagement and supply chain integration without deepening dependencies.
