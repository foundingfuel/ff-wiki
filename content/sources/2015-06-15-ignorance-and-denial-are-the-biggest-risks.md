## Core Argument
Ignorance and denial—not external factors—are entrepreneurs' biggest risks as businesses scale.

## Key Points
- Build humility and treat learning as lifelong; learn from unusual sources across industries.
- Cultivate healthy paranoia and maintain critical insider advisors who challenge without cynicism.
- Institutionalize risk management as formal function: scan, assess, report, educate, mitigate systematically.

## Open Question
How do entrepreneurs maintain the optimism necessary to build while adopting the paranoia necessary to survive?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs scaling businesses must build risk management capabilities as companies grow—critical given India's complex regulatory environment, currency volatility, and interconnected business ecosystems.
