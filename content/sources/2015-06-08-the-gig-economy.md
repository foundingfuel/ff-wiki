## Core Argument
The gig economy atomizes work, enabling Indians to monetize skills flexibly and escape low-wage informal employment.

## Key Points
- India dominates gig platforms (20% of Fiverr users) by offering diverse services from $1–$5 per task.
- Smart arbitrage: buy jobs at $30, resell on Fiverr for $5, pocket $25—no ethical barrier in gig culture.
- Informal workers like domestic help can leap to digital skills, earning $500–$1,000+ monthly, rivaling mid-level IT salaries.

## Open Question
Does the gig economy truly empower marginalized workers or simply offer low-wage micro-tasks that exploit supply-side competition and undercut formal employment standards?

## India Angle
India has 20% of Fiverr's user base—largest globally. The gig economy offers informal workforce like domestic workers and retail clerks a pathway to skill-based, flexible income without geographic constraints, addressing India's employment challenge.
