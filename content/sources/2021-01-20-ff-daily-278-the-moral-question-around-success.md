## Core Argument
Meritocracy generates hubris in winners and resentment in losers, destabilizing democratic societies and eroding solidarity.

## Key Points
- Talent is partly luck; rewarding it as if earned breeds moral hubris among winners and justified resentment among losers.
- Perfect meritocracy erodes humility and shared fate recognition, destroying the solidarity democracies need to function.
- Populist uprisings stem not just from economics but from the psychological harm of being deemed undeserving by merit-based systems.

## Open Question
If pure meritocracy is unjust and corrodes solidarity, what alternative system ensures both fairness and social cohesion without collapsing into nepotism or arbitrariness?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and policymakers must grapple with meritocracy's moral limits as they build institutions and societies; the meritocratic ideal's blind spots could fuel resentment and populism in India's context of unequal opportunity access.
