## Core Argument
Cultural preservation requires institutional scaling beyond individual heroism to ensure sustainability and accessibility.

## Key Points
- Dungarpur shifting from one-man restoration operation to building scalable, team-driven Film Heritage Foundation Centre
- Financial sustainability is critical constraint: film restoration industry must become profitable to survive long-term
- Public access and education strategy (schools, open library, guided tours) aims to build cultural demand and donor base

## Open Question
Can a not-for-profit film restoration ecosystem become financially self-sustaining when the core mission—preserving heritage—has no direct market value?

## India Angle
India's film industry lacks systematic preservation infrastructure despite being world's second-largest producer. Dungarpur's work addresses a critical gap in Indian cultural heritage conservation and demonstrates how entrepreneurial mindset can build institutions where government/market have failed.
