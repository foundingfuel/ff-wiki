## Core Argument
Ethical principles sometimes must yield to achieving greater justice, but the cost to institutional credibility remains unresolved.

## Key Points
- Harold Evans paid for source information despite breaching journalistic ethics to expose corporate negligence and secure compensation.
- The tension between rule-following and outcome-driven action reflects how leaders navigate competing values under pressure.
- Trust depends on predictability of behaviour, yet unpredictability sometimes serves higher moral purposes.

## Open Question
If bending ethical rules to achieve justice is justified, how do institutions prevent erosion of their foundational principles once exceptions become normalized?

## India Angle
Indian journalists, leaders, and professionals face similar ethical trade-offs between institutional integrity and outcomes; relevant to governance, media independence, and business decision-making in contexts with weak institutional safeguards.
