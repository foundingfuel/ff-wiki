## Core Argument
Diversity of mindsets and anthropological perspective drive organizational dynamism and equitable systems.

## Key Points
- Anthropological frameworks reveal how rituals, symbols, and social dynamics shape organizational culture and outcomes.
- Hiring for 'cultural fit' homogenizes thinking; embracing diverse mindsets creates competitive and adaptive organizations.
- Equitable systems—healthcare, media, governance—require architecture centered on individual needs, not privileged access.

## Open Question
How can institutions systematically embed anthropological thinking when existing power structures and tribal patterns actively resist diverse perspectives?

## India Angle
India's challenges in healthcare equity, media ownership concentration, and corporate governance; how anthropological thinking can reshape Indian institutions and policy frameworks toward inclusive systems.
