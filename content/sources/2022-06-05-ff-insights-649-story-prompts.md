## Core Argument
Stories build shared meaning; moral injury demands vulnerable conversation for authentic workplace healing and culture change.

## Key Points
- Story prompts create psychological safety by validating others' experiences and deepening interpersonal bonds.
- Moral injury—trauma from witnessing unethical acts—requires soul care through vulnerable professional dialogue, not self-care alone.
- Leaders must recognize how personal narrative and values alignment determine resilience in high-stakes organizational situations.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically embed storytelling and soul care practices when hierarchical cultures often suppress vulnerability and honest discourse about moral breaches?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces increasingly grapple with moral compromises amid rapid growth; storytelling and soul care practices remain underutilized in Indian corporate culture despite relevance to hierarchical organizational structures.
