## Core Argument
Compassion and self-awareness are essential tools for surviving and transforming when systems break people.

## Key Points
- Reality distortion occurs when success removes all friction and accountability from a person's life.
- Compassion functions as a trainable skill that reduces burnout and enables personal recovery.
- Political and cultural figures often become caricatures of themselves through unchecked power and attention.

## Open Question
How do we extend empathy to privileged figures who've been warped by systems that reward them, while also holding them accountable for harm caused?

## India Angle
Indian cartoonists and satirists have long played a critical role in holding up a mirror to Indian politics and society; RK Laxman's observations about the blurred line between caricature and reality remain acutely relevant to contemporary Indian political discourse and media's role in it.
