## Core Argument
Self-awareness about social media use requires intentional boundaries and honest questioning of underlying motivations.

## Key Points
- Mindlessness is default; understanding motivations enables conscious choice about technology use.
- Predetermined usage times and reflective questions combat impulse-driven social media consumption.
- Validation-seeking, boredom, loneliness, avoidance behaviors drive compulsive tech use patterns.

## Open Question
How do we balance the valid human need for mindless fun and social connection against productivity and presence, without falling into guilt-driven abstinence narratives?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers increasingly grapple with remote work habits and digital addiction; managing social media discipline becomes critical for productivity in distributed work culture growing in Indian tech and startup ecosystems.
