## Core Argument
Tablets with parental controls offer better value than expensive laptops for children's online learning.

## Key Points
- Laptops cost Rs 50-55k minimum; iPads/tablets at Rs 30-35k provide capable alternatives for kids under 12.
- Parental controls on tablets enable secure learning environments without compromising educational access.
- Refurbished/older tablet models further reduce costs while maintaining functionality for online classes.

## Open Question
How do parents balance security and control with digital literacy needs as children grow older and require less restricted access?

## India Angle
Indian parents navigating online schooling during pandemic; device affordability crucial given cost sensitivity (Rs 50k+ laptops vs Rs 30k tablets); parental controls essential in Indian household contexts.
