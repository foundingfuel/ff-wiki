## Core Argument
Outdated capitalism language obscures how modern corporations create intangible value through people, not capital.

## Key Points
- Modern corporations are communities creating intangible value; shareholder primacy and command-and-control structures no longer fit.
- True differentiation is evolutionary and rooted in unique capabilities, not by imitating competitors or copying generic strategies.
- Ethical values must be internalized by firms, not outsourced to ESG departments; trust deficit emerges when products are loved but producers distrusted.

## Open Question
If corporations are communities, not owned entities, who bears accountability for long-term stakeholder interests and whose voice determines strategic direction?

## India Angle
India's positioned at the intersection of industrial and post-industrial economies—a hub for outsourced services and knowledge work. As global firms seek distributed cognitive talent, India must build organizational cultures that move beyond command-and-control toward mediating hierarchies and community-based corporate models.
