## Core Argument
Solving a functional pain point combined with authentic storytelling transforms traditional products into modern status symbols.

## Key Points
- Velcro on veshtis solved slippage problem, making them accessible to new urban customers beyond traditional wearers.
- Founder's personal humiliation at a 5-star hotel motivated perception-change advertising, not just product promotion.
- Ground-level market insight from traveling salesman days revealed demand where industry consensus saw none.

## Open Question
If Nagarajan's business success hinges on changing social perception of traditional dress, can this strategy scale without reinforcing wealth-based status anxiety?

## India Angle
How traditional Indian products (veshti/dhoti) can be rebranded for modern India through functional innovation and storytelling, changing perception among aspirational urban consumers.
