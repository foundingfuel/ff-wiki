## Core Argument
India's mandatory retirement policies are catastrophically misaligned with extending life expectancy and demographic reality.

## Key Points
- Urban Indians age 40+ will likely live to 100, but retire at 60—facing 40 years without income security.
- India will have 330 million seniors in 15 years, yet policymakers haven't adjusted retirement or healthcare infrastructure.
- Biological immortality possible via lifestyle, biomedical research; but poor habits and system failures leave most Indians facing 'massacre' of old age.

## Open Question
If lifespans extend 40+ years post-retirement but pensions and social systems remain unchanged, how will India prevent mass elderly poverty and social collapse?

## India Angle
India faces a demographic time bomb: 120 million seniors in 15 years plus 330 million aged 40+, but policymakers cling to 60/65 retirement ages. Urban Indians live poorly, healthcare systems unprepared, pension systems inadequate for 40-year retirements.
