## Core Argument
True mastery requires unlearning data dependency and trusting intuitive self-belief over external metrics.

## Key Points
- Quantification and data obsession create false control; emotional and intuitive knowledge are critical for genuine learning.
- Institutional structures that apply one-size-fits-all techniques erode people's intuitive capabilities and self-trust.
- Reframing failure as feedback and removing self-imposed mental constraints unlock consistent performance and growth.

## Open Question
How can institutions scale personalized, emotion-aware learning when systems are built on standardization and measurable outcomes?

## India Angle
Applicable to Indian professionals and leaders navigating institutional change—how intuition, self-belief, and personalized learning can counter rigid systems that dominate Indian education and corporate training models.
