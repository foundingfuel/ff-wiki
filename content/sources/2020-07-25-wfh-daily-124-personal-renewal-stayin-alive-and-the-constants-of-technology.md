## Core Argument
Personal renewal and continuous learning throughout life prevents mid-career stagnation and keeps one engaged.

## Key Points
- Mid-career burnout stems from lost purpose; reflection and learning prevent running out of steam.
- Growing up is lifelong; learning in middle years and beyond is as critical as youth learning.
- Founders must add 'values' as a sixth critical risk alongside market, team, technology, product, business model.

## Open Question
How do founders systematically teach and evaluate values-driven entrepreneurship when values are contextual, non-partisan, and geography-dependent?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating mid-career transitions and organizational hierarchies need Gardner's framework for personal renewal; especially relevant in India's intense work culture where burnout and mid-career stagnation are rising concerns.
