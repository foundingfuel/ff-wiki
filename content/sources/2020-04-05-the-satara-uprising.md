## Core Argument
Crisis forces small farmers to discover entrepreneurship, bypass middlemen, earn 40% more through direct-to-home sales.

## Key Points
- Lockdown's labour shortage forced farmers to innovate direct-to-consumer distribution, increasing margins by 40%
- Local administration, social workers, and farmers collaborated to build systems that solved both supply and social distancing problems
- Dr Pol catalysed entrepreneurial mindset by teaching farmers to rely on themselves rather than state support or middlemen

## Open Question
Can this temporary lockdown-driven innovation sustain as a permanent business model once normalcy returns and middlemen regain leverage?

## India Angle
Agricultural supply chain disruption in Maharashtra shows how Indian farmers can bypass middlemen through direct-to-consumer models, enabled by local governance innovation and social entrepreneurship during crises.
