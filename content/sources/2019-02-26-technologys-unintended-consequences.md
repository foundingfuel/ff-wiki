## Core Argument
Technology's positive intent often produces unintended negative consequences requiring systemic checks, not just individual company decisions.

## Key Points
- Tech employees demand ethical alignment between company purpose and work applications, challenging profit-only models.
- Lab-grown meat may solve environmental concerns but risks deepening economic inequality by disrupting small farmers.
- Gene editing creates irreversible unknowns—CCR5 deletion prevents HIV but unexpectedly enhances cognition, exposing ethical gray zones.

## Open Question
Should responsibility for technology's ethical use rest with companies making principled decisions, or with democracies through ballot-box accountability—and what if both fail?

## India Angle
Indian tech companies and startups will face similar employee activism and ethical dilemmas as they scale globally and work with government agencies; requires establishing clear purpose-driven organizational cultures beyond profit maximization.
