## Core Argument
Institutional excellence requires sustained investment in faculty, research, and strategic balance amid competing stakeholder demands.

## Key Points
- Stanford's rise to top-tier status required competitive salaries and investment in research as faculty reputation-builder
- Schools face perpetual tension between teaching excellence, research output, and student outcomes with no permanent solution
- Institutional fragility evident when focus shifts; external shocks (pandemic, rankings drops) expose underlying balance problems

## Open Question
How can educational institutions maintain excellence across teaching, research, and stakeholder satisfaction when these demands are inherently competing and priorities constantly shift?

## India Angle
India's education sector faces similar institutional challenges—balancing research vs. teaching, scaling quality faculty, and competing priorities. The pandemic's impact on school dropouts (60,000 in AP alone) mirrors institutional fragility when pressures mount.
