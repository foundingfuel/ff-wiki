## Core Argument
Reframe rejection as data, personal capital as investable assets, careers as self-owned enterprises.

## Key Points
- Actively seek rejections to normalize 'no' and unlock hidden opportunities through volume.
- Human capital comprises intellectual, social, and emotional resources that erode without continuous renewal.
- Wealth comes from owning unglamorous, durable businesses, not salaries or trendy startups.

## Open Question
If everyone must become their own business owner managing personal capital, who bears responsibility for worker security, skill development, and long-term career progression?

## India Angle
Indian professionals face intense competition and gig-economy pressures; viewing oneself as a business owner and building personal human capital becomes critical for navigating precarity and rapid industry change.
