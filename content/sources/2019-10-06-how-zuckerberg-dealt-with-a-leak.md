## Core Argument
Transparency theater cannot rebuild trust when fundamental ethical issues remain unresolved.

## Key Points
- Internal leak of Zuckerberg's comments signals employee morale erosion despite Facebook's transparency culture.
- Facebook dropped from #1 to #7 on Glassdoor's Best Places to Work due to declining trust and reputation.
- Livestreaming Q&A addresses external perception but fails to resolve deeper credibility gaps on privacy and regulation.

## Open Question
Can a company maintain employee cohesion and external trust when its business model itself is seen as ethically problematic, regardless of how transparent leadership appears?
