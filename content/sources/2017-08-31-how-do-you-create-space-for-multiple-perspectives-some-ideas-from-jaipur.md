## Core Argument
Physical and psychological space design enables authentic dialogue across perspectives, essential for solving India's intractable problems.

## Key Points
- Diverse representation (vocation, age, gender) reveals multiple truths about complex problems.
- Physical setting (flat, open spaces) breaks hierarchical positioning and stereotyping.
- Equal voice principles and listening-over-convincing shifts from positional debate to collaborative problem-solving.

## Open Question
How do you maintain genuine collaboration and mutual responsibility when participants return to their original institutional positions and power structures?

## India Angle
Creating inclusive dialogue forums for solving India's complex governance and corruption challenges requires breaking down positional hierarchies and enabling diverse stakeholders—from farmers to bureaucrats—to collaborate as equals.
