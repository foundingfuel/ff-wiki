## Core Argument
Disruptive technologies emerge from vision plus small incremental steps, not timelines or hype.

## Key Points
- Mars colonization viability depends on sustained technical progress, not ambitious deadlines alone.
- VR/AR's real potential lies in mixed reality applications (education, consultation, travel) not current device specs.
- Incumbent business models resist technological disruption because they misjudge adoption curves and cost trajectories.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between visionary ambition (Musk) and unrealistic hype (Mars One) when both make bold claims about the same frontier?

## India Angle
India's role in emerging tech ecosystems: VR/AR education applications for Indian students (mentioned Bengaluru example), energy storage implications for India's renewable transition, and whether Indian entrepreneurs can compete in space and deep tech sectors.
