## Core Argument
India must shift from outsourcing services to building innovative software products to become an economic powerhouse.

## Key Points
- India's IT sector ($167B) is dominated by low-margin services; few unicorns are true product innovators.
- National Policy on Software Products officially recognizes products as distinct from services, enabling focused support.
- Government initiatives include product registries, incubators, talent development, and preferential procurement to catalyze ecosystem.

## Open Question
How can policy-level support overcome the deeper cultural and talent incentives that have historically pulled India toward service-arbitrage business models rather than innovation risk-taking?

## India Angle
India dominates IT services and has unicorns, yet lacks world-beating software products. The National Policy on Software Products addresses this structural gap by differentiating products from services—critical for India's transition from a service-provider economy to an innovation leader.
