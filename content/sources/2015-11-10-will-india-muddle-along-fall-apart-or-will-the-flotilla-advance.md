## Core Argument
India's future hinges on whether leadership executes structural reforms or governance degrades into policy paralysis.

## Key Points
- Three plausible futures: Flotilla Advances (7.8% growth via reform), Muddling Along (6% with weak reforms), Falling Apart (4.8% via policy failure).
- Economists miss social-political forces; past projections (Soviet Union, Japan) failed because models ignore institutional dynamics.
- Success requires decentralization, fiscal discipline, tax reform (DTC/GST), and investment climate—not just macro aggregates.

## Open Question
If economists consistently underestimate socio-political forces, how reliable are even carefully modeled scenarios in predicting which outcome India will face?

## India Angle
India's economic trajectory depends critically on policy reform implementation and governance capacity. The three scenarios model how India's institutional and political choices determine whether it achieves 7.8% or merely 4.8% growth—a distinction affecting hundreds of millions.
