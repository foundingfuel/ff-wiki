## Core Argument
AI adoption is behavioural, not technical—experiment relentlessly to find problems it solves for you.

## Key Points
- Start with your own search history and problems; don't worry about model differences—just experiment.
- Use AI for drafting, automation, and code generation; examples and iteration beat perfect prompts.
- Older generations and vulnerable populations need AI literacy to recognize deepfakes, scams, and voice cloning.

## Open Question
How do we enable older populations to benefit from AI's personalization potential while protecting them from AI-enabled scams and deepfakes?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organizations need AI literacy urgently—not just for competitive advantage, but to protect against rising AI-enabled scams targeting older generation and less tech-savvy populations. Critical for India's aging demographic and digital divide.
