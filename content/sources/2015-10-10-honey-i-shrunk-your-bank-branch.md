## Core Argument
Banks' physical branch model is obsolete; mobile becomes the 4x5-inch branch, making traditional banking economics uncompetitive.

## Key Points
- Fintech startups and telecom payment banks threaten traditional banking with 100M+ user bases versus 5M app downloads.
- RBI regulatory opening and zero-fee competition model expose banking's unsustainable margin structure.
- Mobile phone becomes the functional equivalent of a branch; consolidation and tech-led transformation are inevitable.

## Open Question
If consolidation is inevitable but government keeps recapitalizing weak banks, how do zombie institutions actually exit the market without systemic collapse?

## India Angle
Indian banks face acute disruption risk from mobile-first fintech players and payment wallets; regulatory opening via RBI is accelerating competition; branch-heavy model unsuited to India's smartphone penetration and tier 2/3 growth
