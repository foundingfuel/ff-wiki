## Core Argument
Rebuild social capital by finding common ground across ideological divides rather than winning arguments.

## Key Points
- Disconnection in polarized societies weakens citizen ability to hold institutions accountable.
- Shared moral foundation on care exists across tribes; start dialogue from there, not debate.
- Online learning dismantles holistic education models; children suffer isolation and loss of play-based discovery.

## Open Question
How can individuals bridge tribal divides when structural incentives (algorithms, media) actively amplify polarization?

## India Angle
India's deep social divisions along caste, religion, and ideology require deliberate bridge-building; polarization undermines collective action needed for development. Online education crisis disproportionately affects India's alternative school movement and working parents in metros.
