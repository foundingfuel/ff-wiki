## Core Argument
Indian beauty rituals become global luxury brand by bridging diaspora nostalgia with Western clean-beauty trends.

## Key Points
- Aavrani packages traditional Indian beauty ingredients (turmeric, neem, coconut oil) as premium US skincare, capturing $1M+ revenue by 2019
- Natural skincare growing 30% year-over-year in $18B US market; large companies acquiring authentic small brands rather than investing in R&D
- First-generation Indian-American founder leveraged personal pain point (inaccessible traditional rituals) and diaspora identity to tap white space in prestige market

## Open Question
How can Aavrani maintain authenticity and growth while scaling globally, especially as large multinationals increasingly acquire natural skincare brands?
