## Core Argument
Aadhaar exclusion stems from policy misuse, not design; platform enables digital inclusion if properly governed.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar is identity infrastructure, not policy itself—usage decisions belong to government, not UIDAI.
- India Stack (UPI, eKYC, DigiLocker) built on Aadhaar enables non-asset credit and market reach to excluded populations.
- Federated data architecture prevents profiling; urgent need for privacy law and policy checks-and-balances.

## Open Question
How can Aadhaar scale financial inclusion benefits while preventing mission creep and mandatory linkages that erode consent and privacy?

## India Angle
Aadhaar as foundational infrastructure for India's digital economy—enabling financial inclusion, reducing leakages in benefit delivery, and creating scalable platforms (India Stack) for reaching unserved populations. Critical for deepening credit markets and reducing asset-based lending barriers.
