## Core Argument
Superior problem-solving requires empathetic observation of others' patterns before acting on insights.

## Key Points
- Agassi won by observing Becker's serving tic across multiple matches before exploiting it strategically.
- Problem-solving in business requires perceiving reality through others' perspectives, not just your own lens.
- Legacy brands survive transitions by combining desirability with availability and showing up consistently.

## Open Question
How do you gather competitive intelligence through deep observation without revealing your knowledge, and when is the right moment to act on hidden insights?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and entrepreneurs can apply Agassi's problem-solving philosophy—deep observation, empathy, and pattern recognition—to navigate competitive markets and understand customer behavior, as illustrated by Reliance Retail's strategy around legacy brands during crises.
