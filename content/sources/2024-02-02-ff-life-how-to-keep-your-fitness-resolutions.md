## Core Argument
Fitness resolutions succeed through internal motivation and six-month habit formation, not January 1 declarations.

## Key Points
- January 1 start dates fail because they ignore physiological readiness and individual energy cycles.
- Six months minimum required to move from action stage to maintenance stage where habits self-sustain.
- Exercise purpose is functional capability—strength, mobility, endurance—not weight loss or appearance.

## Open Question
If most resolutions fail within weeks, why does the gym industry continue marketing January 1 campaigns rather than addressing the psychological barriers to sustained behavior change?

## India Angle
Indian middle-class longevity is increasing, making fitness critical for quality of life in later years; cultural resistance to gym culture and individual accountability patterns are uniquely Indian phenomena.
