## Core Argument
History shows pandemics reshape institutions, markets, and cultures; humility from immigrant experiences drives transformational leadership.

## Key Points
- Pandemics trigger long-term institutional change: 14th-century plague led to European public health frameworks still used today.
- Indian immigrant CEOs chosen for tech leadership because they bring humility and cultural sensitivity absent in previous leaders.
- Wealth inequality and labour markets shift post-pandemic: medieval Europe saw real wage rises and changed demographic patterns.

## Open Question
If Indian immigrant experiences produce better, more humble leaders, what happens to their leadership effectiveness when they're no longer outsiders—does the humility persist or erode with institutional power?

## India Angle
Indian-born tech leaders are reshaping global companies through immigrant humility and inclusive culture; India should examine what this reveals about leadership development and cultural export. Also relevant: crypto policy implications for India's financial systems.
