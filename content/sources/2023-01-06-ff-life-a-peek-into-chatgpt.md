## Core Argument
ChatGPT's value multiplies when constrained with creative prompts, not open-ended queries.

## Key Points
- Adding constraints and context to prompts unlocks ChatGPT's creative potential beyond generic outputs.
- AI can simulate difficult conversations and provide coaching before real-world workplace interactions.
- ChatGPT replaces search engines for accessible, contextual explanations suited to specific audiences.

## Open Question
How do workers balance using AI to rehearse difficult workplace conversations without eroding authentic human negotiation skills and trust-building?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organizations can leverage ChatGPT for skill development, workplace problem-solving, and creative content generation—addressing productivity gaps in a competitive economy while navigating work-life balance challenges common in Indian workplaces.
