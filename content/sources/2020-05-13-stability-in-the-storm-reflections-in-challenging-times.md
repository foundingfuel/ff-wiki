## Core Argument
Crisis leadership requires stepping back to reflect, listen without judgment, and reframe narrative from victimization to meaning-making.

## Key Points
- Traditional tools like scenario planning fail in unprecedented uncertainty; leaders must develop new playbooks.
- Thriving leaders use chaos to reflect, challenge teams, and reimagine business models rather than merely react.
- Clarity of thought and adaptability distinguish leaders; listening deeply to employee struggles drives organizational decisions.

## Open Question
How can leaders balance operational urgency with the reflective space needed for genuine meaning-making when organizational pressures demand immediate action?

## India Angle
Indian leaders navigating unprecedented crises (pandemic, economic volatility) need new mental models beyond traditional scenario planning; organizational listening and empathy become competitive advantages in managing distributed teams and diverse employee circumstances.
