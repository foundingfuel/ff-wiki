## Core Argument
Paytm's success lies in obsessive user acquisition and data extraction from India's underserved masses, not transaction value.

## Key Points
- Volume over value: 1,000 less-active users generate more data than 100 high-value users
- Data-driven experimentation: Bus insurance uncovered Monday-Wednesday travel patterns of middle India
- Platform thinking: Digital wallet as entry point to India Stack ecosystem for financial services expansion

## Open Question
How can a company optimize for user volume and micro-transaction data gathering while maintaining regulatory compliance, especially when policy violations lead to app removal?

## India Angle
Paytm's playbook specifically exploits India's middle and bottom-of-pyramid demographics, leveraging the India Stack ecosystem and deep understanding of Indian consumer behavior patterns—a distinctly Indian growth strategy that wouldn't translate uniformly elsewhere.
