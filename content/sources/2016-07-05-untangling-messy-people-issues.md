## Core Argument
Organizations misapply machine-logic to complex people problems; complexity requires pattern-sensing and stories, not surveys and hypotheses.

## Key Points
- Cynefin framework distinguishes simple, complicated, and complex domains requiring different problem-solving approaches.
- Surveys and hypotheses-driven methods fail for complex people issues due to confirmation bias and limited team understanding.
- Stories and pattern recognition are better tools for understanding culture, trust, leadership, and organizational behavior.

## Open Question
How do leaders systematically scale insights from pattern-sensing and narrative approaches without reverting to measurable, repeatable processes that feel safer but fail?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often import Western management frameworks without adapting to local context; this applies to consulting firms, tech companies, and large enterprises adopting standardized engagement surveys and change programs that fail to account for India's diverse, relationship-driven workplace cultures.
