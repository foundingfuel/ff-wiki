## Core Argument
Naming conventions reveal ideological shifts; transitions expose hidden costs of change.

## Key Points
- Digital-age organizations reject formality; names signal cultural rebellion against institutional authority structures.
- Green energy transition creates new humanitarian crises: cobalt mining exploitation mirrors colonial-era resource extraction patterns.
- Pakistan's military consolidation amid political chaos poses nuclear stability risks India must actively monitor.

## Open Question
How can India pursue climate goals (requiring rare minerals) without perpetuating exploitation in Congo and Xinjiang, or becoming dependent on supply chains controlled by adversaries?

## India Angle
Pakistan's political instability and nuclear risk directly threatens India's security; India must monitor US-Pakistan-China dynamics as US pivots away from Afghanistan. Climate transition creates mineral dependency risks (cobalt, rare earths) that affect India's green ambitions.
