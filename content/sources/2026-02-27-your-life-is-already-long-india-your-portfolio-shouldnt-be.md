## Core Argument
Indians are already long India through income and assets; portfolio concentration amplifies undiversifiable risk.

## Key Points
- Home-country bias combined with India-linked income, housing, and employment creates concentrated exposure to single economy.
- Being bullish on India is sentiment, not asset allocation; country and equity returns diverge over cycles.
- Global diversification is risk governance, not a view on India, and reduces dependence on single geopolitical outcome.

## Open Question
How do investors reconcile emotional attachment to India's growth story with the mathematical case for global diversification without feeling they're abandoning domestic opportunity?

## India Angle
Indians face unique concentration risk by earning, living, and investing entirely in India; understanding global diversification is critical for Indian investors given rupee depreciation trends, geopolitical exposure, and the limits of India's market-cap share globally.
