## Core Argument
Scale creates durable competitive moats through informational advantages, but only if underpinned by ethical governance.

## Key Points
- TV advertising's high cost created scale advantages for already-large brands like P&G, blocking smaller competitors from accessing effective channels.
- Brand recognition functions as informational advantage—consumers prefer known brands over unknown alternatives despite price differences.
- Governance failures stem from promoter choices; no auditor or board has complete transaction visibility; investors pay 25% premium for good governance.

## Open Question
Can scale-driven competitive advantages be sustained in digital markets where information asymmetry is lower, or does the moat simply shift to different dimensions?

## India Angle
Indian businesses must recognize how scale advantages in media and branding create competitive moats; governance failures like IL&FS and Satyam show cost of shortcuts taken by promoters, particularly relevant as Indian companies scale globally.
