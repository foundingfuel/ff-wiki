## Core Argument
Indian e-commerce at crossroads: burn quality matters more than quantity; structural costs demand different economics than global models.

## Key Points
- Burn's value depends on whether it builds foundations (market share, infrastructure, habits) or merely covers overhead
- India's logistics costs 3-4X higher than USA due to fuel, truck costs, road quality—changes break-even math fundamentally
- Flipkart's 'chaos' (3 CEOs, valuation drop) may be disciplined turnaround, not failure—separating signal from noise critical

## Open Question
Can Indian e-commerce founders accept that 'best for company' (disciplined capital efficiency, strategic sales) may not be best for founders' ambitions or ego?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce startups face unique structural disadvantages (3-4X higher logistics costs than USA) requiring different burn strategies; crossroads moment will determine which models survive India's specific cost structure and market conditions.
