## Core Argument
Technology amplifies human behavior; founders must address their own attitudes, not blame tools.

## Key Points
- Technology doesn't cause poor judgment—it accelerates and magnifies existing behavioral patterns.
- Cognitive biases like fundamental attribution error and Dunning-Kruger effect drive poor decisions more than circumstances.
- Self-awareness and humility—not technological fixes—are essential for leadership in transparent, connected environments.

## Open Question
How can high-growth startup cultures that reward decisiveness and confidence also cultivate the humility and self-reflection needed to avoid behavioral missteps in public forums?

## India Angle
Indian startup founders often operate in high-pressure environments with limited institutional checks; understanding behavioral biases is critical for India's emerging entrepreneurial class to avoid self-sabotage in the age of social media amplification.
