## Core Argument
Work-from-home dissolves boundaries that protected women's identity, threatening gender equity progress unless home responsibilities are genuinely redistributed.

## Key Points
- WFH collapses the commute-as-transition space women used to compartmentalize work and domestic identities, intensifying dual burden stress.
- Pandemic threatens 'pipeline problem' in corporate leadership; one-in-five working mothers considering workforce exit versus 11% of fathers.
- Corporate flexibility measures (remote work, flexible schedules) benefit equity only if paired with cultural shifts in household responsibility sharing.

## Open Question
Corporate structures are adapting to enable flexibility, but Indian family hierarchies remain rigid—can workplace policies alone drive gender equity without simultaneous transformation of home-based social hierarchies?

## India Angle
Indian women face compounded pressure from hardcoded domestic roles in family hierarchies and traditions, exacerbated by work-from-home blurring boundaries; Indian tech companies like TCS are adapting infrastructure but cultural shifts lag behind structural changes
