## Core Argument
Nuclear fusion transitions from theoretical research to mission mode after decades of obscurity.

## Key Points
- EuroFusion's 59-megajoule record proves fusion sustainability possible, shifting from science to engineering phase.
- Recent breakthroughs suggest previously intractable fusion problems nearing resolution after 40+ years of stagnation.
- Fusion offers clean, limitless energy without fission's radioactive waste and catastrophic accident risks.

## Open Question
How quickly can fusion transition from lab success to scalable commercial power plants, and will it arrive in time to meaningfully impact climate and energy crises?

## India Angle
India faces severe energy poverty and grid instability; nuclear fusion breakthrough could revolutionize energy access without radioactive waste concerns that plague fission. Critical for India's climate commitments and reducing fossil fuel dependence.
