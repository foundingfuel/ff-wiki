## Core Argument
Lean methodology and alternative business models empower resource-constrained entrepreneurs and media companies.

## Key Points
- Lean startup principles translate into practical guides for leaders managing constraints and testing assumptions systematically.
- Removing money constraints forces creative specialization and reveals genuine customer value beyond monetization tactics.
- Podcasts and direct audience relationships create loyal communities that traditional ad-driven media struggles to build.

## Open Question
How can media companies maintain editorial independence and user trust while relying on platform hosting and algorithmic distribution controlled by tech giants?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and media companies face similar capital scarcity and audience fragmentation challenges; lean methodologies and alternative media models (podcasts, community-driven platforms) offer pathways for bootstrapped growth in a resource-constrained environment
