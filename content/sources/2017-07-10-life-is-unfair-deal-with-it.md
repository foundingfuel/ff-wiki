## Core Argument
History repeats itself; competition is inevitable; businesses failing to anticipate systemic shifts face obsolescence.

## Key Points
- History demonstrates that competition and conflict are biological imperatives, not anomalies
- Indian IT companies missed anticipating Trump-era protectionism and political narrative shifts
- Nationalist rhetoric and new leadership emerge when societies reach tipping points, reshaping business narratives

## Open Question
If competition and cycles are inevitable and predictable through history, why do intelligent executives still fail to anticipate them?
