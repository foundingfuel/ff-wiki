## Core Argument
Ms Marvel is a cultural watershed for marginalized representation; algorithmic suppression keeps India unaware of its significance.

## Key Points
- Marvel took unprecedented risks: non-white female lead from 8-year-old IP with debut actress, sidestepping fetishization for authentic desi storytelling.
- Critical acclaim (96% on Rotten Tomatoes) vastly exceeds viewership, suggesting coordinated suppression rather than organic audience rejection.
- Show's unapologetic desi references (SRK, ammi, family dynamics) make it culturally specific yet globally marginalized by algorithm and prejudice.

## Open Question
If mainstream media genuinely shapes societal perceptions about marginalized groups, why does the media industry itself systematically suppress stories that could achieve this transformation?

## India Angle
Ms Marvel represents a watershed moment for South Asian and Muslim representation in mainstream global media, directly relevant to how Indians consume and are represented in Western pop culture—yet remains invisible to Indian audiences despite starring a character of subcontinental origin.
