## Core Argument
Smart gadgets can solve everyday friction points in time-constrained, hybrid-work and screen-heavy lives.

## Key Points
- Mesh Wi-Fi, drones, sleep aids, smart mugs, and health trackers address specific pain points in modern living.
- Drones shifted from banned to IPL use case through creative problem-solving by startups, enabling new applications.
- Smart devices collect data and automate comfort (temperature, sleep, health metrics) to free cognitive load.

## Open Question
Do convenience gadgets address root causes of time scarcity and poor sleep, or merely manage symptoms while lifestyle remains unsustainable?
