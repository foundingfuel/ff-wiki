## Core Argument
Speed without direction is meaningless; velocity requires direction, courage, and human-centric thinking in digital transformation.

## Key Points
- Velocity means maintaining speed while adhering to direction—avoid meaningless movement at high speed.
- Digital tools are means, not ends; human connection and serving people must guide technology choices.
- Inclusion and decency in workplaces aren't luxuries—they drive both moral and business outcomes for Indian companies.

## Open Question
How do Indian organisations balance the urgency of digital velocity with the patience required for meaningful cultural transformation like LGBTQ inclusion?
