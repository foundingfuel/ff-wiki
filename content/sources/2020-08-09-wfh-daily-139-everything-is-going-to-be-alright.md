## Core Argument
Perspective and self-awareness through meditation can reframe overwhelm into manageable resilience during crisis.

## Key Points
- Mahon's poem offers sincere hope amidst real crises, not denial—perspective as active practice, not naive optimism.
- Meditation forces awareness of body and mind without judgment, revealing disconnection from self beyond career metrics.
- Small consistent habits (10-minute daily meditation) build momentum; imperfection and distraction are features, not bugs.

## Open Question
If perspective is what matters most during crisis, can an app-guided experience of nature and self truly deliver it, or does it risk becoming another productivity hack that misses the point?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers struggling with overwork and burnout during pandemic/WFH transition need practical tools for mindfulness and sustainable productivity—meditation apps represent accessible wellness infrastructure for Indian professionals.
