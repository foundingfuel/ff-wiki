## Core Argument
Founders are life hackers who reject conformist consumption, embodying entrepreneurial ethos that liberates from inherited cultural pressures.

## Key Points
- Startup culture valorizes frugality and resourcefulness over status consumption, creating space for non-conformist behaviour
- Middle-class Indian upbringing creates 'hangover of wants' that drives ambition but also social pressure to consume conspicuously
- Modern founders like those at Byju's, FreeCharge maintain authentic life practices regardless of success, contrasting with corporate conformism

## Open Question
Does startup culture genuinely free people from consumption pressures, or does it simply replace one form of status-seeking with another (frugality as performative identity)?

## India Angle
Indian founders and startup culture offer an alternative to conformist corporate culture prevalent in Indian metros, challenging inherited consumption patterns shaped by class anxiety and inequality visible in cities like Mumbai.
