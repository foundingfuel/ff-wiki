## Core Argument
Sustainability must be core to business model design, not retrofitted; 45 patterns enable systematic integration.

## Key Points
- Sustainability drives efficiency, cost reduction, and revenue while improving talent acquisition and positioning.
- 45 reusable patterns across three types help firms embed sustainability into strategy, not as afterthought.
- Patterns are modular and combinable; no hierarchy exists—flexibility to address specific business challenges.

## Open Question
How can firms balance competing sustainability imperatives when patterns may conflict, and how do they prioritize which patterns to implement first without a hierarchy?

## India Angle
Indian firms face acute resource scarcity and regulatory pressures; embedding sustainability into business models from inception helps compete globally, attract talent, and comply with emerging ESG norms—critical for India's manufacturing and services sectors.
