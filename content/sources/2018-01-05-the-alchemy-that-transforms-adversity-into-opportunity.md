## Core Argument
India's frugal innovation mindset—doing better with less—can become a global competitive advantage and model.

## Key Points
- Frugal innovation solves fundamental problems first (e.g., fridges without electricity), not incremental conveniences.
- India's cultural ethos of frugality and sustainability is a structural advantage compared to US diversity or China's scale mentality.
- Three pillars emerging: sharing economy, maker/DIY economy, circular economy—all powered by human energy and ingenuity.

## Open Question
How can India systematically scale and commercialise frugal innovations beyond pockets of excellence when education systems, incentives, and organisational cultures still favour traditional R&D and Western models?

## India Angle
India's cultural advantage of frugality and sustainability positions it to develop and export a new innovation playbook that the West will adopt. India's scale, diversity, and resource constraints make it a natural laboratory for frugal innovation—from affordable ECG machines to regenerative solutions—which can address both local and global problems.
