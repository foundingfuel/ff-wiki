## Core Argument
Organizations must rebuild trust and reimagine work as young workers reject traditional offices; technology will evolve faster than human adaptation.

## Key Points
- Generational tension inevitable: younger workers expect flexible work; senior managers controlling decisions prefer traditional offices.
- Trust, not surveillance, is the foundation for evaluating remote work performance; mistrust is now the emotional default.
- Current communication tech (Zoom, Teams) are crude prototypes; future tools will dramatically reshape how we collaborate virtually.

## Open Question
How do organizations accelerate cultural trust-building when structural power lies with older managers whose retirement timeline is 5-10 years, but generational expectations demand change now?

## India Angle
Indian organizations face acute generational management tensions as younger, digitally-native workforces challenge traditional office-centric models; trust-building across hierarchies critical for competitive advantage in hybrid work era.
