## Core Argument
Hybrid work demands leaders shift from hierarchical to inspirational leadership; wisdom from aging teaches intentionality over regret.

## Key Points
- Hybrid workforces require leaders to adapt behaviours—inspirational leadership for remote workers, in-person presence for onsite teams.
- Older adults emphasize honesty, seizing opportunities, and expressing feelings now as keys to avoiding lifelong regret.
- Scarcity of attention and resources causes 'tunnelling'—leaders make costly long-term decisions by focusing only on immediate problems.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance the need for flexibility in hybrid models with the human requirement for deep, in-person relational bonds that build institutional trust and culture?

## India Angle
Indian organizations transitioning to hybrid work models need to reimagine leadership practices; elder wisdom on integrity and intentional living offers guidance for building trust-based cultures amid rapid change.
