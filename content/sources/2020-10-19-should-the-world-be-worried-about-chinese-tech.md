## Core Argument
China's state-led AI superpower threatens global digital sovereignty and civil liberties through data dominance.

## Key Points
- China's top-down innovation model differs fundamentally from Silicon Valley's product-first approach, enabling rapid data accumulation.
- Chinese tech giants control massive data flows globally via apps, payments, and gaming, creating surveillance and espionage risks.
- China's AI algorithms risk embedding past discrimination into systems used for facial recognition and governance, compromising civil rights.

## Open Question
How can democracies balance the innovation benefits and consumer convenience of Chinese platforms against legitimate security and privacy concerns without triggering a fragmented digital world?

## India Angle
India must understand China's state-backed innovation model and data-control strategies to protect its own digital sovereignty, especially as Chinese apps and payment systems expand in emerging markets where India also competes.
