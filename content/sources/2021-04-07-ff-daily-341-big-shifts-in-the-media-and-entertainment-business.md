## Core Argument
India's media-entertainment sector is undergoing structural transformation; digital subscriptions and gaming emerging as growth engines.

## Key Points
- Digital ad revenues overtook print for first time in 2020; print advertising fell 41%, circulation down 21%.
- 28 million Indians paid for subscriptions in 2020 (vs 10.5M in 2019); 284M accessed paywalled content via data bundles.
- Online gaming became fastest-growing segment, reaching 360M homes despite adverse state-level regulations.

## Open Question
How can regulatory fragmentation across states (e.g., gaming restrictions) be reconciled with the need for scaled, pan-India digital platforms to compete globally?

## India Angle
Indian media & entertainment sector contracted 24% in 2020; structural shifts emerging—print advertising down 41%, digital overtaking print for first time, 28M Indians now paying for subscriptions (vs 10.5M in 2019), online gaming reaching 360M homes despite regulatory headwinds in some states.
