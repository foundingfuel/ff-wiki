## Core Argument
India's startup crash stems from chasing mega-funding and Western models, not market potential—reset enables smarter, India-centric businesses.

## Key Points
- Mega-funding killed innovation and hunger; created unfocused teams lacking product-market fit discipline.
- Startups targeted only India One (top 15%) assuming extrapolation to 1.25B; structural error in TAM understanding.
- Capital inefficiency endemic—e-tailers burned $3.5B generating $400M in commissions; profitability now forces core model testing.

## Open Question
If India's digital economy potential is real and market opportunities haven't disappeared, what fundamentally different business model and founder mindset prevents repeating the same mega-funding trap?

## India Angle
Indian startups blindly copied Western models targeting only top 15% of market (India One), ignoring 85% of population. Success requires business models that serve India's actual economic structure and purchasing power tiers.
