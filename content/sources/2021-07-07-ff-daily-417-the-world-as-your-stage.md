## Core Argument
Expanding your vision of impact—your 'stage'—determines what opportunities you'll see and achieve.

## Key Points
- Vision precedes opportunity identification; growing your intended stage unlocks possibilities invisible at smaller scales.
- Remote work productivity increases with trust-based leadership, not surveillance; organizational culture must be fundamentally reimagined.
- Efficiency creates fragility; some inefficiency (slack, cash reserves) is protective insurance against inevitable disruptions.

## Open Question
How do you balance the need for vision expansion with the discomfort and unknowns that come with stretching to larger stages—especially when confidence must follow experience, not precede it?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and professionals face similar stage-expansion decisions; ICICI Lombard's hybrid work model offers India-specific case study for post-pandemic workplace transformation and leadership reimagining.
