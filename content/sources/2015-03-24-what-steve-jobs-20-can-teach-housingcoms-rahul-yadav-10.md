## Core Argument
Genius without people skills works only temporarily; transformational leaders must evolve from disruption to stewardship.

## Key Points
- Steve Jobs 1.0 succeeded through vision and technical mastery despite poor people management; Jobs 2.0 learned empathy and listening from Ed Catmull at Pixar.
- Indian entrepreneurs and VCs celebrate Rahul Yadav's aggression as disruptive genius, missing that Jobs' abrasiveness was a flaw he had to outgrow.
- Sustainable leadership requires balancing conceptual skills and technical excellence with people management—the incomplete narrative can damage teams and culture.

## Open Question
Can disruptive genius and people-centric leadership coexist, or must founders choose between game-changing vision and team cohesion?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs like Rahul Yadav are emulating Steve Jobs 1.0's abrasiveness without learning his 2.0 transformation—a critical gap for building sustainable Indian companies and ecosystems.
