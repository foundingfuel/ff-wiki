## Core Argument
US military withdrawal from Afghanistan signals potential shift in global power dynamics and strategic reliability.

## Key Points
- US demonstrated unprecedented air coordination and technological superiority in 2001-2002 but failed to sustain long-term strategic objectives.
- Afghanistan withdrawal raises questions about American commitment to allied nations and capability to project power globally.
- India must reassess defence partnerships and regional security strategy amid shifting geopolitical alignments.

## Open Question
If advanced military technology and coordination cannot secure sustained strategic outcomes, what determines actual geopolitical power in 21st century conflicts?
