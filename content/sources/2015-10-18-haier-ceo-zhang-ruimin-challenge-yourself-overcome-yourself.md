## Core Argument
Proactive organizational reinvention through internal micro-enterprises flattens hierarchies and embeds customer-driven innovation.

## Key Points
- Benchmark against global leaders to expose shortcomings early; compete to learn, not just to win.
- Transform organizational structure before crisis forces it; bigger companies face harder restructuring later.
- Change mindset first, then structure; convince people necessity precedes comfort in volatile markets.

## Open Question
How does a CEO maintain strategic coherence and accountability when a 70,000-person organization fragments into competing micro-enterprises with conflicting incentives?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates and large manufacturers face similar rigidity challenges; Zhang's model of internal micro-enterprises and proactive reinvention offers a blueprint for companies like Bajaj, Mahindra, or Godrej navigating digital disruption and e-commerce threats.
