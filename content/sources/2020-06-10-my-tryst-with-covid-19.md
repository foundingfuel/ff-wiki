## Core Argument
Early symptom detection and oxygen monitoring are critical; stressed healthcare systems demand community-level preparedness solutions.

## Key Points
- Silent hypoxia—low oxygen without breathlessness—killed more than visible symptoms; pulse oximeters became life-saving diagnostic tools.
- India's healthcare system under severe stress; citizens must proactively arrange oxygen cylinders and concentrators rather than rely on hospitals.
- Early detection and rapid medical intervention prevent complications; community pooling for medical equipment protects entire neighborhoods.

## Open Question
How should responsibility for pandemic response be divided between underfunded public health systems and individuals who can afford private preparedness when millions cannot?

## India Angle
Exposes critical gaps in India's healthcare system during COVID-19: shortage of equipped beds, trained workers, and unclear protocols. Advocates for individual preparedness (pulse oximeters, oxygen concentrators) as workaround when public health infrastructure fails.
