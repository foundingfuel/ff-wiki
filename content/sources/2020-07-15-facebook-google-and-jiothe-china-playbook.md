## Core Argument
Global tech giants adopt Chinese indirect investment playbook to navigate India's regulatory landscape and market realities.

## Key Points
- FAANG copying BAT's strategy: investing in Indian companies rather than direct expansion to mitigate regulatory risk.
- Jio investments expected to create exit opportunities for Indian VCs, expanding startup funding pool and ecosystem robustness.
- Google balances geopolitical pressures (US regulation) with India's digitization goals, gaining government alignment and protection.

## Open Question
Can mega-investments in Jio simultaneously strengthen India's startup ecosystem or will they consolidate power and capital away from independent founders?

## India Angle
FAANG's shift to indirect investments (through Jio, local partners) mirrors China's BAT strategy—protecting against Indian regulatory protectionism while accessing India's massive digital market. This shapes India's startup ecosystem funding patterns and tech sovereignty.
