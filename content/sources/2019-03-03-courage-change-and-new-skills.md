## Core Argument
Digital transformation demands courage to change, new skills, and timeless character to lead effectively.

## Key Points
- Digital technology enables multiple ways of organising and thinking, requiring leaders to abandon 'one best way' mentality.
- Courage, change capability, and continuous skill acquisition are essential for harnessing digital transformation at scale.
- Strong character and values remain timeless anchors even as organisations adapt to rapid technological change.

## Open Question
How can organisations maintain timeless character and values while simultaneously embracing continuous digital disruption and reinvention?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations must develop courage to embrace digital transformation and acquire new skills to compete globally; relevant for both startups and large enterprises navigating India's digital-first economy.
