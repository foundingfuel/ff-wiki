## Core Argument
Great leadership transcends technical skill; it requires moral power and character that inspires across cultures and centuries.

## Key Points
- Lincoln's greatness lay in moral power and character, not military or political skill—he inspired reverence globally.
- Two forms of passion: unwavering excellence (father's billiards mastery) vs. adaptive impact-driven reinvention (mother's design-to-education pivot).
- Unbundling systems (OCEN for credit, UPI for payments) reveals new possibilities for solving infrastructure problems.

## Open Question
How do we cultivate and recognize moral leadership in modern institutions when technical expertise and political maneuvering often dominate?

## India Angle
India needs leaders with Lincoln's moral authority and character-driven vision; OCEN example shows how unbundling systems (like credit) can democratize access—critical for India's financial inclusion and governance challenges.
