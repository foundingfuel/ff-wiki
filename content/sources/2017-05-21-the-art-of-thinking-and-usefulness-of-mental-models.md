## Core Argument
Simple, fit-for-purpose mental models outperform complex ones in solving real problems.

## Key Points
- Complexity doesn't equal usefulness; paper planes teach flying physics better than detailed model planes.
- Strategists succeed by using multiple mental models as tools, not seeking one perfect model.
- Fundamentals and simplicity—from golf to business—provide reliable foundations for mastery.

## Open Question
How do leaders know which simple model to apply to which problem without oversimplifying real-world complexity?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders often face complexity paralysis; mastering simple, purpose-built mental models can accelerate decision-making in fast-moving markets and resource-constrained environments.
