## Core Argument
Evolutionary biology offers investing wisdom: prioritize survival over returns through diversification and accept perpetual market cycles.

## Key Points
- Markets and nature are complex systems with unpredictable events and regular boom-bust cycles that cannot be forecasted pre-facto.
- Survival, not victory, is the investment objective; wealth concentration risks catastrophic loss like dinosaur extinction.
- Asset diversification across stocks, bonds, cash, property provides robustness during downturns, even if it feels like drag in bull markets.

## Open Question
How can an investor balance the psychological temptation to concentrate capital in high-return assets during bull markets with the intellectual conviction that diversification is necessary for long-term survival?

## India Angle
Indian investors—both retail and institutional—face volatile markets and psychological pressure similar to global counterparts; applying evolutionary principles could help build more resilient, long-term investment strategies suited to India's boom-bust cycles.
