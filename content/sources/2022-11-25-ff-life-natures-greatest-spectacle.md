## Core Argument
Authentic travel to Africa's Masai Mara offers transformative encounter with nature's spectacle and human meaning.

## Key Points
- Great Migration spectacle of wildebeest, zebras across 16,000 sq km reveals nature's ancient rituals and predator-prey symphony.
- Group of 50-something alumni planning year-long safari demonstrates how shared purpose builds community across life stages.
- Authentic Africa contrasts with commercialized South Africa; raw wilderness triggers deeper connection to literature, history, and human origins.

## Open Question
How does travel meant to escape modern life's constraints inevitably reproduce its planning complexity, logistics, and social hierarchies (gender dynamics in planning, luggage restrictions)?

Indian travellers discovering authentic African wilderness; parallel between colonial histories of India and Kenya; reflection on how travel shapes worldview for Indians seeking meaning beyond material consumption
