## Core Argument
Entrepreneurship is a moral imperative to build sustainable solutions amid technological and economic disruption.

## Key Points
- Ground has shifted dramatically: US imports rose from $54B (1970) to $2.7T (2016), displacing workers globally.
- India's tech context differs: 160M WhatsApp users vs 28M credit cards; UPI can be disruptive game-changer.
- Everyone must think like entrepreneurs to understand systemic shocks and build long-term resilient solutions.

## Open Question
How can policymakers and businesses balance urgent short-term demands (jobs, growth, shareholder returns) with the long-term entrepreneurial ecosystem-building required for sustainable solutions to structural change?

## India Angle
India must build an entrepreneurial ecosystem resilient to systemic shocks; WhatsApp (160M users) and UPI platforms represent India's unique technological leapfrogging opportunity versus traditional financial infrastructure, requiring homegrown solutions not Western imports.
