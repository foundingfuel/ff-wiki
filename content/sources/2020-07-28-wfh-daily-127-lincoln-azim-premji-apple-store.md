## Core Argument
Leaders navigate failure through resilience, perspective, and imagination; success compounds through people, principles, and financial discipline.

## Key Points
- Lincoln's resilience after electoral loss—finding perspective in local victories—shaped his ability to imagine historical possibilities others couldn't see.
- Azim Premji modeled entrepreneurial thinking: achieve lofty goals with limited resources, stay tuned to company culture, and remain human despite success.
- Building successful business first, then deploying financial resources toward transformative missions, is the optimal playbook for impact-driven philanthropy.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the personal humility and uncertainty-navigation Premji demonstrated (negotiating upgrades, asking about lives) with the strategic, scaled thinking required to build transformative enterprises and philanthropy?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders like Azim Premji exemplify how entrepreneurial values—frugality, people-first approach, tight financial management—can build world-class companies and fund transformative philanthropy, a model relevant to India's tech and business ecosystem.
