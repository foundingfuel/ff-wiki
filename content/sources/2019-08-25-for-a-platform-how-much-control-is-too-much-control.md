## Core Argument
Platform success requires balancing control with openness; excessive value extraction punishes platforms, excessive openness enables harm.

## Key Points
- Overcontrol stifles innovation (Bell Labs killing magnetic storage); undercontrol enables abuse (fake news, criminal activity).
- A true platform creates value exceeding the company's own worth; if it hoards value, stakeholders rebel (Zomato Gold case).
- Decentralized design (TCP/IP, Aadhaar principles) enables ecosystem growth; centralized control (Facebook, commercial platforms) concentrates power and limits innovation.

## Open Question
How can platforms remain open enough to foster innovation and fair stakeholder value-sharing while maintaining sufficient control to prevent systemic harms and maintain business viability?

## India Angle
Indian platforms (Aadhaar, Zomato, Oyo, Ola, Swiggy) face acute tension between control and openness. Aadhaar's design principles—inspired by TCP/IP's decentralization—contrast sharply with how commercial Indian platforms restrict partner value capture, as seen in Zomato's crisis.
