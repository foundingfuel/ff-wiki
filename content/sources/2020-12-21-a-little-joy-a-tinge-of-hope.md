## Core Argument
Pandemic forced societal pause, now Indians reclaim normalcy, resilience, and human connection amid recovery.

## Key Points
- Lockdown disrupted fundamental aspects of Indian urban life: markets, streets, social rituals and routines.
- Personal creative adaptation—discovering home through photography—became psychological survival during confinement and uncertainty.
- Gradual recovery visible in streets humming again, people reconnecting, and incremental return to basic social activities.

## Open Question
How can India sustain this fragile recovery and rebuild lost economic/social momentum while remaining vulnerable to future pandemic waves?
