## Core Argument
Account Aggregator framework reframes data sharing as adult-to-adult transactions with consent and tangible benefits.

## Key Points
- AA model enables users to control and share financial data with regulated entities in exchange for specific benefits like loans or advice
- Built on India Stack infrastructure (Aadhaar, UPI, eKYC, DigiLocker), creating ecosystem of digital public goods for financial inclusion
- Consent managers replace paternalistic data collection by corporations/governments, making data transactions transparent and user-controlled

## Open Question
While AA creates consent-based data sharing, the article doesn't address how individual users will navigate the cost-benefit calculus or whether regulatory oversight is sufficient to prevent new forms of data exploitation by financial institutions.

## India Angle
Account Aggregator framework represents India's unique approach to data governance through digital infrastructure, shifting power from corporations/government to individuals in financial data transactions—a model that could reshape how Indians access credit and financial services.
