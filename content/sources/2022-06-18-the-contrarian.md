## Core Argument
Contrarian founder builds iconic Indian tech institution by rejecting conventional wisdom on funding, culture, and work models.

## Key Points
- Zoho bootstrapped from start, betting on cloud when industry skeptical; now positioned as India's SaaS leader
- Vembu prioritizes autonomous culture and long-term infrastructure over metrics-driven scaling, spawning 133+ SaaS entrepreneurs including Freshworks
- Rural-first work strategy and village-based offices reimagine tech talent distribution beyond traditional hubs

## Open Question
Does Sridhar's combative public persona and willingness to challenge established figures undermine or strengthen Zoho's path to becoming an iconic Indian institution like TCS?

## India Angle
Zoho represents India's SaaS opportunity without VC dependency; Vembu's rural-first tech strategy and village relocation challenge Silicon Valley-centric models. Raises questions about how Indian tech leaders should build institutions and compete globally while staying rooted locally.
