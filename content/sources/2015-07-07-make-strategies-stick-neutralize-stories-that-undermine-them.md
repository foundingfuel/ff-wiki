## Core Argument
Strategies fail when leaders ignore anti-stories; acknowledge and replace them with better narratives.

## Key Points
- Anti-stories from past failures are more powerful than future-focused strategic narratives
- Facts and hierarchical refutation cannot defeat anti-stories; only better stories can replace them
- Acknowledging anti-stories via 'mea culpa strategy' neutralizes resistance and builds credibility

## Open Question
How do you scale the mea culpa approach across large organizations where consistent narrative ownership becomes difficult to maintain?

## India Angle
Indian organizations undergoing transformation (IT services, manufacturing, media) often carry deep anti-stories from past failures; leaders must acknowledge these cultural narratives rather than dismiss them with hierarchical authority—critical in consensus-driven Indian workplaces.
