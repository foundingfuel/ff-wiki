## Core Argument
Unprecedented mass remote work during lockdown is transformational if managed right; crisis presents opportunity to evolve organizational capability.

## Key Points
- WFH during crisis differs fundamentally from optional remote work—no physical infrastructure, prolonged duration, psychological strain unique.
- Rituals and boundaries (morning coffee calls, dressing up) create psychological safety and maintain discipline amid isolation.
- Indian managers must be trained in remote leadership; existing policies and meeting etiquette need redesign for distributed teams.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance economic survival needs with ethical obligations to employees during indefinite crisis-driven remote work?

## India Angle
Indian organizations largely lacked WFH culture pre-pandemic; this forced transition demands new management approaches, policies, and training for Indian managers unused to remote work dynamics.
