## Core Argument
AI geopolitics risks dividing world unless Global South nations lead cooperative governance frameworks.

## Key Points
- AI is transformative technology with dual-use potential: medical breakthroughs or existential extinction risk.
- US-China algorithmic race prioritizes dominance over safety; isolation policies may accelerate dangerous military AI.
- India can lead Global South coalition on data sovereignty and technological inclusion, bridging great power divisions.

## Open Question
How can global cooperation on AI safety limits be achieved when nations compete for dominance and ethics means different things in different countries?

## India Angle
India leads Global Partnership on AI with focus on inclusion, access, and data sovereignty. India could anchor a Global South coalition to bridge US-China divide and prevent algorithmic imperialism affecting developing nations.
