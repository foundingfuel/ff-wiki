## Core Argument
Superbosses succeed by treating talent development as core mission, not secondary to short-term performance.

## Key Points
- Superbosses go deeper in mentoring, respect, and motivation than merely good bosses, using apprenticeship-style approaches.
- Strategic talent release—helping best people move on—builds powerful networks that generate long-term dividends.
- Clear, intrinsic vision guides superbosses' decisions on motivation, hiring, and innovation; money is not primary driver.

## Open Question
How can leaders balance pressure to hit short-term numbers with the longer-term investment in talent development that superbosses practice?

## India Angle
Indian companies often operate under short-term pressure and quarterly targets; superboss approach of investing in talent development and networks could differentiate Indian leaders in competitive global markets and improve organizational sustainability.
