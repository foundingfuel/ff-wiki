## Core Argument
2020 exposed leadership unpreparedness for crises while accelerating geopolitical fault lines and systemic inequality.

## Key Points
- World leaders failed coordinated crisis response despite early Covid warnings, signaling governance dysfunction.
- Three concurrent crises—pandemic, climate emergency, nuclear arms escalation—require systemic re-engineering, not incremental fixes.
- US-China tech/trade war and US-Russia multilateralism stance will reshape global order and India's strategic positioning.

## Open Question
Can multilateral institutions genuinely re-emerge if great powers continue pursuing zero-sum competitions in trade, technology, and nuclear arms?

## India Angle
India faces multilevel challenges: geopolitical (arms race involvement), economic (RCEP membership implications), environmental (climate action), and social (digital divide in education). India's policy responses on climate, multilateralism, and tech competition will define its positioning in an increasingly multipolar world.
