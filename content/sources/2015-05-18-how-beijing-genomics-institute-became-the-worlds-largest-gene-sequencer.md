## Core Argument
BGI dominates global genomics through low-cost operations, young talent, and audacious early ambitions.

## Key Points
- Hire young, train in-house: BGI builds workforce from 22-year-olds and dropouts via on-site college, avoiding credential bias.
- Shoot for prestige early: Participated in Human Genome Project at 1% capacity to gain global credibility and network.
- Curiosity and flexibility: Encourages weird projects (glowing pigs) and adapted quickly when leaving Beijing's conventional bureaucracy.

## Open Question
How can BGI maintain scientific rigor and breakthrough innovation while relying on mostly young, inexperienced staff without advanced degrees?

## India Angle
Indian biotech and genomics startups can adopt BGI's model of hiring young talent, building internal training ecosystems, and pursuing ambitious projects early to compete globally and reduce R&D costs—critical for India's emerging biotech sector.
