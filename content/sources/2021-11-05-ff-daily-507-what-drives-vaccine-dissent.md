## Core Argument
Vaccine dissent and societal rudeness stem from emotional disconnects, not ignorance; holistic, context-aware engagement needed.

## Key Points
- Science alone insufficient; emotions, beliefs, and lived experience drive vaccine hesitancy and policy resistance.
- Pandemic-era rudeness shifted from unintentional miscommunication to deliberate protest against perceived injustice and rights violations.
- Gap between policymakers observing from outside and citizens embedded in lived reality fuels 'stuck-ness' and distrust.

## Open Question
How do governments and health institutions rebuild trust when their solutions are scientifically sound but emotionally alienating to the populations they serve?

## India Angle
India faces dual challenges: vaccine hesitancy rooted in cultural beliefs and emotional disconnects, plus a broader civility crisis exposed during COVID—both requiring engagement beyond top-down policy to address underlying sentiments and build trust in institutions.
