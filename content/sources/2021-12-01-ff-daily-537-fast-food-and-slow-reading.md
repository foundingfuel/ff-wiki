## Core Argument
Context and environment shape behaviour more than individual choice; systems thinking needed for safety and media platforms.

## Key Points
- Fast-food dining context (solitary eating) drives 25% engagement with table tents versus 2% in social settings
- Road accidents blamed on driver error ignore systemic factors: road design, vehicles, infrastructure accountability
- Substack solved creator monetisation by bundling publishing, membership, payments—but faces platform scale challenges

## Open Question
How can platforms like Substack maintain creator quality and community value as they scale to hundreds of millions of users without becoming extractive like predecessors (Apple, Facebook)?

## India Angle
India's road safety crisis (1.33 lakh deaths in 2020) mirrors systemic issues beyond driver behaviour; policy focus on enforcement alone ignores design and infrastructure factors. Newsletter platforms like Substack represent new media economics India's creators are entering.
