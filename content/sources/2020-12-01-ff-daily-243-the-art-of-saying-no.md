## Core Argument
Strategic refusal of non-critical requests protects finite time and enables high-impact work.

## Key Points
- Saying yes reflexively is a biological default that leads to time waste and resentment.
- Charlie Munger's five-second-no rule uses structured delay to override automatic agreement impulses.
- Reciprocity creates manipulation risk; valuing arguments over time is a reasoning error.

## Open Question
How do you maintain professional relationships and generosity while systematically refusing most requests without appearing selfish or dismissive?

## India Angle
Indian professionals often struggle with saying no due to hierarchical workplace cultures and social obligation norms; adopting boundary-setting strategies is critical for high performance in India's competitive knowledge economy.
