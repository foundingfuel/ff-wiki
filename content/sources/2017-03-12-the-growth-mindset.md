## Core Argument
Growth mindset—constant learning, expansion, and scale—drives extraordinary organizational and individual achievement.

## Key Points
- Growth mindset prioritizes expansion through learning and scaling over mere novelty or funding.
- Culture and shared purpose—like sports teams—enable organizations to build and sustain growth.
- India's competitive edge lies in indigenous innovation and 'Think in India' rather than imitative manufacturing.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain culture and purpose while pursuing aggressive growth without losing their original values or burning out teams?

## India Angle
India needs a growth mindset in entrepreneurship and policymaking—from 'Make in India' to 'Think in India'—to leapfrog and build scalable, innovative solutions across sectors like healthcare.
