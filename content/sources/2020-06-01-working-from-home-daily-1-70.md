## Core Argument
Staying resilient through failure and building trust are essential for leaders navigating crisis and systemic change.

## Key Points
- Failure and iteration are prerequisites for impact; those who persist outpace those pursuing feel-good endeavors
- Digital education without infrastructure and skills access perpetuates inequality in India's disparate regions
- Trust in leadership, institutions and expertise erodes when politicians undermine science and cooperation

## Open Question
How can leaders build trust while admitting uncertainty, and how do we sequence infrastructure development before deploying digital solutions in under-resourced regions?
