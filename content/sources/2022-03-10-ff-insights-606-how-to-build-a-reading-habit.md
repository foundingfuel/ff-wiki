## Core Argument
Building a reading habit requires gradual, protected practice that prioritizes reflection over productivity culture.

## Key Points
- Start with 30 minutes daily, four days weekly—gradual habit formation beats ambitious but unsustainable schedules.
- Avoid email before reading; the format's fragmentation disrupts the contemplative state deep learning requires.
- Choosing reading over task completion is a values statement rejecting worth-through-productivity paradigm.

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers reconcile the pressure for visible accomplishment with the invisible but essential work of developing deeper understanding?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and knowledge workers need sustained learning habits to compete in rapidly changing job markets; reading as self-cultivation is especially critical given India's educational infrastructure gaps and need for continuous upskilling.
