## Core Argument
InMobi's Miip signals India's emergence as a technology product leader, more significant than Indian executives in global corporations.

## Key Points
- InMobi built a world-class mobile advertising platform through timely market entry and strong funding, now leveraging scale for innovation.
- Miip uses advanced analytics and user dialogue to solve real advertising problems—intent-driven targeting failures that plague existing platforms.
- InMobi's multi-city launch and partner collaborations demonstrate India can produce globally-competitive IP-based companies, not just B2B services.

## Open Question
Can India sustain product innovation leadership without addressing the cultural preference for IT services exports and the capital intensity of scaling globally?

## India Angle
India needs indigenous IP-led technology companies, not just IT services exports. InMobi represents a homegrown platform that can compete globally, signifying India's transition from service provider to product innovator.
