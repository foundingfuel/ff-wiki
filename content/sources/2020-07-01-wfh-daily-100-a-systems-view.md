## Core Argument
Modern problems are systemic and interconnected; outdated worldviews and rule-following alone are insufficient.

## Key Points
- Major crises—energy, climate, finance—are interconnected systemic problems requiring holistic perception shifts.
- Principle-driven behavior beyond rule compliance creates competitive advantage and respect in business ecosystems.
- Indian industries like publishing must evolve rapidly to survive market disruption and redefine value creation.

## Open Question
If systemic problems require systems thinking, how do individuals and large institutions practically break entrenched mental models and processes designed for linear, isolated problem-solving?

## India Angle
Indian institutions and leaders must shift from linear, rule-based thinking to systems thinking to address interconnected challenges like financial security, food systems, and market dynamics. Indian publishing and business sectors exemplify this need for evolved mindsets beyond compliance.
