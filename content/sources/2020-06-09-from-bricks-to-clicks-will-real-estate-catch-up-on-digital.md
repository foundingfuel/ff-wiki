## Core Argument
Real estate must embrace digital sales and post-purchase engagement, but success requires trust, integrated customer journeys, and regulatory enabling.

## Key Points
- Digital adoption forces developers to abandon control of narrative and embrace transparency via comparison portals and social media accountability.
- Seamless digital experience demands orchestrating beyond traditional scope—embedding mortgage appraisal, e-signatures, and online registration into sales journey.
- Regulatory barriers (wet-ink signature mandates, high e-registration eligibility thresholds) undermine digital adoption despite legal frameworks existing.

## Open Question
How can developers build trust through transparency while regulatory systems still require physical signatures and impose arbitrary eligibility criteria that fragment the digital experience?

## India Angle
Indian real estate developers must digitize to survive post-COVID; regulatory barriers (NHB rules, e-signature acceptance, e-registration criteria) prevent seamless online home sales, requiring policy reform alongside business transformation.
