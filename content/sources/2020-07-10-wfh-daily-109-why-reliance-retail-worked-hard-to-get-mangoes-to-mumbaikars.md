## Core Argument
Leaders anticipate future needs and rally people by providing both essentials and emotional comfort during uncertainty.

## Key Points
- Reliance Retail shifted from phase-one essentials focus to providing 'small joys'—culturally relevant comfort items like mangoes for Indian consumers
- Leadership means sensing change and communicating vision that motivates others to follow toward a better future state
- Caste discrimination in Silicon Valley reveals systemic workplace prejudice requiring legal remedies and HR practice reform globally

## Open Question
How can large corporations balance immediate operational efficiency (essentials) with understanding deeper human psychological needs (joy, belonging, dignity) when resources are constrained?

## India Angle
