## Core Argument
Indian liberals are dogmatic pretenders unable to change positions when facts challenge their narratives.

## Key Points
- True liberalism requires intellectual flexibility and evidence-based reasoning; Indian liberals default to noise and ideology instead.
- Indian culture favours tribal conformity over individualism, limiting philosophical independence and rigorous thought.
- Public discourse degenerates into personal attacks rather than proposition-based debate across political divides.

## Open Question
If liberal thought is genuinely difficult to cultivate in India due to cultural factors, how can democratic discourse improve without addressing these deeper systemic values?

## India Angle
Indian liberals lack intellectual rigour and adaptability; unable to engage with evidence-based debate on policy (technology, governance). This reflects deeper cultural tendency toward tribal thinking rather than individualistic, first-principles reasoning—limiting quality of democratic discourse.
