## Core Argument
Prolonged conflicts without resolution are institutionalizing uncertainty as the defining feature of global order.

## Key Points
- Conflicts are hardening into open-ended instability rather than moving toward resolution, creating structural uncertainty.
- Europe's economic dependence on China constrains its strategic choices and enables Beijing to exploit divisions quietly.
- Absence of credible endgames—not active conflict alone—is the central danger to markets, firms, and policymakers.

## Open Question
How can nations pursue 'de-risking' strategies when decoupling requires coordinated action that national economic interests consistently undermine?

## India Angle
India faces similar structural pressures in a multipolar world without clear resolution pathways—balancing great power competition, supply chain dependencies, and the need for strategic autonomy while managing economic vulnerabilities and internal consensus challenges.
