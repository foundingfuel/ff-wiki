## Core Argument
Maintaining distinctiveness requires continuous energy; institutions must resist homogenizing pressures to survive and thrive.

## Key Points
- Differentiation is not automatic—organizations must actively work to maintain what makes them unique and valuable.
- India's welfare and public systems can innovate by piggybacking on existing infrastructure while maintaining decentralized decision-making capacity.
- Psychological languishing—stagnation without depression—is a real cost of crisis fatigue; leaders must address this emotional reality.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain distinctiveness while also building resilient, scalable systems that can be replicated across contexts?

## India Angle
