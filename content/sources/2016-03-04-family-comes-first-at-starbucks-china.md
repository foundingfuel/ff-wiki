## Core Argument
Companies must engage employee families as stakeholders to legitimize non-traditional career paths in collectivist cultures.

## Key Points
- Asian cultures view careers as family accomplishments, not individual choices, creating stigma around service roles.
- Starbucks China holds family forums with 90% participation to reframe barista roles as dignified and valued.
- Housing subsidies, sabbaticals, and family engagement directly address family insecurities about employee welfare and status.

## Open Question
How do companies scale family engagement as a retention strategy across diverse geographies without reducing dignity to a transactional benefit?

## India Angle
India's collectivist family structures and parental expectations around 'respectable' careers mirror China's dynamics. Indian companies in service sectors (retail, hospitality, QSR) face similar talent retention challenges when families devalue frontline roles despite skill requirements.
