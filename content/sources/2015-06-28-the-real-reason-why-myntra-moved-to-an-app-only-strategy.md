## Core Argument
Myntra's app-only pivot signals strategic channel optimization beyond surface-level cost-cutting narratives.

## Key Points
- Myntra closed web operations to concentrate on mobile-first user experience and engagement metrics
- Decision sparked debate about choice vs. optimization—whether limiting channels improves or harms customer experience
- Move reflects broader e-commerce rethinking of sales channels in competitive Indian online retail landscape

## Open Question
Does forcing users onto a single channel (app) through artificial constraints represent genuine optimization or mask deeper profitability challenges?

Indian e-commerce players experimenting with channel strategies to optimize unit economics and user engagement in a price-sensitive, mobile-first market
