## Core Argument
Data presentation shapes decisions; India's unemployed youth need skills matching jobs, not credentials misaligned with market demand.

## Key Points
- How data is framed—percentages vs. absolute numbers, positive vs. negative—significantly influences decision-making outcomes.
- India's unemployment paradox: higher education correlates with higher joblessness in 15-29 age group due to skill-job mismatch.
- Gig mindset—self-responsibility, continuous learning, networked leadership—transforms organizations and improves employee engagement and purpose.

## Open Question
If higher education in India creates unemployability by fostering aversion to blue-collar work, how can the education system be redesigned without destabilizing social aspiration and class mobility?

## India Angle
India faces a critical unemployment crisis among youth, especially in Bihar and UP, where rising education levels paradoxically increase joblessness. Organizations like NDA show how gig mindset can unlock talent in Indian knowledge sector, while data literacy remains essential for policy-makers addressing labor market challenges.
