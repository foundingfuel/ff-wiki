## Core Argument
Rule of law requires robust democratic deliberation on evolving human rights, not just faster courts.

## Key Points
- Citizens in democracies increasingly distrust institutions; both India and US must strengthen democratic processes
- Authoritarian regimes attract more FDI through speed, but democracies must protect rights, not just ease business
- Human rights and liberty are evolving concepts requiring continuous civilizational dialogue, not static constitutional interpretation

## Open Question
How can democracies balance protecting minority rights through judicial independence with majority will expressed through elected assemblies when conceptions of rights themselves are contested and evolving?

## India Angle
India's democratic institutions face legitimacy crises similar to the US, with fundamental rights contested 72 years after Constitution framing. India's rule of law challenges differ from China's authoritarian efficiency, requiring stronger democratic deliberation rather than just judicial speedup.
