## Core Argument
Six technological and behavioral shifts will reshape the internet economy, with Asia driving e-commerce revolution.

## Key Points
- Asia's e-commerce rise driven by infrastructure maturity; Alibaba exceeds Walmart's market cap.
- Mobile-only consumers and payments (not mobile-first) will dominate emerging markets including India.
- IoT, big data analytics, and intelligent devices create new business models and B2B opportunities.

## Open Question
Why did vertical B2B marketplaces fail while horizontal omnibus marketplaces succeeded, and what does this reveal about enterprise transformation inertia versus startup disruption capability?

## India Angle
India is positioned as a major player in the next decade of e-commerce alongside China, with potential for half of the world's top 10 e-commerce companies to emerge from India-China. Mobile-only customers and alternative payment models (like M-Pesa) are particularly relevant for India's internet economy development.
