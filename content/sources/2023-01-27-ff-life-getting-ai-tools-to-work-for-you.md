## Core Argument
AI tools amplify human capability; embrace them as productivity aids, not threats to creative work.

## Key Points
- AI-powered writing tools like Lex solve real workflow problems: headline generation, drafting support, unblocking writer's block.
- Text-to-speech and music generation democratize production—small creators access professional-quality outputs previously affordable only to large organisations.
- Practical experimentation with tools reveals mixed results; adoption requires realistic expectations and willingness to iterate, not blind faith.

## Open Question
If AI-generated outputs improve but remain imperfect, how do creators decide when to accept algorithmic outputs versus investing in human talent—and what does that mean for creative professionals' livelihoods?

## India Angle
Indian content creators, journalists, and businesses can leverage AI tools to overcome resource constraints—outsourcing expensive copywriting, voiceovers, and music production—critical for bootstrapped startups and media houses operating with lean teams.
