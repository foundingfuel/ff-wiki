## Core Argument
Airbnb disrupts traditional hospitality by empowering DIY travelers; India's market remains untapped due to trust and adoption barriers.

## Key Points
- Indian outbound travelers shifting from travel agents to self-curated, tech-enabled experiences; 20M to 50M by 2020.
- Airbnb offers 50% cost savings and local insights vs. hotels, but India has <1% of global listings with safety/cancellation concerns.
- Hotel chains lobby against unregulated platforms over tax fairness and safety standards; regulatory battles shape industry futures.

## Open Question
How can Airbnb overcome Indian travelers' risk aversion (safety, cancellations) without either regulatory crackdowns or diluting its asset-light model?

## India Angle
India is poised to become the world's second-largest outbound travel market (50M travelers by 2020), but lacks homegrown alternatives to Airbnb. Trust concerns and limited homestay adoption outside specific regions create an opportunity gap for Indian entrepreneurs and platforms.
