## Core Argument
Go to original sources, not secondhand interpretations; blindly copying buzzwords like EPS destroys long-term capability.

## Key Points
- Easwaran's meditation insight: scholars' commentary on Shakespeare obscures the original; apply this to business theory and economics.
- Jack Welch's financialization of GE destroyed engineering prowess; IBM's EPS growth via buybacks masked zero underlying growth.
- India tempted to copy failed American playbook—prioritize R&D and manufacturing over cheap finance; challenge 'core competence' dogma that limits vision.

## Open Question
If core competence is a flawed framework that limits ambition (as Vembu argues), what replaces it as a guiding principle for strategic focus?

## India Angle
Indian companies risk blindly copying Western business school models (EPS obsession, financialization over R&D) without examining original sources; Zoho's Vembu warns India should build manufacturing and semiconductor capabilities rather than chase cheap finance like failed American giants.
