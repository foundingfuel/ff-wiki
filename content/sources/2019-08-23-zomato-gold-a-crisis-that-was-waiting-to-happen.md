## Core Argument
Zomato Gold failed because it was a discount programme masquerading as loyalty, extracting disproportionate value from restaurant partners.

## Key Points
- Zomato promised premium, niche Gold programme but scaled to 1M members, becoming mass-market discount platform restaurants couldn't afford.
- Restaurants bore discount costs while Zomato captured growth; misaligned value creation led to inevitable stakeholder revolt and #LogOut campaign.
- Leadership dismissed early warning signs and blamed restaurants rather than addressing structural programme flaws, escalating conflict.

## Open Question
How can digital platforms scaling rapidly balance growth ambitions with fair value distribution to supply-side partners without sacrificing unit economics?

## India Angle
Zomato Gold's collapse reveals how Indian startups scaling rapidly can mismanage stakeholder relationships; critical for understanding unicorn sustainability and restaurant ecosystem dynamics in India's food-tech sector.
