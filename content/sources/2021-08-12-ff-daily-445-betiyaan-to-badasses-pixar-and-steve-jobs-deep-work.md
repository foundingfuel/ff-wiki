## Core Argument
Personal and organizational transformation requires active engagement, ownership, and relentless incremental improvement, not passive change.

## Key Points
- Steve Jobs' leadership evolved through empathy and deeper listening while maintaining rigorous thinking about the problem itself.
- Indian hockey team took ownership of their mission independently of external coaches, committing to daily 0.2% improvements.
- Creative excellence demands ringfenced deep work—3-4 hours of undisturbed focus—not optimized multitasking or constant busyness.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need for transformational leaders with building cultures of ownership where teams drive change regardless of who's in charge?

## India Angle
Indian women's hockey team's transformation from last-place finishers to medal contenders mirrors leadership lessons about culture, ownership, and continuous improvement—applicable to Indian organizations navigating change and building high-performance cultures.
