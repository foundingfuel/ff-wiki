## Core Argument
Organizational design reflects internal politics; geopolitical design reflects power structures, both constraining outcomes.

## Key Points
- Leaders are designers whose choices on strategy, culture, hiring shape organizational output and impact
- Conway's Law: organizations produce designs mirroring their communication structures and internal conflicts
- Geopolitical borders drawn by external powers without understanding local history create persistent instability and conflict

## Open Question
If organizational and geopolitical design are fundamentally constrained by existing power structures, how can transformative change occur without restructuring those power dynamics first?
