## Core Argument
Resource-constrained teams can compete with well-funded rivals by democratizing data insights across the organization.

## Key Points
- Data became a cultural norm for all 15-person team members, not just analysts; real-time traffic dashboards drove editorial decisions.
- Approximation over perfection: cheap, accessible data tools (RSS feeds, Facebook API) replaced expensive sentiment-tracking software to identify content trends.
- Segregating tactical (daily) from strategic (monthly) data contexts prevented confusion and enabled both agile iteration and long-term pattern recognition.

## Open Question
How do teams maintain data rigor and statistical validity when using approximation methods instead of comprehensive Big Data analysis, especially as they scale?

## India Angle
Indian bootstrapped startups competing against well-funded rivals can win through data-driven decision-making without expensive tools or large teams. Applicable to Indian digital media, e-commerce, and content companies operating with resource constraints.
