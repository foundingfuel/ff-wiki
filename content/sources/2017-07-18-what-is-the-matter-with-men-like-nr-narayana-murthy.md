## Core Argument
Founders who cannot distinguish faith from fanaticism refuse succession, creating organizational fault lines and ecosystem harm.

## Key Points
- Faith allows ideas to evolve and outlast creators; fanaticism clings to control and demands conformity.
- Mature founders must recognize when entities outgrow them and place trust in successors to avoid disastrous fault lines.
- Indian founders often promise succession narratives to heirs/teams but fail to relinquish control, creating duplicity and lost potential.

## Open Question
How can founders cultivate the psychological maturity to distinguish between protecting their legacy and imprisoning their creation through refusal to delegate?

## India Angle
Explores how Indian business founders (Narayana Murthy, Infosys co-founders) struggle with succession and letting go, creating organizational instability and ecosystem damage—a critical challenge for Indian Inc.'s maturity and sustainability.
