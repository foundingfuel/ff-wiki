## Core Argument
Leaders must cultivate internal resilience—emotional, mental, relational—to bend under crisis pressure without breaking.

## Key Points
- Resilience requires presence: endurance (handling shock) plus recovery (bouncing back to create new normal).
- Separate facts from feelings—the first arrow (event) from second arrow (mind's story)—to reclaim agency.
- Change follows universal pattern: ending → regeneration → new beginning; leaders must recognize and navigate each stage.

## Open Question
How can leaders simultaneously maintain stability and innovate rapidly when their own emotional state is destabilized by crisis uncertainty?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders—from startups to family enterprises to multinational heads—face acute crises (lockdowns, supply chain collapse, cash flow stress). This framework helps them navigate emotional and mental resilience alongside operational decisions, critical for retaining talent and business continuity in volatile environments.
