## Core Argument
Defensive pessimism, not optimism, drives high performance through anxiety management and exhaustive planning.

## Key Points
- Defensive pessimists perform equally to strategic optimists by converting anxiety into meticulous preparation.
- Imagining worst-case scenarios gives pessimists a sense of control and motivation to prevent failure.
- Making defensive pessimists happy actually sabotages their performance; anxiety fuels their excellence.

## Open Question
If defensive pessimism requires chronic anxiety to function, is it a sustainable high-performance strategy or a psychological burden masquerading as productivity?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders often operate in high-pressure environments (startups, competitive corporate settings, policy work). Understanding defensive pessimism as a legitimate performance strategy challenges cultural narratives around positivity and could reshape how Indian organisations build resilience and manage anxious but capable teams.
