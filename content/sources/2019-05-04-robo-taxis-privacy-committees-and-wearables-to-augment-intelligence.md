## Core Argument
Disruptive technologies reshape business models, but require rethinking privacy, regulation, and intelligence augmentation.

## Key Points
- Tesla's vertical integration (chip design) creates competitive moats; echoes Apple's strategy for platform dominance.
- Facebook's privacy committee signals regulatory pressure globally; India must learn before enacting data protection law.
- Wearables and blockchain promise intelligence augmentation and identity solutions, but risk repeating smartphone's social harms.

## Open Question
Will emerging technologies (wearables, autonomous systems, blockchain) genuinely augment human intelligence and solve real problems, or replicate smartphones' pattern of convenience masking social and cognitive harm?

## India Angle
India may pass a data protection law this year; must learn from Facebook's privacy failures and global regulatory trends. Blockchain identity solutions (ID2020) could address India's 1.1B unidentified population. Autonomous vehicles and wearables will reshape Indian transport and labor markets.
