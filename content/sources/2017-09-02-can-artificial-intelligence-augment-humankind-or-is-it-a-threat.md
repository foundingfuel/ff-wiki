## Core Argument
AI augments human capability rather than replacing it; engineering safety frameworks matters more than existential fears.

## Key Points
- AI shows concrete improvements in vision, translation, and speech recognition; engineers must integrate machine learning into core toolboxes.
- Medical robotics augment surgeon skills and could democratize healthcare access in developing countries through remote-controlled systems.
- Real risks lie in financial market instabilities and inadequately understood algorithmic systems, not sci-fi scenarios of replacement.

## Open Question
If AI investment bubble reflects misunderstanding of technology limits, how do we distinguish genuine capability from hype-driven funding?

## India Angle
India's developing healthcare sector could benefit from AI-augmented medical robotics to expand surgical access; however, building engineering talent and safety frameworks for AI deployment remains critical infrastructure challenge.
