## Core Argument
Automation's economic impact requires reimagining work and welfare; India's identity fragmentation complicates both UBI and jobs-based solutions.

## Key Points
- UBI proponents cite Mincome: unconditional cash improved outcomes (maternal leave, school retention) without killing work incentive.
- Jobs advocates argue joblessness drives self-destructive behavior; welfare alone won't solve purposelessness—people need meaningful work.
- India's identity infrastructure (400M unidentified, fragmented digital records) makes any large-scale welfare program administratively impossible today.

## Open Question
Should India pursue UBI or job guarantees when robots displace workers, but lacks the digital identity infrastructure to implement either at scale?

## India Angle
India's identity crisis—400M unidentified, 500M with unreliable docs, 100M with multiple identities—makes UBI implementation uniquely complex compared to Western debates; any welfare system requires foundational digital identity infrastructure first.
