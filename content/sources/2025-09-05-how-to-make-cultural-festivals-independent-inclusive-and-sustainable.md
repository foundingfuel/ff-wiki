## Core Argument
Community-funded, volunteer-driven festivals create sustainable cultural ecosystems independent of corporate gatekeepers.

## Key Points
- Decentralized funding through communities eliminates single-sponsor control and reduces festival vulnerability to corporate withdrawal.
- Physical and temporary spaces require different programming strategies—year-round venues versus concentrated events serve varied community scales.
- Cultural festivals shape city identity beyond economic labels, attracting talent and enhancing urban appeal beyond startup ecosystems.

## Open Question
How do festivals balance inclusivity and accessibility with the practical constraints of scale, venue capacity, and concentrated programming bursts?

## India Angle
Indian cultural festivals can achieve independence and inclusivity through community-based funding models and volunteer networks, breaking away from corporate dependency—a distinctly Indian challenge given the dominance of corporate patronage in arts and culture sector.
