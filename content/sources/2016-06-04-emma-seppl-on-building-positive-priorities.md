## Core Argument
Prioritizing wellbeing over constant intensity increases productivity, creativity, and prevents burnout.

## Key Points
- Chronic high-stress mode impairs cognitive function and creativity; rest enables breakthrough thinking.
- Energy management through alternating high and low-intensity tasks and brief recovery moments improves outcomes.
- Individual agency in managing mental state exists even when workplace demands cannot be controlled.

## Open Question
How can individuals practice wellbeing-focused strategies in workplaces that explicitly reward and incentivize go-getter intensity, without career penalty?

## India Angle
Indian workplaces, particularly in IT, consulting, and startups, operate on high-intensity 'always-on' culture; Seppälä's research-backed argument for energy management and wellbeing directly challenges the prevalent hustle-glorification mindset that dominates Indian corporate and entrepreneurial ecosystems.
