## Core Argument
Corporate structures fail not from lack of rules, but from leadership's unwillingness to enforce them fairly.

## Key Points
- Tata Hotels had all compliance mechanisms—boards, committees, training—yet failed to act fairly against powerful CEO Rakesh Sarna.
- Companies facing harassment crises employ intimidation, confidentiality weaponization, and media suppression instead of transparent accountability.
- Crisis leadership failures reveal that culture depends entirely on whether leaders show spine to hold peers accountable regardless of seniority.

## Open Question
How can organizations truly embed ethical cultures when structural safeguards exist but enforcement depends on the personal courage of leaders who may face career consequences?

## India Angle
Indian corporations, despite formal compliance mechanisms and board diversity, fail to enforce ethical standards when powerful leaders are involved; systemic weakness in India Inc.'s ability to hold senior executives accountable undermines trust and governance.
