## Core Argument
Team-based culture and clear strategic focus enable rapid pivots; Facebook's mobile turnaround proves organizational structure matters as much as vision.

## Key Points
- Zuckerberg embedded mobile-first requirements into every product team's review process, forcing cultural shift not just structural change.
- Team empowerment allowed experimentation and failure (Paper, Slingshot, Rooms) while maintaining speed and rigor in execution.
- Legacy systems inherited from desktop-era engineers initially hindered innovation; deliberate restructuring around new priority unlocked transformation.

## Open Question
How do you balance empowering teams to experiment and fail fast with maintaining the discipline and rigor needed to convert those experiments into revenue at scale?

## India Angle
Indian companies scaling rapidly often inherit legacy systems and cultures; Facebook's team-based transformation model offers lessons for how Indian tech firms and large enterprises can reorganize around new priorities without losing institutional knowledge.
