## Core Argument
India's military logistics capacity has transformed since 1962, but sustaining border operations remains economically and operationally staggering.

## Key Points
- FOL (fuel, oil, lubricants) accounts for 60% of Ladakh deployment logistics; costs Rs 5 crore daily per brigade
- Extreme environmental conditions—40°C below zero, 40-foot snow—require air bridges and specialized infrastructure like world's highest FOL depot
- Moratoriums create policy dilemma: extending relief delays economic pain but creates unpayable debt burdens for vulnerable populations

## Open Question
How can India sustain indefinite high-cost border operations without compromising fiscal health, and how should governments balance immediate relief against long-term economic solvency?

## India Angle
India's military logistics and operational capacity in high-altitude conflict zones; economic costs of border standoffs; institutional capability gaps vs. 1962; government policy on moratoriums affecting citizens and financial systems
