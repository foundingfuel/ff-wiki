## Core Argument
India's missing medium-term planning horizon reflects deeper cultural patterns that defer collective responsibility to destiny or immediate expediency.

## Key Points
- India oscillates between instant gratification and cosmic fatalism, skipping the 10-20 year institutional planning horizon.
- Municipal governance structures are too weak and fragmented to sustain multi-decade planning needed for cities and infrastructure.
- Cultural narratives anchoring responsibility in cosmic justice create tolerance for disorder and deferral of collective action.

## Open Question
How can India leverage its philosophical sophistication and improvisation skills to build sustained institutional discipline without losing cultural identity or adaptive capacity?

## India Angle
India's cultural orientation toward cosmic destiny and immediate pragmatism creates a governance blind spot: the medium-term institutional planning horizon (10-20 years) needed for sustainable urban development, infrastructure, and state capacity.
