## Core Argument
Crisis reveals true leadership: those who lead with truth, hope, and humility transform organizations and societies.

## Key Points
- Great leaders trust people with truth, not optimism disconnected from reality or facts.
- Hope—not optimism—comes from leaders inspiring collaboration and future possibilities during uncertainty.
- Humility in leadership creates space for others to rise and solve problems together, not alone.

## Open Question
How can leaders maintain hope and positivity while honestly acknowledging systemic inequalities and uncertainties they cannot single-handedly solve?

## India Angle
India's pandemic response exposed deep inequalities affecting daily wage earners and migrant workers; leaders must balance crisis management with addressing systemic societal fault-lines revealed by COVID-19.
