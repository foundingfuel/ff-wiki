## Core Argument
Hero worship as inspiration for excellence and long-term thinking is necessary, not antithetical to democratic values.

## Key Points
- Democratic egalitarianism has eroded the ability to recognize and learn from genuine genius and excellence.
- Zoho's Sridhar Vembu built a world-class company by self-belief, long-term thinking, and questioning conventional wisdom.
- Admiring greatness—not blind obedience—fuels aspiration, culture-building, and resilience through multiple business cycles.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between healthy admiration of excellence and the dangers of cult-like hero worship that stifles independent thinking and accountability?

## India Angle
Indian leaders often operate in contexts where hierarchical respect and guru-shishya traditions exist; reimagining hero worship as inspiration for excellence (not blind obedience) is relevant to building cultures of aspiration while maintaining autonomy—Zoho and Sridhar Vembu exemplify this.
