## Core Argument
Self-awareness of learning style drives performance; acting on this knowledge separates high performers from underperformers.

## Key Points
- Individuals have distinct learning styles—writing, talking, doing, listening—yet most don't act on this self-knowledge.
- Schools and organisations standardise learning methods, penalising those with different styles; awareness enables adaptation.
- India's healthcare system requires government intervention and financing like developed economies, not market-only approaches.

## Open Question
How do organisations balance standardised systems and processes with accommodating diverse individual learning and working styles at scale?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and leaders must recognise diverse learning styles to build high-performance teams; healthcare policy reveals systemic under-investment requiring leadership mindset shift on public financing.
