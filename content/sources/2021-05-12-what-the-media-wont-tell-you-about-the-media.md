## Core Argument
Media industry must pivot from advertising to subscription revenue as consumer platforms shift irreversibly to digital.

## Key Points
- Print advertising will grow short-term due to premium audiences abandoning ad-supported TV for SVOD platforms.
- Vernacular media unexpectedly resilient; subscription recovery at 88-90%, defying decline predictions.
- Industry must abandon product-centric thinking; follow consumers to platforms where they already consume content.

## Open Question
If subscription revenues go to separate players while traditional media operators still depend on advertising, how do legacy publishers actually transition without cannibalizing their existing revenue base?

## India Angle
India uniquely grew all media sectors simultaneously for 15 years; now faces consolidation as digital platforms cannibalize traditional media. Vernacular publishers show resilience (88-90% subscription recovery), but industry must shift from ad-dependent to subscription models to survive.
