## Core Argument
Startups must prioritize profit and sustainability from day one, not treat unprofitability as acceptable startup privilege.

## Key Points
- Startups shouldn't ignore fundamental business laws—revenue in, expenses out, profit or failure.
- Founders obsessed with exit strategies lack commitment to building real, lasting businesses.
- Acting like a mature business from inception, not as a temporary experiment, increases survival odds.

## Open Question
How do capital-intensive, long-cycle innovations (biotech, deep tech) balance immediate profitability demands with necessary patient investment and R&D timelines?

## India Angle
Indian startup ecosystem often mimics Silicon Valley's venture-backed, growth-at-all-costs model; this challenges founders to build profitable, sustainable businesses from day one—critical given India's capital constraints and need for job creation.
