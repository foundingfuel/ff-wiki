## Core Argument
London's walkability and civic order reveal cultural differences in public behaviour between India and UK.

## Key Points
- London's infrastructure and polite pedestrian culture make it exceptionally walkable and accessible for exploration.
- Indian cricket history at Lord's (Dravid, Shastri, Azharuddin) connects the stadium to Indian sporting identity.
- British civic discipline—queue discipline, orderly crowds, rule-following—contrasts sharply with Indian public behaviour norms.

## Open Question
Can India adopt London's civic discipline without losing its characteristic vibrancy and spontaneity, or are these traits culturally incompatible?
