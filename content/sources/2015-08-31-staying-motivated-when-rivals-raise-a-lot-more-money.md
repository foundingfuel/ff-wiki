## Core Argument
Raising capital should match strategy, not ego; excessive funding corrupts execution and culture.

## Key Points
- Capital amount must align with strategic needs, not competitor comparisons or vanity metrics.
- Excessive funding breeds arrogance, erodes frugality culture, and triggers poor strategic decisions.
- Stay focused on business fundamentals; emotional hijacking by rival funding is a distraction from dharma.

## Open Question
How do founders distinguish between healthy competitive motivation and destructive emotional hijacking when evaluating their own fundraising strategy and progress?

## India Angle
Indian startups operate in a hyper-competitive funding environment where capital influx and hype can distract from fundamentals; the Mindtree example and Gita reference anchor this to Indian entrepreneurial context and values.
