## Core Argument
AI amplifies hollow work by replacing thinking with its appearance, masking organisational judgment gaps.

## Key Points
- Indian Supreme Court found lawyers filing fake AI-generated citations; AI accelerated but didn't create poor judgment habits.
- Nandan Nilekani identified implementation gap: expertise and context determine whether AI improves or just multiplies output.
- False productivity—more pages, decks, reports without deeper thinking—now scales faster with AI tools.

## Open Question
How can organisations measure whether AI-enabled productivity actually improves decision-making, or merely creates convincing facades of expertise?

## India Angle
Indian courts and legal profession now facing AI-generated fabricated citations; broader implications for Indian enterprises adopting AI without building critical judgment capacity; relevant to India's consulting, legal and knowledge-work sectors scaling rapidly.
