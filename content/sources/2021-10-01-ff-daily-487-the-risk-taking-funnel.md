## Core Argument
Breakthrough innovation requires progressive risk-taking funnels and celebrating incremental milestones toward meaningful purpose.

## Key Points
- Risk-taking funnel: many small bets early, fewer larger bets later; mirrors drug development economics.
- Purpose and milestones sustain motivation through long, uncertain innovation journeys requiring years to completion.
- Resilience means flexibility and experimentation; diversity of approaches strengthens systems over single rigid solutions.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need to celebrate small wins and maintain morale in long innovation cycles while also maintaining urgency and accountability to avoid endless delays?

## India Angle
Indian pharma and biotech leaders can adopt the risk-taking funnel model for breakthrough drug development; Pratham's 'teaching-at-the-right-level' approach offers a replicable model for Indian schools addressing learning loss and foundational skills gaps post-pandemic.
