## Core Argument
Indian literary agents transform publishing by discovering talent, scouting for publishers, and adding value across the book lifecycle.

## Key Points
- Indian agents have competitive advantage in discovering debut authors rejected by foreign agents due to limited international market for Indian literature.
- Publishers need agents to scout talent because commissioning editors lack time for author discovery amid competing publishing demands.
- Agents add value beyond brokering deals—they understand market trends, navigate disorganized Indian publishing, and build author-publisher bridges.

## Open Question
How can Indian agents retain authors once relationships develop with publishers, especially when competing against established foreign agents with stronger international networks?

## India Angle
Indian literary agents can compete with foreign counterparts by discovering local talent, understanding domestic publishing dynamics, and filling gaps in India's disorganized publishing ecosystem where editors lack bandwidth.
