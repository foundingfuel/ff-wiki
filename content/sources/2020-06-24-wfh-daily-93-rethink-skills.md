## Core Argument
Pandemic accelerated decade-long shift: organizations need generalist leaders and redesigned skill development, not traditional MBA models.

## Key Points
- Business schools must shift from campus-centric to blended, customized learning programs that track individual outcomes.
- Generalist thinking—connecting across industries and contexts—outperforms specialist expertise in volatile environments.
- Career transitions require self-awareness and willingness to apply existing skills in entirely new domains for greater fulfillment.

## Open Question
If generalists outperform specialists in volatile times, how do organizations maintain deep expertise and innovation in critical domains?

## India Angle
Indian business schools and corporations must urgently redesign leadership development to prepare for rapid change; Indian professionals need to develop generalist thinking rather than siloed expertise to stay relevant in a volatile post-pandemic economy.
