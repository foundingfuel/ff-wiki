## Core Argument
Baidu must abandon search-ad dependency and diversify into new revenue models to survive.

## Key Points
- Baidu's 80% search-ad revenue reliance became unsustainable after Wei Zexi scandal exposed ad-result confusion.
- Early success came from ground-level sales strategy, localised products (MP3, Tieba), and regulatory alignment—not just technology.
- Diversification challenge: unlike Alibaba and Tencent, Baidu lacks ecosystem depth and faces Google competition globally.

## Open Question
If Baidu abandons search ads to diversify, does it lose its core competency, and can it compete with already-entrenched Alibaba and Tencent in their domains?

## India Angle
Indian search and e-commerce platforms (Google, Flipkart, Amazon) face similar pressures: over-reliance on single revenue streams, regulatory scrutiny on ad transparency, and competition from diversified tech giants. Baidu's struggle mirrors potential vulnerabilities in India's own internet economy.
