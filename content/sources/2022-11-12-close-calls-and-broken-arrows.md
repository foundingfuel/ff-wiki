## Core Argument
Human judgment in nuclear decision-making prevented catastrophe; AI systems replacing it could end civilization.

## Key Points
- Historical close calls (Cuban Missile Crisis, 1983 Soviet false alarm) were averted by individual human judgment, not systems.
- Accidental nuclear incidents ('broken arrows') caused radioactive releases; control systems proved fallible during Cold War.
- AI-driven early warning systems remove human discretion, risking irreversible escalation from misinterpretation or technical failure.

## Open Question
If human judgment has repeatedly prevented nuclear war but is fallible, how can we ensure AI systems are more reliable without human override capabilities that slow response times?

## India Angle
India, a nuclear-armed nation with Pakistan, faces similar risks of miscalculation and accidental escalation; the article's argument about AI replacing human judgment in nuclear decision-making directly applies to India's strategic command systems and future vulnerabilities.
