## Core Argument
Machine learning's true power lies in human judgment on data quality, interdisciplinary collaboration, and nurturing curiosity from childhood.

## Key Points
- Data quantity matters less than feature engineering and thoughtful data preparation for algorithm training.
- Cross-functional teams with mutual respect across disciplines drive breakthrough AI applications in healthcare, climate, governance.
- Innovation mindset requires early unstructured exploration, observation of nature, and hobbies—not just formal STEM education.

## Open Question
How do we balance the need for rigorous formal STEM training with the emphasis on unstructured, curiosity-driven learning to actually produce more innovators?
