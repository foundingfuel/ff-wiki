## Core Argument
Prioritizing personal happiness over purpose creates misery; true growth requires ego restraint.

## Key Points
- Self-aggrandizement as strategy never produces lasting happiness or sustainable outcomes.
- Tata Neu's super app struggles because it conflates distinct brand equities and customer loyalty patterns.
- Social networks fail because they manage behavior, not topics—requiring collective restraint impossible to enforce.

## Open Question
If happiness comes from restraint and service to others, how do ambitious organizations scale while maintaining genuine purpose-alignment across heterogeneous business units and stakeholders?

## India Angle
Indian businesses like Tata are grappling with how to balance ego-driven expansion with genuine customer value; applies to how Indian entrepreneurs and leaders navigate growth without losing sight of purpose.
