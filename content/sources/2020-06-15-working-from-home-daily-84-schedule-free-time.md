## Core Argument
Scheduling free time as non-negotiable prevents burnout and maintains long-term productivity.

## Key Points
- Paradox of learning: intense focus blocks fresh approaches; success requires balancing persistence with failure.
- Marc Andreessen evolved from no-schedule to scheduling everything including sleep and free time to prevent resentment.
- Trust and cultural sensitivity in crises: Dharavi's COVID response improved when authorities accommodated religious observances and provided care.

## Open Question
How do knowledge workers know when scheduling every activity (including rest) becomes counter-productive constraint rather than liberating structure?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers transitioning to WFH need to balance intense focus with recovery time; Dharavi case shows how trust and cultural sensitivity enable community cooperation during crises—applicable to Indian organizational cultures.
