## Core Argument
Good life requires constant course correction, not perfect planning; adaptability beats initial setup.

## Key Points
- Perfection is a myth—systems succeed through perpetual readjustment, not fixed plans or ideal setups.
- Leadership demands clarity on principles while flexibility on execution; Bedi's conviction vs captain's suggestions.
- Success requires balancing ambition with grounding; Nooyi's mother teaching her to leave crown at garage.

## Open Question
How do you maintain conviction and long-term vision while constantly revising tactics—where's the line between adaptive wisdom and lack of strategy?
