## Core Argument
Self-handicapping is a sophisticated pre-emptive excuse strategy rooted in fear of failure and performance anxiety.

## Key Points
- Self-handicapping involves deliberately creating obstacles before performance to protect self-image regardless of outcome.
- Unlike retrospective excuses, pre-emptive handicaps reduce cognitive dissonance by externalizing failure beforehand.
- High performers use this strategy most, suggesting it masks deep insecurity beneath competence.

## Open Question
If self-handicapping is irrational and self-defeating, why do the brightest and most capable people employ it most frequently?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and students frequently use self-handicapping in competitive environments (exams, corporate hierarchies); understanding this pattern is critical for building high-performance cultures in Indian organizations where face-saving and excuse-making are culturally prevalent.
