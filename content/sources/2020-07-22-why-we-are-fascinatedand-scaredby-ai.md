## Core Argument
AI's social integration outpaces ethical frameworks, creating simultaneous opportunity and existential risk.

## Key Points
- AI already permeates economy and society through design, content generation, and misinformation—before ethics frameworks exist.
- Deepfakes, AI-generated text, and synthetic media blur reality lines, threatening democracy, trust, and individual agency.
- Human behavior remains security weak point; technical solutions alone insufficient without systemic ethical governance.

## Open Question
How do we build ethical guardrails for AI technology that's already embedded in business and society, without halting beneficial innovation?

## India Angle
India faces particular vulnerability to AI-driven misinformation and deepfakes given social media penetration, weak digital literacy, and polarized information ecosystems. India also needs frameworks for responsible AI adoption in governance and business without stifling innovation.
