## Core Argument
India's aviation safety investigation must balance institutional capability-building with public transparency to rebuild confidence.

## Key Points
- First Boeing 787 fatal crash tests India's new Rs 9 crore digital black box lab and technical independence from NTSB.
- Government opacity and media leaks erode public trust; NTSB and international bodies calling for swift, transparent data release.
- Availability heuristic drives public fear despite flying being statistically safer; root cause disclosure critical for confidence recovery.

## Open Question
How can India demonstrate technical independence and build domestic aviation safety capability without appearing defensive or non-cooperative with international aviation bodies?

## India Angle
India's aviation safety investigation capability, institutional transparency, and technical independence from Western agencies—AAIB's first real test of new indigenous black box decoding lab; reflects broader state capacity questions.
