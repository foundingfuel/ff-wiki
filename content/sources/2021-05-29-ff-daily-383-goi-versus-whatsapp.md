## Core Argument
Traceability rules create unprecedented surveillance infrastructure incompatible with fundamental rights and practical feasibility.

## Key Points
- Tagging 100B+ daily messages to track originators creates technological and privacy panopticon never seen before.
- GoI argues WhatsApp policies discriminate against Indian users; WhatsApp counters rules violate constitutional free speech rights.
- Identifying message originators years later requires retroactive tracking of all user communications ever sent.

## Open Question
How can governments balance legitimate security concerns about misinformation/mob violence with the technical impossibility and fundamental rights violations of creating a complete message-origin tracking system?

## India Angle
India-specific: GoI's digital rules mandate message traceability on WhatsApp, raising questions about surveillance infrastructure, constitutional rights, and balancing national security with privacy in a country with 400M+ WhatsApp users.
