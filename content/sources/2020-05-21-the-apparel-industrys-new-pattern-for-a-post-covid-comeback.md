## Core Argument
Post-Covid apparel recovery requires digital transformation, quality mindset shift, and supply chain agility beyond cost competition.

## Key Points
- Direct-to-consumer digital channels and virtual showcases replace trade shows; online experience design becomes competitive differentiator.
- India's competitive advantage lies in distributed manufacturing and quality compliance standards, not cheaper alternatives to China.
- Agile, fast-fashion product development requires supplier collaboration and inventory discipline; speed over disposability is the new model.

## Open Question
How can Indian firms simultaneously pursue global market ambition while building domestic merchant ecosystems and breaking lazy market habits cultivated by a large home market?

## India Angle
Indian apparel industry must shift from low-cost positioning to quality-first exports, leverage anti-China sentiment, build distributed manufacturing, and adopt digital-first models to compete with Bangladesh and Vietnam while capturing emerging markets beyond traditional Western buyers.
