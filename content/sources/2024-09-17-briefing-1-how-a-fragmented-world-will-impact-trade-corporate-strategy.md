## Core Argument
Fragmented blocs reshape global trade; companies must embed geopolitics and sanctions risk into strategy.

## Key Points
- World dividing into US-aligned, China-Russia, and non-aligned blocs with deep economic consequences.
- Protectionism and friend-shoring replace free trade; supply chains redrawn for redundancy, not cost-efficiency.
- India's trade agreement speed and geopolitical positioning critical for FDI, capital flows, and competitive advantage.

## Open Question
How can India preserve economic gains and capital flows while navigating bloc alignment pressures without sacrificing strategic autonomy or legacy relationships?

## India Angle
India faces a critical juncture as a non-aligned power caught between US-China blocs. Its exclusion from RCEP and CPTPP, slow trade deal negotiations, and complex Russia ties create both risks and opportunities if it acts strategically on trade agreements and FDI positioning.
