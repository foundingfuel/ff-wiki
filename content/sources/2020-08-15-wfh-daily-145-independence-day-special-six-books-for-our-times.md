## Core Argument
India must revisit foundational values and ethical frameworks to navigate digital disruption, cultural evolution, and institutional decay.

## Key Points
- Tagore's vision of fearless, free-thinking India contrasts starkly with current polarization and low-level machinations.
- Algorithms and data-driven decisions now intrude into Indian life; we can preemptively adopt ethical frameworks to avoid Western harms.
- Corporate India has globalized without evolving workplace culture—leaders need introspection on listening, process, and dialogue.

## Open Question
How can India preserve its founding ideals of public service, fierce debate, and pluralism while adapting to globalization, technological disruption, and polarized identity politics?

## India Angle
India at 73 must reckon with its founding ideals versus current realities—algorithms, corporate culture, caste/religion tensions, and public service ethos. These books offer frameworks for navigating India's transition from post-colonial idealism to contemporary complexity.
