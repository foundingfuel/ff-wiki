## Core Argument
Labour Codes shift India from protecting jobs to formalizing work, requiring behavioral change, not just legal compliance.

## Key Points
- Codes apply across all sectors (IT, startups, gig platforms), not just manufacturing, forcing services firms to rethink worker classification.
- Gig worker recognition is historic but protection remains cess-funded welfare, not rights-based, leaving ambiguity for platforms and global firms.
- Wage redefinition (50% basic pay minimum) disrupts compensation structures, creating tension between short-term liquidity and long-term worker security.

## Open Question
How can India implement transparent, rule-based labour governance at scale without recreating the discretionary inspector raj that plagued earlier regimes?

## India Angle
India's Labour Codes represent a fundamental restructuring of how 85-90% of the informal workforce is governed, affecting everything from startup hiring to gig platforms. This directly impacts how Indian companies scale, how workers access welfare, and whether the country can formalize its economy without reverting to inspector raj governance.
