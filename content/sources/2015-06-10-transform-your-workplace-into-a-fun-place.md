## Core Argument
Companies competing for talent must transform workplaces into engaging environments to boost retention, productivity and creativity.

## Key Points
- Happy employees reduce attrition, improve attendance, and increase productivity and creativity.
- Positive mood is contagious—employee happiness directly influences colleague and customer satisfaction.
- Non-financial incentives like time-gifting (HCL's Genie) offer scalable alternatives to costly office redesigns.

## Open Question
How do companies balance the high upfront costs of transforming physical workplaces with measurable ROI, especially in capital-constrained Indian firms?

## India Angle
Indian companies like HCL can adopt employee-centric strategies without Silicon Valley budgets—Genie service shows alternative approaches suited to Indian context and constraints.
