## Core Argument
Leaders gain followership through warmth and relational skills, not competence alone.

## Key Points
- Competence without warmth creates resistance and perception of arrogance in teams.
- Body language and speech patterns directly influence how colleagues perceive leader authenticity.
- Relational warmth transforms team dynamics from compliance to genuine followership.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance necessary decisiveness and authority with the vulnerability required for perceived warmth?

## India Angle
Indian hierarchical workplaces often struggle with top-down leadership; warmth and relational competence are critical for leaders navigating traditional structures while building followership.
