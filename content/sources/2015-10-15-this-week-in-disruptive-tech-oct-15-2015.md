## Core Argument
Disruptive technologies are immature; their real impact hinges on ethics, distribution systems, and what humans choose to value.

## Key Points
- Wearables, crypto, and AI are still early-stage; accuracy, integration, and trust remain unsolved problems.
- Blockchain's value lies in backend infrastructure (settlement efficiency) not bitcoin hype; institutional adoption is the real game.
- Technology may eliminate paid jobs but enable unpaid work; wealth distribution becomes critical policy lever.

## Open Question
If machines handle production efficiently, how do we justify or redesign economic systems around work that's no longer compensated—and who decides whether that's acceptable?

## India Angle
Indian startups and enterprises must understand that wearables, blockchain, and robotics are still 1.0 technologies globally—India has time to leapfrog legacy approaches in these sectors. However, the jobs displacement risk is acute for India's labor-dependent economy; policy must address wealth distribution and reskilling.
