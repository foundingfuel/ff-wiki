## Core Argument
India's bazaar culture reshapes technology through user improvisation, not top-down design—a competitive advantage in AI.

## Key Points
- Users, not companies, discover use cases by stretching technology to local needs and contexts.
- India's historical pattern: CRBTs, WhatsApp groups, mobile repurposing show technology adoption follows cultural logic, not designer intent.
- Success in India requires alignment with user behaviour and local context over pursuing technological novelty.

## Open Question
If India's strength lies in localizing and reshaping global technologies rather than building foundational ones, what prevents Indian entrepreneurs from owning the IP and platforms that capture value from these innovations?

## India Angle
India's strength is not in building foundational technologies but in localizing and reshaping global tools to hyperlocal contexts—from voice AI in regional languages to WhatsApp-based business models. This bazaar-style innovation at scale is India's competitive advantage in the AI era.
