## Core Argument
Iterative learning and user feedback transform webinar experience from failure to high-impact educational tool.

## Key Points
- First Masterclass failed due to technical issues; team iterated based on feedback rather than abandoning idea.
- Success metrics: 90% Net Promoter Score attracts quality audiences of entrepreneurs, executives, and educators.
- Key challenges include audience curation, engagement over 90 minutes, and helping learners consolidate insights post-session.

## Open Question
How do you scale deep, reflective learning experiences while maintaining quality and community engagement as audience grows?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and executives need access to quality, curated learning experiences that go beyond noise and enable deep reflection—a gap Founding Fuel is addressing through its Masterclass format.
