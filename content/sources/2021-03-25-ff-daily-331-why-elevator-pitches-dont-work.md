## Core Argument
Effective communication requires three minutes to create engagement; elevator pitches oversimplify complex ideas.

## Key Points
- Three minutes allows time to illustrate value, capture attention, and create engagement based on approach motivation science.
- Institution-building demands leaders enforce non-negotiable standards: research, consulting, and teaching together, not teaching alone.
- Clarity, simplicity, and separating what you want to say from what needs to be said drive compelling communication.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance institutional standards with faculty autonomy and retention without creating brain drain or resentment?

## India Angle
Indian institutions like IIMs and IITs succeeded by enforcing rigorous standards on research, consulting, and teaching—a model relevant for India's aspirations to build world-class institutions amid recent controversies over institutional governance.
