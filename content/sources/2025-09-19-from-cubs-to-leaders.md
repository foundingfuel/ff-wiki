## Core Argument
Leadership develops in stages—from nurtured novice through rivalry and friction to steward and visionary.

## Key Points
- Cubs need safe scaffolding to explore, fail, and learn without fear—mirrors early corporate mentorship.
- Peer friction and competitive 'play' sharpen resilience; mentors guide subtly without smothering.
- Mature leaders shift from claiming ground to stewardship—guardianship over system, not ego-driven dominance.

## Open Question
How do modern flat, fast-moving organisations create the 'safe sanctuaries' for developmental friction that older institutions like TMTC and Gulita provided, without slowing execution?

## India Angle
Indian corporates like Tata, HUL, and Unilever built legendary management training ecosystems (TMTC, TAS, Gulita, Four Acres) that embodied this developmental arc. As these institutions evolve, India's leadership pipeline depends on whether organisations can still provide such 'sanctuaries' for developmental friction.
