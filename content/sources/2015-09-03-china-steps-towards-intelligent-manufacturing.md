## Core Argument
China's intelligent manufacturing push will reshape global supply chains; India must act strategically or lose competitiveness.

## Key Points
- China deploying massive automation (Foxconn's 1M robots) to address rising labor costs and move up value chain
- Made in China 2025 plan uses IoT, cloud computing, big data to transform manufacturing into smart factories
- Implementation challenges include skills gaps, uneven sectoral development, and IP issues that could derail transformation

## Open Question
If China successfully automates and upgrades manufacturing quality, what happens to India's labor-cost advantage, and does India have a credible strategy to compete on innovation rather than just cost?

## India Angle
China's shift to high-tech manufacturing via Made in China 2025 has direct implications for India's competitive positioning. As China automates, India risks inheriting low-cost assembly work but must also prepare for the same automation wave. Indian policymakers and manufacturers need their own intelligent manufacturing strategy to avoid becoming merely the next cheap labor destination.
