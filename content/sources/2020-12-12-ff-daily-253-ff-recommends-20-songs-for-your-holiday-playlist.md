## Core Argument
Music transcends language and circumstance, offering emotional anchors during uncertain times and seasonal transitions.

## Key Points
- Algorithmic recommendations reshape how we discover and rediscover favorite music continuously
- Personal music choices reflect emotional needs rather than social trends or cool factor
- Indian classical and film music carry cultural memory and philosophical depth across generations

## Open Question
How do we balance algorithmic serendipity in music discovery with the deeper, more intentional emotional connections people develop with songs over time?
