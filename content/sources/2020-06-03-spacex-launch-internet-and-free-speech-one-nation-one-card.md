## Core Argument
Build system capacity through partnerships, not isolated capacity; decentralize platforms via protocols, not centralized control.

## Key Points
- NASA's partnership with SpaceX strengthened overall US space capacity more than government-only approach would have.
- Twitter's moderation dilemma shows centralized platforms struggle between neutrality and control; decentralized protocols offer alternative.
- India's crisis programmes (One Nation One Card, UWID) show acceleration of digital infrastructure but need systemic redesign.

## Open Question
If decentralized platforms solve content moderation problems, who ensures accountability and prevents genuine harm without centralized control mechanisms?

## India Angle
India's healthcare and social security systems need systemic capacity-building through public-private partnerships, not just government expansion. Digital infrastructure (One Nation One Card, UWID) can address India's informal economy challenges during crises, but requires rethinking platform governance for India's context.
