## Core Argument
Coaching credentials matter more than reputation; India conflates cricketing greatness with coaching capability.

## Key Points
- Selection committees prioritize familiarity and player reputation over actual coaching experience and credentials.
- Trust breakdown between coach and captain cannot be repaired after public rupture; organizational separation becomes necessary.
- Indian businesses repeat this error: hiring illustrious former CEOs as coaches without examining their actual coaching track record.

## Open Question
If Anil Kumble's cricketing credentials were impeccable and his selection unanimous, why wasn't deeper vetting of his coaching abilities conducted beforehand, and can this be prevented in corporate hiring?

## India Angle
Indian cricket's coach-captain dynamics reveal systemic issues in leadership selection and organizational governance applicable to Indian businesses hiring external coaches and leaders without proper vetting
