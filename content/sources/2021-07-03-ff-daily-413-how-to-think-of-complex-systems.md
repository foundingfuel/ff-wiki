## Core Argument
Understanding complex systems requires identifying right components, not mistaking complexity for complication.

## Key Points
- Complex systems become understandable by isolating simpler interacting components, not by memorising all details.
- Regulators and businesses misalign on e-commerce: price competition vs. level playing field protection create unresolved policy tensions.
- Globalisation's benefits unevenly distributed; Western middle classes' exclusion reshaping political and regulatory sentiment toward Big Tech.

## Open Question
How can regulators design rules that protect fairness and competition without blocking the price-driven innovation models that made platforms valuable to consumers in emerging markets?

## India Angle
India's e-commerce regulations and Big Tech friction reflect broader global tensions, but India's 1.4B market and regulatory approach to flash sales/discounting directly impacts Amazon and Walmart's business models in ways unique to emerging markets.
