## Core Argument
Professional boards can enable family business succession by balancing institutional reputation with pragmatic family continuity.

## Key Points
- Azim Premji leveraged independent directors to resolve conflict between professional management and family succession.
- Rishad's 12-year grooming period and external roles earned credibility beyond family legacy alone.
- Premji's ability to cede control and avoid remote-managing the transition distinguishes this from typical patriarch-led successions.

## Open Question
How can Indian family businesses scale this board-driven governance model when informal power dynamics and founder dominance remain endemic across India Inc.?

## India Angle
Wipro's succession demonstrates that Indian family businesses can professionalize transitions through independent board governance, setting a rare example for India Inc.'s largely ad-hoc succession practices.
