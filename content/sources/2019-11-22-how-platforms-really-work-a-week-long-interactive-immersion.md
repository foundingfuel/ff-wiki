## Core Argument
Platform businesses promise democratization but risk creating monopolies; leaders must understand their power to respond strategically.

## Key Points
- Platform businesses (Uber, Airbnb, Swiggy) have scaled massively but raise questions about monopolistic power and market concentration.
- Every enterprise now faces competition from platform players and must rethink strategic responses to platform disruption.
- Regulators, suppliers, and users must assess both positive and negative impacts of platform technologies on industries and society.

## Open Question
How can platforms simultaneously democratize business access while avoiding the monopolistic concentration that threatens competition and innovation?

## India Angle
Indian platform companies (Ola, Oyo, Swiggy, Zomato) are at the center of this disruption; Indian regulators and enterprises must understand platform power dynamics and monopolistic risks specific to India's market structure and regulatory environment.
