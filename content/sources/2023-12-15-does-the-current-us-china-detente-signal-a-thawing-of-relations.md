## Core Argument
US-China détente masks structural antagonism; tactical pause, not strategic reconciliation.

## Key Points
- Economic interdependence ($700B trade) and mutual MAD prevent decoupling but enable tactical engagement.
- Tech supply chain restrictions (CHIPS Act, export controls) remain the deepest structural obstacle to genuine cooperation.
- US frames policy as 'de-risking'; China interprets it as containment—semantic disagreement reflects unbridgeable strategic mistrust.

## Open Question
If both powers recognize economic interdependence prevents full decoupling and mutual destruction, why does China view every US stabilization attempt as containment rather than genuine floor-setting?

## India Angle
India is explicitly mentioned as a strategic US partner receiving major tech and defense agreements, signaling India's growing importance in US containment strategy against China. For Indian businesses, US-China tensions directly affect supply chains, FDI flows, and India's own tech-geopolitical positioning.
