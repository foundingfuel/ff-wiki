## Core Argument
Chinese brands succeed globally through committed investment, local autonomy, and brand purpose—not just product quality.

## Key Points
- Low brand awareness and quality perception persist despite good products; brand building requires long-term commitment and heavy investment.
- Successful Chinese brands (DJI, Huawei) decentralize decision-making to local operations and invest in market research, not just test-and-withdraw strategies.
- Brand purpose and positioning—not just sales growth targets—differentiate winning global brands from 'me-too' competitors.

## Open Question
How can resource-constrained startups balance the required long-term brand investment with the need to preserve capital and test markets before full commitment?

## India Angle
Indian companies face similar challenges as Chinese brands in global markets—building brand equity beyond product quality, requiring committed long-term investment, local market understanding, and clear brand purpose rather than just sales targets. Indian startups in hardware/tech must learn from DJI and Huawei's playbook.
