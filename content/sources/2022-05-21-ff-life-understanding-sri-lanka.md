## Core Argument
Understanding Sri Lanka's complex history through literature and film reveals nuanced realities beyond polarized narratives.

## Key Points
- Sri Lanka's 2022 economic crisis stems from policy failures, pandemic impact, and structural issues rooted in post-civil war governance.
- Sri Lankan Tamil conflict has deep historical roots in discriminatory policies from 1950s-60s, not merely recent events.
- Personal narratives and artistic works reveal human complexity that simplistic political frameworks obscure or ignore.

## Open Question
How can polarized communities access nuanced understanding of conflict when emotional stakes and personal suffering shape competing narratives?

## India Angle
Sri Lanka's crisis reflects regional instability affecting India; Tamil Nadu's historical ties to Sri Lankan conflict remain politically sensitive; Indian influence competition with China shapes regional dynamics.
