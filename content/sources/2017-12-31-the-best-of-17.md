## Core Argument
Technology innovation must balance disruption with solutions for India's complex societal challenges through locally-relevant entrepreneurial leadership.

## Key Points
- Low-cost, disruptive solutions like Khan Academy and Medtronic Labs exemplify how emerging markets need localised innovation approaches.
- India's e-commerce and startup ecosystems face transition: founders giving way to strategic investors, requiring new structural models.
- Evaluating disruptive tech requires three questions: equal benefit potential, risk-reward clarity, and autonomy versus dependence effects.

## Open Question
How can India build technology solutions that are simultaneously globally competitive, locally affordable, and aligned with policy frameworks that remain disconnected from ground realities?

## India Angle
India faces a critical inflection point where technology-led innovation must solve deep societal problems affordably and locally; policy frameworks in Delhi remain disconnected from ground realities, creating challenges for entrepreneurship and e-commerce scaling.
