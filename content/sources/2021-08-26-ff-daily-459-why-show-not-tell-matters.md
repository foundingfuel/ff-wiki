## Core Argument
Showing, not telling—demonstrating products and authentic presence—persuades investors better than polished presentations.

## Key Points
- Authentic personal connection and ease matter more than formal presentation slides.
- Live product demonstration triggered investor engagement where slides and rhetoric failed.
- Founder shifted from genuine personality to formal mode, losing credibility and momentum.

## Open Question
How can founders balance the need for professionalism and preparation with maintaining authentic, relaxed energy in high-stakes investor meetings?

## India Angle
Indian founders and entrepreneurs often struggle with authentic storytelling in investor pitches; learning to balance narrative with demonstration is critical for accessing capital and building investor trust.
