## Core Argument
Enduring business success requires competing on differentiation and revenue growth, not cost and price.

## Key Points
- Better before cheaper: compete on differentiation instead of cost to build sustainable competitive advantage.
- Revenue before cost: prioritize growing top-line through customer value, not squeezing margins through cost-cutting.
- Systemic alignment: all business decisions must reinforce the core rules, creating cascading benefits across operations.

## Open Question
How do companies in highly price-sensitive markets (like India) adopt differentiation-first strategies without losing market share to cost competitors?

## India Angle
Indian companies often compete primarily on cost and price; this framework challenges that mindset and shows how Indian businesses can build sustainable competitive advantage through differentiation and revenue growth—critical for moving beyond margin-squeezed commodity competition.
