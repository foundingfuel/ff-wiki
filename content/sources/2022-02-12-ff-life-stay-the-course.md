## Core Argument
Sticking to resolutions requires understanding delayed compounding results and rejecting willpower myths.

## Key Points
- New Year functions as psychological 'chapter break' enabling fresh-start narratives and behavioral change.
- Valley of Disappointment causes early abandonment; exponential gains emerge only after sustained effort.
- Chronic disease prevention through lifestyle change matters more than longevity; quality-of-life focus motivates persistence.

## Open Question
If willpower isn't finite and resolutions are psychologically sound, why do most people still fail within weeks despite understanding these principles?

## India Angle
Indian context: most Indians will face chronic disease before death; resolutions around health are culturally relevant given healthcare accessibility and lifestyle disease burden; quality of life in aging years is underexplored in Indian discourse.
