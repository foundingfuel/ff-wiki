## Core Argument
Jazz appreciation requires pattern recognition built through exposure; understanding its history unlocks the genre.

## Key Points
- Music appreciation depends on brain's pattern recognition; surprise comes from subverting established patterns.
- Jazz emerged from African-American cultural memory adapting African musical intervals to Western instruments via 'blue notes'.
- Miles Davis shifted Jazz through modal innovation, freeing soloists from chord restrictions and enabling reharmonisation.

## Open Question
How can Jazz be meaningfully appreciated by newcomers when the genre deliberately requires extensive prior exposure to understand its rule-breaking?
