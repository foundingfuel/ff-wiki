## Core Argument
Complex systems require holding opposing truths simultaneously; opposites coexist and enhance rather than negate each other.

## Key Points
- Paradoxical thinking transcends linear logic; frozen food enabled both fast food and gourmet culture simultaneously.
- China's nuclear expansion ($440B, 150 reactors) demonstrates clear climate strategy; state financing makes nuclear economically viable.
- Geopolitical tensions prevent technology adoption; US, India unlikely adopting Chinese nuclear tech despite safety and cost-effectiveness.

## Open Question
How can India pursue ambitious net-zero goals without adopting cost-effective foreign technology, yet lacks domestic capacity to match China's scale and speed?

## India Angle
India's net-zero 2070 commitment lacks clarity compared to China's nuclear roadmap; India faces geopolitical constraints adopting foreign clean energy tech; crypto-asset appreciation by Indian entrepreneurs reflects emerging digital economy participation.
