## Core Argument
Corporations innovate faster by collaborating with startups; smartest talent exists outside your walls.

## Key Points
- Large companies must adopt 'connected innovation'—co-creation with external ecosystem partners, not just internal labs.
- Four dimensions of thought leadership: translation, assimilation, co-creation, and transformation of management philosophy itself.
- Innovation ecosystems require managing multiple nodes—entrepreneurs, VCs, universities, incubators—each with different goals and collaboration modes.

## Open Question
How do large corporations actually incentivize and structure adoption of 'connected innovation' when their organizational DNA, incentives, and talent management systems remain hierarchical and insular?

## India Angle
Indian corporations must shift from insular R&D models to open innovation ecosystems; management education institutions need to bridge industry-academia gap to prepare leaders for collaborative innovation in digital economy.
