## Core Argument
Competing with non-consumption requires ground-up design, extreme affordability, and immersion in local context.

## Key Points
- De-featuring premium products fails; successful BoP innovation builds ground-up to unique local conditions.
- Extreme affordability (75% functionality at 50% cost, sometimes far deeper) is non-negotiable for scale.
- Immersion and co-creation with end-users unlock insights premium-market approaches miss entirely.

## Open Question
How do companies justify the R&D investment and margin compression required for extreme affordability when BoP volumes may take years to materialize?

## India Angle
Indian businesses must innovate ground-up for 300+ million BoP consumers with limited purchasing power; successful models (HUL Shakti, GE Healthcare, Gillette Guard) show immersion and co-creation unlock unmet rural demand.
