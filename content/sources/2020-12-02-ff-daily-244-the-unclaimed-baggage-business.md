## Core Argument
Unclaimed baggage creates niche retail opportunities; structural economic factors determine pandemic recovery resilience.

## Key Points
- 25 million of 4.3 billion checked bags annually go lost; unsold ones create secondary market opportunities.
- Unclaimed Baggage monopolizes US niche by aggregating airline auctions; India's AAI lacks similar consolidation.
- Economic resilience depends on diversification, currency strength, and flexible policy responses—not just crisis management.

## Open Question
How do you ethically commercialize travelers' lost intimate possessions while respecting privacy and emotional value?

## India Angle
India's Airports Authority conducts similar unclaimed baggage auctions, but lacks the organized secondary market that exists in the US; opportunity for entrepreneurship in logistics and retail of recovered goods, and policy clarity around consumer rights and transparency.
