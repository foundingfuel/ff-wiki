## Core Argument
WFH disruption demands rhythm redesign and marathon pacing, not office relocation to homes.

## Key Points
- Indian homes lack space/separation for full-time work with joint families; leaders must acknowledge this structural reality
- Unsustainable 'business-as-usual' mentality causes fatigue; teams need prioritised work, smaller targets, new routines
- Informal connections disappear in remote setups; leaders must intentionally create spaces for vulnerability, listening, and banter

## Open Question
How do organisations sustain performance on a 'marathon' timeline when business urgency and financial pressures demand immediate results?

## India Angle
Indian organisations unprepared for WFH due to joint family structures, shared spaces, and absence of remote-work infrastructure; leadership must redesign workflows and expectations for Indian home contexts, not just relocate office work.
