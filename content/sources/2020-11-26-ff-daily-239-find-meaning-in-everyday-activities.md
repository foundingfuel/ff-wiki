## Core Argument
Define success backwards; find meaning in everyday work to sustain performance through uncertainty.

## Key Points
- Lawrence Bragg found deep meaning in gardening despite Nobel Prize status—mundane activities carry intrinsic value.
- Vaccination programs fail when efficacy is high but deployment is poor—execution matters as much as strategy.
- Invert decision-making: start with vision of success rather than problem definition when facing ambiguity.

## Open Question
How do we maintain motivation and excellence in routine tasks when society valorizes only extraordinary achievement?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating ambiguity in career transitions and organizational change can adopt outcome-focused decision-making; finding meaning in routine work applies across India's emerging knowledge and service sectors.
