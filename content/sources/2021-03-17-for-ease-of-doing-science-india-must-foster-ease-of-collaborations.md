## Core Argument
India must systematize research collaborations across academia-industry-startups to unlock innovation and boost R&D productivity.

## Key Points
- India's university-industry research collaboration share declined 6.1% CAGR (2009-2013), worse than global -3.7%, while China grew 1.3%.
- Pathbreaking discoveries require multi-level collaborations—advisor-student, postdoc-mentor, inter-institutional—but Indian labs lack scale and infrastructure.
- Covid-era ventilator/vaccine success shows Indian collaboration capability exists; systemic barriers prevent it from becoming default mode.

## Open Question
India demonstrated collaborative R&D capacity during Covid crisis, yet structural incentives and institutional scale persistently suppress routine collaboration—what policy levers can make emergency-mode collaboration the norm?

## India Angle
India's R&D collaboration metrics are declining (-6.1% CAGR 2009-2013) versus global average, and institutional scale lags developed nations. Policy must enable cross-sector collaboration models to meet 2% GDP R&D spending aspiration.
