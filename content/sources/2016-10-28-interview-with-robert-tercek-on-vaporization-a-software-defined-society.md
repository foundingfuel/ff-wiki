## Core Argument
Software replaces physical products; all companies must become software companies or face disruption.

## Key Points
- Vaporization: digital software replaces physical objects across industries—media, music, finance already transformed.
- Network economics: devices cheaper but networks more valuable; manufacturing companies misunderstand this shift.
- No industry immune: healthcare, transportation, education, government will be transformed as software erodes geography constraints.

## Open Question
If software eliminates geographic advantage and enables distributed labour, how do traditional corporate hierarchies and nation-states maintain control and tax revenue?

## India Angle
Indian manufacturing and services sectors face disruption as software-defined models eliminate geographic arbitrage advantages that drove outsourcing. Traditional Indian manufacturers and businesses must reimagine themselves as software-first companies or risk obsolescence.
