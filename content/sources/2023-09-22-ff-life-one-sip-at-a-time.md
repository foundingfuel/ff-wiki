## Core Argument
Niche wine importing thrives by understanding and serving underserved sophisticated consumers willing to explore non-mainstream products.

## Key Points
- Kadakia identified market gap between Indian alcohol preferences and his own sophisticated wine tastes, becoming his own supplier.
- Wine Park succeeded by building trust with high-disposable-income consumers aged 40+ who value experimentation over brand reputation.
- Market evolution shows generational divide: older cohorts seek boutique wines while Gen Z drives growth in Indian whiskeys, gins, craft beers.

## Open Question
How can niche, curated product businesses scale sustainably when their core audience is deliberately limited and resistant to mass-market expansion?

## India Angle
How niche, curated imports can create and serve sophisticated consumer segments in India's evolving alcohol market, particularly among affluent, well-travelled demographics distinct from mass-market trends.
