## Core Argument
Travel reveals nature's raw reality beyond romantic literature and tests human adaptability in untamed environments.

## Key Points
- Michener's poetic vision of migration contrasts sharply with visceral, terrifying wildlife encounters in reality.
- Tanzania's infrastructure and governance feel similar to pre-liberalization India—slow, congested, but rewarding to those patient enough.
- Ngorongoto crater's ecosystem operates as a closed, self-sustaining world where predator-prey relationships define survival.

## Open Question
How do we reconcile our romanticized expectations of wild nature with the brutal, amoral reality of survival—and what does this gap reveal about ourselves?
