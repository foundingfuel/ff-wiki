## Core Argument
Global convergence of problems—scarcity and abundance issues merging—positions India to lead worldwide innovation.

## Key Points
- Chronic diseases, water scarcity, and pollution are no longer regional; they now affect rich and poor nations equally.
- India has decades of experience solving problems under constraint; the West now faces similar challenges.
- Millennials and women worldwide share common aspirations, creating unified constituencies for solutions.

## Open Question
If India has solved these problems through jugaad for decades, why hasn't global adoption happened until wealthy nations faced the same crises?

## India Angle
India faces chronic diseases, water scarcity, and pollution first—but now wealthy nations face identical problems. India's experience solving resource-constrained challenges positions it to lead global solutions and claim soft power leadership.
