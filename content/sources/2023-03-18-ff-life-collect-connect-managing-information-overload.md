## Core Argument
Curating, connecting, and narrating information transforms overwhelm into actionable knowledge systems.

## Key Points
- Information hoarding without purpose wastes effort; Evergreen Notes (atomic, interlinked) prevent passive accumulation.
- Connecting ideas improves memory retention and reveals fresh perspectives; tools like Obsidian visualize these connections.
- Raw ideas are meaningless until embedded in narrative; storytelling (via Pyramid Principle) transforms scattered notes into coherent frameworks.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between deeply connected knowledge systems and elaborate rationalizations that merely feel more organized?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers—startup founders, executives, journalists—face acute information overload given diverse media landscape; systematic personal knowledge management becomes competitive advantage for professional effectiveness and personal growth.
