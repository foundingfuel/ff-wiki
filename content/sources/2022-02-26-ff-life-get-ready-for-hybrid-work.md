## Core Argument
Hybrid work demands wardrobe shift from comfort-first WFH dressing to versatile, sustainable professional basics.

## Key Points
- Hybrid workers prefer versatile, easy-to-maintain separates over trend-driven pieces for office-WFH switching.
- Capsule wardrobes with neutral palettes (8-12 shirts, 4-6 pants for men) enable maximum outfit combinations with minimal pieces.
- Sustainable fabrics like modal and viscose blends replace traditional materials, matching professional needs with comfort and durability.

## Open Question
How do professionals maintain personal style and identity when standardizing to neutral capsule wardrobes for hybrid work environments?
