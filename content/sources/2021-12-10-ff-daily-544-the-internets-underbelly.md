## Core Argument
Sophisticated cyber threats and tech investment opacity demand new assessment frameworks for investors and policymakers.

## Key Points
- Stuxnet demonstrated cyberweapons can destroy critical infrastructure; same code threatens US/global plants indiscriminately.
- Indian tech IPOs require scrutiny: look for cash generation proof, stable leadership, and accounting transparency—not just growth.
- Best tech businesses historically require minimal capital; skepticism warranted when founders seek excessive funding.

## Open Question
How can regulators and investors assess tech companies' true cash generation and safety when operational complexity obscures accountability?

## India Angle
Indian internet companies face IPO scrutiny; investors need frameworks to assess tech valuations and governance. Cyber vulnerabilities affect India's critical infrastructure and digital economy security.
