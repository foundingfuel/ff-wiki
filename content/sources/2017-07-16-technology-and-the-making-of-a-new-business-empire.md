## Core Argument
Tech empires are built through economies of engagement, not scope and scale—an oblique path to dominance.

## Key Points
- Amazon and tech giants pursue growth indirectly via engagement networks rather than traditional expansion metrics.
- Obliquity principle: most profitable companies don't focus solely on profit; happiness isn't found through direct pursuit.
- Business leaders must study history and biology to understand growth imperatives beyond quarterly metrics.

## Open Question
If growth through engagement is superior to scale-based strategies, why do most business leaders still obsess over traditional expansion metrics and quarterly targets?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and business leaders must understand how tech giants are building empires through engagement rather than traditional scale—critical for Indian startups competing globally and large enterprises adapting to new competitive dynamics.
