## Core Argument
Grief and anticipatory anxiety require listening, rituals, community groups, and structured planning—not positivity.

## Key Points
- Listen without advice; develop new rituals to honor loss and maintain connection to departed.
- Tragic optimism—finding meaning in crisis—requires forming support groups and advance care planning.
- Tech-enabled mental health tools (Wysa, Youper, Moodpath) provide accessible, AI-guided emotional support.

## Open Question
How do individuals balance the psychological need for structure and planning around death with the denial and normalcy-seeking that may be necessary for daily functioning?
