## Core Argument
Decentralized systems fail to resist market concentration and state capture, recreating centralized power in new forms.

## Key Points
- Internet platforms intended to democratize have become centralized data monopolies (Amazon, Facebook, Alibaba) beyond regulatory reach.
- Global specialization and trade lowered prices; protectionism risks reversing decades of innovation and affordability gains.
- Distributed trust mechanisms (blockchain, social credit) vulnerable to capture by both market forces and authoritarian governments.

## Open Question
How can societies achieve genuine decentralization and distributed power if all known mechanisms—market, technology, governance—tend toward re-concentration?

## India Angle
India faces twin pressures: managing inflationary impacts of protectionism while building institutional capacity to regulate concentrated digital power. Global supply chain disruptions directly threaten India's IT and manufacturing competitiveness. Policy must balance self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat) with avoiding protectionist traps that raise consumer prices.
