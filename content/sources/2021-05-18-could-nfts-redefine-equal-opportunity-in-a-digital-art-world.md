## Core Argument
NFTs democratize digital art ownership, reducing barriers for women creators and challenging traditional art world gatekeeping.

## Key Points
- NFTs provide verifiable digital ownership through blockchain, reducing art theft while enabling global market access.
- Traditional art world excludes many creators; NFTs bypass gatekeepers and exclusive networks based on merit alone.
- Indian entrepreneurs like Aparajita Jain are building curated NFT platforms to empower women creators in India's emerging crypto art space.

## Open Question
If NFTs truly level playing field for marginalized creators, why do success stories still predominantly feature celebrity endorsements (Paris Hilton, Krista Kim) rather than organic grassroots adoption?

## India Angle
India's art market remains gatekept by traditional institutions; NFTs could democratize access for Indian women artists and creators, bypassing geographic and social barriers to reach global buyers—similar to how fintech disrupted banking in India.
