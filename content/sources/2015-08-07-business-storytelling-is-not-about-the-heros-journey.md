## Core Argument
Business storytelling works through simple, authentic anecdotes, not grand Hero's Journey narratives.

## Key Points
- Business storytelling is 'small s'—anecdotes and experiences that make abstract concepts concrete and memorable.
- Authenticity and personal experience matter more than theatrical skills or complex plot structures.
- Test stories on trusted listeners, state the point explicitly, tell the story, then explain why you believe it.

## Open Question
If business storytelling relies on personal authenticity and individual experiences, how can organizations scale storytelling practices across teams without losing coherence or diluting brand messages?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders often overcomplicate storytelling by chasing Western narrative frameworks; mastering simple, authentic anecdotes could unlock more effective internal communication and customer engagement in Indian organizations.
