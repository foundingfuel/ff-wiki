## Core Argument
Non-expert technologists can solve healthcare crises by asking simple questions and learning from domain experts.

## Key Points
- Preventable blindness in India is both a healthcare and economic crisis; blind persons become family liabilities.
- Founders without healthcare background used tech expertise to innovate low-cost diagnostic solutions.
- Mid-career pivot requires emotional clarity, family buy-in, and exposure to problem scale before committing.

## Open Question
How do technologists with no domain expertise build credibility and avoid costly mistakes when entering unfamiliar sectors like healthcare?

## India Angle
India has 12M+ blind people, 80% preventable cases. Forus Health applies tech entrepreneurship to solve a massive healthcare access problem in rural India where blindness means economic death for families.
