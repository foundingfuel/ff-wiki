## Core Argument
India needs deliberate data governance principles and must reconsider its RCEP exclusion to avoid economic isolation.

## Key Points
- Public data from RTI-eligible sources and government funding should be released as machine-readable, anonymized datasets.
- India's statistical quality is poor; policymakers must prioritize sustained long-term measurement over one-off data collection.
- RCEP exit without alternatives isolates India from Asia's largest trade bloc; rejoining could restore India's historic economic position.

## Open Question
How can India balance legitimate concerns about data misuse and domestic industry protection with the economic necessity of participating in regional trade and data-sharing arrangements?

## India Angle
India must develop a framework for public data release balancing transparency and privacy; RCEP exit represents a significant geopolitical and economic decision with long-term implications for India's regional integration and trade positioning.
