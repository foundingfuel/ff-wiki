## Core Argument
Digital immigrants must actively seek digital solutions first—they're cheaper, flexible, and often better than physical alternatives.

## Key Points
- DigiLocker enables seamless document access without scanning/photocopying, transforming citizen-government interactions.
- Digital tools (iGlass, Sonic, Karabiner) solve physical-world problems more cost-effectively than traditional approaches.
- Deliberate shift to digital-first thinking unlocks flexibility and value that following trends passively cannot deliver.

## Open Question
If digital solutions are demonstrably superior and cost-effective, why do 'digital immigrants' default to physical alternatives despite knowing better?

## India Angle
Digital public infrastructure like DigiLocker exemplifies India Stack's potential to leapfrog physical processes; relevant for Indian citizens adopting digital-first mindsets and government services leveraging digital infrastructure.
