## Core Argument
Deep understanding of individual problems is prerequisite to solving them at scale.

## Key Points
- Jake Stap solved tennis ball pickup by first experiencing his own acute back pain—micro insight drove macro invention.
- Dan Heath argues macro problems require micro understanding; you cannot help 1,000 until you understand helping one.
- Kevin Kelly's 103 pieces of advice emphasize timeless principles: right time is now, create leaders not followers, write things down.

## Open Question
How do you balance deep immersion in individual problems with the need to scale solutions quickly in competitive markets?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders can apply 'solve for one' to local problems—from social enterprises tackling grassroots challenges to corporate innovation; understanding ground-level pain points is critical in India's diverse, fragmented markets before scaling solutions.
