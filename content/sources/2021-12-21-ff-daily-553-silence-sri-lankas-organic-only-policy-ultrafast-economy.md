## Core Argument
Policy transformation fails without farmer consultation, phased implementation, and listening to evidence.

## Key Points
- Sri Lanka's organic ban lacked planning, farmer input, and scientific validation before implementation.
- Ultrafast delivery exploits present bias through artificial scarcity and manufactured impatience, not real need.
- Silence and listening—journalistic and civic—reveal truths that loud proclamations mask.

## Open Question
How do governments balance transformational agricultural change with the practical pace farmers need to adapt, especially in economically vulnerable periods?

## India Angle
Sri Lanka's failed organic-only policy offers critical lessons for India's agricultural policymaking, where similar top-down mandates risk disrupting farm livelihoods without consultation; ultrafast delivery economy thrives in India with Zepto, Dunzo reflecting behavioral vulnerabilities in emerging markets.
