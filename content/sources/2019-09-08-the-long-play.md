## Core Argument
Long-term institutional thinking tolerates failure; rapid scaling without oversight creates invisible risks.

## Key Points
- ISRO's repeated failures (SLV, ASLV, GSLV) were steps toward eventual success through systemic learning.
- Aadhaar enabled Jio's 200M customer acquisition in <2 years but concentrated unprecedented data power without scrutiny.
- Generative adversarial networks (GANs) can create convincing fakes; same logic applies to deepfakes and misinformation.

## Open Question
How can India scale transformational infrastructure (ISRO, Aadhaar, Jio) with tolerance for long-term failure while preventing unchecked data monopolies and technology-enabled misinformation?

## India Angle
India's digital infrastructure (ISRO, Aadhaar) enables breakthrough scale but raises governance questions about data, accountability, and long-term thinking in institutions.
