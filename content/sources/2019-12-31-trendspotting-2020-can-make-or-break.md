## Core Argument
India's internet economy enters maturity: cheap data era ends, unit economics matter, consolidation accelerates.

## Key Points
- Mobile broadband price hikes (40%) signal end of loss-leader telecom subsidies; duopoly consolidation inevitable.
- Startups must demonstrate software-like margins and scalability, not just growth; WeWork/SoftBank reset investor expectations.
- Two internet markets exist: Viewers (consumption-only, price-sensitive) and Consumers (transacting, disposable income); scaling confusion persists.

## Open Question
If price hikes shrink the Internet Viewer market significantly, does India lose the scale advantage that made it the world's largest digital consumer base, or do Consumers alone sustain viable internet economy growth?

## India Angle
India's telecom duopoly, startup ecosystem consolidation, and divergent consumer segments (Internet Viewers vs Consumers) create distinct challenges for scaling businesses in 2020 and beyond.
