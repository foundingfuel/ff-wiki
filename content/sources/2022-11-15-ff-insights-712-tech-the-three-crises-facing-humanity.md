## Core Argument
Technology without self-limiting principles creates three simultaneous crises: human rebellion, environmental breakdown, resource exhaustion.

## Key Points
- Schumacher's 1973 thesis: technology lacks nature's self-balancing mechanisms, acting as 'foreign body' in natural systems.
- Modern civilization faces simultaneous crises in human wellbeing, environmental sustainability, and finite resource depletion.
- Materialist, unlimited-expansion model cannot persist in finite world; collapse timeline depends on expansion success rate.

## Open Question
If technology is essential to solving crises like climate change and pandemic disease, how can we simultaneously accept Schumacher's argument that technology itself is the root cause of these crises?

## India Angle
India's development path must reckon with Schumacher's three crises—balancing growth ambitions with environmental limits and human wellbeing; Avinash Sable's story exemplifies Indians competing globally, challenging the notion that dominance in certain fields belongs only to specific nations.
