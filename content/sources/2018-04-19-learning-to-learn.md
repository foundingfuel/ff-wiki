## Core Argument
Management schools must teach students to learn, not predict future skills, in an unpredictable VUCA world.

## Key Points
- VUCA world makes curriculum design impossible; focus must shift to developing lifelong learners and systems thinkers.
- Deep listening, design thinking, and data analysis are foundational disciplines for navigating complexity and building trustworthy institutions.
- Management schools face a double bind: students need unknowable future skills, yet institutions must teach relevant content efficiently.

## Open Question
How can institutions design curriculum for unknowable futures without abandoning accountability for graduate preparedness and employability?

## India Angle
Indian management schools and vocational institutions must urgently redesign curricula to develop lifelong learners and systems thinkers, equipping graduates for unpredictable futures rather than static job roles—critical as India's workforce navigates rapid technological and economic shifts.
