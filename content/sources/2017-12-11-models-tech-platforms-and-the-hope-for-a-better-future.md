## Core Argument
Multiple mental models and understanding feedback loops are essential for navigating complex policy and technological change.

## Key Points
- No single model captures dynamic complexity; multiple perspectives needed to understand feedback loops.
- Digital platforms (Aadhaar, Swachh Bharat) require both technology and understanding of human behaviour and civic participation.
- India's development success depends on how well institutions manage systemic feedback loops, not just technology deployment.

## Open Question
How can technology platforms designed for efficiency address problems rooted in institutional capacity, human behaviour, and social participation without oversimplifying complexity?

## India Angle
India's development challenges—Aadhaar, Swachh Bharat, digital platforms in government—require understanding feedback loops and complexity; technology alone cannot solve systemic problems without institutional capacity and civic participation.
