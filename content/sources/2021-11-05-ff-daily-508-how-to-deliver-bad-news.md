## Core Argument
Delivering bad news early and transparently strengthens relationships more than concealment or delay.

## Key Points
- Trust requires straightforward communication of bad news, not cover-ups or lies.
- Express concerns immediately when they arise to prevent worse outcomes later.
- Affirm relationship value while sharing difficult information to preserve trust.

## Open Question
How do cultural hierarchies and power dynamics in Indian organisations affect the ability and willingness of employees to surface bad news early to senior leaders?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and managers across startups and large organisations often struggle with transparency culture; this directly applies to building trust-based teams in India's hierarchical corporate environments.
