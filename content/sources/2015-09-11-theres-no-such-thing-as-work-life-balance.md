## Core Argument
Work-life balance is impossible; choose imperfect variety over flawless focus and design life intentionally.

## Key Points
- Humans are generalists, not specialist machines; perfection across all domains is structurally impossible.
- Relative income (money + time) matters more than absolute income; optimise time leverage, not just earnings.
- Design your life around desired lifestyle and freedom, not deferred retirement rewards.

## Open Question
If imperfect variety is wise, how do you decide which domains deserve your limited attention—and who bears the cost of your chosen imperfections?

## India Angle
For Indian professionals climbing the career ladder, this challenges the pervasive cultural narrative that 'hustle' and total work dedication are prerequisites for success—relevant as India's startup ecosystem and corporate culture increasingly valorize 24/7 commitment.
