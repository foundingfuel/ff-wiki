## Core Argument
U.S. H-1B restrictions create structural opportunity for India to become global innovation hub via remote work and distributed talent models.

## Key Points
- H-1B $100K fee signals permanent talent flow shift, not temporary disruption to visa policies.
- India can flip from exporting workers to exporting work by retaining talent and enabling remote global projects.
- EOR platforms and distributed models unlock cross-border work without physical relocation, bypassing visa constraints.

## Open Question
Can India build scaled EOR platforms and policy infrastructure fast enough to capture this moment before Canada, Australia, and other countries establish competing advantages?

## India Angle
India can transition from exporting talent to retaining engineers and exporting remote work globally. Opportunity to build EOR platforms, distributed innovation hubs in tier-2 cities, and capture remote work jobs lost by Canada/Australia if policy supports this shift.
