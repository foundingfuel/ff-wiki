## Core Argument
Winning in hypercompetitive markets requires escaping commoditization through innovation, quality, or branding.

## Key Points
- Deregulation, digitalization, and declining customer loyalty have intensified market competition globally and locally.
- Brand loyalty is shifting: Western consumers are fickle; Chinese consumers are consolidating around quality-positioned brands.
- Pricing power—ability to raise prices without losing customers—is the ultimate competitive moat; lack thereof signals commodity status.

## Open Question
If employee engagement is rising globally and in China specifically, why are companies still struggling to maintain competitive edges—is engagement sufficient without strategic differentiation?

## India Angle
Indian businesses face acute pressure from deregulation and digitalization (e.g., ride-sharing, e-commerce disrupting traditional retail/taxi sectors). Employee engagement at 70% is higher than global average, but maintaining pricing power remains critical for SMEs competing against consolidated global players and domestic startups.
