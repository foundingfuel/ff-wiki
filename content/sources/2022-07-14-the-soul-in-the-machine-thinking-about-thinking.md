## Core Argument
Science's mathematical abstraction omits lived experience; governance needs wisdom beyond measurement.

## Key Points
- Scientific models are incomplete abstractions that cannot fully explain complex reality or human experience.
- Time and space are experienced qualitatively by consciousness, not just measured objectively by instruments.
- Governance and technology decisions require epistemic humility, ethical grounding, and ontological wisdom alongside scientific precision.

## Open Question
If science cannot map complete reality and wisdom requires subjectivity, how do we make scalable, legitimate policy decisions that serve diverse populations without sliding into relativism?

## India Angle
India's development challenges require governance models that move beyond Western scientific reductionism toward epistemologies that integrate local wisdom, lived experience, and ethical frameworks—critical for designing inclusive institutions and technology policy.
