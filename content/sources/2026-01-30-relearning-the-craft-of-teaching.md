## Core Argument
Teaching authority shifts from content mastery to learning architecture; intentional design beats experience and habit.

## Key Points
- Rigour requires depth through foundational ideas, not breadth; Indian academia conflates range with rigour causing cognitive overload.
- Teachers must architect learning structures with deliberate attention to student blindspots, not deliver content via slides.
- Authority flows from helping students think better, not knowing more; presence and engagement replace expertise-based control.

## Open Question
How can teachers systematically redesign courses for depth over coverage within institutional systems that reward coverage and credentialing?

## India Angle
Indian academia's emphasis on coverage and memorisation over deep understanding reflects systemic rigour redefined; applies to IIT, DU, and institutional teaching transformation needed for India's knowledge economy.
