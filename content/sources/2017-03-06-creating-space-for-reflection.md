## Core Argument
Leaders must prioritize reflection over constant activity to unlock innovation and strategic clarity.

## Key Points
- Packed calendars and constant activity prevent deeper thinking and emergence of breakthrough ideas.
- Media and business have conditioned audiences to accept shallow, rushed content over substantive analysis.
- Slowing down and creating space for undivided attention enables better decision-making and leadership.

## Open Question
How can leaders and organizations justify stepping off the treadmill when competitive pressures reward visible activity and rapid response?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and content creators are caught in hyperactivity trap; requires cultural shift toward deliberate reflection and depth to compete globally and make better strategic decisions.
