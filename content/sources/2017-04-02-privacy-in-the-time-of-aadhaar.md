## Core Argument
India needs privacy laws, modern technology infrastructure, and data risk literacy to make Aadhaar work safely.

## Key Points
- No technology is 100% secure; trade-offs between convenience and security are inevitable and require citizen choice.
- Laws alone won't solve India's data privacy crisis; institutional capacity, cyber hygiene standards, and insurance mechanisms are essential.
- Data risk literacy must become as fundamental as numeracy; systemic awareness-building is missing from current policy discussions.

## Open Question
How can India maintain Aadhaar's efficiency gains while building genuine data protection when weak institutional infrastructure and low digital literacy make strong safeguards difficult to implement and enforce?

## India Angle
Aadhaar represents India's unique challenge: balancing digital inclusion and convenience against data security and privacy in a context of weak institutional infrastructure and low data literacy. The government must build consent layers, cyber hygiene standards, and nationwide risk awareness while managing public trust.
