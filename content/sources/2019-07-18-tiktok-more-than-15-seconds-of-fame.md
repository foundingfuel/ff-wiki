## Core Argument
TikTok's virality reveals millennial behavior shifts marketers ignore at their peril.

## Key Points
- TikTok achieved 200M Indian users in under a year, creating micro-celebrities from small-town creators.
- Traditional marketers remain blind to TikTok despite it capturing core customer attention and behaviour.
- Understanding human attitudes beneath the trend is essential for brands to stay relevant with Bharat.

## Open Question
How can brands monetize and market on TikTok without appearing inauthentic when the platform's power lies in organic, user-generated virality?

## India Angle
TikTok's 200M Indian users, especially in small towns, represent a massive shift in how Bharat-based consumers engage with content and create identity. Indian marketers are largely missing this trend, creating both risk and opportunity for brands targeting vernacular audiences.
