## Core Argument
Familiarity breeds comfort, causing us to unconsciously avoid diverse perspectives and information.

## Key Points
- We gravitate toward similarity in relationships, hiring, and choices—even meaningless ones like initials.
- Comfort with the familiar blinds us to alternative perspectives and critical information.
- Organizations must deliberately create friction through diverse teams and direct interactions to overcome cognitive biases.

## Open Question
How do we balance the human need for comfort and belonging with the organizational need for diversity and dissenting views without creating toxic conflict?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations often fall into comfort-driven decision making and homogeneous hiring, limiting diversity of thought; understanding familiarity bias is crucial for building adaptive, innovative Indian organizations
