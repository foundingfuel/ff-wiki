## Core Argument
Digital transformation requires unlearning existing mindsets and incubating new capabilities separately from legacy operations.

## Key Points
- Innovation and technology alone don't ensure digital success; mindset unlearning is the real barrier.
- Actively participate in disrupting your own profitable business rather than resist the inevitable.
- Incubate digital competencies in isolation with dedicated teams, budget, and fresh hires to avoid legacy thinking.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the need to cannibalize profitable existing businesses for digital transformation while managing shareholder expectations and maintaining cash flow?

## India Angle
Indian media and publishing companies face similar disruption pressures; the playbook of incubating digital separately while actively cannibalizing legacy businesses applies directly to India's news, content, and publishing sector navigating the digital shift.
