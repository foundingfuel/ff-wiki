## Core Argument
BJP's electoral success stems from masterful storytelling that creates a unified narrative appealing to India's diverse aspirations.

## Key Points
- Every powerful movement needs a hero—Modi became BJP's central narrative figure, driving 12% of votes alone.
- Effective storytelling crafts big-tent narratives that connect disparate audiences (global aspirations, business reform, social equity, national identity).
- Mission-aligned culture from leadership to grassroots workers amplifies storytelling impact, mirroring Apple's turnaround strategy.

## Open Question
If storytelling and cultural alignment alone drive political success, how do ethical constraints on campaign methods affect long-term legitimacy and democratic health?

## India Angle
BJP's electoral dominance in India is driven by sophisticated storytelling that unifies diverse Indian aspirations—from global power status to social equity—creating a uniquely Indian political narrative that transcends traditional parliamentary politics.
