## Core Argument
Durga Puja transforms Kolkata into a living art gallery, blending tradition with curated modern experiences.

## Key Points
- UNESCO-listed festival functions as world's largest street art installation with city-wide participation
- Multiple access models (premium passes, walking tours, bonedi bari experiences) democratize engagement across visitor preferences
- Curation and storytelling around pandals creates repeat engagement and cultural documentation

## Open Question
How can commercializing access to Durga Puja through premium passes and curated tours preserve its authentic grassroots character and community ownership?

## India Angle
Durga Puja in Kolkata is a uniquely Indian cultural phenomenon with global recognition; understanding how to experience and preserve it matters for cultural continuity and local tourism in India's creative economy.
