## Core Argument
Successful enterprises must proactively disrupt their own business before competitors do to survive.

## Key Points
- Amazon created Kindle to cannibalize its book business; Netflix destroyed its DVD model for streaming.
- Sunk costs, past success bias, and fear of cannibalisation prevent companies from self-disruption.
- M&A is an alternative: buy disruptors rather than build them (e.g., Facebook-WhatsApp for $19B).

## Open Question
How can large organizations reconcile the financial burden of abandoning profitable legacy businesses with shareholder pressure for short-term returns, especially when self-disruption doesn't guarantee success?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises, especially those in traditional sectors (retail, media, manufacturing), face existential risk from digital disruption. Leaders must adopt self-disruption strategies rather than defending legacy business models—critical for survival in fast-changing markets.
