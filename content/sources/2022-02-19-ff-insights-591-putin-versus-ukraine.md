## Core Argument
Putin seeks to undo Ukraine's sovereignty, not merely maintain neutrality; Finland's model offers lessons but different context.

## Key Points
- Putin demands Ukraine's institutional transformation, not just neutrality—driven by fear of westward drift.
- Finland maintained democracy under strict neutrality by shrewdly restructuring economic ties without antagonizing Moscow.
- Putin remembers Finland's escape; Ukraine's path differs because Russian president now refuses buffer-state compromise.

## Open Question
If even neutrality with democratic institutions threatens Putin, what conditions would actually satisfy Russian security concerns without surrendering Ukrainian sovereignty?
