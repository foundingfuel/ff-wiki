## Core Argument
Strategic constraints imposed by foreign partners protected Maruti from political interference and built institutional resilience.

## Key Points
- Krishnamurthy used SMC's approval requirements and equity stake strategically to insulate Maruti from political volatility.
- Public sector vulnerability to ministerial whims demanded creative governance solutions that appeared as capitulation but strengthened long-term interests.
- Vendor confidence required external validation mechanisms—showing how institutional trust must be designed, not assumed.

## Open Question
How can public sector leaders build resilience against political interference without appearing to surrender control or undermine national capability?

## India Angle
How India's public sector leaders navigated institutional instability and political interference to build world-class companies like Maruti; foundational lesson for Indian PSU governance and state capacity.
