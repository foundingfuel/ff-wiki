## Core Argument
Platform businesses are reshaping retail by creating new engagement models that compete with traditional offline commerce.

## Key Points
- Online sales grew 19% on Black Friday, but offline retail remained dominant through creative promotions and hybrid approaches.
- Indian e-commerce platforms captured $3B GMM during festival season, indicating rapid shift in consumer shopping behavior.
- Platform businesses leverage data and engagement to become monopoly-like entities, resetting traditional market dynamics.

## Open Question
If platforms become unavoidable digital intermediaries with monopoly power, what regulatory or business model innovations can preserve fair competition and consumer choice?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce platforms (Flipkart, Amazon) clocked $3B GMV during Navratri-Diwali, signaling platform businesses reshaping Indian consumer habits and retail competition between online and offline models.
