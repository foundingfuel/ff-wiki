## Core Argument
Five simple, universally applicable habits—respect, responsiveness, communication, stewardship, trust—drive organizational culture change at scale.

## Key Points
- Leaders lose ground reality due to yes-men culture and fear of speaking up in hierarchical systems.
- Wipro discovered five behavioural habits through cross-functional task forces, emphasizing what individuals can control.
- Universal, simple habits transcend language, location, and organizational silos to embed cultural change across 100,000+ employees.

## Open Question
How do you ensure five simple habits become embedded behaviour in a massive, geographically distributed organization without becoming performative compliance theatre?

## India Angle
Indian leaders across politics and business struggle with feedback loops and hierarchical structures that prevent ground-truth information from reaching decision-makers. Wipro's cultural transformation under Premji offers a model for Indian organizations to build inclusive feedback systems and distributed accountability.
