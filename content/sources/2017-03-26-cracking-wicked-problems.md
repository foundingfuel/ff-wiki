## Core Argument
Wicked problems require multidisciplinary teams collaborating across sectors, not isolated genius thinking.

## Key Points
- Smart India Hackathon engages 10,000 students across 26 locations to solve real ministry-submitted problems.
- Grand Challenges fund breakthrough innovations in healthcare and sanitation by bringing together 45+ diverse organisations.
- Prize-based competitions and open innovation models unlock solutions that proprietary labs and individual experts cannot.

## Open Question
If wicked problems require diverse stakeholders and multidisciplinary teams, how do you ensure sustained implementation and impact beyond the hackathon or challenge period?

## India Angle
India has unique wicked problems (sanitation, healthcare) that require multidisciplinary collaboration. Smart India Hackathon and Grand Challenges India exemplify how Indian government and philanthropic sectors are mobilising students and innovators to solve societal challenges at scale.
