## Core Argument
Customer-centric companies win by addressing emotional needs before rational feature benefits.

## Key Points
- Emotional appeal drives purchase decisions more than rational benefits.
- Apple exemplifies customer-first thinking that creates competitive differentiation.
- Self-interest and emotional connection must precede feature/benefit messaging.

## Open Question
How do you systematically build emotional connection at scale, especially for commoditized or B2B products where rational benefits traditionally dominate?

## India Angle
Indian startups and established companies often lead with features/specs rather than emotional appeal; Apple's model shows how premium positioning and emotional connection can command pricing power—critical for Indian brands entering global markets.
