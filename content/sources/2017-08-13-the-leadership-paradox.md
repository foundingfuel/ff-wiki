## Core Argument
Leaders justify immoral power consolidation as necessary for greater good, but paradox remains unresolved.

## Key Points
- Historical leaders (Churchill, Roosevelt, Caesar, Octavian) formed alliances with 'devils' claiming larger good then abandoned principles.
- Indian politicians invoke welfare rhetoric to legitimize ethically questionable power acquisitions, echoing ancient patterns.
- Tension: how do transformative goals get achieved without compromising values, or is moral compromise inherent to leadership?

## Open Question
If transformative systemic change requires pooling power across institutions and interests, how can leaders pursue such change without inevitably compromising their stated values?

## India Angle
Indian leaders frequently invoke 'larger good' to justify power consolidation; Nitish Kumar example directly critiques this Indian political pattern and hypocrisy in decision-making.
