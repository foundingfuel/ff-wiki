## Core Argument
Success in India requires recognizing and executing against emerging consumer scripts; legacy size and past wins guarantee nothing.

## Key Points
- Media teaches that execution and consumer resonance matter infinitely more than resources or past success.
- Technology erases competitive moats; smartphone compression means every business boils down to pixels and execution.
- India is ground zero for rapid narrative shifts; leaders must cultivate habit of spotting scripts in culture, behaviour, conversations.

## Open Question
How do incumbent leaders balance the need to spot emerging scripts while protecting current revenue streams that fund exploration and innovation?

## India Angle
India is experiencing compressed change—packing Western decades of transformation into one or two. Digital will disrupt every Indian business; success depends on reading emerging scripts and narratives unique to India's consumer behaviour and operating system.
