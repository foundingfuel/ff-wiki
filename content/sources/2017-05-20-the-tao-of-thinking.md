## Core Argument
High performers clarify end-game purpose, embrace constraints, and prioritize time over money to maximize impact.

## Key Points
- Exceptional leaders spend deliberate time imagining possible futures and ruthlessly evaluate which aligns with larger purpose.
- Constraints and scarcity paradoxically foster creativity; comfort deadens it, forcing diverse perspectives on complex problems.
- Time is the ultimate finite resource; redefining poverty through time lens reveals why material abundance without temporal freedom breeds unhappiness.

## Open Question
If constraints foster creativity and purpose-clarity, why do we continuously accumulate resources and abandon constraints even when they made us happier?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and entrepreneurs need to adopt intentional self-examination and constraint-based thinking to navigate rapid societal transitions; challenges resonate with India's resource-constrained context where creativity through scarcity is culturally embedded.
