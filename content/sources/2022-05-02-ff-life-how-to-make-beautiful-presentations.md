## Core Argument
Well-designed presentation tools and techniques remain essential for effective business communication despite the rise of memos and documents.

## Key Points
- Effective decks (Canva, Pitch, Prezi, Miro) outperform poorly made ones; tools democratize quality design for non-designers.
- Collaboration features matter: Pitch excels at team-based deck creation with assigned slides and real-time feedback loops.
- Slidedocs hybrid format bridges deck-memo divide by balancing visual storytelling with substantive written context for shared documents.

## Open Question
If Amazon and top founders reject PowerPoint in favor of memos, why do presentation tools remain critical for most organizations—and is this a skill gap or a fundamental mismatch in communication formats?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and corporate leaders increasingly need to master presentation skills for pitching to investors, board communication, and organizational alignment—this toolkit helps them move beyond poor-quality decks that undermine credibility.
