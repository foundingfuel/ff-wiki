## Core Argument
Bihar's political choices disproportionately influence India's national narrative due to historical legacy and electoral arithmetic.

## Key Points
- Bihar contributes 40 Lok Sabha seats; losing it signals weakness to New Delhi's power aspirants
- State's symbolic importance rooted in Anti-Emergency movement traces back to 1973-74 Jayaprakash Narayan
- Poverty and resource mismanagement stem from structural, historical, macro-economic policies, not single cause

## Open Question
How can a chronically impoverished state with health indicators worse than sub-Saharan Africa wield such disproportionate power in national politics, and does this concentration of influence serve India's democratic interests?
