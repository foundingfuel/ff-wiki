## Core Argument
Pandemic resilience requires emotional intelligence and self-care as foundation for caring for dependents.

## Key Points
- Accept what cannot be controlled; focus energy on behaviors within immediate sphere of influence
- Emotional awareness and acceptance build resilience better than suppression; use mood-tracking tools
- Quality time with children, teens, and elders—listening without judgment—reduces pandemic-induced stress and isolation

## Open Question
How do individuals balance accepting what they cannot change while maintaining agency to influence their immediate world without burning out?

## India Angle
India's second COVID wave (April 2021) brought unprecedented health infrastructure strain and lockdowns across states; coping strategies must account for multi-generational households, limited mental health access, and cultural approaches to emotional expression
