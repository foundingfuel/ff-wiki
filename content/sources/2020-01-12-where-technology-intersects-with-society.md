## Core Argument
Technology's true power emerges at the intersection of technical and human-centered thinking; managing societal impact requires both.

## Key Points
- The 'magical intersection' of humanities and science—not just engineering—drives transformative innovation and purpose-driven companies.
- India's Aadhaar experience reveals the complexity of scaling tech across state, society, and markets; unintended consequences are difficult to anticipate.
- Systems thinking and collaboration between 'techies and fuzzies' are essential as technology's societal footprint grows increasingly consequential.

## Open Question
How can policymakers and technologists better anticipate and mitigate unintended consequences of large-scale technology deployment when complexity is fundamentally unpredictable?

## India Angle
India's Aadhaar and financial inclusion initiatives exemplify the critical need to align technology deployment with state capacity, societal readiness, and market dynamics—and the difficulty of predicting unintended consequences at scale.
