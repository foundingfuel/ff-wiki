## Core Argument
Strategic napping boosts afternoon productivity; enlightened managers should enable it despite cultural stigma.

## Key Points
- Body naturally enters low-energy state post-lunch due to circadian rhythm opposition; napping counters this dip.
- Research shows 10-20 minute naps significantly increase alertness; 40% of workers would nap if allowed.
- Manager-enabled napping builds trust and late-afternoon productivity, requiring cultural shift from guilt to legitimacy.

## Open Question
How do organizations create psychological safety around rest when productivity culture equates presence with commitment?

## India Angle
Indian workplace culture traditionally stigmatizes rest and downtime; normalizing strategic napping could shift productivity norms in Indian offices and improve employee wellbeing, especially as remote work blurs boundaries.
