## Core Argument
Confusing achievement with purpose creates endless dissatisfaction; clarity on WHY sustains both meaning and performance.

## Key Points
- Achievement (yacht) and success (feeling) are distinct; pursuing one doesn't guarantee the other
- Remote work enables talent redistribution from metros to smaller towns, reversing rural-to-urban migration patterns
- Organisations and individuals drift from their original WHY through relentless pursuit of tangible metrics and growth

## Open Question
How do we help high-achievers distinguish between genuine purpose realignment and mere dissatisfaction masquerading as existential questioning?

## India Angle
Sridhar Vembu's Zoho example shows how Indian entrepreneurs can tap talent and opportunities in India II/III cities rather than metro-centric models. Remote work can reverse brain drain and unlock distributed economic growth across Indian geographies.
