## Core Argument
People sacrifice present happiness for future gains despite knowing happiness matters most.

## Key Points
- Happiness paradox: people prioritize means to happiness (money, status) over happiness itself.
- Entrepreneurs choose uncertainty and hardship over comfort because passion and inspiration matter more.
- Effective systems (like pandemic prevention) require dedicated teams focused on core mission, not peripheral gains.

## Open Question
If people understand happiness is the ultimate goal, why do they consistently choose paths that defer or sacrifice it for external validation?

## India Angle
India's high-performance culture often prioritizes external markers (wealth, status, success) over well-being; relevant for founders, professionals navigating career transitions, and organizational leaders building sustainable cultures.
