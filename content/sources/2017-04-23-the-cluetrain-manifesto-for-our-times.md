## Core Argument
As machines replicate cognitive work, businesses must obsess over customers and co-create value through human-centered design.

## Key Points
- The Cluetrain Manifesto predicted consumer power; businesses now must move beyond efficiency to customer obsession.
- Technology alone doesn't solve problems—scepticism and human-centered thinking remain the last competitive frontier.
- Companies like Amazon and GE prove sustained growth comes from deep customer immersion and co-creation, not just innovation.

## Open Question
If machines eventually replicate emotional intelligence too, what uniquely human value can businesses offer customers, and how should they prepare for that reality?

## India Angle
Indian companies must shift from product-centric to customer-centric models as technology democratizes; understanding emotional/human dimensions becomes competitive advantage in a digitizing economy.
