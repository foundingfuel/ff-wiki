## Core Argument
Fear stems from birth; mindset shifts transform how entrepreneurs and leaders navigate uncertainty and competition.

## Key Points
- Original fear rooted in birth vulnerability persists as adult survival anxiety, requiring conscious reframing
- Indian startups shifted from profitability goals to growth-at-all-costs, optimizing for investor rounds over unit economics
- Addressing fear requires dialogue with one's inner child; addressing startup dysfunction requires boards to demand long-term profitability metrics

## Open Question
How can founders simultaneously embrace fearlessness as a personal philosophy while building organizations disciplined enough to resist the systemic pressures that reward unsustainable growth?

## India Angle
Indian startups increasingly chase valuation over sustainable value creation; cultural shift mirrors broader erosion of long-term thinking in Indian entrepreneurship
