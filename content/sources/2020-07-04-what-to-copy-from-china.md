## Core Argument
Solutions emerge from ground-level problem solvers; India must cultivate entrepreneurial hunger to compete globally like China.

## Key Points
- Best innovations come from workers closest to problems, not top-down mandates or government directives.
- Local market knowledge and ground truth matter more than global technology; fit solutions to local contexts.
- Chinese success stems from entrepreneur hunger to find and serve unmet needs globally, not government push.

## Open Question
How can India systematically replicate China's entrepreneurial hunger and export-driven mindset when its MSME ecosystem remains fragmented and inward-focused?

## India Angle
India needs to build an entrepreneurial ecosystem like China's where businesses proactively seek global markets; currently Indian MSMEs lack the hunger and systems to expand internationally like Chinese entrepreneurs do
