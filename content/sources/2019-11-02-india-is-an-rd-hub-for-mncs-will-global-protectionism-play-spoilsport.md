## Core Argument
Global protectionism threatens India's position as MNC R&D hub despite its proven innovation capabilities.

## Key Points
- GCCs evolved from cost-arbitrage centers to cutting-edge innovation hubs generating $15.7B in FY2019 R&D revenue.
- 79% of US patents by India-based inventors come from MNC R&D centers, showing concentrated IP creation.
- Digital transformation demands (AI, ML, IoT) are strengthening GCC relevance despite protectionist headwinds.

## Open Question
Can GCCs sustain growth and strategic importance if developed economies repatriate R&D jobs, or will their integration with India's startup ecosystem create resilience through local innovation ecosystems?

## India Angle
India's $15.7B GCC R&D ecosystem employs 1M+ technical talent and drives 79% of US patents filed by India-based inventors. Rising global protectionism threatens this engine of capability-building and entrepreneurial talent pipeline for Indian startups.
