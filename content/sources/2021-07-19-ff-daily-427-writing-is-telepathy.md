## Core Argument
Writing is telepathy; careers require multiple pivots and communities need to be partners, not targets.

## Key Points
- Writing transmits ideas across space and time through shared mental imagery and interpretation.
- Career longevity demands accepting multiple jobs over 50+ working years, not linear progression.
- Effective change (vaccination, education) requires community involvement and cultural communication, not top-down assumptions.

## Open Question
How do we design institutions and policies for multi-decade careers when most systems assume linear, single-domain working lives?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and policymakers need to recognize multi-career lifespans and design systems supporting job transitions; vaccine hesitancy piece shows importance of cultural communication and community involvement in public health initiatives.
