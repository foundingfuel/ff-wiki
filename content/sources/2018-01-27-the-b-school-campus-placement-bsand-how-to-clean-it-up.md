## Core Argument
Campus placements prioritize metrics over meaningful career alignment, requiring systemic reforms in selection and industry engagement.

## Key Points
- 70% of MBA graduates quit first jobs within nine months due to poor fit, not lack of opportunity.
- Business schools need two years prior work experience and purpose-driven screening, not just entrance exam performance.
- Senior faculty must engage deeply with emerging sectors beyond traditional FMCG/banking to shape relevant job pipelines.

## Open Question
How can business schools incentivize genuine career fitment when 100% placement rates and ranking metrics reward volume over quality of outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian business schools perpetuate a broken placement system that prioritizes statistics over student-job fitment, misleading both institutions and employers while perpetuating misalignment in India's talent market.
