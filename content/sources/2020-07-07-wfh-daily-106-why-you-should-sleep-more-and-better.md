## Core Argument
Sleep enables cognitive performance and toxin removal; organizations must normalize rest as productivity tool.

## Key Points
- Deep sleep synchronizes neurons, reducing oxygen demand and allowing cerebrospinal fluid to clear brain toxins.
- Stories with understated causal connections are more memorable than explicit narratives or pure data.
- Inclusion at scale requires business rationale alignment, personal connections, and sustained budget commitment.

## Open Question
How can knowledge-intensive industries balance always-on work culture with the scientific evidence that sleep is essential for cognitive function and long-term health?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and organizations need to prioritize sleep and rest culture; pandemic-era remote work in India often erases boundaries between work and personal time, exacerbating sleep deprivation and reducing productivity.
