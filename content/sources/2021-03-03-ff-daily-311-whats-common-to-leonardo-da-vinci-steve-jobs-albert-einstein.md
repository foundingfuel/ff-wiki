## Core Argument
Curiosity and connecting arts with science are traits of transformative leaders; local knowledge beats elite credentials.

## Key Points
- Great innovators share passionate curiosity and blend arts with sciences to spot cross-disciplinary patterns.
- India's elite education system fails rural contexts; local expertise and systems thinking matter more than credentials.
- Viewing problems through ecosystem lens—not individual expertise—enables scalable solutions replicable globally.

## Open Question
How can elite institutions retrain their graduates to value local knowledge and ecosystem thinking over specialist credentials without losing rigor?

## India Angle
India's education system and elite institutions (IIMs, IITs) are critiqued for producing specialists disconnected from local ecosystems; Hande's rural work model offers a replicable systems-thinking approach for India to become a global solutions hub.
