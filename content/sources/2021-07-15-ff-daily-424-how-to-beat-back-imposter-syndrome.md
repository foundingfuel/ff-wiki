## Core Argument
Imposter syndrome is universal among managers; acknowledging furious paddling beneath surface builds authentic leadership.

## Key Points
- Every manager experiences imposter syndrome; recognizing this normalizes vulnerability and builds psychological safety.
- Great leadership requires understanding personal superpowers, flaws, and learning capacity—not perfection or pretense.
- Self-awareness and continuous learning, not external validation, generate confidence to lead authentically.

## Open Question
How do organizations create cultures where leaders openly acknowledge struggle and uncertainty without compromising decision-making authority and team confidence?

## India Angle
Indian managers and professionals navigating rapid career transitions in startup and corporate environments face acute imposter syndrome; addressing this mindset is critical for building resilient leadership culture in Indian organizations scaling at speed.
