## Core Argument
Consumer empathy from ethnographic research beats both gut instinct and big data alone.

## Key Points
- Advertising industry wrongly dismisses research as weakness; creative flourishes mask lack of consumer understanding.
- Real-time data and sample sizes obscure why consumers behave as they do; ethnographic immersion uncovers hidden barriers.
- Kolkata department store fixed misalignment by listening, observing, learning—not surveys—revealing senior citizens' reluctance to enter plush interiors.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale deep consumer immersion practices across multiple markets without losing efficiency and speed to market?

## India Angle
Indian businesses (especially retail and FMCG) over-rely on quantitative metrics and data while dismissing deep consumer immersion; ethnographic research reveals hidden barriers—like SEC C consumers or senior citizens' hesitations—that numbers alone miss, critical for India's diverse market segments.
