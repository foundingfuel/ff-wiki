## Core Argument
Personal stories mobilize action faster than data; structuring narrative, attention, empathy, and tools drives social change.

## Key Points
- Sameer Bhatia's story recruited 24,611 bone marrow donors in 11 weeks via emotional connection over statistics.
- Aaker's framework: Focus, Grab Attention, Engage through empathy, Enable Action with clear tools and templates.
- When urgency fades, storytelling sustains momentum; India's health campaigns must plan post-crisis narrative strategy.

## Open Question
How do you sustain action and funding for a cause once the immediate crisis narrative loses urgency and media attention shifts elsewhere?

## India Angle
Indian nonprofits and social movements must master narrative-driven mobilization to overcome resource constraints; urgency fades but impact needs to persist—critical for post-COVID health campaigns, CSR initiatives, and grassroots activism in India.
