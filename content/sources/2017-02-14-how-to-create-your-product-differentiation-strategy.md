## Core Argument
Commodity products escape price wars through branding, design, origin, and consumer perception strategies.

## Key Points
- Create consumer brands for B2B commodities to de-commodify (Intel Inside, Scotchgard model).
- Geographic origin and historical associations build lasting brand equity across centuries.
- Price psychology: higher prices signal quality and reshape customer perception and experience.

## Open Question
If most differentiation strategies are perception-based rather than product-based, how sustainable are they when competitors adopt similar tactics?

## India Angle
Indian companies, particularly in commodities (basmati rice, textiles, coffee), can leverage geographic origin and branding to command premiums globally. Young Asian firms historically underinvest in marketing; India needs strategic brand-building to compete beyond cost leadership.
