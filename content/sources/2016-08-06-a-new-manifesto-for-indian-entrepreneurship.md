## Core Argument
India needs entrepreneurial leaders focused on inclusive growth, not just startup exits.

## Key Points
- Entrepreneurship must extend beyond startups to large organizations and government institutions
- India's challenges require purposeful ventures addressing systemic issues and social impact
- Stronger ecosystem demands rethinking how leaders develop mindset and contribute to nation-building

## Open Question
How can market-driven entrepreneurship align with systemic social challenges when profit incentives and social purpose often diverge?

## India Angle
Redefining entrepreneurship for India's development challenges—moving beyond startup exits to inclusive, purposeful ventures that strengthen society and economy
