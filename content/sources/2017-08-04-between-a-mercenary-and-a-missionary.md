## Core Argument
Mercenary motivations masked by patriotic language prevent meaningful work and organizational loyalty.

## Key Points
- Conglomerates weaponize nationalist rhetoric to attract talent while compensation alone drives decisions.
- Misalignment between stated purpose and actual motivations creates brittle relationships that fracture quickly.
- Personal maturity—living in the present, releasing past credentials and future dreams—is prerequisite for sustained contribution.

## Open Question
If talented Indians genuinely want to 'build for India,' how do organizations create authentic mission-alignment without relying on patriotic appeals that ultimately ring hollow against material incentives?

## India Angle
Indian talent returning home faces a fundamental misalignment: offers frame 'nation-building' as motivation while compensation structures attract mercenary instincts, revealing how Indian conglomerates exploit patriotism to justify economic opportunism rather than foster genuine mission-driven work.
