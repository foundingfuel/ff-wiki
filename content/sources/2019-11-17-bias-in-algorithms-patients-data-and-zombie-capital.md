## Core Argument
Algorithmic bias, data privacy, and venture capital incentives reveal systemic tensions between tech progress and accountability.

## Key Points
- Distinguish between honest mistakes, individual biases, institutional biases, and structural biases in algorithmic decisions.
- Tech companies' 'ask forgiveness not permission' culture erodes trust when handling sensitive health and financial data.
- VC model's commission-based structure misaligns incentives; most funds don't generate returns competitive with capital deployed.

## Open Question
How can societies benefit from data-driven innovation in healthcare and finance without sacrificing privacy, explainability, and consent—especially when existing regulatory frameworks lag technology deployment?

## India Angle
India is building data-rich infrastructure and privacy laws; algorithmic bias and tech-healthcare data access patterns will directly shape regulatory frameworks and trust in Indian fintech/healthtech sectors.
