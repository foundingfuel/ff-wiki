## Core Argument
India must lead Global South to shape AI governance rules rather than passively accept Western-Chinese frameworks.

## Key Points
- US and China racing toward AGI while negotiating global norms; India risks being sidelined despite strong DPI foundation.
- Proposed 'AI of Humanity Agreement' with compute caps would enable Global South catch-up without constraining developing nations.
- India's pragmatic focus on AI utility sidesteps AGI debate, potentially missing strategic moment to influence global architecture.

## Open Question
How can India balance its stated commitment to inclusive, sovereign AI development with the reality that leading powers are racing toward AGI capabilities that may be inherently uncontrollable by any governance framework?

## India Angle
India must move from passive rule-taker to active rule-shaper in global AI governance. By rallying the Global South around inclusive AI frameworks (like a compute cap), India can influence norms while building indigenous capabilities without being constrained by Western-dominated governance structures.
