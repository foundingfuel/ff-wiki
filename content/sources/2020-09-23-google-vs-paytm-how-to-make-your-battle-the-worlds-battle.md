## Core Argument
Indian founders must fix real Big Tech problems through principled opposition, not self-interested Us-Vs-Them narratives.

## Key Points
- Paytm's Sharma framed a rule violation as anti-Indian colonialism, co-opting legitimate Big Tech concerns for competitive advantage.
- Fixing Big Tech requires principled rule-breakers proving rules are unfair, not guilty parties denying wrongdoing.
- Content moderation legislation globally shows platforms face real accountability pressure beyond founder grievances.

## Open Question
How do startups distinguish between fighting genuinely unfair platform rules versus using nationalism to escape legitimate compliance requirements?

## India Angle
Indian startups like Paytm conflate legitimate Big Tech regulation concerns with self-serving narratives to avoid accountability; India's atmanirbhar agenda risks being weaponized by founders to escape fair platform rules rather than genuinely compete.
