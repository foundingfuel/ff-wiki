## Core Argument
Aadhaar's regulatory uncertainty creates opportunity gaps while incumbent tech giants position alternative identity solutions.

## Key Points
- Government amending laws to allow banks/telecoms voluntary Aadhaar-based KYC, but authentication method clarity remains unclear.
- eSign suspension creates regulatory fog; fintech startups anxious while Adobe and big tech position proprietary alternatives.
- Identity infrastructure becoming strategic business asset—Facebook, Adobe, Reliance competing in 'real identity' verification space.

## Open Question
How can India balance enabling fintech innovation through open identity infrastructure while maintaining privacy and regulatory certainty, without allowing incumbent tech giants to monopolize alternatives?
