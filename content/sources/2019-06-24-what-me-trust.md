## Core Argument
Technology's benefits erode without trust; three domains reveal deepening credibility crises.

## Key Points
- AI-generated fake identities and deepfakes undermine ability to verify authenticity online, creating friction in digital ecosystems.
- Gene-editing ambitions lack sufficient information for responsible action; precautionary principle suggests inaction over reckless innovation.
- Facebook's history of broken promises (WhatsApp data sharing) makes Libra's 'decentralized' claims untrustworthy despite marketing framing.

## Open Question
How do societies reap benefits of transformative technologies while protecting against bad-faith actors when verification itself becomes impossible?

## India Angle
India's emerging digital economy and fintech ambitions (Libra, UPI alternatives) require strong trust frameworks. Without institutional trust, India's 1.4B citizens may resist adopting new platforms, undermining digital inclusion goals and competitive advantage.
