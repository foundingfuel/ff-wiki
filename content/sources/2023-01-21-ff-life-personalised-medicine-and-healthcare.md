## Core Argument
Smartphones are delivering clinically-proven treatments, but Indian pricing resistance threatens viable business models.

## Key Points
- Digital therapeutics leverage psychology and proven behavioral protocols through apps, meeting global regulatory standards like FDA/WHO approval.
- Indian entrepreneurs are innovating at medicine-tech intersection, but consumer price sensitivity creates a fundamental business model challenge.
- Personal health management (weight, lipids, heart rate, addiction) can now happen on smartphones, addressing NCDs that cause 74% of global deaths.

## Open Question
How can Indian digital health entrepreneurs sustain viable businesses when consumers won't pay for clinically-effective treatments, despite global regulatory validation and proven outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs are building FDA/WHO-approved digital therapeutics, but face a critical barrier: Indian consumers' price sensitivity and reluctance to pay for health tech, limiting business model viability despite strong clinical efficacy.
