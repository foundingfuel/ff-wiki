## Core Argument
Real estate recovery hinges on digital execution, prefab construction adoption, and startup partnerships.

## Key Points
- Demand remains resilient; strong balance sheets enable survival through downturn cycles.
- Digital transformation and efficient execution processes define industry recovery speed and profitability.
- Labor scarcity drives adoption of prefabricated construction and asset-as-service business models.

## Open Question
How can traditional developers with entrenched cement-based practices compete with agile startups offering integrated solutions without losing their capital and land advantages?

## India Angle
Indian real estate sector must undergo digital transformation and adopt prefabricated construction to address labor scarcity and capital constraints while competing with emerging proptech startups reshaping developer-buyer relationships.
