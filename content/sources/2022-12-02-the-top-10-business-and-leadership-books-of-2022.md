## Core Argument
Leaders must build psychological safety, embrace vulnerability, and align people around purpose to drive organisational excellence.

## Key Points
- Culture thrives on psychological safety—leaders must model vulnerability and admit mistakes to reduce team tension.
- High-potential employees need consistent praise, development space, and never-dismissed ideas to produce great work.
- Post-Covid workplaces must honor human uniqueness over uniformity; teamwork amplifies individual strengths, not methods.

## Open Question
How do leaders simultaneously create psychological safety while maintaining accountability and high performance standards?
