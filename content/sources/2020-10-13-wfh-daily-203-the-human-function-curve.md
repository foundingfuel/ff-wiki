## Core Argument
Distinguish stretching from stress; leaders must balance knowing, doing, and being for growth.

## Key Points
- Human Function Curve separates productive stretching from harmful stress; boundary is dangerously thin.
- Pandemic-driven anxiety worsens when media overload prevents contextual understanding and learning.
- MBA education must develop knowing, doing, and being equally; currently overemphasizes knowledge alone.

## Open Question
How do individuals and institutions practically identify and maintain the line between stretching and stress before crossing into dysfunction?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating pandemic-driven stress and uncertainty need frameworks to distinguish productive stretching from debilitating stress; MBA curriculum reform in India must similarly balance knowing, doing, and being to prepare leaders for societal responsibility.
