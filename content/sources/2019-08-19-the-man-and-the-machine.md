## Core Argument
Distinguish genuine technological breakthroughs from deceptive hype masquerading as innovation.

## Key Points
- History shows technology expectations swing between naive optimism and dismissive pessimism, repeating cyclically.
- Theranos and Engineer.ai reveal how startups conflate human labor with AI automation to inflate valuations.
- Pilot success doesn't guarantee scalability—Netherlands solar path thrived where French solar road failed.

## Open Question
How can investors and regulators reliably distinguish between legitimate deep tech innovation and sophisticated Turk-like deception when technological possibility boundaries are genuinely expanding?

## India Angle
Engineer.ai, an India-based startup, exemplifies the local tech ecosystem's tendency toward inflated AI claims; critical for Indian investors and policymakers to develop discernment between genuine innovation and marketing hype.
