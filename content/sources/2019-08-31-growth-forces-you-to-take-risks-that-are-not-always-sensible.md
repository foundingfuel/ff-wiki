## Core Argument
Growth demands risky, non-sensible decisions; India must build indigenous tech capacity beyond services dependency.

## Key Points
- India's software product startups face harsher constraints than global counterparts due to ecosystem immaturity.
- Technology companies like Swiggy blur service-product boundaries through data and AI; India must think beyond traditional categories.
- Entrepreneurs solve wicked problems but depend on government facilitation; policy and entrepreneurship must co-evolve.

## Open Question
How can entrepreneurs justify the personal and financial risks of scaling when rational analysis suggests the odds are stacked against them in India?

## India Angle
India's software product ecosystem remains underdeveloped; entrepreneurs face brutal scaling challenges unique to the Indian context, and the country is over-dependent on external technology rather than building indigenous capability.
