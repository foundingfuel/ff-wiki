## Core Argument
Technology solves efficiency problems, not the democratic questions about who deserves what.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar succeeded technically but shifted unsolved socio-political questions about beneficiary eligibility and data privacy to other institutions.
- E-governance conflates service delivery efficiency with democratic governance; the former needs optimized processes, the latter needs deliberation.
- Complex societal issues require democratic debate, not digital solutions—technology cannot replace contested values about entitlements and rights.

## Open Question
How do democracies harness powerful technologies' efficiency gains while preserving space for contentious deliberation about values and entitlements that technology cannot resolve?

## India Angle
Aadhaar exemplifies India's technological ambition but reveals how technology cannot resolve fundamentally political questions about welfare, entitlements, and democratic deliberation that require Indian society to debate priorities.
