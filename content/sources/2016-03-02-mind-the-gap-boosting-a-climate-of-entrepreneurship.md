## Core Argument
India must democratize entrepreneurship beyond tech startups to unlock rural and manufacturing sectors' potential.

## Key Points
- Tech startup dominance masks capital scarcity in manufacturing, SMEs, and rural entrepreneurship despite their 40% employment share.
- Regulatory complexity and foreign capital dependence create systemic risks; Indian corporate VCs and streamlined processes needed.
- Working capital and bank finance gaps, not just equity, are why SMEs fail; targeted financial products essential.

## Open Question
How can India encourage broad-based entrepreneurship in manufacturing and rural areas when venture capital economics inherently favor scalable tech models?

## India Angle
India's startup ecosystem is concentrated in tech hubs while ignoring manufacturing, social, and rural entrepreneurship. Regulatory friction, capital allocation bias toward tech, and weak SME financing undermine broad-based economic growth and employment.
