## Core Argument
AI-enabled fraud and identity systems demand rethinking how we build trustworthy digital infrastructure.

## Key Points
- Fraudsters using deepfake voice tech and AI now outpace detection capabilities, targeting remote workers.
- Zuckerberg's metaverse vision mirrors India Stack's decentralized identity-first architecture, not corporate walled gardens.
- Security firms like Pindrop attempt detection, but asymmetric advantage favors attackers using rapidly advancing tools.

## Open Question
How can open, interoperable digital systems (like metaverse or India Stack) maintain security when fraudsters exploit the same openness that enables innovation?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and businesses face growing AI-enabled fraud risks (Goibibo scam example). India Stack principles offer a model for secure identity infrastructure in metaverse development, contrasting with centralized corporate approaches.
