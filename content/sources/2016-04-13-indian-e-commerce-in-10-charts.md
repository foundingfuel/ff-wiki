## Core Argument
Indian e-commerce is maturing rapidly, but regulation and funding constraints will reshape competition.

## Key Points
- Four of nine Indian unicorns are e-commerce companies, signaling sector prominence and capital attraction.
- Government FDI rules for marketplaces sparked debate on business models, discounts, and fair competition.
- Mobile payments and offline integration will be critical competitive battlegrounds beyond pure online play.

## Open Question
Can India's e-commerce sector sustain growth under tightening regulatory constraints while competing for increasingly cautious VC funding?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce sector maturation in 2016 shows unicorn emergence, regulatory intervention on FDI/discounts, and the tension between venture funding constraints and market growth ambitions specific to India's developing digital economy.
