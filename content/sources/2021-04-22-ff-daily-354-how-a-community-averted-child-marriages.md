## Core Argument
Communities, not institutions, solved child marriage crisis through local networks and adaptive problem-solving.

## Key Points
- 178 child marriages averted in Patna via community 'whisper circles' of women monitoring impending marriages.
- 93% of early warnings came from community members—relatives, neighbors, local workers—not NGOs or authorities.
- Government workers like Aangwadi sevikas informally extended roles, showing institutional flexibility within capacity constraints.

## Open Question
If communities can solve child protection during crises, why are centralized institutions prioritized in normal times—and how do we sustain local momentum once crisis passes?

## India Angle
Indian communities and local institutions (Aangwadi sevikas, ASHA workers) stepping up where central government capacity is limited; decentralized problem-solving model applicable to India's governance challenges.
