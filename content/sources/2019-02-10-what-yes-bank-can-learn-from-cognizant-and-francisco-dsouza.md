## Core Argument
Leadership transitions reveal gaps between Indian banks' crisis management and mature corporate adaptation to fast-moving disruption.

## Key Points
- YES Bank's new leadership must navigate regulatory storms; unlike Cognizant's orchestrated handoff.
- Three Horizon model obsolete—disruption now moves at Horizon 1 speed, requiring different leadership capabilities.
- Board-run companies execute transitions differently than founder-led or crisis-driven institutions.

## Open Question
If disruption cycles compress to Horizon 1 speeds, can traditional leadership succession frameworks—even well-orchestrated ones—prepare organizations fast enough for emerging threats?

## India Angle
Indian banking sector faces simultaneous leadership transitions (YES Bank, ICICI) during regulatory scrutiny; contrasts with mature corporate transition management at Cognizant, highlighting governance maturity gaps in Indian financial institutions.
