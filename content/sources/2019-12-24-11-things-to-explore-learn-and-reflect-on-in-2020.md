## Core Argument
Leaders must cultivate self-awareness, manage biases, and unlearn achievement-obsession to enable genuine growth.

## Key Points
- Conscious leadership requires locating yourself 'above the line'—open, curious, committed to learning versus defensive and right-seeking.
- Cognitive biases flourish under fatigue and stress; deliberate strategies help anticipate and outsmart them in decision-making.
- Schools conflate learning with grades, creating achievement obsession that inhibits original thinking and authentic choice-making.

## Open Question
How can individuals rewired by decades of grade-obsessed education genuinely unlearn these patterns while operating within institutions that still reward those same behaviors?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and professionals navigating traditional hierarchies and examination-driven mindsets need frameworks for self-awareness, conscious leadership, and unlearning to drive organizational transformation.
