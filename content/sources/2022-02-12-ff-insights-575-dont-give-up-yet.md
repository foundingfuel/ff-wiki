## Core Argument
Persistence through eight-week skill thresholds and coaching-based leadership unlock lasting personal and organizational transformation.

## Key Points
- Neural circuitry changes take eight weeks minimum; early judgment of progress leads to premature abandonment.
- Best managers coach by tapping team wisdom, not fixing problems with their own expertise.
- Purpose-driven work earlier in career compounds success; delayed introspection diminishes decades of potential impact.

## Open Question
How do you balance the need for immediate results in competitive startups with the eight-week neurological minimum for sustainable skill change?

## India Angle
Indian startup founders and managers often over-rely on command-and-control or problem-solving approaches; adopting coaching mindsets and understanding neurological timelines for skill-building could improve retention and team capability in India's high-growth, high-turnover tech sectors.
