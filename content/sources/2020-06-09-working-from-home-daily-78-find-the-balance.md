## Core Argument
States must balance economy, security, and social welfare; local governance and transparency are critical levers.

## Key Points
- Kautilya's Arthashastra shows economy and polity are inseparable; state must actively balance competing interests.
- Rural examples like Kunariya village demonstrate how participatory governance and local livelihoods creation build resilience.
- Physical presence in education and local community engagement create irreplaceable value during crisis transitions.

## Open Question
How can governments balance immediate crisis response with long-term institutional strengthening when resources are stretched and competing demands intensify?

## India Angle
Indian state governments must balance competing interests during crises—healthcare, economy, livelihoods—while strengthening local governance capacity and transparency to ensure inclusive development in rural and urban contexts.
