## Core Argument
Leaders must inspire confidence and chart vision; their job is galvanizing people, not amplifying fears.

## Key Points
- Leaders instil confidence and galvanize; they cannot afford to share distraught thoughts or let things drift passively.
- Corporate leaders drive policy through hierarchy; political leaders must convince people and paint visions worth supporting.
- Identifying talent quickly and handing over power while alert ensures successors have basic attributes for trust.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance acknowledging real anxieties and fears while exorcising them without appearing tone-deaf or dismissive of legitimate concerns?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders like Birla and Adani navigate crises and competitive battles; Lee Kuan Yew's leadership philosophy offers lessons for Indian corporate and political leaders managing stakeholder confidence and succession.
