## Core Argument
Chinese consumers defy traditional developing-market logic: they trade up selectively by category while remaining value-conscious, constantly evolving rapidly.

## Key Points
- Chinese consumers segment across tiers and show contradictory spending: premium on status goods, frugal on commodities.
- P&G's misstep was treating China as developing market; premium aspiration accelerated faster than the company anticipated.
- Successful marketing requires real-time learning and rapid unlearning rather than static consumer category frameworks.

## Open Question
How can companies balance portfolio strategies across premium and mass segments when individual consumers constantly shift spending priorities by category rather than income level?

## India Angle
Indian companies expanding to China or facing Chinese competition must understand that premium aspiration coexists with value-consciousness; similar patterns emerging in India's tier-1/2 cities where middle-class consumers display contradictory spending habits across categories.
