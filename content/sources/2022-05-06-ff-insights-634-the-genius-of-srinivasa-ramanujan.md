## Core Argument
Ramanujan's posthumous relevance shows how transformative ideas may precede their own era's questions.

## Key Points
- Ramanujan answered mathematical questions that wouldn't be asked for decades, operating ahead of his time.
- His work now underpins digital encryption, cancer research, and quantum gravity—demonstrating delayed but profound impact.
- Recognition of genius requires institutional frameworks that can identify and nurture unconventional talent.

## Open Question
How can institutions identify and support genius when its significance is unknowable in real-time, and what structures does India need to prevent losing its Ramanujans today?

## India Angle
Ramanujan exemplifies India's untapped intellectual capacity and the tragic loss when genius operates outside institutional support systems—a cautionary tale for how India's educational and institutional ecosystem may be missing future innovators.
