## Core Argument
AI is evolving beyond LLMs toward autonomous discovery systems; humanity must prepare for profound, unpredictable consequences.

## Key Points
- AutoML-Zero creates algorithms from scratch without prior mathematical knowledge or human-designed rules.
- Emerging AI systems are designing novel drugs, materials, and solving complex problems in weeks instead of years.
- Advanced AI (with quantum and neuromorphic computing) poses existential risks requiring urgent governance frameworks.

## Open Question
How do we harness AI's potential for solving humanity's greatest challenges while preventing misuse or unforeseen consequences by systems that may exceed human control?

## India Angle
India risks missing the AI evolution beyond LLMs; urgent need for policy frameworks and research investment in AutoML, drug discovery, and energy systems before advanced AI capabilities arrive.
