## Core Argument
Incremental learning theory builds resilience; fixed mindset makes talented people brittle under pressure.

## Key Points
- Entity theorists see ability as fixed; learning theorists believe mastery comes through deliberate effort
- High-performing individuals often most vulnerable to helplessness when perfectionist image shatters
- Hedonic treadmill resets goals constantly, making external achievement insufficient for sustained happiness

## Open Question
How do we cultivate incremental learning in high-stakes competitive environments that reward immediate results and punish failure?

## India Angle
Indian education system and workplace culture often emphasize fixed intelligence and perfectionism; shifting to incremental learning mindset critical for competitive edge and resilience in volatile economy
