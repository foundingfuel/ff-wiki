## Core Argument
Simputer's failure reveals India's gaps in hardware manufacturing, marketing, and risk funding despite strong design capability.

## Key Points
- Simputer had cutting-edge tech (accelerometer 2004, handwriting annotation) but failed commercially due to market positioning confusion.
- Bottom-of-pyramid focus was visionary but ahead of time; mid-level managers pushed urban features, diluting the core value proposition.
- India lacks ecosystem capabilities: risk funding for hardware, manufacturing sophistication, and expertise in marketing new device categories.

## Open Question
How can India foster hardware innovation at scale when the ecosystem penalises entrepreneurs who pursue non-mainstream market segments, even if they're technically sound?

## India Angle
India's capability gap in hardware manufacturing, marketing new technology categories, and risk funding—critical for Make in India's success in electronics. The Simputer shows India can innovate but struggles with commercialisation and scaling hardware.
