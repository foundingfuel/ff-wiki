## Core Argument
Economic crises amplify ethno-nationalist politics, threatening pluralistic democracies globally, particularly India's.

## Key Points
- Manmohan Singh warned that global slowdown threatened job creation for India's young population amid inequality.
- Religious and ethnic solidarity becomes politically weaponized during uncertain times, strengthening Hindu nationalist BJP.
- India's nuclear standoff with Pakistan remains destabilizing despite development, creating persistent security anxiety.

## Open Question
How can democracies like India harness nationalism for unity while preventing its manipulation into communal violence and authoritarian drift during crises?

Examines India's economic vulnerabilities, internal social tensions (communalism vs. pluralism), and Pakistan relations through Obama's perspective during 2008-09 crisis; reflects structural challenges India faces in managing growth inequality and political polarization.
