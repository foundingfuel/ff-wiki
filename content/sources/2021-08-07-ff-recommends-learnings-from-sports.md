## Core Argument
Sports reveals character under pressure; its lessons on preparation, self-awareness, and leadership apply directly to business.

## Key Points
- Preparation for worst-case scenarios, not just ideal outcomes, separates elite performers from others
- Talent spotting is unreliable; sustained performance depends on role clarity, mentorship, and psychological resilience
- Great leadership requires ego discipline—letting former leaders contribute without insecurity undermines team potential

## Open Question
If talent is overrated and preparation matters most, why do some prepared individuals burn out while others with less obvious talent sustain success over decades?

## India Angle
Indian cricket provides accessible case studies for Indian entrepreneurs and leaders; local examples (Kohli, Dhoni, Paes) make leadership and talent management principles relatable to Indian business context
