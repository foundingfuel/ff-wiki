## Core Argument
India must navigate 2019's pivotal questions on politics, privacy, and technology's societal impact simultaneously.

## Key Points
- Elections 2019 will reshape political narratives beyond corruption-focused messaging in world's largest democracy.
- Data protection law urgent as India becomes data-rich nation managing privacy amid economic growth.
- Technology adoption creates efficiency gains but raises unresolved tensions around job displacement and inclusivity.

## Open Question
How can India harness technology's efficiency benefits while protecting privacy rights and employment, given weak institutional capacity and limited regulatory frameworks?

## India Angle
India faces critical 2019 decisions on elections, data protection, and technology's employment impact—shaping how the nation balances growth, privacy rights, and inclusive development amid rapid digital transformation.
