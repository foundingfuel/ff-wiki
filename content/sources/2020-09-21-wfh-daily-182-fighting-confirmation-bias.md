## Core Argument
Deliberately seek disconfirming evidence to overcome confirmation bias clouding judgment and decisions.

## Key Points
- Confirmation bias leads professionals to ignore contradictory evidence that challenges their narratives.
- Digital platforms and personalized content amplify bias by creating echo chambers of like-minded people.
- Actively write down beliefs and hunt for disconfirming evidence as a practical counterweight.

## Open Question
How do busy professionals and organizations systematically practice disconfirming evidence-seeking when speed and urgency often pressure quick decisions?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders need stronger frameworks to combat confirmation bias in decision-making, especially critical during WFH when information sources narrow and echo chambers strengthen.
