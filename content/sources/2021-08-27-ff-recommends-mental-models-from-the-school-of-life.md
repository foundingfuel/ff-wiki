## Core Argument
Mental models from diverse domains enable better life decisions by helping us think systematically about complex problems.

## Key Points
- Immortality Illusion drives poor financial and lifestyle decisions; awareness of mortality enables long-term planning.
- Inversion—asking what to avoid rather than what to pursue—solves problems that resist conventional problem-solving.
- Radical Inflexibility on core principles (health, family, values) creates sustainable success despite external pressures.

## Open Question
How do we maintain radical inflexibility on principles when organizational and social systems reward flexibility and compromise?

## India Angle
Indian professionals often struggle with work-life boundaries and long-term financial planning; mental models offer a framework for better decision-making across personal and professional contexts relevant to India's high-pressure work culture.
