## Core Argument
Institutionalizing talent spotting and granting autonomy to young leaders determines organizational competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Early recognition of exceptional talent by experienced mentors multiplies chances of developing future leaders.
- Institutionalize leadership development processes rather than relying on individual genius of departing leaders.
- Create psychological safety and autonomy; older leaders must step aside to allow young talent to flourish.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance nurturing young talent through autonomy while maintaining accountability and preventing entitlement without elite cadre systems?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises must adopt systematic talent spotting and leadership grooming like KV Kamath at ICICI Bank and MS Dhoni in cricket, while overcoming cultural reluctance of senior leaders to relinquish control and create space for young talent.
