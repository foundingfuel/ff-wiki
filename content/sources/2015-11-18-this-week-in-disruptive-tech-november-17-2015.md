## Core Argument
Emerging tech disrupts food, security, emotions, and work through competing models—centralised efficiency versus distributed human expertise.

## Key Points
- Food delivery and home-cook networks represent fundamentally different futures: hub-and-spoke versus distributed mesh models.
- Encryption and digital tools blur lines between innovation and security threats, requiring new governance frameworks.
- Microexpression recognition and emotion AI hint at deeper human capability augmentation beyond current applications.

## Open Question
If distributed, human-centric systems are superior, why do centralised, efficiency-driven models continue to dominate and attract capital?

## India Angle
Food tech disruption mirrors India's emerging delivery and home-cook economy; synthetic meat threatens Indian agricultural and political interests; gig economy models like Uber need contextual adaptation for India's informal labour market.
