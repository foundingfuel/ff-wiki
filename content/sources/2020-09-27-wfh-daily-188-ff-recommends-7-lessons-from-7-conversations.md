## Core Argument
Thriving in remote work requires authenticity, boundary-pushing, and unlearning conventional wisdom.

## Key Points
- Authenticity online matters; Gen Z blurs real/virtual distinction while older generations compartmentalize.
- Virtual replicates some aspects but loses critical elements like audience energy and real-world nuance.
- Ignorance of conventions enables entrepreneurs to persist when told 'it's not possible.'

## Open Question
How do we preserve the benefits of virtual connection (scale, access, authenticity) while recovering what's lost in translation (real-world energy, serendipity, embodied interaction)?
