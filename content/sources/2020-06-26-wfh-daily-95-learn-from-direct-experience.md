## Core Argument
Direct experience learning fails when action consequences extend beyond organizational visibility horizons.

## Key Points
- Peter Senge's learning horizon concept: we can't learn from actions whose consequences lie outside our observational range.
- COVID-19 represents structural change, not temporary disruption—organizations must design systems for decision-making under sustained uncertainty.
- Indian IT companies face dual challenge: enabling WFH while capturing delayed feedback loops that drive organizational learning.

## Open Question
How can organizations build accountability and learning when the consequences of today's WFH decisions won't become visible for months or years?

## India Angle
Indian organizations transitioning to WFH must grapple with how to create learning systems where delayed consequences of decisions still drive accountability and growth—critical as Indian businesses navigate permanent structural shifts rather than temporary disruptions.
