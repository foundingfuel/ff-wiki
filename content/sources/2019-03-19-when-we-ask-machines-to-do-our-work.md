## Core Argument
Automation improves efficiency but erodes human capability and organisational resilience when systems fail.

## Key Points
- New professionals lack core skills (surgery, law) due to over-reliance on AI and machines during training.
- Organisations dependent on technology become fragile; system failures expose human unpreparedness for manual override.
- Data should serve public good through government access and charitable stewardship, not just private extraction.

## Open Question
How do we harness technology's efficiency gains without sacrificing the human capability and organisational muscle memory needed to survive system failures?

## India Angle
India's adoption of AI/automation in healthcare, governance, and EV infrastructure must account for skill atrophy in professionals; data governance models like National Statistical Office access could position India as a public-good AI leader.
