## Core Argument
Startups fail due to broken founder trust, built through explicit agreements and transparent communication protocols.

## Key Points
- Founders must align on equity, roles, conflict resolution, and leadership before forming the company.
- Trust emerges from flag-move-revisit-resolve transparency, not avoiding difficult conversations.
- Synchronous communication and zero-latency protocols prevent information asymmetry as teams scale.

## Open Question
How do founders maintain transparent, synchronous communication while managing rapid growth and split responsibilities across multiple geographies and stakeholders?

## India Angle
Critical for India's startup ecosystem where founder teams often lack frameworks for early-stage trust-building; most Indian startups fail due to co-founder conflicts rather than market issues.
