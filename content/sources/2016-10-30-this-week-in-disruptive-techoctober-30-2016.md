## Core Argument
Chatbots, privacy regulation, and blockchain are reshaping digital business architecture globally.

## Key Points
- Chatbots evolving from general assistants to domain specialists (banking, healthcare, support).
- Privacy approaches diverging: West uses law, US uses technology; both have loopholes.
- Blockchain disrupting backend efficiency in cross-border trade and financial processing.

## Open Question
How can societies balance the convenience and efficiency gains of digitization with genuine data privacy and security, when neither legal nor technological approaches have proven fully effective?

## India Angle
India's emerging fintech and digital payments ecosystem must navigate similar privacy-security tensions as the West while competing with China's data-driven approach; blockchain applications could streamline India's cross-border trade and banking inefficiencies.
