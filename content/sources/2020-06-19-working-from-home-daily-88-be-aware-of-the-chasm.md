## Core Argument
Chasms between disciplines harm individuals and societies; pandemic forces recognition of interconnectedness.

## Key Points
- Finance and humanities divide mirrors C.P. Snow's literary-scientist chasm; both remain unresolved.
- Remote work demands sustainable pacing, deliberate self-care, and recognition of underestimated originality.
- Creativity flourishes when managers actively validate team originality and counter systemic self-doubt.

## Open Question
If people systematically underestimate their creativity, how can they validate that underestimation is the problem and not realism about their actual capabilities?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and managers increasingly operate across technical and humanities disciplines; bridging this gap is critical for India's transition from outsourcing to innovation-driven economy and building holistic organizational cultures.
