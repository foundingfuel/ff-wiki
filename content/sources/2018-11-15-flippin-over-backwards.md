## Core Argument
Indian tech founders' hubris and poor governance judgment undermine ecosystem credibility with global investors.

## Key Points
- Both Flipkart co-founders exited within six months of $16B Walmart deal due to leadership and governance lapses
- Binny Bansal failed to disclose serious personal misconduct during due diligence, posing reputational risk to Walmart
- Indian startup ecosystem conflates media hype and awards with actual institutional maturity and global standards compliance

## Open Question
How can Indian entrepreneurs build globally competitive companies if the path to scale requires abandoning founder control, yet founders lack the governance discipline global investors demand?

## India Angle
Indian tech founders' lack of governance maturity and transparency standards when interfacing with global investors; reveals gap between startup hype and institutional readiness in India's entrepreneurial ecosystem
