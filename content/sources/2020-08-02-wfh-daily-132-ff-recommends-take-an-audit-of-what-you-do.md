## Core Argument
Audit and redesign work practices for sustained performance; temporary coping mechanisms become dangerous defaults.

## Key Points
- Extended crisis demands permanent work redesign, not temporary coping stretching hours indefinitely.
- Audit all activities to segregate must-dos from habits that existed only in pre-pandemic world.
- Build rituals—breaks between calls, journaling, reflection—to restore boundaries and energy sustainability.

## Open Question
How do individuals and organizations distinguish between genuinely necessary work and habits masquerading as necessities when remote boundaries have collapsed?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and organizations need to urgently reassess work practices during prolonged WFH to prevent burnout and unsustainability—critical for India's competitive workforce.
