## Core Argument
Design has shifted from cosmetic to economic substance: 84% of S&P 500 value now intangible, design-driven firms outperform 228%.

## Key Points
- Design is now inseparable from digital product offering and user experience, not just aesthetics.
- Winner-take-all internet economics reward design excellence since competitors match functionality but lose on experience.
- Companies installing chief design officers at board level; 50% of design acquisitions happened in 2015-2016 alone.

## Open Question
If design is truly a core economic driver, why do most Indian enterprises still treat it as a support function rather than strategic lever reporting to CEO?

## India Angle
Indian companies like Inditex competitors and fast-fashion players must recognize design as core competitive advantage; Indian startups and legacy businesses need to embed design thinking across org structure, not treat it as cosmetic; rising Indian middle class (200M+) expects designed experiences, not just functional products.
