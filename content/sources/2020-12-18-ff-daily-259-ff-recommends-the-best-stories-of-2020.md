## Core Argument
2020 exposed India's fragile consumer economy; resilience lies in human-centered solutions and local systems innovation.

## Key Points
- Mainstream economics treats labour as commodity; must centre human wellbeing and dignity in policy design.
- India's economy built on 20% surplus income group; vulnerable majority needs local systems support, not just growth metrics.
- Ground-level stories reveal informal economy's precarity—migrant workers, vegetable vendors lack social safety nets in cities.

## Open Question
How can India move from growth-obsessed metrics to wellbeing-focused economics while maintaining investment in public institutions and rural livelihoods simultaneously?

## India Angle
India's 2020 crisis was structural—economic slowdown predating Covid, rural poverty, urban informal sector vulnerability, migrant labour collapse. Solutions require local systems thinking, public institution investment, and human-centered economics beyond GDP metrics.
