## Core Argument
Indian entrepreneurs must reclaim indigenous philosophical traditions instead of mimicking Western capitalism's Promethean model.

## Key Points
- Prometheus myth embodies Western capitalism: endless growth, nature as exploitable resource, individualistic profit-seeking.
- Modern Indian entrepreneurs copy Facebook/Amazon models and Western heroes, abandoning Tata/Murthy legacies and Indian thought.
- Promethean mindset drives Indian business toward short-term gains over innovation, seen in Housing.com and Seemabaddha's corporate ethics.

## Open Question
If Indian entrepreneurship has deep indigenous philosophical alternatives to Promethean thinking, why hasn't the author articulated what those alternatives actually are—and how they translate into practical business models?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs uncritically adopt Western (Promethean) models of endless growth and resource exploitation, losing indigenous philosophical frameworks that could differentiate Indian entrepreneurship and create sustainable, values-driven ventures.
