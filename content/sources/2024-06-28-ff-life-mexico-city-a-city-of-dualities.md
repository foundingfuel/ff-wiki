## Core Argument
Countries reveal themselves through cultural and historical lenses chosen by the observer, not stereotypes.

## Key Points
- Mexico City embeds dualities: Spanish conquest layered on Aztec heritage, past constantly informing present.
- Deliberate cultural curation—food, art, architecture—offers richer understanding than media narratives or preconceptions.
- Public spaces like Zocalo serve as stages for multiple stories: political protests, religious faith, artistic expression simultaneously.

## Open Question
How do travelers avoid projecting their own narratives onto a place while still being shaped by their pre-visit assumptions and media consumption?
