## Core Argument
True strength lies in humility; true progress requires questioning unexamined assumptions about what's right.

## Key Points
- Humility is strength, not weakness—only the powerful can afford genuine humility without ego.
- Scientific progress always disrupts ethical certainties; plant-based meat may solve one problem while creating others.
- India's architects are course-correcting by combining traditional climate-adapted design with modern technology, rejecting blind Western imitation.

## Open Question
How do we distinguish between necessary scientific/social progress and unexamined disruption of what works contextually?

## India Angle
Indian architects and designers are rediscovering indigenous building practices suited to local climate, reversing decades of blind Western adoption post-liberalization—a metaphor for India reclaiming context-specific solutions across sectors.
