## Core Argument
Teaching average salespeople storytelling can boost team sales by 20% through rapport, credibility, and value demonstration.

## Key Points
- Top 20% of salespeople outsell others by 200% primarily through natural storytelling ability, teachable to others.
- B2B solution sales benefit most from storytelling because frontline conversations are the only persuasion method available.
- Stories build rapport, establish credibility, demonstrate value, and handle objections better than data or facts alone.

## Open Question
If storytelling is a natural skill everyone possesses, why do only 20% of salespeople use it effectively, and what prevents the other 80% from developing it despite high ROI?

## India Angle
Indian B2B sales teams, particularly in IT services, consulting, and complex solutions selling, can leverage storytelling to close the 200% performance gap between average and star performers—critical for competing globally.
