## Core Argument
Pandemic constraints can catalyze deeper learning, creativity, and personal growth when reframed as opportunity.

## Key Points
- Parents pivoting from screen-time anxiety to intentional offline activities and skill-building during lockdown.
- Child-led entrepreneurship (Dough & Batter MSME) teaches real-world business principles while building confidence and resilience.
- Growth requires perspective: geological time reminds us human life is brief; pandemic phase is temporary catalyst for change.

## Open Question
How do parents balance necessary online learning with protecting children from excessive screen dependency, especially when the pandemic makes digital tools unavoidable?
