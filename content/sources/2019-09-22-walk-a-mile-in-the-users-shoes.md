## Core Argument
User-centered design and local context beat imported technology; Embrace's sleeping-bag incubator saved 200,000 Indian babies.

## Key Points
- Embrace designed for local constraints: no electricity, transportable, inexpensive, culturally appropriate—not Western features.
- Design thinking requires observing actual users in their environment, not assuming solutions work across contexts.
- Complex problems need local systems solutions, not top-down centralized programs imposed by distant institutions.

## Open Question
If locally-adapted solutions are superior, why do most innovators default to scaling Western best practices instead of investing time in understanding local operating environments?

## India Angle
Indian healthcare startups must solve problems through local context and affordability, not by importing Western solutions; systems thinking and local change agents are critical for scaling impact in India's diverse operating environments.
