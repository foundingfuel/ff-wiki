## Core Argument
Strategic thinking beats speed; Jio's OS play signals India's shift toward building foundational tech infrastructure, not just services.

## Key Points
- Donald Keough's insight: deep thinking beats data-driven consensus; visionary products emerge from thinking, not surveys.
- Reliance Jio reportedly developing JioBook (₹10k laptop) and Jio OS—pursuing vertical integration in hardware-software ecosystem.
- Boston Dynamics case shows rapid iteration becomes possible once foundational tools and pipelines are built; scaling innovation accelerates.

## Open Question
Can Jio's OS succeed without developer ecosystem and third-party app support, or does the low-cost hardware thesis alone justify platform investment?

## India Angle
Reliance Jio's potential JioBook and Jio OS represent India's ambition to build indigenous tech infrastructure and democratize computing through ultra-low-cost devices—critical for a digitally stratified nation with 800M+ internet users but limited hardware affordability.
