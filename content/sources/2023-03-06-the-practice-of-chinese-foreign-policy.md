## Core Argument
China's foreign policy success relies on narrative construction, deception, and exploiting Western blindness while India's China policy underperforms despite superior analysis.

## Key Points
- China uses false narratives and victim-playing to extract concessions and manipulate Western countries into compliance.
- India's experienced diplomats withhold analytical insights due to outdated secrecy rules, weakening public understanding of China.
- India participates in ineffective multilateral platforms like BRICS that primarily benefit China's strategic positioning.

## Open Question
Why do Indian policymakers consistently produce suboptimal outcomes in China relations when Indian diplomats and analysts possess superior strategic understanding of Chinese behavior and intentions?

## India Angle
India's China policy has been suboptimal despite strong diplomatic analysis; Indian policymakers fail to leverage expert insights; India participates in ineffective multilateral forums (BRICS, RIC) that benefit China more; India needs better transparency and public-private knowledge exchange on China strategy.
