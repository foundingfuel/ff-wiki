## Core Argument
Fast-scaling companies must adopt civic responsibility and collaborate with regulators to manage systemic risks.

## Key Points
- Blitzscaling creates civic-scale responsibilities; companies need governance thinking beyond profit maximization.
- Responsible innovation requires transparent stakeholder dialogue with regulators, not defiance or stonewalling.
- Indian vaccine makers prioritized profits over equity despite public narrative of humanitarian contribution.

## Open Question
How can companies balance rapid innovation and profit incentives with genuine public good when regulators lack expertise to govern emerging technologies?

## India Angle
Indian vaccine makers face scrutiny for high profit margins despite public perception of charity; tension between rapid scaling, profit motive, and public health responsibility reflects broader challenge for Indian corporations operating at scale.
