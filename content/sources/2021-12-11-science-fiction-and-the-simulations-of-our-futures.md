## Core Argument
Science fiction reframes present assumptions through future narratives, helping leaders imagine alternative societal futures shaped by technology.

## Key Points
- Sci-fi examines today through tomorrow lens, not predictive but reframing assumptions about technology, politics, and society
- Major corporations and militaries now consult sci-fi writers as strategic tools for understanding future implications
- Explores how algorithms, data systems, and meritocracy could fundamentally reshape governance, caste, and human worth in near-future scenarios

## Open Question
If science fiction is useful for questioning assumptions rather than predicting futures, how do we distinguish between plausible warnings and speculative extrapolations when advising policy and business strategy?
