## Core Argument
Sustainable startup success requires intentional culture rooted in founder values, not policies or artefacts.

## Key Points
- Culture reflects who founders are; it's about daily behaviours and what gets rewarded, not open offices or espoused values.
- Data-driven culture works when teams move from gut instinct to hypothesis-based decisions; analysis paralysis must be actively managed.
- Early-stage founders often skip culture-building for survival; scaling forces codification of intuitive practices, but rare investors prioritise long-term thinking.

## Open Question
How do founders maintain authentic, founder-centric culture while scaling hiring and ensuring it doesn't become brittle doctrine that stifles adaptation?

## India Angle
Indian startups must build intentional cultures reflecting their identity and context, not blindly replicate Silicon Valley models. Bigbasket's approach—combining customer obsession, data-driven decisions, and humble execution—offers a template for Indian founders scaling rapidly.
