## Core Argument
AI enables medical breakthroughs but risks creating bioweapons; global collaboration essential over competitive AI races.

## Key Points
- Narrow AI successfully predicted COVID-19 and accelerated vaccine development; ChatGPT diagnosed rare disorders doctors missed.
- AI can design deadly pathogens and chemicals—North Carolina experiment created 40,000 toxic molecules in six hours.
- Collaboration on shared data centres and joint research across Asia, Africa, Middle East needed; competitive racing invites catastrophe.

## Open Question
How do we enable AI's life-saving medical potential while preventing rogue actors and competitive nations from weaponizing the same capabilities?

## India Angle
India and Asian countries can leverage AI for early disease detection and epidemic prediction to address infectious and rare diseases; however, India must participate in global AI governance frameworks to prevent misuse of AI for bioweapon creation.
