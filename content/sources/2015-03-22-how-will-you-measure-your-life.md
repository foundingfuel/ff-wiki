## Core Argument
Authentic personal impact requires alignment between public and private selves, not external branding.

## Key Points
- Mid-career crises force executives to question whether they prioritized work over personal happiness and relationships.
- Persona stems from self-knowledge about what makes you happy, not from external makeovers or social metrics.
- True influence comes from genuine expertise and integrity, not from curated public images or follower counts.

## Open Question
How do you build genuine influence and impact without engaging in any form of personal branding or image management in an increasingly digital, searchable world?

## India Angle
Indian executives face acute pressure to succeed materially while navigating mid-career crises; the lack of introspection frameworks means many hit 40s without understanding their authentic values or life priorities, especially relevant in India's high-pressure corporate environments
