## Core Argument
Replace anti-stories blocking strategy adoption with authentic counter-stories, not facts or arguments.

## Key Points
- Anti-stories are pre-existing beliefs rooted in experience; rational arguments and assertions cannot defeat them.
- Effective tactics include admitting mistakes, challenging generalizations with specific examples, demonstrating behavioural change, and building authentic leadership capability.
- Leaders must improvise counter-stories authentically using personal experience rather than deliver pre-packaged corporate messaging.

## Open Question
How can organizations scale authentic storytelling across multiple leaders without it becoming formulaic or losing credibility as it cascades down the hierarchy?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often struggle with strategy execution due to hierarchical cultures and deeply embedded beliefs; this framework directly addresses how Indian leaders can overcome resistance to change through authentic storytelling rather than top-down directives.
