## Core Argument
Effective storytelling requires dramatic tension, character investment, and strategic detail—not information overload.

## Key Points
- Good stories build emotional investment through character and drama before revealing resolution.
- Irrelevant details kill narratives; selective information matters more than comprehensive facts.
- Disinformation threatens democracy when platforms amplify falsehoods faster than journalists can counter.

## Open Question
How can institutions restore trust in journalism when social media platforms prioritize engagement over accuracy, especially in democracies with weak regulatory capacity?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and policymakers must master storytelling for communication effectiveness; India's media ecosystem faces similar disinformation challenges as Philippines and Russia, requiring stronger institutional accountability.
