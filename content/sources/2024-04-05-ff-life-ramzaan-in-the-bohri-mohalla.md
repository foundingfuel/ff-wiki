## Core Argument
Tradition and modernity coexist in Bohri Mohalla as heritage food businesses adapt to stay relevant amid urban redevelopment.

## Key Points
- Bohri Mohalla's iconic food heritage draws diverse crowds during Ramzaan, blending community tradition with modern consumer experience.
- Dawoodi Bohra traders evolved from British-era chawls to multi-generational businesses, with some relocating while maintaining mohalla ties.
- Young entrepreneurs like Badshah Sweets brothers innovate within heritage, adapting family recipes and formats to compete in modern retail spaces.

## Open Question
How can traditional family businesses preserve their authentic identity and community anchoring while scaling through modern retail formats and competition?

## India Angle
Mumbai's Bohri Mohalla as a case study of how traditional Indian communities and businesses navigate modernisation while preserving cultural identity and food heritage during Ramzaan.
