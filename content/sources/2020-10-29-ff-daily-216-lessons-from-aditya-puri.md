## Core Argument
Crisis leadership requires communicating uncertainty, distributing power, and building high-trust teams deliberately.

## Key Points
- Assemble A-teams with complementary expertise and relationships to differentiate in competitive markets
- Regular structured leadership cadence (Monday meetings) maintains alignment while granting operational autonomy to teams
- Counterintuitive crisis leadership: communicate without answers, share power, and prioritize psychological safety over control

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the need for decisive action in crisis with honest communication about uncertainty, when stakeholders demand clarity?

## India Angle
Aditya Puri's leadership model at HDFC Bank—building A-teams, structured governance, work-life balance—offers a proven blueprint for Indian banking and organizational leadership during transformation and crisis.
