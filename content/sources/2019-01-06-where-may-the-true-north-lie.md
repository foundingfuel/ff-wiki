## Core Argument
True North for individuals and organisations lies in continuous self-improvement and questioning, not New Year resolutions.

## Key Points
- Scale-obsessed development ignores equity, dignity, and local diversity—India's problems need contextual, not monolithic solutions.
- Real freedom requires daily discipline, attention, and genuine care for others—not unconscious default-setting.
- Self-directed growth comes from asking better questions, especially ones you're not asking yourself.

## Open Question
How do large-scale interventions (like Aadhaar) achieve social impact without reducing people to passive consumers rather than active citizens?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and leaders must balance scale (Aadhaar-sized ambitions) with dignity, equity, and citizen agency—a tension unique to India's development context and diversity challenges.
