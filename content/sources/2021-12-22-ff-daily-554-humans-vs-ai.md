## Core Argument
Humans retain advantage in tacit knowledge and interpersonal skills, but AI progress threatens even these domains.

## Key Points
- Polanyi's Paradox: tacit human knowledge cannot be codified, making it hard for AI to replicate complex problem-solving.
- Moravec's Paradox: AI masters analytical tasks but struggles with perception and mobility that children master naturally.
- Cloud Robotics and Deep Learning could eventually close the human-machine gap through exponential learning rates.

## Open Question
If experiential, intuitive human skills are what differentiate us from machines, yet our education system actively suppresses their development, how will the next generation compete?

## India Angle
Indian education system mirrors the rote-learning trap Vijapurkar describes; AI advancement could exacerbate skill gaps if India doesn't shift from textbook-dependent to experiential learning models.
