## Core Argument
Slowing down intentionally creates awareness and self-discovery that rushing through experiences cannot provide.

## Key Points
- Conscious deceleration opens sensory awareness and deeper observation of surroundings and self.
- Juxtaposition of ancient and modern reveals paradoxes that prompt identity questioning.
- Mindfulness practice yields unexpected rewards—encountering the Toucan, unexpected human connections—unavailable to rushed travellers.

## Open Question
How much of the discovered self-awareness comes from intentional slowness versus the privilege of freedom to travel and pause—can this practice be accessible to those without such mobility?
