## Core Argument
COVID-19 demands we reimagine economy's foundations: resilient systems with dignity and place for everyone, not old normal.

## Key Points
- The pandemic exposes inequality: those who built cities now abandoned without shelter, food, or water while privileged isolate safely.
- Three-horizon thinking required: immediate medical response, life-sustaining solutions for all, and long-term resilient economy design.
- Hope and shared vision are emotional necessities during crisis; choosing ideals helps navigate compromises when resources are scarce.

## Open Question
How do we balance swift centralized response to immediate medical crisis with building the decentralized, inclusive systems needed for long-term economic resilience?

## India Angle
India's post-COVID economy must address the stark divide between gated communities and informal workers who built its infrastructure; rebuilding requires inclusive foundations that don't repeat the exclusion of migrant workers and slum dwellers.
