## Core Argument
Cool brands transcend promises by boosting customer self-esteem and enabling desired social projection.

## Key Points
- Cool brands satisfy customers while making them happy through enhanced self-esteem and identity projection
- Three DNA attributes: deliver promise, boost confidence, enable peer perception management without explicit communication
- Cool brands require continuous evolution; what's cool degrades with generational shifts and requires strategic repositioning

## Open Question
How can brands remain cool across generations when cultural values shift, given that yesterday's cool (smoking) becomes today's taboo?

## India Angle
Indian brands like Lux have succeeded by understanding generational coolness through Bollywood celebrity endorsement; Indian entrepreneurs must build brands that evolve with cultural shifts while maintaining relevance across generations.
