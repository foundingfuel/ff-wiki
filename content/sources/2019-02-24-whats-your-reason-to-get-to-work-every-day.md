## Core Argument
Meaningful work matters more than money; pursuing wealth alone creates misery despite success.

## Key Points
- Money is insufficient measure of success; meaningfulness of work drives engagement and satisfaction.
- Wealth accumulation without purpose creates upward spiral disconnected from original enterprise goals.
- GDP inadequately measures prosperity; nations and individuals need alternative frameworks valuing fulfillment.

## Open Question
How do individuals and organizations balance financial security with purposeful work when systems reward only material output?

## India Angle
India's entrepreneurial ecosystem prioritizes wealth creation and valuations over purpose; Indian professionals face pressure to chase material success rather than meaningful work, echoing global but acute in Indian startup/corporate culture.
