## Core Argument
AI collapses industry boundaries, making careers and strategies about repurposing capabilities across contexts, not defending silos.

## Key Points
- Industry boundaries dissolve when AI lets companies combine capabilities from different sectors into new business models.
- Job value derives from rare skills, coordination ability, and risk assumption—not tasks, which AI commoditizes quickly.
- Career strategy must shift from linear progression to portfolio approach: continuously learn, signal capabilities, and monetize strategically.

## Open Question
If typing became accessible to everyone (killing typists), how do knowledge workers identify durable sources of value when AI makes most cognitive tasks commoditized?

## India Angle
Indian companies across auto, retail, insurance, and power sectors can repurpose assets and capabilities into new revenue streams via AI. Usage-based insurance models already emerging in India exemplify this shift. Career advice directly applicable to Indian professionals navigating rapid AI disruption in knowledge economy.
