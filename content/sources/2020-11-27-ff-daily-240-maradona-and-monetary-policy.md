## Core Argument
Control requires feedback and agency; monetary policy works through expectation management, not direct force.

## Key Points
- Control differs from causation—controllers need feedback to modulate action; bullets are caused but uncontrolled.
- Maradona beat defenders by running straight because they expected lateral movement—monetary policy similarly exploits market expectations.
- Free speech at corporate festivals faces tension: sponsors seek influence while festivals claim editorial independence.

## Open Question
How can institutions balance corporate sponsorship with editorial freedom when boundaries on speech inevitably erode commitment to open discourse?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers and RBI officials can learn from Maradona-monetary policy analogy: how expectations management and signaling can influence economic outcomes without heavy-handed intervention—relevant as India navigates inflation, growth, and market confidence.
