## Core Argument
High-impact leaders combine competence with warmth; competence alone breeds envy, not followership.

## Key Points
- Competence without warmth triggers envy and resentment; leaders must score high on both dimensions to be admired.
- Warmth is behaviorally learnable: through language choices, active listening, body language, and consistent appreciation.
- Measurable leadership impact: use 360-degree appraisals asking if people feel good in your presence and trust confiding in you.

## Open Question
The article prescribes warmth behaviors but doesn't address whether these can feel inauthentic when forced, or how to maintain such behaviors under high-pressure decision-making.

## India Angle
Indian professionals often prioritize task competence over interpersonal warmth in hierarchical corporate cultures; this challenges the command-and-control management style prevalent in many Indian enterprises, proposing warmth as equally critical for contemporary leadership.
