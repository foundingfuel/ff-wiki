## Core Argument
Overnight success requires years of accumulated experience and layered technological foundations, not sudden inspiration.

## Key Points
- Pokémon Go's success built on 20 years of prior innovation: Pokémon franchise, Ingress, Google Maps and geospatial data.
- AR technology raising societal questions about reality vs. illusion, local vs. global capital capture, and human-machine relationships.
- Bitcoin's incentive mechanism (diminishing mining rewards offset by rising valuations) demonstrates how economic design shapes technology adoption.

## Open Question
Can Pokémon Go's market expansion and AR adoption benefits offset concerns about privacy, safety, and wealth concentration in Silicon Valley and Kyoto-based companies?

## India Angle
India's emerging AR/VR market and local businesses' vulnerability to global tech platforms capturing consumer spending; questions about data privacy and surveillance in an Indian context where regulatory frameworks are still developing.
