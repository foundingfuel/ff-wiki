## Core Argument
Practicing kindness, consuming uplifting art, and self-care are antidotes to pandemic-induced despair and burnout.

## Key Points
- Helping others and community engagement creates meaning and prevents burnout during crises.
- Art, literature, and film enable flow states and psychological respite from distress.
- Self-kindness through sleep, emotional awareness, and meditation builds resilience during uncertainty.

## Open Question
How do individual coping mechanisms address systemic institutional failures that created the despair in the first place?
