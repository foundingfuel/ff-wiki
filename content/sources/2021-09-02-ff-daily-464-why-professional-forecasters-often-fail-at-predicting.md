## Core Argument
Professional forecasters fail by overfitting noise to data, creating models that look good theoretically but fail in practice.

## Key Points
- Overfitting mistakes noise for signal, producing overly specific solutions that don't generalize to new situations.
- Overfit models perform worse in real-world predictions despite appearing superior on historical data—a dangerous double bind.
- Digital transformation requires embedding adaptive thinking across organizations, not just adopting technology as a goal itself.

## Open Question
How can organizations distinguish between meaningful patterns and noise when making decisions under uncertainty, especially in emerging markets with limited historical data?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and policymakers often rely on forecasts for critical decisions—from monetary policy to market entry. Understanding overfitting bias is crucial for Indian entrepreneurs and leaders making decisions with limited historical data in rapidly changing markets.
