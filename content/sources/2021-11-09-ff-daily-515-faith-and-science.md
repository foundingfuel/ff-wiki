## Core Argument
Individual good intentions paradoxically worsen outcomes in complex systems without structural policy redesign.

## Key Points
- Darwin delayed publishing evolution despite conviction because it conflicted with his wife Emma's faith and grief.
- Greta's Dilemma shows environmentally conscious individuals can collectively cause greater harm without systemic rule changes.
- Complex, strategically-connected global systems require understanding interdependencies, not just individual moral action.

## Open Question
How do we design policies that harness individual moral consciousness without relying on systems that structurally punish good intentions?

## India Angle
India's development challenges—climate action, pandemic response, policy reform—require navigating similar tensions between individual intentions and collective outcomes; systems thinking approach critical for Indian policymakers designing effective environmental and social policies.
