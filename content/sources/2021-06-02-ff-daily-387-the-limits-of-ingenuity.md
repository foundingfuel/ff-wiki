## Core Argument
Ingenuity alone cannot solve systemic crises; founders must rethink growth models and personal transitions.

## Key Points
- Climate change risks (refugee flows, monsoon disruption) exceed what human ingenuity alone can solve without environmental cooperation.
- Founder transitions require honest self-assessment of S-curves, family values, and whether staying drives business or personal growth.
- Bootstrapping vs VC funding reflects deeper choice: chase growth and valuations or pursue profits and sustainability.

## Open Question
If ingenuity has built civilizations but climate instability precedes their collapse, can entrepreneurial solutions outpace environmental tipping points without first solving the climate problem itself?

## India Angle
India faces dual crises: climate volatility (monsoon disruption, cyclones) threatening millions, while entrepreneurs must navigate between bootstrapping and VC-driven growth models. Both demand rethinking ingenuity's limits and sustainable business practices.
