## Core Argument
Platform redesign must align structure with institutional operation to surface coherence and reduce friction.

## Key Points
- Rebuild prioritised making system relationships visible rather than aesthetic changes alone.
- Resisted speed-driven engagement patterns; chose depth through quieter interface and reduced prompts.
- Iterative approach allowed structure to evolve alongside editorial thinking instead of locking design early.

## Open Question
How do you balance the need for platform evolution and reader engagement with the discipline required to maintain structural clarity and resist growth-at-all-costs pressures?

## India Angle
Indian media and publishing platforms often struggle with discoverability and reader retention; this approach—prioritising depth over volume—offers a countermodel to speed-driven engagement metrics that dominate Indian digital media.
