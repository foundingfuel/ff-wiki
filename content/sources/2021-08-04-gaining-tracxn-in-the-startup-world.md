## Core Argument
Generalist founders with diverse experiences build stronger global companies than specialists; India offers talent and market advantages.

## Key Points
- Generalists with exposure across industries outperform specialists as entrepreneurs; broad skills matter more than deep specialization.
- Building globally from India works when founders understand that enterprise customers value quality data over price and workflows.
- Founder's market now: entrepreneurs must research investors, build relationships early, and negotiate from position of strength.

## Open Question
If Indian talent and location enable global scaling, why do most successful Indian startups remain India-focused or serve Western markets rather than building truly diversified global customer bases?

## India Angle
Indian startup ecosystem advantage: talent pool in Bangalore for data analytics, ability to build global companies from India, and evolving VC market dynamics favoring founders over investors—creating opportunities for Indian entrepreneurs to scale globally.
