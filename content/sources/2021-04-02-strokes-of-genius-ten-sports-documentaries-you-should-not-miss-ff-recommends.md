## Core Argument
Sports documentaries transcend entertainment by capturing universal human moments that uplift spirit and build community across time.

## Key Points
- Documentaries enable fans to relive and recontextualize iconic sporting moments with emotional depth beyond live viewing.
- Great sports stories reveal character—talent often accompanied by arrogance, eccentricity, paranoia as explored through Fischer and Ali.
- Women's sports documentaries reintroduce audiences to cricket afresh, building connections with new athletes like Indian stars Mandhana and Yadav.

## Open Question
How do sports documentaries balance celebrating individual genius with exploring the systemic ecosystems (like Soviet chess cultivation) that produce champions?
