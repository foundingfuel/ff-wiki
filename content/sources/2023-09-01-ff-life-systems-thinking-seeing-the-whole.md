## Core Argument
Systems thinking transcends scientific reductionism, enabling better decisions and collaboration across disagreement.

## Key Points
- Scientific thinking alone is incomplete; systems thinking reveals interconnected feedback loops and root causes.
- Systems thinking enables working productively with people you disagree with by seeing holistic patterns.
- Learning organizations integrate personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, and systems thinking for sustainable change.

## Open Question
If systems thinking requires seeing the whole, how do practitioners navigate competing systemic priorities when solving for one creates unintended consequences elsewhere?

## India Angle
Systems thinking offers Indian leaders and policymakers a framework to navigate polarization and complex governance challenges—particularly relevant given India's diverse stakeholders and complicated institutional problems highlighted in 'Despite the State.'
