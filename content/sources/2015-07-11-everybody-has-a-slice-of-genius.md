## Core Argument
Innovation thrives through collective genius, not solo visionaries; leaders enable rather than direct.

## Key Points
- Breakthrough innovation requires diverse teams collaborating, not individual 'aha moments' or heroic leaders.
- Leaders set the stage for innovation by creating context; they don't perform or control the process themselves.
- Millennials and new generations want to co-create futures, not follow visionary leaders—requiring leadership model shifts.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance their own expertise and vision with the need to step back and enable others' contributions without abdicating responsibility for outcomes?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often struggle with hierarchical decision-making; this collective genius model challenges traditional command-and-control leadership prevalent in large Indian corporations and could unlock innovation in startups and established firms.
