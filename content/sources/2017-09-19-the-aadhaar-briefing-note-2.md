## Core Argument
Aadhaar's strength lies in user incentives, but systemic fraud risks and institutional opacity undermine trust-building.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar's structure incentivizes truthful enrollment but remains vulnerable to enrolment-process hacking and fake ID generation.
- UIDAI's defensive communication after security incidents damages trust; government response quality matters more than incident prevention.
- Privacy-security trade-off requires robust regulatory checks; competitive markets alone cannot ensure data protection without accountability.

## Open Question
Can Aadhaar serve both financial inclusion/tax collection and constitutional privacy rights without a Supreme Court-enforced privacy framework with real teeth?

## India Angle
Aadhaar's design incentivizes correct self-reporting but faces fraud vulnerabilities; India must balance digital identity scaling with privacy safeguards and institutional accountability, unlike Western precedents like Equifax.
