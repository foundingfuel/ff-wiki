## Core Argument
Flawed data conclusions stem from domain ignorance, confirmation bias, and correlation-causation confusion.

## Key Points
- Deep domain understanding precedes meaningful data analysis; programmatic ads require rethinking variables as real-time flows, not static optimisation.
- Sudden data spikes trigger confirmation bias; treat sharp improvements skeptically and disaggregate analysis to isolate actual drivers.
- Mitigate correlation-causation error by holding conclusions tentatively, waiting for pattern validation, and assigning devil's advocates to challenge reasoning.

## Open Question
How can operators build confidence in data insights without risking over-attachment to correlations they mistake for causation, especially under time pressure?
