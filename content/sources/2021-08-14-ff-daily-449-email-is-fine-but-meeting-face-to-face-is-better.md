## Core Argument
Face-to-face interaction unlocks hidden information and relationship dynamics that email cannot, critical for negotiation and leadership.

## Key Points
- Email limits negotiation effectiveness by giving counterparts time to self-protect and hiding nonverbal cues and tone.
- Startup CEOs must optimize time allocation away from low-value activities toward unblocking teams and scaling culture through documentation.
- Personal, direct interaction creates vulnerability and admission of limitations that email cannot facilitate.

## Open Question
How do distributed, remote-first teams build trust and uncover critical information without defaulting to inefficient in-person meetings?

## India Angle
Indian startups and leaders must balance remote work efficiency with face-to-face relationship-building for deals, negotiations, and culture-setting—especially critical post-pandemic when hybrid work is normalizing.
