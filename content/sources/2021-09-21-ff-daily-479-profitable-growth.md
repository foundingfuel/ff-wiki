## Core Argument
Profitable growth drives organizational energy and talent retention; stagnation breeds internal decay and attrition.

## Key Points
- Good growth must be profitable, organic, differentiated, and sustainable—not all revenue growth creates shareholder value.
- Growing organizations tap into employee psychological energy, enabling career building and retention; non-growing ones spawn infighting and departures.
- Growth psychology shapes culture: expansion creates opportunity and morale; contraction triggers negative dynamics and talent loss.

## Open Question
How do organizations pursue sustainable profitable growth when market conditions or competitive pressures force short-term cost-cutting that undermines the long-term psychological and cultural benefits Charan describes?

## India Angle
Indian companies must recognize that profitable growth directly impacts talent retention and organizational morale—critical for competing globally. Growth psychology is especially relevant for Indian startups and mid-market firms transitioning to scale.
