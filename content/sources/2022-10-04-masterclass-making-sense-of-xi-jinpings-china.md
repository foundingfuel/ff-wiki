## Core Argument
Xi Jinping's third term signals China's shift toward personalistic control, autarky, and assertive foreign policy with implications for India's geopolitical standing.

## Key Points
- Xi consolidating power through constitutional changes, personnel shifts, and new party chairman position despite age/term limits.
- China pivoting toward self-reliance in core sectors, hardened supply chains, and national security state model limiting innovation.
- Internal party dissent visible in military leadership meetings and selective omission of Xi references suggest potential resistance beneath surface unity.

## Open Question
How much real dissent exists within China's party-military hierarchy if decision-making is theoretically predetermined, and can genuine opposition emerge or will consensus always be manufactured?

## India Angle
India must understand Xi's consolidation of power and China's pivot toward self-reliance and hardened foreign policy, as it directly affects India's security, geopolitical positioning in the US-China rivalry, and economic competition in the region.
