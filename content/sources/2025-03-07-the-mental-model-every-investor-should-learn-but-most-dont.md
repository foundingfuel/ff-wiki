## Core Argument
Mental models of market cycles help investors manage psychology and avoid reactive decisions during inevitable fluctuations.

## Key Points
- Markets alternate between bull and bear cycles with local maxima/minima only knowable in hindsight, not real-time.
- Loss aversion bias makes downturns feel 3x worse than equivalent gains, triggering panic selling.
- Long-term market growth emerges from many cycles; individual episodes of drama are normal, not predictive.

## Open Question
If maxima and minima are unknowable in real-time, how can this mental model actually guide better investment decisions rather than just provide retrospective comfort?
