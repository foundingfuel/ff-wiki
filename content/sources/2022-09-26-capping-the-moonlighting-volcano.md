## Core Argument
Hardline anti-moonlighting stances risk eroding trust; companies must navigate generational shifts in work expectations.

## Key Points
- IT firms deploying surveillance, contract tightening, and hybrid mandates to curb moonlighting may trigger mass attrition
- Hypocrisy exists: Wipro acquired TopCoder legitimising competitor freelancing, then cracked down on its own employees
- Young workers reject office return after remote success; employers face trust erosion if using blunt control tools

## Open Question
How can employers protect legitimate business interests (IP, confidentiality, focus) without deploying invasive monitoring that destroys the psychological contract with workers?

## India Angle
India's IT/ITES sector—the backbone of tech exports—faces a generational clash between traditional employer control and employee autonomy. How Indian companies adapt will define competitiveness amid global talent wars.
