## Core Argument
Air India's privatization will attract multiple bidders driven by India's fast-growing aviation market and critical operational assets.

## Key Points
- India is world's third-largest domestic aviation market with Asia-Pacific driving 50% of new passenger growth through 2036.
- Airport slot scarcity at major hubs (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru) makes Air India's existing peak-hour slots strategically invaluable.
- Air India's engineering wing, pilot roster, and maintenance capabilities address acute industry-wide talent and infrastructure shortages.

## Open Question
Will foreign consortium bidders prioritize India's long-term aviation ecosystem development or extract short-term financial value from acquired assets?

## India Angle
Air India divestment represents a pivotal moment for India's aviation sector—strategic bidders will reshape domestic market competition, infrastructure utilization, and India's position in global aviation networks.
