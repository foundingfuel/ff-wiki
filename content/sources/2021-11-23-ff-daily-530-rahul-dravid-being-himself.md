## Core Argument
Authenticity and integrity in leadership—being yourself—matter more than performance alone.

## Key Points
- Dravid's willingness to acknowledge imperfections and step aside for others reflects mature leadership.
- Farm law withdrawal shows cracks in government omnipotence; dialogue and reform must follow.
- Mental health professionals face burnout crisis; systemic capacity strain reveals hidden human costs.

## Open Question
How do institutions balance performance pressures with the wellbeing and integrity of people within them—and does acknowledging systemic failure lead to constructive reform or sharper conflict?

## India Angle
Indian institutions and leaders—from cricket to agriculture to mental health—face pressures to perform while navigating complex systemic issues; authenticity and integrity in leadership matter especially in a democracy learning to hold power accountable.
