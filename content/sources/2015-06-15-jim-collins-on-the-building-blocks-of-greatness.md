## Core Argument
Enduring greatness stems from unchanging principles, not companies; principles transcend eras of chaos.

## Key Points
- Greatness is defined by sustained superior returns plus purpose beyond profit over 15+ years.
- Principles that enabled greatness across 1880s-1990s chaos remain valid despite modern disruption.
- Historical analysis reveals whether observed phenomena are principles or temporary blips in markets.

## Open Question
If principles are truly timeless across radical change (world wars, industrial revolutions, digital disruption), why have so many of Collins' original 'visionary companies' failed—and what does this reveal about the predictive power of his framework?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and business leaders can apply Collins' principles-based framework to build enduring enterprises despite India's volatile macro environment, regulatory shifts, and rapid technological disruption—testing whether timeless principles transcend India's unique business context.
