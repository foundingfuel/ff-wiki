## Core Argument
Character-revealing connection stories build rapport better than credential-based introductions in professional settings.

## Key Points
- Connection stories reveal character traits and values more effectively than resume-based introductions to differentiate oneself.
- Positioning yourself in a supporting role rather than the lead reduces perception of boasting while building credibility.
- Organizations embedding values through storytelling see deeper adoption than traditional workshops; stories enable lived culture.

## Open Question
How can leaders ensure connection stories authentically reflect organizational values without becoming performative tools that employees see through, especially in large hierarchical companies?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and professionals can use connection stories to build trust and rapport in hierarchical organizational cultures where credentials alone no longer differentiate; especially relevant as Indian businesses scale and require stronger internal cohesion around values beyond formal qualifications.
