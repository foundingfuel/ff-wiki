## Core Argument
Physical wellness infrastructure and ancient wisdom both critical for navigating unprecedented crises effectively.

## Key Points
- Poor posture from WFH causes compressed nerves, numbness, and chronic pain—preventable through ergonomic fixes.
- Proper workstation setup requires screen at eye level, back support, external keyboard, and standing desk rotation.
- Neck pillow support during sleep prevents nerve stress cascading into hand and arm pain—overlooked sleep factor.

## Open Question
How do resource-constrained Indian workers implement ergonomic best practices when equipment is expensive, space is limited, and lockdowns restrict access to purchases?

## India Angle
Indian remote workers navigating WFH without proper infrastructure—practical solutions for those in cramped spaces, limited equipment access during lockdowns, and cultural acceptance of makeshift setups using available household items.
