## Core Argument
Communitarian capitalism built on stakeholder trusteeship outperforms short-term shareholder primacy and heroic CEO mythology.

## Key Points
- Professional managers as trustees balancing customers, employees, shareholders, and communities create sustained value over time.
- Family businesses and startups naturally embody communitarian structures; scaling requires preserving shared purpose and stakeholder ownership.
- Cultural shift toward teamwork and institutional belonging matters more than legal structures for genuine stakeholder capitalism.

## Open Question
How do startups and scaling companies retain communitarian values and shared ownership once they require external capital, professional hierarchies, and shareholder governance?

## India Angle
Indian family businesses' communitarian model offers a competitive advantage over Western shareholder-primacy model; requires rethinking professional management succession and stakeholder governance as India scales enterprises globally.
