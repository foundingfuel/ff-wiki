## Core Argument
Leaders must shift from telling to asking, enabling many-to-many conversations over hierarchical one-to-many directives.

## Key Points
- Facilitation transforms strategy from leader prerogative into collaborative co-creation across all organizational levels.
- New generation of Indian workers demands self-expression and voice; ignoring this causes disillusionment and attrition.
- Good facilitators ask questions, create space, and reject consultant/trainer roles that prescribe solutions.

## Open Question
How can facilitation prevent groupthink and dominance by loudest voices while ensuring meaningful dialogue at scale in argumentative cultures like India?

## India Angle
India faces urgent generational gap as young, confident workforce demands voice and co-creation, not top-down command. Facilitation skills critical for Indian leaders to retain talent and remain relevant.
