## Core Argument
Indians adapting to post-pandemic mobility through cycling and shared micro-mobility, reflecting permanent lifestyle shifts.

## Key Points
- Unprecedented bicycle demand in India as commuters avoid public transport and ride-hailing costs amid pandemic concerns.
- Bike-sharing apps offering long-term rentals and electric options enabling low-risk experimentation before purchase commitment.
- Consumer behaviour shifting toward keeping same vehicle throughout trips to minimize hygiene risks and friction from frequent changes.

## Open Question
Will pandemic-driven cycling adoption sustain as a permanent shift in Indian urban mobility, or revert once perceived health risks decline?

## India Angle
Indian urban mobility post-pandemic, including challenges with ride-hailing affordability, public transport reluctance, supply chain disruptions from China tensions, and adaptation of bike-sharing platforms like Yulu for safer commuting in cramped urban environments.
