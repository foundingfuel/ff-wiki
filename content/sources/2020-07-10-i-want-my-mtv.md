## Core Argument
Crisis drives consumers toward trusted legacy brands, potentially reversing years of premiumization and small-batch market gains.

## Key Points
- Consistency and repeatable experience trump discovery during uncertainty; large brands win on predictability.
- Nostalgia activates childhood memories as stress relief; familiar foods/brands provide psychological comfort in lockdown.
- Newly acquired premium habits are fragile; reverting to legacy brands during crisis may create permanent behavioural shifts.

## Open Question
Will this shift back to legacy brands persist post-pandemic, or is it a temporary psychological response to acute crisis uncertainty?

## India Angle
Indian consumers (like those buying Britannia, Haldiram's, Thums Up) are reverting to trusted legacy brands during crises; Doordarshan-Amul case shows how nostalgia-driven marketing works in India's mass media context.
