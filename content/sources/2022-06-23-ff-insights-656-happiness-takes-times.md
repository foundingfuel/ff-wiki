## Core Argument
Happiness and meaningful change require patience; instant gratification erodes what matters most.

## Key Points
- All happiness-producing processes—learning, relationships, growth—take time, not moments of sudden good fortune.
- Bollywood elites trapped in South Mumbai bubble make films disconnected from India's reality and diverse audiences.
- Grounded, field-based approaches to expertise (Gadgil's ecology) serve societies better than detached insider thinking.

## Open Question
If systems built on fame, wealth and appearance inevitably discourage us, how do individuals sustain meaning without rejecting society's structures entirely?

## India Angle
Indian film industry has lost connection with India's diverse audiences by catering to elite narratives; parallel example of Madhav Gadgil shows how grounded, field-based thinking serves India's ecological future better than disconnected expertise.
