## Core Argument
India's housing crisis requires listening to small entrepreneurs whose practical solutions are ignored by government institutions.

## Key Points
- Small builders deliver 90% of affordable housing but are excluded from policy conversations with government.
- Better dialogue between government, entrepreneurs, and institutions solves complex challenges in mission mode.
- Social media echo chambers prevent thoughtful deliberation on difficult issues of national importance.

## Open Question
How can governments systematically hear from ground-level entrepreneurs when institutional structures and social media fragmentation both work against genuine dialogue?

## India Angle
India's affordable housing crisis remains unsolved because policymakers exclude small entrepreneurs who deliver 90% of supply, choosing instead to engage large developers with no interest in Rs 5-20 lakh segment. Dialogue and institutional listening are critical gaps.
