## Core Argument
Hope, catalyzed by one voice, transforms collective resilience and enables survival through adversity.

## Key Points
- Admiral McRaven's mudflat anecdote shows singing unified a suffering team by shifting psychological state.
- One person—Washington, Lincoln, King, Mandela, Malala—can shift hope and change the world.
- India faces 2021 challenges (vaccine distribution, economic recovery, geopolitics) requiring hope-driven leadership and resilience.

## Open Question
How do leaders authentically generate hope in systemic crises without dismissing real material hardship or becoming performative?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations can harness hope and collective resilience as a counterweight to systemic challenges—vaccine distribution inequity, economic inequality, geopolitical volatility—requiring cultural shifts beyond Western management models.
