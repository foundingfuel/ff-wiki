## Core Argument
Leaders create culture; the same person thrives or merely survives based on environment and management approach.

## Key Points
- Environment and leadership culture determine employee engagement more than individual capability or job role.
- Transparency and accountability (monthly reporting) aren't short-termism; guidance that constrains strategy is.
- Three-year vision with ranges allows strategic flexibility while maintaining investor trust and long-term focus.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance genuine care for people with accountability structures without reverting to surveillance-based management?

## India Angle
Indian companies often struggle with command-and-control management; Four Seasons model shows how care-based leadership drives engagement—critical for India's talent retention challenges.
