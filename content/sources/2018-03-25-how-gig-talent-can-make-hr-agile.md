## Core Argument
Gig talent enables HR functions to scale expertise rapidly while maintaining organizational agility.

## Key Points
- Gig workers provide deep expertise on-demand, not cost reduction—often paid equal to or above employees.
- Hiring gig talent globally reduces expensive travel-based deployment and delivers capability at point of need.
- HR itself should adopt 20-40% gig headcount to adapt quickly to transformation, M&A, and strategic projects.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance the flexibility and expertise benefits of gig talent with the cultural cohesion and institutional knowledge that permanent employees provide?

## India Angle
India's large population and skills gap make gig talent a practical solution to unemployment and underemployment. Vocational education expansion will increase gig workforce availability for Indian businesses.
