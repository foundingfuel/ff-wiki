## Core Argument
System redesign, not component swapping, makes emerging technology viable at scale.

## Key Points
- Thandasherry didn't retrofit solar onto existing ferries; he redesigned entire system—halving weight and power needs.
- Kerala SWTD's primary motivation was cost reduction, not environmental impact—pragmatism drives adoption.
- First-year success: 365,000 passengers, 34,800L diesel saved, operational break-even achieved.

## Open Question
If solar ferries become cheaper, will waterway infrastructure develop to match—creating a virtuous cycle like roads and cars, or remain underinvested?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneur demonstrates how deep tech innovation can solve local infrastructure challenges (Kerala's water transport) while building commercially viable models—shows India doesn't need to import solutions but can develop them indigenously with domain expertise.
