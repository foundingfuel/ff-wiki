## Core Argument
Governments weaponise regulation against foreign tech companies; TikTok echoes IBM's 1977 exit from India.

## Key Points
- George Fernandes forced IBM and Coca-Cola out in 1977 citing unfair practices and nationalist demands under FERA legislation.
- Trump threatened TikTok ban citing data security and censorship concerns, demanding American ownership as precedent for market access.
- Pattern shows internet fragmenting into geopolitical blocs as nations prioritise sovereignty over cross-border tech integration.

## Open Question
How can governments legitimately balance data security/sovereignty concerns against innovation loss and consumer harm from forced divestitures or bans?

## India Angle
India's 1977 precedent of ejecting IBM and Coca-Cola under nationalist pressure mirrors current US-China tech tensions; India's 2020 TikTok ban shows how countries use regulation to assert sovereignty over foreign platforms.
