## Core Argument
Digital-age reputation is co-created, not controlled; leaders must listen more than broadcast.

## Key Points
- Early warning signals from frontline employees and customers prevent crises before CEO escalation.
- Human touch and offline conversation de-escalate public grievances faster than standardized bot responses.
- Circular, reciprocal dialogue replaces hierarchical messaging; authenticity shown through action, not communication.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the need for fast, decisive crisis response with the principle of listening to multiple perspectives without ceding narrative control to misinformation?

## India Angle
Indian corporations facing reputational crises on social media (L&T cited) must adopt listening-first culture and fast offline engagement rather than traditional top-down communication. Generational gap between digital-native employees/consumers and legacy leadership structures is acute in India's rapidly evolving companies.
