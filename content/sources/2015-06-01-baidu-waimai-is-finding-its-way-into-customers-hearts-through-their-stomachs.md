## Core Argument
Food delivery apps exploit restaurant-consumer asymmetry by solving convenience and discovery simultaneously.

## Key Points
- Baidu Waimai bridges gap between restaurants lacking delivery infrastructure and consumers unwilling to travel
- Subsidies, gamified discounts, and payment integrations drive user acquisition and repeat behaviour
- Real-time tracking and ratings transform delivery services into Yelp-like discovery platforms, not just logistics

## Open Question
How sustainable is the model when margin-destroying subsidies and discounts drive growth, and when customer loyalty appears fragile across competing platforms?

## India Angle
Indian startups in food delivery (Swiggy, Zomato) can learn from Baidu Waimai's playbook: subsidies to acquire users, payment integrations, restaurant arbitrage, and real-time tracking. The O2O model's success in China shows potential for Indian metros where delivery distances and inconvenience are similar pain points.
