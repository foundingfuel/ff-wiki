## Core Argument
Organizations must reimagine work post-pandemic; 'No' to old norms enables hybrid model innovation.

## Key Points
- 65% of remote workers prefer hybrid models; preferences vary significantly by age and geography.
- Organizations must learn to say 'No' to established working norms to successfully transform work culture.
- India's response to hybrid work likely differs from US due to infrastructure, hierarchy, and talent dynamics.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance employee autonomy and preference for flexible work with maintaining organizational culture, collaboration, and equitable access across age groups and geographies?

## India Angle
Indian companies face unique hybrid work challenges: smaller metros lack robust work-from-home infrastructure, hierarchical cultures resist remote work, talent concentration in metros creates real estate dependencies. A Zoom-SurveyMonkey US poll raises questions about what Indian worker preferences actually are—likely very different given demographic, infrastructure, and organizational culture differences.
