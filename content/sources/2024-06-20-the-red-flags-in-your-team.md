## Core Argument
Character assessment during hiring prevents costlier organizational damage than any talent acquisition benefit.

## Key Points
- Red flags—self-serving, impulsive individuals—rarely change and contaminate organizational culture despite sensitivity training.
- Leaders must conduct rigorous character vetting upfront and terminate disruptive individuals decisively to protect team morale.
- Strong performance cultures prioritize character fit, clear expectations, and honest conversations over raw talent accumulation.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance the commercial upside of hiring high-performing 'rainmakers' with the invisible but cumulative damage red flags inflict on culture and retention?

## India Angle
Indian organizations often prioritize talent and credentials over character assessment; family-run businesses and startups particularly struggle with accountability when hiring high performers with behavioral red flags, affecting team morale and long-term culture.
