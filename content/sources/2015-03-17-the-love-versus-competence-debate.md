## Core Argument
Success requires both passion and competence; family business succession demands questioning inherited assumptions.

## Key Points
- Love without competence (high love, low competence) requires mentoring in business fundamentals before scaling.
- Competence without love (high competence, low competence) leads to boredom and eventual business deterioration.
- Exit decisions carry unintended social consequences beyond financial planning—identity, standing, and family dynamics matter.

## Open Question
How does a founder distinguish between protecting their legacy and enabling a child's authentic path when both choices carry profound personal and social costs?

## India Angle
Directly addresses Indian family business succession—a critical governance challenge where emotion, tradition, and competence often clash. Relevant to India's large family business ecosystem and inheritance patterns.
