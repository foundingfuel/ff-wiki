## Core Argument
Leaders must move from unconscious to analytical empathy using theory and frameworks for genuine sensemaking.

## Key Points
- Three empathy levels: unconscious alignment, noticing discomfort, analytical empathy grounded in theory and context.
- Analytical empathy requires systematic frameworks, evidence assembly, and theoretical grounding—not mere observation or data.
- Indian VC ecosystem shows bias in founder selection; analytical empathy could challenge heuristics like IIT-ranking filters.

## Open Question
How do organizations scale analytical empathy as a decision-making practice when unconscious empathy and intuition often feel faster and sufficient?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organizations often operate at basic empathy levels; adopting Madsbjerg's framework could transform how Indian founders build inclusive cultures and how investors move beyond IIT-bias shortcuts to truly understand founders and markets.
