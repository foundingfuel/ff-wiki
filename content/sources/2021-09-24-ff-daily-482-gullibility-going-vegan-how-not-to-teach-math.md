## Core Argument
Self-deception and confirmation bias plague decision-making; critical thinking demands questioning comfortable narratives.

## Key Points
- Executives ignore uncomfortable truths when data contradicts their preferred narratives, as seen in Enron, Coca-Cola, and Detroit automakers.
- Veganism exemplifies deliberative ethical inquiry—a reasoned choice against cognitive dissonance, not mere trendy activism.
- Speed-based math testing stresses students and contradicts how real mathematics works: deep, iterative problem-solving over decades.

## Open Question
How do individuals balance societal expectations and personal convenience with ethical decision-making when the truth demands uncomfortable choices?

## India Angle
Indian education system heavily emphasizes speed and rote learning; rethinking pedagogy and food ethics reflects deeper cultural shifts in how Indians evaluate personal choices against societal norms and institutional pressure.
