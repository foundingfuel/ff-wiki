## Core Argument
Storytelling with character-driven narratives is the foundational skill for effective public speaking in audio-first platforms.

## Key Points
- Stories with protagonists, tension, and resolution engage audiences emotionally better than complex explanations.
- Key storytelling elements: relatable character, built tension, appropriate detail level, satisfying resolution.
- Audio platforms require mastery of narrative structure to hold attention without visual aids.

## Open Question
If everyone adopts the same storytelling formula, does it become formulaic and lose its power to differentiate speakers and ideas?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and entrepreneurs increasingly use platforms like Clubhouse and audio-first media; mastering public speaking and storytelling is critical for Indian founders seeking visibility and influence in global and domestic markets.
