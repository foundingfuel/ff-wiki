## Core Argument
Indian fintech must evolve from tech-first scaling to human-centric partnerships and regulatory collaboration.

## Key Points
- Fintech optimism replaced by market reality: VC funding down 70% in H1 2023, only one unicorn profitable
- Technology alone insufficient; startups pivoting to SaaS, partnerships show algorithms cannot capture human complexity
- Future shaped by: incumbent collaboration, moving beyond tech, and regulatory relationships—not disruption narratives

## Open Question
How can fintech achieve financial inclusion if profitable models require moving away from consumer lending and into B2B SaaS—the original problem they aimed to solve?

## India Angle
Indian fintech has shifted from unbridled growth optimism to strategic introspection; success now depends on partnerships with incumbents, regulatory navigation, and understanding human behaviour beyond algorithms—critical for achieving actual financial inclusion at scale.
