## Core Argument
Excellence without adaptation becomes fragility when external constraints collide with hardened internal logic.

## Key Points
- IndiGo's crisis stemmed from institutional muscle memory treating elasticity as infinite rather than adaptive.
- Regulatory shift in pilot fatigue limits exposed an optimisation philosophy that had never truly been stress-tested.
- Dominant infrastructure players' internal miscalculations cascade systemically, affecting entire ecosystems, not just shareholders.

## Open Question
How can organisations maintain the discipline that creates excellence while remaining adaptive to hard external constraints they cannot engineer away?

## India Angle
IndiGo's crisis exposes how India's dominant infrastructure players can fail not through incompetence but through excellence-gone-rigid, with systemic implications for aviation, regulation enforcement, and how Indian organisations treat human capacity under pressure.
