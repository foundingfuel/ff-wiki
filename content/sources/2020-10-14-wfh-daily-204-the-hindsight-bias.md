## Core Argument
Hindsight bias makes us overestimate our predictive ability, causing dangerous overconfidence in decisions.

## Key Points
- Hindsight bias creates false clarity about past events, making outcomes seem inevitable when they weren't.
- Awareness alone doesn't prevent hindsight bias; journaling predictions and comparing against outcomes is necessary.
- Reading historical primary sources reveals world's true unpredictability, countering retrospective textbook narratives.

## Open Question
How can decision-makers confidently act while genuinely accepting their poor forecasting abilities and the world's inherent unpredictability?

## India Angle
Relevant for Indian business leaders and professionals who need to avoid overconfidence bias in decision-making, particularly in a fast-changing startup and corporate environment where hindsight bias can distort strategic retrospectives.
