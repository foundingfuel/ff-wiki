## Core Argument
Gandhi's salt march demonstrates five replicable publicity tactics: universal appeal, low barriers to participation, dramatic narrative structure, climactic moments, and leveraging influential mediators.

## Key Points
- Choose campaigns with universal resonance across demographics and geographies to maximize participation.
- Reduce friction for audience participation by offering multiple entry points and varying commitment levels.
- Script campaigns as multi-act dramas with suspense and built-in narrative arcs to sustain media engagement.

## Open Question
How do entrepreneurs balance authentic mission-driven work with calculated dramatic storytelling without manipulating audiences or sacrificing credibility?

## India Angle
Demonstrates how Indian entrepreneurs can leverage mass mobilization, simplicity, and narrative drama to create disproportionate impact—a lesson from India's independence movement applicable to modern startup communications and social movements.
