## Core Argument
Embedding behavioural science principles into business strategy drives customer behaviour change at near-zero cost.

## Key Points
- Loss aversion, scarcity, and social proof make customers act irrationally in predictable ways.
- Companies like Zara, Amazon Prime, and OnePlus weaponize psychological triggers to increase spending and loyalty.
- Hot buttons (scarcity language) and price anchoring bypass rational decision-making to drive immediate purchases.

## Open Question
If businesses systematically exploit cognitive biases to make customers behave irrationally, what are the ethical boundaries—or should profit motive override consumer autonomy?

## India Angle
Indian businesses (Bagpiper, OnePlus, e-commerce players) can leverage behavioural science principles to drive customer acquisition and spending without heavy marketing costs—critical for price-sensitive Indian markets.
