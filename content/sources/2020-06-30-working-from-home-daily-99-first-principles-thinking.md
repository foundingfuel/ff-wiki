## Core Argument
Question inherited systems and processes; redesign education and institutions from first principles, not past practices.

## Key Points
- De Mello's ashram cat parable reveals how systems persist without reason; we rationalize obsolete practices over time
- MBA curricula must teach 'learning how to learn' and mental model creation as business rules now change every 5 years, not 20
- PV Narasimha Rao exemplifies first-principles thinking: appearing harmless while secretly planning radical economic reinvention, rewarding capability over loyalty

## Open Question
How do you implement radical first-principles redesign in institutions with entrenched stakeholders who benefit from status quo?

## India Angle
Indian MBA programmes and business schools need to rethink curriculum from first principles—moving away from outdated systems toward teaching learning agility, critical thinking, and adaptability for a rapidly changing economy. Also relevant: how Indian leaders navigate reinvention despite institutional resistance.
