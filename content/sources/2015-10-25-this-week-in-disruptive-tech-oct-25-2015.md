## Core Argument
Exponential technologies like 3D printing, AI, and drones are reshaping industries but follow S-curve patterns, not infinite growth.

## Key Points
- We underestimate long-term tech impact while overestimating short-term; early adopter disappointment masks future potential.
- Networked intelligence—where systems learn collectively—promises significant leaps but will eventually plateau per S-curve dynamics.
- Sharing economy disrupts traditional employment, creating tension between flexibility/opportunity and loss of benefits/job security.

## Open Question
If AI and automation continuously improve through network learning, what prevents concentration of power and wealth among platform owners controlling these networks?

## India Angle
India's infrastructure challenges (roads, bridges, wildlife conservation) and emerging gig economy adoption present opportunities for drone/AI applications; regulatory frameworks for sharing economy still evolving.
