## Core Argument
Professional entrepreneurs—who own problems, stay alert, and ignore hierarchy—are what boards demand for CEO roles.

## Key Points
- Take ownership of outcomes regardless of title; think and act like you own the business.
- Stay externally aware—constantly observe competition, customers, trends; cultivate productive unrest to avoid inertia.
- Cut through hierarchy, empower teams as force multipliers, and focus obsessively on outcomes over activity.

## Open Question
How do you maintain ownership mentality and external awareness in large bureaucratic organizations without burning out or becoming isolated from formal power structures?

## India Angle
Indian companies competing in complex, multi-front markets need entrepreneurial leaders; examples like ICICI, Mahindra, and Tata show how empowering people with entrepreneurial mindset transforms organizations at scale.
