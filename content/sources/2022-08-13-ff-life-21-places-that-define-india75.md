## Core Argument
India's transformation was built by overlooked institutions and people whose stories deserve wider recognition.

## Key Points
- Akashvani Bhavan in Kolkata served as birthplace of Bengal's cultural renaissance, hosting legendary artists and preserving rare voice archives.
- Bahadurshah Zafar Marg in Delhi became India's Fleet Street, where journalists documented democracy's chaos and held power accountable during Emergency.
- The project sought untold stories beyond well-documented political events—including science institutions, art, theatre, and new cultural sensibilities.

## Open Question
How do we systematically uncover and preserve the stories of transformative but lesser-known institutions before they fade from collective memory?

## India Angle
Celebrates lesser-known Indian institutions and places that shaped modern India's cultural, media, and institutional identity—specifically the role of regional centers in nation-building.
