## Core Argument
India-China cooperation could reshape global order, but historical fractures and resource pessimism limit potential.

## Key Points
- Mao warned that 20-year India-China alignment would be unstoppable; 1962 war shattered this vision permanently.
- Resource scarcity predictions fail because breakthrough technologies and new reserves emerge unpredictably and continuously.
- Cultural products like Pasoori demonstrate soft power and people-to-people bridges where diplomacy alone struggles.

## Open Question
Why does India struggle to leverage cultural and soft power tools for geopolitical alignment when hard power solutions have repeatedly failed?

## India Angle
India-China relations remain fractured since 1962; missed opportunity for regional cooperation. India faces resource constraints and innovation challenges requiring strategic foresight. Cultural bridges like Pasoori show soft power potential between South Asian neighbors.
