## Core Argument
Air travel in 2023 shaped by technology adoption and infrastructure upgrades, but user experience lags behind announcements.

## Key Points
- Free wi-fi on flights and digital check-in systems promise convenience but create trade-offs (always-on work culture).
- DigiYatra facial recognition reduces airport friction from 45+ minutes to 10-15 minutes, but UI issues limit adoption.
- Infrastructure and technology improvements driven by post-pandemic traffic rebound and airline profitability pressures.

## Open Question
Digital convenience features enable constant work connectivity (free wi-fi), potentially eroding the psychological relief air travel once provided—raising the question of whether faster, smoother travel serves passenger wellbeing or only business productivity.

## India Angle
DigiYatra represents India's push toward digital-first infrastructure at scale; adoption challenges reveal gaps between policy intent and user experience in Indian airports, with implications for how India builds tech-enabled citizen services.
