## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs should outsource mundane tasks cheaply to focus on high-value work that requires personal intervention.

## Key Points
- Startup founders must account for every rupee spent; low-cost outsourcing via Fiverr makes financial sense.
- Delegating routine work frees founding teams to focus on strategy, product, and higher-order decisions.
- Digital platforms enable lean organizations where 3 people can operate like much larger teams.

## Open Question
If founders outsource all routine work, how do they develop the operational discipline and attention to detail needed to scale sustainably?

## India Angle
For Indian startups with limited budgets, outsourcing via platforms like Fiverr enables lean operations and allows founders to focus on strategy rather than administrative overhead—critical for competing with resource-constrained teams.
