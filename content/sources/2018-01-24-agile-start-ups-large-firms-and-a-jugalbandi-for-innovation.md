## Core Argument
Corporations, startups, and academia must collaborate through 'jugalbandi' to unlock India's innovation potential.

## Key Points
- Established firms need startup agility; startups need corporate resources and distribution channels
- Academia bridges theory and practice, enabling knowledge transfer across the ecosystem
- Open innovation models create interdependencies that strengthen India's collective competitive advantage

## Open Question
How can large corporations maintain focus on core business while genuinely integrating startup innovation without bureaucratic friction?

## India Angle
How Indian large corporations can partner with startups and academia to build a sustainable innovation ecosystem, addressing India's need for homegrown innovation capacity
