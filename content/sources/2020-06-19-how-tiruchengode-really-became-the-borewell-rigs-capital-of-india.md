## Core Argument
Industrial clusters emerge from path-dependent historical chains, not deliberate planning or single catalytic problems.

## Key Points
- Tiruchengode's borewell rig dominance traces to ox-cart repair skills → truck bodybuilding → machinery adaptation for drilling.
- Similar water crises elsewhere didn't create clusters; Tiruchengode had accumulated workshop capacity and artisanal expertise.
- Historical proximity to textile trade (Erode, Salem) seeded carpentry/blacksmith culture essential for later industries.

## Open Question
If clusters are path-dependent on historical accident, how can policymakers intentionally create new industrial ecosystems without waiting centuries for organic evolution?
