## Core Argument
Growth mindset and social proximity drive organizational culture and human potential development.

## Key Points
- With growth mindset and right teaching, people exceed perceived potential limits
- Indian MBA education shifting from Western-centric to India-contextual while maintaining global methodology
- Physical distancing doesn't require social distancing; proximity nurtures culture and shared purpose

## Open Question
How can organizations maintain social proximity and culture cultivation when remote work becomes permanent, especially in India's distributed, hierarchical organizational structures?

## India Angle
Indian companies and MBA programs must balance global methodologies with local applicability; Indian organizations need to rethink workplace culture post-pandemic while maintaining connection and proximity for value cultivation.
