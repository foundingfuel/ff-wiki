## Core Argument
Complex problem-solving requires 'stretch collaboration' with ideological opponents, not controlled consensus.

## Key Points
- Conventional collaboration assumes control and harmony; stretch collaboration embraces discord and cocreation.
- WFH erodes social bonds; intentional relationship-building is productivity and commitment multiplier.
- Economic paradigm shift demands more local, just, resilient systems—requiring new working relationships.

## Open Question
How do you build trust-based collaboration with people you fundamentally disagree with or distrust without compromising on core principles?

## India Angle
Indian organizations moving to WFH must navigate deeply hierarchical cultures while practicing 'stretch collaboration'—working with colleagues across caste, class, and ideological divides. The economic paradigm shift Maira describes (local, just, resilient) directly challenges India's liberalization model, requiring new working relationships.
