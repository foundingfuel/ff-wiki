## Core Argument
Dalio's principles-based leadership is outdated; AI and algorithms now outperform human-driven hedge fund management.

## Key Points
- Dalio's 'radical transparency' culture works only for those who tolerate brutal feedback; most cannot.
- AI and machine learning increasingly outperform human managers in complex financial instruments, yet Dalio hasn't adapted.
- Success from Industrial Revolution principles doesn't guarantee relevance in algorithmic finance era.

## Open Question
If Dalio's principles are universal and time-tested, why are they failing to acknowledge the superiority of algorithmic trading over human judgment?

## India Angle
Hedge funds are nascent in India with policymakers still grappling with their nature; Dalio's influence on global financial decisions impacts Indian markets and investors despite limited local understanding.
