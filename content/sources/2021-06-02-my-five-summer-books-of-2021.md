## Core Argument
Pandemic-era leaders need courage, adaptive leadership, and behavior-change tactics to navigate relentless change.

## Key Points
- Courage isn't innate—it stems from values, confidence, humility, and institutional commitment, requiring deliberate practice.
- Sustainable behavior change requires reducing barriers, enhancing willpower, and making goals enjoyable rather than drudgery.
- Adaptive leadership means leading across networks without formal authority, embracing productive distress, and experimental approaches.

## Open Question
How do individuals balance personal identity and meaning-making through work while preparing for career transitions where work may no longer define them?

## India Angle
Indian professionals navigating post-pandemic workplace transitions need frameworks for courage, adaptive leadership, and behavior change—especially in hierarchical organizations where speaking up remains challenging.
