## Core Argument
Reframing—adopting new mental models—is critical to navigating complexity and change in technology, economics, and information ecosystems.

## Key Points
- Computers excel at memory/reasoning; humans must develop superior framing—ability to create mental models for new situations.
- Economics adopted physics-based equilibrium frame; should shift to biology-based evolution frame for complex modern economies.
- Social media exploits victimhood narratives and misinformation; without economic sustainability, media will become political propaganda.

## Open Question
How do we develop capacity for reframing at scale across institutions and populations when most people are comfortable with existing mental models and resistant to cognitive change?

## India Angle
Indian policymakers and media must adopt new mental models to address complex challenges like misinformation, political polarization, and economic regulation—moving beyond inherited frameworks that no longer fit modern realities.
