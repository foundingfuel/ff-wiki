## Core Argument
Peak performance is fundamentally a mental battle; physical limits are secondary to psychological mastery.

## Key Points
- Elite athletes across extreme conditions consistently cite mental discipline as the decisive factor in success.
- High performers share common psychological frameworks despite vastly different domains and competitive contexts.
- Understanding what drives extreme performers offers transferable lessons for organizational and personal excellence.

## Open Question
If all battles are fought in the head, what specific mental techniques or training frameworks actually work, and how can they be systematically taught to non-athletes?

## India Angle
Indian high performers and organizations can learn mental resilience frameworks from extreme athletes competing in Himalayan conditions; applicable to building world-class Indian talent in competitive fields.
