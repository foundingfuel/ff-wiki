## Core Argument
Books shape how we navigate change; seven selections reflect mid-year recalibration on resilience, trust, and India's evolution.

## Key Points
- Ganbatte exemplifies perseverance through adversity; sailors don't freeze but actively row forward.
- Office Shock reveals trust as emotional default in remote work; mistrust spreads faster than rational discourse.
- Biographies of Vajpayee and Ambedkar illuminate India's ideological tensions and nation-building contradictions.

## Open Question
How do we build trust and cross-generational collaboration in an age when digital media amplifies mistrust as the default emotion?
