## Core Argument
Performance emerges from respecting human ability, not appraising it; motivation requires trust in difficult times.

## Key Points
- Performance is the multiplying force when financial and human capital converge effectively.
- Appraisals are subjective judgements; management focuses on improving performance through respect and trust.
- Motivation through commitment, not compliance, separates mechanical management from organizations that unlock human potential.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain accountability and measurable standards while shifting from appraisal-driven to trust-based performance management?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises often rely heavily on subjective appraisal systems; this framework helps leaders build respect-based management cultures that treat human capital with the dignity required for sustainable growth in competitive markets.
