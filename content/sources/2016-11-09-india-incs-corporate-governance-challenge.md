## Core Argument
Tata-Mistry battle reveals systemic corporate governance weaknesses requiring boardroom culture and transparency reform.

## Key Points
- Independent directors' role and accountability in protecting stakeholder interests remains unclear in Indian boards
- Transparency and fairness standards in boardroom decision-making need structural strengthening across India Inc
- Governance failures at marquee companies signal need for wider systemic debate and best-practice evolution

## Open Question
How can Indian boards balance founder/promoter control with genuine independent director authority without paralyzing decision-making?

## India Angle
Tata-Mistry dispute exposes critical gaps in Indian boardroom practices—independent director efficacy, stakeholder protection, and transparency standards that affect how India Inc operates and attracts global capital.
