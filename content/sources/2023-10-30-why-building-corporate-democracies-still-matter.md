## Core Argument
Employee-first cultures create sustainable competitive advantage where products commoditize and innovation gets copied quickly.

## Key Points
- Real value creation happens at customer-employee intersection, not through product innovation alone
- Decentralized leadership empowering frontline employees enables faster decision-making and relationship-building
- Treating employees as self-directed adults replaces carrot-and-stick management with intrinsic motivation

## Open Question
If employee-first culture is so effective, why did HCL dilute it after the HP joint venture, and how do firms sustain this approach amid pressure for standardization and scale?

## India Angle
Indian companies like HCL and Tata Motors demonstrate that employee-first culture can drive breakthrough results; challenges existing Drucker/Gandhi customer-centric orthodoxy deeply embedded in Indian business schools and PSU thinking.
