## Core Argument
Diversifying identity beyond work creates resilience and paradoxically improves performance everywhere.

## Key Points
- Identity must be enacted through action, not just belief—identification without behaviour provides no protection.
- Multiple identities cross-fertilise skills; investment in diverse roles strengthens each one simultaneously.
- Financial anxiety about survival drives work obsession; reexamining lifestyle needs is necessary precondition for identity diversification.

## Open Question
How do those in genuine financial precarity (common in India) actually reduce lifestyle without sacrificing dignity or safety nets for dependents?

## India Angle
Indian professionals, especially in transition or uncertain employment, need to build identity resilience beyond work—critical given India's economic volatility and changing job market dynamics.
