## Core Argument
Balance analytical and empathetic neural networks to become effective leaders in volatile times.

## Key Points
- Two brain networks (analytic and empathetic) suppress each other; awareness and deliberate practice enable balance.
- Pandemic enabled rekindling of old relationships; selective friendships naturally evolve without formal breakups.
- India's community-based healthcare structures can strengthen pandemic response if empowered by government.

## Open Question
How can managers rapidly develop empathetic capacity when organizational structures and incentives historically rewarded analytical thinking?

## India Angle
Indian managers and professionals need to balance analytical and empathetic intelligence in a pandemic-affected workplace; Indian healthcare system's reliance on community-based organizations mirrors global LMIC strategy; Indian business education must evolve to address hybrid work realities.
