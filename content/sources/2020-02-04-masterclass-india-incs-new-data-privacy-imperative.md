## Core Argument
Data protection law enables safe data sharing, unlocking India's development potential while requiring businesses to restructure processes, governance, and IT systems.

## Key Points
- Regulation creates customer confidence and unlocks digital economy benefits, including financial inclusion for underbanked populations.
- Consent framework requires businesses to redesign processes: consent recording, data localization, governance layers, and vendor management.
- India's approach differs from GDPR—solving for scale, geographical complexity, cost efficiency, and 'how' over 'what'.

## Open Question
How can businesses implement sophisticated data governance and consent frameworks without prohibitive compliance costs, especially for smaller firms operating across India's geographical complexity?

## India Angle
India's Data Protection Bill will fundamentally reshape how Indian businesses—from banks to startups—organize data operations, governance structures, and compliance frameworks. It positions India to build a data-driven economy with customer trust, unlock financial inclusion, and compete globally while balancing innovation with protection.
