## Core Argument
India's growth story depends on understanding behavioural economics, debt-fuelled optimism, and institutional credibility amid policy debates.

## Key Points
- Amazon's omnichannel retail entry signals serious India commitment and competitive advantage over Chinese rivals
- Urban youth debt adoption reflects optimism about future and financial system maturity, not desperation
- India ranks 103rd in human capital development, below regional peers, requiring urgent policy attention

## Open Question
How can policymakers harness young Indians' optimism and debt-fuelled consumption while avoiding systemic financial risks and addressing the human capital gap?

## India Angle
India's economic slowdown, demographic dividend potential, Aadhaar implementation, and human capital development lag relative to peers—all critical for sustained growth and policy effectiveness.
