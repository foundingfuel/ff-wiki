## Core Argument
Simple ergonomic fixes and sustained behaviour change can reverse pandemic-driven posture deterioration across age groups.

## Key Points
- Monitor at eye level, inclined footrest, and proper chair are highest-impact, lowest-effort interventions for posture.
- Pandemic accelerated posture problems via screen time, reduced movement, poor home workstations, especially in 20-40 age group.
- Women, elderly, and schoolchildren most affected; requires sustained behaviour change beyond one-time ergonomic purchases.

## Open Question
How do low-cost ergonomic fixes sustain long-term behaviour change when the article suggests permanent improvement requires ongoing discipline most people lack?

## India Angle
Indian women face disproportionate posture challenges due to household work burden; pandemic worsened WFH ergonomics across age groups; Indian cooking demands long standing hours causing specific knee/back pain patterns.
