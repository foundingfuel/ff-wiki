## Core Argument
Effective sensemaking requires pausing emotional reactions, demanding data transparency, and recognizing when ignorance is chosen.

## Key Points
- Complexity can become an excuse for avoiding truth-seeking; ignorance is sometimes a deliberate choice, not inevitable.
- Governments must collect and release critical data; India sits on vaccine and COVID data unavailable to external analysts.
- Emotional pause before sharing information (search your feelings) is foundational to rational decision-making and storytelling.

## Open Question
How do we balance the legitimate difficulty of understanding complex systems with the need to act decisively despite incomplete information?

## India Angle
India's data secrecy around COVID-19 vaccine effectiveness and reinfection rates exemplifies how complexity becomes an excuse for opacity; Indian policymakers must adopt Harford's framework of demanding transparency and statistical rigor in governance.
