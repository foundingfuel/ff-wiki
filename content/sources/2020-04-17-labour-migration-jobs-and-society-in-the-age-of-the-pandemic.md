## Core Argument
Pandemic's unprecedented speed demands deliberate economic reset built on local capacity, not top-down recovery.

## Key Points
- 200-300M construction workers, 100M MSME employees face immediate cash flow crisis; work cannot go digital.
- No historical precedent: India losing 30% GDP in weeks vs. Great Depression's annual erosion.
- Solution requires local entrepreneurship and decentralized problem-solving, not centralized policy alone.

## Open Question
How can India reskill 200+ million informal workers who lack digital literacy and whose livelihoods depend on physical presence, especially when recovery timelines and job availability remain unknown?

## India Angle
India's 200-300 million informal workers—construction, MSMEs, daily wage earners—face immediate collapse without government cash flow intervention; urgently requires local, decentralized solutions vs. top-down policy to prevent social tension and inequality-driven insurgency.
