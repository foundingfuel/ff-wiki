## Core Argument
Unconstrained growth hits limits; tech giants must pivot business models or face regulatory dismantling.

## Key Points
- Zuckerberg sacrifices Facebook's ad-dependent town square model to capture faster-growing private messaging and small groups.
- Elizabeth Warren's platform utility proposal threatens tech giants' data monetization by mandating algorithm transparency and restricting data sharing.
- India's digital platforms face urgent need to shape regulation proactively before global precedents force compliance.

## Open Question
How can platforms monetize privacy-focused services without data harvesting when that's been their core revenue engine?

## India Angle
Facebook's privacy pivot and Warren's Big Tech breakup proposals will influence how Indian platforms like Reliance Jio operate; India must proactively shape regulation before global frameworks force compliance.
