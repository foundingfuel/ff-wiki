## Core Argument
Sustainable problem-solving requires deliberate slowness and root-cause analysis, not addiction to quick fixes.

## Key Points
- Toyota's 'why' method and humility expose root causes faster than hurried solutions.
- Women leaders show better crisis performance through employee empathy, adaptability, and integrity.
- Collective grief and time for reflection must precede legislative solutions to systemic failures.

## Open Question
How can organizations embed slow thinking into cultures structured around speed, growth metrics, and quarterly outcomes without losing competitive advantage?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and policymakers must embrace 'slow thinking' to address systemic crises like healthcare gaps and worker protection, rather than quick-fix approaches; parliamentary discourse on constitutional guarantees like right to health requires patience and deliberation over crisis-driven reactivity.
