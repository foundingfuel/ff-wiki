## Core Argument
Emerging technologies like 3D printing, AI, autonomous vehicles reshape industries; adoption pace and regulatory readiness determine winners.

## Key Points
- 3D printing most disruptive in healthcare—customizable, complex objects surpass traditional manufacturing capabilities.
- AI approaching exponential growth phase; question isn't capability but speed of advancement and regulatory adaptation.
- Self-driving cars, IoT, cybersecurity raise unresolved ethical and regulatory questions about liability, privacy, security.

## Open Question
Technologies promise massive societal benefits (lives saved, efficiency gained) but create unresolved ethical dilemmas (autonomous vehicle liability, hacker control, privacy erosion) that regulation hasn't caught up with.

## India Angle
India lags in 3D printing adoption for healthcare and manufacturing; cybersecurity emerging as critical national security concern per India's NSA; autonomous vehicles regulatory frameworks need development.
