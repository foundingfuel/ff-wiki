## Core Argument
CEO hubris, inflated by media praise and compensation gaps, systematically leads to overpaying for acquisitions.

## Key Points
- Each favorable media article increases acquisition premium by 4.8%, demonstrating how external validation distorts judgment.
- Overconfidence stems from recent performance success, media attention, and compensation hierarchy gaps between executives.
- Leaders must recognize behavioral biases in decision-making to avoid costly strategic errors during organizational transitions.

## Open Question
How can organizations create governance structures that counteract individual leader biases without stifling the confidence needed for bold strategic moves?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs and founders, especially in acquisitions and scaling phases, face similar hubris risks; understanding these behavioral biases is critical for board governance and investor decision-making in India's M&A ecosystem.
