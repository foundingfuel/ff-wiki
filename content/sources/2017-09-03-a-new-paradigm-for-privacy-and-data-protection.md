## Core Argument
Privacy protection requires rethinking institutional accountability and collaborative stakeholder engagement across public and private sectors.

## Key Points
- Supreme Court privacy judgment forces government and private players to reassess data handling obligations and accountability.
- Business ethics dimension missing from data protection discourse; companies must internalize responsibility beyond legal compliance.
- Institutional accountability frameworks (public and private) need redesign to prevent harm from data misuse.

## Open Question
How can India balance innovation in data-driven industries (HealthTech, fintech) with strong privacy protections when enforcement mechanisms and accountability frameworks remain underdeveloped?

## India Angle
India's Supreme Court privacy judgment creates urgent need for data protection frameworks affecting government, tech startups (HealthTech), and citizen rights; requires Indian institutions to establish accountability standards before regulatory vacuum widens.
