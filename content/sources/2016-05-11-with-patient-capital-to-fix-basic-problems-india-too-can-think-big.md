## Core Argument
India needs patient, multi-billion dollar capital to build infrastructure solving fundamental problems over decades, not venture timescales.

## Key Points
- Developed economies can pursue platforms/marketplaces because basic infrastructure exists; India entrepreneurs must build the stack themselves.
- Major barriers: 800 languages, 95% cash transactions, 250M students needing jobs, fragmented agriculture, healthcare underfunding—require systemic solutions.
- Traditional 7-10 year VC funds seeking quick exits won't solve India's infrastructure challenges; holding company structures with patient capital are essential.

## Open Question
If India's fundamental problems require decades of patient capital investment, how do entrepreneurs attract such funding when global capital markets reward faster returns?

## India Angle
India's entrepreneurs must build foundational infrastructure (language, payments, healthcare, farm access) that developed markets take for granted; requires patient capital and decades-long commitment, not traditional VC timelines.
