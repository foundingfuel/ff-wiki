## Core Argument
AI's monocultural lens threatens linguistic diversity; India must build culturally intelligent systems or risk irrelevance.

## Key Points
- AI models cluster in Protestant European/English-speaking worldviews despite multilingual capability, marginalizing Global South languages.
- Language shapes cognition; losing languages loses distinct ways of thinking embedded in untranslatable concepts like thairaav.
- Cultural intelligence—not just localization—should be a primary AI performance metric alongside accuracy and speed.

## Open Question
How do we measure and enforce cultural intelligence in AI systems when the metrics themselves are shaped by the dominant cultural lens that built them?

## India Angle
India's 22 scheduled languages and 780+ indigenous tongues risk marginalization by AI systems built in English-dominant worldviews. This threatens cognitive diversity and cultural knowledge systems; India can lead in 'context engineering' for multilingual, culturally intelligent AI rather than outsourcing its cultural operating system.
