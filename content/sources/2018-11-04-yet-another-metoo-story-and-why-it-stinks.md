## Core Argument
Crisis response reveals organisational culture; Indian companies must embrace transparency to retain talent and rebuild trust.

## Key Points
- Google's transparent crisis handling under Pichai contrasts sharply with Tata group's tepid response to harassment allegations.
- New generation workers demand psychological safety and fairness; companies ignoring this face existential risk regardless of legacy.
- Sexual harassment crises offer transformation opportunities—how leaders respond signals genuine commitment to culture change.

## Open Question
How can large, legacy-bound Indian institutions move fast enough to meet generational demands for workplace safety without losing operational stability?

## India Angle
Indian conglomerates like Tata group lag far behind global peers in responding to sexual harassment crises transparently; generational expectations for safe, fair workplaces are reshaping India Inc's survival prospects.
