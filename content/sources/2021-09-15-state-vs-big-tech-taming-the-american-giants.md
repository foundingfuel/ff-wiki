## Core Argument
Global tech regulation is accelerating through antitrust enforcement and privacy laws, driven by bipartisan pressure.

## Key Points
- US regulatory landscape shifting from laissez-faire to bipartisan antitrust support, with FTC gaining new enforcement teeth
- Big Tech accused of monopolistic gatekeeping—buying competitors rather than competing, stifling innovation and employment
- Fragmented state-level regulation in US contrasts with unified EU GDPR and China's top-down approach

## Open Question
How can regulators enforce meaningful tech rules without stifling innovation and competitiveness, especially when different countries adopt conflicting approaches?

## India Angle
India must learn from US, EU, and China's regulatory approaches as it shapes its own tech regulation strategy; Indian policymakers face similar monopoly challenges with foreign tech giants while building domestic capacity
