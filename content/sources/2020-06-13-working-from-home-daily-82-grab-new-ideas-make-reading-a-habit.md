## Core Argument
Reading with purpose—grabbing ideas and acting on them—builds leadership strength more than any other time investment.

## Key Points
- Daily 30-minute reading habit across formats builds leadership strength and new insights over time.
- Idea-grabbing requires both exposure to new information and temperament to execute sensible action.
- State capacity and inequality matter more than regime type in pandemic response effectiveness.

## Open Question
How do busy professionals develop the temperament and discipline to grab ideas and act on them, when most struggle even with consistent reading habits?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and professionals need deliberate reading habits and idea-grabbing discipline to build leadership strength; state capacity building (governance quality) is critical for India's pandemic resilience and economic inequality reduction.
