## Core Argument
Concentrate on the best work; for travelers, preparation and personal responsibility matter more than rules others follow.

## Key Points
- Sturgeon's Law: 90% of all output is mediocre; focus criticism on best work, not dregs.
- Pre-flight preparation—research airport rules, download state apps, print documents—reduces stress and contact.
- Personal discipline and awareness essential; cannot rely on others following social distancing or safety protocols.

## Open Question
How do individuals maintain safety and composure when systems and fellow travelers consistently violate established protocols?
