## Core Argument
Resource constraints spark breakthrough innovation; India can lead frugal engineering by reframing scarcity as competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Entrepreneurs see problems as innovation opportunities, turning scarcity into breakthroughs benefiting communities and enterprises.
- India's resource constraints provide natural advantage for frugal innovation leadership and rewriting global innovation playbooks.
- B-schools must overhaul research culture to address industry challenges and support entrepreneurial experimentation solving real problems.

## Open Question
Can India's unbranded, small entrepreneurs actually solve systemic problems like affordable housing without sustained government support and institutional backing?

## India Angle
India's resource scarcities position it uniquely to lead in frugal innovation; entrepreneurs can solve endemic problems like affordable housing by rewriting innovation playbooks adapted to constraint-driven contexts.
