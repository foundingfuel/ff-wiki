## Core Argument
Mergers extract financial efficiency but destroy creative value; holding companies need new ideas, not scale.

## Key Points
- Mergers prioritize shareholder returns over client innovation, addressing none of today's marketing challenges.
- Holding companies shifted from protecting agencies to financial cost-cutting under finance-driven leadership.
- Rebalancing branding versus performance, and reorganizing around client industries, offer genuine competitive advantage.

## Open Question
If consolidation and cost-cutting don't create competitive advantage, why do rational financial leaders continue pursuing mergers when the real opportunity lies in building new organizational models?

## India Angle
Indian advertising agencies face existential risk as global consolidation marginalizes legacy local brands like Ulka, Mudra, and Lintas. Indian operations are secondary to US-centric merger logic, threatening local talent, expertise, and independent creative voices.
