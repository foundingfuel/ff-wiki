## Core Argument
Outsiders solve entrenched problems by abandoning insider expertise and applying distant analogies.

## Key Points
- John Davis solved a 20-year oil spill problem by borrowing concrete vibrator concept from construction, not chemistry.
- Industry experts get trapped by accumulated knowledge; outsiders see problems with fresh perspective unburdened by assumptions.
- Nicobar islanders' historical isolation knowledge proved more valuable than mainland public health systems during COVID.

## Open Question
If outsider perspective is valuable, how do organizations systematically invite or institutionalize outsider thinking without losing domain depth required for execution?

## India Angle
India's tech talent war mirrors global pattern; local/indigenous knowledge systems (Nicobar example) offer solutions outsiders miss—relevant for Indian policymakers designing public health and innovation systems that leverage community expertise rather than top-down approaches.
