## Core Argument
Emotional intelligence and trust-building are critical organizational competencies, especially in remote work environments.

## Key Points
- Untrained emotions undermine technological progress; societies must develop emotional literacy systematically
- Trust-first business models (ID Fresh's honor system) outperform surveillance-based approaches in India
- Remote work demands heightened emotional self-management; Romantic spontaneity replaced by reflective practice

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically train emotional competence when it traditionally develops through informal socialization, now disrupted by remote work?

## India Angle
Indian businesses like ID Fresh Foods demonstrate that trust-based models and emotional intelligence can drive success; relevant for Indian leaders navigating post-pandemic hybrid work and customer relationships
