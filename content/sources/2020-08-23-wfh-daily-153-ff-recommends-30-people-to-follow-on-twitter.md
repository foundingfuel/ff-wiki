## Core Argument
Use Twitter as a personal knowledge system by following curated experts with strong India perspectives, not algorithms.

## Key Points
- Transform Twitter from algorithm-driven noise into a structured personal knowledge management (PKM) system using lists.
- Curated 30 handles selected for India opinion, intellectual rigor, and audience relevance—not follower counts.
- Silence as a professional tool: mastering interviews and learning requires restraint, listening, and letting others fill gaps.

## Open Question
How does one maintain intellectual independence and diverse viewpoints when deliberately filtering Twitter into curated lists, risking new echo chambers?

## India Angle
Curated list of 30 Indian and India-focused Twitter handles across investing, current affairs, tech, and foreign policy—designed to help Indian professionals build personal knowledge systems aligned with India-specific perspectives.
