## Core Argument
Christensen's disruptive innovation theory fundamentally reshaped how leaders understand market competition and industry disruption.

## Key Points
- Disruptive innovation explains how low-end market entrants can threaten established competitors by moving upmarket.
- Christensen's work became essential reading for business leaders globally, influencing strategy at Intel, Apple and Indian corporations.
- The theory provided frameworks for predicting industry evolution, helping decision-makers navigate competitive threats.

## Open Question
How well does Christensen's disruptive innovation theory hold up in fast-moving digital markets where traditional market segmentation becomes fluid and incumbent advantages erode faster than the theory predicts?

## India Angle
Christensen's disruptive innovation theory became foundational to how Indian business leaders understood competitive strategy and market evolution in the 2000s; the theory helped Indian entrepreneurs identify opportunities at the bottom of the market where incumbent players ignored segments.
