## Core Argument
Complex systems require interdisciplinary, non-siloed thinking rather than mechanistic frameworks from single domains.

## Key Points
- Einstein's sustained focus on intangible, unprovable questions demonstrates value of long-term curiosity beyond immediate urgency.
- Aadhaar's scale revealed that traditional problem-solving frameworks fail in dynamic, complex systems; uncertainty and hypotheses must be embraced.
- History's finest minds (da Vinci, Newton, Einstein) synthesized across disciplines rather than confined thinking to single domains.

## Open Question
How do large-scale projects (like Aadhaar) balance the need for frameworks and execution with acceptance that outcomes cannot be fully predicted?

## India Angle
Aadhaar's complexity required interdisciplinary thinking beyond traditional frameworks—a lesson for Indian policymakers and leaders building large-scale systems in dynamic, uncertain contexts.
