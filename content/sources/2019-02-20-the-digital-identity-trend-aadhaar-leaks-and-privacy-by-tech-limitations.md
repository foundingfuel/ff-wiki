## Core Argument
Digital identity proliferation risks privacy erosion; technological limits must design safeguards into systems, not rely on external enforcement.

## Key Points
- Aadhaar leaks are often misattributed to UIDAI, creating false alarms that desensitize society to real cybersecurity threats.
- Digital identity expansion across government (schools, livestock, property) increases state capacity but amplifies privacy and data security risks.
- Incentive structure design—paying for outputs not outcomes—proved critical to Aadhaar's success but demonstrates how perverse incentives easily emerge.

## Open Question
How can India balance the state's legitimate need for digital identity systems to improve governance and reduce fraud, against citizens' right to privacy and protection from data breaches across fragmented institutional ecosystems?

## India Angle
India's Aadhaar system exemplifies tensions between state capacity building, digital identity ambitions, and privacy risks—critical for India's governance infrastructure and citizen data security as multiple agencies leverage it.
