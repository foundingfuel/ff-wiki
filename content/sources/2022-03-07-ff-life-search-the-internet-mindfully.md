## Core Argument
Mindful internet use requires understanding how ad-driven algorithms shape information access and exploring alternatives.

## Key Points
- Google's $147B ad revenue model creates conflicts: ads ranked as top results, SEO inflates search pollution, personalization creates echo chambers.
- DuckDuckGo offers privacy (no profiling, no tracking) but lacks Google's algorithmic sophistication; email service strips embedded trackers and ads.
- Reddit's volunteer moderation and serendipity-driven discovery provide alternative to algorithmic curation, though underutilized in India.

## Open Question
How do users balance convenience and personalization of algorithmic platforms against privacy erosion and information distortion, especially when alternatives are less sophisticated?

## India Angle
Indian internet users heavily dependent on Google for information; rising awareness of data privacy and algorithmic bias relevant to India's digital-first population; Reddit underutilization in India presents untapped alternative for knowledge discovery.
