## Core Argument
China pivots from reactive to assertive positioning as Trump reshapes global order through transactional diplomacy.

## Key Points
- China's 'development rights' becomes fourth red line, challenging climate/tech constraints imposed by West.
- Real domestic growth ~2.2% despite 5% headline GDP; deflation and weak consumption fuel generational discontent.
- Tentative India-China thaw emerging, conditional on border stabilization and deeper economic integration.

## Open Question
Can China maintain assertiveness in U.S. negotiations while addressing domestic consumption crisis rooted in generational income inequality without fiscal stimulus?

## India Angle
India faces strategic opportunities amid U.S.-China tensions and potential thaw with China; must leverage technology transfer, border stability, and economic partnerships to strengthen its position in reshaping global trade architecture.
