## Core Argument
Platforms like Twitter require government oversight because their externalities harm society beyond users' control.

## Key Points
- Twitter's network effects make exit-based market discipline ineffective; traditional product competition logic doesn't apply.
- Social media platforms harm non-users through cascading effects—misinformation, coordination of violence, electoral manipulation reach beyond platform boundaries.
- Twitter's reactive content moderation is systematically slow, often identifying abuse only after 100+ harmful tweets, affecting 37% of users.

## Open Question
How can governments effectively regulate global platforms for public safety without either stifling free speech or enabling authoritarian censorship, especially when platforms themselves remain unclear on content governance priorities?

## India Angle
Indian parliamentary scrutiny of Twitter's content moderation practices reflects India's struggle to regulate global platforms affecting domestic elections, communal harmony, and information flows without formal regulatory frameworks.
