## Core Argument
Return-to-office mandates mask deeper failure: inability to redesign work and rebuild managerial judgment beyond rules.

## Key Points
- Covid forced redesign—asynchronous work, outcome dashboards, trust through clarity—not goodwill. Regression now risks losing hard-won capability.
- Managers struggle with flexibility because it demands judgment over rules. Fairness requires differentiation, not identical treatment across roles.
- Blanket attendance rules feel safe but erode organizational capability—talent development, decision quality, and adaptive management weaken silently.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain the managerial capability required for flexible, outcome-based work when most leaders were trained in—and feel safer with—rule-based control systems?

## India Angle
Indian managers, trained in hierarchical control and rule-based systems, face the same challenge: whether to retreat to familiar attendance mandates or develop judgment-based leadership—critical as Indian startups and enterprises compete for global talent and navigate hybrid work models.
