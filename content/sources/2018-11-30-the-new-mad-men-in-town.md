## Core Argument
Traditional ad agencies must transform from creative-centric to tech-enabled integrators or risk obsolescence.

## Key Points
- Full-service consolidation strategy alone fails; clients prefer specialized partners and direct platform relationships.
- Facebook and Google's data advantage fundamentally disrupted agency value proposition and client buying behavior.
- Organizational culture rigidity prevents agencies from pivoting fast enough to tech-led service models consultancies now offer.

## Open Question
How can legacy creative-led agencies rebuild culture for agility when employee embeddedness in old ways makes transformation 'herculean'?

## India Angle
Indian marketing services agencies face similar pressures as global players—digital disruption, data concentration at platforms, consulting firms entering the space. Organizational agility and culture change remain critical competitive challenges for Indian agencies before global trends fully localize.
