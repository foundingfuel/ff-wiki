## Core Argument
Excellence requires deliberate practice with expert feedback, trusted messengers, and institutional listening—not hours alone.

## Key Points
- Top performance needs focused practice, expert feedback loops, and concentration—not just time investment.
- Vaccine hesitancy overcomes through trusted local providers and community leaders, not advertising alone.
- Indian entrepreneurs need institutional governance 'hearing aids' to balance founder equity with stakeholder listening.

## Open Question
How can Indian companies balance founder control with institutional listening without diluting entrepreneurial vision or enabling founder accountability?

## India Angle
Indian students, parents, and entrepreneurs need world-class performance frameworks and decision-making approaches as India builds institutional capacity and tackles vaccine hesitancy, all while navigating post-pandemic transitions.
