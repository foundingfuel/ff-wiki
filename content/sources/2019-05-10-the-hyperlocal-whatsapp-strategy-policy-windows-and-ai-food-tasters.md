## Core Argument
Platforms enable unexpected innovations; policy windows require entrepreneurs ready to seize fleeting opportunities.

## Key Points
- WhatsApp groups create hyperlocal food marketplaces, turning messaging into commerce without platform design intention
- Policy windows open briefly when problems, solutions, and political will align; Golden Quadrilateral and Aadhaar are Indian examples
- AI automation (food tasting in China) raises both job displacement and new opportunity creation questions

## Open Question
How do you systematically prepare for policy windows that open unpredictably, or is seizing them inherently reactive rather than strategic?

## India Angle
India-specific: WhatsApp hyperlocal food markets in Bangalore bypass traditional delivery platforms; Aadhaar as policy window example; UPI payments integration. Global comparisons with China's AI food tasting, but lens remains on Indian entrepreneurial adaptation of platforms.
