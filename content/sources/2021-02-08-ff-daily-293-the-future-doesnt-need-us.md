## Core Argument
Advanced AI may render human decision-making obsolete, creating dependency or elite control scenarios.

## Key Points
- If machines make better decisions, humans may abdicate control, losing practical agency over their futures.
- Complete dependence on machines creates an inescapable trap—turning them off becomes impossible without societal collapse.
- Retained human control risks concentrating power in tiny elites while masses become economically superfluous.

## Open Question
How can societies ensure human agency and dignity persist if machines provably outperform humans at all decision-making—without either surrendering autonomy or creating oppressive elite control?

## India Angle
As India rapidly adopts AI and automation, questions about job displacement, elite control of technology, and societal dependence on machines become urgent for policymakers and business leaders navigating digital transformation.
