## Core Argument
India must transform education and massively increase R&D investment to compete globally in the knowledge economy.

## Key Points
- Online education enables lifelong learning but deepens inequity; rural-urban digital divide is severe (4% vs 23% computer access).
- R&D investment disparity: India spends 0.65% GDP vs China 2.2%, Korea 4.8%, Israel 5%; this directly impacts competitiveness.
- Countries with focused R&D priorities (Israel in cybersecurity, China in AI) build excellence through education + infrastructure investment combined.

## Open Question
How can India expand online education access to disadvantaged populations while simultaneously building the research infrastructure needed for global competitiveness, given severe resource constraints?

## India Angle
India lags peers like China, Korea, Israel in R&D investment (0.65% vs 2-5% GDP) and research output. Online education access disparity (4% rural computer access vs 23% urban) threatens equity. India must prioritize education and R&D to compete in knowledge economy and attract value-adding investments.
