## Core Argument
Mastering uncertainty requires fundamentals, embracing loss of control, and converting fear into creative energy.

## Key Points
- Deep skill, experience and judgment matter more than technology when facing unprecedented crises.
- Surrendering the illusion of control and accepting uncertainty paradoxically reduces anxiety and unlocks agency.
- Building habits and routines frees mental energy to navigate ambiguity with creativity rather than panic.

## Open Question
How do we balance preparation through mastery of fundamentals with acceptance that outcomes remain fundamentally uncontrollable?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and organizations facing unprecedented crises (pandemic, economic disruption) need frameworks to navigate fear and uncertainty through fundamentals, reflection, and habit-building rather than relying solely on management theories.
