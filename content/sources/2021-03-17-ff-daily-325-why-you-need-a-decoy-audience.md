## Core Argument
Presenting to curious non-experts improves expert communication by removing politeness-induced under-explanation.

## Key Points
- Experts unconsciously under-explain to avoid insulting knowledgeable audiences, causing them to talk past each other.
- Decoy audiences of informed amateurs interrupt and flag confusion, forcing clearer exposition of complex ideas.
- This method benefits all parties: experts refine their thinking, amateurs get tutorials, and knowledge transfer improves.

## Open Question
How do you scale this decoy audience approach in large organizations or public forums where controlled audience composition isn't feasible?

## India Angle
Indian experts and academics often struggle with similar communication challenges in research presentations and conferences; adopting the 'decoy audience' method could improve clarity in India's knowledge-sharing institutions and corporate training programs.
