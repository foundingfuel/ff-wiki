## Core Argument
Hoarding gifts/resources creates psychological and social toxicity; generosity and circulation preserve both culture and humanity.

## Key Points
- Lewis Hyde's allegory: hidden gifts become destructive toads, forcing perpetual feeding and migration through the world.
- Ajay Gandhi exemplified how institutions thrive on freedom, dialogue without personal offense, and authentic human connection across hierarchies.
- Post-pandemic children internalize struggle as personal failure when narrative framing pigeonholes them into deficit-based stories.

## Open Question
How do we foster generosity and circulation of gifts in a modern economy fundamentally built on property accumulation and individual ownership?

## India Angle
Indian society navigating post-pandemic recovery must balance material consumption desires with deeper questions of generosity, community bonds, and psychological well-being—especially for children and institutional culture-building.
