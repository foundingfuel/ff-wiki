## Core Argument
Gen Z redefines Diwali gifting through experience, nostalgia, and digital subscriptions over traditional luxury items.

## Key Points
- Gen Z preferences shift from conventional gifts (chocolates, sweets, dry fruits) to experiential and digital options.
- Portable tech, instant cameras, and software subscriptions appeal to younger generations seeking practical utility.
- Custom merchandise and niche fashion accessories drive emotional connection and personal identity expression.

## Open Question
How can older generations bridge the gap between traditional Diwali gifting customs and evolving Gen Z values without losing cultural meaning?

## India Angle
Indian gift-giving traditions during Diwali are shifting as Gen Z and millennials reject conventional choices; understanding this generational preference gap is critical for Indian retailers and family networks navigating cultural transition.
