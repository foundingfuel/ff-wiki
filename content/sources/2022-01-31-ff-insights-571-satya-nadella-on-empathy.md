## Core Argument
Empathy transforms organizational culture; personal challenges deepen leaders' capacity for human-centered change.

## Key Points
- Nadella's empathy-driven Microsoft transformation began with a mentor's question, deepened by his son's cerebral palsy diagnosis
- Beginner's mind—holding multiple possibilities rather than expert certainty—prevents overcomplication and reveals true mastery
- Personality-driven leadership (Kohli example) can overshadow actual results; impact of individual presence often overstated in modern organizations

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the personal growth empathy demands with organizational pressure for demonstrable results and aggressive performance?

## India Angle
Indian leaders navigating transformation in tech and corporate sectors must balance aggressive drive with empathy—relevant as Indian companies scale globally and face culture-building challenges post-pandemic
