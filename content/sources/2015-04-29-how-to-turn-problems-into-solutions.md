## Core Argument
Frame business problems as solution opportunities to achieve transformational, not incremental, competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Low-cost carriers identified pain points and converted them into business model innovations.
- Reframing questions drives transformational solutions rather than incremental improvements.
- Blue Riband case shows how constraint-based thinking can revitalize mature products.

## Open Question
How do you distinguish between reframing that leads to transformational change versus rationalizing incremental fixes as strategic innovation?

## India Angle
Indian examples (low-cost carriers, Blue Riband gin) demonstrate how domestic companies can reframe constraints into competitive advantages—critical for Indian firms competing globally.
