## Core Argument
Organizations must shift from rigid planning to dynamic steering—staying purposeful while responding real-time to feedback.

## Key Points
- Detailed upfront planning creates false control; real-world feedback demands continuous course correction.
- WFH enables geographic arbitrage, forcing companies to choose between location-based pay or global equity.
- Holacracy replaces command-and-control with organic, purpose-driven adaptation to changing conditions.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain strategic coherence and fairness while embracing real-time responsiveness and decentralized decision-making?

## India Angle
Indian IT companies like TCS and Infosys must decide on location-based pay adjustments as WFH enables talent migration within India; Holacracy principles challenge traditional hierarchical management prevalent in Indian enterprises.
