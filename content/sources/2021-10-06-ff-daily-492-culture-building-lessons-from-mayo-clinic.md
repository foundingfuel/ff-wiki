## Core Argument
Strong organizational culture requires deliberate, continuous training aligned with mission and values, not just process copying.

## Key Points
- Mayo invests 17 courses/year per employee, 940,000+ registrations annually across campuses, embedding culture through systematic training.
- Culture shift required: medical residents must unlearn solo excellence from school and adopt team-based problem-solving at point of care.
- Culture cannot be copied; it emerges from years of aligned recruiting, training, management discipline, and mission coherence.

## Open Question
How can organizations systematically replicate Mayo's culture-building without the decades of institutional stability and resources that enable such commitment?

## India Angle
Indian hospitals and professional services firms struggle with culture-building at scale; Mayo Clinic's model offers lessons for India's healthcare and services sectors where knowledge-sharing and teamwork remain nascent compared to individualistic training traditions.
