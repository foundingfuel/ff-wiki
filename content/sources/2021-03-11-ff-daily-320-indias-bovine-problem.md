## Core Argument
India's inability to cull infected cattle creates $3.4B losses; vaccination alone insufficient without systemic solutions.

## Key Points
- Brucellosis causes abortions, infertility, reduced milk production in 200 million Indian cattle, costing $3.4B annually
- Cultural and legal prohibitions on culling force sick animals into overcrowded gaushalas, preventing disease containment
- 2025 eradication target requires vaccination plus complementary control measures; vaccination-only approach deemed insufficient

## Open Question
How can India eradicate brucellosis by 2025 when cultural prohibitions prevent culling and gaushalas are at capacity, requiring solutions beyond vaccination alone?

## India Angle
India's cattle population (200 million) and cultural constraints on culling create a unique policy challenge: brucellosis costs $3.4B annually while gaushalas overflow. Vaccination alone cannot solve this without complementary measures, requiring creative governance solutions that respect cultural sensitivities.
