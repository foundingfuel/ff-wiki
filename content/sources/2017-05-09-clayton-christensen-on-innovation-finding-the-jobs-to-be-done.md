## Core Argument
Innovation succeeds through understanding customer jobs, not luck or trend-chasing.

## Key Points
- Jobs Theory defines customer progress sought in specific circumstances, not generic needs.
- Spot jobs through non-consumption, workarounds, avoided tasks, and product misuses.
- Understanding causal purchase mechanisms replaces gambling-based innovation with predictable product development.

## Open Question
How do you systematically identify jobs in emerging markets where customer articulation of needs is unclear or contextual variability is extremely high?

## India Angle
Indian startups and established companies often innovate reactively; Jobs Theory offers a systematic framework for Indian businesses to move from hit-or-miss product development to understanding actual customer progress needs—critical for scaling in price-sensitive, diverse markets.
