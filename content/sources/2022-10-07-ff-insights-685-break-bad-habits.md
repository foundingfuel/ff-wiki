## Core Argument
Build new habits by gradual skill escalation, not by fighting old patterns directly.

## Key Points
- Brains excel at building circuits but struggle to unbuild them; replacement is more effective than removal.
- Progressive difficulty and small, consistent repetition build new neural pathways and override old habits.
- Real-world examples—shyness clinic's social fitness model—prove structured, escalating practice works better than introspection.

## Open Question
If habit replacement requires months of gradual practice, how do individuals and organizations sustain motivation and patience when quick-fix culture dominates?

## India Angle
India has established expertise in randomized trials (Banerjee's Nobel Prize) and faces unique challenges in habit-building and skill development across education and workplace contexts where infrastructure and coaching models need scaling.
