## Core Argument
Individualism and shareholder primacy have hollowed institutions, requiring communitarian counterbalance for sustainable success.

## Key Points
- CEO hero worship obscures truth: lasting firms are built by high-performing teams, not charismatic individuals.
- Shareholder value doctrine since 1970s replaced professional management ethos, destroying purpose-driven cultures (Boeing case).
- Populism thrives on inequality but deepens it by allying with elites rather than solving root causes.

## Open Question
If populist leaders genuinely reflect widespread resentment but lack coherent ideology, how can societies address inequality without either ideological clarity or return to discredited elite consensus?

## India Angle
Indian businesses and policymakers must recognize how excessive individualism and shareholder-first thinking erodes institutional strength and team-based excellence—critical for building world-class firms and governance capacity in a developing economy.
