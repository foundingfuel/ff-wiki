## Core Argument
Disruptive leaders succeed by defying norms through high risk appetite, intuition, and self-interest that paradoxically creates institutional value.

## Key Points
- Mavericks like Musk, Alexander, Modi succeed outside normative frameworks despite polarization and criticism.
- Deep intuition and high risk appetite let them see opportunity in ambiguity others find paralyzing.
- Their success creates wealth and privilege that provides access enabling further rule-breaking.

## Open Question
If oversized ambition and selfishness are necessary for creating institutional value, how do societies balance enabling such leaders with protecting stakeholders from their potential excesses?
