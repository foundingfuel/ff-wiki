## Core Argument
Credibility is your most valuable negotiation leverage; build it through small promises kept before big deals arise.

## Key Points
- Credibility unlocks entry to negotiations and breaks deadlock when mistrust dominates; it cannot be faked under pressure.
- Differentiators should strengthen value, not compete on price; undermining your positioning erodes long-term competitive advantage.
- Imaginative perspective-taking—advising a friend, viewing from deathbed, consulting trusted voices—clarifies tough decisions beyond rational analysis.

## Open Question
How do you build credibility in fast-moving startup environments where reputation takes years but market windows are narrow?

## India Angle
Indian negotiators and business leaders navigating complex deals—from startup funding to corporate mergers—must invest in credibility early; Ignatius's decision-making framework offers cultural resonance given India's strong philosophical traditions.
