## Core Argument
Time-based thinking transforms how we measure investment returns and workplace performance.

## Key Points
- Returns compound multiplicatively over time; annual benchmarks obscure this reality
- Institutional incentive structures (annual fees, yearly targets) contradict long-term investing principles
- Hybrid work is becoming non-negotiable; resistance to flexibility signals organizational misalignment with workforce expectations

## Open Question
How can institutions redesign incentive structures and performance metrics when the existing system rewards short-term thinking?

## India Angle
Indian pension funds and asset managers face similar incentive misalignment issues; hybrid work adoption will reshape Indian corporate culture and real estate demand differently across metros and tier-2 cities
