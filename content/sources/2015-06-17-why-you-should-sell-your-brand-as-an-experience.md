## Core Argument
Brands must sell experiences engaging all five senses to build emotional bonds and customer advocacy.

## Key Points
- Product Economy focused on transactions; Service Economy added convenience; Experience Economy creates lasting emotional connections.
- Starbucks exemplifies experience economy by engaging sight, sound, smell, taste, touch simultaneously to create memorable experiences.
- Pleasurable sensory experiences generate memories that compel repeat business and deepen customer-brand relationships over time.

## Open Question
How can experience economy principles scale profitably for mass-market or price-sensitive segments where premium positioning may be unfeasible?

## India Angle
Indian brands and retailers (QSR, cafes, retail) can leverage experience economy principles to differentiate in competitive markets and build emotional loyalty beyond price competition, critical for emerging consumer segments.
