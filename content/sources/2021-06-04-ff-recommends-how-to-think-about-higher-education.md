## Core Argument
Pandemic disrupts education systems; students must choose between local excellence and international prestige based on passion, not assumptions.

## Key Points
- Digital divide is widening—underprivileged students face learning loss while rich students experience lag; teachers lack digital fluency.
- High-quality Indian universities like Ashoka rival international peers; prioritize passion and great teachers over brand names.
- Keeping family close during uncertainty and avoiding economic risk may outweigh career prestige in current times.

## Open Question
How do we balance individual aspiration for world-class education against systemic inequities and pandemic-driven uncertainty without either settling or overextending families?

## India Angle
Indian students face unique pandemic-driven disruption to board exams and university admissions; debate centers on quality of emerging Indian institutions (Ashoka, ISB) versus international options, with implications for brain drain and family stability during crisis.
