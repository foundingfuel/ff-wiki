## Core Argument
AI's greatest potential lies in augmenting human capability, not competing with it, to solve societal challenges.

## Key Points
- Narrative frames AI as humans versus machines; reality is complementary partnership solving real problems.
- Saqib Shaikh example shows AI restoring human agency and independence, not replacing it.
- Solving climate, disease, ignorance requires cooperation over competition—a systems-level shift in thinking.

## Open Question
If AI's promise is collaboration, why do organizations and policymakers continue investing in automation that eliminates jobs rather than tools that augment human capability?

## India Angle
Indian enterprises and leaders must shift from viewing AI as a replacement threat to embracing human-AI collaboration—critical for sectors like healthcare, accessibility, and poverty reduction where India has acute needs.
