## Core Argument
Civic technology enables real-time governance monitoring to close gaps between reported progress and actual impact in Swachh Bharat Mission.

## Key Points
- Civic tech provides granular, real-time data and citizen feedback to track performance across distributed government systems.
- Large-scale behaviour change requires bridging subjective perception, objective metrics, and third-party validation simultaneously.
- Tech-enabled monitoring across government-to-government and citizen-to-government axes increases accountability and corrects implementation shortfalls.

## Open Question
How can civic tech solutions sustain commitment and effective stakeholder management across multiple levels when centuries-old habits and 70 years of neglect persist?

## India Angle
Swachh Bharat Mission's success depends on tech-enabled accountability across 4,041 urban local bodies; civic tech can bridge gaps between reported progress, citizen perception, and ground reality—critical for India's sanitation transformation.
