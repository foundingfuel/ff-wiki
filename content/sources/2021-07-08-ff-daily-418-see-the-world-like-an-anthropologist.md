## Core Argument
Anthropological thinking—seeing the familiar as strange—reveals hidden patterns and blind spots in business and life.

## Key Points
- Empathy and listening to 'strange' perspectives builds resilience in interconnected, globalised systems vulnerable to contagion.
- Understanding others through contrast reveals our own invisible assumptions, biases, and mental maps.
- Anthropologists use group-level analysis like X-rays to uncover half-hidden patterns organisations collectively inherit unknowingly.

## Open Question
How do leaders systematically adopt anthropological thinking when organisational pressures demand speed and quantifiable metrics over deep observation and listening?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders navigating rapid globalisation need anthropological mindsets to understand diverse markets, consumer behaviours, and cultural blind spots—critical for scaling beyond homogeneous contexts and building inclusive organisations.
