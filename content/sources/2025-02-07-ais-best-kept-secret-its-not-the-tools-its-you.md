## Core Argument
AI's value lies not in the tools themselves, but in how you prompt and leverage them to amplify thinking.

## Key Points
- Effective AI use requires intentional prompt design—asking ChatGPT to predict panellist insights rather than summarise produced better preparation.
- AI works best when assigned specific roles (executive assistant, briefing officer, debate partner) rather than generic queries.
- Continuous iteration and experimentation—testing new prompts and pushing AI with follow-up questions—unlocks capabilities most users never discover.

## Open Question
If AI's real value is in how you use it rather than the tool itself, how do organisations systematically teach this judgment to employees who lack domain expertise or executive experience to know what good questions to ask?

## India Angle
Indian professionals and leaders need to move beyond tool-adoption to intentional AI use—developing the judgment, prompting skills, and executive thinking that transform AI from commodity to competitive advantage in Indian workplaces.
