## Core Argument
Witnessing Antarctica's fragile beauty forces reckoning with human insignificance and environmental responsibility.

## Key Points
- Author's internal conflict: environmentalist principles clashing with desire to experience endangered ecosystems.
- Strict protocols (biosecurity, waste management) reflect Antarctica's extreme vulnerability to human contamination.
- Expedition team expertise (cetologists, glaciologists, naturalists) transforms tourism into environmental education.

## Open Question
Can visiting fragile ecosystems like Antarctica for personal transformation be justified when human presence itself accelerates their degradation?

## India Angle
A group of 65 Indians undertaking a transformative journey to witness climate impacts firsthand, raising questions about conscious consumption versus experiential learning that Indian middle-class travellers increasingly grapple with.
