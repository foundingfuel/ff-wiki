## Core Argument
India's affordable housing crisis demands ecosystem builders bridging local entrepreneurs' frugality with corporate capital and governance.

## Key Points
- Large conglomerates lack frugal mindset; local operators lack capital and governance—opposite skill gaps create opportunity.
- Government subsidies misdiagnose problem: issue is supply-side constraints, not demand generation.
- Standardized products fail; micro-markets demand localized solutions with local knowledge and empathy.

## Open Question
If local entrepreneurs possess frugality and market knowledge while corporates have capital, why hasn't the market naturally converged on a partnership model that combines both strengths at scale?

## India Angle
India's 5M annual affordable housing deficit vs 500K supply represents the world's largest unmet opportunity, yet large corporates fail while 20,000 local entrepreneurs supply 90%. Success requires bridging frugal local operators with capital and governance—a distinctly Indian systems problem.
