## Core Argument
Creative courage and adaptability transcend external circumstances; institutional rigidity stifles innovation and cultural progress.

## Key Points
- Husain's self-directed path—rejecting formal credentials for hands-on mastery—exemplifies entrepreneurial risk-taking.
- His equanimity about destruction reflects resilience; yet forced exile shows how intolerance constrains creative ecosystems.
- Institutional antagonism to modern art mirrors broader Indian resistance to transformation and forward-thinking ideas.

## Open Question
How can societies protect artistic freedom and enable creative pioneers when majoritarian sentiment views modern expression as threatening or unpatriotic?

## India Angle
MF Husain's exile reflects India's struggle to balance artistic freedom with majoritarian sentiment; mirrors ongoing tensions between modernism and tradition in Indian cultural institutions and governance
