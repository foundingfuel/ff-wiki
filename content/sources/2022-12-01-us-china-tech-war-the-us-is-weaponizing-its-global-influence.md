## Core Argument
US weaponizes global economic structures to contain China's tech rise, reshaping global supply chains and alliances.

## Key Points
- US export controls use extraterritorial reach via the 'foreign product rule' to restrict non-US firms from supplying China
- America lacks economic incentives to build coalitions like it did post-Cold War; global alignment is uncertain
- Supply chain bifurcation will force multinationals like Apple to maintain separate ecosystems for Chinese and non-Chinese markets

## Open Question
The US seeks to contain China through export controls while simultaneously pushing 'America First' policies—unclear what benefits other nations gain from joining this containment without reciprocal economic commitments.

## India Angle
India must strengthen its semiconductor and tech manufacturing capabilities, diversify supply chains away from China dependence, and assert its voice in international tech standards-setting forums to avoid being sidelined in the US-China tech competition.
