## Core Argument
Russia-Ukraine war reshapes global order; institutional arrangements and trust frameworks require fundamental rethinking.

## Key Points
- Ukraine's treaty-protected sovereignty violated by Russia; multilateral security guarantees proven unenforceable by UN permanent members.
- NATO expansion anxiety and Russian insecurity created self-fulfilling prophecy; neutrality options squandered by successive Ukrainian governments.
- War duration and endgame uncertainty make cascading economic, humanitarian, and institutional impacts unpredictable and prolonged.

## Open Question
How can smaller nations secure sovereignty when the UN Security Council's permanent members themselves breach protective treaties, and what institutional alternatives exist?

## India Angle
India must navigate geopolitical realignment; implications for trade, energy security, UN voting patterns, and relationships with both Russia and Western allies. Critical for India's strategic autonomy and economic interests.
