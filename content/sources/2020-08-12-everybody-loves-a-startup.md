## Core Argument
Long entrepreneurial journey requires deep market knowledge, stakeholder conviction, and embracing failure as growth.

## Key Points
- Six-year journey from idea to launch reveals slow, iterative path to building Indian consumer brands.
- Indian manufacturing ecosystem requires custom solutions: fabric sourcing, fit adaptation, relationship-building with Tiruppur suppliers.
- Family skepticism transformed into partnership through tangible product validation and inclusive decision-making process.

## Open Question
How do early-stage founders balance the pressure to move fast with the time required to understand unfamiliar markets and build reliable supplier relationships in India?

## India Angle
Indian direct-to-consumer apparel startup navigating manufacturing challenges in Tiruppur, addressing fit issues specific to Indian body types, and building a consumer-first brand in a rapidly evolving Indian market.
