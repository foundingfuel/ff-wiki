## Core Argument
China's effectiveness stems from meritocratic performance management within Party structures, not monolithic authoritarianism, offering India institutional lessons.

## Key Points
- China's Communist Party operates as internal marketplace of ideas with performance-based career progression through mayoral roles.
- Single measurable goal—economic growth—aligned incentives across leadership, though creating debt and environmental externalities.
- China's financial challenges mirror India's (bad debts, weak debt markets, social safety net strain) despite different political systems.

## Open Question
How can China maintain its growth-at-all-costs model as externalities mount and growth slows while preserving Party monopoly without political liberalization?

## India Angle
India must move beyond the myth that China's authoritarianism enables faster decision-making; understanding China's internal complexity and performance management systems offers lessons for Indian bureaucratic reform without sacrificing democratic principles.
