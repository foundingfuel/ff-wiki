## Core Argument
Recognize all unfair advantages—beauty, intelligence, geography—to optimize human potential matching, not suppress them.

## Key Points
- Life distributes advantages unequally from birth; denying this sets people up for failure rather than success.
- All human endeavour relies on unfair edges—athletic ability, charisma, intellect—yet we selectively judge only some as illegitimate.
- Systems should identify and nurture all natural skills to self-align talent with opportunity, not enforce false equality.

## Open Question
If we openly leverage all unfair advantages including beauty and charisma, how do we prevent these from becoming tools of exploitation or reinforcing historical hierarchies of privilege?

## India Angle
Indian society's democratic ideals often mask inherited inequalities (caste, class, access to education); the article challenges India's need to recognize unfair advantages to design better systems for talent identification and meritocratic sorting rather than pretending equality exists.
