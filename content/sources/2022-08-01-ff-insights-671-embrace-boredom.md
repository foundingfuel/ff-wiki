## Core Argument
Deep focus requires training your brain to tolerate boredom; constant digital relief rewires minds away from concentration.

## Key Points
- Intense concentration is a trainable skill, not motivation—requires hours of practice like physical training.
- Smartphones fill every moment of boredom, preventing mental rewiring needed for deep work and flow.
- Character matters more than intellect for ethical decisions that shape lives and organizations daily.

## Open Question
How can knowledge workers build deep focus practices when institutional and technological systems are designed to maximize distraction and interruption?

## India Angle
Indian workers in construction and manual labor face heat-humidity crises with no institutional support; focus and deep work require mental discipline increasingly difficult amid constant digital distraction—a challenge for Indian knowledge workers and entrepreneurs building high-performance cultures.
