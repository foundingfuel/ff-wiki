## Core Argument
Western dominance erodes; India must master diplomatic ambiguity across fragmenting global order.

## Key Points
- US retreating from multilateralism it created, shrinking gatekeeping power in global institutions
- India's strategic flexibility expands in diffused world, but demands nimble diplomacy avoiding rigid alignments
- China structural headwinds limit its rise; India can offer alternative modernity models in niches like digital

## Open Question
How can India simultaneously strengthen ties with the US (critical for economic transformation) while reducing dependence on US commitment to Indo-Pacific, given Trump's unpredictability?

## India Angle
India must navigate a multipolar world by leveraging US-China rivalry and diffused middle-power influence to expand strategic autonomy, while managing complex relationships with China, Pakistan, Russia, and the US without over-dependence on any single partner or legacy assumptions.
