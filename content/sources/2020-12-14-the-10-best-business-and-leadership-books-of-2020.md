## Core Argument
Leadership authenticity, institutional thinking, and proactive problem-solving define how organisations navigate crises and change.

## Key Points
- Leaders must prioritise institutional interests over personal ego and build transparent, trust-based cultures.
- Systemic failures require governments and institutions to step in; denial and reactive approaches fail in crises.
- Technological displacement demands reimagining work as social-psychological issue, not just economic one.

## Open Question
If machines increasingly replace human work and engagement is already low, how do we design meaningful leisure and dignity-based social structures before technological unemployment becomes widespread?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and organisations can learn from these books about building trust-based cultures, avoiding institutional decline through succession planning, and rethinking work policies post-pandemic—especially relevant as Indian companies scale globally and navigate technological disruption.
