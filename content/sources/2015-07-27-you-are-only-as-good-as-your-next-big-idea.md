## Core Argument
Sustained competitive advantage is dead; companies must exploit transient advantages through rapid iteration and diversification.

## Key Points
- Industry boundaries are dissolving—Alibaba spans e-commerce, finance, entertainment, taxis—making traditional competitive moats obsolete.
- Barriers to entry collapse via technology; companies must constantly hunt new advantage sources or vanish like Vancl.
- Strategic moves: acquire/partner for missing expertise, hire elite talent, encourage experimentation from ground level without losing accountability.

## Open Question
How do companies balance the risky, expensive pursuit of transient advantages in new domains with the need to maintain core business profitability and focus?

## India Angle
Indian tech and e-commerce companies face similar pressures as Chinese peers—they must continuously innovate beyond core business or risk obsolescence. Relying on single competitive advantages in fintech, retail, or ride-hailing is risky; ecosystem expansion and transient advantage strategy are increasingly critical for survival.
