## Core Argument
Happiness measurement is methodologically flawed; regulators must balance corporate power concentration against regulatory overreach.

## Key Points
- Self-reported happiness surveys distorted by context, priming effects, and recent events rather than true well-being
- Big Tech monopolies concentrate power over merchants, developers, and users, but regulatory alternatives risk politicization
- Psychological resilience during crises exceeds expectations; people adapt creatively, though aggregate data masks individual suffering

## Open Question
How can policymakers measure genuine well-being to design better policies when self-reported metrics are inherently unreliable and context-dependent?

## India Angle
India ranked 139 in World Happiness Report; ONDC initiative aims to curb digital monopolies controlled by Big Tech firms; measuring well-being crucial for Indian policymakers designing inclusive development.
