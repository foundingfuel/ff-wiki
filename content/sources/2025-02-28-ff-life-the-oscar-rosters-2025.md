## Core Argument
2025 Oscar slate shows strong filmmaking despite budget constraints, but writing quality remains inconsistent.

## Key Points
- Best Picture contenders include two musicals, two foreign films with varied quality in writing and execution.
- Recurring theme of female protagonists losing grip on reality appears across nominations.
- Budget constraints haven't diminished filmmaking ambition, though narrative depth remains uneven across nominees.

## Open Question
How can films with exceptional production design and performances overcome weak scripts and muddled messaging to achieve lasting cultural impact?
