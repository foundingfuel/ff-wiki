## Core Argument
Lighting quality in video calls shapes how others perceive you and impacts message delivery.

## Key Points
- Facial expressions become primary communication channel when body language is unavailable on video.
- Consistent, soft lighting positioned at face level is achievable through affordable LED lamps and daylight optimization.
- Small setup details—lamp color temperature, angle, second light source—meaningfully improve professional appearance in remote meetings.

## Open Question
How much should professionals invest in home office setup, and does unequal access to lighting solutions create invisible status hierarchies in remote work culture?
