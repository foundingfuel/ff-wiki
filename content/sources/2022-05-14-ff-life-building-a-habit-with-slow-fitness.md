## Core Argument
Slow, mindful fitness built on small daily habits outperforms high-intensity routines for sustainable behavior change.

## Key Points
- Mind-body connection and internal awareness replace external metrics (calories, weights) as motivation
- Compound micro-practices (sukshma abhyaas) accumulate small gains that sustain long-term habit formation
- Functional, holistic movement prevents injury and integrates fitness into daily life beyond class sessions

## Open Question
How do we reconcile the need for measurable progress in fitness with the finding that metrics-driven approaches demotivate and unsustainable behavior?

## India Angle
Resonates with Indian wellness traditions (yoga, martial arts) and challenges Western fitness culture's metrics-obsession; relevant for Indian professionals dealing with sedentary work stress
