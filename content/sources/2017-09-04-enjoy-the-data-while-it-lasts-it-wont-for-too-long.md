## Core Argument
Tech companies now colonise the world through data; Indians are trapped in algorithmic systems that reshape thought.

## Key Points
- Seven of world's top 10 companies are now technology-driven, replacing traditional energy and industrial firms.
- Telecom operators and tech platforms wage war for user data, irrespective of profitability, suggesting data's hidden value.
- Algorithms create 'filter bubbles'—personalised information universes that alter how individuals encounter ideas and form opinions.

## Open Question
If data-driven tech companies are now more valuable than all traditional industries combined, what is the actual source of their value—and who truly owns and benefits from the data Indians generate?

## India Angle
India's massive telecom market and billions of new internet users are becoming the battleground for tech giants and incumbents. The Supreme Court's privacy ruling comes as companies harvest Indian data through algorithms Indians don't understand, with implications for a digitally vulnerable population.
