## Core Argument
China's strategic 5G investment and standards-setting could reshape global technology leadership and competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- 5G will generate $12 trillion economic output globally by 2035 and enable IoT, autonomous vehicles, and smart cities.
- China invested heavily in 5G coordination (IMT-2020 group, Made in China 2025) to avoid being a follower like in 3G/4G transitions.
- Huawei and ZTE's technological breakthroughs position China to shape global 5G standards, protecting billions in R&D investment.

## Open Question
How can countries that aren't standards-setters protect their technological sovereignty and avoid dependency on others' 5G infrastructure and standards?

## India Angle
India's telecom infrastructure and 5G adoption will be shaped by global standards set by China, US, and Europe. Indian operators (Jio, Airtel, Vodafone) face pressure to adopt dominant standards; India risks being a follower rather than shaper of 5G ecosystem, impacting IoT, manufacturing competitiveness, and job creation.
