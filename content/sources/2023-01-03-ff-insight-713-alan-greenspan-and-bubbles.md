## Core Argument
Systemic failures occur when short-term costs of correction exceed political will to act.

## Key Points
- Greenspan ignored asset bubble warnings because fixing them required Congressional mandate changes and political reshaping
- Pharmaceutical regulation bill weakens standards under industry pressure, lowering active ingredient thresholds from 90-110% to 70%
- Indian youth exploit unvetted platforms (IMDb, Google algorithms) to fabricate eminence through coordinated false credentials

## Open Question
How do institutions balance necessary short-term stability against long-term systemic risk when the costs of prevention are politically unbearable?

## India Angle
India's pharmaceutical regulation faces weakening standards through proposed bill decriminalization; regulatory capture risks mirror global bubble-blindness but in critical health sector. Also highlights India's unique digital gaming-the-system hustle economy.
