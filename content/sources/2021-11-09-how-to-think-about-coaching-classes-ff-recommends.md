## Core Argument
Hybrid schooling demands rethink of coaching model; personalized remote tutoring from Tier II cities emerges as viable alternative.

## Key Points
- Post-COVID hybrid learning breaks traditional school-coaching binary; students need differentiated support based on career clarity.
- Qualified teachers in Tier II cities now serve urban markets via online tutoring, unbounded by geography; NCERT alignment enables scalability.
- One-to-one remote tutoring (₹5,000+/subject/month) offers psychological comfort, syllabus clarity, and measurable confidence gains vs. mass coaching.

## Open Question
How do parents balance the psychological and academic benefits of personalized tutoring against its high cost and long-term sustainability in middle-income Indian households?
