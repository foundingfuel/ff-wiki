## Core Argument
Healthy boundaries in work and life require intentional design, but digital contexts and organizational silos complicate enforcement.

## Key Points
- Boundaries across work modes (creating, curating, communicating) boost focus and personal mastery.
- Mission Gati Shakti reveals how institutional silos between 18 ministries undermine infrastructure coordination despite top-level commitment.
- Remote/hybrid teams paradoxically increase loneliness; psychological safety and smaller decision-making units may help reconnect dispersed workforces.

## Open Question
How do organizations maintain boundaries and autonomy while breaking down silos needed for coordination—without recreating fragmentation elsewhere?

## India Angle
Applies globally, but Indian context surfaces in Mission Gati Shakti example—shows how boundaries and silos between ministries hamper infrastructure coordination, a critical challenge for India's development and organizational effectiveness.
