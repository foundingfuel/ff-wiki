## Core Argument
Geopolitical realignment around US-China rivalry and democratic-autocratic divide reshapes global order.

## Key Points
- Biden restored US multilateralism and Western alliance cohesion while attempting to contain China through diplomatic pressure.
- US-Russia achieved nuclear arms control dialogue breakthroughs despite tensions, reducing existential risk from weapons escalation.
- Israel formed coalition government excluding Netanyahu, proving alternatives to entrenched leadership exist through pragmatic coalition-building.

## Open Question
