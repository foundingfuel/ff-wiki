## Core Argument
Global credentials blind returnees to India's complexity; ground experience matters more than parachuted expertise.

## Key Points
- India operates as three distinct economies (India One, Two, Three) with different income levels and dynamics.
- International credentials create cognitive blind spots about local realities and systemic inequality within India.
- Invisible hierarchies exist even within India One; wealth distance creates inability to see those serving.

## Open Question
How can global expertise meaningfully contribute to India if the very context-blindness that comes with international success prevents understanding of local realities?

## India Angle
Indian leaders and returnees must understand India's fragmented realities—three distinct economies with different contexts—rather than imposing global frameworks. Local knowledge and ground experience matter as much as international credentials.
