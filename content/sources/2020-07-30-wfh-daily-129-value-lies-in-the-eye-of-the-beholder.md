## Core Argument
Value and perception are subjective; context shapes how we evaluate what's real versus fake.

## Key Points
- Perception shapes reality more than objective facts—a real diamond appears fake in metro, fake appears real on celebrities
- WFH disrupts traditional workplace hierarchies and visibility cues that signal status and value
- Tech's systemic importance amplifies both society's problems and human instincts when connecting everyone

## Open Question
If value and perception are entirely subjective, how do organizations maintain meritocratic evaluation systems in remote-work environments where traditional status cues disappear?
