## Core Argument
India must reframe AI strategy from productivity tools to scientific discovery and national security capabilities.

## Key Points
- US and China prioritizing AI for discovery science and strategic capability, not just consumer applications
- India's space, atomic energy, and vaccine achievements provide foundation for responsible AI-led scientific breakthroughs
- Advanced AI poses strategic risks—biosecurity, cyber resilience, decision-support systems—requiring national security assessments

## Open Question
How can India balance global AI collaboration and talent mobility with the need for sovereign control over strategically sensitive AI systems and knowledge?

## India Angle
India must shift from consumer AI applications to advanced AI for scientific discovery and national security, leveraging its track record in space, atomic energy, and vaccines while developing sovereign AI capabilities and governance frameworks aligned with strategic autonomy.
