## Core Argument
Great teachers transform through belief and reflection, not content delivery—a lesson for India's education system.

## Key Points
- Teacher belief in students creates self-confidence that drives commitment and excellence beyond academics.
- Observing character and values (neat stack of papers, questioning assumptions) seeds long-term integrity.
- Questioning and unraveling assumptions matters more than transmitting knowledge—builds thinking capability.

## Open Question
If great teaching is about belief, observation, and questioning rather than content, how do systems scale this approach across classrooms where teacher-student ratios remain high and selection pressures demand standardized outputs?

## India Angle
Indian education system often focuses on rote learning and credentials; these stories highlight how transformative teaching—rooted in belief, observation, and questioning—can reshape trajectories, particularly relevant for IIT-JEE coaching culture and institutional learning at places like IIM-A.
