## Core Argument
Crypto's value lies in solving coordination problems in finance, not mathematical complexity.

## Key Points
- Finance fundamentally moves money through time; individuals and economies need matching supply-demand pairs.
- Traditional finance bottlenecked by coordination costs and trust requirements in finding counterparties.
- Web3 technologies (trust, composability, permissionless innovation) offer alternative infrastructure to legacy financial systems.

## Open Question
If crypto solves coordination and trust problems, why do regulatory bodies like RBI reject it, and how do these competing visions of financial infrastructure coexist?

## India Angle
India's crypto taxation policy (30%), RBI's stance on legality, and applicability to India's large unbanked/seasonal income populations (farmers, wedding photographers) who need alternative financial infrastructure.
