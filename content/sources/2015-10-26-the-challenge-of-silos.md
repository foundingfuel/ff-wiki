## Core Argument
Organizational silos enable specialization but inhibit information flow, cross-functional problem-solving, and data analytics effectiveness.

## Key Points
- Specialization creates efficiency but silos fragment information flows, slow decisions, and breed inter-departmental competition.
- Organizations like Cleveland Clinic and Facebook show silos can be managed through restructuring, job rotation, and intentional cross-boundary collaboration.
- India's bureaucratic silos prevent integrated data analytics and holistic problem-solving on critical national challenges.

## Open Question
How can large organizations maintain the efficiency benefits of specialization while breaking down silos enough to enable integrated problem-solving and data analytics?

## India Angle
India's government agencies and state governments struggle with silos, hampering single-window clearance systems and preventing integrated use of big data for solving critical problems—a competitive disadvantage as the country pursues digital transformation.
