## Core Argument
Indian motorcycle industry achieved global dominance through self-reliance and indigenous capability-building after breaking foreign partnerships.

## Key Points
- Three Indian OEMs (Hero, Bajaj, TVS) control 77% of world's largest motorcycle market, outperforming foreign competitors.
- Post-JV splits, Indian companies invested heavily in R&D, vendor development, and manufacturing excellence; Hero cut royalties 88% while growing sales 50%.
- Deep consumer understanding, supply chain mastery (TVS won Deming Award 2002), and import intensity under 5% demonstrate true self-reliance.

## Open Question
If motorcycle industry is the role model for Atmanirbhar Bharat, why haven't other Indian industries replicated this success—is it industry-specific, or a failure of strategy and policy elsewhere?

## India Angle
Indian motorcycle industry demonstrates Atmanirbhar Bharat model—three domestic OEMs now dominate 77% of world's largest motorcycle market through self-reliance, vendor ecosystems, and indigenous R&D post-JV dissolution.
