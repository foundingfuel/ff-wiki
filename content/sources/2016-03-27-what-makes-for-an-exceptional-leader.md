## Core Argument
Exceptional leaders make tough decisions for team success, challenge themselves relentlessly, and build lasting systems over personal glory.

## Key Points
- Decisiveness requires making unpopular choices (like dropping Warne) when it serves long-term team mission and institutional continuity.
- Leaders must challenge their own limitations and push teams toward continuous improvement rather than short-term wins or popularity.
- Exceptional leaders build systems and culture that outlive them; success is measured by what team accomplishes after they're gone.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the immediate organizational need for decisiveness and unpopular calls with building trust and morale among talented but difficult team members?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders can adopt Waugh's model of decisive decision-making and long-term team building over popularity, particularly relevant in hierarchical Indian corporate contexts where tough calls often get deferred.
