## Core Argument
Film industry is bifurcating into two speeds while streaming redefines prestige and auteurs gain budgetary power.

## Key Points
- Streaming studios are strategically gaming awards by qualifying films theatrically while leveraging prestige for platform legitimacy.
- Nominated films operate at two extremes: hyperkinetic, genre-bending spectacles or meditative, emotionally introspective pieces—a response to competing with streaming's pacing.
- Big budgets ($100-175mn) now fund visionary auteur films instead of superhero franchises, giving directors creative freedom previously unavailable.

## Open Question
If audiences are trained by streaming's frenetic pacing, why are meditative slow films competing successfully for awards—and what does this suggest about who actually controls taste-making (critics, academy, or audiences)?

## India Angle
Indian filmmakers and streaming platforms (like those emerging domestically) can observe how global streamers are cracking awards legitimacy and how budget allocation is shifting away from spectacle-driven content toward vision-driven auteur films—relevant for content strategy and positioning.
