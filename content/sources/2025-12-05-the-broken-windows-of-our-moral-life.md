## Core Argument
Moral decline spreads like broken windows—small unchecked violations normalise decay across society and organisations.

## Key Points
- Individual moral convenience has replaced collective morality; rules are optional if authorities aren't watching.
- Broken Windows Theory applies to both streets and offices—one violation normalises decay across entire systems.
- Rebuilding requires dialogue, humility, and viewing personal behaviour as reflecting on the whole community.

## Open Question
How can societies rebuild collective moral standards when the infrastructure for shared conversation and accountability has already eroded, and individual incentives reward rule-breaking over compliance?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and public life suffer from a crisis of collective moral standards, where individual convenience has replaced shared ethics. From traffic violations to workplace misconduct, the 'broken windows' phenomenon reflects India's struggle to build institutional integrity and civic responsibility at scale.
