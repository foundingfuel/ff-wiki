## Core Argument
Digital nomad programs create economic opportunities for cities by attracting globally mobile, high-earning remote workers.

## Key Points
- 40+ countries now offer digital nomad visas with tax exemptions to attract remote workers and their spending.
- Tulsa Remote generated $14 in economic activity per $1 spent, proving ROI of talent attraction programs.
- Companies using WFA can sponsor workers for nomad programs, workcations, or international offsite hubs like Cisco's Venywhere.

## Open Question
How can smaller towns and developing regions compete with established digital nomad hubs without creating unsustainable cost inflation or dependency on transient populations who may not embed long-term?

## India Angle
India risks losing talent to countries offering digital nomad visas and WFA incentives. Indian cities and states could adopt similar programs to attract remote workers globally and retain talent, while also addressing brain drain in smaller cities.
