## Core Argument
Design products around willingness-to-pay early, not price later, to reduce innovation failure rates.

## Key Points
- 72% product failure rate stems from flawed process design, not market unpredictability or inherent risk.
- Willingness-to-pay research at inception reveals which features matter, preventing costly misfits.
- Success depends on value-cost-price alignment, not post-hoc pricing of misaligned products.

## Open Question
If customer willingness-to-pay varies by segment and context, how do companies systematically explore those variations early without paralysing the innovation process?

## India Angle
Indian companies often replicate Western innovation models without validating willingness-to-pay with customers first; this framework directly addresses the 76% failure rate in Asia by embedding customer economics into product design from inception.
