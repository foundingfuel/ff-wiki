## Core Argument
Leaders must allow team mistakes to build capability; selection for optimism and problem-solving matters more than just competence.

## Key Points
- Preventing team mistakes disempowers individuals and reduces bold action; contingency planning makes mistake tolerance viable.
- Optimal teams combine physical capability with psychological resilience—optimism, problem-solving mindset, and fear tolerance drive success.
- The Big Four framework (goal-setting, mental rehearsal, self-talk, breathing) builds stress resilience and moves decision-making from panic to clarity.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance creating a psychologically safe environment for mistakes with maintaining accountability and performance standards in resource-constrained or high-stakes environments?

## India Angle
Indian corporate leaders often over-manage and under-delegate due to hierarchical norms; adopting mistake-tolerant cultures requires mindset shift from command-control to trust-based leadership, critical for scaling organizations and retaining talent.
