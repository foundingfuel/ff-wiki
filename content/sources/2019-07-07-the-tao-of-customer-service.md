## Core Argument
Prevent problems through system design, not solve them faster through technology or process optimization.

## Key Points
- Measure customer service by problem prevention, not response speed—feedback loops between teams matter most.
- Managers should anticipate and prevent problems early rather than react to crises after they occur.
- Technology (automation, bots) can distract from the core customer need—speed of resolution isn't the real job.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance investing in preventive system design against the immediate pressure to demonstrate visible customer service efficiency metrics?

## India Angle
Indian startups like ShopX demonstrate how founders can build lean, efficient operations by focusing on root-cause prevention rather than reactive customer service—a model relevant for India's resource-constrained startup ecosystem.
