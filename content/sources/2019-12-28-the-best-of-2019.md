## Core Argument
India's 2019 reveals technology's dual nature and demands leaders rethink purpose, ethics, and human-centric innovation.

## Key Points
- Corporate upheavals like Chanda Kochhar's fall expose mission drift and faulty success measures in leadership.
- Technology simultaneously destroys jobs and lifts nations from poverty; context determines outcome, not tech itself.
- Platform businesses reshape markets by exploiting regulation gaps; incumbents need strategic rethinking, not reactive responses.

## Open Question
If technology is context-dependent for India's growth, how should entrepreneurs and policymakers decide which tech investments truly serve development versus extractive platform economics?

## India Angle
India's 2019 landscape shaped by technology adoption among youth, political narratives, and the dual impact of tech on jobs and poverty—critical for Indian business leaders navigating disruption and purpose-driven innovation.
