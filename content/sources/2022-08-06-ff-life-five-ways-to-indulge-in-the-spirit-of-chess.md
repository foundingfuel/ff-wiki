## Core Argument
Chess transcends sport: a cultural artifact teaching strategy, raising questions about human-machine relations, inspiring art.

## Key Points
- Chess principles like strategic thinking and questioning assumptions apply beyond the board to business and life.
- Human-AI competition in chess reveals our complex relationship with technology and raises questions about replacement versus augmentation.
- Chess has inspired artists, filmmakers, and novelists to explore deeper themes about culture, politics, and human struggle.

## Open Question
Can chess principles transcribe meaningfully to complex real-world scenarios, or are we overfitting strategy lessons from a bounded game to unbounded life?

## India Angle
Chess Olympiad hosted in India after Putin's invasion redirects global attention to Indian chess heritage; Chennai as epicenter of Indian grandmasters showcases India's cultural and intellectual capital in competitive sport.
