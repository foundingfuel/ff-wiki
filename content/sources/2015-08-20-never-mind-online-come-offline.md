## Core Argument
Indian e-commerce's gold lies offline: hyper-local platforms enabling traditional retailers outperform pure-play online retailers.

## Key Points
- Online retailers face low customer retention, thin margins despite $6B+ spending on 40M customers
- Hyper-local platforms (Grofers, Peppertap) leveraging offline retailers' physical presence are the real opportunity
- Offline retail penetration at 8% in India creates massive gap; Tier 2/3 consumers prefer accessible digital-enabled local shopping

## Open Question
If hyper-local platforms succeed by enabling offline retailers, won't they face the same unit economics and customer acquisition cost challenges as pure online players, or does their lower burn model fundamentally change the equation?

## India Angle
Indian e-commerce has spent $6B acquiring 40M customers with low retention; real value lies in offline retailers and hyper-local logistics platforms that serve under-penetrated Tier 2/3 markets where organized retail captures only 8% of the market.
