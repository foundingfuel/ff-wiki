## Core Argument
Purpose and profit are not opposing forces; purpose-driven enterprises create sustainable value for multiple stakeholders.

## Key Points
- Forus Health founders solved preventable blindness while serving poor patients and generating investor returns.
- Successful innovation requires iterative development, customer feedback, and clear principles beyond profit maximization.
- Missionaries (purpose-driven leaders) outperform mercenaries in long-term ventures requiring sustained commitment.

## Open Question
How can entrepreneurs simultaneously maximize shareholder returns and serve low-income customers without subsidies or compromising on profitability?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs must recognize that purpose-driven ventures (like Forus Health solving preventable blindness) can achieve both social impact and profitability, essential for scaling solutions to India's healthcare and development challenges without relying solely on government or NGOs.
