## Core Argument
Instant gratification expectation rewires behaviour; patience and delayed outcomes now offer competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Technology speed expectations cascade into impatience across life domains, reducing patience for complex, long-term endeavours.
- Most fake news readers also seek mainstream sources; echo chambers are shallow, suggesting curiosity survives hyperconnection.
- Developer-creators treat side projects as portfolio experiments (launching often) rather than betting-the-farm, increasing odds of breakthrough.

## Open Question
How do we harness technology's speed benefits while cultivating the patience required for meaningful innovation and relationships?

## India Angle
Indian startups and developers face pressure to deliver faster (10-minute deliveries like Blinkit), but this mirrors global instant-gratification trends that undermine patience-based excellence; Indian creator economy workers could benefit from understanding randomness and portfolio approaches rather than seeking guaranteed outcomes.
