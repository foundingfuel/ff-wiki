## Core Argument
Leaders must systematically catalog stories by extracting key elements and tagging them for reliable retrieval in presentations.

## Key Points
- Stories are memorable but retrieval fails without deliberate cataloging systems; tagging and notes apps solve this.
- Extract only essential story elements; non-critical details can be forgotten without losing the core message.
- Discuss stories with colleagues to discover multiple interpretations and create richer, more versatile tags for future use.

## Open Question
How can leaders balance the spontaneous, authentic use of stories on-the-fly with the mechanical overhead of cataloging and tagging, without sacrificing either effectiveness or narrative authenticity?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and managers can adopt structured storytelling practices to enhance executive presence and influence—critical in hierarchical organizational cultures where narrative authority drives decision-making and mentorship.
