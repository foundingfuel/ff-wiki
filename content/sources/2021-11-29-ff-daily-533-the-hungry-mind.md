## Core Argument
Curiosity and conscientiousness rival intelligence in predicting academic and professional success.

## Key Points
- Personality traits (conscientiousness, grit) account for 4x more exam variance than IQ in elite students.
- Intellectual curiosity—seeking effortful cognitive activity—has comparable impact to conscientiousness on achievement.
- Data science tools democratize technical fields, enabling diverse talent to succeed through soft skills and storytelling ability.

## Open Question
If curiosity and conscientiousness are learnable traits, why does education systems still focus on IQ-based selection and streaming rather than cultivating hungry minds?

## India Angle
Indian education system and workforce must recognize that curiosity and conscientiousness matter as much as raw intelligence—critical for competing globally while addressing India's learning outcomes crisis beyond exam scores.
