## Core Argument
Grand challenges model drives technological disruption by mobilizing entrepreneurs, universities, and government to solve near-future problems innovatively.

## Key Points
- Grand challenges leverage collaborative ecosystems—private sector, universities, government—to solve complex problems at scale.
- Autonomous vehicles, spectrum optimization, and space tech show how decade-old challenges become today's market disruptors.
- India must adopt mission-driven R&D in dark matter, genomics, energy storage, and cyber-physical systems to become knowledge producer.

## Open Question
How can India ensure grand challenges drive sustainable domestic innovation capacity rather than merely chase external funding and prizes?

## India Angle
India must adopt mission-mode R&D involving private sector, universities, and entrepreneurs to transition from net consumer to net producer of knowledge. Grand challenges in spectrum, energy, agriculture, and genomics can unlock indigenous innovation and disrupt global markets.
