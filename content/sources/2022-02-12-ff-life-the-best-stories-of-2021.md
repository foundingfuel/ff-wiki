## Core Argument
Reflection on 2021's best stories reveals India's emerging geopolitical and digital influence amid global uncertainty.

## Key Points
- Russia's outsized global influence despite economic weakness challenges assumptions about power and relevance.
- Indian media's evolution reflects advertiser-driven business models reaching limits; institutional sustainability requires rethinking.
- India's institutional builders prioritized long-term mission over personal glory, creating world-class institutions with scarce resources.

## Open Question
How can India maintain institutional integrity and inclusive digital governance while facing pressure to monetize and compete with centralized models like China's?
