## Core Argument
Blended commerce success depends on organizational agility and cultural transformation, not technology alone.

## Key Points
- Retailers must become nimble disruptors of their own operating models to serve digital-first customers effectively.
- Technology is an enabler; excessive complexity and choice paradox can alienate customers seeking simple conveniences.
- Moving from data-rich to data-driven requires cultural shift: digital champions, younger talent, and removal of decision-making bottlenecks.

## Open Question
How can legacy retailers balance technology deployment with customer simplicity when their core constraint is supply chain efficiency, not innovation capacity?

## India Angle
Indian retailers must transform legacy operations to compete in blended commerce; supply chain inefficiencies and organizational inertia are bigger barriers than technology itself for Indian companies versus global peers.
