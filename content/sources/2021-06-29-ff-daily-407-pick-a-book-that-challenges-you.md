## Core Argument
Challenge yourself with difficult books and purposeful work to develop skills; stretch beyond comfort zones.

## Key Points
- Reading only comfortable material entertains but doesn't improve reading ability or critical thinking.
- Organizations need people who can design experiments, interpret data, and lead culture change simultaneously.
- Poorly organized meetings waste time and create confusion; clarity of objective and attendees matters most.

## Open Question
How do organizations systematically develop the rare 'person who can do it all'—designer, analyst, change agent, and visionary—when most curricula and training programs remain siloed?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers and organizations need to build a culture of deliberate learning and purposeful work—moving beyond rote information consumption to develop critical thinking skills essential for competing in a knowledge economy.
