## Core Argument
Purpose-driven networks, not large organizations, solve complex systemic problems at population scale.

## Key Points
- Systemic social problems require customized local solutions; one-size-fits-all approaches fail.
- Networked organizations balance movement energy with organizational structure better than rigid hierarchies.
- Collective impact requires coordination around shared goals, not centralized command-and-control authority.

## Open Question
How do purpose-driven networks maintain voluntary commitment and alignment without developing the hierarchical structures that reduce their flexibility and adaptive capacity?

## India Angle
India's scale and complexity demand networked solutions rather than large monolithic organizations; collective impact models are critical for achieving SDGs across 1.4B people with diverse local contexts.
