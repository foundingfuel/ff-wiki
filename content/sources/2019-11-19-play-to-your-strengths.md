## Core Argument
Organizations succeed at transformation by leveraging insider knowledge over one-size-fits-all consultant solutions.

## Key Points
- Inside consultants with operational intimacy drive better transformation outcomes than external advisors applying generic best practices.
- Career development for internal change leaders requires breadth across roles, continuous learning, and strategic mobility decisions.
- Transformation requires right mindset and organizational practices, not just technical expertise or external resources.

## Open Question
How can organizations retain and develop talented insiders as transformation agents when external consulting careers offer higher compensation and prestige?

## India Angle
Indian firms often rely heavily on external consultants for transformation; building internal capability and leveraging insider knowledge is particularly critical for Indian organizations navigating digital and structural change with limited resources.
