## Core Argument
Resource-constrained startups can outcompete war-chest-funded competitors through focus, quality, agility, and always-on execution.

## Key Points
- Focus on one core value proposition; reject feature creep despite attractive technology options.
- Quality over quantity: fewer, higher-impact content pieces generate better engagement and organic reach.
- Agile organization with decentralized decisions, flexible hiring, and real-time analytics drive competitive advantage.

## Open Question
How do resource-constrained teams sustain the psychological and operational demands of 24/7 real-time execution without burnout while maintaining quality?

## India Angle
Indian startups with limited funding can compete against well-funded incumbents by focusing on strategic execution, content quality, and real-time adaptation—directly relevant to India's resource-constrained entrepreneurial ecosystem.
