## Core Argument
Incumbents must self-disrupt with superior solutions before competitors destroy their business models.

## Key Points
- Consumer behavior shift: screens must come to consumers, not vice versa; mobile dominates (900M screens vs 170M TV).
- Content creation democratized: technology barriers removed; everyone can now create and publish content.
- Leadership challenge: guide organizations through cannibalization of existing business models for survival.

## Open Question
How can legacy organizations overcome organizational inertia and short-term earnings pressure to cannibalize profitable existing businesses before disruption forces them to?

## India Angle
Indian incumbents (Star India, Future Group, retail) face disruption from digital-first competitors; need proactive self-disruption strategy to remain competitive against Paytm, Uber models.
