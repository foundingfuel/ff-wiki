## Core Argument
Multi-layered alliances now shape geopolitics more than ideology; economies cannot afford hard decoupling despite geopolitical tensions.

## Key Points
- China-Russia economic integration deepens China's geopolitical leverage while maintaining Moscow's fierce independence.
- ASEAN, India, and other regions must balance competing powers to sustain trade and growth without choosing hard blocs.
- Europe cannot decouple from China despite economic tensions; protectionism offers limited solutions to overcapacity problems.

## Open Question
How can nations maintain geopolitical independence and security interests when deep economic entanglement with rivals makes ideological alignment with allies economically unsustainable?

## India Angle
India faces a critical strategic balancing act between China and the US-led alliance, requiring careful navigation of trade relationships while managing geopolitical pressures in the Indo-Pacific region without hard decoupling from either bloc.
