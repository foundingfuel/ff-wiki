## Core Argument
Breakthrough ideas emerge by seeking new contexts and perspectives outside your own problem space.

## Key Points
- Immersive learning in unfamiliar contexts generates empathy and new problem-solving lenses.
- Adding more resources without reflection often leads to suboptimal outcomes; reframing matters more.
- Innovation happens at intersections where diverse ideas, people, and cultures collide and combine.

## Open Question
How do organisations systematically create and sustain the time and psychological safety needed for cross-context learning when immediate operational pressures demand more of the same?

## India Angle
Indian businesses entering competitive markets (e.g., Renault Kwid) and leaders need cross-context learning and diverse perspectives to innovate beyond traditional constraints and resource-heavy approaches.
