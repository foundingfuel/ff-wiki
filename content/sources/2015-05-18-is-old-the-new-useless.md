## Core Argument
Age bias in tech perpetuates myth that innovation requires youth; experienced founders consistently build transformative companies.

## Key Points
- McDonald's (52), Coca-Cola (55), KFC (65) and tech founders like Workday's David Duffield (64) prove mature founders build billion-dollar businesses.
- Steve Jobs' greatest innovations—iMac, iPod, iPhone, iPad—came after 45, contradicting mythology around young disruption.
- Experienced leaders self-eliminate from opportunities due to internalized age bias, depriving organizations of judgment, wisdom and balance youth alone lacks.

## Open Question
If experience and wisdom are demonstrably valuable for building enduring companies, why does the startup ecosystem continue rewarding youth over maturity, and what would it take to genuinely shift this cultural belief?

## India Angle
India's cultural tradition celebrates wisdom across ages, yet startup ecosystem mimics Silicon Valley's youth bias, creating self-limiting beliefs among experienced leaders and missing talent pools critical for building sustainable businesses.
