## Core Argument
In a mutating world, answers lie at intersections of diverse disciplines and stakeholder perspectives, not silos.

## Key Points
- Change is now mutation, not evolution—unprecedented and requiring admission that traditional answers fail.
- Facilitated conversations between government, business, social sectors reveal common ground across conflicting worldviews.
- Multi-disciplinary thinking and ninth-path innovation solve problems no single lens or pre-defined approach can address.

## Open Question
If the best answers lie at intersections of worldviews, how do facilitators remain neutral enough to help stakeholders genuinely examine issues beyond their own prisms?

## India Angle
Indian organisations and government must adopt multi-stakeholder facilitated conversations to solve complex problems like open defecation, where divergent worldviews prevent alignment despite shared intent.
