## Core Argument
CEOs must lead cultural transformation on sexual harassment through open conversations, not policy alone.

## Key Points
- Culture shapes proactive behaviour through peer norms; compliance only addresses violations after they occur.
- Leadership credibility depends on transparent handling of harassment cases and public accountability of offenders.
- Women distrust due process because organizations misuse it to protect powerful men rather than find truth.

## Open Question
How can organizations balance due process protections with the #metoo movement's demand for implicit trust in accusers, especially when leadership has repeatedly failed women?

## India Angle
Indian CEOs and business leaders must visibly champion harassment-free workplaces through culture, not just compliance; high-profile cases like Phaneesh Murthy and RK Pachauri show how Indian organizations have failed women despite public ethics claims.
