## Core Argument
Mesh networks solve home connectivity gaps better than range extenders, enabling seamless hybrid work in Indian homes.

## Key Points
- Mesh networks provide automatic switching and high-speed coverage without manual network changes, unlike range extenders.
- Guest network isolation on mesh systems solves privacy concerns when sharing Wi-Fi with visitors in shared homes.
- Home layout, not just size, determines mesh purchase decisions; Indian homes' spread layouts require multi-unit configurations.

## Open Question
How do consumers balance future-proofing (Wi-Fi 6 compatibility) against affordability when imported mesh systems cost 2x more in India than globally?

## India Angle
Indian homes and hybrid work setups face Wi-Fi dead zones; mesh networks are gaining relevance as affordable solutions (₹5,000-₹80,000+) become accessible, though buying decisions remain complex due to layout variability and import pricing disparities.
