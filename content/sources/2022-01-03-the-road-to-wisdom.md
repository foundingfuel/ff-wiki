## Core Argument
Wisdom emerges through iterative error-correction; nonprofits innovating India's social safety net deserve venture-grade capital.

## Key Points
- Nonprofits like SNEHA and Pratham Books filled critical gaps pandemic created for India's poorest while VCs funded startups generously.
- Pandemic closed schools for 168M+ children globally; unrestricted grants and risk capital remain scarce for social sector.
- Wisdom comes from repeated, diminishing errors; infinite game mindset yields joyful, sustainable approaches versus finite winning obsession.

## Open Question
Why does venture capital flow freely to startups but nonprofits addressing existential social crises struggle for unrestricted funding—what does this reveal about our values?
