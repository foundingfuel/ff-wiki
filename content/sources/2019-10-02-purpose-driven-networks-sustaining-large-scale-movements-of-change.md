## Core Argument
Purpose-driven networked organizations sustain large-scale change better than rigid hierarchies or ephemeral movements.

## Key Points
- SDG challenges are complex, interconnected problems requiring customized local solutions, not standardized products from large organizations.
- Movements generate energy for change but dissipate post-victory; networks offer structure with flexibility needed for sustained transformation.
- India's pilot projects fail to scale because societal issues are systemic and interdependent—cannot be solved piecemeal or in silos.

## Open Question
How do purpose-driven networks maintain coherence and momentum without formal hierarchy, while ensuring accountability and preventing capture by external parties?

## India Angle
India's 'graveyard of successful pilots' problem: inability to scale development solutions beyond micro-level. Illustrates how systems thinking and networked organizations can address interconnected societal challenges (health, water, caste, gender) that business-style scaling cannot solve.
