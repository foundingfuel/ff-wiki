## Core Argument
2022 marked geopolitics trumping economics, exposing cracks in global order and opening new opportunities.

## Key Points
- Friend-shoring reshapes global trade away from open systems toward geopolitically-aligned blocs, creating supply chain opportunities for India.
- Xi's sudden Covid-policy reversal revealed limits of authoritarian control and exposed the party-state's disconnect from public sentiment.
- Collapse of statesmanship across major powers prioritizes recklessness over cooperation, yet nuclear fusion breakthrough offers climate hope.

## Open Question
How can India capitalize on supply chain shifts from China while maintaining strategic autonomy and avoiding forced geopolitical alignment?

## India Angle
India faces pressure to choose geopolitical sides amid friend-shoring but has opportunity in supply chain relocation from China; must balance middle-ground diplomacy with manufacturing ambitions while avoiding protectionism.
