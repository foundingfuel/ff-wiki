## Core Argument
Liberal institutions are pragmatic tools for managing diversity; abandoning them invites violent conflict.

## Key Points
- Liberalism emerged as a system to peacefully manage religious and ideological diversity after 150-year European wars.
- Core principle: tolerance means citizens need not agree on ultimate values but must respect individual autonomy.
- Diverse societies moving toward ethnic/religious nationalism risk returning to violent conflict—relevant to India and US alike.

## Open Question
If liberal tolerance must have limits (when tolerance itself is challenged), who decides where those limits lie, and doesn't that decision itself require a shared value system?

## India Angle
India, like the US, is a diverse pluralistic society where liberal values of tolerance and institutional frameworks for managing diversity are critical to preventing religious/communal violence. The article warns that abandoning liberal principles in favor of ethnic or religious nationalism poses direct risks to India's stability.
