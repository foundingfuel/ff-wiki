## Core Argument
Well-intentioned price controls often backfire, pricing out vulnerable borrowers from credit markets entirely.

## Key Points
- Price caps on interest rates eliminate access rather than affordability for marginal borrowers lacking collateral.
- India's MSMEs depend on shadow credit when formal banking denies them; regulation must balance protection with access.
- New models—messaging commerce via WhatsApp, AI credit algorithms—could democratize financing if regulatory barriers fall.

## Open Question
How can regulators protect borrowers from exploitation without eliminating the informal credit lifeline that millions of MSMEs depend on for survival?

## India Angle
India's MSME sector faces dual challenges: limited access to formal credit and regulatory interventions (like price controls) that may worsen access. Price caps on shadow banking could push small businesses further into informal channels, while messaging commerce and AI-driven credit assessment offer new pathways for MSME financing and growth.
