## Core Argument
Leadership in crisis requires systems thinking, digital innovation, and localism as competitive advantage.

## Key Points
- Systems and business discipline matter as much as disruption; old economy and new economy need integration.
- Digital transformation succeeds when teaching/content quality remains central; online reach scales differently from in-person.
- Decentralization and autopilot systems enable crisis leadership; individuals fine-tune, systems execute.

## Open Question
How do bootstrapped, localist businesses like Zoho compete globally while maintaining values, versus VC-funded unicorns optimizing for scale?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders can learn from global best practices (Savio Kwan, Roger Martin) while understanding localism as competitive advantage (Zoho's Sridhar Vembu); Mumbai's pandemic response shows how Indian public institutions can innovate through decentralization and systems thinking.
