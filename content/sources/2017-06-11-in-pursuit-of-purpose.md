## Core Argument
Purpose-driven missions sustain entrepreneurs through crises more effectively than profit motives alone.

## Key Points
- Phil Knight built Nike on equipping athletes, not profit—this core mission sustained the team through financial instability.
- Money is necessary but not sufficient; treating purpose as secondary leads to unhappy, unmotivated work lives.
- The future workplace values belonging and meaning; gig economies must deliver purpose alongside flexibility to retain talent.

## Open Question
How do early-stage startups in resource-constrained markets (like India) balance immediate survival needs with building larger purpose-driven cultures?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and large company leaders must recognize that sustainable success requires purpose beyond profit. This applies to founders building new ventures and intrapreneurs in established firms navigating India's complex business environment.
