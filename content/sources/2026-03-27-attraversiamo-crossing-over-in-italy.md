## Core Argument
Travel as deliberate surrender collapses imagined worlds into lived transformation and understanding.

## Key Points
- Unplanned travel allows collapse of scripted expectations into authentic lived experience and discovery.
- Confronting fragility at Pompeii contrasts with lightness of Amalfi, revealing layers of human existence.
- Renaissance Florence models how patronage and intellectual freedom enable artists to transcend craftsmanship.

## Open Question
How does one balance surrender to the moment with intentional design of meaningful experiences, especially when both seem equally valuable?
