## Core Argument
Pandemics end socially when societies normalize pathogen coexistence, not when cases disappear.

## Key Points
- Expert perception filters noise; experience enables selective attention and fewer cognitive demands
- Scarcity and constraint create engagement; Wordle succeeded by limiting access, not maximizing it
- Pandemic ends when collective behavior shifts from crisis response to endemic normalcy

## Open Question
If pandemic 'ends' when people stop reacting to it, have we truly contained the threat or merely accepted living with it?

## India Angle
India's pandemic response and exit strategy must balance public health metrics with social acceptance; how Indians are adapting to endemic COVID mirrors global patterns but with unique healthcare capacity and policy challenges
