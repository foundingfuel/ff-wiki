## Core Argument
Convert customers into brand advocates to drive growth without expensive advertising.

## Key Points
- Brand advocates are more valuable than merely satisfied or loyal customers.
- Organic advocacy reduces reliance on paid advertising and marketing budgets.
- Building customer communities creates sustainable competitive advantage through word-of-mouth.

## Open Question
How do you systematically identify and nurture advocates without creating inauthentic or transactional relationships that undermine genuine loyalty?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs often operate with constrained marketing budgets; leveraging customer advocacy as organic growth strategy is particularly relevant for bootstrapped startups and SMEs scaling without large advertising spend.
