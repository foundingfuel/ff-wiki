## Core Argument
Organizational learning requires structured reflection, community support, and preventing problems before they arise.

## Key Points
- Teams need dedicated time for collective reflection on emotions, learnings, and gratitude to strengthen culture.
- Truth exists at ecosystem edges; middle management and stakeholders hold problem-prevention insights leaders miss.
- Post-crisis recovery demands reclaiming basic joys—human connection, movement, presence—alongside business resumption.

## Open Question
How do organizations balance urgent business recovery with the deeper work of reflection and emotional processing that builds resilience?

## India Angle
Indian organizations need to institutionalize reflection practices; mid-day meal scheme example shows how problem prevention at systemic level creates generational impact in India.
