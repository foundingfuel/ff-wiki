## Core Argument
US-China tech war will balkanize the internet, with India as a crucial contested sphere of influence.

## Key Points
- China evolved from manufacturing imitator to AI peer-competitor with full-spectrum technological capabilities.
- Control of IoT nodes and internet infrastructure has become weaponized interdependence and geopolitical leverage.
- India risks being caught between superpowers; US firms rapidly filling space vacated by banned Chinese apps.

## Open Question
Can India navigate strategic autonomy and pragmatism while both superpowers compete for influence, or will it be forced into ideological alignment with either bloc?

## India Angle
India sits at the centre of US-China tech competition; the banning of Chinese apps and subsequent US investment scramble reveals India as a critical geopolitical battleground. Indian tech ecosystem faces pressure to choose sides while managing national interests pragmatically.
