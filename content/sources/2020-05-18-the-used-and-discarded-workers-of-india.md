## Core Argument
Employer freedom without worker dignity destroys long-term value; data proves investing in workers outperforms cost-cutting.

## Key Points
- Bain study validated that companies treating workers as assets outperform peers despite identical market conditions
- Contract workers comprising 50%+ of large company workforces lack equal wages/representation despite doing identical work
- 96% of India's workforce operates outside formal protections; labour reforms risk expanding this vulnerability

## Open Question
If investing in workers provably improves long-term performance, why do most Indian employers persist with cost-cutting contractor models, and will labour law reforms enable or accelerate this contradiction?

## India Angle
India's labour law reforms risk deepening exploitation of 96% of informal/contract workers by prioritizing employer flexibility over worker protection, particularly exposed during COVID-19 lockdowns when millions were abandoned.
