## Core Argument
Tesla dominates through value migration to clean transportation, but true sustainability remains ambiguous.

## Key Points
- Tesla shifted customer value from transportation to clean transportation, capturing willingness to pay premium.
- Software-first design reduces maintenance costs and ownership burden, appealing to time-poor millennials.
- Battery production and coal-powered grids create hidden environmental costs offsetting EV green credentials.

## Open Question
How can EVs claim environmental superiority when their lifecycle emissions and power source dependency vary dramatically by geography, and does perceived sustainability matter more to consumers than actual environmental impact?

## India Angle
India's 62% coal-dependent power grid undermines EV environmental claims; Tesla Model 3's affordability and software-first design appeal to India's aspirational millennials, but sustainability benefits are compromised by India's energy infrastructure reality.
