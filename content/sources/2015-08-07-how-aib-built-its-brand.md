## Core Argument
Digital-native comedy collectives can scale from niche creators to mainstream entertainment brands by understanding youth culture.

## Key Points
- AIB shifted comedy from niche to mainstream via YouTube, reaching millions of urban Indian youth with irreverent content
- Four creative founders built sustainable venture by formalizing as company and partnering with management/distribution expertise (OML)
- Digital platforms enabled direct audience connection, bypassing traditional gatekeepers and creating new career pathways for comedians

## Open Question
How do comedy collectives monetize sustained virality and maintain creative authenticity while scaling into a formal, commercially-driven organization?

## India Angle
AIB democratised comedy for Indian youth via digital platforms, moving entertainment from niche elite spaces to mainstream accessible content, reshaping what young Indians consume and aspire to as careers.
