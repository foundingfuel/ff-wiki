## Core Argument
Knowledge and political ideas have measurable half-lives; ignoring this enables monopolies and stifles entrepreneurship.

## Key Points
- Medical knowledge becomes obsolete within 45 years; practitioners must continuously update or risk harm.
- Indian politicians have ~3-year effective tenure despite 5-year terms due to honeymoon, stagnation, and opposition cycles.
- Power-holders resist challenger narratives and new ideas to maintain status quo, harming innovation and choice.

## Open Question
How do societies incentivize continuous learning and renewal when power structures inherently resist disruption?

## India Angle
Indian politicians operate with a ~3-year effective half-life despite 5-year terms; regulatory bodies struggle to enforce obsolescence of outdated practices; media control proposals reflect power consolidation rather than evidence-based governance.
