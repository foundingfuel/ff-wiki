## Core Argument
Mastery requires mentors who deliver tough love and reality; institutions prioritizing profit over feedback culture prevent growth.

## Key Points
- Masters suffered through criticism and setbacks; they can gauge weakness and provide proper challenges for advancement.
- Indian TV newsrooms suppress dissent through 'arm-twisting' when journalists question editorial decisions based on TRP pressure.
- Future divides will separate those with access to lifelong learning and honest feedback from those denied it.

## Open Question
How can organizations cultivate mastery-building mentorship when commercial survival pressures incentivize silencing critical feedback and alternative viewpoints?

## India Angle
Indian newsrooms exemplify the tension between mastery-driven mentorship and commercial pressure; young journalists silenced when questioning editorial choices reveals how 'tough love' mentoring is absent in high-pressure media environments where survival trumps development.
