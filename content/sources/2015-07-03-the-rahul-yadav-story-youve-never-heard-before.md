## Core Argument
Founder brilliance alone cannot scale; organisational capability and experienced leadership determine long-term success.

## Key Points
- Young founders possess exceptional vision and intensity but struggle with transition from startup outfit to scalable organisation
- VCs back people and talent but cannot substitute for professional management capability needed at scale
- Founder-investor misalignment emerges when rapid growth outpaces organisational infrastructure and governance maturity

## Open Question
If VCs are skilled at backing talent, why do they not proactively ensure founders build necessary organisational capabilities before scaling becomes unmanageable?
