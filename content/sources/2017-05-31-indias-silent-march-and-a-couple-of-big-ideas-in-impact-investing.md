## Core Argument
Impact investing in India's foundational services and financial inclusion through IndiaStack will drive the nation's 30-year growth trajectory.

## Key Points
- Impact funds investing in microfinance, healthcare, agriculture, education are positioned for India's significant future growth opportunities.
- IndiaStack digital platforms can solve India's credit access crisis by building data trails and enabling instant KYC at low cost.
- India's 9% household debt-to-GDP ratio reflects informal credit dependence, not debt aversion—formal financial inclusion remains untapped.

## Open Question
How can global capital markets focused on AI, technology, and scaled disruption be convinced to patient, multi-billion dollar investments in India's mundane but foundational sectors like agriculture and basic services?

## India Angle
India's household debt-to-GDP ratio of 9% and informal credit dependence presents a massive opportunity for impact investing and financial inclusion through IndiaStack. Digital platforms like UPI and eKYC can unlock formal credit access for underserved populations, addressing both creditworthiness perception and customer acquisition costs.
