## Core Argument
WFH stress isn't distraction but two worlds colliding; managing it requires rituals, self-awareness, and realistic acceptance of limitations.

## Key Points
- Create personal rituals (exercise, meditation) before work to 'get into the zone' and manage stress physiology
- Acknowledge reality of home environment constraints rather than fight them; adjust expectations and boundaries
- Monitor your stress indicators (heart rate, breathing) and prioritise recovery time over productivity lists

## Open Question
How do individual stress-management techniques address systemic WFH problems—unclear work boundaries, expectation of constant availability, inadequate home infrastructure—that plague remote workers?

## India Angle
Indian knowledge workers navigating WFH during lockdown face compounded pressures—lack of domestic help, multi-generational households, boundary-less communication culture via WhatsApp. Solutions must account for India's specific domestic and social contexts.
