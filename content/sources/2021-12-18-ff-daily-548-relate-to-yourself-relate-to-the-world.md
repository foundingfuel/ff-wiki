## Core Argument
Self-relationship through solitude enables authentic connection with the world and sustained creative capacity.

## Key Points
- Solitude is a learnable practice, not isolation—achievable even in crowds through mental centering.
- Deep personal nourishment in solitude creates capacity to give meaningfully to others and organisations.
- Creative breakthroughs require alternating between focused task networks and default mode networks for insight.

## Open Question
How can high-performance cultures systematically protect solitude and reflection time when every system incentivises constant connection and reactivity?

## India Angle
Indian professionals facing burnout in high-intensity work environments need to reclaim solitude and self-relationship as foundational practices for sustained performance and leadership effectiveness.
