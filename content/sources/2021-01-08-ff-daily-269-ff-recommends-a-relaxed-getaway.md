## Core Argument
Safe domestic travel requires detailed planning, word-of-mouth vetting, and meticulous hygiene protocols during pandemic recovery.

## Key Points
- Driving holidays minimize exposure; six-hour radius limits breaks and contact points during travel.
- Word-of-mouth verification of hotel protocols more reliable than advertised Covid-19 safety measures.
- Small details—sanitized ID returns, masked staff, disinfected tables—rebuild guest confidence in hygiene.

## Open Question
How do destinations balance reopening for economic recovery while genuinely maintaining the rigorous hygiene standards that reassure cautious travelers?

## India Angle
Post-Covid travel recovery in India: domestic driving holidays as safe getaway option; leveraging Indian infrastructure (ferries, national parks, resorts) with hygiene protocols.
