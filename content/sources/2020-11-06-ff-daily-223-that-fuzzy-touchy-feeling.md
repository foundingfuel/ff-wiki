## Core Argument
Pandemic revealed touch as human necessity while technology offers new tactile connection possibilities.

## Key Points
- Touch taboo created by pandemic and #MeToo exposes deeper human need for physical connection.
- Technology like haptic devices could bridge distance for elderly, isolated, and orphaned populations globally.
- Professional excellence requires continuous learning, skill refreshes, and competing for relevance each season.

## Open Question
How can technology genuinely replace human touch without creating new dependencies or further isolating vulnerable populations?
