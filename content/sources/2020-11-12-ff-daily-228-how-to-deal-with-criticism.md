## Core Argument
Uninformed criticism loses power when leaders remain unmoved and focused on substantive work, not emotional reaction.

## Key Points
- LKY dismissed Safire's attacks as 'gadfly' noise irrelevant to Singapore's progress, refusing emotional investment.
- Safire violated his own journalism principle by criticizing LKY without ever visiting Singapore or meeting him.
- Leaders who engage in shouting matches over abuse-laden criticism lose the argument; silence and continued work prove more powerful.

## Open Question
If leaders should ignore uninformed critics, how do they distinguish between noise and legitimate accountability concerns that deserve response?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders and policymakers face intense scrutiny in polarized times; LKY's framework for handling uninformed criticism applies to navigating reputation challenges in India's fractious media and political environment.
