## Core Argument
Crisis leadership requires emotional equanimity, reframed objectives, and present-focused decision-making over speed.

## Key Points
- Emotional equanimity and signal control are foundational; leaders must size situations objectively before acting.
- Reframe immediate objectives away from end-term goals; focus on containment and equilibrium restoration first.
- Credibility through accountability and transparent, empathetic messaging builds trust when institutions face existential threats.

## Open Question
How do leaders balance the pressure to reassure stakeholders and maintain credibility with the need to make decisions under uncertainty when full information isn't available?

## India Angle
Indian business leaders, especially in startups and logistics, face similar existential crises; the framework applies directly to how Indian entrepreneurs manage reputation, stakeholder trust, and capital preservation during disruptions like Covid-19.
