## Core Argument
Selling is empathy-led, process-driven discipline, not aggressive winner-take-all masculinity.

## Key Points
- Listening to customers and understanding their needs builds lasting relationships and trust.
- Early exposure to sales builds self-confidence and teaches crucial entrepreneurial mindset lessons.
- Modern selling requires knowledge-intensive, empathy-driven approach applicable across products, services, and ideas.

## Open Question
How do sales professionals maintain integrity and customer-centricity while hitting aggressive revenue targets in competitive markets?

## India Angle
Indian entrepreneurs and leaders need to reframe selling as empathy-driven relationship-building rather than aggressive transaction-seeking—critical for scaling Indian startups and enterprises in competitive markets.
