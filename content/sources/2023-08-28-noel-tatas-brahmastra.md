## Core Argument
Zudio perfects the Turns Retailer model, combining Zara's supply chain precision with affordable pricing for emerging Indian consumer classes.

## Key Points
- Expansion into non-metro towns reflects organized retail's shift toward India's new consuming classes in underserved geographies.
- Zudio's 'patient playbook'—testing, iterating, perfecting before scale—contrasts with Reliance's full-throttle expansion approach.
- Fast turnover with limited SKUs (core styles 35% of sales) enables good quality at half Westside prices in tier-3+ markets.

## Open Question
Can Zudio sustain its model if faster, more aggressive competitors (Reliance's Yousta, Shein-backed plays) replicate the formula without the patience phase?

## India Angle
Zudio's expansion into Tier 3+ towns (Jharsuguda, Dimapur, Imphal) signals organized retail's penetration of 'Bharat'—unlocking consumption in previously underserved geographies. This demonstrates how Indian businesses can leverage improved logistics, GST infrastructure, and supply chain discipline to serve new consumer classes beyond metros.
