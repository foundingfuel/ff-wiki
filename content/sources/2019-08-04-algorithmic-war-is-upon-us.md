## Core Argument
China's state-backed AI ecosystem and data access through apps threaten India's technological autonomy and strategic independence.

## Key Points
- China combines state sponsorship with entrepreneurial ventures to build integrated AI ecosystems across healthcare and consumer services.
- Chinese apps already have deep user penetration in India, positioning them to capture vast data for training superior AI models.
- India risks becoming dependent on Chinese AI for critical insights on healthcare trends, treatment options, and strategic intelligence.

## Open Question
How can India develop indigenous AI capability fast enough to prevent strategic dependency on Chinese systems without compromising privacy or stifling innovation?

## India Angle
India faces immediate risk of AI dominance by China through data capture via ubiquitous Chinese apps and smartphones, threatening sovereignty over healthcare insights and creating dependency on foreign AI systems for critical information.
