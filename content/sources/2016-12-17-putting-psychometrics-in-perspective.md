## Core Argument
Psychometric tools cannot reliably measure human behavior or predict leadership success; their misuse filters out exceptional leaders.

## Key Points
- Psychometric tools lack calibrated measurement scales for human behavior; Likert scales offer judgment, not quantifiable measurement.
- KV Kamath's challenge revealed that 49 of 50 proven leaders would have been rejected by standard IQ and profiling tests.
- Blind adoption of off-the-shelf Western assessment tools ignores their limitations and unintended consequences for talent selection.

## Open Question
If psychometric tools are fundamentally flawed at measuring behavior, what rigorous yet human-centered alternative should organizations use for leader selection and development?

## India Angle
Indian organizations blindly adopt Western psychometric tools for hiring managers and leaders without understanding limitations or validating against local contexts and track records—a critical gap for talent development in India's growing companies.
