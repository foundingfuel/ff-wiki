## Core Argument
Organizational culture integration matters more than technical capability when bridging private and public sectors.

## Key Points
- Private and public sectors have different cultural norms (first names vs. honorifics) that create friction beyond surface-level etiquette.
- Successful collaboration requires both sides to acknowledge each other's strengths and coexist rather than compete for ownership.
- Leaders must actively manage cultural integration; tensions diffuse through sustained working relationships and understanding.

## Open Question
How can government retain ownership and accountability of large digital projects while successfully absorbing private-sector innovation practices without diluting bureaucratic authority structures?

## India Angle
Illustrates India's core institutional challenge: integrating private-sector efficiency and innovation culture with government bureaucracy's hierarchy and accountability structures—critical for large-scale projects like Aadhaar and digital transformation initiatives.
