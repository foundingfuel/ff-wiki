## Core Argument
Weak social ties expose people to diverse views, challenging echo chambers and enabling broader perspective-taking.

## Key Points
- Facebook's weak ties expose users to opposing political views more than offline relationships do
- Online platforms create accidental diversity by connecting acquaintances who wouldn't interact in physical spaces
- This inadvertent exposure may reduce political polarization despite homophilic friendship networks

## Open Question
If weak ties reduce polarization, why do social media platforms still amplify political division and outrage despite their structural diversity?

## India Angle
India's WTO proposal to suspend pharma IP rights during Covid shows how weak social connections (diverse viewpoints) matter for policy debate; relevant to India's innovation ecosystem and access-to-medicine challenges
