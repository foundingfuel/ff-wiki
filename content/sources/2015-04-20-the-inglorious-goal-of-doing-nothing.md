## Core Argument
Pursuing 'nothing' after success atrophies human potential; meaningful contribution, not leisure, fulfils our nature.

## Key Points
- Money alone doesn't unlock freedom; the belief in 'Duck You Money' is a delusion without purpose.
- Empty time doesn't auto-convert to achievement; mastery requires systematic effort equivalent to work.
- Human brains evolved for complexity and challenge; designing life around simplicity and peace contradicts our wiring.

## Open Question
How do we honour legitimate desires to escape toxic work cultures without normalising a culture where success equals withdrawal from meaningful contribution?

## India Angle
India's emerging wealth class faces a crisis of purpose post-exit; the 'retire by 40' phenomenon reflects broader tension between escaping India's demanding corporate culture and the risk of societal brain drain when talented founders abandon contribution.
