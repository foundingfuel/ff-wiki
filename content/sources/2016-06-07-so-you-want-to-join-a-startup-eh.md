## Core Argument
Rationality in startup market makes early-stage joining valuable if you prioritize learning over security.

## Key Points
- Startups now operate more sensibly post-hype cycle; sanity returning to market valuations and hiring
- Self-assessment crucial: ambition, uncertainty tolerance, and work-life trade-offs determine startup fit
- Focus on learning and grit over salary in formative years; only those who can afford risk should join

## Open Question
How do young graduates reconcile the long-term career benefits of startup exposure with immediate financial pressures and family expectations in India's risk-averse culture?

## India Angle
