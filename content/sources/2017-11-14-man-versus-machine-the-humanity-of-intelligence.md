## Core Argument
AI cannot replace human empathy; trust emerges from understanding, not algorithms alone.

## Key Points
- Sriram's 'Sriram Recommends' succeeded because it encoded personal empathy and lived experience into curation, not algorithmic filtering.
- Amazon acquired Goodreads and Apple employs humans alongside AI because algorithms lack the intuitive understanding needed to inspire exploration beyond past behavior.
- Designing for human needs—safety, dignity, choice—requires lived understanding that AI cannot independently generate without human input.

## Open Question
If AI fundamentally cannot empathize or inspire discovery beyond algorithmic prediction, can any amount of human oversight solve this, or does it just delay the obsolescence of human judgment?

## India Angle
Indian retail and consumer trust ecosystems rely on personal relationships and human judgment; as AI scales in India, the challenge is embedding human empathy into algorithmic systems while preserving the trust-building that founders like Sriram exemplified in early modern Indian retail.
