## Core Argument
Boards add competitive advantage when structured by competency, engaged continuously, and positioned as strategic partners, not compliance gatekeepers.

## Key Points
- Map required competencies first, then recruit individuals—not the reverse—to ensure strategic fit.
- Move beyond quarterly meetings: monthly updates and ad-hoc access enable boards to add ongoing value.
- Board members need specialist expertise plus generalist understanding; they must embrace critique and consensus-building.

## Open Question
How do founder-led companies balance founder authority with genuine board independence and challenge when the founder controls the board agenda?

## India Angle
Indian family-run and founder-led businesses often treat boards as compliance entities; Mariwala's model shows how founder-CEOs can architect boards as strategic assets, a critical practice for scaling Indian companies globally and professionally.
