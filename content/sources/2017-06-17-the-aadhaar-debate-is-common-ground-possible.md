## Core Argument
Aadhaar debates collapse into polarization; moving beyond ideology to examine design, implementation, scope, and legal frameworks enables productive dialogue.

## Key Points
- Polarized debates descend into questioning intent and motivations rather than examining specific technical and policy choices.
- Five distinct argument categories exist—design, implementation, scope, speed, legal framework—but collapse into binary good/bad framings.
- Moving past ideology to pragmatic analysis allows participants to support some aspects while critiquing others, expanding productive participation.

## Open Question
Can India develop institutional mechanisms to separate technical/policy critique from ideological attribution of intent in transformative state projects?

## India Angle
Aadhaar is a uniquely Indian policy challenge requiring reasoned debate beyond polarization; how India navigates this shapes governance credibility and citizen trust in state capacity.
