## Core Argument
AI mastery requires hands-on experimentation, not theory; educators must recalibrate learning goals from knowledge retention to critical thinking.

## Key Points
- Experimentation beats analysis paralysis—start using AI tools immediately rather than studying them theoretically.
- Education must shift from testing memory to teaching thinking, as AI outpaces human knowledge retrieval and processing.
- AI natives vs. immigrants divide emerges; those who adapt early gain advantage, raising questions about equity and inclusion.

## Open Question
How do societies ensure equitable adaptation for 'AI immigrants' who can't or won't adapt, especially in education systems where early movers gain structural advantage?

## India Angle
Indian education system faces recalibration as AI disrupts traditional learning models; opportunity to reshape teaching from memorization to critical thinking, with research underway on how AI reshapes elite vs. mainstream schools.
