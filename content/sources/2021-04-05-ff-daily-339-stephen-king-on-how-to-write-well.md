## Core Argument
Mastery requires deliberate practice: read voraciously and write constantly; there are no shortcuts.

## Key Points
- Writing skill demands serious work; competency requires commitment, not talent alone.
- Reading widely—across mediocre and great work—is foundational to developing a writer's craft.
- Eliminating distractions (TV, endless browsing) creates space for deep reading and better writing.

## Open Question
If writing is teachable through reading and practice alone, why do so few people who read and write actually develop into good writers?
