## Core Argument
Hard work alone is insufficient; ownership, collaboration, and human connection drive sustainable high performance.

## Key Points
- Musk's ownership model creates intrinsic motivation but demands extreme personal sacrifice and limits collaborative benefits.
- Reverse migration reveals employers must compete on wages, amenities, and human treatment to retain workers.
- Leaders must model new behaviours explicitly; silent standard-setting fails—culture requires visible, repeated reinforcement.

## Open Question
How do organisations balance the individual velocity gains of solo deep work against the long-term resilience and retention benefits of collaboration and psychological safety?

## India Angle
Indian manufacturers face similar labour retention challenges post-pandemic; leadership practices that build trust and fair treatment will determine competitiveness as workers gain mobility and choice.
