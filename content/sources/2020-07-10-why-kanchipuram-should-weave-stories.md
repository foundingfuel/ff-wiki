## Core Argument
Kanchipuram weavers must leverage storytelling to justify premium pricing while technology produces mass alternatives.

## Key Points
- Kanchipuram's competitive advantage lies in know-how and brand, not natural resources like other clusters
- Cultural meaning—auspiciousness, exclusivity, ritual significance—defines product value beyond the physical sari
- Dual coexistence model: handwoven premium products differentiated through stories, technology-enabled mass production at lower cost

## Open Question
How can traditional craftspeople remain economically viable when technology can replicate their product at scale, especially when younger generations abandon the trade for better wages elsewhere?

## India Angle
Kanchipuram silk saris represent a distinctly Indian cultural product facing modernization pressures; the solution of storytelling-driven branding applies directly to Indian MSMEs competing globally while preserving craft heritage.
