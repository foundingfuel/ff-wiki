## Core Argument
Entrepreneurs must reject business lacking competence, equality, trust, and mutual gain despite revenue pressure.

## Key Points
- Say no when you lack competence and customer's reputation depends on delivery—enthusiasm without capability harms everyone.
- Egoless behaviour doesn't mean servility; refuse customers who demand self-respect sacrifice or won't accept disagreement.
- Screen customers on four criteria: professional value-add, sense of equality, trust, and commercial win-win proposition.

## Open Question
How do resource-constrained early-stage founders in India balance survival (taking any business) with principle-based customer selection without jeopardizing growth?

## India Angle
For Indian entrepreneurs scaling businesses, understanding when to reject business is crucial in markets where customer relationships are transactional and power imbalances common; this framework helps founders preserve organizational culture while pursuing growth.
