## Core Argument
Effective speaking is a teachable superpower; presentation literacy should become education's fourth R alongside reading, writing, and arithmetic.

## Key Points
- Zoom fatigue stems from speakers' self-absorption rather than medium itself; ancient storytelling principle still applies to modern communication.
- Women lost jobs disproportionately during lockdown despite comprising only 11% of workforce, continuing patterns from demonetization and GST.
- Merit-based individualism obscures societal dependence on undervalued essential workers; dignity of work requires rethinking rewards and esteem.

## Open Question
If presentation literacy is teachable and empowering, why does Zoom-based communication—which should democratize speaking—produce fatigue rather than engagement?

## India Angle
India's disproportionate impact on women's employment during lockdown reflects systemic barriers to female labour participation; Sandel's merit critique applies to Indian hierarchies that undervalue essential workers in gig economy and informal sectors.
