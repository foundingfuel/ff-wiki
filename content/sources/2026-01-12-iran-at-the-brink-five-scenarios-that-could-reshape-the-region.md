## Core Argument
Iran's internal collapse could reshape Middle East geopolitics with cascading effects on global energy, trade routes, and India's regional ambitions.

## Key Points
- Protests driven by economic crisis and regime discontent pose regime collapse risk amid international pressure from US, Israel, and Reza Pahlavi.
- Iran's instability threatens 22% of global oil flows through Strait of Hormuz, Qatar gas supplies to China/EU/India, and critical Indian infrastructure projects.
- Five scenarios range from temporary suppression to regime collapse; fourth scenario of Pahlavi-led transition backed by US/Israel carries greatest uncertainty for proxy management.

## Open Question
How can a post-regime transition authority credibly dismantle Iran's proxy networks (Hezbollah, Hamas, Houthis) without triggering internecine violence or external intervention?

## India Angle
Iran's instability directly threatens India's strategic interests: IMEC corridor delays, disruption of INSTC and Chabahar port operations, and vulnerabilities in energy supply chains via the Persian Gulf and Qatar gas contracts.
