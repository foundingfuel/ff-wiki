## Core Argument
AI disruption is inevitable; leaders must redefine purpose beyond automation and profit maximization.

## Key Points
- AI will create unprecedented free time; the moral question is how society uses it—for profit, purpose, or reflection.
- India Stack + cloud computing + AI convergence offers pathway to serve India's poor, per Nilekani-Nadella dialogue.
- Technology has outpaced society's ability to understand it; old business models (stockpiling) must shift to crowdsourcing and experimentation.

## Open Question
If automation frees human time and resources, who decides how they're redistributed—markets, government, or individuals—and how do we prevent wealth concentration rather than liberation?

## India Angle
India's AI opportunity lies in leveraging India Stack and cloud integration (Nadella-Nilekani) to benefit the poor; domestic leaders must navigate disruption while maintaining purpose beyond profit—critical for India's development trajectory.
