## Core Argument
Traditional retailers must adopt omni-channel strategies to compete with e-tailers and serve non-linear millennial shoppers.

## Key Points
- Millennial shoppers use multiple channels seamlessly—social media, reviews, stores, price comparison, mobile checkout—not linear paths.
- E-tailers exploit 'fit lifting' behavior; traditional retailers must embrace omni-channel presence across web, apps, social, payment gateways.
- Progressive retailers win by leveraging unique advantages: trained consultants, smaller stores with warehouse access, price transparency, extended warranties, personalized finance.

## Open Question
How can traditional retailers justify the technology investment and operational complexity of omni-channel retailing when e-tailers have first-mover advantage, deep pockets, and lower cost structures?

## India Angle
Indian traditional retailers—from organized chains to unorganized stores—must urgently adapt to millennial shopping patterns or lose market share to aggressive e-commerce players. This is critical for protecting domestic retail employment and brick-and-mortar ecosystems.
