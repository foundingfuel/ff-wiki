## Core Argument
EV adoption in India depends less on technology than on solving home charging and affordability for mass market.

## Key Points
- Experience superiority (smoothness, quietness) drives adoption among early adopters willing to pay premium.
- Economics favourable for two-wheelers and premium car buyers with home charging; unclear for mass-market four-wheelers.
- Home charging access and residential electricity rates are gatekeepers to mainstream EV viability in India.

## Open Question
How can EVs achieve mass-market penetration for passenger cars when affordability and home-charging infrastructure remain inaccessible to most Indian buyers, despite the experience advantage being undisputed?

## India Angle
EV adoption in India is bifurcated: two-wheelers are mainstream (1 in 5), but cars lag (1 in 50). The economics work for premium city buyers with home charging, but mass-market viability depends on residential electricity access—a critical infrastructure and affordability challenge unique to India.
