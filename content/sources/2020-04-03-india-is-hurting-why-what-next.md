## Core Argument
India's slowdown stems from inequality and weak institutions; recovery requires local solutions and faster learning systems.

## Key Points
- Top-down centralized approaches fail in India's diversity; local adaptive solutions with basic rules work better.
- Rich-poor divide amplified by crisis; public investment in health, education, infrastructure drives growth.
- Policy-practice gap cripples startups: tax breaks, debt schemes have near-zero uptake due to poor implementation.

## Open Question
India has excellent technological connectivity even in poor districts, yet helplessness rises and migration to opportunity centers declines—why does better information access not translate to better outcomes?

## India Angle
India's economic slowdown is rooted in unequal wealth distribution, weak public goods investment, and policy-implementation gaps. Top-down solutions fail in India's diversity; local adaptive systems and faster institutional learning are essential for recovery.
