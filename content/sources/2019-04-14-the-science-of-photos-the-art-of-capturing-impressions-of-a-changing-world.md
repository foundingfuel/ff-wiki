## Core Argument
Technological disruption mirrors artistic movements—both emerge when tools obsolete old practices, forcing creative adaptation.

## Key Points
- Photography displaced realistic painting but enabled impressionism's focus on light, movement, and subjective perception.
- India's real challenge is raising wages and productivity, not preventing job losses from automation.
- Technology-driven small enterprises, not massive factories, will generate employment and adapt to changing consumer behaviour.

## Open Question
If technology eliminates certain skills and ways of working, how do we ensure transitions don't leave behind those unable to adapt quickly enough?

## India Angle
India faces a wages crisis, not jobs crisis; technology can boost productivity and create employment through small, nimble enterprises rather than displacing workers. India's challenge is applying technology to improve productivity while building skills for a changing workforce.
