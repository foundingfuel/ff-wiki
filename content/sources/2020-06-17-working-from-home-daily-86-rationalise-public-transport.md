## Core Argument
Public transport systems need staggered timings and inter-agency coordination to prevent post-crisis congestion spikes.

## Key Points
- European cities stagger school/office start times to reduce peak-hour transit demand and congestion.
- Successful implementation requires collaboration between transit operators, city governments, and institutions.
- Crisis amplifies existing system weaknesses; calm, data-driven decision-making essential for institutional response.

## Open Question
How can India's fragmented municipal governance and competing institutional priorities align on staggered timings when even developed economies struggle with inter-agency coordination?

## India Angle
Indian cities face severe public transport congestion; staggered timings model from Europe/Israel could help metros like Delhi, Mumbai manage post-pandemic commute flows and reduce infrastructure strain.
