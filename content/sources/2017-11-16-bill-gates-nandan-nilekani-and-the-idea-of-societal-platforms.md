## Core Argument
Societal platforms enable systems-level change faster than traditional pipes by involving multiple stakeholders at scale.

## Key Points
- India's development lag demands urgency; traditional problem-solving methods insufficient for scale and speed needed.
- Platforms attract producers and consumers; scale faster than pipe models by accommodating niche and mass solutions simultaneously.
- Systems change requires addressing underlying patterns, not one-time interventions; societal platforms coordinate stakeholders toward lasting transformation.

## Open Question
How do societal platforms avoid becoming extractive like commercial platforms, and who ensures accountability when multiple actors coordinate without clear hierarchical control?

## India Angle
India's human development crisis requires speed and scale; societal platforms offer a model to move beyond traditional government schemes and single-point interventions, critical for India's inequality and poverty challenges.
