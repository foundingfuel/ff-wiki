## Core Argument
Managers accept 25% failure rate in hiring despite zero-defect standards elsewhere; identifying 'right stuff' requires rethinking selection metrics.

## Key Points
- Typical manager has ~33% superb hires, 40% adequate, 25% wrong—unacceptable by manufacturing standards.
- Current hiring metrics fail to predict actual job performance; deeper introspection needed on selection criteria.
- Identifying 'right stuff' in people requires different approach than conventional assessment methods.

## Open Question
If conventional screening doesn't predict success, what specific assessment method actually identifies high performers before hiring?

## India Angle
Indian managers and organisations face similar hiring challenges; with rapid scaling in startups and large companies, improving talent selection accuracy from 75% to higher rates is critical for sustainable growth.
