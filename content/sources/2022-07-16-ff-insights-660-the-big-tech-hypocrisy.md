## Core Argument
Big Tech's climate commitments mask inaction on emissions reduction; pledges exceed actual progress.

## Key Points
- Tech companies prioritize carbon removal investments over preventing emissions at source despite having operational control.
- Microsoft and Salesforce emissions grew despite climate pledges, revealing misalignment between stated goals and business expansion.
- Institutional barriers and government apathy require entrepreneurs and pioneers to create parallel systems outside state infrastructure.

## Open Question
If Big Tech companies cannot reduce their own operational emissions despite resources and expertise, how credible is their leadership in scaling carbon removal technology for others?

## India Angle
India faces similar institutional challenges—government apathy, corruption, and regulatory barriers to innovation in critical sectors like healthcare and climate tech. The article highlights how private pioneers must bypass bureaucratic obstacles to address public goods, a recurring pattern in Indian development.
