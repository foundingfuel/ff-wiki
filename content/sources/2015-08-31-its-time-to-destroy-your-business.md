## Core Argument
Legacy businesses must destroy their core models and become digital platforms to survive disruption.

## Key Points
- Algorithms, digitization, and sensors are rendering conventional FMCG models obsolete.
- Winning companies own consumer data and relationships, not just products.
- Platform strategy—not product excellence—is the new competitive moat.

## Open Question
How can established FMCG companies with deep distribution and brand equity in physical retail genuinely transition to platform models without cannibalizing their core revenue streams?

## India Angle
Indian FMCG leaders like Marico must fundamentally reimagine their business models from product-centric to digital-platform-centric to survive. This requires overcoming the legacy thinking that has dominated Indian family businesses and adopting Silicon Valley-style platform strategies.
