## Core Argument
Stillness and intentional slowness unlock adaptability; crisis forces digital transformation and peer collaboration.

## Key Points
- Peacebird empowered frontline workers to experiment with online sales channels during lockdown, turning crisis into growth
- Indian entrepreneurs with disabilities used WhatsApp groups and Zoom to connect, learn, and identify new market opportunities like mask manufacturing
- Multichannel integration (online+offline) and rapid retraining of employees became competitive advantages during forced business model shifts

## Open Question
How do we sustain the agility and peer-learning networks built during crisis once normal operations resume?

## India Angle
Indian MSMEs and entrepreneurs with disabilities face similar lockdown challenges; At Par's example shows how Indian entrepreneurs adapted digitally through WhatsApp and Zoom, enabling peer networks and business pivots—relevant for India's informal economy and inclusive entrepreneurship
