## Core Argument
Simple rules and heuristics outperform complex models when deciding under uncertainty and limited information.

## Key Points
- Risk involves known probabilities; uncertainty requires adaptive toolboxes of tested heuristics and experience.
- Ecological rationality—knowing which heuristic fits which context—matters more than pure logical reasoning.
- Interdisciplinary teams in informal settings innovate better by breaking disciplinary silos through daily interaction.

## Open Question
If simple heuristics work better than complex models in uncertainty, why do organisations and institutions continue investing heavily in algorithmic complexity and big data solutions?

## India Angle
Indian research scholars and institutions need to embrace heuristics and bounded rationality frameworks for decision-making in uncertain environments—especially relevant for Indian businesses navigating volatile markets, healthcare systems improving diagnostic literacy, and AI developers building locally-relevant solutions.
