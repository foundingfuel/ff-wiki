## Core Argument
ITC's 40% stake retention in demerged hotels signals defensive strategy amid Reliance's aggressive hospitality push.

## Key Points
- ITC retaining 40% stake suggests hesitation to fully exit hotels; market questions whether takeover fears drove decision.
- Reliance-Oberoi partnership fundamentally reshapes three-way race between Tatas, ITC, and now Reliance-Oberoi combine.
- ITC's hotels division underperformed despite capital intensity; 20% capital employed generated single-digit returns.

## Open Question
Does ITC's 40% retention reflect genuine business synergies or protective anxiety about hostile takeover threats, and will this hybrid structure undermine the demerger's core value creation thesis?

## India Angle
ITC's demerger reshapes India's hospitality sector structure; Reliance-Oberoi alliance signals consolidation among India's three major hotel operators, with implications for competitive dynamics and foreign investor involvement in Indian hospitality.
